43186: Test Wulf Pounce
Used by the routine at HandlerWulf.
Makes the Wulf pounce (sprite IDs 88 and 92) when it is moving left, is in the same room as the player, is to the right of the player and the player is attacking to the right (sprite IDs 40 to 47). TestWulfPounceRight does the same for a Wulf moving right.
Input
IX Actor record of the Wulf

43193 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the pounce timer of the Wulf.

43199 LD A,(IX+6) Fetch the X velocity of the Wulf.
43202 AND A Jump to TestWulfPounceRight if the Wulf is moving right.

43216 SUB 40 Subtract 40.
43218 CP 8 Return if the player is not attacking to the right.
