44993: Limit Player Velocity
Used by the routine at AcceleratePlayer.
Limits a velocity to the top speed of the player, which depends on the colour of the player. The top speed is 32 for red (INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)), 64 for cyan (INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) and 48 otherwise. The velocity may be negative.
Input
A Velocity
IX Actor record of the player
Output
A The limited velocity
LimitPlayerVelocity 44993 PUSH AF Stash the velocity on the stack.
44994 LD A,(IX+5) Fetch the colour of the player.
44997 CP 66 Jump to SetSlowSpeedLimit if the player is red (INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)), which slows the player.
44999 JR Z,SetSlowSpeedLimit
45001 CP 69 Jump to SetFastSpeedLimit if the player is cyan (INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)), which speeds the player up.
45003 JR Z,SetFastSpeedLimit
45005 LD B,48 Set the normal maximum speed in B to 48.
This entry point is used by the routines at SetSlowSpeedLimit and SetFastSpeedLimit.
LimitPlayerVelocity_Restore 45007 POP AF Restore the velocity from the stack.
