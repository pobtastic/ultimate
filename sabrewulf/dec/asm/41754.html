41754: Colour Pickup
Used by the routines at HandlerAmuletPiece and HandlerExtraLife.
Sets the attributes of the screen cells covered by a pickup, which is a sprite 16 pixels high and two cells wide, using ColourEntity.
Input
IX Actor record of the pickup
ColourPickup 41754 LD HL,528 Set the height of the pickup in L to 16 and the number of columns in H to 2.
41757 LD (38565),HL Store the height and the number of columns in New_Sprite_Height and New_Sprite_Width.
41760 JP ColourEntity Jump to ColourEntity to colour the pickup.
