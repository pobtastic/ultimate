43520: Land Sabreman
Used by the routine at HandlerFlyingSabreman.
Makes the player sit down after flying through the air. The sprite ID goes up by one (to 65 or 69), a counter of 63 is set, the player is drawn and a noise is played.
Input
IX Actor record of the player
LandSabreman 43520 INC (IX+0) Move the player on to the sitting sprite.
43523 LD (IX+2),63 Set the sitting counter of the player to 63.
43527 CALL DisplayActor Display the player.
43530 JP PlayNoiseSound Jump to PlayNoiseSound to play the noise.
