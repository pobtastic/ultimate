39612: Spawn Flame
Used by the routine at UpdateCounters.
Called once every time all of the actors have been processed, after SpawnNastie. If the start-of-turn delay is over, the player is alive and the actor record at ImmortalData_SpareFlame is empty then a counter (FlameSpawnDelay) is counted down; when it reaches zero a flame (sprite ID 52) is started at a position chosen by PlaceFlame, unless that position is unsuitable.
Return unless the player is alive (the sprite ID of the player's actor is in the range 16-47).
