45139: Update Player Direction
Used by the routine at GamePlay_Start.
Sets the direction the player faces to match the way the player has moved. Bits 2-3 of the sprite ID hold the direction: 0 is left, 4 is right, 8 is up and 12 is down. The vertical movement is tested first, so if the player has moved both horizontally and vertically then the horizontal direction is used. The walking animation is also advanced (see AnimateWalk).
Input
D New Y position
E New X position
IX Actor record of the player
