44139: Reset Actors
Used by the routine at InitialisePlayer.
Copies the initial state of the 12 actors in InitialActors into the actor table at ImmortalData, which sets up the player and the immortal actors for a new game.
ResetActors 44139 LD HL,44151 Point HL at the initial state of the actors at InitialActors.
44142 LD DE,38658 Point DE at the actor table at ImmortalData.
44145 LD BC,144 Set the number of bytes to copy in BC to 144 (12 actors of 12 bytes).
44148 LDIR Copy the actors.
44150 RET Return.
