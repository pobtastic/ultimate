40526: Aim Flame Up
Used by the routine at AimFlameAtPlayer.
Reached when the player is above a flame. This writes 64 to the X velocity (*IX+6) of the flame and not to the Y velocity (*IX+7), which looks like a mistake in the original code.
Input
IX Flame record
AimFlameUp 40526 LD (IX+6),64 Set the X velocity of the flame to 64.
40530 RET Return.
