43298: Choose Wulf Direction
Used by the routine at HandlerWulf.
Sets the direction of the Wulf. If the Wulf is not in the same row of rooms as the player then nothing happens, except that IX+2 is reset. Otherwise, if IX+2 is zero, it is set to 1 and the Wulf is sent to the left or the right depending on whether the player is in a room to its left or right. If they are in the same room then the X positions are used instead (see CompareWulfPosition).
Input
IX Actor record of the Wulf
