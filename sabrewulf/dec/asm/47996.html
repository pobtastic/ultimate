47996: Clear Play Area Attribute Buffer
Used by the routines at GameComplete, ShowAmulet, HandlerPoems, GameOver_1UP_Messaging, EnterRoomBelow, InitialiseGame, SwitchPlayer and HandlerGameMenu.
Sets the attributes of the play area to 64 which is everything except the two rows at the top of the screen that hold the banner.
