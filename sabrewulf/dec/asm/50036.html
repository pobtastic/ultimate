50036: Amulet Graphics Table
The graphics which make up the amulet on the amulet screen (see ShowAmulet). There are four entries of four bytes, each of which is the address of a graphic followed by its X and Y position, and then a zero address ends the table. The first entry is used for the first bit of the amulet pieces collected (see 38669).
The graphics are the amulet outlines at Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Left, Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Right, Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Left and Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Right.
