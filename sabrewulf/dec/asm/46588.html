46588: New High Score
Used by the routine at GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Called when the game has ended to see whether either of the scores has made it into the hall of fame. The player with the highest score is dealt with first (see NewHighScore_Enter) and then the other player, so if both players have scored enough the higher score is entered first.
