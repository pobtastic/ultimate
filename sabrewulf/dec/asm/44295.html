44295: Handler: Rhino
Handles a rhino (sprite IDs 48 to 51). Bit 1 of the sprite ID is set when the rhino is facing right.
The rhino moves with its X velocity (see MoveActor) and stops and turns around if it is blocked, when its speed is also reset. If it touches the player then the player is hurt and the rhino turns around, unless the player is attacking, in which case the rhino is knocked back instead (see KnockBackRhino).
Input
IX Actor record of the rhino

This entry point is used by the routine at HandlerHippoWarthog.

Test whether the rhino has touched the player.

44337 RRCA Rotate right, so bit 1 is now in bit 0.
44338 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
44340 LD D,A Store the result in D (the hit direction for TestActorHurtingPlayer).
44341 LD BC,3868 Set the reach used to test whether the rhino is touching the player in BC to 3868.

44347 JR NC,UpdateActor Jump to UpdateActor if the rhino is not touching the player.
44349 LD A,(38660) Fetch the hit direction of the player from 38660.
44352 AND A Jump to KnockBackRhino if the player was not hit, which means the rhino was hit by the sword.
