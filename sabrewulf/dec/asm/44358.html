44358: Update Actor
Used by the routines at HandlerHippoWarthog, HandlerWulf, HandlerTribesman, HandlerRhino and KnockBackRhino.
Updates the display of an actor which has been moved. If the actor is in the same room as the player then it is drawn (see UpdateActorInRoom). If the actor is not in the room of the player but it was in the room last time (the room is held in Previous_Room) then it is removed from the screen.
Input
IX Actor record
UpdateActor 44358 LD A,(38659) Fetch the room of the player from 38659.
44361 LD C,A Stash the room of the player in C.
44362 CP (IX+1) Jump to UpdateActorInRoom if the actor is in the same room as the player.
44365 JR Z,UpdateActorInRoom
44367 LD A,(38562) Fetch the previous room of the actor from Previous_Room.
44370 CP C Return if the actor was not in the room of the player.
44371 RET NZ
44372 JP RemoveEntity Jump to RemoveEntity to remove the actor from the screen.
