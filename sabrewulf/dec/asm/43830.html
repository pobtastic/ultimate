43830: Test Actor Hurting Player
Used by the routines at HandlerHippoWarthog, HandlerSleepingHippoWarthog, HandlerChasingNastie, HandlerWanderingNastie, HandlerTribesman and HandlerRhino.
Tests whether an actor is touching the player, and if so whether the actor hurts the player. The test is only made when the player has the normal colour of 71 (see TestOrchidTouched) and is in the same room as the actor.
If the player is walking, or is attacking in the direction away from the actor, then they are hit and the direction they are hit in is written to 38660 (1 if bit 0 of D is set, or 2 otherwise). If the player is attacking towards the actor then the actor is hit and no hit direction is written.
Input
B How far above the actor the player can be
C How far to the right of the actor the player can be
D Bit 0 is set if the hit direction should be 1
IX Actor record
Output
F Carry flag set if the actor is touching the player
