45105: Get Whole Pixels
Used by the routines at CalculateNewPosition and MoveActor.
Divides a signed velocity by sixteen, to give the number of whole pixels to move. The direction is kept. For example, 48 gives 3 and 208 (-48) gives 253 (-3).
Input
A Velocity
Output
A Pixels to move
GetWholePixels 45105 AND A Jump to GetWholePixels_Positive if the velocity is positive.
45106 JP P,GetWholePixels_Positive
45109 NEG Make the velocity positive.
