42624: Handler: Orchid
Handles an orchid (sprite IDs 72 to 79). An orchid goes through a cycle of growing, staying in bloom for a short time, shrinking and then staying hidden for a while. The lowest three bits of the sprite ID are the stage of growth and a timer in IX+6 and IX+7 counts down how long it is before the next change (see ChangeOrchidStage). The timer only counts down while the player is alive and the game delay timer in GameDelayTimer is zero.
Once the orchid has finished growing it can be touched by the player (see TestOrchidTouched). The stem of the orchid is coloured bright green and the flower is coloured with the colour of the orchid.
