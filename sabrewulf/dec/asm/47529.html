47529: Display Entity
Used by the routines at HandlerOrchid, DisplayActor and GamePlay_Start.
Moves an entity on the screen, by erasing the sprite at its old position (saved by StoreEntity) and drawing it at its new one.
Both sprites are drawn with XOR, a row at a time, so that the rows are alternated between the two sprites and the screen is never without the sprite for long. If the sprites are offset vertically, the rows of the sprite that has no matching row in the other one are drawn first.
Before After DisplayEntity and ColourEntity

47545 JP M,DisplayEntity_NewLower Jump to DisplayEntity_NewLower if the sprite has moved down the screen.
The old sprite is lower than the new one, so draw the bottom rows of the old sprite on their own first.
47548 LD C,A Copy the number of rows that stand alone to C.
47549 LD A,(38563) Fetch the number of rows of the old sprite from Old_Sprite_Row_Count.
47552 CP C Compare it with the number of rows that stand alone.
47553 JP C,XORSpriteLoop_Reload Jump to XORSpriteLoop_Reload if the old sprite has no more rows than that, so that the sprites do not overlap and are each drawn in full.
47556 SUB C Subtract the number of rows that stand alone.
47557 LD (38563),A Write the number of rows that remain to Old_Sprite_Row_Count.
47560 JP XORSpriteLoop Jump to XORSpriteLoop to draw those rows of the old sprite on their own.
