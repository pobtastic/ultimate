47132: Slide Actor Past Objects
Used by the routines at ChoosePosition, HandlerFlame, HandlerHippoWarthog, HandlerChasingNastie, HandlerWanderingNastie, HandlerWulf, HandlerFlyingSabreman, HandlerTribesman, HandlerRhino, PlayerMovement and GamePlay_Start.
Stops an actor moving through the objects of the room. The new position of the actor is tested with TestObjectOverlap against each of the objects in the layout of the room (the pointers to the layouts are in RoomTable, found from the room with Layout). If it overlaps with an object then the movement is tried in the X direction only and the Y direction only so that the actor can slide along the object. If neither works then the actor is left where it was.
The Y position of an actor is the position of its feet, and the Y position of an object is the top of it.
Input
B Height of the actor
C Width of the actor
DE New position of the actor, with Y in D and X in E
IX Actor record
Output
DE The position that the actor can move to
