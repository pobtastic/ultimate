45359: Enter Room Below
Used by the routine at MoveActor.
Used by MoveActor when an actor has gone off the bottom of the screen. The actor is put at the top of the room below (the room number increases by 16) and its X position is changed to the entry point of the room, which is held in RoomEntryXPositions for the layout of the room (see Layout).
If the actor is not the player (the sprite ID is not 16 to 47) then that is all. If it is the player then the new room is drawn and the delay before the flames appear is started.
Input
IX Actor record
Output
D New Y position
E New X position
