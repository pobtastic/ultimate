42319: Handler: Wandering Nastie
Handles the nastie types which wander about: the spider (sprite IDs 96 to 99), scorpion (100 to 103), parrot (108 to 111), monkey (112 to 115) and all of the nastie types which are only possible later in the game (152 to 159 and 176 to 195).
The nastie moves with a velocity chosen by ChooseVelocity, which also makes it pause for a while. It chooses a new velocity when the timer in IX+2 runs out or when it is blocked. It bounces off the edges of the screen, is stopped by objects in the room, and is blown up (by BlowUpActor) if it touches the player or if the player is not alive.
