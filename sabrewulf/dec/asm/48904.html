48904: Play Appearing Sound
Used by the routine at HandlerAppearingNastie.
Plays six cycles of a tone with a half-period which depends on the low three bits of the sprite ID, so the sound changes as the sprite changes.
Input
IX Actor record
PlayAppearingSound 48904 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the sprite ID of the actor.
48907 CPL Flip the bits.
48908 RRCA Rotate right three times.
48909 RRCA
48910 RRCA
48911 AND %11100000 Keep only bits 5-7.
48913 LD B,A Store the result in B (the half-period).
48914 LD C,6 Set the number of cycles in C to 6.
48916 JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
