45495: Start Turn
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Sets up the delays at the start of a turn of a player, removes any orchid effect, takes one life from the player and displays the new number of lives. The start-of-turn delay (GameDelayTimer) is also what flashes the score label of the player (see GamePlayEntry).
StartTurn 45495 LD HL,38658 Point HL at the player's actor at ImmortalData.
45498 LD A,16 Set the nastie spawn delay in NastieSpawnDelay to 16.
45500 LD (38577),A
45503 LD A,128 Set the flame spawn delay in FlameSpawnDelay to 128.
45505 LD (38580),A
45508 LD A,128 Set the start-of-turn delay in GameDelayTimer to 128.
45510 LD (38581),A
45513 XOR A Clear the orchid effect timer in OrchidEffectTimer.
45514 LD (38576),A
45517 LD HL,38589 Point HL at the number of lives at ActivePlayer_Lives.
See Infinite Lives Player 2.
45520 DEC (HL) Decrease the number of lives by one.
45521 JP DisplayPlayerLives Jump to DisplayPlayerLives to update the lives display.
