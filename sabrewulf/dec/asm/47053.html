47053: Display Lives
Used by the routines at GameComplete, AddLife, HandlePlayerDeath and StartTurn.
Shows the number of lives of each player at the top of the screen as a digit followed by a small picture of Sabreman. If a player has no lives left then spaces are printed. The lives of player 1 are on the left and the lives of player 2 are on the right.
