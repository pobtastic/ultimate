42150: Blow Up Actor
Used by the routines at HandlerFlame, HandlerAppearingNastie, HandlerChasingNastie and HandlerWanderingNastie.
Plays an explosion noise, awards points for the actor and starts the actor on the sprites for blowing up (sprite IDs 8 to 13 which are handled by HandlerDyingNastie and HandlerRemoveNastie).
Input
IX Actor record
BlowUpActor 42150 CALL PlayExplosionSound Play the explosion sound.
42153 CALL AwardActorPoints Award points for the actor.
42156 LD (IX+0),8 Set the sprite ID of the actor to 8 ("Disappearing: Frame 2").
42160 JP DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the actor.
