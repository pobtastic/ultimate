40726: Handler: Guardian
Handles the guardian (sprite IDs 148-149). Nothing happens unless the guardian is in the same room as the player.
Until all four pieces of the amulet have been collected (see 38669) the guardian is animated, and if it touches the player (see TestActorHurtingPlayer_Room) then the player is hit with a hit direction of 128 which makes the player fall straight down.
Once all four pieces have been collected the guardian steps aside to let the player past. It walks to the left if the X position of the player is 128 or more, but it does not move further left than 64. Otherwise it walks to the right, but it does not move further right than 191.
