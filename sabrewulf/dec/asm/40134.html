40134: Print Percentage Explored
Used by the routine at GameOver_1UP_Messaging.
Works out how much of the jungle the player has explored and prints the result as a percentage on the game over screen.
The number of rooms visited (the bits set in the bitmap at VisitedRooms) plus the number of amulet pieces collected (the bits set in the low four bits of 38669) is converted into a percentage of 0260 (the 256 rooms plus the four amulet pieces). The result is held as two BCD bytes in Percentage_Hundreds (the hundreds) and PreviousGameOptions (the tens and units) before being printed.
