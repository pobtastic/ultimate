42851: Load Orchid
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and DrawRoomContents.
Sets up the orchid for the room the player is in, using the state of the orchid held in TableOrchidTypes and its position in TableOrchidPositions. The record is built in OrchidData. This is the opposite of SaveOrchid.
Input
IX Actor record of the player
LoadOrchid 42851 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the room of the player.
42854 CALL HandlerOrchidType Find the state of the orchid for the room.
42857 LD DE,38874 Point DE at the orchid's actor record at OrchidData.

42868 LD (DE),A Set the sprite ID of the orchid.

42909 LD (DE),A Set the high byte of the timer of the orchid.
