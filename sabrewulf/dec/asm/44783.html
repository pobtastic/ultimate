44783: Game Play Start
Used by the routine at GamePlayEntry.
Handles Sabreman while walking (sprite IDs 16 to 31). The controls are read, with the effects of the orchids applied, and turned into an acceleration of 7 in each direction. The player is then moved, tested against the objects in the room and the walking animation is updated. If "fire" is pressed then the player starts to attack (see StartAttack). The velocities are then reduced by one so the player slides to a halt. Finally the player is displayed.
The effect of an orchid depends on the colour of the player: yellow (INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) makes the player sit down, magenta (INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) reverses the controls, red (INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) lowers the top speed and cyan (INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) increases the top speed. The colour white (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) is normal.
