40337: Handler: Flame
Handles a flame (sprite IDs 52-55) which has been started by SpawnFlame. A flame which is not in the player's room is counted off by ExpireActor. In the player's room it burns for a limited time, drifting around in a random direction (which is changed by ChooseFlameDirection whenever its timer runs out or it is blocked) and bouncing off the edges of the screen. It flickers by changing sprite and colour every time it is handled, and it plays a crackling sound. If it touches the player (while the player is coloured INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) the player is hit. When its time is up, or the player is dead, the flame is blown up.
