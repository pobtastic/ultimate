42145: Blow Up Flame
Used by the routine at HandlerFlame.
Blows up a flame which has burnt for its full time. This sets a delay of 16 in FlameSpawnDelay before another flame can be started and then blows up the actor.
Input
IX Flame record
BlowUpFlame 42145 LD A,16 Set the flame spawn delay in FlameSpawnDelay to 16.
