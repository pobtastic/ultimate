41204: Messaging: Poem 3
The poem shown when three pieces of the amulet have been collected (see DisplayPoem). It is made up of four messages, each of which is an X position, a Y position (both in pixels), an attribute byte and then the text, with bit 7 set on the last character.

12 18 71 YOU AMASS
5 20 69 AND FIND THE KEEPER YOU
12 22 69 MAY PASS
