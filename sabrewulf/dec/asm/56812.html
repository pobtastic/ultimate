56812: Room Object Table
The objects found in the rooms, with two bytes for each of the 256 rooms (indexed by the room). A byte of zero means there is no object. Otherwise bits 6-7 choose one of the four positions in the layout of the room (see ObjectLocation) and bits 0-3 are the type of the object, which gives the sprite ID when 128 is added.
The table is filled in by GenerateRoomObjects when a new game starts. The table for the player who is not playing is kept in RoomObjectTable_Stored.
