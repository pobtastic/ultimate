43424: Knock Player Left
Used by the routine at KnockPlayerBack.
Sends the player flying to the left.
Input
IX Actor record of the player
KnockPlayerLeft 43424 LD (IX+0),68 Set the sprite ID of the player to 68 ("Sabreman: Flying Right").
43428 LD (IX+6),253 Set the X velocity of the player to 253 (moving left).
43432 JP DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the player.
