46473: Print Character
Used by the routines at HallOfFame, PrintStringColour, Score_1UP, EnterInitials and DisplayPlayerLives.
Prints a character from the font pointed to by the system variable CHARS onto the screen, at an address on the screen, and moves to the next column.

After PrintScreen
