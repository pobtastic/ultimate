47883: Store Entity
Used by the routines at HandlerFlame, HandlerHippoWarthog, HandlerSleepingHippoWarthog, HandlerGuardian, HandlerAppearingNastie, HandlerChooseNastie, HandlerDyingNastie, HandlerChasingNastie, HandlerWanderingNastie, HandlerYellowOrchid_StandUp, HandlerOrchid, HandlerWulf, HandlerWulfPouncing, HandlerWulfLanding, HandlerFlyingSabreman, HandlerSittingSabreman, HandlerTribesman, HandlerRhino, PlayerMovement, GamePlay_Start and EnterRoomBelow.
Saves the position, the sprite ID and the room of an actor, so that the sprite can be erased from its old position later (see DisplayEntity and RemoveEntity). The values are saved in Previous_X_Position (X), Previous_Y_Position (Y), Previous_Sprite_Id (sprite ID) and Previous_Room (room).
Input
IX Actor Entity
StoreEntity 47883 LD A,(IX+3) Copy the X position of the actor to Previous_X_Position.
47886 LD (38560),A
47889 LD A,(IX+4) Copy the Y position of the actor to Previous_Y_Position.
47892 LD (38561),A
47895 LD A,(IX+0) Copy the sprite ID of the actor to Previous_Sprite_Id.
47898 LD (38559),A
47901 LD A,(IX+1) Copy the room of the actor to Previous_Room.
47904 LD (38562),A
47907 RET Return.
