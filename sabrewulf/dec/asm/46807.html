46807: Enter Initials
Used by the routine at NewHighScore_Enter.
Lets the player choose three initials for the hall of fame, using the controls (see HandlerControls). "Left" and "right" step through the letters (space followed by "A" to "Z"), and "fire" accepts the letter and moves to the next one. The initials are written to the hall of fame and drawn on the screen.
