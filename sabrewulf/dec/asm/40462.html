40462: Choose Flame Direction
Used by the routine at HandlerFlame.
Starts a new direction timer for a flame (64 frames) and chooses its velocity. On every other call the velocity is random: the X velocity comes from the table at FlameVelocities and the Y velocity from the counter and the Z80 refresh register. On the other calls the flame heads towards the player (see AimFlameAtPlayer).
Input

ChooseFlameDirection 40462 LD (IX+2),64 Set the direction timer of the flame to 64.

40469 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
40471 JR NZ,AimFlameAtPlayer Jump to AimFlameAtPlayer to aim the flame at the player if bit 0 of the pass counter is set.
Choose a random velocity.
