48957: Play Amulet Piece Sound
Used by the routine at HandlerTribesman.
Plays eight cycles of a tone. The half-period is the distance to the nearest amulet piece, plus the X and Y positions of the actor, rotated left twice.
Input
A Distance to the nearest amulet piece
IX Actor record
PlayAmuletPieceSound 48957 ADD A,(IX+3) Add the X position of the actor to the distance.
48960 ADD A,(IX+4) Add the Y position of the actor to the distance.
48963 RLCA Rotate left twice.
48964 RLCA
48965 LD B,A Store the result in B (the half-period).
48966 LD C,8 Set the number of cycles in C to 8.
48968 JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
