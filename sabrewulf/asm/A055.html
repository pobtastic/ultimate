A055: Messaging: Poem 1
The poem shown when one piece of the amulet has been collected (see DisplayPoem). It is made up of four messages, each of which is an X position, a Y position (both in pixels), an attribute byte and then the text, with bit 7 set on the last character.
X Position Y Position Attribute Text
04 10 47 ONE PIECE IS ALL THAT YOU
0B 12 47 HAVE FOUND
05 14 45 COLLECT THREE MORE AND
09 16 45 OUTWARD BOUND
Poem_1_Copy A055 DEFB $20,$80 X / Y position.

A080 DEFB $45 Attribute.

A099 DEFB $45 Attribute.
