A7F8: Table: Orchid Types
The state of the orchid for each of the 30 room layouts, held as two bytes. This is the state when the game starts, and is updated by SaveOrchid as the player moves between rooms.
Byte Bits Contents
00 0-2 The stage of growth
3 The direction of growth (set when the orchid is shrinking)
4-7 The high byte of the timer
01 0-2 The colour
3-7 The low byte of the timer
