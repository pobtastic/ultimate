A4B3: Award Actor Points
Used by the routine at BlowUpActor.
Adds points to the score for blowing up an actor. The points are 100 plus a value of 65 75 85 or 95 depending on bits 1-2 of the sprite ID, so they are 165, 175, 185 or 195.
Input
IX Actor record
AwardActorPoints A4B3 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the sprite ID of the actor.
A4B6 RLCA Rotate the sprite ID left three times.
A4B7 RLCA
A4B8 RLCA
A4B9 AND %00110000 Keep only the type of the actor (bits 4-5).
A4BB ADD A,$65 Add 65.
A4BD LD C,A Stash the low byte of the points in C.
A4BE LD B,$01 Set the high byte of the points in B to 01.
A4C0 JP AddPointsToScore Jump to AddPointsToScore to add the points to the score.
