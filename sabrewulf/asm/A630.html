A630: Bring Actor Closer
Used by the routines at HandlerSleepingHippoWarthog and HandlerTribesman.
Moves an actor which is two or more rows of rooms away from the player to a room in the row above or below the player (never the player's own room), placing it at the position in TableOrchidPositions for the layout of the new room. Half of the time it is moved to the row above, and half of the time to the row below.
Input
IX Actor record

A636 RET Z Return if the actor is in the same room as the player.

Work out how many rows of rooms are between the player and the actor.
