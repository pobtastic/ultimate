B61E: Enter High Score
Used by the routine at NewHighScore.
Works out whether a score is high enough to go in the hall of fame. If it is then every entry below it is moved down a place, the score is put in the gap and the player is asked to register their initials (see EnterInitials).
The number of places that the score beat is stored in HighScorePosition, so 01 means that the score only beat the sixth place and 06 means that it is the new top score.
