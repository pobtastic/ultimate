BBAE: Draw Room Contents
Used by the routine at DrawCurrentRoom.
Calls the routines which set up the contents of the room (SaveOrchid, LoadOrchid and SetUpRoomObjects) and then draws every actor in the room with DrawRoomActors.
DrawRoomContents BBAE CALL SaveOrchid Save the orchid of the room.
BBB1 CALL LoadOrchid Load the orchid of the room.
BBB4 CALL SetUpRoomObjects Set up the objects in the room.
BBB7 CALL DrawRoomActors Draw the actors in the room.
BBBA RET Return.
