B6AB: Previous High Score
Used by the routine at NewHighScore_Enter.
Moves a pointer to a high score back to the previous entry (from the 6th entry towards the 1st one). Each entry is six bytes long.
Input
DE Pointer to an entry
Output
DE Pointer to the entry before it
NewHighScore_Next B6AB DEC DE Decrease DE by six.
B6AC DEC DE
B6AD DEC DE
B6AE DEC DE
B6AF DEC DE
B6B0 DEC DE
B6B1 RET Return.
