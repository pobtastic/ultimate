BF6D: Play Wave Sequence
Used by the routines at HandlerFlyingSabreman, PlayOrchidFlashBeep, PlayFlameSound, PlayExplosionSound, PlayAppearingSound, PlayHighTone, PlayLowTone, PlayAmuletPieceSound and PlayFootstepSound.
Plays a number of cycles of a square wave, one after the other.
Input
B Duration of wave
C Number of times to repeat
PlayWaveSequence BF6D CALL PlaySquareWave Play a cycle of the wave.
BF70 DEC C Decrease the repeat counter by one and loop back to PlayWaveSequence until all of the cycles have been played.
BF71 JR NZ,PlayWaveSequence
BF73 RET Return.
