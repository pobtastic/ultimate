B473: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routines at HandlerHallOfFame and HandlerGameMenu.
Prints the eight lines of text of the game selection menu, each with its own attribute (see GameSelection_Attributes), at the positions in GameSelection_Position. The entry point at GameMenu_Loop is also used to print the hall of fame, which has its own tables.
