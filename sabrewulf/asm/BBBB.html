BBBB: Draw Room Actors
Used by the routine at DrawRoomContents.
Works through the actor table from ImmortalData_Actors and draws (with DrawEntity) every actor whose room (byte 01) is the same as the room the player is in (9703).
DrawRoomActors BBBB PUSH IX Stash the actor record pointer on the stack.
BBBD LD IX,$970E Point IX at the first actor after the player at ImmortalData_Actors.
Draw the actor if it is in the same room as the player.
DrawRoomActors_Loop BBC1 LD A,($9703) Fetch the room of the player from 9703.
BBC4 CP (IX+$01) Draw the actor if it is in the room of the player.
BBC7 CALL Z,DrawEntity
Move on to the next actor, until the end of the actor table is reached.
BBCA LD DE,$000C Set the size of an actor record in DE to 0C.
BBCD ADD IX,DE Move onto the next actor.
BBCF PUSH IX Copy the pointer to the actor into HL.
BBD1 POP HL
BBD2 LD DE,$982E Point DE at the end of the actor table at StoredActorTable.
BBD5 AND A Subtract the end of the actor table from the pointer to the actor.
BBD6 SBC HL,DE
BBD8 JR C,DrawRoomActors_Loop Jump to DrawRoomActors_Loop if there are more actors to draw.
BBDA POP IX Restore the actor record pointer from the stack.
BBDC RET Return.
