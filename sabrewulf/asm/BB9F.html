BB9F: Clear Buffers
Used by the routine at GameInitialisation.
Writes 00 to every address from GameOptions to SecurityCheck.
ClearBuffers BB9F LD HL,$9692 Point HL at the first game variable at GameOptions.
BBA2 LD BC,$02C8 Set the number of bytes to clear in BC to 02C8.
This entry point is used by the routine at GameInitialisation.
ClearBuffers_Loop BBA5 LD (HL),$00 Clear a byte of the game variables.
BBA7 INC HL Move onto the next byte.
BBA8 DEC BC Decrease the byte counter by one.
BBA9 LD A,B Loop back to ClearBuffers_Loop until all of the bytes have been cleared.
BBAA OR C
BBAB JR NZ,ClearBuffers_Loop
BBAD RET Return.
