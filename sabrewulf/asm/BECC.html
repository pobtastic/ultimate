BECC: Play Flame Sound
Used by the routine at HandlerFlame.
Plays sixteen cycles of a tone, with a half-period taken from the table at FlameSoundTable. The entry used depends on the low four bits of the frame counter, which gives the flame a crackling sound.
PlayFlameSound BECC LD BC,$BEDE Point BC at the flame sound table at FlameSoundTable.
BECF LD A,($9693) Fetch the low byte of the game timer from GameTimer.
BED2 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
BED4 LD L,A Use it as the offset into the table.
BED5 LD H,$00
BED7 ADD HL,BC Add the table to find the half-period.
BED8 LD B,(HL) Fetch the half-period of the tone from the table into B.
BED9 LD C,$10 Set the number of cycles in C to 10.
BEDB JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
