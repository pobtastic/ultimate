B01A: Move Actor Left
Used by the routine at MoveActor.
Used by MoveActor when the actor is moving left. The new X position is found by taking the speed away from the current position. If it goes below zero the actor has moved into the room on the left.
Input
A X velocity in pixels (negative)
D New Y position
IX Actor record
MoveActorLeft B01A NEG Make the speed positive.
B01C LD E,A Stash the speed in E.
B01D LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the X position of the actor.
B020 SUB E Subtract the speed.
B021 LD E,A Stash the new X position in E.
B022 JP C,EnterRoomLeft Jump to EnterRoomLeft if the actor has left the screen on the left.
B025 JR MoveActor_Vertical Jump to MoveActor_Vertical to move the actor vertically.
