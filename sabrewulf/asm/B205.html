B205: Switch Player
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerDeath.
Used when a player has died in a two player game. Swaps over the state of the two players, clears the screen and makes the new player stand up with the normal colour, then carries on at InitialiseGame_StartTurn.
SwitchPlayer B205 CALL ChangePlayer Swap over the states of the two players.
B208 CALL ClearPlayArea_Attributes Clear the attribute buffer of the play area.
B20B CALL ClearPlayArea Clear the play area.
B20E LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the sprite ID of the player.
B211 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
B213 OR %00010000 Set bit 4, giving a sprite ID of 10 to 17.
B215 LD (IX+$00),A Set the new sprite ID of the player.
B218 LD (IX+$05),$47 Set the colour of the player to INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
B21C JR InitialiseGame_StartTurn Jump to InitialiseGame_StartTurn to start the turn.
