BEC6: Play Orchid Flash Beep
Used by the routine at GamePlay_Start.
Plays a short, low beep of eight cycles. It is made when the player changes between white and the colour of the orchid, as the effect of the orchid wears off (see GamePlay_Start).
PlayOrchidFlashBeep BEC6 LD BC,$A008 Set the half-period in B to A0 and the number of cycles in C to 08.
BEC9 JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the beep.
