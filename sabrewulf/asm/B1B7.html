B1B7: Start Turn
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Sets up the delays at the start of a turn of a player, removes any orchid effect, takes one life from the player and displays the new number of lives. The start-of-turn delay (GameDelayTimer) is also what flashes the score label of the player (see GamePlayEntry).
StartTurn B1B7 LD HL,$9702 Point HL at the player's actor at ImmortalData.
B1BA LD A,$10 Set the nastie spawn delay in NastieSpawnDelay to 10.
B1BC LD ($96B1),A
B1BF LD A,$80 Set the flame spawn delay in FlameSpawnDelay to 80.
B1C1 LD ($96B4),A
B1C4 LD A,$80 Set the start-of-turn delay in GameDelayTimer to 80.
B1C6 LD ($96B5),A
B1C9 XOR A Clear the orchid effect timer in OrchidEffectTimer.
B1CA LD ($96B0),A
B1CD LD HL,$96BD Point HL at the number of lives at ActivePlayer_Lives.
See Infinite Lives Player 2.
B1D0 DEC (HL) Decrease the number of lives by one.
B1D1 JP DisplayPlayerLives Jump to DisplayPlayerLives to update the lives display.
