BB7C: Clear Play Area Attribute Buffer
Used by the routines at GameComplete, ShowAmulet, HandlerPoems, GameOver_1UP_Messaging, EnterRoomBelow, InitialiseGame, SwitchPlayer and HandlerGameMenu.
Sets the attributes of the play area to 40 which is everything except the two rows at the top of the screen that hold the banner.
ClearPlayArea_Attributes BB7C LD HL,$5840 HL=5840(attribute buffer).
BB7F JR ResetAttributes_FromAddress Jump to ResetAttributes_FromAddress.
