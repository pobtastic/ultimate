BB24: Colour Entity
Used by the routines at ColourPickup, DisplayActor, DrawActor and GamePlay_Start.
Colours the attribute cells covered by the sprite that has just been drawn with DrawEntity using the colour held in byte 05 of the actor. Only cells with a black paper are changed (see ColourRows), so the colour of anything already drawn behind the sprite is kept.
