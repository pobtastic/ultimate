BF3D: Play Amulet Piece Sound
Used by the routine at HandlerTribesman.
Plays eight cycles of a tone. The half-period is the distance to the nearest amulet piece, plus the X and Y positions of the actor, rotated left twice.
Input
A Distance to the nearest amulet piece
IX Actor record
PlayAmuletPieceSound BF3D ADD A,(IX+$03) Add the X position of the actor to the distance.
BF40 ADD A,(IX+$04) Add the Y position of the actor to the distance.
BF43 RLCA Rotate left twice.
BF44 RLCA
BF45 LD B,A Store the result in B (the half-period).
BF46 LD C,$08 Set the number of cycles in C to 08.
BF48 JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
