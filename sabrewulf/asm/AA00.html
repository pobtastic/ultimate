AA00: Land Sabreman
Used by the routine at HandlerFlyingSabreman.
Makes the player sit down after flying through the air. The sprite ID goes up by one (to 41 or 45), a counter of 3F is set, the player is drawn and a noise is played.
Input
IX Actor record of the player
LandSabreman AA00 INC (IX+$00) Move the player on to the sitting sprite.
AA03 LD (IX+$02),$3F Set the sitting counter of the player to 3F.
AA07 CALL DisplayActor Display the player.
AA0A JP PlayNoiseSound Jump to PlayNoiseSound to play the noise.
