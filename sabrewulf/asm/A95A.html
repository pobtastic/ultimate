A95A: Compare Wulf Position
Used by the routine at ChooseWulfDirection.
Sends the Wulf to the left if the player is to its left, or otherwise to the right. This is used when the Wulf and the player are in the same room.
Input
IX Actor record of the Wulf
CompareWulfPosition A95A LD A,($9705) Fetch the X position of the player from 9705.
A95D CP (IX+$03) Jump to ChooseWulfDirection_Left if the player is to the left of the Wulf.
A960 JR C,ChooseWulfDirection_Left
A962 JR SendWulfRight Jump to SendWulfRight.
