AA0D: Handler: Sitting Sabreman
Handles the player after they have landed (sprite IDs 41 42 45 and 46). When the counter in IX+02 runs out the player either starts again or loses a life (see HandlePlayerDeath). If the player has no lives left then they are shown as dead when the counter reaches 30.
Input
IX Actor record of the player
HandlerSittingSabreman AA0D CALL StoreEntity Remember the previous position of the player.
AA10 DEC (IX+$02) Decrease the sitting counter by one and jump to HandlePlayerDeath if it has run out.
AA13 JR Z,HandlePlayerDeath
AA15 LD A,($96BD) Fetch the number of lives from ActivePlayer_Lives.
AA18 AND A Jump to HandlePlayerDeath_Draw if the player has lives left.
AA19 JR NZ,HandlePlayerDeath_Draw
AA1B LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the sitting counter of the player.
AA1E CP $30 Jump to HandlePlayerDeath_Draw if the sitting counter is not 30.
AA20 JR NZ,HandlePlayerDeath_Draw
AA22 INC (IX+$00) Move the player on to the dead sprite.
AA25 JR HandlePlayerDeath_Draw Jump to HandlePlayerDeath_Draw.
