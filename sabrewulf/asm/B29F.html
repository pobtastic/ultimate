B29F: Show Hall Of Fame
Used by the routine at HandlerHallOfFame.
Clears the panel in the middle of the screen, prints the eight lines of text of the hall of fame (the title, the six positions and the copyright message) and then prints the six scores and initials from Top_HighScore_1, one under the other.
