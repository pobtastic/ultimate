A2F8: Add Life
Used by the routine at HandlerExtraLife.
Gives the player an extra life, up to a maximum of nine, and updates the lives on the screen.
AddLife A2F8 LD A,($96BD) Fetch the number of lives from ActivePlayer_Lives.
A2FB CP $09 Return if the player already has 09 lives.
A2FD RET NC
A2FE INC A Add a life.
A2FF LD ($96BD),A Store the number of lives in ActivePlayer_Lives.
A302 JP DisplayPlayerLives Jump to DisplayPlayerLives to update the lives display.
