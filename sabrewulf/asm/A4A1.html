A4A1: Blow Up Flame
Used by the routine at HandlerFlame.
Blows up a flame which has burnt for its full time. This sets a delay of 10 in FlameSpawnDelay before another flame can be started and then blows up the actor.
Input
IX Flame record
BlowUpFlame A4A1 LD A,$10 Set the flame spawn delay in FlameSpawnDelay to 10.
A4A3 LD ($96B4),A
