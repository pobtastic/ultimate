AC6B: Reset Actors
Used by the routine at InitialisePlayer.
Copies the initial state of the 0C actors in InitialActors into the actor table at ImmortalData, which sets up the player and the immortal actors for a new game.
ResetActors AC6B LD HL,$AC77 Point HL at the initial state of the actors at InitialActors.
AC6E LD DE,$9702 Point DE at the actor table at ImmortalData.
AC71 LD BC,$0090 Set the number of bytes to copy in BC to 90 (0C actors of 0C bytes).
AC74 LDIR Copy the actors.
AC76 RET Return.
