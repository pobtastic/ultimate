9F16: Handler: Guardian
Handles the guardian (sprite IDs 94-95). Nothing happens unless the guardian is in the same room as the player.
Until all four pieces of the amulet have been collected (see 970D) the guardian is animated, and if it touches the player (see TestActorHurtingPlayer_Room) then the player is hit with a hit direction of 80 which makes the player fall straight down.
Once all four pieces have been collected the guardian steps aside to let the player past. It walks to the left if the X position of the player is 80 or more, but it does not move further left than 40. Otherwise it walks to the right, but it does not move further right than BF.
Input
IX Actor record
HandlerGuardian 9F16 LD A,($9703) Fetch the room of the player from 9703.
9F19 CP (IX+$01) Compare with the room of the guardian.
9F1C RET NZ Return if the guardian is in a different room.
9F1D CALL StoreEntity Remember the previous position of the guardian.
9F20 LD A,($970D) Fetch the amulet pieces collected from 970D.
9F23 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
9F25 CP $0F Jump to HandlerGuardian_StepAside if all four amulet pieces have been collected.
9F27 JR Z,HandlerGuardian_StepAside
9F29 CALL AnimateRhino Animate the guardian.
9F2C LD BC,$170F Set how far above and to the right of the guardian the player can be in BC to 170F.
9F2F CALL TestActorHurtingPlayer_Room Test whether the guardian is touching the player.
9F32 JP NC,DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the guardian if the player is not touching it.
9F35 LD A,$80 Set the hit direction of the player in 9704 to 80.
9F37 LD ($9704),A
9F3A CALL PlayWarbleSound Play the warble sound.
9F3D JP DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the guardian.
All four pieces have been collected. Walk to the left if the player is in the right half of the screen.
HandlerGuardian_StepAside 9F40 LD A,($9705) Fetch the X position of the player from 9705.
9F43 BIT 7,A Jump to HandlerGuardian_Right if the player is in the left half of the screen.
9F45 JR Z,HandlerGuardian_Right
9F47 LD (IX+$06),$E0 Set the X velocity of the guardian to E0 (walking to the left).
9F4B CALL MoveActor Work out the new position of the guardian.
9F4E LD A,E Fetch the new X position of the guardian.
9F4F CP $40 Compare with 40.
HandlerGuardian_Limit 9F51 JP C,DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the guardian if it has reached the limit of its walk.
9F54 LD (IX+$03),E Set the new X position of the guardian.
9F57 CALL AnimateRhino Animate the guardian.
9F5A JP DisplayActor Jump to DisplayActor to display the guardian.
Walk to the right.
HandlerGuardian_Right 9F5D LD (IX+$06),$20 Set the X velocity of the guardian to 20 (walking to the right).
9F61 CALL MoveActor Work out the new position of the guardian.
9F64 LD A,E Fetch the new X position of the guardian.
9F65 CP $C0 Compare with C0.
9F67 CCF Invert the carry flag.
9F68 JR HandlerGuardian_Limit Jump to HandlerGuardian_Limit.
