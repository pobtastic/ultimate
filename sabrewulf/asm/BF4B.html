BF4B: Play Footstep Sound
Used by the routines at PlayerMovement and GamePlay_Start.
Makes the sound of a footstep when a player has moved. The footstep counter at FootstepCounter is incremented and a short tone is played whenever the low three bits are zero, which alternates between two pitches (using bit 3). A cyan player (the effect of a cyan orchid) is quicker, so the counter is incremented twice.
Input
IX Actor record
PlayFootstepSound BF4B CALL PlayFootstepSound_Step Make the footstep sound.
BF4E LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the colour of the player.
BF51 CP $45 Return unless the player is cyan (INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)), who makes the footstep sound a second time.
BF53 RET NZ
Increment the counter and make the sound if it is time for one.
PlayFootstepSound_Step BF54 LD HL,$96B3 Point HL at the footstep counter at FootstepCounter.
BF57 INC (HL) Increase the footstep counter by one.
BF58 LD A,(HL) Fetch the footstep counter.
BF59 BIT 3,A Jump to PlayFootstepSound_Low if bit 3 of the counter is zero.
BF5B JR Z,PlayFootstepSound_Low
BF5D AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
BF5F RET NZ Return unless the footstep counter is a multiple of eight.
BF60 LD BC,$4004 Set the half-period in B to 40 and the number of cycles in C to 04.
BF63 JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
PlayFootstepSound_Low BF65 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
BF67 RET NZ Return unless the footstep counter is a multiple of eight.
BF68 LD BC,$6004 Set the half-period in B to 60 and the number of cycles in C to 04.
BF6B JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to play the sound.
