AA6A: Game Over
Used by the routines at GameOver_Controller and GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Shows the "GAME OVER PLAYER" message, with the percentage of the jungle explored, and plays a tune. The message is for player 1 when entered at GameOver_1UP_Messaging and for player 2 when entered at GameOver_2UP.
Before After GameOver_1UP_Messaging
Set up altering the "GAME OVER PLAYER X" message for 1UP.
GameOver_1UP_Messaging AA6A LD A,"1"+$80 A="1" + 80 (escape character).
Print the messaging and pause to show it for a period of time.
GameOver_Write AA6C LD ($AAD3),A Write ASCII player number to AAD3(GameOver_Text).
AA6F CALL ClearPlayArea_Attributes Clear the attribute buffer of the play area.
AA72 CALL ClearPlayArea Clear the play area.
AA75 LD DE,$AAD4 Point DE at the "ADVENTURE COMPLETED" message at AdventureCompleted_Text.
AA78 LD HL,$7820 Set the print position in HL (L is the X position and H is the Y position) to 7820.
AA7B CALL PrintStringColour Print the message.
AA7E LD DE,$AAC1 Point DE at the "GAME OVER PLAYER" message at GameOver_Text.
AA81 LD HL,$6038 Set the print position in HL to 6038.
AA84 CALL PrintStringColour Print the message.
AA87 CALL PrintRoomsVisited Print the percentage of the jungle explored.
AA8A LD DE,$BD28 Point DE at the game over tune at MusicData_GameOver.
AA8D CALL PlayMusic Play the tune.
Introduce a pause by counting down from 10000 twice.
AA90 LD B,$02 Set the number of delays in B to 02.
AA92 LD HL,$0000 Set the delay counter in HL to 00 (which counts down from 10000).
AA95 JP Pause Jump to Pause to pause.
