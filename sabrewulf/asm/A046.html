A046: Select Poem
Used by the routine at DisplayPoem.
Points DE at the first message of a poem and then prints it (04 lines) by jumping into Poem_Start.
Poem 1.
Poem_1_Reference A046 LD DE,$A055 Point DE at the first message of poem 1 at Poem_1_Copy.
A049 JR Poem_Start Jump to Poem_Start to print the poem.
This entry point is used by the routine at DisplayPoem.
Poem 2.
Poem_2_Reference A04B LD DE,$A0A7 Point DE at the first message of poem 2 at Poem_2_Copy.
A04E JR Poem_Start Jump to Poem_Start to print the poem.
This entry point is used by the routine at DisplayPoem.
Poem 3.
Poem_3_Reference A050 LD DE,$A0F4 Point DE at the first message of poem 3 at Poem_3_Copy.
A053 JR Poem_Start Jump to Poem_Start to print the poem.
