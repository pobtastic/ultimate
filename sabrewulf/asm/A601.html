A601: Velocity Table
The velocities which can be chosen by ChooseVelocity, in sixteenths of a pixel. Four of them are negative (up or left) and four are positive (down or right).
VelocityTable A601 DEFB $A0 Negative velocities.
A602 DEFB $B0
A603 DEFB $C0
A604 DEFB $D0
A605 DEFB $30 Positive velocities.
A606 DEFB $40
A607 DEFB $50
A608 DEFB $60
