A79F: Save Orchid
Used by the routine at DrawRoomContents.
Stores the state of the orchid in OrchidData back into the table in TableOrchidTypes, for the room which it was in (held in Moved_Actor_Room), so that it is as it was left when the player returns to the room. This is the opposite of LoadOrchid.
