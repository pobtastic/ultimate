AEEF: Game Play Start
Used by the routine at GamePlayEntry.
Handles Sabreman while walking (sprite IDs 10 to 1F). The controls are read, with the effects of the orchids applied, and turned into an acceleration of 07 in each direction. The player is then moved, tested against the objects in the room and the walking animation is updated. If "fire" is pressed then the player starts to attack (see StartAttack). The velocities are then reduced by one so the player slides to a halt. Finally the player is displayed.
The effect of an orchid depends on the colour of the player: yellow (INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) makes the player sit down, magenta (INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) reverses the controls, red (INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) lowers the top speed and cyan (INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) increases the top speed. The colour white (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)) is normal.

Handles reversing the controls as a result of the magenta orchid.
AF18 LD A,C Fetch the controls.
AF19 XOR %00001111 Flip bits 0-3 (swapping left with right and up with down).
AF1B LD C,A Store the reversed controls in C.
This entry point is used by the routine at StopAttack.
GamePlay_Controls AF1C LD A,C Fetch the controls.
AF1D LD ($96AC),A Store the controls in Player_Controls.
Turn the controls into an acceleration in E (X) and D (Y).
AF20 LD A,E Fetch the X acceleration.
AF21 LD B,$07 Set the amount to accelerate by in B to 07.
AF23 BIT 1,C Jump to GamePlay_Left if "right" is not being pressed.
AF25 JR Z,GamePlay_Left
AF27 ADD A,B Add the acceleration for "right".
GamePlay_Left AF28 BIT 0,C Jump to GamePlay_AccelerationX if "left" is not being pressed.
AF2A JR Z,GamePlay_AccelerationX
AF2C SUB B Subtract the acceleration for "left".
GamePlay_AccelerationX AF2D LD E,A Store the X acceleration in E.
AF2E LD A,D Fetch the Y acceleration.
AF2F BIT 3,C Jump to GamePlay_Up if "down" is not being pressed.
AF31 JR Z,GamePlay_Up
AF33 ADD A,B Add the acceleration for "down".
GamePlay_Up AF34 BIT 2,C Jump to GamePlay_AccelerationY if "up" is not being pressed.
AF36 JR Z,GamePlay_AccelerationY
AF38 SUB B Subtract the acceleration for "up".
GamePlay_AccelerationY AF39 LD D,A Store the Y acceleration in D.
AF3A CALL AcceleratePlayer Accelerate the player.

This entry point is used by the routine at HandlerYellowOrchid_StandUp.
Display the player. While an orchid effect is about to wear off (the timer in OrchidEffectTimer is below 2F) the player flashes between the colour of the orchid and white, with a sound at the start of each phase.
GamePlay_Display AF7A CALL DisplayEntity Move the player on the screen.
AF7D LD A,($96B0) Fetch the orchid effect timer from OrchidEffectTimer.
AF80 AND A Jump to ColourEntity to colour the player if there is no orchid effect.
AF81 JP Z,ColourEntity
AF84 CP $2F Jump to ColourEntity to colour the player if the orchid effect timer is 2F or more.
AF86 JP NC,ColourEntity
AF89 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
AF8B CP $08 Jump to GamePlay_OrchidPhase if bit 3 of the orchid effect timer is set.
AF8D JR NC,GamePlay_OrchidPhase
AF8F AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
AF91 CALL Z,PlayOrchidFlashBeep Play the orchid flash beep if the timer is at the start of the white phase.
AF94 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the colour of the player (the colour of the orchid).
AF97 PUSH AF Stash the colour of the orchid on the stack.
AF98 LD (IX+$05),$47 Set the colour of the player to INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
AF9C CALL ColourEntity Colour the player.
AF9F POP AF Restore the colour of the orchid from the stack.
AFA0 LD (IX+$05),A Set the colour of the player back to the colour of the orchid.
AFA3 RET Return.
The player is drawn in the colour of the orchid.
GamePlay_OrchidPhase AFA4 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
AFA6 CALL Z,PlayOrchidFlashBeep Play the orchid flash beep if the timer is at the start of the orchid phase.
AFA9 JP ColourEntity Jump to ColourEntity to colour the player.
