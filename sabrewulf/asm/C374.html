C374: Amulet Graphics Table
The graphics which make up the amulet on the amulet screen (see ShowAmulet). There are four entries of four bytes, each of which is the address of a graphic followed by its X and Y position, and then a zero address ends the table. The first entry is used for the first bit of the amulet pieces collected (see 970D).
The graphics are the amulet outlines at Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Left, Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Right, Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Left and Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Right.
AmuletGraphicsTable C374 DEFW Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Right Graphic Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Right.
C376 DEFB $80,$58 X/ Y position.
C378 DEFW Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Left Graphic Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Bottom_Left.
C37A DEFB $50,$58 X/ Y position.
C37C DEFW Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Right Graphic Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Right.
C37E DEFB $80,$38 X/ Y position.
C380 DEFW Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Left Graphic Graphic_Amulet_Outline_Top_Left.
C382 DEFB $50,$38 X/ Y position.
C384 DEFW $0000 Terminator.
