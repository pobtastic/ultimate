BF2F: Play Noise Sound
Used by the routine at LandSabreman.
Plays 30 tones, each with a pitch worked out from the counter, which gives a noisy sound.
PlayNoiseSound BF2F LD C,$30 Set the number of tones in C to 30.
PlayNoiseSound_Loop BF31 LD A,C Fetch the tone counter.
BF32 XOR %01010101 Flip bits 0, 2, 4 and 6.
BF34 ADD A,C Add the tone counter.
BF35 LD B,A Store the result in B (the half-period).
BF36 CALL PlaySquareWave Play the tone.
BF39 DEC C Decrease the tone counter by one and loop back to PlayNoiseSound_Loop until all of the tones have been played.
BF3A JR NZ,PlayNoiseSound_Loop
BF3C RET Return.
