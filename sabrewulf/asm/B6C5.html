B6C5: Messaging: High Score Suffixes
The text for the place of a score which has beaten the sixth entry down to the second one. These are used to complete the message on the new high score screen. The last entry is blank, as a new top score is just "highest".
HighScore_Suffixes B6C5 DEFM "6TH" "6TH".
