BF17: Play Warble Sound
Used by the routines at HandlerSleepingHippoWarthog, HandlerGuardian, HandlerTribesman and KnockBackRhino.
Plays 30 tones, using PlaySquareWave. The pitch of each one is worked out from a counter which counts down from 30 which gives a warbling sound.
PlayWarbleSound BF17 LD C,$30 Set the number of tones in C to 30.
PlayWarbleSound_Loop BF19 LD A,C Fetch the tone counter.
BF1A OR %00011000 Set bits 3-4.
BF1C DAA Decimal adjust the result, so the pitch jumps about.
BF1D LD B,A Store the result in B (the half-period).
BF1E CALL PlaySquareWave Play the tone.
BF21 DEC C Decrease the tone counter by one and loop back to PlayWarbleSound_Loop until all of the tones have been played.
BF22 JR NZ,PlayWarbleSound_Loop
BF24 RET Return.
