A207: Show Amulet
Used by the routine at TestPlayerProgress.
Clears the play area and draws the amulet in the middle of the screen. The amulet is drawn as four graphics, one for each piece. Every piece is drawn as a plain red silhouette, unless it has been collected, in which case it is drawn in full colour. Each of the graphics is drawn by DrawRoom_Process, which holds two sets of attributes for each graphic, the second of which is only used when AttributeSetFlag is set.
Once the amulet has been drawn the poem is shown by HandlerPoems.
