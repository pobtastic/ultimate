D7E4: Test Entity Cells
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Tests each cell of the level that the entity's box covers, by calling TestGroupObjects, and retests a cell when a collision moves the entity. When bit 6 of the flags is set, a z within one page of FF is stored in field 1F after a zero top byte has been snapped up by one unit. A hit planted by HandleOverlap then turns the heading away from it. Purpose of bit 6 is not yet known.
Input
IX The entity
The cell is the high byte of x and the high byte of y. A collision can move those bytes, and the extent can make the entity cover the next cell.
