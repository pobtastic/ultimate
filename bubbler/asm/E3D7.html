E3D7: Draw Gauge Bar
Used by the routine at DrawPanel.
Draws the empty bar of one of the two gauges, which is made of a picture of the left end, nine copies of a picture of the middle and a picture of the right end. The bar is then filled by UpdateGauge.
Input
HL The position of the left end
DrawGaugeBar E3D7 LD DE,$E3FA Point DE at the colour rectangle.
E3DA CALL FillRectangle Colour the bar.
E3DD LD DE,$E3FD Point DE at the picture of the left end.
E3E0 CALL DrawPicture Draw it.
E3E3 LD A,$08 Set the distance to the next piece to 08.
E3E5 ADD A,L Add the x position.
E3E6 LD L,A Move across to the next piece.
E3E7 LD B,$09 Set the number of middle pieces to 09.
DrawGaugeBar_Loop E3E9 PUSH BC Save the counter.
E3EA LD DE,$E407 Point DE at the picture of the middle piece.
E3ED CALL DrawPicture Draw it.
E3F0 LD A,$08 Set the distance to the next piece to 08.
E3F2 ADD A,L Add the x position.
E3F3 LD L,A Move across to the next piece.
E3F4 POP BC Restore the counter.
E3F5 DJNZ DrawGaugeBar_Loop Repeat until all of the middle pieces are drawn.
E3F7 JP DrawPicture Jump to DrawPicture.
