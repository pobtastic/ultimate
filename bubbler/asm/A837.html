A837: Pause Game
Used by the routines at ShowEnding and RunFrame.
Pauses the game, if SPACE or CAPS SHIFT is being pressed, until one of them is pressed again. The message 'PAUSED' scrolls in and out across the window.
PauseGame A837 LD IY,($5CF1) Bring back IY from SavedIY.
A83B CALL TestPauseKeys Test whether SPACE or CAPS SHIFT is being pressed.
A83E RET Z Return if not.
PauseGame_Release A83F CALL TestPauseKeys Test whether SPACE or CAPS SHIFT is being pressed.
A842 JR NZ,PauseGame_Release Jump back until the keys have been released.
A844 LD HL,$FF0C Set the scroll counter to 0C and the character index to FF.
A847 LD ($A921),HL Store them in ScrollCounter and A922.
A84A LD A,$06 Set the length of the message to 06...
A84C LD ($A572),A ...in the operand of the instruction at A571.

A850 HALT ...and wait for the next frame.
A851 DI Disable interrupts.
A852 LD HL,$A831 Point HL at PauseMessage.
A855 CALL ScrollMessage Scroll the message by one step.

A85B CALL ScrollMessage Scroll the message by another step.
A85E LD HL,($A921) Fetch the scroll counter and the character index.
A861 LD DE,$060C Set the values at which the message is completely in.

A865 SBC HL,DE Compare them.
A867 JR NZ,PauseGame_ScrollIn Jump back to scroll the message until it is completely in.
PauseGame_Wait A869 CALL Wait Wait for a short time.
A86C CALL TestPauseKeys Test whether SPACE or CAPS SHIFT is being pressed.
A86F JR Z,PauseGame_Wait Jump back until one of them is pressed.

A88E CALL ScrollMessage Scroll the message by one step.
