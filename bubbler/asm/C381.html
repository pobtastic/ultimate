C381: Draw Block Shape
Used by the routine at DrawBlock.
Copies the four step bytes of the shape into this routine and sign-extends each one, then draws the block. A shape below 09 and shape 11 is drawn here a row at a time. Shapes 09 to 10 are drawn by DrawBlockShapeThree and shapes 12 to 15 by DrawBlockShapeTwo. DrawBlock patches in the shape number and the row count.
Input
IY The shape
Each step byte of the shape is sign-extended into a word that the row loops add to the screen address and the sprite address.
