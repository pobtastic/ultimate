D47A: Test Entity Pair
Used by the routine at TestEntityCollisions.
Tests whether two entities overlap on x, then y, then z, using TestOverlap. The three extents are fields 0B 0C and 0D. The y test and the z test move IX so that those extents sit at the displacement the test reads. An overlap is stored at OverlapDistances for x, DDD1 for y and DDD3 for z. A puff with no width flags then scores, from the table at ScoreTable, and becomes a sparkle. A whirler meeting the player, a spider and a shot all burst. A shot is then removed. A pickup is collected with CollectItem, and the player is moved clear with HandleOverlap_Compare.
Input
IX The entity that is being moved
IY The other entity
