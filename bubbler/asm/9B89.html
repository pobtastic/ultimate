9B89: Update Foam Entity
Updates an entity of the type 0A which is made from the first kind of creature at CreatureTemplates. Roughly once in every 20 frames it flips bits of field 1D and once in every 40 frames it chooses a new direction towards the player.
Input
IX The entity
UpdateFoamEntity 9B89 LD A,($5F9B) Fetch the random number from 5F9B.
9B8C AND %00011111 Use bits 0-4.
9B8E JR NZ,UpdateFoamEntity_Test Jump to UpdateFoamEntity_Test unless they are all zero.
9B90 LD A,(IX+$1D) Read field 1D of the entity.
9B93 XOR %11000100 Flip bits 2, 6 and 7.
9B95 LD (IX+$1D),A Store it in field 1D of the entity.
UpdateFoamEntity_Test 9B98 CALL TestEntityRemoval Call TestEntityRemoval to test whether the entity should be removed.
9B9B CALL IncreaseEntityCounter Call IncreaseEntityCounter.
9B9E BIT 7,(IX+$23) Test bit 7 of field 23 of the entity.
9BA2 CALL NZ,ResetEntityMovement Call ResetEntityMovement if it is set.
9BA5 LD A,($5F9A) Fetch the low byte of the seed from RandomNumber_Seed.
9BA8 AND %00111111 Use bits 0-5.
9BAA CALL Z,FacePlayer Call FacePlayer to choose the direction towards the player if they are all zero.
9BAD LD B,$01 Set the size of the step to 01.
9BAF CALL TurnEntity Call TurnEntity to turn the entity towards the wanted heading.
9BB2 SET 7,(IX+$11) Set bit 7 of the heading of the entity.
9BB6 JP UpdateEntity_Move Jump to UpdateEntity_Move to carry on updating the entity.
