9EE3: Play Short Buzz
Used by the routine at CreatePuff.
Plays a buzz of 0D pulses with a seed of 14.
PlayShortBuzz 9EE3 LD C,$0D Set the number of pulses to 0D.
9EE5 LD E,$14 Set the seed to 14.
9EE7 JP PlayBuzz Jump to 9ED5 to play the buzz.
