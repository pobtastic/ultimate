CA17: Shift View Left Nibble
Shifts the screen buffer to the left by four pixels, by rotating the nibbles of each row.
ShiftViewLeftNibble CA17 LD B,$80 Set the row count to 80.
CA19 LD HL,$F1BA Point HL at the last byte of the screen buffer.
CA1C XOR A Clear the nibble shifted in from the right.
ShiftViewLeftNibble_Loop CA1D RLD Shift the row four pixels to the left.
