ACF4: Append Entity Item
Used by the routine at PrepareEntityItem.
Adds an entity to the draw list without preparing a table record for it. Used for entity types 0C and 10.
Input
IX The entity
AppendEntityItem ACF4 EXX Keep the other registers aside.
ACF5 LD HL,($B06C) The next free entry of the draw list.
ACF8 LD (HL),$01 Mark the entry as an entity and move on.
ACFA INC HL
ACFB PUSH IX Write this entity's address into the entry.
ACFD POP DE
ACFE LD (HL),E
ACFF INC HL
AD00 LD (HL),D
AD01 INC HL
AD02 LD ($B06C),HL The list continues after this entry.
AD05 EXX Bring the other registers back.
AD06 RET Return.
