C76D: Draw Block Bytes
Used by the routines at TestBlockRow_Draw and DrawBlockSection.
Draws the bytes of a row of a block into the screen buffer. A column below 10 is off the view, and a column of C0 is drawn only when bits 1-2 of DrawingFlags are clear. The tile is read through the stack. Those bits clear draw the bytes plain, with C611; otherwise DrawShiftedRows_Two shifts them.
Input
A The column
HL The address of the data
