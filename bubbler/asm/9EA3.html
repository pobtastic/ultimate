9EA3: Play Fanfare
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Plays three rising sweeps of clicks and then the tune at FanfareTune.
PlayFanfare 9EA3 LD C,$03 Set the number of sweeps in C to 03.
PlayFanfare_Sweep 9EA5 LD B,$FF Start the sweep with a length of FF.
PlayFanfare_Click 9EA7 CALL PlayClick Play a click.
9EAA DJNZ PlayFanfare_Click Shorten the length and jump back until the sweep has finished.
PlayFanfare_Pause 9EAC DJNZ PlayFanfare_Pause Pause for a moment.
9EAE DEC C Decrease the number of sweeps by one.
9EAF JR NZ,PlayFanfare_Sweep Jump back until all of the sweeps have been played.
9EB1 LD DE,$9EBF Point DE at FanfareTune.
9EB4 JP PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic to play the tune.
