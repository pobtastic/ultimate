ADE9: Row Routine Table
The addresses of the routines that draw the rows of a sprite, used by DrawSprite. The first four draw sprites that are byte aligned and are for widths of one to four bytes, and the last four are for shifted sprites.
RowRoutineTable ADE9 DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_One
ADEB DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_Two
ADED DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_Three
ADEF DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows
ADF1 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_One
ADF3 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_Two
ADF5 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_Three
ADF7 DEFW DrawShiftedRows
