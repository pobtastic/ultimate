9F79: Scratch Items
Two buffers, each holding a copy of an item from the draw list that has been expanded into the same layout as an entity, for SortDrawList. The positions are copied in at offsets 02 05 and 08 and the sizes at offsets 0B 0C and 0D are always 06 06 and 01.
