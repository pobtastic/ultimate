DF1F: Start Game
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Sets up everything for a new game: the lives, the scores, the gauges, the bottles and the details of the second player if there is one. The entry point at StartGame_Life is also used to start each life, and resets the player state.
Colour the area at the bottom of the screen.
