D6CE: Test Edge Overlap
Used by the routine at TestGroupObjects.
Compares a patched position with a patched edge that has been moved back by four. The carry is set when the position is below that edge. TestGroupObjects patches both operands before the call.
Both operands are patched before the call. The edge is moved back by four and the position is compared with it.
