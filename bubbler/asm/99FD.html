99FD: Create Random Creature
Used by the routines at CreateSpawnedEntity_Creature and MakeCreature.
Sets up an entity as a creature of a random kind, using SetUpCreature.
Input
IX The entity
CreateRandomCreature 99FD CALL GetRandomNumber Call GetRandomNumber to get a random number.
9A00 LD A,($5F9A) Fetch the low byte of the seed.
9A03 AND %00000011 Use bits 0-1 to make the kind of the creature.
9A05 LD C,$3F Set the mask for the random heading to 3F.
9A07 JP SetUpCreature Jump to SetUpCreature.
