9B66: Reset Entity Movement
Used by the routines at UpdateFoamEntity, UpdateWhirlerEntity and UpdateBatEntity.
Resets bit 7 of the heading of an entity. If field 1F of the entity is not zero then this routine does not return to its caller, but jumps to UpdateEntity_Move to carry on updating the entity. Otherwise bit 7 of field 23 is reset too.
Input
IX The entity
ResetEntityMovement 9B66 RES 7,(IX+$11) Reset bit 7 of the heading.
9B6A LD A,(IX+$1F) Read field 1F of the entity.
9B6D INC A Add one.
9B6E CP $02 Compare it with 02.
9B70 POP HL Drop the return address into HL.
9B71 JP NC,UpdateEntity_Move Jump to UpdateEntity_Move to carry on updating the entity if the value was 02 or more.
9B74 RES 7,(IX+$23) Reset bit 7 of field 23 of the entity.
9B78 JP (HL) Return to the caller.
