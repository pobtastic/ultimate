9B63: Update Basic Entity
Updates an entity of the type 01 02 or 03 by passing it straight on to UpdateEntity.
Input
IX The entity
UpdateBasicEntity 9B63 JP UpdateEntity Jump to UpdateEntity to carry on updating the entity.
