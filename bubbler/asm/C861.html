C861: Clear Rows
Used by the routines at ScrollViewDown and ScrollViewUp.
Clears C0 bytes, which are eight rows of the screen buffer, by pushing zeroes on the stack.
Input
HL The address of the end of the bytes to clear
ClearRows C861 LD ($5CDA),SP Write SP at SavedStackPointer.
C865 LD SP,HL HL moves into SP.
C866 LD B,$60 B is 60.
C868 LD DE,$0000 DE is 00.
ClearRows_Loop C86B PUSH DE Remember DE.
C86C DJNZ ClearRows_Loop Repeat from C86B while B still counts.
C86E LD SP,($5CDA) Fetch the word at SavedStackPointer into SP.
C872 RET Return.
