D202: Follow Path
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Moves an entity that is following a path from the level data, which is the case when fields 24 and 25 are not both zero. Bits 0-2 of field 25 choose a three-byte step from a table. Bits 6-7 of field 24 can replace that step: 40 lowers the entity and can play the long buzz, 80 moves it back along y, and C0 moves it on along x. The three bytes of a looked-up step are added to x, y and z. A step above 08 finishes the path. The player can be scored and shown the filled cup, and a path position is then copied into the entity before the falling sweep plays.
