C72E: Draw Block Row Data
Used by the routine at DrawShortRow.
Finds the tile for a row in the table at 666E flips it with FlipBlockData when its top bit differs, and draws it with DrawBlockBytes. A row size of 10 is drawn at once. Any other size is given a limit of the column plus 10.
The column offset is four times the row, less 07. The tile index and its top bit are patched, as they are for DrawBlockRow.
