60E8: Leave Title Menu
Used by the routine at ShowTitleMenu.
Used when the title menu is finished. It records which control methods were chosen and plays the start tune. The tune routine returns to the caller of ShowTitleMenu.
LeaveTitleMenu 60E8 DI Disable interrupts.
60E9 LD A,($6281) Fetch the control method chosen for player 1 from 6281.
60EC LD ($60FB),A Store it in Player1ControlMethod.
60EF LD A,($6284) Fetch the control method chosen for player 2 from 6284.
60F2 LD ($60FC),A Store it in Player2ControlMethod.
60F5 LD DE,$6048 Point DE at StartTune.
60F8 JP PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic to play the tune.
