9ABE: Make Entity Sound
Used by the routine at UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity.
Plays four short tones, with a pitch which depends on field 20 of the entity, and carries on with the update of the entity.
Input
IX The entity
MakeEntitySound 9ABE LD A,(IX+$20) Read field 20 of the entity.
9AC1 XOR %00011111 Flip bits 0-4.
9AC3 LD B,A Keep it in B.
9AC4 ADD A,A Multiply it by four...
9AC5 ADD A,A
9AC6 ADD A,B ...and add it to make five times the value.
9AC7 ADD A,$10 Add 10 to make the length of the tones.
9AC9 LD B,A Set the length of the tones in B.
9ACA CALL PlayClick Call PlayClick to play a tone.
9ACD CALL PlayClick Call PlayClick to play a tone.
9AD0 CALL PlayClick Call PlayClick to play a tone.
9AD3 CALL PlayClick Call PlayClick to play a tone.
9AD6 JP UpdateEntity_Project Jump to UpdateEntity_Project to carry on updating the entity.
