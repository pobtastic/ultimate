9F4E: Play Medium Buzz
Used by the routine at UpdateExtraRecord.
Plays a buzz of 0F pulses with a seed of 0F.
PlayMediumBuzz 9F4E LD C,$0F Set the number of pulses to 0F.
9F50 LD E,$0F Set the seed to 0F.
9F52 JP PlayBuzz Jump to 9ED5 to play the buzz.
