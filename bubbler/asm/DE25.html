DE25: Reset Entities
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Clears the entities other than the player, and marks every extra record free. The player is copied from the template at PlayerTemplate and the jump counter is cleared. If BottleFlag is set, the player's animation low nibble and the control byte are set to 05. SetEntitySize then measures the sprite.
ResetEntities DE25 LD IX,$B566 Start at the first entity after the player.
