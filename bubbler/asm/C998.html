C998: Shift Right Table
The addresses of the routines that shift the screen buffer to the right, which are used by ScrollViewRight. They shift by a nibble, a byte and a pair of pixels.
ShiftRightTable C998 DEFW ShiftViewRightPixels,$CB71,$CB23
