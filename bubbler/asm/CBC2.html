CBC2: Shift View Right Pixels
Shifts the screen buffer to the right by two pixels, by rotating the bits of each row through the stack.
ShiftViewRightPixels CBC2 LD ($5CDA),SP Save the stack pointer.
CBC6 LD SP,$E5BB Point the stack at the first byte of the screen buffer.
CBC9 LD B,$80 Set the row count to 80.
ShiftViewRightPixels_Loop CBCB XOR A Clear the pixels shifted in from the left of the row.
CBCC POP DE Shift the leftmost two bytes of the row two pixels to the right.
