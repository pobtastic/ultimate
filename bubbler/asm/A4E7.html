A4E7: Scroll Stage Message
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Scrolls the message 'STAGE' and the number of the stage across the window, one frame at a time, while the gauges are updated.

A4E9 LD ($A572),A ...in the operand of the instruction at A571.
A4EC LD A,($DEDF) Fetch the number of the current level from LevelNumber.
A4EF INC A Add one to make the number of the stage.
A4F0 LD ($A597),A Store it as the last character of the message at A597.
A4F3 LD HL,$FF0E Set the scroll counter to 0E and the character index to FF.
A4F6 LD ($A921),HL Store them in ScrollCounter and A922.
