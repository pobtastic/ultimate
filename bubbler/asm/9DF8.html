9DF8: Increase Entity Counter
Used by the routines at UpdateFoamEntity, UpdateWhirlerEntity, UpdateBatEntity, UpdateDropEntity, UpdateSaucerEntity and UpdateDomeEntity.
Increases the counter in the high nibble of field 16 of an entity by one, if it is not zero, and wraps it to zero after 08. Then plays a click of a random length.
Input
IX The entity
IncreaseEntityCounter 9DF8 LD A,(IX+$16) Read field 16 of the entity.
9DFB LD B,A Keep it in B.
9DFC AND %11110000 Keep the counter.
9DFE RET Z Return if it is zero.
9DFF ADD A,$10 Add one to the counter.
9E01 CP $90 Jump to IncreaseEntityCounter_Store if it is below 90.
9E03 JR C,IncreaseEntityCounter_Store
9E05 XOR A Set the counter to zero.
IncreaseEntityCounter_Store 9E06 LD C,A Keep the new counter in C.
9E07 LD A,B Fetch the old value of field 16.
9E08 AND %00001111 Keep the low nibble.
9E0A OR C Combine it with the new counter.
9E0B LD (IX+$16),A Store it in field 16 of the entity.
9E0E LD A,($5F9A) Fetch the low byte of the seed from RandomNumber_Seed.
9E11 AND %00111111 Use bits 0-5.
9E13 INC A Add one.
9E14 JP PlayClick Jump to PlayClick to play a click.
