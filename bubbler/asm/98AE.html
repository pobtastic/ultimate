98AE: Clear Entity
Used by the routines at CreatePickup, CreateSpawnedEntity and UpdateSaucerEntity.
Sets all of the bytes of an entity to zero, using the stack.
Input
IX The entity
ClearEntity 98AE PUSH IX Copy the address of the entity into HL (using the stack).
98B0 POP HL
98B1 LD DE,$0026 DE is 26 the size of an entity.
98B4 ADD HL,DE Point HL at the end of the entity.
98B5 LD ($98D0),SP Save the stack pointer in the operand of the instruction at 98CF.
98B9 LD SP,HL Point the stack at the end of the entity.
98BA LD E,$00 DE is 00.
98BC PUSH DE Write 00 to the 26 bytes of the entity, 02 bytes at a time.
