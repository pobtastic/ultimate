D014: Decay Drift
Used by the routine at UpdateDrift.
Moves the drift of an entity, which is held in field 20 one step towards zero.
Input
HL The displacement
IX The entity
DecayDrift D014 LD A,(IX+$20) Read the drift.
D017 AND %00011111 Use the magnitude in the low five bits.
D019 RET Z The drift is already zero.
D01A PUSH HL Remember the displacement.
D01B LD A,$01 The nudge is one.
D01D BIT 7,(IX+$20) A negative drift is nudged the other way.
D021 JR NZ,DecayDrift_Nudge
D023 NEG The nudge is minus one.
DecayDrift_Nudge D025 CALL UpdateDrift_Apply Nudge the drift.
D028 LD A,D The nudge crossed zero when the signs differ.
D029 XOR (IX+$20)
D02C RLA
D02D JR NC,DecayDrift_Keep
D02F LD (IX+$20),$00 Clear the drift and restore the displacement.
D033 POP HL
D034 RET Return.
DecayDrift_Keep D035 POP DE Keep the updated displacement.
D036 RET Return.
