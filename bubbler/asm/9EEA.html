9EEA: Play Tiny Buzz
Used by the routine at UpdateSparkleEntity.
Plays a buzz of 04 pulses with a seed of 03.
PlayTinyBuzz 9EEA LD C,$04 Set the number of pulses to 04.
9EEC LD E,$03 Set the seed to 03.
9EEE JP PlayBuzz Jump to 9ED5 to play the buzz.
