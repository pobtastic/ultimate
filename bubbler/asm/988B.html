988B: Spawn Kinds
The kinds of entity which a spawn point can make, for UpdateSpawnPoints. Each kind is described by seven bytes: the address of the first of its entity slots, the number of slots, the type of entity and the three sizes of the entity. The kind 03 is not used.
SpawnKinds 988B DEFW $B64A The address of the first entity slot of kind 00.
988D DEFB $01 The number of entity slots of kind 00.
988E DEFB $06 The type of entity of kind 00.
988F DEFB $10,$10,$03 The three sizes of entity of kind 00.
9892 DEFW EntitiesEntity_01 The address of the first entity slot of kind 01.
9894 DEFB $02 The number of entity slots of kind 01.
9895 DEFB $08 The type of entity of kind 01.
9896 DEFB $08,$08,$08 The three sizes of entity of kind 01.
9899 DEFW EntitiesEntity_01 The address of the first entity slot of kind 02.
989B DEFB $02 The number of entity slots of kind 02.
989C DEFB $00 The type of entity of kind 02.
989D DEFB $08,$08,$08 The three sizes of entity of kind 02.
98A0 DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 Kind 03 which is not used.
98A7 DEFW EntitiesEntity_03 The address of the first entity slot of kind 04.
98A9 DEFB $02 The number of entity slots of kind 04.
98AA DEFB $0E The type of entity of kind 04.
98AB DEFB $04,$04,$04 The three sizes of entity of kind 04.
