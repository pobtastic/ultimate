E376: Draw Frame Corners
Used by the routine at DrawFrame.
Draws the pictures for the corners of a frame at the left and the right of one of its edges.
Input
A The vertical position
IX The entry of the frame
DrawFrameCorners E376 LD H,A Copy the y position into H.
E377 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the x position of the frame.
E37A SUB $08 Subtract 08 to put the corner beside the left end of the bar.
E37C LD L,A Copy it into L.
E37D CALL DrawPicture Draw the left corner.
E380 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the width of the frame.
E383 INC A Add one.
E384 ADD A,A Double it.
E385 ADD A,A Double it again.
E386 ADD A,A Double it a third time, to find the width in pixels.
E387 ADD A,L Add the x position of the left corner.
E388 LD L,A Copy it into L.
E389 JP DrawPicture Jump to DrawPicture.
