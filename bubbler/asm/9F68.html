9F68: Play Brief Buzz
Used by the routine at CreateShot.
Plays a buzz of 0A pulses with a seed of 26.
PlayBriefBuzz 9F68 LD C,$0A Set the number of pulses to 0A.
9F6A LD E,$26 Set the seed to 26.
9F6C JP PlayBuzz Jump to 9ED5 to play the buzz.
