A3FE: Collect Bottle
Used by the routine at TestGroupObjects.
Handles the player collecting a bottle, if the flag at BottleFlag is set. The score is increased, the bottle is shown as an empty cup, and the number of bottles left is decreased. If there are none left then the exit is opened.
