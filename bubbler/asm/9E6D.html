9E6D: Update Dome Entity
Updates an entity of the type 06 which rises by one unit of its third coordinate on every frame until the countdown in field 23 reaches zero, when it is freed.
Input
IX The entity
UpdateDomeEntity 9E6D CALL IncreaseEntityCounter Call IncreaseEntityCounter.
9E70 DEC (IX+$23) Decrease field 23 of the entity by one.
9E73 JP Z,FreeEntity Jump to FreeEntity to free the entity if it is zero.
9E76 LD H,(IX+$08) Fetch the first byte of the third coordinate of the entity.
9E79 LD L,(IX+$09) Fetch the second byte of the third coordinate of the entity.
9E7C INC HL Add one.
9E7D LD (IX+$08),H Store the first byte of the third coordinate of the entity.
9E80 LD (IX+$09),L Store the second byte of the third coordinate of the entity.
9E83 JP UpdateEntity_Project Jump to UpdateEntity_Project to carry on updating the entity.
