87AB: Level Settings
The settings of each level, which are used by SetLevelGraphics. There are eight bytes for each level. The first is the attribute of the game area, which is stored at E11E and the second is the number of bottles that are to be collected. The three words that follow are the tiles that SetLevelGraphics sets for the entries at 666E 669C and 6670 of the table at TileTable. The entry at 6672 is chosen from the third of them.
