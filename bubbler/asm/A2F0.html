A2F0: Update High Score
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Compares the five digits of the score at ScoreText_PlayerOne with the five digits of the high score at HighScoreText and, if the score is higher, copies it over the high score.
UpdateHighScore A2F0 LD HL,$E1A0 Point HL at the digits of the high score.
A2F3 LD DE,$E132 Point DE at ScoreText_PlayerOne.
A2F6 LD B,$05 Set a counter of five digits.
UpdateHighScore_Compare A2F8 LD A,(DE) Fetch a digit of the score.
A2F9 CP (HL) Compare it with the same digit of the high score.
A2FA RET C Return if the score is lower.
A2FB JR NZ,UpdateHighScore_Copy Jump to UpdateHighScore_Copy if the score is higher.
A2FD INC DE Move onto the next digit of the score.
A2FE INC HL Move onto the next digit of the high score.
A2FF JR UpdateHighScore_Compare Jump back to compare the next digit.
UpdateHighScore_Copy A301 LD DE,$E1A0 Point DE at the digits of the high score.
A304 LD HL,$E132 Point HL at ScoreText_PlayerOne.
A307 LD BC,$0005 Set the number of digits.
A30A LDIR Copy the score over the high score.
A30C RET Return.
