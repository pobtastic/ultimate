9662: Add Score
Used by the routines at CollectBottle, CountDownTime, FollowPath, TestEntityPair and CollectItem.
Adds points to the score of the current player, which is kept as six digits in three bytes of binary coded decimal, and awards an extra life each time the score passes a multiple of 5000 points (see D619). It then prints the new score.
Input
B The highest two digits of the points, in binary coded decimal
C The lowest two digits of the points, in binary coded decimal
AddScore 9662 LD A,($DEFB) Fetch the highest two digits of the score and keep them in D.
9665 LD D,A
9666 LD A,($DEFA) Fetch the middle two digits of the score.
9669 LD E,$00 Clear the flag in E, which will show whether the score was already past the 5000 point mark.
966B CP $50 Jump to AddScore_Add if they are below 50.
966D JR C,AddScore_Add
966F INC E Set the flag.
AddScore_Add 9670 LD HL,$DEF9 Point HL at the score.
9673 LD A,C Fetch the lowest two digits of the points.
9674 ADD A,(HL) Add the lowest two digits of the score.
9675 DAA Adjust the result to binary coded decimal.
9676 LD (HL),A Store the new lowest two digits.
9677 INC HL Move onto the middle two digits.
9678 LD A,B Fetch the middle two digits of the points.
9679 ADC A,(HL) Add the middle two digits of the score, with the carry.
967A DAA Adjust the result to binary coded decimal.
967B LD (HL),A Store the new middle two digits.
967C INC HL Move onto the highest two digits.
967D LD A,$00 A is 00.
967F ADC A,(HL) Add the highest two digits of the score, with the carry.
9680 DAA Adjust the result to binary coded decimal.
9681 LD (HL),A Store the new highest two digits.
9682 LD A,E Fetch the flag.
9683 AND A Test it.
9684 JR NZ,PrintScore Jump to PrintScore if the score was already past the 5000 point mark.
9686 LD A,($DEFA) Fetch the middle two digits of the score.
9689 CP $50 Compare them with 50.
968B CALL NC,CollectItem_ExtraLife Call D619 to award an extra life if the score has now passed the 5000 mark.
968E JR PrintScore_Digits Jump to PrintScore_Digits to print the score.
