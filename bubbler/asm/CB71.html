CB71: Shift View Right Nibble
Shifts the screen buffer to the right by four pixels, by rotating the nibbles of each row.
ShiftViewRightNibble CB71 LD B,$80 Set the row count to 80.
CB73 LD HL,$E5BB Point HL at ScreenBuffer.
ShiftViewRightNibble_Loop CB76 XOR A Clear the nibble shifted in from the left and shift the row four pixels to the right.
