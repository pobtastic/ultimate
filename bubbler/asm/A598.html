A598: Message Character
A text record, in the format used by PrintText, for the character that ScrollMessage adds on the right of a message that is scrolling.
MessageCharacter A598 DEFB $A0,$BE,$00,$81 The position, colour and flags.
A59C DEFB $00 The character.
