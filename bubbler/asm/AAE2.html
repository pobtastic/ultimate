AAE2: Attribute Rectangle
A rectangle of attributes for FillScratchRectangle, which has three bytes.
AttributeRectangle AAE2 DEFB $00 The width in character cells.
AAE3 DEFB $00 The height in character cells.
AAE4 DEFB $00 The attribute.
