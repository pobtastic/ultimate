A59D: Gauges
Two gauges which are drawn as horizontal bars, for UpdateGauge. Each has four bytes: the current length of the bar in pixels, the column of the left end of the bar, and a word whose high byte is the target length. The word of the first gauge is counted down as time passes (see A59F).
Gauges A59D DEFB $00 The current length of the first gauge.
A59E DEFB $00 The column of the left end of the first gauge.
A59F DEFW $6000 The target of the first gauge.
Gauges_Second A5A1 DEFB $00 The current length of the second gauge.
A5A2 DEFB $15 The column of the left end of the second gauge.
A5A3 DEFW $6000 The target of the second gauge.
