60FE: Show Title Menu
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the title menu and runs it until the player presses "0" to start the game. While it waits it plays the title tune, highlights the chosen player option and scrolls a message along the bottom of the screen.
Pressing "A" or "B" chooses a one or two player game. Pressing "1" or "2" steps player 1 or player 2 onto the next control method, which moves the marker of that player down the list of options.

The main loop of the menu.
ShowTitleMenu_Loop 617A EI Enable interrupts.
617B LD A,($5CB9) Fetch the low byte of the attribute address of the highlighted option.
617E LD L,A Copy it into L.
617F CP $AE Jump to ShowTitleMenu_TestOnePlayer if the two player option is highlighted.
6181 JR NZ,ShowTitleMenu_TestOnePlayer
6183 LD A,$7F Read from the keyboard;

0 1 2 3 4
7F SPACE SYMBOL SHIFT M N B

6188 AND %00010000 Test the bit for "B".
618A JR Z,ShowTitleMenu_MoveMarkers Jump to ShowTitleMenu_MoveMarkers if "B" is not being pressed.

618E JR ShowTitleMenu_SetPlayers Jump to ShowTitleMenu_SetPlayers.
ShowTitleMenu_TestOnePlayer 6190 LD A,$FD Read from the keyboard;

0 1 2 3 4

6192 CALL ReadKeyboard

6196 JR NC,ShowTitleMenu_MoveMarkers Jump to ShowTitleMenu_MoveMarkers if "A" is not being pressed.

ShowTitleMenu_SetPlayers 6199 LD ($60FD),A Store the number of players.

ShowTitleMenu_ResetHighlight 61A6 LD (HL),$42 Set the attribute of the option which is no longer highlighted to the normal colour.
61A8 INC L Move onto the next attribute.
61A9 DJNZ ShowTitleMenu_ResetHighlight Decrease the width counter by one and jump back to ShowTitleMenu_ResetHighlight until the whole option has been reset.
Move the markers, if needed.
ShowTitleMenu_MoveMarkers 61AB EI Enable interrupts.
61AC LD IX,$627F Point IX at the record of the marker of player 1 at MarkerRecords.
61B0 LD E,$01 E is 01 the bit of the "1" key.
61B2 CALL MoveMarker Call MoveMarker to move the marker of player 1.
61B5 LD IX,$6282 Point IX at the record of the marker of player 2 at 6282.
61B9 INC E E is 02 the bit of the "2" key.
61BA CALL MoveMarker Call MoveMarker to move the marker of player 2.
Wait for the next frame.
ShowTitleMenu_WaitFrame 61BD LD A,($5CBA) Fetch the last value of FRAMES that was seen from 5CBA.
61C0 LD B,A Copy it into B.
61C1 LD A,($5C78) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
61C4 CP B Compare it with the last value.
61C5 JR Z,ShowTitleMenu_WaitFrame Jump back to ShowTitleMenu_WaitFrame until it has changed.
61C7 LD ($5CBA),A Store the new value in 5CBA.
61CA DI Disable interrupts.
Cycle the colours of the highlighted option.
61CB LD B,$09 Set the width of the highlight in B to 09.
61CD LD A,($5CB9) Fetch the low byte of the attribute address of the highlighted option.
61D0 LD L,A Copy it into L.
61D1 LD H,$58 Point H at the attribute rows.
61D3 LD A,($5CB8) Fetch the animation frame from TitleAnimationFrame.
61D6 DEC A Decrease it by one.
61D7 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
61D9 LD ($5CB8),A Store it.
ShowTitleMenu_Highlight 61DC OR %01000000 Make the attribute bright with the ink from A.
61DE LD (HL),A Store it.
61DF INC L Move onto the next attribute.
61E0 INC A Move onto the next ink colour.
61E1 AND %00000111 Keep the ink colour in the range 00-07.
61E3 DJNZ ShowTitleMenu_Highlight Decrease the width counter by one and jump back to ShowTitleMenu_Highlight until the whole option has been done.

Scroll the message along the bottom of the screen.

6215 LD BC,$1607 Set the width in B to 16 bytes and the height in C to 07 rows.
6218 CALL ScrollAreaLeft Call ScrollAreaLeft to scroll the message frame left by one pixel.
