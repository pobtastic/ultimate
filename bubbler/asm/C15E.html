C15E: Draw Block
Used by the routines at DrawItems, DrawRightStrip and DrawLeftStrip.
Draws a block from the drawing variables at DrawingVariables. The three sizes at RecordDetails supply the column and row sizes, from the high nibble of each byte. The shape index comes from the object's frame, unless a record has patched DrawBlock_Index so that it loads the record's own index. The shape byte is looked up from that index, and one section is drawn for each 40 in the byte.
The flags at ObjectFlagsTable, indexed by the shape kind, choose the layout. Bit 7 clear draws the block as it is. Bits 5-6 clear mirror it, 20 draws it as it is, 40 raises it, and any other value moves the origin back by 10. Kinds 11 and 12 use a column size of one and lower the screen position by the third size. Each section is drawn by DrawBlockShape.
