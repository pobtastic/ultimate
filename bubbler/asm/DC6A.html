DC6A: Handle Overlap
Used by the routine at TestGroupObjects.
Moves an entity off the overlap whose axis is the smallest of the three held at OverlapDistances. The x axis is handled by HandleOverlapFirst and the y axis by HandleOverlapSecond. On z, a player under a flagged block changes frame, the difference is planted for TestEntityCells, and the vertical speed pushes the entity clear. A saucer sets the high byte of its vertical speed to 02 first.
Input
IX The entity
IY The other entity or object
The smallest overlap chooses the axis. A saucer is pushed up before the choice is made.
