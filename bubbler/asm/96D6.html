96D6: Choose Second Side
Used by the routine at CreatePickup.
CreatePickup_SecondSide 96D6 DEC A Decrease the chosen side by one.
96D7 JR NZ,CreatePickup_ThirdSide Jump to CreatePickup_ThirdSide unless the second side is chosen.
96D9 LD DE,$00A0 DE is A0 the offset for the second side.
96DC LD C,E E moves into C.
96DD LD B,D D moves into B, so the offset is also BC.
96DE LD A,$20 A is 20 the direction for the second side.
96E0 JR CreatePickup_Place Jump to CreatePickup_Place.
