C418: Draw Block Shape Three
Used by the routine at DrawBlockShape.
Draws a block whose shape number is 09 to 10. DrawBlockShape has already copied the shape's steps into this routine. The shape number chooses the edges: shapes below 0B use one edge, 0B and 0C leave the edges alone, 0D and 0E use two, and 0F and 10 lengthen the row count by 0E as well. Each column is drawn a row at a time by DrawBlockRow.
The shape number is in A. It chooses how many edge steps this column adds, and which edges are written before a single-row column.
