A581: Copy Column
Used by the routine at ScrollMessage.
Copies a column of seven pixel lines, one byte wide, from the address C0 bytes after the destination address to the destination address.
Input
HL The destination address
CopyColumn A581 LD E,L Copy the low byte of the destination address into E.
A582 LD D,H Copy the high byte into D.
A583 LD A,$C0 Set the offset to C0.
A585 CALL AddToAddress Add the offset to the source address.
This entry point is used by the routine at ShowTitleMenu.
CopyColumn_Copy A588 LD B,$07 Set the number of lines to 07.
CopyColumn_Loop A58A LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the source.
A58B LD (DE),A Store it at the destination.
A58C INC H Move down a pixel line in the source.
A58D INC D Move down a pixel line in the destination.
A58E DJNZ CopyColumn_Loop Jump back until all of the lines have been copied.
A590 RET Return.
