9F2A: Play Long Buzz
Used by the routine at FollowPath.
Plays a buzz of 38 pulses with a seed of 7F.
PlayLongBuzz 9F2A LD C,$38 Set the number of pulses to 38.
9F2C LD E,$7F Set the seed to 7F.
9F2E JP PlayBuzz Jump to 9ED5 to play the buzz.
