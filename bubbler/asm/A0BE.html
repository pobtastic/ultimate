A0BE: Draw Order Table
The routines for each result of the comparison in SortDrawList. The result is 09 times the relation along the first coordinate plus 03 times the relation along the second plus the relation along the third, where the relation is 00 if the other item is before this one, 01 for an overlap and 02 if it is after.
