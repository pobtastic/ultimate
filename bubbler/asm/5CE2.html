5CE2: View Position
The position of the view, as two words. It is set by StartLevel when a level is started and is changed when the view is moved (see MoveView). The high bytes at 5CE3 and 5CE5 are used by GetCellPosition as the position of the player.
ViewPosition 5CE2 DEFW $0000
5CE4 DEFW $E73A
