DFAB: Remove Object Entities
Used by the routines at BuildView and RunGame.
Removes each of the four entities that were made for objects of the level by AddObjectEntity, and clears the flag of each of the objects.
RemoveObjectEntities DFAB LD IX,$B670 Point IX at B670.
DFAF LD B,$04 B is 04.
DFB1 LD DE,$0026 DE is 26.
RemoveObjectEntities_Loop DFB4 LD A,(IX+$00) Read field 00 of the entity.
DFB7 AND A See whether A is zero.
DFB8 JR Z,RemoveObjectEntities_Next Jump to RemoveObjectEntities_Next if the zero flag is set.
DFBA LD H,(IX+$1A) Read field 1A of the entity into H.
DFBD LD L,(IX+$1B) Read field 1B of the entity into L.
DFC0 INC HL Advance HL.
DFC1 INC HL Advance HL.
DFC2 RES 7,(HL) Reset bit 7 of the byte at the address in HL.
DFC4 LD (IX+$00),$00 Set field 00 of the entity to 00.
DFC8 LD (IX+$01),$80 Set field 01 of the entity to 80.
RemoveObjectEntities_Next DFCC ADD IX,DE IX takes DE.
DFCE DJNZ RemoveObjectEntities_Loop Repeat from RemoveObjectEntities_Loop while B still counts.
DFD0 RET Return.
