98D3: Face Player
Used by the routines at CreateShot, UpdatePickupEntity, UpdateFoamEntity, UpdateWhirlerEntity, UpdateBatEntity and UpdateSaucerEntity.
Chooses the direction in which an entity should head to move towards the player. If the direction (bits 4 and 5 of field 20 of the entity) is already the right one it is left alone, otherwise the new direction is set along with a random value in bits 1-3.
Input
IX The entity
FacePlayer 98D3 LD B,$00 Clear the direction bits in B.
98D5 LD A,($B542) Fetch the first byte of the first coordinate of the player from B542.
98D8 SUB (IX+$02) Subtract the first byte of the first coordinate of the entity.
98DB JR NZ,FacePlayer_FirstDone Jump to FacePlayer_FirstDone if they are different.
98DD LD A,($B543) Fetch the second byte of the first coordinate of the player from B543.
98E0 SUB (IX+$03) Subtract the second byte of the first coordinate of the entity.
FacePlayer_FirstDone 98E3 JR NC,FacePlayer_Second Jump to FacePlayer_Second if the player is not before the entity on the first coordinate.
98E5 LD B,$10 Set bit 4 of the direction.
FacePlayer_Second 98E7 LD A,($B545) Fetch the first byte of the second coordinate of the player from B545.
98EA SUB (IX+$05) Subtract the first byte of the second coordinate of the entity.
98ED JR NZ,FacePlayer_SecondDone Jump to FacePlayer_SecondDone if they are different.
98EF LD A,($B546) Fetch the second byte of the second coordinate of the player from B546.
98F2 SUB (IX+$06) Subtract the second byte of the second coordinate of the entity.
FacePlayer_SecondDone 98F5 LD A,B Fetch the direction.
98F6 JR NC,FacePlayer_Compare Jump to FacePlayer_Compare if the player is not before the entity on the second coordinate.
98F8 XOR %00110000 Flip bits 4 and 5 of the direction.
FacePlayer_Compare 98FA LD B,A Keep the direction in B.
98FB LD A,(IX+$20) Read field 20 of the entity.
98FE AND %00110000 Use bits 4 and 5.
9900 XOR B Compare them with the new direction.
9901 RET Z Return if the entity is already heading that way.
9902 LD A,($5F9A) Fetch the low byte of the random seed from RandomNumber_Seed.
9905 AND %00001110 Use bits 1-3.
9907 OR B Add the new direction.
9908 LD (IX+$20),A Store it in field 20 of the entity.
990B RET Return.
