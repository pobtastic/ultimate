E461: Wipe View In
Used by the routines at ShowEnding and RunGame.
Wipes the picture on to the screen by calling DrawWipeStep once for each step of the wipe, which goes down from 78 to 08 in twos.
WipeViewIn E461 LD A,$10 A is 10.
E463 LD ($5CE9),A Write A at ScrollValue.
E466 LD A,$78 A is 78.
E468 LD ($5CD8),A Write A at ScratchValue.
E46B LD IY,($5CF1) Fetch the word at SavedIY into IY.
E46F EI Enable interrupts.
WipeViewIn_Loop E470 LD A,($5CD8) Read the byte at ScratchValue.
E473 DI Disable interrupts.
E474 CP $08 Compare A with 08.
E476 RET Z Return if the zero flag is set.
E477 LD IY,($5CF1) Fetch the word at SavedIY into IY.
E47B EI Enable interrupts.
E47C DEC A Step A back.
E47D DEC A Step A back.
E47E LD ($5CD8),A Write A at ScratchValue.
E481 CALL DrawWipeStep Call DrawWipeStep.
E484 JP WipeViewIn_Loop Jump to E470.
