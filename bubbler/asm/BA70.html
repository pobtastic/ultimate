BA70: Draw Level Objects
Used by the routines at BuildView, ScrollViewLeft and ScrollViewRight.
Draws the objects of the level that are near the player. The nine cells around the position of the player are tried in turn, and the objects of the group in each cell are drawn with DrawGroupObjects. There are two entry points, which choose between two versions of the drawing code that is patched at C3D8.
DrawLevelObjects BA70 LD DE,$C59A Draw each row with DrawBlockRow.
BA73 JR DrawLevelObjects_Start
This entry point is used by the routines at ScrollViewDown and ScrollViewUp.
DrawLevelObjects_Second BA75 LD DE,$C717 Test each row with TestBlockRow instead.
This entry point is used by the routine at CollectBottle.
