9F6F: Play Shot Sound
Used by the routine at UpdateSaucerEntity.
Plays six clicks, each of length 80.
PlayShotSound 9F6F LD BC,$8006 Set the length of the clicks to 80 and the number of clicks to 06.
PlayShotSound_Click 9F72 CALL PlayClick Play a click.
9F75 DEC C Decrease the number of clicks by one.
9F76 JR NZ,PlayShotSound_Click Jump back until they have all been played.
9F78 RET Return.
