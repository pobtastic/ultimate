E42F: Wipe View Out
Used by the routines at ShowEnding and RunGame.
Clears the bytes at 4FC6 and at F0CD and then wipes the picture away from the screen by calling DrawWipeStep once for each step of the wipe, which goes up from 07 to 77 in twos.
