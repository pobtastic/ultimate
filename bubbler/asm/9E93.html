9E93: Play Click
Used by the routines at ShowEnding, MakeEntitySound, UpdateBurstEntity, IncreaseEntityCounter, PlayFanfare, PlayRisingSweep, PlayFallingSweep, PlayShortRisingSweep, PlaySlowFall, PlayShotSound and CountDownTime.
Plays one cycle of a tone in which the pulse and the gap are equally long.
Input
B The length of the pulse and the gap
PlayClick 9E93 LD A,$10 Set the speaker bit.

9E9E LD A,B Save the length in A.
PlayClick_Gap 9E9F DJNZ PlayClick_Gap Wait for the length of the gap.
9EA1 LD B,A Restore the length in B.
