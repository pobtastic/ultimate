40611: Play Fanfare
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Plays three rising sweeps of clicks and then the tune at FanfareTune.
PlayFanfare 40611 LD C,3 Set the number of sweeps in C to 3.
PlayFanfare_Sweep 40613 LD B,255 Start the sweep with a length of 255.
PlayFanfare_Click 40615 CALL PlayClick Play a click.
40618 DJNZ PlayFanfare_Click Shorten the length and jump back until the sweep has finished.
PlayFanfare_Pause 40620 DJNZ PlayFanfare_Pause Pause for a moment.
40622 DEC C Decrease the number of sweeps by one.
40623 JR NZ,PlayFanfare_Sweep Jump back until all of the sweeps have been played.
40625 LD DE,40639 Point DE at FanfareTune.
40628 JP PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic to play the tune.
