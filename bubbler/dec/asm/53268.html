53268: Decay Drift
Used by the routine at UpdateDrift.
Moves the drift of an entity, which is held in field 32 one step towards zero.
Input
HL The displacement
IX The entity
DecayDrift 53268 LD A,(IX+32) Read the drift.
53271 AND %00011111 Use the magnitude in the low five bits.
53273 RET Z The drift is already zero.
53274 PUSH HL Remember the displacement.
53275 LD A,1 The nudge is one.
53277 BIT 7,(IX+32) A negative drift is nudged the other way.
53281 JR NZ,DecayDrift_Nudge
53283 NEG The nudge is minus one.

53288 LD A,D The nudge crossed zero when the signs differ.
53289 XOR (IX+32)
53292 RLA
53293 JR NC,DecayDrift_Keep
53295 LD (IX+32),0 Clear the drift and restore the displacement.
53299 POP HL
53300 RET Return.
DecayDrift_Keep 53301 POP DE Keep the updated displacement.
