24478: Get Random Number
Used by the routines at CreateSpawnedEntity, CreateRandomCreature, UpdatePickupEntity and UpdateScreen.
Makes a pseudo-random number by mixing the byte at a moving address in the game memory into a seed. The address moves on by one byte each time and wraps within 32768-40959.
Output
A The high byte of the new seed
DE The new seed
