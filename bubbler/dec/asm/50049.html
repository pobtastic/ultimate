50049: Draw Block Shape
Used by the routine at DrawBlock.
Copies the four step bytes of the shape into this routine and sign-extends each one, then draws the block. A shape below 9 and shape 17 is drawn here a row at a time. Shapes 9 to 16 are drawn by DrawBlockShapeThree and shapes 18 to 21 by DrawBlockShapeTwo. DrawBlock patches in the shape number and the row count.
Input
IY The shape
Each step byte of the shape is sign-extended into a word that the row loops add to the screen address and the sprite address.
