41210: Draw Other Item First
Used by the routine at ResolveFullOverlap.
The other item must be drawn before this one, so it becomes the item under consideration. The addresses of the entries in the draw list which have been tried are kept in a list at TriedEntries, and if the other item is already in that list then there is a loop and the other item is moved to the sorted list now.
