44096: Build Draw List
Used by the routines at RunFrame and RunGame.
Builds the list of items to be drawn for the current frame, at DrawList. Each item is three bytes: a flag byte followed by the address of the record to draw. The flag is 1 for an entity (see Entities), 2 for one of the 12 extra records at ExtraRecords and 3 for one of the 20-byte records at Records. The list ends with a zero byte, and DrawListPointer holds the address of the next free item.
Every entity that is in use and not hidden is prepared with PrepareEntityItem. The matching extra record is then prepared as well, if it has been flagged as visible.
Start with an empty list and with no table records, and go through the 12 entities and their extra records.
