42369: Copy Column
Used by the routine at ScrollMessage.
Copies a column of seven pixel lines, one byte wide, from the address 192 bytes after the destination address to the destination address.
Input
HL The destination address
CopyColumn 42369 LD E,L Copy the low byte of the destination address into E.
42370 LD D,H Copy the high byte into D.
42371 LD A,192 Set the offset to 192.
42373 CALL AddToAddress Add the offset to the source address.
This entry point is used by the routine at ShowTitleMenu.
CopyColumn_Copy 42376 LD B,7 Set the number of lines to 7.
CopyColumn_Loop 42378 LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the source.
42379 LD (DE),A Store it at the destination.
42380 INC H Move down a pixel line in the source.
42381 INC D Move down a pixel line in the destination.
42382 DJNZ CopyColumn_Loop Jump back until all of the lines have been copied.
42384 RET Return.
