38544: Print Score
Used by the routine at AddScore.
Prints the score of the current player in the score panel. When it is entered at PrintScore, an extra life is awarded if the highest two digits of the score have changed.
Input
D The highest two digits of the score before the points were added
PrintScore 38544 LD A,(57083) Fetch the highest two digits of the score.
38547 CP D Compare them with the old highest two digits.
38548 CALL NZ,CollectItem_ExtraLife Call 54809 to award an extra life if they have changed.
