46027: Build Patterned Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawSprite.
Builds a copy of a sprite in the buffer at 23408 for an entity whose sprite has a pattern. The pattern is chosen by the entity and by the frame counter at FrameCounter, taken from the table at PatternTable, and each pair of bytes of the sprite is combined with a rotating mask from the table.
Input
A Bits 4-7 of field 22 of the entity, shifted
DE The address of the sprite
Output
DE The address of the buffer
The countdown nibble, already shifted down, selects the pattern. The low bits of the frame counter choose which mask, and which way it rotates.
