42731: Get Direction Vector
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Finds the sine and cosine of a heading in units of 1/64 of a turn, using the table at DirectionTable. Bits 0-3 of the heading are the angle within a quadrant, and bits 4-5 are the quadrant. This entry point takes the heading from the entity.
Input
A The heading (when entered at GetDirectionVector_Heading)
IX The entity (when entered at GetDirectionVector)
Output
BC The first component (a signed number)
HL The second component (a signed number)
