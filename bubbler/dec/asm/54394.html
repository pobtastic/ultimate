54394: Test Entity Pair
Used by the routine at TestEntityCollisions.
Tests whether two entities overlap on x, then y, then z, using TestOverlap. The three extents are fields 11 12 and 13. The y test and the z test move IX so that those extents sit at the displacement the test reads. An overlap is stored at OverlapDistances for x, 56785 for y and 56787 for z. A puff with no width flags then scores, from the table at ScoreTable, and becomes a sparkle. A whirler meeting the player, a spider and a shot all burst. A shot is then removed. A pickup is collected with CollectItem, and the player is moved clear with HandleOverlap_Compare.
