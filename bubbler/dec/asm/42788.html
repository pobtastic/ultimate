42788: Draw Direction Gauge
Used by the routines at UpdateDirectionGauge, StartGame and DrawPanel.
Draws a direction gauge, which shows the heading in ScratchValue by a needle with a ball at its tip. The picture of the dial is copied into the buffer at 62743 the needle is drawn into it, and the buffer is then copied to the screen. The picture is seven bytes wide and 28 pixel lines high.
