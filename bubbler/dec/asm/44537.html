44537: Project Player Entity
Used by the routines at RunFrame and RunGame.
Projects the player entity with ProjectEntity, then works out how far the entity has moved on the screen since the last frame. The previous value is kept in PlayerScreenPosition and the change in 23789.
Input
IX The player entity
