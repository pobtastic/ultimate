24808: Leave Title Menu
Used by the routine at ShowTitleMenu.
Used when the title menu is finished. It records which control methods were chosen and plays the start tune. The tune routine returns to the caller of ShowTitleMenu.
LeaveTitleMenu 24808 DI Disable interrupts.
24809 LD A,(25217) Fetch the control method chosen for player 1 from 25217.
24812 LD (24827),A Store it in Player1ControlMethod.
24815 LD A,(25220) Fetch the control method chosen for player 2 from 25220.
24818 LD (24828),A Store it in Player2ControlMethod.
24821 LD DE,24648 Point DE at StartTune.
24824 JP PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic to play the tune.
