46400: Entities
The entities. There are 12 of them, each of 38 bytes, and the first is the player. Field 0 is the type, which is zero when the entity is not in use. Field 1 is the frame, and its bit 7 hides the entity. Fields 2 and 3 are the x coordinate, 5 and 6 the y coordinate and 8 to 10 the z coordinate. Field 11 is the extent on the x axis, 12 the extent on the y axis and 13 the extent on the z axis. Field 14 is the column on the screen, 15 the row and 17 the heading. Field 16 is the speed, with the current speed in bits 0-3 and the maximum in bits 4-7. Field 30 holds the flags. Bit 7 is set when the entity is moving fast. Bit 6 selects the higher frame when UpdateExtraRecord builds the extra record, and is tested again by TestEntityCells. Purpose of bit 6 is not yet known.
