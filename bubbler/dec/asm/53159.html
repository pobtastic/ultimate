53159: Update Drift
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Adds the amount in A to the drift in field 32. Bits 0-1 are the amount and bit 2 makes it negative. A zero amount is handled by DecayDrift. An eighth of the resulting drift is added to the displacement in HL, which is then clamped to the limit in the high nibble of B.
Input
A The amount, in bits 0-1, with bit 2 for the direction
HL The displacement
IX The entity
Output
HL The limited displacement
