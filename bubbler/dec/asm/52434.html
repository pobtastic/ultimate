52434: Settle Player
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Updates the player entity with MoveEntity, with its speed set to zero and its heading flag cleared, and then restores the speed. The entry point at SettlePlayer goes on to set the player to frame 3 at RunFrame_Land, the entry point at SettlePlayer_Continue goes on to the end of the player update at RunFrame_Entities and the entry point at SettlePlayer_Update returns.
