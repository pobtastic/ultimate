39051: Spawn Kinds
The kinds of entity which a spawn point can make, for UpdateSpawnPoints. Each kind is described by seven bytes: the address of the first of its entity slots, the number of slots, the type of entity and the three sizes of the entity. The kind 3 is not used.
SpawnKinds 39051 DEFW 46666 The address of the first entity slot of kind 0.
39053 DEFB 1 The number of entity slots of kind 0.
39054 DEFB 6 The type of entity of kind 0.
39055 DEFB 16,16,3 The three sizes of entity of kind 0.
39058 DEFW EntitiesEntity_01 The address of the first entity slot of kind 1.
39060 DEFB 2 The number of entity slots of kind 1.
39061 DEFB 8 The type of entity of kind 1.
39062 DEFB 8,8,8 The three sizes of entity of kind 1.
39065 DEFW EntitiesEntity_01 The address of the first entity slot of kind 2.
39067 DEFB 2 The number of entity slots of kind 2.
39068 DEFB 0 The type of entity of kind 2.
39069 DEFB 8,8,8 The three sizes of entity of kind 2.
39072 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0,0 Kind 3 which is not used.
39079 DEFW EntitiesEntity_03 The address of the first entity slot of kind 4.
39081 DEFB 2 The number of entity slots of kind 4.
39082 DEFB 14 The type of entity of kind 4.
39083 DEFB 4,4,4 The three sizes of entity of kind 4.
