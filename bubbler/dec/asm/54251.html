54251: Test Entity Collisions
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Tests the entity against each of the other entities with TestEntityPair. A puff and a shot are not tested. A player skips puffs and sparkles, and a saucer skips shots. A disc, a free slot and a hidden entity are skipped. The direction bytes are 32 during the tests. Afterwards the low bytes of x and y, relative to the patched origins, replace them for TestEntityCells. The step in MoveEntityBack is removed for the tests and then put back.
Input
IX The entity
The direction bytes start at 32 so a hit during the tests has a neutral component. The type of this entity is patched into the skip below.
