56869: Reset Entities
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Clears the entities other than the player, and marks every extra record free. The player is copied from the template at PlayerTemplate and the jump counter is cleared. If BottleFlag is set, the player's animation low nibble and the control byte are set to 5. SetEntitySize then measures the sprite.

56875 LD DE,38 Each entity is 38 bytes.
