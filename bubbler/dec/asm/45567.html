45567: Add Item Record
Used by the routine at DrawObject.
Adds a 20-byte record to the table at Records, from the entity in IX and the details held at CellPosition, DrawingPosition, RecordDetails and 23797. The operand of the first instruction is the address of the next free record, and is updated each time. The number of records is kept in RecordCount. Kinds 17 and 18 set both sizes to 8 and set bit 2 of bytes 3 and 6.
Input
IX The entity
IY is the new record. Its bytes are filled from the entity and from the cell, the drawing position and the three sizes.
