55004: Test Block Overlap
Used by the routine at TestWideOverlap.
Tests the entity against one axis of a block. TestGroupObjects patches the two edges in first. Bit 6 of the object's flags selects the x edges and the x extent; otherwise the y edges and the y extent are used. The overlap is stored for that axis, and for the player the side is mixed into the flags. No overlap on z sets the flags to 255.
Input
IX The entity
IY The object
Bit 6 of the object's flags selects the axis. The two edges loaded below are patched by TestGroupObjects.
