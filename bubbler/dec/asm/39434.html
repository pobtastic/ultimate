39434: Update Pickup Entity
Updates an entity of the type 15 which is made by CreatePickup. While the frame of the entity is 0 or 1 it moves up or down on its third coordinate to line up with the player, and in one frame in every 128 on average, it changes into frame 2. Frame 3 is set up from a random choice of the table at PickupFrames, and later frames count down in field 32 until the entity is turned into one of type 7.
