50518: Flip Wide Block Data
Used by the routine at FlipBlockData.
Flips the data of a block horizontally, as FlipBlockData does, for blocks that are wider than 16 pixels.
Input
IY The shape
The mirror of each byte is taken from the table on page 249. The count is the shape's position byte.
