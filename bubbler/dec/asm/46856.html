46856: Extra Records
One extra record of 13 bytes for each of the entities. Bit 7 of field 9 is set while the record is in use. Fields 0 and 1 are the column and the row. UpdateExtraRecord writes the adjusted x coordinate to fields 2 and 3 the adjusted y coordinate to fields 4 and 5 and the adjusted z coordinate to fields 6 and 7. Field 11 holds the width in bits 0-3 and the frame in bits 4-7. Field 12 holds the height in bits 0-5, and its bit 7 copies bit 6 of the entity flags.
