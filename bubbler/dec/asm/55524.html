55524: Test Group Objects
Used by the routine at TestEntityCells.
Finds the group of objects at a cell of the level with FindGroup and then tests the entity against each of the objects, which are three bytes each. The low five bits of the group header are the number of objects. Objects of type 132 134 and 6 are skipped. A solid block stops the entity. Type 129 is a bottle and is collected with CollectBottle. Type 133 sets bit 1 of ExitMessageFlag, the exit-closed message, while BottlesLeft is not zero. A type 131 and a type 133 once no bottles are left, is solid: the entity is pushed back to the edge and FindPathAtPosition looks for a path there. Other objects are matched with TestObjectMatch. The smaller gap is kept in field 31 and bit 6 of the flags is cleared. Purpose of that gap is not yet known.
