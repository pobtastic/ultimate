47054: Update Extra Record
Used by the routine at UpdateExtraRecords.
Builds the extra record for an entity, and fills the temporary entity at TemporaryEntity so that SetEntitySize can measure its sprite. Field 31 chooses the size class: below 23 is class 0, below 37 is class 1, and any higher value is class 2. A size index of zero or 255 leaves the record unset.
Bit 6 of the flags in field 30 selects the higher frame. The frame written for the temporary entity is the class when the bit is clear, and the class plus three when it is set. The same bit is copied into bit 7 of byte 12 of the extra record. Purpose of the flag beyond that choice is not yet known. The instructions at UpdateExtraRecord_Unused would step the shape bits of the height and then start a burst, but they are not reached: the class is only 0, 1 or 2, so the jump at UpdateExtraRecord_HigherFrame is always taken. The saved zero flag then makes the height adjustment subtract field 31 rather than add the z extent.
The adjusted x coordinate is the entity's x plus half of field 11 its x extent, and the adjusted y coordinate is the entity's y plus half of field 12 its y extent. Both are reduced by a margin taken from the class. The z coordinate is the entity's z less field 31 or 256 when that would leave the high byte zero. Bytes 2 and 3 of the extra record receive the adjusted x, bytes 4 and 5 the adjusted y, and bytes 6 and 7 the adjusted z. Byte 11 holds the width in bits 0-3 and the frame in bits 4-7. Byte 12 holds the height in bits 0-5.
Input
IX The entity
IY The extra record
Output
A Zero if the record was set up, otherwise non-zero
