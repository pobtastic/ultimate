54914: Test Wide Overlap
Used by the routine at TestGroupObjects.
Tests the entity against a block with TestBlockOverlap after widening the x and y edges by half the matching extent, less four. For the test, the edge path takes its size from A, and a miss on z returns the distance instead of setting the flags to 255. The edges and those two patches are put back.
