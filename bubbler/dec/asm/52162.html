52162: Shift View Right Pixels
Shifts the screen buffer to the right by two pixels, by rotating the bits of each row through the stack.
ShiftViewRightPixels 52162 LD (23770),SP Save the stack pointer.
52166 LD SP,58811 Point the stack at the first byte of the screen buffer.
52169 LD B,128 Set the row count to 128.
ShiftViewRightPixels_Loop 52171 XOR A Clear the pixels shifted in from the left of the row.
52172 POP DE Shift the leftmost two bytes of the row two pixels to the right.
