39782: Reset Entity Movement
Used by the routines at UpdateFoamEntity, UpdateWhirlerEntity and UpdateBatEntity.
Resets bit 7 of the heading of an entity. If field 31 of the entity is not zero then this routine does not return to its caller, but jumps to UpdateEntity_Move to carry on updating the entity. Otherwise bit 7 of field 35 is reset too.
Input
IX The entity
ResetEntityMovement 39782 RES 7,(IX+17) Reset bit 7 of the heading.
39786 LD A,(IX+31) Read field 31 of the entity.
39789 INC A Add one.
39790 CP 2 Compare it with 2.
39792 POP HL Drop the return address into HL.
39793 JP NC,UpdateEntity_Move Jump to UpdateEntity_Move to carry on updating the entity if the value was 2 or more.
39796 RES 7,(IX+35) Reset bit 7 of field 35 of the entity.
39800 JP (HL) Return to the caller.
