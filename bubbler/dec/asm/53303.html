53303: Move Entity
Used by the routines at SettlePlayer, RunFrame and UpdateEntity.
Moves an entity for one frame, which is used for all of the types of entity that move. The entity is moved along its heading by the speed in fields 18-21 changed by its direction vector from GetDirectionVector, and the vertical speed in fields 18 and 19 is applied to the height. Purpose of the individual steps is not yet known.
