42544: Create Puff
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Makes an entity of the type 5 (see UpdatePuffEntity) at the position of another entity, using one of the two hidden entities from 46590, and plays a buzz. Does nothing if both entities are in use.
Input
IX The other entity

42548 LD B,2 Look at 2 entities.
42550 CALL FindHiddenEntity Call FindHiddenEntity to find a hidden entity.

42558 LD (IY+0),5 Set the type of the new entity to 5.
