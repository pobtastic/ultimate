39641: Update Growing Bubble Entity
Updates an entity of the type 8. Field 32 counts the frames and field 35 holds the state of the entity. The entity moves up or down on its third coordinate depending on field 31.
Input
IX The entity
UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity 39641 INC (IX+32) Increase field 32 of the entity by one.
39644 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the frame of the entity.
39647 AND %00011111 Keep the number of the frame.
39649 CP 6 Jump to UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Speed unless it is 6.
39651 JR NZ,UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Speed
39653 BIT 1,(IX+35) Jump to MakeCreature if bit 1 of field 35 of the entity is set.
39657 JP NZ,MakeCreature
UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Speed 39660 LD A,(IX+31) Read field 31 of the entity.
39663 INC A Add one.
39664 LD HL,40 Set the speed to 40 to move up.
39667 CP 36 Jump to UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_SetSpeed if it is below 36.
39669 JR C,UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_SetSpeed
39671 LD HL,65496 Set the speed to 65496 to move down.
UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_SetSpeed 39674 LD (IX+20),H Store the first byte of the speed in field 20 of the entity.
39677 LD (IX+21),L Store the second byte of the speed in field 21 of the entity.
39680 LD L,(IX+35) Read field 35 of the entity.
39683 BIT 0,L Jump to UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Change if bit 0 is set.
39685 JR NZ,UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Change
39687 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the frame of the entity.
39690 AND %00011111 Keep the number of the frame.
39692 CP 5 Jump to MakeEntitySound if it is below 5.
39694 JP C,MakeEntitySound
39697 JR NZ,UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Countdown Jump to UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Countdown if it is above 5.
39699 LD A,(24474) Fetch the low byte of the seed from RandomNumber_Seed and keep it in B.
39702 LD B,A
39703 AND %11000000 Use bits 6-7.
39705 JR Z,UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Change Jump to UpdateGrowingBubbleEntity_Change if they are zero.
