43299: Print Text
Used by the routines at ShowTitleMenu, PrintScore, CollectBottle, ScrollMessage, ShowExitMessage, RunFrame, CollectItem, ShowPlayerNumber, RunGame and PrintTextRecords.
Prints a text record. A text record has four bytes followed by the characters: the x position in pixels, the y position in pixels of the bottom line of the text, the attribute and the number of characters in bits 0-5. Bit 7 of the number of characters selects double height, and bits 6 and 7 together select double height and double width.
Input
DE The address of the text record
Output
DE The address after the text record
