53118: Multiply
Used by the routine at ScaleDisplacement.
Multiplies the byte in D by the byte in C.
Input
D One byte
C The other byte
Output
HL The product
The byte in D is multiplied by the byte in C. Eight bits are tested, and each bit that is set adds the shifted byte into the product.
Multiply 53118 LD H,0 The product starts at zero, with eight bits to test.

53121 LD L,H
53122 LD E,D
53123 LD A,8
53125 JR Multiply_Bit Test the first bit.
Multiply_Loop 53127 DEC A One fewer bit remains.
53128 RET Z The product is ready.

53137 ADD HL,BC Add the shifted byte into the product.
53138 JP Multiply_Loop The next bit.
