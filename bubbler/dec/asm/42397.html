42397: Gauges
Two gauges which are drawn as horizontal bars, for UpdateGauge. Each has four bytes: the current length of the bar in pixels, the column of the left end of the bar, and a word whose high byte is the target length. The word of the first gauge is counted down as time passes (see 42399).
Gauges 42397 DEFB 0 The current length of the first gauge.
42398 DEFB 0 The column of the left end of the first gauge.
42399 DEFW 24576 The target of the first gauge.
Gauges_Second 42401 DEFB 0 The current length of the second gauge.
42402 DEFB 21 The column of the left end of the second gauge.
42403 DEFW 24576 The target of the second gauge.
