45977: Collect Overlapping Item
Used by the routine at DrawItems.
Adds the entity in IX to the table of overlapping items that is being built by DrawItems. Four bytes are stored: the screen position from fields 14 and 15 the width from field 22 (times eight) and the height from field 23.
Input
IX The entity
HL' Pointer to the next free entry in the table
C' The number of entries
Output
HL' Updated pointer
