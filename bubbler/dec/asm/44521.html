44521: Row Routine Table
The addresses of the routines that draw the rows of a sprite, used by DrawSprite. The first four draw sprites that are byte aligned and are for widths of one to four bytes, and the last four are for shifted sprites.
RowRoutineTable 44521 DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_One
44523 DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_Two
44525 DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows_Three
44527 DEFW DrawUnshiftedRows
44529 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_One
44531 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_Two
44533 DEFW DrawShiftedRows_Three
44535 DEFW DrawShiftedRows
