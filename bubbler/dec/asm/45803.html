45803: Test Record Overlap
Used by the routines at DrawItems and TestRecordsAgainstEntity.
Tests whether the entity in IX overlaps the 20-byte record in IY. If it does then bit 7 of field 10 of the record is set.
Input
IX The entity
IY The 20-byte record
The record's drawing position, plus its third size, has to meet the entity on z. The addresses in bytes 16 and 18 then have to meet it on x and y.
