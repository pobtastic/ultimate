57897: Draw Panel
Used by the routines at SwitchPlayer and RunGame.
Draws the panel at the edges of the screen. This is made of four frames, which are drawn with DrawFrame using the table at FrameTable, the pictures and the text of the panel, and the attributes of the rectangles at PanelRectangles. The direction gauges are also drawn.
DrawPanel 57897 CALL UpdatePanelDigits Refresh the digits of the lives and bottles in the panel text.
Draw the four frames of the panel: the message box, the game window and the frames of the two direction gauges.
