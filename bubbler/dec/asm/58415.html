58415: Wipe View Out
Used by the routines at ShowEnding and RunGame.
Clears the bytes at 20422 and at 61645 and then wipes the picture away from the screen by calling DrawWipeStep once for each step of the wipe, which goes up from 7 to 119 in twos.
