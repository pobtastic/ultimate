53083: Scale Displacement
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Works out the displacement of an entity along an axis. The high byte of the speed word at EntitySpeed is multiplied by the value in BC, which is part of a direction vector, and divided by 16. The sign is taken from B.
Input
BC The part of the direction vector
Output
HL The displacement
