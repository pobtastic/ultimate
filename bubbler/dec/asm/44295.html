44295: Prepare Entity Item
Used by the routine at BuildDrawList.
Converts the position of the entity with ConvertEntityPosition and adds it to the draw list. Entity types 12 and 16 are only appended with AppendEntityItem. Any other type is also given a record in the table of item records, which holds a copy of the rows of the screen buffer (which are 24 bytes wide) that lie under the entity, so that RestoreItemRows can put them back after the entity has been drawn.
Input
IX The entity
HL Pointer to the next free byte in the table of item records
Output
HL Updated pointer
