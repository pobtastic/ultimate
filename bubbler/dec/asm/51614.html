51614: Adjust View Origin
Used by the routines at BuildView and ScrollViewLeft.
Adds an amount to the origin of the view, which is held in ViewPosition, 23780 and ViewOriginSum, and changes the scroll position at DrawingFlags to suit.
Input
E The amount
D The high byte of the amount, which is cleared by this entry point
AdjustViewOrigin 51614 LD D,0 The amount is a single byte.
This entry point is used by the routines at BuildView and ScrollViewRight.
