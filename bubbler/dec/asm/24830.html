24830: Show Title Menu
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the title menu and runs it until the player presses "0" to start the game. While it waits it plays the title tune, highlights the chosen player option and scrolls a message along the bottom of the screen.
Pressing "A" or "B" chooses a one or two player game. Pressing "1" or "2" steps player 1 or player 2 onto the next control method, which moves the marker of that player down the list of options.
