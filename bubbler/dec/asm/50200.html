50200: Draw Block Shape Three
Used by the routine at DrawBlockShape.
Draws a block whose shape number is 9 to 16. DrawBlockShape has already copied the shape's steps into this routine. The shape number chooses the edges: shapes below 11 use one edge, 11 and 12 leave the edges alone, 13 and 14 use two, and 15 and 16 lengthen the row count by 14 as well. Each column is drawn a row at a time by DrawBlockRow.
The shape number is in A. It chooses how many edge steps this column adds, and which edges are written before a single-row column.
