45166: Draw Items
Used by the routines at RunFrame and RunGame.
Draws every item in the sorted draw list at SortedDrawList, in order. The flag in each item selects what is drawn: 1 is an entity, 2 is one of the extra records (which is first copied into the temporary entity at TemporaryEntity) and 3 is one of the 20-byte records.
For a 20-byte record, the items drawn before it that overlap it are collected into the table at 45491 and then the record is drawn with DrawBlock. An extra record is drawn with DrawEntityItem after its column, row, frame, width and height have been copied into the temporary entity.
