24165: Show Ending
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Used when all five levels have been completed. It prints the congratulation message over the game window, saves the window and clears it, and then scrolls the saved picture back in underneath the message. After a long pause the message flashes, with a clicking sound which rises in pitch, and the window is scrolled away by WipeViewOut.
