43791: Control Player
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Reads the controls and makes the player entity turn, jump and move forward. The controls are a byte with one bit for each direction, as read by ReadControls: bit 0 and bit 1 turn the player, bit 2 makes the player jump if it is on the ground, and bit 3 makes the player move forward. While the player is held (fields 36 and 37 are not both zero) bits 2-4 are ignored. The rate of turning builds up while a turn control is held, and is kept in TurningRate.
