41150: Draw Order Table
The routines for each result of the comparison in SortDrawList. The result is 9 times the relation along the first coordinate plus 3 times the relation along the second plus the relation along the third, where the relation is 0 if the other item is before this one, 1 for an overlap and 2 if it is after.
