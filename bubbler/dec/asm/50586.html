50586: Draw Block Row
Used by the routines at DrawBlockShape, DrawBlockShapeTwo and DrawBlockShapeThree.
Draws one row of a block into the screen buffer. A row outside the view is left undrawn. The tile is the word at 26222 indexed by the patched row count, and FlipBlockData flips it when its top bit differs. Two masked bytes are drawn, then the routine steps up one screen row, for the height of the row.
Input
HL The first coordinate of the position
DE The second coordinate of the position
The position arrives in the alternate registers. A row whose page, column or size is outside the view is left undrawn.
