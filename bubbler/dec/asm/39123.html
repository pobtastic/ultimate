39123: Face Player
Used by the routines at CreateShot, UpdatePickupEntity, UpdateFoamEntity, UpdateWhirlerEntity, UpdateBatEntity and UpdateSaucerEntity.
Chooses the direction in which an entity should head to move towards the player. If the direction (bits 4 and 5 of field 32 of the entity) is already the right one it is left alone, otherwise the new direction is set along with a random value in bits 1-3.
Input
IX The entity
FacePlayer 39123 LD B,0 Clear the direction bits in B.
39125 LD A,(46402) Fetch the first byte of the first coordinate of the player from 46402.
39128 SUB (IX+2) Subtract the first byte of the first coordinate of the entity.
39131 JR NZ,FacePlayer_FirstDone Jump to FacePlayer_FirstDone if they are different.
39133 LD A,(46403) Fetch the second byte of the first coordinate of the player from 46403.
39136 SUB (IX+3) Subtract the second byte of the first coordinate of the entity.
FacePlayer_FirstDone 39139 JR NC,FacePlayer_Second Jump to FacePlayer_Second if the player is not before the entity on the first coordinate.
39141 LD B,16 Set bit 4 of the direction.
FacePlayer_Second 39143 LD A,(46405) Fetch the first byte of the second coordinate of the player from 46405.
39146 SUB (IX+5) Subtract the first byte of the second coordinate of the entity.
39149 JR NZ,FacePlayer_SecondDone Jump to FacePlayer_SecondDone if they are different.
39151 LD A,(46406) Fetch the second byte of the second coordinate of the player from 46406.
39154 SUB (IX+6) Subtract the second byte of the second coordinate of the entity.
FacePlayer_SecondDone 39157 LD A,B Fetch the direction.
39158 JR NC,FacePlayer_Compare Jump to FacePlayer_Compare if the player is not before the entity on the second coordinate.
39160 XOR %00110000 Flip bits 4 and 5 of the direction.
FacePlayer_Compare 39162 LD B,A Keep the direction in B.
39163 LD A,(IX+32) Read field 32 of the entity.
39166 AND %00110000 Use bits 4 and 5.
39168 XOR B Compare them with the new direction.
39169 RET Z Return if the entity is already heading that way.
39170 LD A,(24474) Fetch the low byte of the random seed from RandomNumber_Seed.
39173 AND %00001110 Use bits 1-3.
39175 OR B Add the new direction.
39176 LD (IX+32),A Store it in field 32 of the entity.
39179 RET Return.
