51735: Shift View Left Nibble
Shifts the screen buffer to the left by four pixels, by rotating the nibbles of each row.
ShiftViewLeftNibble 51735 LD B,128 Set the row count to 128.
51737 LD HL,61882 Point HL at the last byte of the screen buffer.
51740 XOR A Clear the nibble shifted in from the right.
ShiftViewLeftNibble_Loop 51741 RLD Shift the row four pixels to the left.
