40122: Update Saucer Entity
Updates an entity of the type 9 which is made from the fourth kind of creature at CreatureTemplates. On odd frames it turns towards the player, and on average once in every 32 frames it fires a new entity of the type 14 from the position of this one, if there is a free entity to use.
Input
IX The entity

40125 LD A,(41693) Fetch the low byte of the frame counter from FrameCounter.
40128 RRA Rotate bit 0 into the carry flag.
40129 CALL C,FacePlayer Call FacePlayer on odd frames to choose the direction towards the player.
40132 LD B,2 Set the size of the step to 2.
40134 CALL TurnEntity Call TurnEntity to turn the entity towards the wanted heading.
40137 LD A,(24474) Fetch the low byte of the seed from RandomNumber_Seed.

40148 LD IX,46514 Point IX at EntitiesEntity_03.
40152 LD B,2 Look at 2 entities.
40154 CALL FindFreeEntity Call FindFreeEntity to find a free entity.

40159 CALL PlayShotSound Call PlayShotSound.
