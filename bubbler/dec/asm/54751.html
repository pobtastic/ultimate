54751: Collect Item
Used by the routine at TestEntityPair.
Deals with an entity touching an entity of the type 15. Nothing happens while the counter in the high nibble of field 22 of the other entity is running, or while its frame (the low five bits of field 1) is below 4. Frames 4 and 5 burst the touching entity (see StartBurst) and free the other entity. Frames of 6 or more are collected if the touching entity is the player: 256 points are added to the score and the other entity starts to disappear. Frame 7 also gives an extra life, which is printed, and plays the rising sweep. Frame 8 gives 2304 more points, and the other frames give 336 more points and fill the first gauge. Purpose of the individual frames is not yet known.
