47728: Draw Level Objects
Used by the routines at BuildView, ScrollViewLeft and ScrollViewRight.
Draws the objects of the level that are near the player. The nine cells around the position of the player are tried in turn, and the objects of the group in each cell are drawn with DrawGroupObjects. There are two entry points, which choose between two versions of the drawing code that is patched at 50136.
