53762: Follow Path
Used by the routine at MoveEntity.
Moves an entity that is following a path from the level data, which is the case when fields 36 and 37 are not both zero. Bits 0-2 of field 37 choose a three-byte step from a table. Bits 6-7 of field 36 can replace that step: 64 lowers the entity and can play the long buzz, 128 moves it back along y, and 192 moves it on along x. The three bytes of a looked-up step are added to x, y and z. A step above 8 finishes the path. The player can be scored and shown the filled cup, and a path position is then copied into the entity before the falling sweep plays.
