51053: Draw Block Bytes
Used by the routines at TestBlockRow_Draw and DrawBlockSection.
Draws the bytes of a row of a block into the screen buffer. A column below 16 is off the view, and a column of 192 is drawn only when bits 1-2 of DrawingFlags are clear. The tile is read through the stack. Those bits clear draw the bytes plain, with 50705; otherwise DrawShiftedRows_Two shifts them.
