38944: Create Shot
Used by the routine at CreateSpawnedEntity.
Makes a shot at the spawn point. The shot is nudged on by three along both axes, given a random heading in the low four bits, and sent off with the moving flag set.
Input
IX The shot
CreateShot 38944 CALL PlayBriefBuzz Play the brief buzz.
38947 LD A,(24475) Fetch a random number.
38950 AND %00001111 Keep a heading from 0 to 15.
38952 LD (IX+32),A Keep that heading aside.
38955 LD A,(IX+3) Nudge the low byte of the x coordinate on by three.
38958 ADD A,3
38960 LD (IX+3),A
38963 LD A,(IX+6) Nudge the low byte of the y coordinate on by three.
38966 ADD A,3
38968 LD (IX+6),A
38971 CALL SetUpShotSprite Set up the shot sprite.
38974 CALL FacePlayer Face the shot towards the player.
38977 LD A,(IX+32) Fetch the heading that was kept aside.
38980 OR %10000000 Mark the shot as moving.
38982 LD (IX+17),A Use that value as the heading.
38985 JP UpdateSpawnPoints_Advance Go on to the next spawn point.
