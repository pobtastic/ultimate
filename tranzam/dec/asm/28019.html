28019: Set Speed
Used by the routines at HandlerCanAccelerate and UpdatePlayerCar.
Stores the new speed, draws the speed gauge and moves the car. The direction is split into the quadrant (the top two bits, held in 24081) and the position in that quadrant (the top five bits of Player_Direction). The table at MovementTable is then used to share out the speed between the Y and X directions; the X part uses the table from the other end.
Input
A Speed
HandlerAccelerate 28019 LD (24077),A Write the new speed to Speed.
