25165: Hide Radar Blip
Used by the routines at OffsetBlipLeft, OffsetBlipDown, LocateRadarBlip and CollisionCup.
Hides a blip by setting its column to 0 then jumps into LocateRadarBlip to set its row to 0 as well.
Input
IX Radar blip record
HideRadarBlip 25165 XOR A Write 0 to the column of the blip record.
25166 LD (IX+2),A
25169 JR LocateRadarBlip_StoreRow Jump to LocateRadarBlip_StoreRow.
