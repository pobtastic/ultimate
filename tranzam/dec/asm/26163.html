26163: Handler: Car
Moves and draws one of the two cars (the entities at Entity_Car_1 and Entity_Car_2), which chase the player. The car is drawn at its position relative to the player, but only when it is closer than 88 map units in both directions. It is then steered towards the player, bounces off objects, and destroys the player if it hits.
Byte Contents
0 State (2), or the sprite ID while the car is being drawn
1 X position on the screen
2 Y position on the screen
3 Colour (0 or 2), or the attribute while the car is drawn
4-5 X position (twice the map position)
6-7 Y position (twice the map position)
8 X speed (bit 7: moving to lower X; bits 0-4: speed)
9 Y speed (bit 7: moving to lower Y; bits 0-4: speed)
10 Flags (bit 0: car is drawn; bit 7: faster car)
11 Sprite ID
Before After
Input
