25992: Update Terrain Dots
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayer.
Moves the dots which are scattered over the play area, to give the impression that the car is moving. Nothing happens unless the car has moved since last time. Each of the 16 dots at TerrainDots is then redrawn by UpdateTerrainDot. The low bytes of the player's X (flipped) and Y positions are saved in PlayerWorldPosition so the dots can be erased from where they were drawn.
