27364: Messaging: Blank Place Name
Used to overwrite the place name on the screen. The first byte is the attribute, followed by the spaces to print.
Blank_Text_Attribute 27364 DEFB 118 Attribute: 118 (bright yellow ink on yellow paper).
Blank_Text 27365 DEFM " "," "+128 Contains 15 spaces, the last with bit 7 set.
