25306: Locate Radar Cup
Used by the routine at UpdateRadar.
Finds a cup to show on the radar. Each of the 8 cups in CupTable is checked in turn: if the map entry still has the cup sprite ID (32) and the cup is less than 12 cells (of 32 map units) from the player horizontally, then it is placed on the radar by LocateRadarBlip. Only the first such cup is used. If there is no cup then the blip is hidden.
