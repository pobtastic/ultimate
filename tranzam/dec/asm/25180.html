25180: Update Radar
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayer.
Updates the radar. A blip is shown by clearing a 2 by 2 block of pixels, and removed again by setting them. The player is always the blip at the centre. The other blips are the two cars (at Entity_Car_1 and Entity_Car_2) and the first cup within range, which are tracked by three four-byte records starting at RadarBlips. The first two bytes of a record hold the column and row that were drawn last time and the next two bytes hold the new column and row, or zero if the target is not on the radar.
