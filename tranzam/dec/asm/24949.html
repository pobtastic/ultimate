24949: Set Day Playarea Attributes
Used by the routines at GameInitialisation, GameOver and HandlerCup.
Selects day time, using bright black on yellow (INK:BLACK, PAPER:YELLOW (BRIGHT)) for the play area background.
SetDayAttributes 24949 XOR A Write 0 (day time) to Flag_DayNight.
