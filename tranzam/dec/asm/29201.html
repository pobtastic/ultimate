29201: Reset Screen Buffer
Used by the routine at ResetAttributes.
Clears the display file by filling it with 0. The screen attributes are left as they were.
ResetScreen 29201 LD HL,16384 HL=16384 (screen buffer).
29204 LD B,88 B=88 (i.e. finish once we reach the start of the attribute buffer).
29206 LD C,0 C=0 (value to write).
