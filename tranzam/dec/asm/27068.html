27068: Draw HUD Panel
Used by the routine at CreateWindow.
Draws the sidebar (the leftmost 8 columns of the screen) from the tile layout at SidebarLayoutData and the attributes at SidebarAttributes. Each tile is printed with PrintScreen while the character set pointer refers to the tiles at UDG_Tiles.
