24068: Player X Position
The position of the player's car along the map. It is a 24-bit number made up of a fraction of a map unit and then the position in map units. The fraction is needed because the car moves by less than one map unit each frame (see HandlerAccelerate). The map is 6144 map units wide.
PlayerMapPosition_Fraction 24068 DEFB 44 Fraction of a map unit.
PlayerMapPosition 24069 DEFW 3632 Position in map units.
