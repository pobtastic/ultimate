27870: Display Miles/ Time
Used by the routines at CreateWindow and HandlerPlayer.
Prints the miles travelled (six digits) and the time (one digit for the hours, two for the minutes and two for the seconds) in the sidebar.
Before After
DisplayMiles 27870 LD HL,16481 Set the screen position of the miles, 16481.
27873 LD DE,24109 Point DE at the miles (Miles).
27876 LD B,3 Set the byte counter to 3 (the miles are 3 bytes, so 6 digits).
27878 CALL PrintScore_Loop Print the miles using PrintScore_Loop.
27881 LD HL,16417 Set the screen position of the time, 16417.
This entry point is used by the routine at HandlerCup.
DisplayTime_Minutes 27884 LD DE,24113 Point DE at the time (Time_1).
27887 LD B,2 Set the byte counter to 2 (the single hours digit and the two minutes digits).
27889 CALL PrintScore_SecondDigit Call PrintScore_SecondDigit to skip the first digit of the hours byte.
27892 LD DE,24115 Point DE at the seconds (Time_3).
This entry point is used by the routine at PrintBestTime.
DisplayTime_Seconds 27895 INC HL Move on one column to leave a gap before the seconds.
27896 LD B,1 Set the byte counter to 1 (the two seconds digits).
27898 JP PrintScore_Loop Jump to PrintScore_Loop to print the seconds.
