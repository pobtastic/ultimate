607D: Display Congratulations Messaging
Used by the routine at HandlerCup.
Prints the four lines of the message shown when all eight cups have been collected. It sets up the shadow registers, as GameMenu does, and then reuses the printing loop at GameMenu_Loop.
Before After
DisplayCongratsMessaging 607D LD A,$50 Write the X position of the message text, 50 to Temp_Text_Pos_X.
607F LD ($5E48),A
6082 LD DE,$6091 Point DE at the table of line colours (CongratsMessaging_Attributes).
6085 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
6086 LD HL,$6095 Point HL' at the table of line Y positions (CongratsMessaging_Position).
6089 LD DE,$6099 Point DE' at the congratulations text (Message_Congrats).
608C LD B,$04 Set the line counter to 04 one for each line of the message.
608E JP GameMenu_Loop Jump to GameMenu_Loop to print the lines of the message.
