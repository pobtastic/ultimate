7170: Mask Sprite
Used by the routines at DisplayEntity, RemoveEntity, DrawEntity, DrawTwoBytes and ActorUpdateSizeNext.
Erases the old sprite and draws the new sprite, a line at a time going up the screen. One line of the old sprite is erased by clearing its bits from the screen, then one line of the new sprite is drawn, and so on until both are done. The old sprite is held in the normal registers and the new sprite in the shadow registers. In each set C holds the number of lines left, DE the sprite data and HL the screen address.
