6588: Update Terrain Dots
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayer.
Moves the dots which are scattered over the play area, to give the impression that the car is moving. Nothing happens unless the car has moved since last time. Each of the 10 dots at TerrainDots is then redrawn by UpdateTerrainDot. The low bytes of the player's X (flipped) and Y positions are saved in PlayerWorldPosition so the dots can be erased from where they were drawn.
Before After
UpdateTerrainDots 6588 LD HL,($5E34) Fetch the player position used last time (PlayerWorldPosition).
658B LD A,($5E05) Fetch the low byte of the player's X position on the map (PlayerMapPosition).
658E CPL Flip all the bits, as the dots move the opposite way to the car.
658F LD E,A Keep the flipped X position in E.
6590 LD A,($5E08) Fetch the low byte of the player's Y position on the map (PlayerMapPositionY) and keep it in D.
6593 LD D,A
Check if the player has moved.
6594 AND A Reset the carry flag.
6595 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
6596 SBC HL,DE Compare the new player position with the position used last time.
6598 POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
6599 RET Z Return if the player has not moved.
659A PUSH DE Stash the new player position on the stack.
659B LD IX,$65FD Point IX at the first terrain dot (TerrainDots).
659F LD B,$10 Set the dot counter to 10.
UpdateTerrainDots_Loop 65A1 PUSH BC Stash the dot counter on the stack.
65A2 CALL UpdateTerrainDot Update the dot using UpdateTerrainDot.
65A5 INC IX Point IX at the next dot.
65A7 INC IX
65A9 POP BC Restore the dot counter from the stack.
65AA DJNZ UpdateTerrainDots_Loop Decrease the dot counter by one and loop back to UpdateTerrainDots_Loop until all 10 dots have been updated.
65AC POP HL Restore the new player position from the stack.
65AD LD ($5E34),HL Write HL to PlayerWorldPosition.
65B0 RET Return.
