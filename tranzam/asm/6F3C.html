6F3C: Place Object
Used by the routine at DrawObjects.
Works out where an object found by DrawObjects is on the screen and draws it. The object's slot in the table at Object_1_State is found (or a free slot is used), the old sprite is erased, the new one is drawn and coloured, and then the car is checked to see if it has hit the object. The car is at the centre of the play area, so the screen position of the object is its distance from the car (Object_Distance_X and Object_Distance_Y) added to, or subtracted from, the position of the car (98 and 68).
Before After
Work out the Y position. Return if the object is off the top or bottom of the screen.
