6B2B: Scale Value
Used by the routine at CollisionCup.
Converts a map position held in a single byte into map units, by shifting D right through E so that DE is D * 0100 shifted right by a fixed number of bits. ScaleValue multiplies by 40 ScaleValue_DivideByEight multiplies by 20 (used for the X position of a map point) and ScaleValue_DivideBySixteen multiplies by 10 (used for the Y position of a map point).
Input
D Value to scale
Output
DE Scaled value
ScaleValue 6B2B PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
6B2C LD B,$02 Set the shift counter to 02.
6B2E JR ScaleValue_Shift Jump to ScaleValue_Shift.
This entry point is used by the routines at CollisionCup, DrawObjects, FindFirstObject and UpdateCurrentObject.
Multiply by 20.
ScaleValue_DivideByEight 6B30 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
6B31 LD B,$03 Set the shift counter to 03.
ScaleValue_Shift 6B33 LD E,$00 Clear the low byte of the scaled value.
ScaleValue_Loop 6B35 SRL D Shift D right, moving bit 0 into the carry flag.
6B37 RR E Rotate the carry flag into E.
6B39 DJNZ ScaleValue_Loop Decrease the shift counter by one and loop back to ScaleValue_Loop until all of the shifts have been done.
6B3B POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
6B3C RET Return.
