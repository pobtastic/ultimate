5E00: Best Time
The best time so far, as three BCD bytes. The hours are a single digit (in the low nibble). See Time_1 for the time of the current game.
BestTime_1 5E00 DEFB $00 Hours.
BestTime_2 5E01 DEFB $06 Minutes.
BestTime_3 5E02 DEFB $00 Seconds.
