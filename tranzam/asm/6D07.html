6D07: Increase Speed
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerCar.
Adds 04 to the speed, unless that would take it to the top speed of C0 or more, in which case the speed is left as it is.
HandlerCanAccelerate 6D07 LD A,($5E0D) Fetch the speed of the car (Speed).
6D0A ADD A,$04 Add 04 to the speed.
6D0C CP $C0 Jump to HandlerAccelerate_Gauge if the speed has reached the top speed (C0 or more).
6D0E JR NC,HandlerAccelerate_Gauge
6D10 JR HandlerAccelerate Jump to HandlerAccelerate to write the new speed.
