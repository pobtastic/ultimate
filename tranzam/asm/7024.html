7024: Next Object Slot
Used by the routine at PlaceObject.
Moves on to the next slot when looking for a free slot for an object. If all of the slots are used then the object is not drawn.
NextObjectSlot 7024 INC HL Increment HL by three.
7025 INC HL
7026 INC HL
7027 DJNZ PlaceObject_FindSlot Decrease the slot counter by one and loop back to PlaceObject_FindSlot until all of the slots have been checked.
7029 RET Return.
