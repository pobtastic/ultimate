5E68: Radar Blips
The three blips on the radar (see UpdateRadar): the first car, the second car and the nearest cup. Each is four bytes: the column and row where the blip is drawn, and then the column and row where it will be drawn next. A column and row of 00 and 00 mean that there is no blip.
RadarBlips 5E68 DEFB $20,$20,$20,$20
5E6C DEFB $20,$20,$20,$20
5E70 DEFB $02,$00,$78,$5C
