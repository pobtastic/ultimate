624D: Hide Radar Blip
Used by the routines at OffsetBlipLeft, OffsetBlipDown, LocateRadarBlip and CollisionCup.
Hides a blip by setting its column to 00 then jumps into LocateRadarBlip to set its row to 00 as well.
Input
IX Radar blip record
HideRadarBlip 624D XOR A Write 00 to the column of the blip record.
624E LD (IX+$02),A
6251 JR LocateRadarBlip_StoreRow Jump to LocateRadarBlip_StoreRow.
