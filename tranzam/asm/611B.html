611B: Sounds: Cup/ Refuel
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayer.
Plays the next step of the sound made when a cup is collected or the car is refuelled. This is called every frame but only makes a sound on every other frame. The step counter is held at SoundCounter. For the refuel sound it counts down from 07 and bit 7 is clear; each step has a higher pitch than the one before. For the cup sound it starts at 87 (bit 7 set) and each step has a lower pitch than the one before. Each step plays 20 square waves using PlaySquareWave, and there are six of them. The cup sound: The refuel sound:
SoundsCupRefuel 611B LD A,($5E29) Fetch the game timer from GameTimer.
611E AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
6120 RET NZ Return on every other frame.
6121 LD HL,$5E46 Point HL at the step counter (SoundCounter).
6124 LD A,(HL) Fetch the step counter.
6125 BIT 7,A Jump to SoundsCup if bit 7 is set, as that is the cup sound.
6127 JR NZ,SoundsCup
6129 AND A Return if the step counter is zero, as there is no sound to play.
612A RET Z
612B DEC A Decrease the step counter by one.
612C LD (HL),A Write the new step counter back to SoundCounter.
612D RET Z Return if the step counter has reached zero, as the sound is over.
Work out the period of the waves from the step counter.
SoundsRefuel 612E RLCA Multiply the step counter by 04 by rotating it left twice.
612F RLCA
6130 AND %00011100 Keep only bits 2-4.
6132 OR %00100000 Set bit 5.
6134 LD D,A Set the period of the waves.
6135 LD C,$20 Set the wave counter to 20.
SoundsCupRefuel_Loop 6137 CALL PlaySquareWave Play a square wave using PlaySquareWave.
613A DEC C Decrease the wave counter by one and loop back to SoundsCupRefuel_Loop until all 20 waves have been played.
613B JR NZ,SoundsCupRefuel_Loop
613D RET Return.
The cup sound keeps bit 7 set in the step counter, and inverts the step so that the pitch falls rather than rises.
SoundsCup 613E AND %01111111 Keep only the step counter (bits 0-6).
6140 RET Z Return if the step counter is zero, as the sound is over.
6141 DEC A Decrease the step counter by one.
6142 OR %10000000 Set bit 7 so that the sound stays the cup sound.
6144 LD (HL),A Write the new step counter back to SoundCounter.
6145 AND %01111111 Keep only the step counter (bits 0-6).
6147 RET Z Return if the step counter has reached zero.
6148 CPL Flip the bits, so that the pitch falls with each step.
6149 JR SoundsRefuel Jump to SoundsRefuel.
