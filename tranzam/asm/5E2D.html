5E2D: Miles
The miles travelled, as six BCD digits (see UpdatePlayerCar and DisplayMiles).
Miles 5E2D DEFB $00 Digits 1 and 2.
5E2E DEFB $00 Digits 3 and 4. Each time this changes the player is awarded points.
5E2F DEFB $06 Digits 5 and 6.
