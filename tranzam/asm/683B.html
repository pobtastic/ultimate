683B: Car Direction Table
The angle of travel of a car, which is 00 when moving vertically and 04 when moving horizontally. Each row is for a different value of bits 2-4 of the Y speed and each column is for a different value of bits 2-4 of the X speed. The first entry is FF as the car is not moving.
