6F10: Calculate Screen Address
Used by the routines at MenuWriteText, HandlerCup, DisplayBestTime, DisplayPlaceName, PrintStringColour, FlashPlayerMarker, DisplayNightDriver, PlotPixels, PrepareOldSprite and PrepareActorSprite.
Converts pixel co-ordinates into the matching address in the screen buffer.
Input
HL Pixel co-ordinates (H is the Y position, counting down from the top of the screen, and L is the X position)
Output
HL Screen buffer address
