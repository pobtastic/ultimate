6031: Messaging: Score/ Hi-Score Text
The line of text printed along the top of the play area by 68F2. The first byte is the colour attribute (INK:BLACK, PAPER:YELLOW (BRIGHT)). The score is printed over the first run of spaces by Score_1UP and the high score over the last run by Score_HI.
