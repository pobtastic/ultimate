7101: Display Entity
Used by the routines at HandlerExplosion, HandlerCar and PlaceObject.
Erases the sprite of an actor from where it was last drawn (the old state held in the actor buffer at Actor_Position_X) and draws it at its new position. Both sprites are handled a line at a time by MaskSprite, but if the old and the new Y positions are different then the lines of one sprite that stick out below the other are handled first.
Before After
Input
IX Actor entity
DisplayEntity 7101 LD A,($5E22) Fetch the sprite ID of the old sprite (Actor_SpriteID).
7104 AND A Jump to DrawEntity if there is no old sprite, as there is nothing to erase.
7105 JP Z,DrawEntity
7108 CALL PrepareActorSprite Call PrepareActorSprite to prepare the new sprite.
710B EXX Switch to the shadow registers (to keep the new sprite).
710C CALL PrepareOldSprite Call PrepareOldSprite to prepare the old sprite.
710F LD A,($5E21) A=Actor_Position_Y (the old Y position).
7112 SUB (IX+$02) Subtract the new Y position.
7115 JP Z,ActorUpdateSize Jump to ActorUpdateSize if the old and new Y positions are the same.
7118 JP M,DisplayEntity_NewLower Jump to DisplayEntity_NewLower if the new sprite is lower down than the old sprite.
711B LD C,A C=the number of lines that the old sprite sticks out below the new sprite.
711C LD A,($5E25) A=CurrentSpriteHeight (the number of lines in the old sprite).
711F CP C Jump to ActorUpdateSize if the old sprite has fewer lines than this.
7120 JP C,ActorUpdateSize
7123 SUB C Subtract the lines that stick out.
7124 LD ($5E25),A Write the number of lines left to CurrentSpriteHeight.
7127 JP MaskSprite Jump to MaskSprite to erase the lines that stick out.
The new sprite is lower down, so it has some lines to draw before any lines are erased.

712B NEG Make the result positive.
712D LD C,A C=the number of lines that the new sprite sticks out below the old sprite.
712E LD A,($5E26) A=HeightLines (the number of lines in the new sprite).
7131 CP C Jump to ActorUpdateSizeFlipReg if the new sprite has fewer lines than this.
7132 JP C,ActorUpdateSizeFlipReg
7135 SUB C Subtract the lines that stick out.
7136 JP ActorUpdateHeightAndMask Jump to ActorUpdateHeightAndMask to store the number of lines left and to draw the lines that stick out.
