Map

The map is 1800 by 0C00 units, listed at MapPoints as 0197 points. Each point is an obstacle, a cup or a place. Places have bit 6 set and take their names from PlaceNames; they are the fuel stops from Los Angeles in the west to New York in the east. Cups have sprite ID 20. Their X is fixed, and RandomiseCups picks a new Y from 20 to 9F each round; the gold cups sit in the middle of that band, and the dotted line is the range. The red car is the start written to PlayerMapPosition and PlayerMapPositionY. Y counts up from the south, as on the sidebar drawn by PlotMapPoints. The picture uses the daytime colours from DayObjectAttributes on the yellow play area. Drag to move, and use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom.
