6FBD: Move Robbie Down
Used by the routine at Robbie_CarryingCan.
Moves Robbie, and the can, down two pixels unless he is at the bottom.
Input
IX Robbie object
Robbie_CarryingCan_Down 6FBD LD A,(IX+$02) A=the Y position of Robbie.
6FC0 ADD A,$02 Add 02.
6FC2 CP $B8 Jump to Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire if Robbie is at the bottom.
6FC4 JP NC,Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire
6FC7 JR Robbie_CarryingCan_StoreY Jump to Robbie_CarryingCan_StoreY.
