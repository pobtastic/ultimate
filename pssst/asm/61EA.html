61EA: Select Game
Used by the routine at GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Shows the game selection menu and reads the keys "1" to "5" until the player starts the game by pressing "5". Keys "1" and "2" choose the number of players, keys "3" and "4" choose the control method, and the chosen options are shown by flashing the menu lines.
Before After pressing "2" and "4" (the chosen lines flash)
