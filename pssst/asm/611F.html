611F: Show Game Over For Active Player
Used by the routine at PlayerTurnEnds.
Shows the "GAME OVER" message for whichever player is currently active.
GameOver_Controller 611F LD A,($5E1E) Jump to GameOver_1UP if the current player (Current_Player) is 00 (1UP).
6122 AND A
6123 JR Z,GameOver_1UP
6125 JR GameOver_2UP Else, jump to GameOver_2UP.
