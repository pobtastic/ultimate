7249: Collect Bonus Item
Used by the routine at UpdateBonusItem.
Empties the cubby hole and removes the bonus item, and adds 1000 points (as BCD) to the score. Then it plays a tone, and goes back to the start of the main loop at MainLoop_NewPass.
Input
HL The cubby hole
IX The bonus item
