65BA: Handle Leech Weapon Hit
Used by the routine at UpdateLeech.
A weapon has hit the leech. A streamer kills it, the repellent spray stuns it and a smoke bomb has no effect.
Input
A State of the weapon
IX Leech object
LeechWeaponHit 65BA AND %00111111 Keep only bits 0-5 (the type of the weapon).
65BC CP $12 Jump to KillLeech if the weapon is a streamer.
65BE JR Z,KillLeech
65C0 CP $08 Jump to UpdateLeech_NoWeaponHit if it is not the repellent spray.
65C2 JP NZ,UpdateLeech_NoWeaponHit
65C5 CALL StunBug Call StunBug to stun the leech.
65C8 JP UpdateLeech_NoWeaponHit Jump to UpdateLeech_NoWeaponHit.
