611B: Show Game Over For 2UP
Used by the routines at GameOver_Controller and GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Sets up the "GAME OVER PLAYER 2" message and then shows it using GameOver_Write.
Before After
Set up altering the "GAME OVER PLAYER X" message for 2UP.
GameOver_2UP 611B LD A,"2"+$80 A="2" + 80 (escape character).
611D JR GameOver_Write Jump to GameOver_Write.
