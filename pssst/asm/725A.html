725A: Place Bonus Item Right
Used by the routine at SpawnBonusItem.
The cubby hole is on the right, so the bonus item is put at the right hand side.
Input
A The cubby hole number
IX The bonus item
SpawnBonusItem_Right 725A LD (IX+$01),$E8 Write E8 to the X position of the bonus item.
725E SUB $05 Subtract 05 to give the row number.
