60FD: Show Game Over For 1UP
Used by the routines at GameOver_Controller and GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Shows "GAME OVER PLAYER 1" or "GAME OVER PLAYER 2" on a blank game window and pauses for a couple of seconds. The player number is written over the last character of the message at GameOver_Text.
Before After
Set up altering the "GAME OVER PLAYER X" message for 1UP.
GameOver_1UP 60FD LD A,"1"+$80 A="1" + 80 (escape character).
This entry point is used by the routine at GameOver_2UP.
Print the messaging and pause to show it for a period of time.
GameOver_Write 60FF LD ($6175),A Write ASCII player number to 6175.

This entry point is used by the routine at LevelComplete.
PauseForCount 6110 LD HL,$0000 HL=00 (the start of a countdown of 10000 steps).
GameOver_Pause 6113 DEC HL Decrease the countdown by one.
6114 LD A,H Loop back to GameOver_Pause until the countdown has reached zero.
6115 OR L
6116 JR NZ,GameOver_Pause
