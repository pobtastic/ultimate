6BC7: Fire Smoke Bomb Right
Used by the routine at FireWeapon.
Fires a smoke bomb to the right of Robbie, if he is not too close to the right edge of the play area.
Input
A The X position of Robbie
HL The weapon object
FireSmokeBomb_Right 6BC7 CP $C0 Return if Robbie is too close to the right edge.
6BC9 RET NC
6BCA ADD A,$18 Add 18 to the X position (so it starts to the right of Robbie).
6BCC LD (HL),$04 Write 04 (a smoke bomb moving right) to the state of the weapon.
6BCE JR FireWeapon_SmokeColour Jump to FireWeapon_SmokeColour.
