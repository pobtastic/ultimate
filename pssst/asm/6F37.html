6F37: Check Vertical Movement Facing Right
Used by the routine at Robbie_CarryingCan.
Robbie is carrying a can and is facing right. He can only move up or down when he is away from the cubby hole columns.
Input
A The X position of Robbie
Robbie_CarryingCan_FacingRight 6F37 CP $18 Jump to Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire if Robbie is too far to the left to move vertically.
6F39 JR C,Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire
6F3B CP $CC Jump to Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire if Robbie is too far to the right to move vertically.
6F3D JR NC,Robbie_CarryingCan_Fire
6F3F JR Robbie_CarryingCan_VerticalInput Jump to Robbie_CarryingCan_VerticalInput.
