708F: Update High Score
Used by the routine at GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Finds the higher of the scores of the two players and, if that is higher than the high score at High_Score, makes it the new high score. The scores are stored as three BCD bytes with the most significant byte first.
UpdateHighScore 708F LD HL,($5E05) HL=the first two bytes of the score of 1UP.
7092 LD DE,($5E08) DE=the first two bytes of the score of 2UP.
7096 LD A,L Swap H and L so that the most significant byte is in H.
7097 LD L,H
7098 LD H,A
7099 LD A,E Swap D and E so that the most significant byte is in D.
709A LD E,D
709B LD D,A
709C AND A Compare the first two bytes of the scores.
709D SBC HL,DE
709F JR C,UpdateHighScore_2UP Jump to UpdateHighScore_2UP if 2UP has the higher score.
70A1 JR NZ,UpdateHighScore_1UP Jump to UpdateHighScore_1UP if 1UP has the higher score.
70A3 LD A,($5E07) E=the last byte of the score of 1UP.
70A6 LD E,A
70A7 LD A,($5E0A) A=the last byte of the score of 2UP.
70AA CP E Compare the last bytes of the scores.
70AB JR C,UpdateHighScore_1UP Jump to UpdateHighScore_1UP if 1UP has the higher score.
UpdateHighScore_2UP 70AD LD HL,$5E08 HL=2UP_Score_1 (the score of 2UP).
UpdateHighScore_Compare 70B0 PUSH HL Stash the pointer to the score on the stack.
70B1 LD DE,$5E00 DE=High_Score (the high score).
70B4 LD B,$03 Set the byte counter to 03.
UpdateHighScore_Loop 70B6 LD A,(DE) Jump to UpdateHighScore_Copy if the high score is lower than the score.
70B7 CP (HL)
70B8 JR C,UpdateHighScore_Copy
70BA JR NZ,UpdateHighScore_Done Jump to UpdateHighScore_Done if the high score is higher than the score.
70BC INC HL Move onto the next byte of each.
70BD INC DE
70BE DJNZ UpdateHighScore_Loop Decrease the byte counter by one and loop back to UpdateHighScore_Loop until all three bytes have been compared.
UpdateHighScore_Done 70C0 POP HL Restore the pointer from the stack.
70C1 RET Return.
UpdateHighScore_Copy 70C2 POP HL Restore the pointer from the stack.
70C3 LD DE,$5E00 DE=High_Score (the high score).
70C6 LD BC,$0003 BC=03.
70C9 LDIR Copy the score to the high score.
70CB RET Return.
UpdateHighScore_1UP 70CC LD HL,$5E05 HL=1UP_Score_1 (the score of 1UP).
70CF JR UpdateHighScore_Compare Jump to UpdateHighScore_Compare.
