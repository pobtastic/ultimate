72CC: Create Bug Right
Used by the routine at CreateBug.
The cubby hole is on the right, so the bug is put at the right hand side.
Input
A The cubby hole number
IX The bug
CreateBug_Right 72CC LD (IX+$01),$E0 Write E0 to the X position of the bug.
72D0 SUB $05 Subtract 05 to give the row number.
72D2 JR CreateBug_Row Jump to CreateBug_Row.
