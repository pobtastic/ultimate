670E: Colour Open Flower
Used by the routine at UpdateBloomingFlower.
Writes the attributes of the open flower into the attribute buffer, using the table at FlowerAttributes, which is nine cells wide and seven cells high. The colour of the centre of the flower changes from yellow to magenta, unless the level is a multiple of four (and not the first level), which are the levels that award an extra life.

IX Flower object
