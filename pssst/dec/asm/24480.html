24480: Reset Cubby Holes
Used by the routines at LevelNew and ResetPlayerData.
Copies the default contents of the ten cubby holes (five down each side of the play area) from DefaultState_CubbyHoles to CubbyHoles, and cancels any bonus item object at State_BonusItem.
