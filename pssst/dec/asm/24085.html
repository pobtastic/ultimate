24085: Leaf Growth Flags
Used to grow the leaves of the flower (see GrowLeaf and UpdateFlower).
  • Bit 6 is flipped each time a leaf is added, so that the leaves alternate between the right and the left of the flower.
  • Bit 0 is set when a leaf is added and is reset when the flower moves, to stop another leaf being added until then.
