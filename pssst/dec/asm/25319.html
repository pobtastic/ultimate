25319: Write Menu Line
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Prints one line of the game selection menu. This is similar to PrintStringColour, but the attribute comes from Current_MenuAttribute and the text has no attribute byte. It is called with the shadow registers selected, so that HL' and DE' hold the position and the text.

After
