29118: Spawn Bonus Item
Used by the routines at SpawnBug and CreateBug.
Updates the bonus item if there is one. Otherwise, it puts a new bonus item into a cubby hole, with a chance of one in two hundred and fifty-six. The hole is chosen using the game timer, from the first ten, and has to be empty. The hole is marked with 255 and the type of the bonus item is a random one of 11 12 or 13 (with 11 more likely). It goes back to the start of the main loop at MainLoop_NewPass.
