24384: Reset Flower And Leaves
Used by the routines at LevelNew and ResetPlayerData.
Restores the flower from its default state at DefaultState_Flower and sets the four leaf objects inactive.
ResetFlowerLeaf 24384 LD HL,24358 Copy 8 bytes of data from DefaultState_Flower to State_Flower.
24387 LD DE,24204
24390 LD BC,8
24393 LDIR
24395 LD HL,24212 HL=Leaf_1_State (the first leaf object).
24398 LD B,4 Set the leaf counter to 4.
24400 JP SetObjectInactive Jump to SetObjectInactive to set all four leaf objects inactive.
