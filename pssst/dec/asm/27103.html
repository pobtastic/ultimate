27103: Fire Streamer Right
Used by the routine at FireStreamer.
Fires a streamer to the right of Robbie, if he is not too close to the right edge of the play area.
Input
A The X position of Robbie
HL The weapon object
FireStreamer_Right 27103 CP 184 Return if Robbie is too close to the right edge.
27105 RET NC
27106 ADD A,24 Add 24 to the X position (so it starts to the right of Robbie).
27108 LD (HL),18 Write 18 (a streamer moving right) to the state of the weapon.
27110 JR FireStreamer_Colour Jump to FireStreamer_Colour.
