28815: Update High Score
Used by the routine at GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Finds the higher of the scores of the two players and, if that is higher than the high score at High_Score, makes it the new high score. The scores are stored as three BCD bytes with the most significant byte first.

28818 LD DE,(24072) DE=the first two bytes of the score of 2UP.
28822 LD A,L Swap H and L so that the most significant byte is in H.
28823 LD L,H
28824 LD H,A
28825 LD A,E Swap D and E so that the most significant byte is in D.

28835 LD A,(24071) E=the last byte of the score of 1UP.
28838 LD E,A

28843 JR C,UpdateHighScore_1UP Jump to UpdateHighScore_1UP if 1UP has the higher score.

UpdateHighScore_Compare 28848 PUSH HL Stash the pointer to the score on the stack.
