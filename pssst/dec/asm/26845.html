26845: Calculate Midges Movement
Used by the routine at UpdateMidges.
Prepares for the movement of the midges along one axis: a 16-bit value which has the position in H and the fraction of a pixel in L, and a distance to move which is 32 times the speed (so the distance is up to 480 which is just under two pixels).
Input
B The fraction of a pixel (in the high nibble)
C The speed
H The position
Output
DE The distance to move
HL The position and the fraction
