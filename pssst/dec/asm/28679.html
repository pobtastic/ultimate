28679: Put Can In Cubby Hole
Used by the routine at Robbie_CarryingCan_CubbyColumn.
Robbie puts the spray can into the cubby hole in HL, and becomes Robbie empty-handed. The can is moved to the cubby hole and, with IX+12 set to 1 is then made inactive by UpdateSprayCan.
Input
HL The cubby hole
