27591: Fire Smoke Bomb Right
Used by the routine at FireWeapon.
Fires a smoke bomb to the right of Robbie, if he is not too close to the right edge of the play area.
Input
A The X position of Robbie
HL The weapon object
FireSmokeBomb_Right 27591 CP 192 Return if Robbie is too close to the right edge.
27593 RET NC
27594 ADD A,24 Add 24 to the X position (so it starts to the right of Robbie).
27596 LD (HL),4 Write 4 (a smoke bomb moving right) to the state of the weapon.
27598 JR FireWeapon_SmokeColour Jump to FireWeapon_SmokeColour.
