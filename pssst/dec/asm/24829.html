24829: Show Game Over For 1UP
Used by the routines at GameOver_Controller and GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Shows "GAME OVER PLAYER 1" or "GAME OVER PLAYER 2" on a blank game window and pauses for a couple of seconds. The player number is written over the last character of the message at GameOver_Text.
