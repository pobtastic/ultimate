28465: Draw Object
Used by the routines at UpdateLeech, StartEatingFlower, UpdateMidges, UpdateRepellentSpray, UpdateSlug, UpdateSmokeBomb and GamePlayEntry.
Erases the sprite of an object from its previous position, draws it at the new position and then colours it. This is the end of the update routines for most of the objects.
Input
IX Object
DrawObject 28465 CALL DisplayEntity Erase the sprite at the previous position and draw it at the new position using DisplayEntity.
28468 JP ColouriseSprite Jump to ColouriseSprite to colour the sprite.
