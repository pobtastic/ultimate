29753: Reset Screen Buffer
Used by the routine at CreateWindow.
Clears the screen buffer to zero, but leaves the attribute buffer alone.
ResetScreen 29753 LD HL,16384 HL=16384 (the start of the screen buffer).
29756 LD B,88 B=88 (i.e. finish once we reach the start of the attribute buffer at 22528).
29758 LD C,0 C=0 (value to write).
