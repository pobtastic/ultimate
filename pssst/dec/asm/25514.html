25514: Check Bug Leaf Collision
Used by the routine at UpdateBugCommon.
Checks whether the bug being updated (IX) is touching any of the four leaf objects. If it is on top of an active leaf, the leaf is marked as being eaten and it returns straight away, so only one leaf is eaten at a time.
LeafCollision 25514 LD HL,24212 HL=Leaf_1_State (the first leaf object).
25517 LD B,4 Set the leaf counter to 4.
25519 LD DE,8 DE=8 (the size of an object, used to move onto the next leaf).
LeafCollision_Loop 25522 PUSH HL Stash the address of the leaf on the stack.
25523 LD A,(HL) If the current leaf is not active, jump to LeafCollision_Next.
25524 AND A
25525 JR Z,LeafCollision_Next
Is the leaf within 9 pixels of the bug horizontally?
25527 INC HL A=the X position of the leaf.
25528 LD A,(HL)
25529 SUB (IX+1) Subtract the X position of the bug.
25532 JP P,LeafCollision_Test_X Make the horizontal distance positive.
25535 NEG
LeafCollision_Test_X 25537 CP 10 Jump to LeafCollision_Next if the leaf is 10 or more pixels away.
25539 JR NC,LeafCollision_Next
Is the leaf within 9 pixels of the bug vertically?
25541 INC HL A=the Y position of the leaf.
25542 LD A,(HL)
25543 SUB (IX+2) Subtract the Y position of the bug.
25546 JP P,LeafCollision_Test_Y Make the vertical distance positive.
25549 NEG
LeafCollision_Test_Y 25551 CP 10 Jump to LeafCollision_Next if the leaf is 10 or more pixels away.
25553 JR NC,LeafCollision_Next
The bug is on the leaf, so the leaf starts to shrink away.
25555 INC HL Skip the colour attribute to point to the leaf animating flag.
25556 INC HL
25557 LD A,1 Write 1 to the animating flag and the "being eaten" flag of the leaf (see UpdateLeaf).
25559 LD (HL),A
25560 INC HL
25561 LD (HL),A
25562 POP HL Restore the address of the leaf from the stack.
25563 RET Return.
Move onto the next leaf object.
LeafCollision_Next 25564 POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
25565 ADD HL,DE Add the object size to move onto the next leaf.
25566 DJNZ LeafCollision_Loop Decrease the leaf counter by one and loop back to LeafCollision_Loop until all four leaves have been checked.
25568 RET Return.
