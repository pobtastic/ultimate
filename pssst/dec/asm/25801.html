25801: Play Smoke Sound
Used by the routine at CreateWeapon.
Plays 8 square waves of falling pitch.
PlaySmokeSound 25801 LD C,8 Set the wave counter in C to 8.
PlaySmokeSound_Loop 25803 LD A,C Fetch the wave counter.
25804 CPL Invert all of the bits in A.
25805 RLCA Rotate A left once.
25806 LD D,A Set the duration of the wave in D.
25807 CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave.
25810 DEC C Decrease the wave counter by one.
25811 JR NZ,PlaySmokeSound_Loop Loop back to PlaySmokeSound_Loop until all of the waves have been played.
25813 RET Return.
