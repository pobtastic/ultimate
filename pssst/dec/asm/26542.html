26542: Update Midges
Updates a swarm of midges. If the midges touch Robbie then both explode. A repellent spray kills them (worth 80 points), a smoke bomb stuns them, and a streamer has no effect. When they reach the flower they start to eat it.
Otherwise they fly towards a target (Robbie, or the flower if that is closer) and use a speed and a direction in each axis, so that they speed up when flying towards the target and slow down, stop and turn round when flying away from it. IX+5 holds the horizontal motion and IX+6 holds the vertical motion. In each of them the low nibble is the speed (0 to 15) and the high nibble is the fraction of a pixel left over from the last move (see CalculateMidgesMovement).
Input
IX Midges object
The object fields used are:
Field Use
+0 bit 6 Moving left (the midges move right if this is not set)
+0 bit 7 Moving down (the midges move up if this is not set)
+4 bit 2 Stunned
+4 bit 3 Eating the flower
+5 Horizontal motion (or the stun timer while stunned)
+6 Vertical motion
UpdateMidges 26542 CALL UpdateBugCommon Do the common bug checks using UpdateBugCommon.
26545 DEC E Decrease the collision result in E by one (zero means that the midges are touching Robbie).
26546 JP Z,ExplodeRobbie Jump to ExplodeRobbie if the midges are touching Robbie.
26549 CALL CheckWeaponHit Call CheckWeaponHit to check whether a weapon has hit the midges.
26552 DEC E Decrease the collision result in E by one (zero means that a weapon has hit the midges).
26553 JR NZ,UpdateMidges_NoWeaponHit Jump to UpdateMidges_NoWeaponHit if there was no hit.
26555 LD A,C A=the state of the weapon, and jump to MidgesWeaponHit if the weapon is active.
26556 AND A
26557 JP NZ,MidgesWeaponHit
This entry point is used by the routine at MidgesWeaponHit.
UpdateMidges_NoWeaponHit 26560 BIT 3,(IX+4) Jump to EatFlower if the midges are eating the flower.
26564 JP NZ,EatFlower
26567 CALL BugFlowerCollision Check whether the midges have reached the flower using BugFlowerCollision.
26570 DEC E Decrease the collision result in E by one (zero means that the midges have reached the flower).
26571 JR Z,StartEatingFlower Jump to StartEatingFlower if the midges have reached the flower, to start eating it.
Are the midges stunned?
26573 BIT 2,(IX+4) Jump to UpdateMidges_Horizontal if the midges are not stunned.
26577 JR Z,UpdateMidges_Horizontal
26579 DEC (IX+5) Decrease the stun timer in IX+5 by one.
26582 JP NZ,DrawObject Jump to DrawObject if the midges are still stunned, to draw them without moving.
26585 RES 2,(IX+4) Reset bit 2 of IX+4 (the stun has ended).
Horizontal movement. Find the target and see which side of it the midges are on.
UpdateMidges_Horizontal 26589 CALL FindBugTarget Find the target using FindBugTarget, returning HL pointing to the X position of the target.
26592 LD A,(IX+5) B=the horizontal motion from IX+5.
26595 LD B,A
26596 AND 15 C=the speed (the low nibble).
26598 LD C,A
26599 LD A,B B=the fraction (the high nibble).
26600 AND 240
26602 LD B,A
26603 LD A,(HL) A=the X position of the target.
26604 SUB (IX+1) Subtract the X position of the midges.
26607 JP P,UpdateMidges_TargetRight Jump to UpdateMidges_TargetRight if the target is to the right.
26610 BIT 6,(IX+0) Jump to UpdateMidges_TargetLeft if the midges are moving right.
26614 JP Z,UpdateMidges_TargetLeft
The target is to the left and the midges are moving left, so speed up (to a maximum of 15) and move left.
UpdateMidges_AccelerateLeft 26617 LD A,C A=the speed.
26618 CP 15 Increase the speed by one, up to a maximum of 15.
26620 JR NC,UpdateMidges_MoveLeft
26622 INC A
UpdateMidges_MoveLeft 26623 LD C,A C=the new speed.
26624 LD H,(IX+1) H=the X position of the midges.
26627 CALL CalculateMidgesMovement Work out the distance to move using CalculateMidgesMovement.
26630 AND A Subtract the distance from the position.
26631 SBC HL,DE
26633 LD A,H A=the new X position.
26634 CP 26 Jump to UpdateMidges_StoreX if the midges have not reached the left edge.
26636 JR NC,UpdateMidges_StoreX
26638 RES 6,(IX+0) Reset bit 6 of IX+0 (turn the midges round to move right).
UpdateMidges_StoreX 26642 LD (IX+1),A Write the new X position.
26645 LD A,L A=the fraction (the high nibble of L).
26646 AND 240
26648 OR C Add the speed.
26649 LD (IX+5),A Write the horizontal motion to IX+5.
26652 LD A,(IX+1) A=the X position of the midges.
26655 CP 24 Jump to DrawObject to draw the midges without moving vertically if they are too far to the left.
26657 JP C,DrawObject
26660 CP 208 Jump to DrawObject to draw the midges without moving vertically if they are too far to the right.
26662 JP NC,DrawObject
Vertical movement works in the same way, with the target being above or below the midges.
