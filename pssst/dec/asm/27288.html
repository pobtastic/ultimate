27288: Update Slug
Updates a slug. If the slug is touching Robbie then both explode. A weapon hitting the slug either kills it (a smoke bomb, worth 53 points) or stuns it (a streamer), and the repellent spray has no effect. When the slug reaches the flower it starts to eat it.
Otherwise it crawls around, moving once every four updates: 4 pixels horizontally, bouncing off the sides of the play area, and 1 pixel vertically (but only when it is in the middle of the play area).
Input
IX Slug object
The object fields used are:
Field Use
+0 bit 6 Moving right
+0 bit 7 Moving down
+4 bit 3 Eating the flower
+5 Stun timer
+6 Y position when the slug started eating
