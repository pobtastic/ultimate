25012: Stop Flashing Player Label
Used by the routine at GamePlayEntry.
Clears the FLASH attribute on the three cells of a "1UP" or "2UP" label.
Input
HL Position of the label (X in L, Y in H)
StopFlashingPlayerLabel 25012 CALL AttributeAddress Convert the position to an attribute buffer address using AttributeAddress.
25015 LD B,3 Set the cell counter to 3 (the three characters of the label).
StopFlashingPlayerLabel_Loop 25017 LD A,(HL) Fetch the attribute of the cell.
25018 AND %01111111 Keep only bits 0-6 (reset the FLASH bit).
25020 LD (HL),A Write the attribute back.
25021 INC HL Move onto the next cell.
25022 DJNZ StopFlashingPlayerLabel_Loop Decrease the cell counter by one and loop back to StopFlashingPlayerLabel_Loop until all three cells have stopped flashing.
25024 RET Return.
This entry point is used by the routine at FlashPlayerLabel.
Use the position of "2UP" instead.
FlashPlayerLabel_2UP 25025 LD HL,216 HL=0216 (the position of "2UP").
25028 JR FlashPlayerLabel_Set Jump to FlashPlayerLabel_Set.
