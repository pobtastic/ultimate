41917: Sounds: Inventory
Used by the routine at PickUpItem.
Plays the tone for picking up an item, which is 64 cycles with a half period of 64.
SoundsInventory 41917 LD BC,16448 B=64 (the half period) and C=64 (the number of cycles).
41920 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
