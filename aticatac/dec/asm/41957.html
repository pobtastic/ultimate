41957: Sounds: Touching
Used by the routines at CheckPlayerTouch and HandlerMushroom.
Queues a sound that is played by SoundEffect, one note on each pass of the main loop.
SoundsTouching 41957 LD BC,25616 B=100 (the sound handler) and C=16 (the count).
