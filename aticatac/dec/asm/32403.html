32403: Process Room Item
Used by the routine at DrawRoom.
Runs the handler of one of the items in a room's list (see DrawRoom). The list entry is the address of the item in the initial entity data; subtracting 30077 converts it to the address of the live record. Door records are two 8-byte halves (one for each of the two rooms they join), so the second half is used if the first is not for the current room. The handler is looked up in HandlerTable2 and returns to Decor_Loop.
