43860: Room Shape 2 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shape 2 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with 255. The last byte is 255 to end all of the lists, and the corners are those at RoomShape_02_Coordinates.
RoomShape_02_Lines 43860 DEFB 0,9,7,1,255 Start at corner 0 and draw lines to corners 9 7 and 1.
43865 DEFB 2,1,3,11,255 Start at corner 2 and draw lines to corners 1 3 and 11.
43870 DEFB 4,3,5,13,255 Start at corner 4 and draw lines to corners 3 5 and 13.
43875 DEFB 6,5,15,7,255 Start at corner 6 and draw lines to corners 5 15 and 7.
43880 DEFB 8,7,9,15,255 Start at corner 8 and draw lines to corners 7 9 and 15.
43885 DEFB 10,1,9,11,255 Start at corner 10 and draw lines to corners 1 9 and 11.
43890 DEFB 12,3,11,13,255 Start at corner 12 and draw lines to corners 3 11 and 13.
43895 DEFB 14,5,13,15,255 Start at corner 14 and draw lines to corners 5 13 and 15.
43900 DEFB 255 Terminator.
