41944: Sounds: Walk Low
Used by the routine at SoundsWalking.
The second part of SoundsWalking, which plays the lower of the two footsteps. The sample for SoundsWalking includes both footsteps, so there is no separate sample for this one.
SoundsWalkLow 41944 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
41946 RET Z Return if the counter is even.
41947 LD BC,24580 B=96 (the half period) and C=4 (the number of cycles).
41950 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
