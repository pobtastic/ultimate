43480: Room Shape 11 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shape 11 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with 255. The last byte is 255 to end all of the lists, and the corners are those at RoomShape_0B_Coordinates.
RoomShape_0B_Lines 43480 DEFB 0,1,255 Start at corner 0 and draw lines to corner 1.
43483 DEFB 2,3,255 Start at corner 2 and draw lines to corner 3.
43486 DEFB 255 Terminator.
