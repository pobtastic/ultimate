38221: Open Doorway
Used by the routines at CheckDoorKey, HandlerClock and HandlerDoorACGExit.
Resets bit 3 of the attribute of both halves of a door.
Input
IX One half of the door
OpenDoorway 38221 LD A,(IX+5) Fetch the attribute of the door.
38224 AND %11110111 Reset bit 3.

38231 CALL GetOtherDoorHalf Call GetOtherDoorHalf to point IX at the other half of the door.

38237 AND %11110111 Reset bit 3.
38239 LD (IX+5),A Write it back.
