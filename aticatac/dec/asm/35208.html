35208: Handle Frankenstein
The handler for Frankenstein (sprite IDs 116 to 119). He steers towards the player. If he touches the player while the player is holding the spanner (sprite ID 139 with attribute 69) then he is hit instead and the player is given 4096 points, otherwise TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small takes some energy. The sprite comes from the bottom two bits of the counter at CounterLow. The tail at HandlerBoss_Move is also used by the other bosses.
