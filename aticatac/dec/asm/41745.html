41745: Menu Icons
Used by the routine at GameInitialisation.
Draws the nine icons of the menu. The data for each one is the same as an entity record, which is copied from the table at MenuEntities to EntityToDraw, drawn with DrawEntity and then coloured with SetEntityAttributes.
Before After
MenuIcons 41745 LD IX,41341 Point IX at the entity to draw at EntityToDraw.
41749 LD HL,41777 Point HL at the table of icons at MenuEntities.
41752 LD B,9 B=9 (the number of icons).
MenuIcons_Loop 41754 PUSH BC Stash the icon counter on the stack.
41755 LD DE,41341 Copy 8 bytes of data from HL to EntityToDraw.
41758 LD BC,8
41761 LDIR
41763 PUSH HL Stash HL and DE on the stack.
41764 PUSH DE
41765 CALL DrawEntity Call DrawEntity to draw the entity.
41768 CALL SetEntityAttributes Call SetEntityAttributes to set the attributes of the entity.
41771 POP DE Restore DE and HL from the stack.
41772 POP HL
41773 POP BC Restore the icon counter from the stack.
41774 DJNZ MenuIcons_Loop Decrease the icon counter by one and loop back to MenuIcons_Loop until all nine icons have been drawn.
41776 RET Return.
The sprite IDs are stored +1 higher than their actual value.
