35690: Creature Defaults
The 16-byte record that SpawnCreature copies into a free record to make a new creature. It starts as a sparkle (see HandlerSparkle).
Offset Description
0 Sprite ID: 88 (the first frame of the sparkle)
1 Room ID (set to the room of the player)
2 The creature that the sparkle turns into (set from Creatures)
3 X position (set at random)
4 Y position (set at random)
5 Attribute: INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)
6 Direction: 0
7 Step counter: 0
8 X velocity: 2 (set at random)
9 Y velocity: 2 (set at random)
10 Bobbing counter: 0
11 Waypoint X position: 0
12 Waypoint Y position: 0
13 Counter: 16
14 Sparkle counter: 32
15 Removal counter: 0
CreatureDefaults 35690 DEFB 88,0,92,104,104,68,0,0
35698 DEFB 2,2,0,0,0,16,32,0
