35456: Handle Monk And Bat
The handler for the monk (sprite IDs 148 to 151) and the second kind of bat (152 to 155). It is the same as HandlerWitch, but the counter is restarted at 32 and when it runs out the X velocity is also set to -2 or 2 depending on the bottom bit of FRAMES. The attribute is INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
Input
IX The monk or bat entity
