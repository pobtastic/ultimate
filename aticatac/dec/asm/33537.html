33537: Handle Bat
The handler for a bat, which has the sprite IDs 106 and 107. If the player is in another room then CountDownCreature counts the bat down until it is removed. Otherwise the bat is counted, tested for touching the player (see CheckPlayerTouch) and for being hit by a weapon (see CheckWeaponHit), and then it flies. The step it is on in its flight pattern is held at offset 9 of the entity, and BatFlightPattern holds the distance to fly on each axis for each step. The low bits of offset 8 hold the direction: bit 0 is set when moving right, bit 1 is set when moving down and bit 2 swaps which of the two distances is used for each axis. The bat turns around at the walls.

33579 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.

33584 JP NZ,HandlerBatlet_Move Jump to HandlerBatlet_Move unless the pattern has restarted.
33587 LD A,R Fetch a random number (from the refresh register).
33589 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
33591 LD (IX+8),A Update the direction of the bat.

33692 JP NC,PopCreature

The bat has reached a wall so reverse its direction.
