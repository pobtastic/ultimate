43503: Room Shapes 0 3 and 4 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shapes 0 3 and 4 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with 255. The last byte is 255 to end all of the lists, and the corners are those in the coordinates of each shape (RoomShape_00_Coordinates for the first).
RoomShapes_00_03_04_Lines 43503 DEFB 0,1,3,4,255 Start at corner 0 and draw lines to corners 1 3 and 4.
43508 DEFB 2,1,3,6,255 Start at corner 2 and draw lines to corners 1 3 and 6.
43513 DEFB 5,1,4,6,255 Start at corner 5 and draw lines to corners 1 4 and 6.
43518 DEFB 7,3,4,6,255 Start at corner 7 and draw lines to corners 3 4 and 6.
43523 DEFB 255 Terminator.
