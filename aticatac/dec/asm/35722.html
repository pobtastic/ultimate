35722: Draw Chicken
Used by the routines at TakeEnergyFromPlayer, HandlerFoodItem, HandlerKnight, HurtPlayer, PreparePlayer and HandlerMushroom.
Updates the picture of the roast chicken so that it shows the energy of the player. The energy in PlayerEnergy and the level that was last drawn in ChickenLevel are both divided by eight, and nothing is done if they are the same. When the energy has gone up, the picture of the full chicken is drawn with a height of the new level. When it has gone down, the part of the empty chicken (see Graphic_Chicken_2) below that level is drawn, by pointing the entry for it in the graphics table at 42534 at the data for that row and temporarily writing a new width and height there.
