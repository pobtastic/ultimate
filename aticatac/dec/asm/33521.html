33521: Handle Sword
The handler for a thrown sword, which has the sprite IDs 56 to 63. Sets the attribute, points the sprite in the direction of travel and then runs the movement code that is shared by all of the weapons.
Input
IX The weapon entity
HandlerSword 33521 CALL StoreEntity Call StoreEntity to save the position of the weapon.
33524 LD (IX+5),70 Set the attribute of the sword to INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
33528 LD HL,60063 Point HL at the Y velocity of the weapon at PlayerWeapon_Y_Velocity.
33531 CALL SwordDirection Call SwordDirection to choose the sprite of the sword.
33534 JP HandlerWeapon Jump to HandlerWeapon to move the weapon.
