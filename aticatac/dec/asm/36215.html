36215: Control Player
Used by the routines at HandlerWizard, HandlerSerf and HandlerKnight.
Moves the entity of a character, using the controls. Called by the handlers of the knight and the serf (and the wizard). The velocities of the entity are kept at offsets 6 (X) and 7 (Y), and have four bits after the point, so only the top four bits make up the whole pixels moved each frame. They are changed by UpdateVelocity, slowed by SlowVelocity and limited by CheckRoomBounds and CheckObstacles before ApplyMovement moves the entity. The bits at offset 2 are the number of steps still to be taken in the bottom four, and in bits 4-5 a flag that is set when movement is blocked along the X axis (bit 4) or the Y axis (bit 5).
Input
B The change made to a velocity each frame by the controls
C The fastest X velocity
the Y velocity is limited by B
D The amount that the Y velocity is slowed by each frame
E The amount that the X velocity is slowed by each frame
IX The entity of the character
