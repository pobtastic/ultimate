41922: Sounds: Drop Item
Used by the routine at DropItem.
Plays the tone for dropping an item, which is 128 cycles with a half period of 32.
SoundsDropItem 41922 LD BC,8320 B=32 (the half period) and C=128 (the number of cycles).
41925 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
