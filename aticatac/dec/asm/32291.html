32291: Draw Room
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Reached at the end of every pass of MainLoop. Looks up the list of items for the current room in RoomTable and runs the handler of each one through ProcessRoomItem. Each entry in a list is the address of an item record in the initial entity data at Initialisation_Player. Once the list is finished the counter at CounterLow is incremented and, on the first pass, DrawRoomEntities draws all of the entities in the room and bit 0 of GameFlags is set. The random number seed at RandomSeed is then stirred, the pick-up key, the pause keys and the food are dealt with and the main loop is restarted, unless the player is in room 142 which ends the game with GameComplete.
