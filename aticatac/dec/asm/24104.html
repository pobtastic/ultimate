24104: Player Energy
The energy of the player, which is shown as the chicken at the side of the screen. It starts at 240 (see PreparePlayer_Energy). It is decreased when the player is hurt (see HurtPlayer) and increased by eating food (see HandlerFoodItem). When it reaches zero a life is lost.
