36591: Update Velocity
Used by the routine at ControlPlayer.
Changes the velocities of an entity that is controlled by ControlPlayer, and works out how many whole pixels it will move this frame. Each velocity (at offsets 6 and 7) has the change added, and is then limited to the fastest speed in either direction. The top four bits of the velocity are the pixels to move, which is GetWholePixels. While the steps of an automatic walk are being counted down by AutoWalkStep (the bottom four bits of offset 2) the pixels moved are 0 or 2 instead, depending on whether the velocity is below 16 with the sign of the velocity.
Input
DE The change in each velocity (X in E and Y in D)
HL The fastest velocity (X in L and Y in H)
IX The entity
Output
DE The pixels to move (X in E and Y in D)
