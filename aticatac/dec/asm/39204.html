39204: Spawn Food
Used by the routine at DrawRoom.
Called by the main loop. When the counter at CounterLow is zero and bit 0 of CounterHigh is reset, the next of the food items from FoodItems to 60888 is looked at (the food pointer in FoodPointer moves on by 8 bytes, and starts again at FoodItems after the last one). If the item is not in the room of the player and is not already in use then it is made one of the foods (sprite IDs 80 to 87), using FRAMES.

39207 LD C,A
