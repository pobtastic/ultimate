37708: Shift Inventory
Used by the routines at HandlerPickupItem and HandlerBlank.
Moves the first two slots of the inventory (see Inventory_Slot1) along to be the second and third slots.
ShiftInventory 37708 LD HL,24119 Point HL at the end of the second slot at Inventory_Slot2_Attribute.
37711 LD DE,24123 Point DE at the end of the third slot at Inventory_Slot3_Attribute.
37714 LD BC,8 BC=0008 (the size of two slots).
37717 LDDR Copy the two slots, working backwards.
37719 RET Return.
