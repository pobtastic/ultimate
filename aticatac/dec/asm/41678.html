41678: Display Lives
Used by the routines at StartGame and PreparePlayer.
Draws an icon of the character for each life that the player has left (up to three) in the side panel, and clears the icon of each life that has been lost. The icon is the first sprite of the character that was chosen in the menu (GameOptions bits 3-4).

41733 POP BC Restore the counters from the stack.

41735 JP P,DisplayPlayerLives_Count
41738 LD C,0 C=0 (no lives left to draw).
