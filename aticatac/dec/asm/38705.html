38705: Fall Through Trapdoor
Used by the routine at HandlerTrapOpen.
Used by HandlerTrapOpen for a trapdoor that is open. If the player is in range of the trapdoor (24 in each direction), the screen is cleared and the frame of room 150 is drawn. Then for 128 frames of rings of colour (see RippleAttributes) spread out from the centre of the screen, while the centre flashes and clicks are made. Then the player goes through the door with EnterDoor. If the player is not in range then the trapdoor is just shown by HandlerRoomItem.
