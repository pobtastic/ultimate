37357: Handle Big Door Exit
The handler for the doors that are bigger than normal (sprite ID 3), which the player goes through from a greater distance than HandlerDoorExit.
Input
IX The door entity
HandlerDoorBigExit 37357 LD BC,8224 BC=8224 (the range of the door).
37360 JR HandlerDoorExit_Check Jump to HandlerDoorExit_Check to check whether the player is in range of the door.
