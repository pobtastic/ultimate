34226: Check Player Touch
Used by the routines at HandlerBatlet, HandlerPumpkin, HandlerGhostlet, HandlerSpikey, HandlerBlob, HandlerGhoul, HandlerMummy, HandlerDracula, HandlerFrankenstein, HandlerDevil, HandlerWitch, HandlerMonk and HandlerHunchback.
Checks whether an entity is touching the player. The player is touching an entity if the player is a character sprite (a sprite ID of 1 to 48), is in the same room and is within 11 pixels of it on both axes. When it touches, the byte at offset 2 of the player is set to 1 and the sound of touching is made.
Input
IX The entity to check
Output
E 1 if the player touches the entity, otherwise 0
