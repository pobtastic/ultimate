41349: Clear Sprite
Used by the routines at DrawInventoryItem and DisplayPlayerLives.
Clears the pixels in the area under a sprite, which is two bytes wide and 20 rows high, working up from the position of an entity.
Input
IX The entity
ClearSprite 41349 LD L,(IX+3) L=the entity X position, and H=the entity Y position.
41352 LD H,(IX+4)
41355 CALL ScreenAddress Call ScreenAddress to get the screen address of the position.
41358 LD B,20 B=20 (the number of rows).
ClearSprite_Row 41360 LD (HL),0 Clear the first byte of the row.
41362 INC L Move to the next byte.
41363 LD (HL),0 Clear the second byte of the row.
41365 DEC L Move back to the first byte.
41366 CALL ScreenPosition1PixelAbove Call ScreenPosition1PixelAbove to move up one pixel row.
41369 DJNZ ClearSprite_Row Decrease the row counter by one and loop back to ClearSprite_Row until it is zero.
41371 RET Return.
