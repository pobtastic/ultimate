48724: Room Shape 10 Coordinates
The coordinates of the corners of the frame of room shape 10 (see RoomShapeTable), each of which is an X position followed by a Y position. The corners are numbered from 0 and the lines between them are listed at RoomShapes_09_0A_Lines. It is used by rooms 49 50 52 66 73 77 and 81. These are the same corners as shape 9 (at RoomShape_09_Coordinates) with the X and Y positions swapped. This data is held at the start of the font table, where the graphics for the characters below the space would be (see MainFont).
