24112: Inventory 1
The slot of the inventory for the item that has been carried for the shortest time. A slot is the address of the entity of the item (two bytes, low byte first, or 0 if the slot is empty), then the sprite ID and the attribute of the item.
Inventory_Slot1 24112 DEFW 6
Inventory_Slot1_Sprite_Id 24114 DEFB 0 The sprite ID of the item.
24115 DEFB 44 The attribute of the item.
