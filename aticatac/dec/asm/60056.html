60056: Player Weapon
The 16-byte record for the weapon that the player throws, which is a copy of Initialisation_Weapon. The second half is the entity for the sound effects (see Initialisation_Sound).
PlayerWeapon 60056 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0 The sprite ID, room, flags, X position, Y position and attribute of the weapon.
PlayerWeapon_X_Velocity 60062 DEFB 0 The X velocity of the weapon.
PlayerWeapon_Y_Velocity 60063 DEFB 0 The Y velocity of the weapon.
PlayerWeapon_Sound 60064 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0 The second half, which is the sound entity.
