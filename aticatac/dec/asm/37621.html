37621: Handle Pickup Item
The handler for the items that the player can pick up (sprite IDs 128 to 142). If the pick up key is not pressed then ClearPickupFlag clears the flag for it. Otherwise nothing is done if another item has just been picked up (bits 0-1 of PickupFlags), or if the player is not a character, or if the player is not touching the item (see CheckPlayerTouching). If they are, the flags are set, the item that has been carried the longest is dropped (see DropItem), the others move along (see ShiftInventory), the item is picked up (see PickUpItem) and the inventory is drawn by DrawInventory.
