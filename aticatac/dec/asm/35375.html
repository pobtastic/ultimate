35375: Handle Witch
The handler for the witch (sprite IDs 144 to 147). The counter at offset 13 is counted down from 16 and when it reaches zero the witch gets a new random velocity (see RandomiseVelocity) with the Y velocity halved. The sprite faces right when the X velocity is not negative, and the bottom bit of the counter animates it. The movement itself is done by MoveCreature.
