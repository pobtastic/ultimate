36736: Get Whole Pixels
Used by the routine at UpdateVelocity.
Converts a velocity, which has four bits after the point, to the number of whole pixels to move. This is the velocity divided by sixteen, rounded towards zero.
Input
A The velocity (signed)
Output
A The whole pixels
GetWholePixels 36736 AND A Test the sign of the velocity.
36737 JP P,GetWholePixels_Positive Jump to GetWholePixels_Positive if it is positive.
36740 NEG Make the velocity positive.
36742 RRCA Shift the velocity right four times, to drop the bits after the point.
