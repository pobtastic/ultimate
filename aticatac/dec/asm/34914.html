34914: Handle Mummy
The handler for the mummy (sprite IDs 112 to 115). If the mummy touches the player then TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small takes some energy. Then the mummy picks a target to steer towards with SteerCreature. If the leaf (ItemEntity_Leaf, the copy of Initialisation_Leaf) is in the room then it is the target, and when the mummy reaches it the leaf is moved to room 107. Otherwise, when bit 7 of offset 6 is reset and the red key (ItemEntity_Red_Key, the copy of Initialisation_Key_Red) is in the room, the mummy goes backwards and forwards between two waypoints that are kept at offsets 11 and 12 and when bit 7 is set (or the red key is not in the room) the target is the player. The sprite comes from the bottom two bits of the counter at CounterLow, and then HandlerBoss_Move does the rest.
