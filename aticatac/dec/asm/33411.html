33411: Throw Sword
Used by the routine at HandlerSerf.
Launches a sword for the serf, unless a weapon is already in flight or the player is in a doorway. The sprite ID of the sword depends on the direction that the weapon is travelling in (56 to 63), as worked out by the same method that SwordDirection uses.
