37362: Handle Door Exit
Used by the routines at HandlerDoorOpen and HandlerClock.
The handler for the doors that are always open (sprite IDs 1 and 2 in HandlerTable2). It uses CheckPlayerInRange to check whether the player is in range of the door, and then EnterDoor takes them through it. Whether or not they do, the door is then shown by HandlerRoomItem.
Input
IX The door entity
HandlerDoorExit 37362 LD BC,4369 BC=4369 (the range of the door).
This entry point is used by the routines at HandlerDoorBigExit and HandlerDoorACGExit.
HandlerDoorExit_Check 37365 CALL CheckPlayerInRange Call CheckPlayerInRange to see whether the player is in range of the door.
37368 CALL C,EnterDoor Call EnterDoor if the player is in range.
37371 JP HandlerRoomItem Jump to HandlerRoomItem to handle a room item that does nothing.
