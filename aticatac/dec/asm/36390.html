36390: Handle Knight
The handler for the knight (sprite IDs 1 to 16) when it is the character being played. It is the same as HandlerSerf, except that the slowing is 3 on each frame and the fire button throws an axe (see ThrowAxe). The second part of this routine is used by the other characters as well: it makes creatures (see SpawnCreature), takes energy from the player every sixteen frames, and displays the character.
