44009: Room Shapes 5 to 8 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shapes 5 6 7 and 8 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with 255. The last byte is 255 to end all of the lists, and the corners are those in the coordinates of each shape (RoomShape_05_Coordinates for the first).
