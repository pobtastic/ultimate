36292: Handle Serf
The handler for the serf (sprite IDs 33 to 48) when it is the character being played. The serf is moved by ControlPlayer, with a change in velocity of 32 a top speed of 32 and a slowing of 1 on each frame. When it is moving, every fourth frame the next frame of the animation is chosen for the direction of travel (the four sets are left, right, up and down) and the sound of walking is made. Then the fire button throws a sword (see ThrowSword), and HandlerCharacter_Finish does the rest.
