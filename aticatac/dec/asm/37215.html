37215: Handle Door Open
The handler for the doors that are open (sprite IDs 33 and 35 in HandlerTable2) and that close after a time, as made by RandomiseDoors. On every other frame (when bit 0 of CounterLow is reset) the door timer at DoorTimer is counted down. When it has reached zero, and the player is not in a doorway (see CheckDoorway), HandlerDoor_Toggle closes the door. Otherwise the door is just checked for being used as an exit (see HandlerDoorExit).
Input
IX The door entity
HandlerDoorOpen 37215 LD A,(24082) Fetch the counter from CounterLow.
37218 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
37220 JP NZ,HandlerDoorExit Jump to HandlerDoorExit on every other frame.
37223 LD A,(24110) Fetch the door timer from DoorTimer.
37226 AND A Test whether the door timer is zero.
37227 JR Z,HandlerDoorOpen_CheckDoorway Jump to HandlerDoorOpen_CheckDoorway if it is.
37229 DEC A Decrease the door timer by one.
37230 LD (24110),A Update the door timer in DoorTimer.
37233 JP HandlerDoorExit Jump to HandlerDoorExit to handle the exit of the door.
HandlerDoorOpen_CheckDoorway 37236 LD A,(24109) Fetch the doorway flag from InDoorway.
37239 AND A Test whether the player is in a doorway.
37240 JP NZ,HandlerDoorExit Jump to HandlerDoorExit if the player is.
37243 JR HandlerDoor_Toggle Jump to HandlerDoor_Toggle to restart the door timer and toggle both halves of the door.
