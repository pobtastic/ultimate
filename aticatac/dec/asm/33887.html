33887: Handle Pumpkin And Spider
The handler for the pumpkin (sprite IDs 76 and 77) and the spider (92 and 93). If the player is in another room then CountDownCreature counts the creature down until it is removed. Otherwise the creature is counted and checked for being hit by a weapon (CheckWeaponHit) or touching the player (CheckPlayerTouch). Offset 6 of the creature holds the direction it is heading in (bit 0 is set for right and bit 1 is set for down). A new direction is chosen at random every sixteen steps, and the velocities at offsets 8 and 9 build up by one every other step towards 2 or -2. The movement itself is done by MoveCreature.
Input
IX The creature entity

Choose a new direction every sixteen steps.
33929 LD A,(IX+7) Fetch the step counter of the creature.

33938 AND %00000011 Keep only bits 0-1.
33940 LD (IX+6),A Update the direction of the creature.

33961 JR Z,HandlerPumpkin_VelocityY
33963 INC (IX+8) Increment the X velocity by one.
This entry point is used by the routine at MoveCreature.

33976 CP 2 Jump to HandlerPumpkin_Animate if it is already 2.
33978 JR Z,HandlerPumpkin_Animate

This entry point is used by the routine at MoveCreature.
Move on to the next step, and animate the creature by changing its sprite.
HandlerPumpkin_Animate 33983 INC (IX+7) Increment the step counter of the creature.

33989 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.

33994 LD (IX+0),A Write the new sprite ID back to the creature.
