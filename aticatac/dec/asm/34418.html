34418: Handle Spikey And Ghost
The handler for the spikey (sprite IDs 94 and 95) and the ghost (104 and 105). The counter at offset 10 runs from -7 up to 6 and half of it is added to the Y position, which makes the creature bob up and down as it moves. A new random velocity is chosen every time that the counter wraps round. The movement itself is done by MoveCreature.
Input
IX The creature entity

34433 INC (HL) Increment the number of creatures by one.
34434 LD (IX+15),0 Write 0 to offset 15 of the creature, so it is not removed.

34441 DEC E Jump to HandleTouchingCreature if the creature touched the player.

34448 DEC E Jump to PopCreature if the creature was hit by a weapon.

Animate the creature by changing the sprite every other step.
34452 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the bobbing counter from offset 10.
34455 RRA Halve the bobbing counter.
34456 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
34458 LD C,A Keep the animation frame in C.
34459 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the sprite ID of the creature.
34462 AND %11111110 Reset bit 0.
34464 ADD A,C Add the animation frame to the sprite ID.
34465 LD (IX+0),A Update the sprite ID of the creature.
34468 LD DE,(24093) Fetch the size of the room from CurrentRoom_Width.
Move on to the next step of the bobbing. A new velocity is chosen when the counter reaches 7.
34472 INC (IX+10) Increment the bobbing counter.
34475 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the bobbing counter.
34478 CP 7 Jump to HandlerSpikey_Bob unless it has reached 7.
34480 JR NZ,HandlerSpikey_Bob
34482 CALL RandomiseVelocity Call RandomiseVelocity to give the creature a new random velocity.
34485 LD (IX+10),249 Set the bobbing counter to -7.
Add half of the counter to the Y position of the creature and keep it inside the room.
HandlerSpikey_Bob 34489 SRA A Halve the counter, keeping its sign.
34491 ADD A,(IX+4) Add the Y position of the creature.
34494 LD C,A Keep the new Y position in C.
34495 SUB 104 Subtract 104 (the vertical centre of the room).
34497 JP P,HandlerSpikey_CheckBottom Jump to HandlerSpikey_CheckBottom if the new position is below the centre.
34500 NEG Make the distance from the centre positive.
34502 CP D Jump to HandlerSpikey_Store if the new position is still inside the room.
34503 JR C,HandlerSpikey_Store
34505 LD (IX+9),2 Set the Y velocity of the creature to 2.
34509 SET 1,(IX+6) Set bit 1 of the direction of the creature, so it is heading down.
34513 BIT 7,(IX+10) Jump to HandlerSpikey_Store if the bobbing counter is positive.
34517 JR Z,HandlerSpikey_Store
34519 LD (IX+10),0 Reset the bobbing counter to 0.
HandlerSpikey_Store 34523 LD (IX+4),C Update the Y position of the creature.
34526 JP MoveCreature Jump to MoveCreature to move the creature.
HandlerSpikey_CheckBottom 34529 CP D Jump to HandlerSpikey_Store if the new position is still inside the room.
34530 JR C,HandlerSpikey_Store
34532 RES 1,(IX+6) Reset bit 1 of the direction of the creature, so it is heading up.
34536 LD (IX+9),254 Set the Y velocity of the creature to -2.
34540 LD (IX+10),249 Set the bobbing counter to -7.
34544 JR HandlerSpikey_Store Jump to HandlerSpikey_Store to store the Y position of the creature.
