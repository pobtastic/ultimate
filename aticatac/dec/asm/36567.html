36567: Hurt Player
Used by the routine at HandleTouchingCreature.
Takes 32 from the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy, which is what happens when a creature touches them (see HandleTouchingCreature). The chicken is redrawn by DrawChicken. If the energy is gone then LoseLife takes a life.
HurtPlayer 36567 LD A,(24104) Fetch the energy of the player from PlayerEnergy.
36570 SUB 32 Subtract 32.
36572 JR Z,HurtPlayer_NoEnergy Jump to HurtPlayer_NoEnergy if the energy is zero.
36574 JR NC,HurtPlayer_Update Jump to HurtPlayer_Update if there is some energy left.
36576 XOR A A=0 (no energy left).
HurtPlayer_NoEnergy 36577 LD (24104),A Update the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy.
36580 CALL DrawChicken Call DrawChicken to redraw the chicken.
36583 JR LoseLife Jump to LoseLife to take a life from the player.
HurtPlayer_Update 36585 LD (24104),A Update the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy.
36588 JP DrawChicken Jump to DrawChicken to redraw the chicken.
