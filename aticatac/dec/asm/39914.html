39914: Draw Room Frame
Used by the routine at EnterRoom.
Clears GameFlags (so the next pass of MainLoop draws the items in the room), reads the room's attribute from RoomAttributes and draws the frame. The entry point at DrawRoomFrame_Room draws the frame of the room in A, which is how FallThroughTrapdoor draws the frame of room 150. The entry for the shape of the room in RoomShapeTable gives the width and height of the floor (written to CurrentRoom_Width and CurrentRoom_Height), the coordinates of the corners of the frame and the lists of the lines to draw between them (see TunnelFrameCoordinates and TunnelFrameLines for the frame of room 150). Each list has a corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, ending with 255. The list of lists ends with 255.
Input
A The room (at the entry point DrawRoomFrame_Room)
