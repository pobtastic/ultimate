A9D8: Room Shape 0B Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shape 0B (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists, and the corners are those at RoomShape_0B_Coordinates.
RoomShape_0B_Lines A9D8 DEFB $00,$01,$FF Start at corner 00 and draw lines to corner 01.
A9DB DEFB $02,$03,$FF Start at corner 02 and draw lines to corner 03.
A9DE DEFB $FF Terminator.
