988B: Handle Mushroom
The handler for the mushroom (sprite ID A1). While the player is touching it (see CheckPlayerTouching), the energy in PlayerEnergy is decreased by one every frame, the chicken is drawn again with DrawChicken and a sound is made with SoundsTouching. When the energy reaches zero the mushroom is removed by RemoveMushroom. The attribute of the mushroom cycles round the colours in MushroomAttributes, changing every fourth frame (using offset 06), and the attributes are set by SetItemAttributes.
Input
IX The mushroom entity
