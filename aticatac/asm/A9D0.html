A9D0: Room Shape 0B Coordinates
The coordinates of the corners of the frame of room shape 0B (see RoomShapeTable), each of which is an X position followed by a Y position. The corners are numbered from 00 and the lines between them are listed at RoomShape_0B_Lines. It is used by room 8E.
RoomShape_0B_Coordinates A9D0 DEFB $04,$BF Corner 00.
A9D2 DEFB $04,$00 Corner 01.
A9D4 DEFB $1F,$BF Corner 02.
A9D6 DEFB $1F,$00 Corner 03.
