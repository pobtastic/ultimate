A3FE: Clear Sound
Used by the routines at SoundEffect, SoundEffectNext and SoundEffectEating.
Clears the first byte of the sound entity, so that it does nothing on the following passes of the main loop.
Input
IX The sound entity
ClearSound A3FE LD (IX+$00),$00 Write 00 to the first byte of the entity.
A402 RET Return.
