8ED7: Hurt Player
Used by the routine at HandleTouchingCreature.
Takes 20 from the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy, which is what happens when a creature touches them (see HandleTouchingCreature). The chicken is redrawn by DrawChicken. If the energy is gone then LoseLife takes a life.
HurtPlayer 8ED7 LD A,($5E28) Fetch the energy of the player from PlayerEnergy.
8EDA SUB $20 Subtract 20.
8EDC JR Z,HurtPlayer_NoEnergy Jump to HurtPlayer_NoEnergy if the energy is zero.
8EDE JR NC,HurtPlayer_Update Jump to HurtPlayer_Update if there is some energy left.
8EE0 XOR A A=00 (no energy left).
HurtPlayer_NoEnergy 8EE1 LD ($5E28),A Update the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy.
8EE4 CALL DrawChicken Call DrawChicken to redraw the chicken.
8EE7 JR LoseLife Jump to LoseLife to take a life from the player.
HurtPlayer_Update 8EE9 LD ($5E28),A Update the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy.
8EEC JP DrawChicken Jump to DrawChicken to redraw the chicken.
