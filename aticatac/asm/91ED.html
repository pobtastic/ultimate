91ED: Handle Big Door Exit
The handler for the doors that are bigger than normal (sprite ID 03), which the player goes through from a greater distance than HandlerDoorExit.
Input
IX The door entity
HandlerDoorBigExit 91ED LD BC,$2020 BC=2020 (the range of the door).
91F0 JR HandlerDoorExit_Check Jump to HandlerDoorExit_Check to check whether the player is in range of the door.
