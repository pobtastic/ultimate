95DA: Update Clock
Used by the routine at UpdateFixedEntities.
Called whenever FRAMES has changed. Once 32 frames have passed, FRAMES is reduced by that, the seconds in ClockSeconds are increased (in BCD), the minutes in ClockMinutes when the seconds reach 60 and the hours in ClockHours when the minutes reach 60 and then the clock is printed on the screen.
ClockTick 95DA LD A,($5C78) Fetch FRAMES.
95DD CP $32 Return if fewer than 32 frames have passed.
95DF RET C
95E0 SUB $32 Subtract 32 as a second has passed.
95E2 LD ($5C78),A Update FRAMES.
95E5 LD HL,$5E3F Point HL at the clock seconds at ClockSeconds.
95E8 LD A,(HL) Fetch the seconds.
95E9 INC A Increment the seconds.
95EA DAA Convert it to BCD.
95EB LD (HL),A Write it back.
95EC CP $60 Jump to ClockTick_Print if the seconds have not reached 60.
95EE JR NZ,ClockTick_Print
95F0 LD (HL),$00 Reset the seconds to 00.
95F2 DEC HL Move HL on to the minutes.
95F3 LD A,(HL) Fetch the minutes.
95F4 INC A Increment the minutes.
95F5 DAA Convert it to BCD.
95F6 LD (HL),A Write it back.
95F7 CP $60 Jump to ClockTick_Print if the minutes have not reached 60.
95F9 JR NZ,ClockTick_Print
95FB LD (HL),$00 Reset the minutes to 00.
95FD DEC HL Move HL on to the hours.
95FE LD A,(HL) Fetch the hours.
95FF INC A Increment the hours.
9600 DAA Convert it to BCD.
9601 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
9603 LD (HL),A Write it back.
Print the clock.
ClockTick_Print 9604 LD HL,$40C8 HL=40C8 (screen buffer location).
This entry point is used by the routine at GameStats.
PrintClock 9607 CALL ScreenAddress Call ScreenAddress to get the screen address.
960A LD DE,$5E3D Point DE at the clock hours at ClockHours.
960D LD B,$02 B=02.
960F CALL PrintScore_LowDigit Call PrintScore_LowDigit to print the last digit of the hours and both digits of the minutes.
9612 LD DE,$5E3F Point DE at the clock seconds at ClockSeconds.
9615 INC HL Move HL on to the next character position.
9616 LD B,$01 B=01.
9618 JP PrintScore_Loop Jump to PrintScore_Loop to print both digits of the seconds.
