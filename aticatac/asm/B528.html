B528: Graphic Attributes: Ghost Picture
The attributes of the Ghost Picture graphic (graphic ID 11). It is 04 cells wide and 03 cells high, and these are the first two bytes of the data. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Ghost_Picture.
Graphic_Ghost_Picture_Attributes B528 DEFB $04 Width = 04 bytes.
B529 DEFB $03 Height = 03 bytes.
B52A DEFB $FF,$FF,$FF,$FF
B52E DEFB $FF,$FF,$FF,$FF
B532 DEFB $FF,$FF,$FF,$FF
