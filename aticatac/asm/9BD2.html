9BD2: Calculate Attribute Address
Used by the routines at MenuWriteText, FlashScore, GetGraphicAttributeDetails, SetEntityAttributes, PrintStringColour and DrawSidePanelAttrs.
Converts the pixel position of a point into the address in the attribute buffer of the character cell that holds it.
Input
H The Y position (the pixel line down from the top)
L The X position (the pixel across from the left)
Output
HL The attribute buffer address
AttributeAddress 9BD2 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
9BD3 LD A,L Fetch the X position.
9BD4 RRCA Rotate right three times, which divides the X position by 08.
9BD5 RRCA
9BD6 RRCA
9BD7 AND %00011111 Keep only bits 0-4 (the column).
9BD9 LD L,A Keep the column in L.
9BDA LD A,H Fetch the Y position.
9BDB RLCA Rotate left twice.
9BDC RLCA
9BDD LD C,A Keep the Y position, rotated, in C.
9BDE AND %11100000 Keep only bits 5-7 (the character row within the third of the screen).
9BE0 OR L Combine it with the column.
9BE1 LD L,A Keep the low byte of the attribute buffer address in L.
9BE2 LD A,C Fetch the Y position, rotated.
9BE3 AND %00000011 Keep only bits 0-1 (the third of the screen).
9BE5 OR %01011000 Set bits 3-4 and 6, so the address is in the attribute buffer (58).
9BE7 LD H,A Keep the high byte of the attribute buffer address in H.
9BE8 POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
9BE9 RET Return.
