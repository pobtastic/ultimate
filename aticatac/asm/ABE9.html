ABE9: Room Shapes 05 to 08 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shapes 05 06 07 and 08 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists, and the corners are those in the coordinates of each shape (RoomShape_05_Coordinates for the first).
RoomShapes_05_to_08_Lines ABE9 DEFB $01,$FF Corner 01 has no lines to draw.
