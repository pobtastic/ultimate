A064: Attribute Jump Table
The routines which colour the attribute cells under an entity, chosen by SetEntityAttributes_Moved from the way the entity has moved. The direction is the horizontal movement plus the vertical movement.
Direction Movement Routine
00 Unchanged AttrDrawUnchanged
01 Right AttrDrawMovingRight
02 Left AttrDrawMovingLeft
03 (not possible) CreateDelay
04 Down AttrDrawMovingDown
05 Down and right AttrDrawMovingDownRight
06 Down and left AttrDrawMovingDownLeft
07 (not possible) CreateDelay
08 Up AttrDrawMovingUp
09 Up and right AttrDrawMovingUpRight
0A Up and left AttrDrawMovingUpLeft
