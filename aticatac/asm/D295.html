D295: Graphic Attributes: Pumpkin Picture
The attributes of the Pumpkin Picture graphic (graphic ID 25). It is 04 cells wide and 02 cells high, and these are the first two bytes of the data. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Pumpkin_Picture.
Graphic_Pumpkin_Picture_Attributes D295 DEFB $04 Width = 04 bytes.
D296 DEFB $02 Height = 02 bytes.
D297 DEFB $46,$46,$46,$46
