957D: Check Doorway
Used by the routine at ControlPlayer.
Counts how many of the two axes an entity is beyond the edge of the floor of the room. The floor is centred on 58 across and 68 down, and on each axis it extends one more than the width in CurrentRoom_Width or the height in CurrentRoom_Height from the centre. The count is written to InDoorway, which is zero when the entity is not in a doorway.
Input
IX The entity
CheckDoorway 957D PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.
957E LD B,$00 B=00 (the count).
9580 LD HL,($5E1D) HL=*CurrentRoom_Width (the width in L and the height in H).
9583 INC L Increment the width.
9584 INC H Increment the height.
9585 LD E,(IX+$03) Fetch the X position of the entity.
9588 LD D,(IX+$04) Fetch the Y position of the entity.
958B LD A,E Fetch the X position.
958C SUB $58 Subtract 58 to get the distance from the centre of the floor.
958E JP P,CheckDoorway_X Jump to CheckDoorway_X if the result is positive.
9591 NEG Negate it, to make the distance positive.
CheckDoorway_X 9593 CP L Jump to CheckDoorway_Y if the entity is within the width of the floor.
9594 JR C,CheckDoorway_Y
9596 INC B Increment the count of axes that it is beyond.
CheckDoorway_Y 9597 LD A,D Fetch the Y position.
9598 SUB $68 Subtract 68 to get the distance from the centre of the floor.
959A JP P,CheckDoorway_Compare Jump to CheckDoorway_Compare if the result is positive.
959D NEG Negate it, to make the distance positive.
CheckDoorway_Compare 959F CP H Jump to CheckDoorway_Finish if the entity is within the height of the floor.
95A0 JR C,CheckDoorway_Finish
95A2 INC B Increment the count of axes that it is beyond.
CheckDoorway_Finish 95A3 LD A,B Fetch the count.
95A4 LD ($5E2D),A Update the doorway flag in InDoorway.
95A7 POP DE Restore DE from the stack.
95A8 RET Return.
