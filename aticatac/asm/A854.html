A854: Room Attribute Table
Two bytes for each of the 97 room IDs (00 to 96): the attribute used to colour the room (read by DrawRoomFrame), and the shape of the frame of the room, which is the number of an entry in RoomShapeTable. Room 96 has shape 0C whose frame is the tunnel frame at TunnelFrameCoordinates.
