A3C2: Sounds: Drop Item
Used by the routine at DropItem.
Plays the tone for dropping an item, which is 80 cycles with a half period of 20.
SoundsDropItem A3C2 LD BC,$2080 B=20 (the half period) and C=80 (the number of cycles).
A3C5 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
