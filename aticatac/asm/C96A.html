C96A: Graphic Attributes: Wall Trophy
The attributes of the Wall Trophy graphic (graphic ID 16). It is 02 cells wide and 02 cells high, and these are the first two bytes of the data. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Wall_Trophy.
Graphic_Wall_Trophy_Attributes C96A DEFB $02 Width = 02 bytes.
C96B DEFB $02 Height = 02 bytes.
C96C DEFB $45,$45
C96E DEFB $45,$45
