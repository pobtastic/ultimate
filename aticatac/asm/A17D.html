A17D: Entity To Draw
A scratch entity record, which the routines that draw the side panel fill in before drawing it as an entity. Only the sprite ID, the position and the attribute are used for drawing.
EntityToDraw A17D DEFB $00 The sprite ID.
A17E DEFB $00,$00 Not used when drawing.
A180 DEFB $00 The X position.
A181 DEFB $00 The Y position.
A182 DEFB $00 The attribute.
A183 DEFB $00,$00 Not used when drawing.
