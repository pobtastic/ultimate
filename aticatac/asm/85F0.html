85F0: Count Down Creature
Used by the routines at HandlerBatlet, HandlerPumpkin, HandlerSparkle, HandlerGhostlet, HandlerSpikey, HandlerBlob, HandlerPop, HandlerGhoul, HandlerWitch and HandlerMonk.
Used by the handlers of the creatures when the player is not in the room of the creature. The counter at offset 0F of the entity is counted down and when it reaches zero the creature is removed (see HandlerWeapon_Remove).
Input
IX The creature entity
CountDownCreature 85F0 DEC (IX+$0F) Decrease the counter at offset 0F by one.
85F3 RET NZ Return if it is not zero.
85F4 JP HandlerWeapon_Remove Jump to HandlerWeapon_Remove to remove the creature.
