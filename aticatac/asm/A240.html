A240: Draw Side Panel Attributes
Used by the routine at EnterRoom.
Sets the attributes of the side panel. The whole panel is filled with a colour that is picked from the attribute of the room, and then a shape and some bands of colour are put on top. ClearPlayArea_Row, which fills a block of cells with a value, does most of the work.
Before After
DrawSidePanelAttrs A240 LD HL,$00C0 HL=C0 (the Y position is 00 and the X position is C0), which is the top left of the side panel.
A243 CALL AttributeAddress Call AttributeAddress to get the attribute address of the position.
A246 LD BC,$0818 B=08 (the width of the panel in cells) and C=18 (the number of rows).
Pick the colour for the panel. Take the inverse of bits 0-2 of the attribute of the room, unless that is less than 02 when 44 is used.
A249 LD A,($5E1A) Fetch the room attribute from CurrentRoom_Attribute.
A24C CPL Flip the bits.
A24D AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
A24F CP $02 Jump to DrawSidePanelAttrs_Colour if the colour is 02 or more.
A251 JR NC,DrawSidePanelAttrs_Colour
A253 LD A,$44 A=44.
DrawSidePanelAttrs_Colour A255 LD E,A Keep the attribute of the panel in E.
A256 PUSH DE Stash the attribute of the panel on the stack.
DrawSidePanelAttrs_Row A257 PUSH BC Stash the width and the number of rows on the stack.
A258 PUSH HL Stash the attribute address on the stack.
DrawSidePanelAttrs_Cell A259 LD (HL),E Write the attribute of the panel to the cell.
A25A INC L Move to the next cell.
A25B DJNZ DrawSidePanelAttrs_Cell Decrease the width by one and loop back to DrawSidePanelAttrs_Cell until the row is done.
A25D POP HL Restore the attribute address from the stack.
A25E LD BC,$0020 BC=0020.
A261 ADD HL,BC Move down a row.
A262 POP BC Restore the width and the number of rows from the stack.
A263 DEC C Decrease the number of rows by one and loop back to DrawSidePanelAttrs_Row until all of the rows have been set.
A264 JR NZ,DrawSidePanelAttrs_Row
Fill a square of three by three cells, and then a cell and a square of two by two cells below it, with the attribute of the room. Then put the attribute of the panel back in the middle of the three by three square.
A266 LD HL,$90C8 HL=90C8 (the Y position is 90 and the X position is C8).

A278 LD BC,$0202 B=02 (the width) and C=02 (the number of rows).
A27B CALL ClearPlayArea_Row Call ClearPlayArea_Row to fill the cells with the attribute of the room.
A27E LD HL,$98D0 HL=98D0 (the Y position is 98 and the X position is D0).
A281 CALL AttributeAddress Call AttributeAddress to get the attribute address of the position.

A285 LD (HL),E Write it to the cell.
