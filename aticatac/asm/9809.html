9809: Tunnel Frame Lines
The lines that join the corners in TunnelFrameCoordinates, which are drawn by DrawRoomFrame. For each square there are two lists, which each have the corner to start from followed by the corners to draw lines to, ending with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists.
