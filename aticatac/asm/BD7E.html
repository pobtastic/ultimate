BD7E: Room Shapes 09 and 0A Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shapes 09 and 0A (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists. The corners are in RoomShape_09_Coordinates for shape 09 and RoomShape_0A_Coordinates for shape 0A.
