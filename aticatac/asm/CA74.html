CA74: Graphic Attributes: Door Shut
The attributes of the Door Shut graphic (graphic ID 20). It is 04 cells wide and 03 cells high, and these are the first two bytes of the data. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Door_Shut.
Graphic_Door_Shut_Attributes CA74 DEFB $04 Width = 04 bytes.
CA75 DEFB $03 Height = 03 bytes.
CA76 DEFB $43,$47,$47,$43
CA7A DEFB $43,$47,$47,$43
CA7E DEFB $43,$43,$43,$43
