C986: Graphic Attributes: Trap
This block holds 2 sets of attributes, one for each of the graphic IDs 18 and 19. Each set is 04 cells wide and 04 cells high, and starts with its width and height. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Trap_2 and Graphic_Trap_1 respectively.
