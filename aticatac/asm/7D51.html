7D51: Messaging: Copyright
The first byte is the attribute used for the whole message (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT)), followed by the text, which ends with a character that has bit 7 set. It is printed by GameMenu.
Message_Copyright 7D51 DEFM $47 Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) + "© 1983 A.C.G. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED".
7D52 DEFM "%"
7D53 DEFM "1983 A.C.G. ALL RIGHTS RESERVE","D"+$80
