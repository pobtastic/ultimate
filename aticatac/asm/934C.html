934C: Shift Inventory
Used by the routines at HandlerPickupItem and HandlerBlank.
Moves the first two slots of the inventory (see Inventory_Slot1) along to be the second and third slots.
ShiftInventory 934C LD HL,$5E37 Point HL at the end of the second slot at Inventory_Slot2_Attribute.
934F LD DE,$5E3B Point DE at the end of the third slot at Inventory_Slot3_Attribute.
9352 LD BC,$0008 BC=0008 (the size of two slots).
9355 LDDR Copy the two slots, working backwards.
9357 RET Return.
