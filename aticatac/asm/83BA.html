83BA: Get Bat Step
Used by the routine at HandlerBatlet.
Looks up the distances for the current step of the flight pattern of a bat in BatFlightPattern. Bit 2 of the direction of the bat is returned in the Z flag (which is set when the axes are not swapped).
Input
IX The bat entity
Output
HL The address of the first of the two distances
GetBatStep 83BA LD C,(IX+$09) Fetch the step of the flight pattern.
83BD SLA C Double the step number, to get an offset of two bytes for each step.
83BF LD B,$00 Clear the high byte of the offset.
83C1 LD HL,$83CA Point HL at the bat flight pattern at BatFlightPattern.
83C4 ADD HL,BC Find the entry for this step in the flight pattern.
83C5 BIT 2,(IX+$08) Test bit 2 of the direction of the bat, and return.
83C9 RET
