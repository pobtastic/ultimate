845F: Handle Pumpkin And Spider
The handler for the pumpkin (sprite IDs 4C and 4D) and the spider (5C and 5D). If the player is in another room then CountDownCreature counts the creature down until it is removed. Otherwise the creature is counted and checked for being hit by a weapon (CheckWeaponHit) or touching the player (CheckPlayerTouch). Offset 06 of the creature holds the direction it is heading in (bit 0 is set for right and bit 1 is set for down). A new direction is chosen at random every sixteen steps, and the velocities at offsets 08 and 09 build up by one every other step towards 02 or -02. The movement itself is done by MoveCreature.
Input
IX The creature entity
HandlerPumpkin 845F CALL StoreEntity Call StoreEntity to save the position of the creature.
8462 LD A,($EA91) Fetch the room of the player from PlayerRoom.
8465 CP (IX+$01) Jump to CountDownCreature if the creature is not in the same room as the player.
8468 JP NZ,CountDownCreature
846B LD HL,$5E25 Point HL at the number of creatures at NumCreatures.
846E INC (HL) Increment the number of creatures by one.
Check whether the creature has been hit by a weapon or touched the player.
846F CALL CheckWeaponHit Call CheckWeaponHit to check whether the weapon has hit the entity.
8472 DEC E Jump to PopCreature if the creature was hit by a weapon.
8473 JP Z,PopCreature
8476 CALL CheckPlayerTouch Call CheckPlayerTouch to check whether the entity is touching the player.
8479 DEC E Jump to HandleTouchingCreature if the creature touched the player.
847A JP Z,HandleTouchingCreature
847D LD DE,($5E1D) Fetch the size of the room from CurrentRoom_Width.
8481 LD (IX+$0F),$00 Write 00 to offset 0F of the creature, so it is not removed.
8485 LD (IX+$05),$46 Set the attribute of the entity to INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
Choose a new direction every sixteen steps.
8489 LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the step counter of the creature.
848C AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
848E JR NZ,HandlerPumpkin_Velocity Jump to HandlerPumpkin_Velocity if the result is not zero.
8490 LD A,R Fetch a random number (from the refresh register).
8492 AND %00000011 Keep only bits 0-1.
8494 LD (IX+$06),A Update the direction of the creature.
Only change the velocities on every other step.
HandlerPumpkin_Velocity 8497 BIT 0,(IX+$07) Jump to HandlerPumpkin_Animate if bit 0 of the step counter is set.
849B JR NZ,HandlerPumpkin_Animate
849D BIT 0,(IX+$06) Jump to MoveCreature_SlowLeft if the creature is not heading right.
84A1 JP Z,MoveCreature_SlowLeft
84A4 LD A,(IX+$08) Fetch the X velocity of the creature.
84A7 CP $02 Jump to HandlerPumpkin_VelocityY if it is already 02.
84A9 JR Z,HandlerPumpkin_VelocityY
84AB INC (IX+$08) Increment the X velocity by one.
This entry point is used by the routine at MoveCreature.
HandlerPumpkin_VelocityY 84AE BIT 1,(IX+$06) Jump to MoveCreature_SlowUp if the creature is not heading down.
84B2 JP Z,MoveCreature_SlowUp
84B5 LD A,(IX+$09) Fetch the Y velocity of the creature.
84B8 CP $02 Jump to HandlerPumpkin_Animate if it is already 02.
84BA JR Z,HandlerPumpkin_Animate
84BC INC (IX+$09) Increment the Y velocity by one.
This entry point is used by the routine at MoveCreature.
Move on to the next step, and animate the creature by changing its sprite.
HandlerPumpkin_Animate 84BF INC (IX+$07) Increment the step counter of the creature.
84C2 LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the step counter of the creature.
84C5 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
84C7 XOR (IX+$00) Exclusive-or the sprite ID with A, which flips bit 0 of it on every other step.
84CA LD (IX+$00),A Write the new sprite ID back to the creature.
