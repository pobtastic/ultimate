A13B: Draw Inventory
Used by the routines at EnterRoom, HandlerPickupItem and HandlerBlank.
Draws the three items that the player is carrying in the side panel, one for each slot of the inventory at Inventory_Slot1. The slots are drawn 10 pixels apart.
Before After
DrawInventory A13B LD DE,$2CC8 D=2C (the Y position) and E=C8 (the X position) of the first slot.
A13E LD HL,$5E30 Point HL at the first slot of the inventory at Inventory_Slot1.
A141 LD B,$03 B=03 (the number of slots).
DrawInventory_Loop A143 CALL DrawInventoryItem Draw the item in the slot with DrawInventoryItem.
A146 LD A,E Fetch the X position of the slot.
A147 ADD A,$10 Add 10 to move on to the X position of the next slot.
A149 LD E,A Keep the X position of the next slot in E.
A14A DJNZ DrawInventory_Loop Decrease the slot counter by one and loop back to DrawInventory_Loop until all of the slots are drawn.
A14C RET Return.
