A982: Room Shape Table
One entry of six bytes for each of the 0D shapes that the frame of a room can have, read by DrawRoomFrame. The entry has the width and the height of the floor, the address of the coordinates of the corners of the frame and the address of the lists of the lines to draw between those corners. The shape of a room comes from its entry in RoomAttributes. No room uses shapes 06 or 07.
