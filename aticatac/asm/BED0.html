BED0: Room Shape 09 Coordinates
The coordinates of the corners of the frame of room shape 09 (see RoomShapeTable), each of which is an X position followed by a Y position. The corners are numbered from 00 and the lines between them are listed at RoomShapes_09_0A_Lines. It is used by rooms 36 37 3F 44 47 4A 4F and 52. These are the same corners as shape 0A (at RoomShape_0A_Coordinates) with the X and Y positions swapped. This data is held at the start of the font table, where the graphics for the characters below the space would be (see MainFont).
