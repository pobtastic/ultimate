AB54: Room Shape 02 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shape 02 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists, and the corners are those at RoomShape_02_Coordinates.
RoomShape_02_Lines AB54 DEFB $00,$09,$07,$01,$FF Start at corner 00 and draw lines to corners 09 07 and 01.
AB59 DEFB $02,$01,$03,$0B,$FF Start at corner 02 and draw lines to corners 01 03 and 0B.
AB5E DEFB $04,$03,$05,$0D,$FF Start at corner 04 and draw lines to corners 03 05 and 0D.
AB63 DEFB $06,$05,$0F,$07,$FF Start at corner 06 and draw lines to corners 05 0F and 07.
AB68 DEFB $08,$07,$09,$0F,$FF Start at corner 08 and draw lines to corners 07 09 and 0F.
AB6D DEFB $0A,$01,$09,$0B,$FF Start at corner 0A and draw lines to corners 01 09 and 0B.
AB72 DEFB $0C,$03,$0B,$0D,$FF Start at corner 0C and draw lines to corners 03 0B and 0D.
AB77 DEFB $0E,$05,$0D,$0F,$FF Start at corner 0E and draw lines to corners 05 0D and 0F.
AB7C DEFB $FF Terminator.
