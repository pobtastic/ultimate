8283: Throw Sword
Used by the routine at HandlerSerf.
Launches a sword for the serf, unless a weapon is already in flight or the player is in a doorway. The sprite ID of the sword depends on the direction that the weapon is travelling in (38 to 3F), as worked out by the same method that SwordDirection uses.
Input
IX The player entity

82A0 AND A Jump to ThrowSword_SetSprite if the weapon is not moving horizontally.
82A1 JR Z,ThrowSword_SetSprite

82A6 DEC C Decrease the direction number by one, as the weapon is moving left.

82AC LD HL,$EA98 Point HL at the player weapon at PlayerWeapon.

82B0 JP LaunchWeapon Jump to LaunchWeapon to launch the sword.

82B4 JR ThrowSword_SetSprite Jump to ThrowSword_SetSprite to set the sprite of the sword.
The weapon is moving horizontally only.

82BB LD C,$06 C=06 (the direction number for left).

ThrowSword_MovingRight 82BF LD C,$02 C=02 (the direction number for right).
