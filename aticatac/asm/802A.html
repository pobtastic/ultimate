802A: Handler Table 2
The address of the handler for each kind of room item, door or other fixture, indexed by the first byte of its record (the graphic ID, see GraphicsTable). ProcessRoomItem reads the entry and calls the handler. IDs that are not used point to CreateDelay, which does nothing.
