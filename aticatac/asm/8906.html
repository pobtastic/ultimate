8906: Handle Dracula
The handler for Dracula (sprite IDs 7C to 7F). If Dracula touches the player then TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small takes some energy. When the player is holding the crucifix (sprite ID 8A with attribute 46) Dracula heads away from the player, otherwise he heads towards the player when they are in the same room. If they are not, then about once every 0100 frames he goes to a random room, if the shape of that room (see GetRoomValue) is less than 03.
Input
IX The Dracula entity
HandlerDracula 8906 CALL StoreEntity Call StoreEntity to save the position of Dracula.
8909 CALL CheckPlayerTouch Call CheckPlayerTouch to check whether the entity is touching the player.
890C DEC E Call TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small if Dracula touched the player.
890D CALL Z,TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small
See if the player is holding the crucifix.
8910 LD DE,$468A DE=468A (the attribute and the sprite ID of the crucifix).
8913 CALL FindInventoryItem Call FindInventoryItem to look for the crucifix in the inventory.
8916 JR NZ,HandlerDracula_CheckRoom Jump to HandlerDracula_CheckRoom if the player is not holding it.
The player has the crucifix, so steer towards the player and then reverse the velocities to run away.
8918 LD DE,($EA93) Fetch the position of the player from EA93.
891C CALL SteerCreature Call SteerCreature to steer the creature towards its target.
891F LD A,(IX+$08) Fetch the X velocity.
8922 NEG Reverse the direction of the X velocity.
8924 LD (IX+$08),A Update the X velocity.
8927 LD A,(IX+$09) Fetch the Y velocity.
892A NEG Reverse the direction of the Y velocity.
892C LD (IX+$09),A Update the Y velocity.
892F JR HandlerDracula_SetSprite Jump to HandlerDracula_SetSprite to choose the sprite of Dracula.
Check whether Dracula is in the same room as the player.
HandlerDracula_CheckRoom 8931 LD A,($EA91) Fetch the room of the player from PlayerRoom.
8934 CP (IX+$01) Jump to HandlerDracula_OtherRoom if Dracula is in a different room.
8937 JR NZ,HandlerDracula_OtherRoom
8939 LD DE,($EA93) Fetch the position of the player from EA93.
893D CALL SteerCreature Call SteerCreature to steer the creature towards its target.
Choose the sprite and the area that Dracula can move in.
HandlerDracula_SetSprite 8940 LD A,($5E12) Fetch the counter from CounterLow.
8943 RRA Divide the counter by four.
8944 RRA
8945 AND %00000011 Keep only bits 0-1.
8947 ADD A,$7C Add 7C to get the sprite ID of Dracula.
8949 LD (IX+$00),A Update the sprite ID of Dracula.
894C LD DE,$3434 DE=3434 (the area).
894F JP HandlerBoss_Move Jump to HandlerBoss_Move to move the boss.
Dracula is in a different room, so every so often send him to a random one.
HandlerDracula_OtherRoom 8952 LD (IX+$0B),$68 Set the X position of the waypoint to 68.
8956 LD (IX+$0C),$68 Set the Y position of the waypoint to 68.
895A CALL SteerCreature Call SteerCreature to steer the creature towards its target.
895D LD A,($5C78) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
8960 AND A Jump to HandlerDracula_SetSprite unless the low byte of FRAMES is zero, which happens once in every 0100 frames.
8961 JP NZ,HandlerDracula_SetSprite
8964 LD A,R Fetch a random number (from the refresh register).
8966 AND %01111111 Keep only bits 0-6.
8968 LD C,A Keep the random room in C.
8969 CALL GetRoomValue Call GetRoomValue to look up the value for the room.
896C CP $03 Jump to HandlerDracula_SetSprite if the value for the room is 03 or more.
896E JP NC,HandlerDracula_SetSprite
8971 LD A,($EA91) Fetch the room of the player from PlayerRoom.
8974 CP C Jump to HandlerDracula_SetSprite if the random room is the room of the player.
8975 JP Z,HandlerDracula_SetSprite
8978 LD (IX+$01),C Update the room of Dracula.
897B JR HandlerDracula_SetSprite Jump to HandlerDracula_SetSprite to choose the sprite of Dracula.
