9565: Close Doorway
Used by the routines at CheckDoorKey, HandlerClock, UpdateDoorway and HandlerDoorACGExit.
Sets bit 3 of the attribute of both halves of a door.
Input
IX One half of the door
CloseDoorway 9565 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the attribute of the door.
9568 OR %00001000 Set bit 3.
956A LD (IX+$05),A Write it back.
956D PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
956F CALL GetOtherDoorHalf Call GetOtherDoorHalf to point IX at the other half of the door.
9572 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the attribute of the other half.
9575 OR %00001000 Set bit 3.
9577 LD (IX+$05),A Write it back.
957A POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
957C RET Return.
