95A9: Make Tombstone
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayerVanishing.
Makes a tombstone (sprite ID 8F) where the player is. The four entities from ItemEntity_Tombstone_1 are searched for one that is not in use. If there is one, it is made a copy of the player, apart from the sprite ID, and with the attribute of the player set to 45 first. Then it is drawn.
MakeTombstone 95A9 LD HL,$EAE8 HL=ItemEntity_Tombstone_1 (the first of the four entities to search).
95AC LD DE,$0008 DE=0008 (the size of an entity).
95AF LD B,$04 B=04 (the number of entities to look at).
MakeTombstone_Loop 95B1 LD A,(HL) Fetch the sprite ID of the entity.
95B2 AND A Jump to MakeTombstone_Create if the entity is not in use.
95B3 JR Z,MakeTombstone_Create
95B5 ADD HL,DE Move HL on to the next entity.
95B6 DJNZ MakeTombstone_Loop Decrease the entity counter by one and loop back to MakeTombstone_Loop until all four have been checked.
95B8 RET Return.
MakeTombstone_Create 95B9 LD A,$45 Write 45 to the attribute of the player.
95BB LD ($EA95),A
95BE PUSH HL Stash the address of the entity on the stack.
95BF LD (HL),$8F Write 8F to the sprite ID of the entity.
95C1 EX DE,HL Swap DE and HL, so that DE points at the entity.
95C2 INC DE Move DE on to the room of the entity.
95C3 LD HL,$EA91 Point HL at the room of the player at PlayerRoom.
95C6 LD BC,$0007 BC=0007.
95C9 LDIR Copy the seven bytes from the player to the entity.
95CB POP HL Restore the address of the entity from the stack.
This entry point is used by the routine at GameComplete.
Draw the entity that HL points to.
DrawEntityAtHL 95CC PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
95CE PUSH HL Copy HL into IX, as the entity to draw.
95CF POP IX
95D1 CALL DrawEntity Call DrawEntity to draw the entity.
95D4 POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
95D6 RET Return.
