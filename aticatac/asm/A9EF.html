A9EF: Room Shapes 00 03 and 04 Lines
The lines that join the corners of the frame of room shapes 00 03 and 04 (see RoomShapeTable). Each list has the corner to start from, followed by the corners to draw lines to, and ends with FF. The last byte is FF to end all of the lists, and the corners are those in the coordinates of each shape (RoomShape_00_Coordinates for the first).
RoomShapes_00_03_04_Lines A9EF DEFB $00,$01,$03,$04,$FF Start at corner 00 and draw lines to corners 01 03 and 04.
A9F4 DEFB $02,$01,$03,$06,$FF Start at corner 02 and draw lines to corners 01 03 and 06.
A9F9 DEFB $05,$01,$04,$06,$FF Start at corner 05 and draw lines to corners 01 04 and 06.
A9FE DEFB $07,$03,$04,$06,$FF Start at corner 07 and draw lines to corners 03 04 and 06.
AA03 DEFB $FF Terminator.
