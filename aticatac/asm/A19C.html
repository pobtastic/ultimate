A19C: Add Points To Score
Used by the routines at PopCreature and HandlerFrankenstein.
Adds some points to the score, which is held as three bytes of BCD starting at Score_1 (the most significant byte is first). It then carries on to print the score.
Before After
Input
BC The points to add to the score (as BCD)
AddPointsToScore A19C LD HL,$5E2C Point HL at the last byte of the score at Score_3.
A19F LD A,(HL) Fetch the last byte of the score.
A1A0 ADD A,C Add C.
A1A1 DAA Convert the result to BCD.
A1A2 LD (HL),A Update the last byte of the score.
A1A3 DEC HL Move on to the middle byte of the score.
A1A4 LD A,(HL) Fetch the middle byte of the score.
A1A5 ADC A,B Add B, with the carry.
A1A6 DAA Convert the result to BCD.
A1A7 LD (HL),A Update the middle byte of the score.
A1A8 DEC HL Move on to the first byte of the score.
A1A9 LD A,(HL) Fetch the first byte of the score.
A1AA ADC A,$00 Add 00 (i.e. just the carry flag).
A1AC DAA Convert the result to BCD.
A1AD LD (HL),A Update the first byte of the score.
