A846: Graphic Attributes: Table
The attributes of the Table graphic (graphic ID 12). It is 04 cells wide and 03 cells high, and these are the first two bytes of the data. They are followed by one attribute for each cell. The pixels are at Graphic_Table.
Graphic_Table_Attributes A846 DEFB $04 Width = 04 bytes.
A847 DEFB $03 Height = 03 bytes.
A848 DEFB $45,$45,$45,$45
A84C DEFB $42,$42,$42,$42
A850 DEFB $42,$42,$42,$42
