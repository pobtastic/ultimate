8A15: Take Energy From Player
Used by the routine at HandlerHunchback.
Called when a boss has touched the player. It takes 10 (at TakeEnergyFromPlayer) or 08 (at TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small) from PlayerEnergy. The chicken is then redrawn by DrawChicken, which returns to the caller. If the player did not have enough energy then the return address is discarded and LoseLife is used to take a life.
Input
IX The boss entity
TakeEnergyFromPlayer 8A15 LD A,($5E28) Fetch the energy of the player from PlayerEnergy.
8A18 SUB $10 Take 10 off the energy of the player.
8A1A JR C,TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Dead Jump to TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Dead if the player has run out of energy.
8A1C JR TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Update Jump to TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Update to update the energy of the player.
This entry point is used by the routines at HandlerMummy, HandlerDracula, HandlerFrankenstein and HandlerDevil.
TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Small 8A1E LD A,($5E28) Fetch the energy of the player from PlayerEnergy.
8A21 SUB $08 Take 08 off the energy of the player.
8A23 JR C,TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Dead Jump to TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Dead if the player has run out of energy.
TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Update 8A25 LD ($5E28),A Update the energy of the player in PlayerEnergy.
8A28 JP DrawChicken Jump to DrawChicken to update the roast chicken.
TakeEnergyFromPlayer_Dead 8A2B POP HL Discard the return address.
8A2C JP LoseLife Jump to LoseLife to take a life from the player.
