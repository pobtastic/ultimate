A1AE: Print Scores
Used by the routine at DrawSidePanel.
Prints the score, as six digits, in the side panel.
Before After
Score_1UP A1AE LD HL,$BFCC Write MainFont_Zero to FontPointer.
A1B1 LD ($5E01),HL
A1B4 LD HL,$50C8 HL=50C8 (the Y position is 50 and the X position is C8).
This entry point is used by the routine at GameStats.
PrintScore A1B7 CALL ScreenAddress Call ScreenAddress to get the screen address of the position.
A1BA LD DE,$5E2A Point DE at the first byte of the score at Score_1.
A1BD LD B,$03 B=03 (the number of bytes).
This entry point is used by the routines at ClockTick and GameStats.
PrintScore_Loop A1BF LD A,(DE) Fetch the next byte of the score.
A1C0 RRCA Rotate right four times, which moves the high digit into the low nibble.
A1C1 RRCA
A1C2 RRCA
A1C3 RRCA
A1C4 AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
A1C6 CALL PrintScreen Call PrintScreen to print the digit.
This entry point is used by the routine at ClockTick.
PrintScore_LowDigit A1C9 LD A,(DE) Fetch the byte of the score again.
A1CA AND %00001111 Keep only bits 0-3.
A1CC CALL PrintScreen Call PrintScreen to print the digit.
A1CF INC DE Move on to the next byte of the score.
A1D0 DJNZ PrintScore_Loop Decrease the byte counter by one and loop back to PrintScore_Loop until all the bytes of the score have been printed.
A1D2 RET Return.
