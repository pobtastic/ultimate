D253: Graphic: Pumpkin Picture
The pixels of the Pumpkin Picture graphic, which is graphic ID 25. It is 04 bytes wide and 10 pixels high. The width and height are the first two bytes of the data, and they are followed by the pixels. The colours are set by the attributes at Graphic_Pumpkin_Picture_Attributes.
