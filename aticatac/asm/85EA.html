85EA: Handle Touching Creature
Used by the routines at HandlerBatlet, HandlerPumpkin, HandlerGhostlet, HandlerSpikey, HandlerBlob, HandlerGhoul, HandlerWitch and HandlerMonk.
Called when a creature has touched the player. The player loses energy (see HurtPlayer) and the creature turns into a pop (see PopCreature).
Input
IX The creature entity
HandleTouchingCreature 85EA CALL HurtPlayer Call HurtPlayer to take energy from the player.
85ED JP PopCreature Jump to PopCreature to turn the creature into a pop.
