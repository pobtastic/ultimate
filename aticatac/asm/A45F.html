A45F: Sounds: Appear
Used by the routine at HandlerPlayerAppearing.
Plays a tone of 10 cycles. The half period is made from the seventh byte of the entity (flipped, rotated left once and limited to the range 40 to 7F), so it changes as the byte does.
Input
IX The entity
SoundsAppear A45F LD A,(IX+$06) Fetch offset 06 of the entity.
A462 CPL Flip the bits.
A463 RLCA Rotate left once.
A464 AND %00111111 Keep only bits 0-5.
A466 OR %01000000 Set bit 6.
A468 LD B,A Keep the half period in B.
A469 LD C,$10 C=10 (the number of cycles).
A46B JP SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
