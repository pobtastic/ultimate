C9EC: Graphic: Wall Shield
The pixels of the Wall Shield graphic, which is graphic ID 1D. It is 02 bytes wide and 10 pixels high. The width and height are the first two bytes of the data, and they are followed by the pixels. The colours are set by the attributes at Graphic_Wall_Shield_Attributes.
