9213: Draw Item Graphic
Used by the routines at DrawChicken, HandlerDoorClosed and HandlerTrapOpen.
Draws the graphic of an entity at its position, using DrawGraphic.
Input
IX The entity
DrawItemGraphic 9213 LD E,(IX+$03) Fetch the X position of the entity.
9216 LD D,(IX+$04) Fetch the Y position of the entity.
9219 LD C,(IX+$00) Fetch the graphic of the entity.
921C LD B,(IX+$05) Fetch the attribute of the entity.
921F JP DrawGraphic Jump to DrawGraphic to draw the graphic.
