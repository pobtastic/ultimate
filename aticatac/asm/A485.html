A485: Sounds: Eating
Used by the routine at HandlerFoodItem.
Queues a sound that is played by SoundEffectEating, one note on each pass of the main loop.
SoundsEating A485 LD BC,$A010 B=A0 (the sound handler) and C=10 (the count).
A488 JP QueueSound Jump to QueueSound to queue the sound.
