5E2A: Score
The score, which is six decimal digits held as three bytes of BCD. The most significant byte is first. AddPointsToScore adds to it and Score_1UP prints it.
Score_1 5E2A DEFB $F5 The most significant two digits.
Score_2 5E2B DEFB $AC The middle two digits.
Score_3 5E2C DEFB $36 The least significant two digits.
