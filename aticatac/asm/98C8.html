98C8: Remove Mushroom
Used by the routine at HandlerMushroom.
Used by HandlerMushroom when the player has no energy left. The mushroom is removed and a life is lost with LoseLife.
Input
IX The mushroom entity
RemoveMushroom 98C8 CALL RemoveEntity Call RemoveEntity to remove the mushroom.
98CB LD (IX+$00),$00 Write 00 to the sprite ID of the mushroom.
98CF JP LoseLife Jump to LoseLife to take a life from the player.
