BE54: Room Shape 0A Coordinates
The coordinates of the corners of the frame of room shape 0A (see RoomShapeTable), each of which is an X position followed by a Y position. The corners are numbered from 00 and the lines between them are listed at RoomShapes_09_0A_Lines. It is used by rooms 31 32 34 42 49 4D and 51. These are the same corners as shape 09 (at RoomShape_09_Coordinates) with the X and Y positions swapped. This data is held at the start of the font table, where the graphics for the characters below the space would be (see MainFont).
