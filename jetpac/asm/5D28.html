5D28: Explosion Sound Parameters
Explosion sound parameters for frequency and duration.
Explosion_Param_SFX 5D28 DEFB $7D Frequency is 0C or 0D.
5D29 DEFB $4C Length is always set to 04.
5D2A DEFB $CC,$CC,$CC,$2C,$35,$0E Unused.
