A73C: Start Small Explosion
Used by the routines at Handler_ThePip, Handler_PlayerShot, ScoreSmallHit and TickThenExplode.
Turn the actor into the first frame of a small explosion (type 64) and pick its colour. Handler_SmallExplosion animates it from there.
Input
IX Actor slot
""Small Explosion: Frame 1"".
StartSmallExplosion A73C LD (IX+$00),$64 Write 64 to *IX+00.
A740 JR StartExplosion_PickOffset Jump to StartExplosion_PickOffset.
