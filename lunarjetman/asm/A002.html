A002: Wrap World X
Used by the routines at InitialiseJetman, Handler_Pickups, Handler_AlienBase, Handler_JetmanDeath, MoveBySpeed and Handler_Bombs.
Add BC to a world X and keep bits 0-4 of the high byte.

BC Offset
Output

Add the offset and wrap the high byte to bits 0-4.
