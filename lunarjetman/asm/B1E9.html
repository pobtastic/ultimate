B1E9: Advance Position
Used by the routine at TickThenExplode.
Add the horizontal speed to the fractional X accumulator in *IX+10 (bits 1-3 only) and add the carried step to the world X at TickThenExplode_AddX. AdvanceAnimation_AddYNibble does the same for the vertical thrust, using *IX+11 and continues at TickThenExplode_AddY.
Input
A Horizontal speed
IX Actor slot
Add the speed to the fraction and keep the carry as the step.
AdvanceAnimation B1E9 ADD A,(IX+$10) A+=*IX+10.
B1EC LD C,A C=A.
B1ED AND %00001110 Keep only bits 1-3.
B1EF LD (IX+$10),A Write A to *IX+10.
Rotate the carry bits of the sum down to make the whole step.
B1F2 LD A,C Copy the sum into A.
B1F3 RRCA Rotate the high nibble down into bits 0-3.
