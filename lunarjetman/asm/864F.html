864F: Clear Sprite Scratch
Used by the routines at Handler_AlienBase and Handler_JetmanDeath.
Zero SpriteRowsLeft and SpriteHeight, then XOR the actor's cached sprite onto the screen again (erasing it) through WriteSpriteRow.

IX Actor slot
Before ClearSpriteScratch After ClearSpriteScratch
