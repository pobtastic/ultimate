8AF7: Position Mask Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawObjectSprite.
Convert the actor's world position into a screen column. If it is on screen, work out the pixel shift, the screen address and the sprite size, and cache them on the actor.
Input
IX Actor slot
PositionMaskSprite 8AF7 LD L,(IX+$02) Fetch the actor's world X position.
8AFA LD H,(IX+$03)
8AFD LD BC,($607A) Subtract the scroll position at Jetman_Position.
8B01 AND A
8B02 SBC HL,BC
8B04 LD BC,$0078 Add the 0078 draw offset.
8B07 ADD HL,BC
8B08 LD A,H Fetch the high byte of the screen column.
8B09 AND %00011111 Keep only bits 0-4.
8B0B JP NZ,ResetSpriteFields Jump to ResetSpriteFields if the actor is off-screen.
8B0E LD (IX+$01),L Store the screen column in *IX+01.
Look up the sprite graphic and work out the pixel shift from the screen column.
