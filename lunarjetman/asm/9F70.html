9F70: Handler: Pickups
Keep the collectible objects in the world. When the pickup slots are empty, refill them, choose a world X away from Jetman and the rover, place the pickups from it and then advance the level.
Refill Object_Slot_1 from Pickup_Defaults when those slots are empty.
Handler_Pickups 9F70 CALL ScanObjectSlots_Test Call ScanObjectSlots_Test.
9F73 RET NZ Return if any pickup slot is still in use.
9F74 LD HL,$A068 Copy the 90 bytes of pickup templates from Pickup_Defaults into the pickup slots at Object_Slot_1.
9F77 LD DE,$6108
9F7A LD BC,$0090
9F7D LDIR
Seed the candidate world X in HL from GameTimer and RandomNumber.
9F7F LD A,($5E42) Fetch GameTimer.
9F82 LD H,A Stash it in H.
9F83 LD A,($5E40) Fetch RandomNumber.
9F86 LD L,A Stash it in L.
Keep the new world X at least 10 away from Jetman_Position and Rover_Position.
Handler_Pickups_AwayFromJetman 9F87 LD A,($607B) Fetch the high byte of Jetman_Position.

Derive the other pickup world X values by adding offsets and wrapping. MaskWorldX hands the wrapped result back in HL, so each offset is added to the world X written just before it.

Advance the level, and award an extra life every fourth level.

Award an extra life and queue a jingle, then redraw the lives.

Turn the level into the missile launcher countdown at Missile_Launcher_Countdown (the higher the level, the shorter it is), then jump to PrintPlayerLevels. Rotating left twice multiplies the level by 04. Inverting and masking that to bits 2-5 gives 3C minus 04 for each level, so adding 51 leaves a countdown that falls by 04 per level, from 8D to 51.
Level Rotate x2 Invert Keep bits 2-5 Add 51
00 00 FF 3C 8D
01 04 FB 38 89
02 08 F7 34 85
07 1C E3 20 71
0F 3C C3 00 51
10 and up FF 00 00 51
Handler_Pickups_TestLevel10 9FEA LD A,($5EA0) Fetch ActivePlayer_Level.
9FED CP $10 Compare it against 10.
9FEF CALL NC,Handler_Pickups_ForceMaximumLevel Call Handler_Pickups_ForceMaximumLevel if the level is at least 10.
9FF2 RLCA Rotate the level left twice.
9FF3 RLCA
9FF4 CPL Invert the bits.
9FF5 AND %00111100 Keep only bits 2-5.
9FF7 ADD A,$51 Add 51.
9FF9 LD ($617F),A Write it to Missile_Launcher_Countdown.
9FFC JP PrintPlayerLevels Jump to PrintPlayerLevels.
Levels of 10 and above use FF in place of the level, so they all get the shortest countdown (51). The rotate would otherwise wrap the high bits of the level round into the result.
Handler_Pickups_ForceMaximumLevel 9FFF LD A,$FF A=FF.
