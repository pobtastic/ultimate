B3A3: Message: Teleporting
Blanks the play area, then prints the "TELEPORTING" message repeatedly while cycling its INK colour, playing a tone on each pass.
Blank the play area and start the TELEPORTING cycle.
MessageTeleporting B3A3 DI Disable interrupts.
B3A4 CALL BlankPlayArea Call BlankPlayArea.
B3A7 LD B,$10 Set a colour cycle counter in B for 10 loops.
MessageTeleporting_Loop B3A9 PUSH BC Stash BC (the colour cycling counter) on the stack.
Handle printing "" messaging.
B3AA LD HL,$7058 HL=7058 (screen location).
B3AD LD DE,$B3D0 DE=String_Teleporting.
B3B0 CALL PrintStringColour Call PrintStringColour.
Pause so the player can read the message. This inner loop runs once per INK change, so the wait is 2000*10.
B3B3 LD HL,$2000 HL=2000 (counter).
Teleporting_Pause B3B6 DEC HL Decrease HL by one.
B3B7 LD A,L Jump back to Teleporting_Pause until HL is zero.
B3B8 OR H
B3B9 JR NZ,Teleporting_Pause
Handle cycling the text INK colour.
B3BB LD A,($B3D0) A=the attribute byte of String_Teleporting.
B3BE INC A Increment A by one.
B3BF AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2 (INK).
B3C1 JR NZ,MessageTeleporting_SetBright Jump to MessageTeleporting_SetBright if A was not zero.
B3C3 INC A Increment A by one.
MessageTeleporting_SetBright B3C4 OR %01000000 Set bit 6 (BRIGHT).
B3C6 LD ($B3D0),A Store A at String_Teleporting to overwrite the attribute byte.
Restore the counter, play the repeated tone and loop until the counter is zero.
B3C9 POP BC Restore BC (the colour cycling counter) from the stack.
B3CA CALL PlayRepeatedTone Call PlayRepeatedTone.
B3CD DJNZ MessageTeleporting_Loop Decrease the counter by one and jump back to MessageTeleporting_Loop until it is zero.
B3CF RET Return.
