9ECA: Animate From Scroll
Use the low bits of Jetman_Position minus the actor world X as the animation frame.
Input
IX Actor slot
AnimateFromScroll 9ECA LD A,($607A) Fetch Jetman_Position.
9ECD SUB (IX+$02) Subtract the actor world X.
9ED0 AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
Sprite IDs begin at 1A ("Lunar Rover: Left Frame 1").
9ED2 ADD A,$1A Add 1A for the sprite IDs.
9ED4 LD (IX+$00),A Write it to the actor type.
9ED7 CALL DrawActorSprite Call DrawActorSprite.
9EDA JR Handler_RoverFrames_TestInactive Jump to Handler_RoverFrames_TestInactive.
