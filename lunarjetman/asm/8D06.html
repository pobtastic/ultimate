8D06: Handler: Jetman Movement
Read the controls, spend fuel when flying, and update Jetman's facing and animation.
Input
IX Actor slot
Face Jetman from LEFT/ RIGHT, or coast the current horizontal speed toward a rest.
Handler_JetmanMovement 8D06 CALL HandlerControls Call HandlerControls.
8D09 BIT 1,A Jump to FaceJetmanLeft if LEFT is pressed.
8D0B JP Z,FaceJetmanLeft
8D0E BIT 0,A Jump to FaceJetmanRight if RIGHT is pressed.
8D10 JP Z,FaceJetmanRight
8D13 LD A,(IX+$05) Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Read if the horizontal speed is zero.
8D16 AND A
8D17 JR Z,Handler_JetmanMovement_Read
8D19 JP P,Handler_JetmanMovement_CoastRight Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_CoastRight if Jetman is moving right.
Coast a leftward speed back toward rest.
8D1C ADD A,$02 Add 02 to the speed.
8D1E CP $D0 Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded if it is now less than D0.
8D20 JR C,Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded
8D22 CP $E0 Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded if it is at least E0.
8D24 JR NC,Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded
8D26 ADD A,$10 Add a further 10 to leave the mid-band.
8D28 JR Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded.
Coast a rightward speed back toward rest.
Handler_JetmanMovement_CoastRight 8D2A ADD A,$FE Decrease the speed by 02.
8D2C CP $40 Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded if it is now at least 40.
8D2E JR NC,Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded
8D30 CP $30 Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded if it is less than 30.
8D32 JP C,Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded
8D35 SUB $10 Subtract a further 10 to leave the mid-band.
This entry point is used by the routines at FaceJetmanRight and FaceJetmanLeft.
Handler_JetmanMovement_Grounded 8D37 LD (IX+$05),A Write the speed to *IX+05.
This entry point is used by the routines at FaceJetmanRight and FaceJetmanLeft.
