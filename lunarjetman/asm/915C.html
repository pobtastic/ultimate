915C: Draw New Column
Used by the routine at ApplyJetmanSpeed.
When Jetman crosses a cell boundary, point HL at the new terrain cell and DE at the screen column that has scrolled into view.

HL World X
DE Pixel step
Output
HL Pointer to the cell after the drawn one
DE Screen address one character row below the column
