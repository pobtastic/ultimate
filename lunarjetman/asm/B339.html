B339: Bounce Jetman Right
Write 40 as Jetman's horizontal speed so he bounces away to the right, then rejoin TickThenExplode_Loop.
Input
HL Jetman_Speed (Jetman's horizontal speed)
BounceJetmanRight B339 LD (HL),$40 Write 40 to *HL.
B33B JR TickThenExplode_Loop Jump to TickThenExplode_Loop.
