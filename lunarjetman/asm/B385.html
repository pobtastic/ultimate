B385: Reset Alien States
Used by the routines at LevelNew and ShowLaserEstimate.
Writes 00 to every alien state to clear it.
ResetAlienStates B385 LD HL,$61B0 HL=Alien_Slot_1.
There are six alien slots, each is 18 bytes long.
B388 LD B,$90 Set a byte counter in B for 90 loops.
ResetAlienStates_Loop B38A LD (HL),$00 Write 00 to *HL.
B38C INC HL Increment HL by one.
B38D DJNZ ResetAlienStates_Loop Decrease the counter by one and jump back to ResetAlienStates_Loop until it is zero.
B38F RET Return.
