B8FF: Sounds: Explosion
Falling pitch played when an explosion animation ends.
Play a falling-pitch explosion, stepping D down from 07.
SoundsExplosion B8FF LD D,$07 D=07.
SoundsExplosion_Loop B901 LD A,D Copy the step into A.
B902 DAA Convert it to packed BCD.
B903 XOR %11110000 Flip bits 4-7.
B905 RLCA Rotate it left.
B906 LD C,A Copy it into the pitch.
Mix two bits of GameTimer into the pitch to add noise.
B907 LD A,($5E42) Fetch GameTimer.
B90A RRCA Rotate it right three times.
B90B RRCA
B90C RRCA
B90D AND %01100000 Keep only bits 5-6.
B90F XOR C Flip the bits from the pitch.
B910 LD C,A Write the mixed pitch.
Play the note, then repeat for the next step.
B911 CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave.
B914 DEC D Decrease the step by one.
B915 JR NZ,SoundsExplosion_Loop Jump back to SoundsExplosion_Loop until it is zero.
B917 RET Return.
