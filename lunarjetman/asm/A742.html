A742: Handler: Small Explosion
Step a small explosion through its frames (types 64 to 66), then clear the slot once it reaches type 67.
Input
IX Actor slot
Draw the current explosion frame and step the type.
Handler_SmallExplosion A742 CALL DrawObjectSprite Call DrawObjectSprite.
A745 CALL ColouriseSprite Call ColouriseSprite.
A748 INC (IX+$00) Increment the actor type by one.
A74B RET Return.
Play the explosion sound, then clear the slot.
Handler_SmallExplosion_PlaySound A74C LD A,(IX+$0A) Fetch *IX+0A.
A74F AND A Jump to Handler_SmallExplosion_ClearType if it is zero.
A750 JR Z,Handler_SmallExplosion_ClearType
A752 CALL SoundsExplosion Call SoundsExplosion.
Handler_SmallExplosion_ClearType A755 CALL ClearMaskScratch Call ClearMaskScratch.
A758 LD (IX+$00),$00 Write 00 to the actor type.
A75C RET Return.
