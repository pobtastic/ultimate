A313: Pickup Colours
Four colour bytes. Handler_Pickup writes one to the actor attribute each frame.
Pickup_Colours A313 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A314 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A315 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A316 DEFB $46 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
