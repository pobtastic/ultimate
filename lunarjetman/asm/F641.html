F641: Get Level Digits
Used by the routine at PrintPlayerLevels.
Converts the level of the active player into two digits using Convert_Base10, with a level of FF treated as 00. The units digit is returned in C and the tens digit in A.
Output
A Tens digit of the level
C Units digit of the level
