A84C: Initialise New Game
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Sets the starting level and lives for both players. The loop runs twice, clearing the level parity and carrying flag, initialising the terrain and then swapping the player states using ChangePlayer. The 2UP starting lives and level are written afterwards.
Write the starting level and lives for both players.
