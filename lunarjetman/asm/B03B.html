B03B: Convert To Debris
Used by the routine at Handler_RoverFrames.
Turn Jetman, if he is riding the rover, or else the parked rover into falling debris (type E0).
Convert Jetman or the rover into falling debris.
Handler_Missile_FallingDebris B03B LD A,($6078) Fetch Jetman_Slot.
B03E SUB $12 Subtract 12.
B040 CP $08 Compare it against 08.
B042 LD BC,$E004 Set B to the falling debris type E0 and C to 04. Loading BC keeps the flags from the compare.
B045 JR C,Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris Jump to Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris if Jetman is riding the rover.
When the rover is parked, turn it into falling debris.
B047 LD A,B Copy E0 into A.
B048 LD ($6090),A Write it to Rover_Slot.
B04B LD A,C Copy 04 into A.
B04C LD ($6098),A Write it to 6098.
B04F LD DE,$6092 DE=Rover_Position.
B052 CALL FillDebrisSlots Call FillDebrisSlots.
B055 RET Return.
When Jetman is on the rover, turn Jetman into falling debris.
Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris B056 LD A,B Copy E0 into A.
B057 LD ($6078),A Write it to Jetman_Slot.
B05A LD A,C Copy 04 into A.
B05B LD ($6080),A Write it to Jetman_Flags.
B05E LD DE,$607A DE=Jetman_Position.
B061 CALL FillDebrisSlots Call FillDebrisSlots.
B064 RET Return.
