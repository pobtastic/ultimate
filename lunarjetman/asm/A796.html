A796: Test Laser Hits
Used by the routine at LaserBeam_Animate.
Walk the alien and object slots and test each one for a laser hit.
Test the six alien slots, then object slots 01 and 03.

A799 LD DE,$0018 DE=0018.

A79E CALL TestLaserHits_Loop Call TestLaserHits_Loop.
