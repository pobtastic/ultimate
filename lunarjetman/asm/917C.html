917C: Draw Terrain Column
Used by the routines at LocateTerrainCell and DrawTerrain.
Copy eight bytes of terrain graphic from TerrainUDGs into the screen column in DE.
Input
HL Terrain cell pointer
DE Screen address
Copy eight bytes of terrain graphic into the screen column.

917D PUSH DE
