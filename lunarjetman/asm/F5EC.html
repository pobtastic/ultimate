F5EC: Print 1UP/ 2UP Levels
Used by the routines at CreateWindow and Handler_Pickups.
Colours the level display for each player green, then prints each player's level as "L" followed by two digits (1UP on the left, 2UP on the right). Flag_ActivePlayer decides which of the active and inactive player levels is used for each.
Colour the 1UP level green. Both levels are on pixel row 08 (character row 01): 1UP starts at X=48 (column 09) and 2UP starts at X=A0 (column 14).
PrintPlayerLevels F5EC LD HL,$0848 Set the 1UP level pixel co-ordinates in HL (Y=08 X=48).
F5EF CALL ColourLevelsGreen Call ColourLevelsGreen to colour the three level characters green.
Colour the 2UP level green.
F5F2 LD HL,$08A0 Set the 2UP level pixel co-ordinates in HL (Y=08 X=A0).
F5F5 CALL ColourLevelsGreen Call ColourLevelsGreen to colour the three level characters green.
Is the active player 1UP or 2UP?
