A8B5: Lose Life
Used by the routine at CountDeathTimer.
Spend a life for the active player. In a 2UP game, swap to the other player when they still have lives left.
1UP path.
LoseLife A8B5 LD A,($5E00) A=*GameOptions.
A8B8 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
A8BA JR NZ,LoseLife_2UP Jump to LoseLife_2UP if this is a 2UP game.
LoseLife_Loop A8BC LD A,($5EA1) Jump to GameOver_Check_Player_2UP if ActivePlayer_Lives is zero.
A8BF AND A
A8C0 JR Z,GameOver_Check_Player_2UP
A8C2 JP LevelInitialisation Jump to LevelInitialisation.
2UP path.
LoseLife_2UP A8C5 LD A,($5EA5) Jump to LoseLife_Loop if InactivePlayer_Lives is zero.
A8C8 AND A
A8C9 JR Z,LoseLife_Loop
A8CB LD A,($5EA1) Call GameOver_Check_Player_1UP if ActivePlayer_Lives is zero.
A8CE AND A
A8CF CALL Z,GameOver_Check_Player_1UP
A8D2 CALL ChangePlayer Call ChangePlayer.
A8D5 XOR A Write 00 to PlayerInactive.
A8D6 LD ($5E6C),A
A8D9 LD A,($5E65) Flip Flag_ActivePlayer to the other player.
A8DC CPL
A8DD LD ($5E65),A
A8E0 JP LevelInitialisation Jump to LevelInitialisation.
