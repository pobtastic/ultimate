43136: 1UP/ 2UP Swapper
Used by the routines at NewGame and LoseLife.
Swaps the active and inactive player data. Exchanges the four player-state bytes at ActivePlayer_Level and InactivePlayer_Level, 1024 bytes of terrain between TerrainData_Active and TerrainData_Inactive, and 288 bytes of actor slots between Rover_Slot and Actor_List_End.
This routine "swaps" the data between DE and HL.
These are all the player states.
