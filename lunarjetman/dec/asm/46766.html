46766: Move By Speed
Used by the routines at ClampObjectToGround, Handler_FallingSprite and FlyThrownBomb.
Add the actor's horizontal speed to its world X and return the Y plus thrust in A.
Input
IX Actor slot
Add the actor's horizontal speed to its world X.
MoveBySpeed 46766 LD A,(IX+5) Copy the actor speed into C.
46769 LD C,A
46770 LD B,0 Clear B.
46772 AND A Jump to MoveBySpeed_AddWorldX if the speed is positive.
46773 JP P,MoveBySpeed_AddWorldX
46776 DEC B Sign-extend the speed into BC.
Add the sign-extended speed to the world X and wrap it.
MoveBySpeed_AddWorldX 46777 LD L,(IX+2) Fetch the actor world X.
46780 LD H,(IX+3) Fetch the high byte.
46783 CALL MaskWorldX Call MaskWorldX.
46786 LD (IX+2),L Write the wrapped low byte back.
46789 LD (IX+3),H Write the wrapped high byte back.
Return the actor Y plus its thrust.
46792 LD A,(IX+4) Fetch the actor Y.
46795 ADD A,(IX+6) Add the actor thrust.
46798 RET Return.
