45957: Reset Alien States
Used by the routines at LevelNew and ShowLaserEstimate.
Writes 0 to every alien state to clear it.
ResetAlienStates 45957 LD HL,25008 HL=Alien_Slot_1.
There are six alien slots, each is 24 bytes long.
45960 LD B,144 Set a byte counter in B for 144 loops.
ResetAlienStates_Loop 45962 LD (HL),0 Write 0 to *HL.
45964 INC HL Increment HL by one.
45965 DJNZ ResetAlienStates_Loop Decrease the counter by one and jump back to ResetAlienStates_Loop until it is zero.
45967 RET Return.
