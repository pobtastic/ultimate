45115: Convert To Debris
Turn Jetman, if he is riding the rover, or else the parked rover into falling debris (type 224).
Convert Jetman or the rover into falling debris.
Handler_Missile_FallingDebris 45115 LD A,(24696) Fetch Jetman_Slot.
45118 SUB 18 Subtract 18.
45120 CP 8 Compare it against 8.
45122 LD BC,57348 Set B to the falling debris type 224 and C to 4. Loading BC keeps the flags from the compare.
45125 JR C,Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris Jump to Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris if Jetman is riding the rover.
When the rover is parked, turn it into falling debris.
45127 LD A,B Copy 224 into A.
45128 LD (24720),A Write it to Rover_Slot.
45131 LD A,C Copy 4 into A.
45132 LD (24728),A Write it to 24728.
45135 LD DE,24722 DE=Rover_Position.
45138 CALL FillDebrisSlots Call FillDebrisSlots.
45141 RET Return.
When Jetman is on the rover, turn Jetman into falling debris.
Handler_Missile_JetmanDebris 45142 LD A,B Copy 224 into A.
45143 LD (24696),A Write it to Jetman_Slot.
45146 LD A,C Copy 4 into A.
45147 LD (24704),A Write it to Jetman_Flags.
45150 LD DE,24698 DE=Jetman_Position.
45153 CALL FillDebrisSlots Call FillDebrisSlots.
45156 RET Return.
