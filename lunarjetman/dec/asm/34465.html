34465: Position Sprite
Used by the routines at PositionAndDrawSprite, DrawSprite and MaskSprite.
Convert the actor's world position into a screen column relative to Jetman_Position, then fetch its sprite.
Input
IX Actor slot
Convert the actor world X into a screen column.
PositionSprite 34465 LD L,(IX+2) Fetch the actor's world X position.
34468 LD H,(IX+3)
34471 LD BC,120 Add the 0120 draw offset.
34474 ADD HL,BC
34475 LD BC,(24698) Fetch the scroll position from Jetman_Position.
34479 AND A Subtract the scroll position.
34480 SBC HL,BC
34482 LD A,H Fetch the high byte of the screen column.
34483 AND %00011111 Keep only bits 0-4.
34485 JR NZ,ResetSpriteFields Jump to ResetSpriteFields if the actor is off-screen.
34487 LD A,L Jump to ResetSpriteFields if the column is at least 240.
34488 CP 240
34490 JR NC,ResetSpriteFields
Store the column and look up the actor sprite.
34492 LD (IX+1),L Store the screen column in *IX+1.
34495 CALL FetchActorSprite Call FetchActorSprite.
