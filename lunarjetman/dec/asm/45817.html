45817: Drop World Objects
Used by the routine at LevelNew.
Set bit 7 (falling) on world slots 1 and 2 unless they are carried, clear the low flag bits of slot 3, update the bomb flags and turn an occupied slot 4 into a platform.
Set bit 7 (falling) on world slot 1 unless bit 3 (carried) is set.

45820 BIT 3,A Jump to HideDistantObjects_Slot2Flags if bit 3 is set.
45822 JR NZ,HideDistantObjects_Slot2Flags

45826 LD (24752),A Write it back.
Do the same for world slot 2.

Clear bits 0-1 of the flags of world slot 3.
