35560: Clear Mask Scratch
Used by the routines at Handler_PlayerShot and Handler_SmallExplosion.
Zero SpriteRowsLeft and SpriteHeight, reload the actor's cached mask sprite with ReloadMaskCache and carry on drawing at MaskSprite.
