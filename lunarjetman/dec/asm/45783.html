45783: Kill Jetman
Turn Jetman into the dying type (220) and bounce him away, then continue into HideDistantObjects.

IX Actor slot

45786 CALL QueueTune Call QueueTune.

45792 LD (HL),220 Write 220 as Jetman's type.
