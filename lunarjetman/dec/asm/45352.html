45352: Handler: Alien Base Plasma
Handle the alien base plasma (types 144-151). Jump to Handler_Missile_ExplodeObjects when it is close enough to its target (Handler_Missile_TestJetmanRange). Otherwise kill Jetman if he is close (TickThenExplode_KillJetman), try to spawn a linked 200 object (SpawnLinkedObject), step the frame through 144-151 and colour the sprite.
