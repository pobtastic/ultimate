45265: Test Target Range
Used by the routines at Handler_SidewaysMissile and Handler_AlienBasePlasma.
Return carry if this actor is close enough to its target: Jetman when he is riding the rover, otherwise the parked rover. The world X difference is scaled by 8 and must be less than 0192 and the target must be 0-31 below the actor.

IX Actor slot
