36705: Clear Jetman Thrust
Stop vertical thrust, leave Jetman's Y position unchanged and go on to spend fuel.
Input
IX Actor slot
Stop vertical thrust and skip the Y position update.
ClearJetmanThrust 36705 LD (IX+6),0 Clear the vertical thrust.
B is the fuel-spend base added at Handler_JetmanMovement_FuelFromSpeed. 16 spends extra fuel; the gravity path uses 0.
36709 LD B,16 Set the fuel-spend base to 16.
36711 JP Handler_JetmanMovement_FuelFromSpeed Jump to Handler_JetmanMovement_FuelFromSpeed.
