44342: Test Actor Side
Used by the routines at Handler_RoverFrames, Handler_MissileLauncher and Handler_SidewaysMissile.
Test whether this actor is to the left of Jetman. The world wraps at 8192 so carry is set when Jetman's world X (Jetman_Position) is less than 4096 ahead of the actor.
Input
IX Actor slot
Handler_Missile 44342 LD HL,(24698) Fetch Jetman_Position.
This entry point is used by the routine at AlignToRover.
Handler_Missile_FetchActorX 44345 LD C,(IX+2) Fetch the actor world X.
44348 LD B,(IX+3) Fetch the high byte.
This entry point is used by the routines at InitialiseJetman and Handler_Bombs.
Return carry if HL is ahead of BC (to its right) by less than 4096 (allowing for the wrap at 8192).
