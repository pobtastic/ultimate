42812: Start Small Explosion
Used by the routines at Handler_ThePip, Handler_PlayerShot, ScoreSmallHit and TickThenExplode.
Turn the actor into the first frame of a small explosion (type 100) and pick its colour. Handler_SmallExplosion animates it from there.

""Small Explosion: Frame 1"".
