45730: Test Jetman Range
Used by the routines at PlaceSpawnedAlien, Handler_ThePip, Handler_UpwardMissile, Handler_SidewaysMissile, Handler_AlienBasePlasma and TickThenExplode.
Return carry if this actor is touching Jetman. This entry uses a vertical range of 14; TickThenExplode_TestJetman takes the range in E. Carry is clear while the delay timer at GameDelayTimer is running or Jetman's type is 17 or above.
Input
IX Actor slot
TickThenExplode_CloseRange 45730 LD E,14 E=14.
This entry point is used by the routines at Handler_MissileLauncher, Handler_AlienBase and Handler_MissileSpawner.
