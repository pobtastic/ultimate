49115: Draw Flipped Shifted Columns
Draws the columns of a sprite which is flipped and shifted. There is a piece of code for each of up to five columns of the sprite, and the code for the width of the sprite is where the jump at SpriteJumpOpcode starts. Each piece puts a column into two bytes of the buffer, using the table for the shift to turn the mask and the pixels round and split them in two. It works from right to left.
The jump at SpriteJumpOpcode starts here for five columns.
DrawFlippedShiftedColumns 49115 CPL Invert the screen byte.
49116 POP DE Restore the mask into E and the pixels into D from the stack.
49117 LD L,E Clear the screen bits under the part of the mask which is in this byte.
49118 OR (HL)
49119 CPL
49120 LD L,D Add the part of the pixels which is in this byte.
49121 OR (HL)
49122 LD (BC),A Store the byte and move on to the byte on the left.
49123 DEC BC
49124 DEC H Point H at the table for the bits which move into the next byte, and fetch that screen byte.

49126 LD A,(BC)
49127 CPL Clear the screen bits under the part of the mask which has moved into it.
49128 OR (HL)
49129 CPL
49130 LD L,D Add the part of the pixels which has moved into it.
49131 OR (HL)
49132 INC H Point H back at the first table.
