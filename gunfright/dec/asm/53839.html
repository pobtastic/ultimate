53839: Calculate Screen Address
Used by the routines at HandleMenuBullet and DrawMenuSprite.
Finds the address on the screen of a pixel. The vertical position is measured from the bottom of the screen.
Input
H The vertical position, from the bottom
L The horizontal position
Output
HL The address on the screen
Stash DE, and copy the position to it.
CalculateScreenAddress 53839 PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.
53840 EX DE,HL Copy the position to DE.
Work out the column from the horizontal position.
53841 LD A,E Divide the horizontal position by eight.
53842 RRCA
53843 RRCA
53844 RRCA
53845 AND %00011111 Keep the column.
53847 LD L,A Store it.

53848 LD A,D Invert the vertical position and multiply it by four.
53849 CPL
53850 RLCA
53851 RLCA
53852 AND %11100000 Keep the character row within the third.
53854 OR L Make the low byte of the address.

53857 CPL
