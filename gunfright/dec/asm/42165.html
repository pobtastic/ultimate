42165: Money Bag Template
The initial contents of a slot for a money bag. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
MoneyBagTemplate 42165 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0 Type, position, speed, size and counters, which CreateMoneyBag fills in.
42173 DEFB 16,16,0,0,0,0
42179 DEFW 42181 The sprite list of a money bag.
42181 DEFB 248,4,41,128 The sprite list: sprite 41 at offset (248, 4), then the terminator. .
