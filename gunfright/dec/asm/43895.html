43895: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Shows the title screen, which has three revolvers on the left and the game name at the top. A bullet shoots out of each revolver and leaves a line of text behind it: the number of players, the controls, and "START GAME". Keys 1 and 2 change the first two and fire their bullet again, and key 3 starts the game. It returns when the bullet for key 3 has been fired.
