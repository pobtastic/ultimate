46112: Create Outlaw
Used by the routine at CreateTownObjects.
Called every frame in the town. Puts the outlaw (an object of type 10 or 22) in a random cell of the town in the fifth object slot if the slot is free and the cell is not blocked. The outlaw is made faster, and given type 22, if a random number is less than sixteen times the level (the level is reduced by four from level sixteen).
