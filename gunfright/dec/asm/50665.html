50665: Clip Wall Strip Top
Used by the routine at DrawWallStrip.
Skips the rows of a wall tile that are below PlayfieldBuffer. A is the number of those rows, negated. It starts drawing at 72 and jumps to DrawWallStrip_Rows.
Input
A The number of rows below the buffer, negated
HL The address of the image
Skip the rows that are below the buffer.

50672 RET Z
