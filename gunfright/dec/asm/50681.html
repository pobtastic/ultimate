50681: Draw Wall Strip
Used by the routine at DrawWallStrips.
Draws a wall tile, sixteen pixels wide, into PlayfieldBuffer. The image has the same format as DrawStripGraphic, but it replaces the buffer. The masks from WallTileMasks are written into the AND instructions. The image is flipped if FlipFlag is not zero.
Input
D The vertical position of the bottom of the tile
E The horizontal position of the tile
IY The address of the tile number
