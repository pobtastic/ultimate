50327: Clip Edge Strip Top
Used by the routine at DrawStripGraphic.
Skips the rows of an image that are below PlayfieldBuffer. A is the number of those rows, negated. It starts drawing at 72 and jumps to DrawStripGraphic_Draw.
Input
A The number of rows below the buffer, negated
HL The address of the image
Skip the rows that are below the buffer.
