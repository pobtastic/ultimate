43221: Handle Tumbleweed
Type 23: a tumbleweed rolling about next to the sheriff. It follows one of two routes, stepping through the route one byte at a time; the high nibble of each byte is the vertical speed and the low nibble the horizontal speed, both signed. Bits 6 and 7 of byte 5 mirror the route, and they change every time a route ends, so the tumbleweed wanders in a different way each time. It spins by flipping its sprite every frame, and it hurts the sheriff if they touch. It goes away when the sheriff is two cells away.
Disappear if the sheriff is more than two cells away.
HandleTumbleweed 43221 LD C,2 Set the distance to two cells.
43223 CALL CheckDistance Check how close the sheriff is.

Work out the vertical speed from the high nibble.

43256 SRA A

43270 LD (41493),A Store it as the vertical speed.
Work out the horizontal speed from the low nibble.
43273 LD A,(HL) Fetch the step again.

43277 BIT 3,C Jump to HandleTumbleweed_TestHorizontal unless the nibble is negative.

43287 JR Z,HandleTumbleweed_DoubleHorizontal
43289 NEG Negate the speed to mirror the route.
HandleTumbleweed_DoubleHorizontal 43291 SLA A Double the speed.
43293 LD (41492),A Store it as the horizontal speed.
Move the tumbleweed.
43296 CALL CheckWallsEachWay Set the direction the tumbleweed is facing from its speed.

43305 XOR %01000000 Flip the sprite horizontally.
43307 LD (IX+7),A Store the flags.
43310 CALL CheckSheriffTouch Check whether the tumbleweed is touching the sheriff.
43313 JP C,KillSheriff Hurt the sheriff if it is.
43316 RET Return.
The route has ended, so choose the next one.

43322 LD (IX+10),A Choose the route with it.
43325 LD (IX+11),0 Write 0 to byte 11 to go back to the first step.
