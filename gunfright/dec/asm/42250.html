42250: Check Top Score
Used by the routine at CheckSheriffLost.
Called when the last life of the last player has been lost. It works out which player scored most, and if that score beats the top score of the day it shows the "WELL DONE" screen and stores the new top score. Either way the game then goes back to the title screen.
The player states are swapped first so that the first player's score is in the current position.
