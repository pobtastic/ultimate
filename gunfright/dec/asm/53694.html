53694: Read View Key
Used by the routines at ReadInterface2Joystick, ReadKempstonJoystick, ReadCursorJoystick and ReadKeyboard.
Sets bit 5 of the controls if Z or SYMBOL SHIFT is pressed, then makes a copy in B in which left and right are never both set, and bits 2 and 3 are never both set.
Input
C The controls
Output
B The controls, with bits 0 and 1, and 2 and 3, made exclusive
C The controls
Read the key which rotates the view.
