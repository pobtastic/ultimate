54055: Draw Side Pictures
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Draws a block of tiles of 4 by 10 from SidePictureTiles, on each side of the lower part of the screen of any stage which is not the town. The block at the left is drawn from right to left so that it is a reflection of the block at the right.
Return if this is the town.
