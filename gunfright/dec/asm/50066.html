50066: Draw Cell
Used by the routine at DrawTown.
Draws the outline of a cell of the town, using the lists of tiles in the table at TileListPointers. There are two lists for each kind of cell: one for the edges at the bottom, drawn from the left corner, and one for the edges at the top, drawn from the right corner. Each list is drawn as two runs of eight tiles which are 16 pixels wide, using the first byte of each pair of bytes in the list, in the shape of a V for the bottom and an upside-down V for the top. When the view is rotated the two lists change places.
