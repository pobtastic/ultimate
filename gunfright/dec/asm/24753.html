24753: Drawing Orders
The orders in which the cells around the sheriff are drawn by DrawTown, as thirty-two entries of five bytes. The entry is chosen by which of five of the cells next to the sheriff have anything in them. Each byte is the offset of a cell and the kind of drawing which is done in it.
