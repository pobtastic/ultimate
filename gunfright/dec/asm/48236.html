48236: Choose Outlaw Body
Used by the routine at HandleWaitingOutlaw.
Chooses which of the two sprite lists for the body of the outlaw to draw. He takes a step every fourth frame while he is moving.
Input
IX The address of the outlaw
Output
DE The address of the sprite list
Take a step every fourth frame while he is moving.
ChooseOutlawBody 48236 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the horizontal speed.
48239 OR (IX+3) Combine it with the vertical speed.
48242 JR Z,ChooseOutlawBody_Step Jump if he is not moving.
48244 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the number of moves left.
48247 OR (IX+11) Combine it with the timer.
48250 JR Z,ChooseOutlawBody_Step Jump if both are zero.
48252 LD A,(41494) Fetch the frame counter.
48255 AND %00000011 Keep a number from 0 to 3.
48257 JR NZ,ChooseOutlawBody_Step Jump unless it is every fourth frame.
48259 INC (IX+14) Take a step.
Choose the sprite list for the step.
ChooseOutlawBody_Step 48262 LD A,(IX+14) Fetch the step.
48265 AND %00000001 Keep the lowest bit.
48267 LD DE,46506 Point DE at BodyImageNumbers.
48270 RET Z Return if the step is even.
48271 LD DE,46509 Point DE at BodyImageNumbers_SecondStep.
48274 RET Return.
