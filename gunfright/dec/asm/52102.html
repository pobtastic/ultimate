52102: Turn Sheriff
Used by the routine at HandleSheriff.
Turns the sheriff to face the way the controls say. Bits 6 and 7 are the heading, and bits 0 to 2 count down a turn. Left and right turn him a quarter when the controls are the keyboard or bit 3 of Options is clear. Otherwise he turns a quarter at a time towards the direction that is pressed, and bit 2 is set when he faces it.
Input
E The controls
IX The address of the sheriff
Output
E The controls
Jump to SetFacingFlags while a turn is still counting.
