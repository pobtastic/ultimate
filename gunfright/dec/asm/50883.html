50883: Wall Tile Masks
The four masks used by DrawWallStrip to keep the bits of the buffer on the left of a wall tile. The index is the shift to the right in twos of pixels: 0, 2, 4 or 6.
WallTileMasks 50883 DEFB 0 Shift 0: 0.
50884 DEFB 192 Shift 2: 192.
50885 DEFB 240 Shift 4: 240.
50886 DEFB 252 Shift 6: 252.
