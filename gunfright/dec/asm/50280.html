50280: Draw Strip
Used by the routines at DrawCell and DrawStripsLeftToRight.
Draws one tile of the edge of a cell of the town, if its position is inside the area where the town is drawn, which is from 17 to 207 horizontally and below 256 vertically. The tile number comes from the list at IY, and is changed to the number of an image by a table at TileImageNumbers and then by the swap flag.
Input
IY The address of the tile in its list
C One to swap the image for the other of its pair
Return if the tile is outside the play area horizontally.
