47454: Muzzle Flash Template
The initial contents of a slot for a muzzle flash (type 15). The final two bytes point to the sprite list, whose sprite number is set by HandleMuzzleFlash.
MuzzleFlashTemplate 47454 DEFB 15,0,0,0,0,0,0,0 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
47462 DEFB 0,0,0,0,0,0
47468 DEFW 47470 The sprite list of the flash.
47470 DEFB 0,0,0,128 The sprite list.
