50507: Draw Cell With Walls
Used by the routine at DrawTown.
Draws the walls of a building in a cell of the town, using the lists of tiles in TileListPointers. The list is chosen from the code of the cell and ViewRotation. Nothing is drawn if the cell is empty. A list holds sixteen steps of two tiles each, one above the other. They are drawn in two runs of eight, going down and to the right and then up and to the right, with the second run flipped by FlipFlag, to make the two walls which can be seen. Each step is drawn by DrawWallStrips, and nothing more is drawn when all of the bits in ColumnFlags are set.
