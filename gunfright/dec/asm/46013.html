46013: Create Town Objects
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Called every frame. In the town stage it updates the prices and makes new objects if there are free slots: bystanders, the outlaw, the pointer, money bags, cacti, the horse and the tumbleweed.
Return unless this is the town stage.
CreateTownObjects 46013 LD A,(41575) Return if this is a stage outside the town, which is the duel or the bonus stage.
