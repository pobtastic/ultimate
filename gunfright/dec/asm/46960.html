46960: Shoot Bystander
Used by the routine at HandleBystander.
Turns the bullet which has hit the object into a puff of smoke, and then goes on to take the fine for shooting a bystander.
Input
IY The address of the bullet
ShootBystander 46960 LD (IY+0),7 Turn the bullet into a puff of smoke.
46964 LD (IY+12),2 Set the length of the puff to two frames.
Continue on to TakeFine.
