43393: Create Horse
Used by the routine at CreateTownObjects.
Called every frame in town. While the sheriff is on foot it puts a horse (an object of type 20) in the eighth object slot, two cells from the sheriff, if the slot is free.
While the sheriff is on foot, use the eighth slot if it is free.
