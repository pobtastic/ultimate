53323: Copy Buffer To Screen
Used by the routine at CopyBufferAndClear.
Copies PlayfieldBuffer to the screen, from the bottom row up. Each row copies 22 bytes. In the town the copy sits beside the panel.
Keep the stack pointer, and point it at the playfield on the screen.
