54100: Add To Bounty
Used by the routines at HandleMoneyBag, SetUpPlayer, GiveReward, HandleBag and ReloadRevolver.
Adds an amount to Bounty, as SubtractFromBounty subtracts from it. It also writes 0 to BulletUnpaidFlag, so the revolver may be loaded again.
Input
BC The amount, as four packed decimal digits
Allow the revolver to be loaded.
