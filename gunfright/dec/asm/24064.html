24064: Start Game
Used by the routine at DecryptGame.
The first instruction of the game once it has been moved down into place by DecryptGame. Interrupts are disabled for the whole game, which polls the keyboard and times everything by the speed of its own code.
GameStart 24064 DI Disable interrupts.
24065 JP InitialiseGame Jump to InitialiseGame to start the game.
