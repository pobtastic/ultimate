44067: Messaging: Title Text
The copyright message and the game name. Each is an attribute byte, the characters and a zero terminator. The 91 in the copyright message is the copyright symbol of the font, and the 92 bytes are full stops.

44075 DEFM "A" "A".

44078 DEFB 92 Full stop.
44079 DEFM "G" "G".
44080 DEFB 92 Full stop.
44081 DEFB 0 Terminator.
