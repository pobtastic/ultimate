50587: Draw Wall Strips
Used by the routine at DrawCellWithWalls.
Draws two wall tiles, one above the other, for a step along the edge of a cell. A bit in ColumnFlags is set to record that the column has a wall in it, and if it already has one the step is skipped. The horizontal position in ScreenHorizontalPosition chooses the bit, and is written into the BIT instruction at DrawWallStrips_Test and the SET instruction at 50623. The lower tile is drawn at ScreenVerticalPosition by DrawWallStrip, and the upper tile 64 above it. The step is skipped if either position is off the screen. It always moves on to the next two tiles of the list.
Input
BC The count of steps in B
IY The address of the first tile of the step in its list
Return unless the column is on the screen.
