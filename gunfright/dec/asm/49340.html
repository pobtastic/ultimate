49340: Sheriff Template
The initial contents of a slot for the sheriff when he comes back after losing a life. It is an object of type 25 which waits sixteen frames before the sheriff arrives, at the place where he starts. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
SheriffTemplate 49340 DEFB 25,136,4,240,4,0,128,0 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
49348 DEFB 16,16,0,0,16,0
49354 DEFW BystanderSpriteLists_DeadFigure The sprite list, which is a dead figure.
