50159: Draw Strips Left To Right
Used by the routine at DrawCell.
Draws a list of sixteen tiles for the bottom edges of a cell, going down and to the right for the first eight and up and to the right for the second eight. The second eight are drawn flipped.
Input
A The number of the list in the table at TileListPointers

50166 POP IY

50168 XOR A Write 0 to *FlipFlag.
50169 LD (41554),A
50172 LD BC,2048 Set a count of eight tiles, without swapping any.
