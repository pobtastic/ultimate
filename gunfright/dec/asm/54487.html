54487: Print Text
Used by the routines at GameMenu, DrawBounty and DrawTelegramFrame.
Prints a text from Font, one tile for each character. The text starts with an attribute and ends with 0.
Input
DE The address of the attribute and the text
HL The screen address
Fetch the attribute.
