44834: Play Buzz
Used by the routine at ReloadRevolver.
Plays a short, rough buzz made of twelve pulses of different lengths.
PlayBuzz 44834 LD C,12 Set the number of pulses.
PlayBuzz_Loop 44836 LD A,C Fetch the number of the pulse.
44837 XOR %10100101 Change it.
44839 ADD A,C Add it to the number of the pulse.
44840 LD B,A Use it as the length of the pulse.
44841 CALL PlaySquareWave Play the pulse.
44844 DEC C Count down the pulses.
44845 JR NZ,PlayBuzz_Loop Loop back until all of the pulses have been played.
44847 RET Return.
