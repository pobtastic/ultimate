49225: Check Sheriff Lost
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Called every frame. If ObjectSlot_01, which holds the sheriff, and ObjectSlot_15, which holds the crosshair, are both empty, then a stage is starting or the sheriff has been lost. The routine takes a life away. When there is no life left it shows "GAME OVER" and works out the top score, or if there are two players it moves on to the other player. Otherwise it makes the sheriff arrive in the town, or the crosshair in the duel and the bonus stage.
