47755: Check For Early Draw
Used by the routine at HandleWaitingOutlaw.
Reads the controls while the outlaw is waiting. If the player moves the crosshair or fires, the outlaw is provoked and starts to draw at once, and "DRAW!!" appears in yellow, if it is not already on the screen.
