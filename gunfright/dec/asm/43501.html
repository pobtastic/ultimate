43501: Create Cactus
Used by the routine at CreateTownObjects.
Called every frame in town. Cacti stand in eight fixed cells of the town (see CactusPositions). When the sheriff comes within two cells of one, and the seventh object slot is free, the routine puts a cactus (an object of type 19) in the slot.
Return if the seventh slot is in use.
