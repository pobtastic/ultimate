49400: Draw Town
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Called every frame. In the town, it draws the walls and the objects of the cells which are around the sheriff, in an order which is chosen so that the cells nearer the viewer are drawn after the ones behind them. The order depends on which of five of the cells next to the sheriff's cell have buildings in them, and is taken from five-byte entries in a table at DrawingOrders. Each of the five bytes holds the offset of a cell from the sheriff's cell (bits 4 and 5 for the offset in the column, bits 6 and 7 for the offset in the row) and a kind of drawing to do in it (bits 0 and 1): 0 draws the objects in the cell, 1 draws the walls and 2 draws the edges. The sheriff's cell and three of its neighbours are drawn last. The town is a map of sixteen cells by sixteen, and when the view is rotated the cell numbers are turned round.
Return if this is not the town.
