51575: Reload Revolver
Used by the routines at HandleCrosshair and HandleSheriff.
Loads one bullet on every even frame while FireTimer is zero and BulletUnpaidFlag is clear. Reloading starts when BulletsLoaded is empty and ReloadingFlag is then set until the cylinder holds six. Each bullet takes BulletPrice from Bounty. If the bounty cannot pay, BulletUnpaidFlag is set.
Return unless it is time to load a bullet.
