44674: Wait For A While
Used by the routines at CheckTopScore, ShowPlayerNumber and GiveReward.
Waits by calling Wait several times. HL is zero after the first call, so every call after the first counts down 65536 times.
Input
B The number of delays
HL The length of the first delay
WaitForAWhile 44674 CALL Wait Count down.
44677 DJNZ WaitForAWhile Loop back until the delays are over.
44679 RET Return.
