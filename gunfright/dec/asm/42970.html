42970: Type Telegram
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Called every frame in town. It makes the lines of the telegram appear one letter at a time, like a telegram being typed. Each line has a counter which is 14 once the line is complete. The first line which is not complete shows a blinking cursor on alternate frames, and on the other frames prints its next letter and clicks.
