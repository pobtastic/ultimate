54183: Draw Town Panel
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Draws the panel at the left of the screen of the town, which is 8 tiles wide and 18 high and has the wanted poster in it. The panel is drawn from pairs of bytes, which are the attribute and the tile, at PanelTiles. Then it prints the name of the outlaw, draws the picture of his face from four images, one above another, and draws the reward. This does nothing when TownOffset is zero.
Return if this is not the town.
