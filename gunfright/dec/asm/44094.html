44094: Initial Menu Entries
The initial state of the four menu entries, which is copied to MenuEntries. Each entry has four bytes: its state, the horizontal position of its bullet, the vertical position of its bullet and a counter. The first entry reads the keys, and the other three are the revolvers.
InitialMenuEntries 44094 DEFB 5,0,0,0 Entry for the keys: state 5.
44098 DEFB 0,0,152,0 Entry for the first revolver.
44102 DEFB 0,0,96,0 Entry for the second revolver.
44106 DEFB 0,0,40,0 Entry for the third revolver.
