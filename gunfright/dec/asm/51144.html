51144: Remove All Objects
Used by the routines at FinishTownStage, EndDuel, GiveReward, CheckSheriffLost and ReturnToTown.
Jumps to FillMemory to fill 240 bytes from ObjectSlot_01 with zero, which empties all fifteen object slots.
Set the address and the count.
RemoveAllObjects 51144 LD HL,41597 Point HL at ObjectSlot_01.
