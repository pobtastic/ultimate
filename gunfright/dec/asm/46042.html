46042: Handle Outlaw
Types 10 and 22: the outlaw who is wanted at this level. A bullet hit sets OutlawShotFlag to one, which makes FinishTownStage end the stage. Otherwise the outlaw wanders about the town: if he touches the sheriff, the sheriff dies. Type 22 is made by CreateOutlaw instead of type 10 when the outlaw is to be a little faster.
Check whether a bullet has hit the outlaw.
HandleOutlaw 46042 CALL CheckBulletHit Check whether a bullet has hit the outlaw.

46047 LD A,1 Set OutlawShotFlag.
46049 LD (41482),A
46052 JR HandleOutlaw_Draw Jump to HandleOutlaw_Draw to draw the outlaw.
