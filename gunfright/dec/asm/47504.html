47504: Make Bullet Hole
Used by the routine at HandleMuzzleFlash.
Puts a bullet hole (an object of type 16) in a random place on the screen, in the first free slot of the first thirteen, which are searched downwards from the thirteenth. It makes a burst of noise and removes the muzzle flash.
