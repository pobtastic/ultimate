50908: Project Cell
Used by the routines at DrawScenery, DrawCell and DrawCellWithWalls.
Works out where the corner of the cell last set by GetMapCell is on the screen. With the distances from the sheriff as dx and dy, the horizontal position is (dx + dy + 240) / 2 and the vertical position is (dy - dx + 432) / 4. They are put in ScreenHorizontalPosition and ScreenVerticalPosition. When bit 0 of ViewRotation is set, the sheriff's position is turned round.
