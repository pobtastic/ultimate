41559: Bounty
The bounty of the current player, as three bytes of packed decimal digits with the most significant byte first. This is the start of the nineteen bytes of state for the current player.
Bounty 41559 DEFB 255,192 The two most significant bytes.
Bounty_LastByte 41561 DEFB 0 The least significant byte.
