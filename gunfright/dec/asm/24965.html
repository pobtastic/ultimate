24965: Wall Rectangle Lists
The lists of rectangles of walls, which are used to stop objects from walking through the walls. Each rectangle is four bytes: the horizontal and vertical positions of its middle, then its half width and half height. A zero byte ends a list. The middle of a cell is 128 a full side has a half length of 64 and a thin wall has a half length of 2. A gap in a side is a doorway.
