42453: Update Prices
Used by the routine at CreateTownObjects.
Called every frame, but only does anything when the low byte of the frame counter is 189 so once every 256 frames. It picks one of the three amounts on the telegram at random: the fine, the price of a horse or the price of a bullet. If the telegram has finished typing it chooses a new price from a table and retypes that line. The higher the level, the more prices the table offers, so prices tend to rise as the game goes on.
