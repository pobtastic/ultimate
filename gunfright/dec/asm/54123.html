54123: Subtract From Bounty
Used by the routines at ReloadRevolver and TakeFromBounty.
Subtracts an amount from Bounty, as AddToBounty adds to it.
Input
BC The amount, as four packed decimal digits
Output
F The carry flag is set if the amount is more than the bounty
Subtract the lowest two digits.
