43455: Handle Horse
Type 20: a horse waiting in the street. It goes away if the sheriff is three cells away. If the sheriff touches it, the horse is removed, the sheriff turns into a mounted sheriff (type 21) who has a higher top speed, and the price of the horse is taken from the bounty.
HandleHorse 43455 LD C,3 Set the distance to three cells.
43457 CALL CheckDistance Check how close the sheriff is.
43460 JP NC,HandleBody_Remove Remove the horse if the sheriff has moved away.
43463 CALL CheckSheriffTouch Check whether the sheriff is touching the horse.
43466 RET NC Return if not.
43467 LD A,8 Write 8 to SoundNotesLeft so that the sound lasts eight notes.
43469 LD (41532),A
43472 LD HL,44735 Point SoundNoteAddress at the notes of the horse sound.
43475 LD (41533),HL
43478 LD (IX+0),0 Remove the horse.
43482 LD A,18 Raise the top speed of the sheriff to 18.
43484 LD (41503),A
43487 LD (IY+0),21 Turn the sheriff into a mounted sheriff.
43491 LD BC,(41564) Fetch the price of a horse.
43495 CALL TakeFromBounty Take it from the bounty.
43498 JP DrawBountyDigits Jump to DrawBountyDigits.
