5FB1: Town Map
The map of the town, as sixteen rows of sixteen cells. The code of each cell says what is in it. Zero is empty ground, one is the edge of the town, and the codes from two to 19 are buildings and other things. The codes choose the lists of walls at WallRectanglePointers and the lists of tiles at TileListPointers.
