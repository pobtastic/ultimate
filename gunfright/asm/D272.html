D272: Add Town Offset To HL
Used by the routines at ReloadRevolver, FireRevolver, FlashChamber, CopyBufferToScreen, DrawTelegramFrame, DrawRevolverDisplay and DrawBorder.
Adds TownOffset to a screen address, so a layout drawn at the left of the screen sits beside the town panel.
Input
HL The screen address
Output
HL The new address
AddTownOffsetToHL D272 LD A,($A267) Fetch TownOffset.
