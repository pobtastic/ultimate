CC97: Move Player Forward
Used by the routine at SlowPlayerDown.
Sets the speed and moves the sheriff one step in the direction of his heading, stopping at walls. A step plays PlayFootstepSound and PlayMovementSound, and moves on to the next walking frame.
Input
A The speed
IX The address of the sheriff
Set the speed and check the walls. Keep the flags from before the check for PlayFootstepSound.
MovePlayerForward CC97 LD (IX+$05),A Set the speed.
CC9A CALL SetSpeedsFromHeading Call SetSpeedsFromHeading.
CC9D LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the flags.
CCA0 PUSH AF Stash the flags on the stack.
CCA1 CALL TestMove Call TestMove.
CCA4 POP AF Restore the flags from the stack.
Play the sounds and move the sheriff.
CCA5 CALL PlayFootstepSound Call PlayFootstepSound.
CCA8 CALL MoveObject Call MoveObject.
CCAB CALL PlayMovementSound Call PlayMovementSound.
Continue on to NextSpriteNumber.
