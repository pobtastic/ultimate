BACA: Messaging: Draw Text
The two "DRAW!!" messages, each of which is an attribute byte (red, then yellow), characters and a zero byte. Each "!" is the character code 5D which is why they are not text.
DrawMessages BACA DEFB $42 Attribute: INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BACB DEFM "DRAW" "DRAW"
BACF DEFB $5D,$5D Two exclamation marks.
BAD1 DEFB $00 Terminator.
DrawMessages_Yellow BAD2 DEFB $46 Attribute: INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAD3 DEFM "DRAW" "DRAW"
BAD7 DEFB $5D,$5D Two exclamation marks.
BAD9 DEFB $00 Terminator.
