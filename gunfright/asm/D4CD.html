D4CD: Telegram Text
The word which is printed in the frame of the telegram, which starts with its attribute and ends with zero.
TelegramText D4CD DEFB $4F Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLUE (BRIGHT).
D4CE DEFM "TELEGRAM" "TELEGRAM".
D4D6 DEFB $00 The end of the text.
