D27C: Draw Lives
Used by the routines at CheckSheriffLost and DrawGameScreen.
Draws 04 sheriff hats from LifeIconTiles in the lower left of the town screen. The hats for the lives in Lives are in INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT), and the hats for the lives which have been lost are in black. This does nothing when TownOffset is zero.
