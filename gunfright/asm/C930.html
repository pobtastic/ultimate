C930: Handle Sheriff
Used by the routine at HandleMountedSheriff.
Type 01: the sheriff in the town. It counts down ProtectionTimer, calls ReloadRevolver, reads the controls, then turns, moves and fires. The sprites are written to SheriffSpriteList.
