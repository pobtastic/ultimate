AC23: Messaging: Title Text
The copyright message and the game name. Each is an attribute byte, the characters and a zero terminator. The 5B in the copyright message is the copyright symbol of the font, and the 5C bytes are full stops.
TitleTexts AC23 DEFB $47 Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
AC24 DEFB $5B Copyright symbol.
AC25 DEFM " 1985 " " 1985 ".
AC2B DEFM "A" "A".
AC2C DEFB $5C Full stop.
AC2D DEFM "C" "C".
AC2E DEFB $5C Full stop.
AC2F DEFM "G" "G".
AC30 DEFB $5C Full stop.
AC31 DEFB $00 Terminator.
TitleTexts_GameName AC32 DEFB $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
AC33 DEFM "GUN FRIGHT" "GUN FRIGHT".
AC3D DEFB $00 Terminator.
