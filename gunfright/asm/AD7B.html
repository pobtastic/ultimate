AD7B: Number Of Players Texts
The table of texts for the number of players, followed by the texts. Bit 7 of the last character of each text is set. The characters with bit 7 set are the final 45 ("E") of each text.
NumberOfPlayersTexts AD7B DEFW $AD7F The addresses of the texts.
AD7D DEFW $AD8E
AD7F DEFM "ONE PLAYER GAM","E"+$80 "ONE PLAYER GAME".
AD8E DEFM "TWO PLAYER GAM","E"+$80 "TWO PLAYER GAME".
