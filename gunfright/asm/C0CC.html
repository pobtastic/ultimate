C0CC: Advance Frame Counter
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Adds one to the frame counter, and then updates the random numbers.
AdvanceFrameCounter C0CC LD HL,($A216) Fetch the frame counter.
C0CF INC HL Add one.
C0D0 LD ($A216),HL Store the new frame counter.
Continue on to UpdateRandomNumbers.
