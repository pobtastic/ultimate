C4A7: Draw Strip Graphic
Used by the routines at DrawScenery and DrawStrip.
Draws an image which is sixteen pixels wide into PlayfieldBuffer, combining it with what is already there. The image starts with a byte for the number of rows, followed by two bytes for each row. It is drawn from the bottom row up, and is cut off at the bottom and top of the buffer. The position is in ScreenHorizontalPosition and the image is moved to the right by 00, 02, 04 or 06 pixels, using ShiftTables and ReversedShiftTables. The image is flipped if FlipFlag is not zero.
Input
D The vertical position of the bottom of the image
E The horizontal position of the image
HL The address of the image
