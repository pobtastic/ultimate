AE88: Wait
Used by the routines at WaitForAWhile and ShowGameOver.
Waits by counting a register pair down to zero.
Input
HL The count
Wait AE88 DEC HL Subtract one from the count.
AE89 LD A,H Jump to Wait if the count is not zero.
AE8A OR L
AE8B JR NZ,Wait
AE8D RET Return.
