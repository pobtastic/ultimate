B7DA: Messaging: Game Over Text
The two messages of the game over screen. Each is an attribute byte, the characters and a zero terminator.
GameOverTexts B7DA DEFB $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
B7DB DEFM "GAME OVER" "GAME OVER".
B7E5 DEFB $00 Terminator.
GameOverTexts_Player B7E6 DEFB $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
B7E7 DEFM "PLAYER " "PLAYER ".
B7EE DEFB $3F The number of the player, written by ShowGameOver.
B7EF DEFB $00 Terminator.
