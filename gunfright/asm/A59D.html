A59D: Messaging: Score Text
The messages shown when the top score has been beaten. Each is an attribute byte, the characters and a zero terminator. The 5E in the last message is the dollar sign of the font, and the score digits are written over the spaces after it.
ScoreMessages A59D DEFB $43 Attribute: INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A59E DEFM "WELL DONE" "WELL DONE".
A5A7 DEFB $00 Terminator.
ScoreMessages_Player A5A8 DEFB $44 Attribute: INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A5A9 DEFM "PLAYER " "PLAYER ".
A5B0 DEFB $20 The number of the winning player, written by CheckTopScore_Winner. It is a space until then.
A5B1 DEFB $00 Terminator.
ScoreMessages_TodaysTop A5B2 DEFB $46 Attribute: INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A5B3 DEFM "TODAYS TOP" "TODAYS TOP".
A5BD DEFB $00 Terminator.
ScoreMessages_ScoreIs A5BE DEFB $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A5BF DEFM "SCORE IS" "SCORE IS".
A5C7 DEFB $00 Terminator.
ScoreMessages_Score A5C8 DEFB $47 Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
A5C9 DEFB $5E The dollar sign.
A5CA DEFM " " A space.
ScoreMessages_ScoreDigits A5CB DEFM " " Room for the six digits of the score.
A5D1 DEFB $00 Terminator.
