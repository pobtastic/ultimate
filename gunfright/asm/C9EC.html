C9EC: Fire Revolver
Used by the routines at HandleCrosshair and HandleSheriff.
Fires the revolver if the fire control is pressed, BulletsLoaded is not zero, and neither ReloadingFlag nor FireHeldFlag is set. It calls MakeShot, takes one from BulletsLoaded, and colours the chamber unless there were six. FireTimer and FireHeldFlag are then set.
Input
E The controls
IX The address of the object which fires
Return unless a new shot can be made.
