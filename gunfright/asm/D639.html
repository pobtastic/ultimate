D639: Draw Tile
Used by the routines at PrintTelegram, TypeTelegram, DrawTownPanel, DrawTelegramFrame, PrintText, DrawTileBlock, DrawTileBlockReflected, DrawBorder, DrawTileColumn and DrawTileRow.
Draws a tile of eight by eight pixels and sets its attribute.
Input
A The number of the tile, with bits 6 and 7 which say how to reflect it
BC The address of the tile set
HL The screen address
A' The attribute
DrawTile D639 CALL DrawTilePixels Draw the tile.
