AF5F: Play Wave Sequence
Used by the routines at PlaySoundEffect, PlayMovementSound, PlayRumble, PlayTypingSound, PlayPauseSound and PlayShortTone.
Plays a tone.
Input
B The length of a pulse
C The number of pulses
PlayWaveSequence AF5F CALL PlaySquareWave Play a pulse.
AF62 DEC C Count down the pulses.
AF63 JR NZ,PlayWaveSequence Loop back until all of the pulses have been played.
AF65 RET Return.
