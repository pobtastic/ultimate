AF22: Play Buzz
Used by the routine at ReloadRevolver.
Plays a short, rough buzz made of twelve pulses of different lengths.
PlayBuzz AF22 LD C,$0C Set the number of pulses.
PlayBuzz_Loop AF24 LD A,C Fetch the number of the pulse.
AF25 XOR %10100101 Change it.
AF27 ADD A,C Add it to the number of the pulse.
AF28 LD B,A Use it as the length of the pulse.
AF29 CALL PlaySquareWave Play the pulse.
AF2C DEC C Count down the pulses.
AF2D JR NZ,PlayBuzz_Loop Loop back until all of the pulses have been played.
AF2F RET Return.
