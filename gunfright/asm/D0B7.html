D0B7: Clear Buffer
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, GiveReward and ReturnToTown.
Clears PlayfieldBuffer by pushing zeroes onto the stack, 18 bytes in each of 70 rows.
Output
HL The address of the start of the buffer
Point at the end of PlayfieldBuffer, and fill with zero.

Keep the stack pointer, and point it at the end of the buffer.
