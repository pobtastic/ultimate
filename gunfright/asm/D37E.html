D37E: Draw Bounty
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Draws Bounty. In the town it also prints BountyText. It draws the symbol which goes next to the number.
Print the text in the town.
DrawBounty D37E LD A,($A267) Fetch TownOffset.
D381 AND A
D382 JR Z,DrawBounty_SkipText Jump to DrawBounty_SkipText if this is not the town.
D384 LD HL,$50A0 Point HL at 50A0 (in the screen buffer).
D387 LD DE,$D31F Point DE at BountyText.
Print the text.
D38A CALL PrintText Call PrintText.
Draw the symbol.
DrawBounty_SkipText D38D CALL DrawBountySymbol Call DrawBountySymbol.
