A830: Telegram Lines
The six lines of the telegram panel, each 12 bytes long. The first byte is the number of letters typed so far, which is 0E once the line is complete. Then come the screen address of the line, its attribute and fourteen characters. The score lines have room after the dollar sign for the digits. The 5C in the high score line is the full stop of the font.
