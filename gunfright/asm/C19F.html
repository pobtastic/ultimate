C19F: Draw Cell Objects
Used by the routine at DrawTown.
Draws all of the objects which are in a cell of the town, with the ones which are furthest from the viewer first.
Input
HL The cell: the column in L and the row in H
DrawCellObjects C19F CALL FindObjectsInCell Make a list of the objects in the cell.
