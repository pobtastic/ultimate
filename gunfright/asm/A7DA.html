A7DA: Type Telegram
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Called every frame in town. It makes the lines of the telegram appear one letter at a time, like a telegram being typed. Each line has a counter which is 0E once the line is complete. The first line which is not complete shows a blinking cursor on alternate frames, and on the other frames prints its next letter and clicks.
TypeTelegram A7DA LD A,($A267) Fetch the town flag.
A7DD AND A Return if the game is not in town.
A7DE RET Z
A7DF LD B,$06 Set the number of lines to six.
A7E1 LD HL,$A830 Point HL at TelegramLines.
A7E4 LD DE,$0012 Set the size of a telegram line in DE.
A7E7 LD A,($A267) Fetch the town flag, which is also the column of the panel and keep the panel column in C.
A7EA LD C,A
TypeTelegram_NextLine A7EB LD A,(HL) Fetch the counter of the line.
A7EC CP $0E Jump to TypeTelegram_StashLine if the line is not complete.
A7EE JR NZ,TypeTelegram_StashLine
A7F0 ADD HL,DE Move on to the next line.
A7F1 DJNZ TypeTelegram_NextLine Loop back until every line has been checked.
A7F3 RET Return.
Find the position of the next letter of the line.
TypeTelegram_StashLine A7F4 PUSH HL Stash the address of the line on the stack.
A7F5 PUSH AF Stash the counter of the line on the stack.
A7F6 INC HL Move on to the low byte of the screen address.
A7F7 LD E,(HL) Fetch the low byte of the screen address.
A7F8 INC HL Move on to the high byte.
A7F9 LD D,(HL) Fetch the high byte of the screen address.
A7FA INC HL Move on to the attribute.
A7FB EX DE,HL Swap the screen address into HL.
A7FC ADD A,C Add the counter and the column of the panel to the address.
A7FD CALL AddAToHL Point HL at the position of the next letter.
A800 LD A,(DE) Fetch the attribute.
A801 INC DE Move on to the first letter.
A802 EX AF,AF' Keep the attribute in the alternate accumulator.
A803 LD A,($A216) Fetch the low byte of the frame counter.
A806 AND %00000001 Keep bit 0.
A808 JR Z,TypeTelegram_RestoreCounter Jump to TypeTelegram_RestoreCounter on even frames.
On odd frames draw the cursor in the next position.
A80A ADD A,$5E Convert the odd frame into the number of the cursor character.
A80C CALL ConvertCharacter Convert the character to the number of its tile.
A80F LD BC,$8EA8 Point BC at Font.
A812 CALL DrawTile Draw the cursor.
A815 POP AF Retrieve the counter of the line.
A816 POP HL Retrieve the address of the line.
A817 RET Return.
On even frames print the next letter and click.
TypeTelegram_RestoreCounter A818 POP AF Retrieve the counter of the line.
A819 EX DE,HL Swap the pointer to the letters into HL.
A81A CALL AddAToHL Point HL at the next letter of the line.
A81D EX DE,HL Swap the pointer back into DE.
A81E LD A,(DE) Fetch the letter.
A81F CALL ConvertCharacter Convert the letter to the number of its tile.
A822 PUSH AF Stash the tile number on the stack.
A823 CALL PlayTypingSound Play a click.
A826 POP AF Retrieve the tile number.
A827 LD BC,$8EA8 Point BC at Font.
A82A CALL DrawTile Draw the letter.
A82D POP HL Retrieve the address of the line.
A82E INC (HL) Increase the counter of the line.
A82F RET Return.
