A9BF: Handle Horse
Type 14: a horse waiting in the street. It goes away if the sheriff is three cells away. If the sheriff touches it, the horse is removed, the sheriff turns into a mounted sheriff (type 15) who has a higher top speed, and the price of the horse is taken from the bounty.
HandleHorse A9BF LD C,$03 Set the distance to three cells.
A9C1 CALL CheckDistance Check how close the sheriff is.
A9C4 JP NC,HandleBody_Remove Remove the horse if the sheriff has moved away.
A9C7 CALL CheckSheriffTouch Check whether the sheriff is touching the horse.
A9CA RET NC Return if not.
A9CB LD A,$08 Write 08 to SoundNotesLeft so that the sound lasts eight notes.
A9CD LD ($A23C),A
A9D0 LD HL,$AEBF Point SoundNoteAddress at the notes of the horse sound.
A9D3 LD ($A23D),HL
A9D6 LD (IX+$00),$00 Remove the horse.
A9DA LD A,$12 Raise the top speed of the sheriff to 12.
A9DC LD ($A21F),A
A9DF LD (IY+$00),$15 Turn the sheriff into a mounted sheriff.
A9E3 LD BC,($A25C) Fetch the price of a horse.
A9E7 CALL TakeFromBounty Take it from the bounty.
A9EA JP DrawBountyDigits Jump to DrawBountyDigits.
