D04B: Copy Buffer To Screen
Used by the routine at CopyBufferAndClear.
Copies PlayfieldBuffer to the screen, from the bottom row up. Each row copies 16 bytes. In the town the copy sits beside the panel.
Keep the stack pointer, and point it at the playfield on the screen.
CopyBufferToScreen D04B LD ($A21D),SP Set SavedStackPointer.
D04F LD HL,$4FDB Point HL at 4FDB (in the screen buffer).
Move it to the right in the town.
D052 CALL AddTownOffsetToHL Call AddTownOffsetToHL.
D055 LD SP,HL Point the stack pointer at it.
D056 LD B,$70 Set a count of 70 rows.
D058 LD HL,$D702 Point HL at PlayfieldBuffer_FirstRowEnd.
