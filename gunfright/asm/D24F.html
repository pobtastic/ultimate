D24F: Calculate Screen Address
Used by the routines at HandleMenuBullet and DrawMenuSprite.
Finds the address on the screen of a pixel. The vertical position is measured from the bottom of the screen.
Input
H The vertical position, from the bottom
L The horizontal position
Output
HL The address on the screen
Stash DE, and copy the position to it.
CalculateScreenAddress D24F PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.
D250 EX DE,HL Copy the position to DE.
Work out the column from the horizontal position.
D251 LD A,E Divide the horizontal position by eight.
D252 RRCA
D253 RRCA
D254 RRCA
D255 AND %00011111 Keep the column.
D257 LD L,A Store it.
Work out the character row within a third of the screen.
D258 LD A,D Invert the vertical position and multiply it by four.
D259 CPL
D25A RLCA
D25B RLCA
D25C AND %11100000 Keep the character row within the third.
D25E OR L Make the low byte of the address.
D25F LD L,A
Work out the third of the screen.
D260 LD A,D Invert the vertical position and divide it by eight.
D261 CPL
D262 RRCA
D263 RRCA
D264 RRCA
D265 AND %00011000 Keep the third.
D267 LD H,A Start the high byte.
Work out the line within the character row, and make the high byte of the address.
D268 LD A,D Invert the vertical position.
D269 CPL
D26A AND %00000111 Keep the line within the character row.
D26C OR H Combine it with the third, add 38 and set the high byte.
D26D ADD A,$38
D26F LD H,A
D270 POP DE Restore DE from the stack.
D271 RET Return.
