A4B5: Money Bag Template
The initial contents of a slot for a money bag. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
MoneyBagTemplate A4B5 DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters, which CreateMoneyBag fills in.
A4BD DEFB $10,$10,$00,$00,$00,$00
A4C3 DEFW $A4C5 The sprite list of a money bag.
A4C5 DEFB $F8,$04,$29,$80 The sprite list: sprite 29 at offset (F8, 04), then the terminator. .
