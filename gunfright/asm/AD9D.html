AD9D: Controls Texts
The table of texts for the type of controls, followed by the texts, and then the text of the last menu line. Bit 7 of the last character of each text is set.
ControlsTexts AD9D DEFW $ADA5 The addresses of the texts.
AD9F DEFW $ADAD
ADA1 DEFW $ADBE
ADA3 DEFW $ADCD
ADA5 DEFM "KEYBOAR","D"+$80 "KEYBOARD".
ADAD DEFM "KEMPSTON JOYSTIC","K"+$80 "KEMPSTON JOYSTICK".
ADBE DEFM "CURSOR JOYSTIC","K"+$80 "CURSOR JOYSTICK".
ADCD DEFM "INTERFACE I","I"+$80 "INTERFACE II".
ControlsTexts_StartGame ADD9 DEFM "START GAM","E"+$80 "START GAME".
