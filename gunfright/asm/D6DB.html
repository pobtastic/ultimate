D6DB: Clear Screen
Used by the routines at CheckTopScore, ShowPlayerNumber, GameMenu, ShowGameOver and DrawGameScreen.
Clears the pixel bytes of the screen.
ClearScreen D6DB LD HL,$4000 Point HL at 4000 (in the screen buffer).
D6DE LD BC,$1800 Set the number of bytes.
Continue on to FillMemory.
