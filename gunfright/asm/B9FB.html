B9FB: Handle Waiting Outlaw
Type 0C: the outlaw in the duel before he draws. He wanders about the screen, changing direction a random number of times (the count in IX+0A), and at the end of the last move he draws his gun. If the player moves or fires before then he draws at once. He is hit if the crosshair is on him when the player fires.
Count down the current move, and draw if he has finished.
HandleWaitingOutlaw B9FB CALL CheckForEarlyDraw Check whether the player has provoked the outlaw.
B9FE DEC (IX+$0B) Count down the timer for the current move.
BA01 LD A,(IX+$0B) Fetch the timer.
BA04 JR NZ,HandleWaitingOutlaw_Move Jump if the move is not over.
BA06 OR (IX+$0A) Combine the timer, which is zero, with the number of moves left.
BA09 JP Z,StartOutlawDrawing Jump to make the outlaw draw if he has made all his moves.
BA0C CALL ChooseOutlawMove Choose a new move.
Keep him on the screen and move him.
HandleWaitingOutlaw_Move BA0F CALL KeepOutlawOnScreen Keep the outlaw on the screen, which reverses his speed at an edge.
BA12 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the flags which show whether he has reached an edge.
BA15 AND %00001111 Keep the four bits.
BA17 JR Z,HandleWaitingOutlaw_Update Jump if he has not reached an edge.
BA19 LD (IX+$0B),$01 Make the move end on the next frame.
HandleWaitingOutlaw_Update BA1D CALL MoveDuelOutlaw Move the outlaw.
This entry point is used by the routines at StartOutlawDrawing and HandleDrawingOutlaw.
Check whether the crosshair has hit him.
HandleWaitingOutlaw_CheckHit BA20 CALL CheckCrosshairOnOutlaw Check whether the crosshair is on the outlaw.
BA23 JR NC,HandleWaitingOutlaw_DrawBody Jump if the outlaw has not been hit.
BA25 LD A,$80 Set the flag which shows that the outlaw has been hit.
BA27 LD ($A212),A Store the flag which shows the outlaw has been hit.
BA2A LD (IX+$00),$11 Turn the outlaw into a shot outlaw.
BA2E LD (IX+$0C),$00 Start at the first frame of falling.
Draw the body of the outlaw.
HandleWaitingOutlaw_DrawBody BA32 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the horizontal position of the outlaw and keep it in L.
BA35 LD L,A
BA36 LD ($A220),A Store it as the horizontal pixel position for the sprites.
BA39 LD H,(IX+$04) Fetch the vertical position of the outlaw into H.
BA3C CALL CalculateBufferAddress Find the address in the buffer.
BA3F CALL ChooseOutlawBody Choose the sprites of the body of the outlaw.
BA42 LD B,$03 Set the number of sprites to three.
BA44 CALL DrawWideSprites Draw the body of the outlaw into the buffer.
Continue on to DrawOutlawFace.
