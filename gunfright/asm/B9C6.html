B9C6: Bullet Hole Template
The initial contents of a slot for a bullet hole (type 10). The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
BulletHoleTemplate B9C6 DEFB $10,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
B9CE DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00
B9D4 DEFW $B9D6 The sprite list of a bullet hole.
B9D6 DEFB $F0,$F0,$0B,$80 The sprite list: sprite 0B at offset (F0, F0). .
