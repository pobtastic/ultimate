B9DD: Advance Gun Frame
Used by the routine at HandleOutlawGun.
Moves the outlaw's gun on to its next frame. If the player has provoked the outlaw, which is when DrawMessageState is one, the gun moves on every frame. Otherwise it moves on after a delay which is shorter at the higher levels of skill.
Input
IX The address of the gun
AdvanceGunFrame B9DD LD A,($A211) Fetch DrawMessageState.
B9E0 CP $01 Jump to AdvanceGunFrame_Next to move to the next frame at once if the outlaw has been provoked.
B9E2 JR Z,AdvanceGunFrame_Next
B9E4 LD A,(IX+$05) Jump to AdvanceGunFrame_Reset if the delay is zero.
B9E7 AND A
B9E8 JR Z,AdvanceGunFrame_Reset
B9EA DEC (IX+$05) Count down the delay.
B9ED RET Return.
The delay has finished, so set a new one from the skill level.
AdvanceGunFrame_Reset B9EE LD A,($A261) Fetch Skill.
B9F1 CPL Invert it.
B9F2 AND %00000111 Keep a number from 00 to 07 which is smaller at the higher skill levels.
B9F4 LD (IX+$05),A Set the delay.
AdvanceGunFrame_Next B9F7 INC (IX+$0C) Move on to the next frame.
B9FA RET Return.
