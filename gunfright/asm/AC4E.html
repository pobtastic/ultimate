AC4E: Menu Entries
The state of the four menu entries. Each entry has four bytes: its state, the horizontal position of its bullet, the vertical position of its bullet and a counter. The states are 00 (the revolver has not yet fired), 01 (the text is complete), 02 and 03 (the revolver is firing), 04 (the bullet is flying and the text is being typed) and 05 (the entry which reads the keys).
MenuEntries AC4E DEFB $00,$FC,$20,$1C Entry for the keys.
MenuEntries_FirstRevolver AC52 DEFB $08,$00,$00,$70 Entry for the first revolver.
MenuEntries_SecondRevolver AC56 DEFB $20,$0E,$04,$10 Entry for the second revolver.
MenuEntries_ThirdRevolver AC5A DEFB $00,$E0,$40,$0E Entry for the third revolver.
