6185: Wall Rectangle Lists
The lists of rectangles of walls, which are used to stop objects from walking through the walls. Each rectangle is four bytes: the horizontal and vertical positions of its middle, then its half width and half height. A zero byte ends a list. The middle of a cell is 80 a full side has a half length of 40 and a thin wall has a half length of 02. A gap in a side is a doorway.
