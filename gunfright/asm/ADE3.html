ADE3: Draw Revolver
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Draws a revolver, which is a block of seven by five tiles.
Input
HL The screen address
A The attribute
DrawRevolver ADE3 EX AF,AF' Keep the attribute in the alternate accumulator.
ADE4 LD DE,$ADED Point DE at RevolverTiles.
ADE7 LD BC,$0705 Set the width to seven and the height to five.
ADEA JP DrawTileBlock Draw the block of tiles.
