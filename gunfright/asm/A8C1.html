A8C1: Tumbleweed Template
The initial contents of a slot for a tumbleweed. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
TumbleweedTemplate A8C1 DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
A8C9 DEFB $10,$10,$00,$00,$00,$00
A8CF DEFW $A8D1 The sprite list of a tumbleweed.
A8D1 DEFB $F4,$00,$2B,$80 The sprite list: sprite 2B at offset (F4, 00), then the terminator. .
