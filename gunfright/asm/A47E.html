A47E: Create Money Bag
Used by the routine at CreateTownObjects.
Creates a money bag (an object of type 18) in the seventh object slot when the player's bounty has dropped below $100. The bag appears close to the sheriff, in a free cell.
Return unless the bounty is below $100 and the slot is free.
CreateMoneyBag A47E LD HL,$A257 Return unless the first two bytes of the bounty are both zero, which means it is less than $100.
