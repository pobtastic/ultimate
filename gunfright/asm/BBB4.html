BBB4: Messaging: Bonus Text
The message which is shown before the stage of money bags: an attribute byte, characters and a zero byte.
BonusMessage BBB4 DEFB $46 Attribute: INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBB5 DEFM "BONUS" "BONUS"
BBBA DEFB $00 Terminator.
