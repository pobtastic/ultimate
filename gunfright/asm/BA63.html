BA63: Check Crosshair On Outlaw
Used by the routine at HandleWaitingOutlaw.
Checks whether the player's shot is on the outlaw. The outlaw cannot be hit once he has fired his first shot.
Input
IX The address of the outlaw
Output
F The carry flag is set if the outlaw has been hit
