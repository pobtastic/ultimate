B0A9: Reward Tune
The tune which is played when the player is given the reward for the outlaw. Each byte is a note: bits 0 to 5 are the number of the note in NoteTable (00 is a rest) and bits 6 and 7 are one less than the length. FF ends the tune.
RewardTune B0A9 DEFB $23,$25,$27,$23,$22,$23,$25,$22
B0B1 DEFB $20,$22,$23,$20,$1F,$20,$22,$1B
B0B9 DEFB $1F,$22,$27,$25,$23,$25,$23,$22
B0C1 DEFB $23,$22,$20,$22,$20,$1F,$E0,$FF
