AE82: Wait For A While
Used by the routines at CheckTopScore, ShowPlayerNumber and GiveReward.
Waits by calling Wait several times. HL is zero after the first call, so every call after the first counts down 10000 times.
Input
B The number of delays
HL The length of the first delay
WaitForAWhile AE82 CALL Wait Count down.
AE85 DJNZ WaitForAWhile Loop back until the delays are over.
AE87 RET Return.
