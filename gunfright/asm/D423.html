D423: Draw Reward
Used by the routine at DrawTownPanel.
Draws the reward for the outlaw on the poster, from OutlawRewards for Level. It is three bytes of packed decimal digits, without the zeroes at the start. The digits are drawn from one byte before Font_Digits, so they are drawn one line lower.
Find the reward for the level.

D426 LD C,A
D427 ADD A,A Multiply it by 03.

D429 LD HL,$B56E Point HL at OutlawRewards.
D42C CALL AddAToHL Call AddAToHL.
D42F EX DE,HL Put the address in DE.
Set the screen address.

Skip the leading zeroes.
