CA5D: Make Shot
Used by the routine at FireRevolver.
Makes a shot. It plays PlayRumble. If TownOffset is zero it jumps to MakeFlash. In the town it puts a type 02 bullet in ObjectSlot_02, ObjectSlot_03 or ObjectSlot_04: a free slot, or a slot that is not a bullet, or a bullet that is not drawn. The bullet copies the firer's position and heading, and has speed 0C.
Input
IX The address of the object which fires
Output
F The carry flag is set if a shot was made
Play the rumble, or make a flash outside the town.
