A8D5: Handle Tumbleweed
Type 17: a tumbleweed rolling about next to the sheriff. It follows one of two routes, stepping through the route one byte at a time; the high nibble of each byte is the vertical speed and the low nibble the horizontal speed, both signed. Bits 6 and 7 of byte 05 mirror the route, and they change every time a route ends, so the tumbleweed wanders in a different way each time. It spins by flipping its sprite every frame, and it hurts the sheriff if they touch. It goes away when the sheriff is two cells away.
Disappear if the sheriff is more than two cells away.
HandleTumbleweed A8D5 LD C,$02 Set the distance to two cells.
A8D7 CALL CheckDistance Check how close the sheriff is.
A8DA JP NC,HandleBody_Remove Remove the tumbleweed if the sheriff has moved away.
Fetch the next step of the route.
A8DD LD A,(IX+$0A) Fetch the number of the route, zero or one.
A8E0 LD BC,$A94B Point BC at TumbleweedRoutes.
A8E3 CALL GetTableEntry Point HL at the start of the route.
A8E6 LD A,(IX+$0B) Fetch the position in the route.
A8E9 INC (IX+$0B) Move on to the next step.
A8EC CALL AddAToHL Point HL at the step.
A8EF LD A,(HL) Fetch the step.
A8F0 CP $80 Jump to HandleTumbleweed_RouteEnd if this is the end of the route.
A8F2 JR Z,HandleTumbleweed_RouteEnd
Work out the vertical speed from the high nibble.
A8F4 SRA A Shift the high nibble into the bottom of the byte, keeping the sign.
A8F6 SRA A
A8F8 SRA A
A8FA SRA A
A8FC BIT 6,(IX+$05) Jump to HandleTumbleweed_DoubleVertical unless the route is mirrored vertically.
A900 JR Z,HandleTumbleweed_DoubleVertical
A902 NEG Negate the speed to mirror the route.
HandleTumbleweed_DoubleVertical A904 SLA A Double the speed.
A906 LD ($A215),A Store it as the vertical speed.
Work out the horizontal speed from the low nibble.
A909 LD A,(HL) Fetch the step again.
A90A LD C,A Keep the tumbleweed step in C.
A90B AND %00001111 Keep the low nibble.
A90D BIT 3,C Jump to HandleTumbleweed_TestHorizontal unless the nibble is negative.
A90F JR Z,HandleTumbleweed_TestHorizontal
A911 OR %11110000 Extend the sign of the nibble into the top half.
HandleTumbleweed_TestHorizontal A913 BIT 7,(IX+$05) Jump to HandleTumbleweed_DoubleHorizontal unless the route is mirrored horizontally.
A917 JR Z,HandleTumbleweed_DoubleHorizontal
A919 NEG Negate the speed to mirror the route.
HandleTumbleweed_DoubleHorizontal A91B SLA A Double the speed.
A91D LD ($A214),A Store it as the horizontal speed.
Move the tumbleweed.
A920 CALL CheckWallsEachWay Set the direction the tumbleweed is facing from its speed.
A923 CALL MoveObject Move the tumbleweed.
A926 LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the flags of the object.
A929 XOR %01000000 Flip the sprite horizontally.
A92B LD (IX+$07),A Store the flags.
A92E CALL CheckSheriffTouch Check whether the tumbleweed is touching the sheriff.
A931 JP C,KillSheriff Hurt the sheriff if it is.
A934 RET Return.
The route has ended, so choose the next one.
HandleTumbleweed_RouteEnd A935 LD A,($A218) Fetch a random number.
A938 AND %00000001 Keep bit 0.
A93A LD (IX+$0A),A Choose the route with it.
A93D LD (IX+$0B),$00 Write 00 to byte 0B to go back to the first step.
A941 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the mirror flags.
A944 ADD A,$40 Add 40 to change how the route is mirrored.
A946 LD (IX+$05),A Store the flags.
A949 JR HandleTumbleweed Start again with the new route.
