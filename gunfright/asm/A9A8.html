A9A8: Horse Template
The initial contents of a slot for a horse. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
HorseTemplate A9A8 DEFB $00,$80,$00,$80,$00,$00,$00,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters.

A9B6 DEFW $A9B8 The sprite list of a horse.
A9B8 DEFB $EC,$00,$37 The sprite list: sprite 37 at offset (EC, 00), sprite 36 at offset (FC, 01), then the terminator. .
