A7CB: Reset Fine
Used by the routine at SetUpPlayer.
Gives the fine its starting amount of $100 if the amount is zero.
ResetFine A7CB LD HL,$A25A Point HL at the fine.
A7CE LD A,(HL) Fetch the low byte of the fine.
A7CF INC HL Move on to the high byte.
A7D0 OR (HL) Return if the fine is not zero.
A7D1 RET NZ
A7D2 LD DE,$A861 Point DE at TelegramLines_FineDigits.
A7D5 LD BC,$0100 Set the starting fine of $100 in BC.
A7D8 JR ResetBulletPrice_SetPrice Store the fine and convert it to digits.
