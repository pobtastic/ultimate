CC8A: Speed Player Up
Used by the routine at MovePlayer.
Raises the speed halfway towards TopSpeed and keeps it even.
Input
IX The address of the sheriff
Output
A The speed
Work out the average of the speed and TopSpeed.
SpeedPlayerUp CC8A LD A,($A21F) Fetch TopSpeed.
CC8D SUB (IX+$05) Subtract the speed.
CC90 SRL A Halve the difference.
CC92 ADD A,(IX+$05) Add the speed.
CC95 AND %11111110 Keep it even.
Continue on to MovePlayerForward.
