7E08: Sprite: Flash 1

The outlaw in the duel uses all four for his muzzle flash (see HandleMuzzleFlash), and the flash of the sheriff's gun uses the last one (see MakeFlash). The first and third are also the two frames of the puff of smoke (see HandleSmoke). It is sprite 07 2 characters (16 pixels) wide and 16 rows high.
