BFDB: Draw Flipped Shifted Columns
Draws the columns of a sprite which is flipped and shifted. There is a piece of code for each of up to five columns of the sprite, and the code for the width of the sprite is where the jump at SpriteJumpOpcode starts. Each piece puts a column into two bytes of the buffer, using the table for the shift to turn the mask and the pixels round and split them in two. It works from right to left.
The jump at SpriteJumpOpcode starts here for five columns.
DrawFlippedShiftedColumns BFDB CPL Invert the screen byte.
BFDC POP DE Restore the mask into E and the pixels into D from the stack.
BFDD LD L,E Clear the screen bits under the part of the mask which is in this byte.
BFDE OR (HL)
BFDF CPL
BFE0 LD L,D Add the part of the pixels which is in this byte.
BFE1 OR (HL)
BFE2 LD (BC),A Store the byte and move on to the byte on the left.
BFE3 DEC BC
BFE4 DEC H Point H at the table for the bits which move into the next byte, and fetch that screen byte.
BFE5 LD L,E
BFE6 LD A,(BC)
BFE7 CPL Clear the screen bits under the part of the mask which has moved into it.
BFE8 OR (HL)
BFE9 CPL
BFEA LD L,D Add the part of the pixels which has moved into it.
BFEB OR (HL)
BFEC INC H Point H back at the first table.
