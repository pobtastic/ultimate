AF4F: Play Short Tone
Used by the routines at HandleSheriffEntrance and HandleDying.
Plays a short tone. The higher the number, the higher the tone.
The sounds played by this routine are:
Effect Sound
Sheriff Entrance Sound
Dying Sound
Input
A The number
PlayShortTone AF4F ADD A,A Multiply the number by four.
AF50 ADD A,A
AF51 CPL Invert it.
AF52 LD B,A Use it as the length of a pulse.
AF53 LD C,$04 Set the number of pulses.
AF55 JR PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
