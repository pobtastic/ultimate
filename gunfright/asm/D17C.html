D17C: Read Keyboard
Used by the routine at ReadControls.
Reads the controls from the keyboard. Left is X, V, M or B, right is C or N, up is any key from A to G or from H to ENTER, fire is any key from Q to T or from Y to P, and down is any key from 1 to 5 or from 6 to 0.
