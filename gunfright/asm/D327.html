D327: Draw Side Pictures
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Draws a block of tiles of 04 by 0A from SidePictureTiles, on each side of the lower part of the screen of any stage which is not the town. The block at the left is drawn from right to left so that it is a reflection of the block at the right.
Return if this is the town.
DrawSidePictures D327 LD A,($A267) Fetch TownOffset.
D32A AND A
D32B RET NZ Return if this is the town.
