A6FC: Print Telegram
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Prints the whole telegram panel in town. It converts the bullet price, the horse price and the fine into digits, prints the first four lines of the telegram and, in a game for two players, the line which shows the score of the player who is not currently playing.
