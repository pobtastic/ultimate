CED0: Test Move Left
Checks the walls for an object that is moving in the direction of heading three. The front edge is the horizontal position minus the horizontal size. The two ends of that edge, now and after the move, are checked with CheckWallHorizontalReverse.
Input
IX The address of the object
Output
F The carry flag is set if the object hit a wall
Find the front edge and the lower end.
TestMoveLeft CED0 LD L,(IX+$01) Fetch the horizontal position of the object.
CED3 LD H,(IX+$02)
CED6 LD C,(IX+$08) Fetch the horizontal size.
CED9 LD B,$00
CEDB AND A Subtract it to get the front edge in DE.
CEDC SBC HL,BC
CEDE EX DE,HL
CEDF LD L,(IX+$03) Fetch the vertical position of the object.
CEE2 LD H,(IX+$04)
CEE5 LD C,(IX+$09) Fetch the vertical size.
CEE8 AND A Subtract it to get the lower end.
CEE9 SBC HL,BC
Check the cell at the lower end.

CEF0 POP HL Restore the end and the front edge from the stack.
