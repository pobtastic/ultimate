C6C3: Wall Tile Masks
The four masks used by DrawWallStrip to keep the bits of the buffer on the left of a wall tile. The index is the shift to the right in twos of pixels: 00, 02, 04 or 06.
WallTileMasks C6C3 DEFB $00 Shift 00: 00.
C6C4 DEFB $C0 Shift 02: C0.
C6C5 DEFB $F0 Shift 04: F0.
C6C6 DEFB $FC Shift 06: FC.
