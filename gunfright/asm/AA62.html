AA62: Cactus Template
The initial contents of a slot for a cactus. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
CactusTemplate AA62 DEFB $13,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
AA6A DEFB $10,$10,$00,$00,$00,$00
AA70 DEFW $AA72 The sprite list of a cactus.
AA72 DEFB $F4,$00,$2A,$80 The sprite list: sprite 2A at offset (F4, 00), then the terminator. .
