AE98: Play Static
Used by the routine at MakeBulletHole.
Plays a burst of noise by using the first 80 bytes of the ROM as the length of each pulse.
PlayStatic AE98 LD HL,$0000 Point HL at the start of the ROM.
AE9B LD C,$80 Set the number of pulses.
PlayStatic_Loop AE9D LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the ROM.
AE9E INC HL Move on to the next byte.
AE9F AND %00111111 Keep the bottom six bits.
AEA1 LD B,A Use them as the length of the pulse.
AEA2 CALL PlaySquareWave Play the pulse.
AEA5 DEC C Count down the pulses.
AEA6 JR NZ,PlayStatic_Loop Loop back until all of the pulses have been played.
AEA8 RET Return.
