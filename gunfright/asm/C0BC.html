C0BC: Sheriff Template
The initial contents of a slot for the sheriff when he comes back after losing a life. It is an object of type 19 which waits sixteen frames before the sheriff arrives, at the place where he starts. The final two bytes point to the sprite list.
SheriffTemplate C0BC DEFB $19,$88,$04,$F0,$04,$00,$80,$00 Type, position, speed, size and counters.
C0C4 DEFB $10,$10,$00,$00,$10,$00
C0CA DEFW BystanderSpriteLists_DeadFigure The sprite list, which is a dead figure.
