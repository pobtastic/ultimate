C85B: Bag Speeds
One number for each of the eight skill levels, from 0F to 2B in steps of 04. MakeBag uses it as the upper bound of the sideways speed and, doubled, as the downward speed.
BagSpeeds C85B DEFB $0F Skill 00: 0F.

C85D DEFB $17 Skill 02: 17.
C85E DEFB $1B Skill 03: 1B.
C85F DEFB $1F Skill 04: 1F.
