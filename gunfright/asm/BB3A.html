BB3A: Give Reward
Used by the routine at HandleDyingOutlaw.
Called when the outlaw has finished dying. Plays a tune and adds the reward for this outlaw to the player's bounty, then adds a life to make up for the one which the next stage takes, and moves on to the next level. The skill level goes up every second level, to a maximum of seven. Levels past the nineteenth go back to the sixteenth. After every fourth level the player plays a bonus stage in which money bags fall; otherwise the stage ends.
