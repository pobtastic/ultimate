D463: Draw Telegram Frame
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Draws the frame of the telegram, which has the word at TelegramText in its lower edge. The corners and the edges are the same tiles at the top and the left, drawn reflected for the bottom and the right.
Set the attribute and the tile set.
