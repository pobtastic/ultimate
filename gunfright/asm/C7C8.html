C7C8: Remove All Objects
Used by the routines at FinishTownStage, EndDuel, GiveReward, CheckSheriffLost and ReturnToTown.
Jumps to FillMemory to fill F0 bytes from ObjectSlot_01 with zero, which empties all fifteen object slots.
Set the address and the count.
RemoveAllObjects C7C8 LD HL,$A27D Point HL at ObjectSlot_01.
C7CB LD BC,$00F0 Set BC to F0.
C7CE JP FillMemory Jump to FillMemory.
