D6E1: Fill Memory
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, KillSheriff, RemoveAllObjects and TakeFromBounty.
Clears a number of bytes to zero, or fills them with the value of E at FillMemory_WithValue.
Input
BC The number of bytes
HL The address
FillMemory D6E1 LD E,$00 Fill with zero.
This entry point is used by the routine at SetAttributes.
FillMemory_WithValue D6E3 LD (HL),E Store the value, move on and count down the bytes.
D6E4 INC HL
D6E5 DEC BC
D6E6 LD A,B
D6E7 OR C
D6E8 JR NZ,FillMemory_WithValue Go back until they are all done.
D6EA RET Return.
