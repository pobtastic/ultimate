C967: Figure Sprite Numbers
The lower and middle sprite numbers for each walking frame. The lowest two bits choose the step, and bit 2, set from FacingFlags, chooses the second set.
Bit 2 clear
Frame Lower Middle
00 Sprite_Legs_3 Sprite_Torso_1
01 Sprite_Legs_1 Sprite_Torso_1
02 Sprite_Legs_4 Sprite_Torso_2
03 Sprite_Legs_1 Sprite_Torso_2
Bit 2 set
Frame Lower Middle
04 Sprite_Legs_6 Sprite_Torso_3
05 Sprite_Legs_5 Sprite_Torso_3
06 Sprite_Legs_7 Sprite_Torso_4
07 Sprite_Legs_5 Sprite_Torso_4
Bit 2 clear.
