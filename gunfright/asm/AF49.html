AF49: Play Pause Sound
Used by the routine at PauseGame.
Plays a longer, low tone.
PlayPauseSound AF49 LD C,$10 Set the number of pulses.
AF4B LD B,$80 Set the length of a pulse.
AF4D JR PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
