ADD8: Find Object Screen Position
Used by the routines at SpawnEnemy and PrepareDraw.
Works out where an object is on the screen from its position on the planet and the position of the part of the planet that is on the screen. If the object is off the screen, its slot is freed. Otherwise the horizontal and vertical screen positions are stored at offsets 11 and 12, and the object is flagged as visible with bit 7 of offset 10. The wrap flag (bit 6 of offset 10) is set if the object has crossed the edge of the part of the planet that is on the screen.
Input

IX Address of the object
