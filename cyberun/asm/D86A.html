D86A: Draw Edge Pixels
Used by the routine at EndSegment.
Draws the pixels that finish the edge of a flat segment when the horizontal offset is not a multiple of eight. Nothing is drawn unless the segment is flat, its shape code (see EndShape) is 13 or 93 and steps have been flagged. The four bytes come from EdgePatternsRight or EdgePatternsLeft, depending on the type, and are stored going up the screen at the address in SegmentScreenAddress, except that only 02 are stored when the segment is at the top or bottom of the area.
