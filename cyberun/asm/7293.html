7293: Play Crash Sound
Used by the routine at CheckTerrainCollision.
Plays a burst of noise of 1C clicks. It is used when the ship hits the terrain. The entry point at PlayNoise makes the noise for any number of clicks.
PlayCrashSound 7293 LD C,$1C The crash is 1C clicks.
PlayCrashSound_Pattern 7295 LD E,$27 The crash uses pattern 27.
This entry point is used by the routines at PlayUnusedSound, PlayShortCrashSound, PlayExplosionSound, PlayShotSound, PlayPatternedNoise and PlayNoiseBurst.
PlayNoise 7297 LD A,C The click length starts from the clicks left.
7298 XOR E Flip the bits of A that are set in E.
7299 ADD A,A Double it to make the on-time.
729A LD B,A That on-time is passed to the click.
729B AND %00011111 Keep the bits of A that are set in 1F.
729D LD D,A The low five bits are the off-time.
729E CALL MakeClick Call MakeClick.
72A1 DEC C One click of the noise has been played.
72A2 JR NZ,PlayNoise Jump to PlayNoise if the zero flag is clear.
72A4 RET Return.
