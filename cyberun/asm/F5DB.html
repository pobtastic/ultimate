F5DB: Text: Player
The text record for the number of the player. The last character, which is changed by PrintPlayer, is the digit one or two.
TextPlayer F5DB DEFB $38,$67,$C5,$08
F5DF DEFB $0F,$0B,$00,$18,$04,$11,$26,$00 "PLAYER 1"
