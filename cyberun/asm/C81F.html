C81F: Update Horizontal Scroll Speed
Used by the routine at CheckTerrainCollision.
Adds the horizontal speed of the ship to the fraction in HorizontalScrollFraction and works out how many whole pixels of horizontal scroll that makes, limited by the value in HorizontalLimit in the same way as UpdateVerticalScrollSpeed for the vertical scroll. The result is stored in HorizontalSpeed.

HL Horizontal speed
