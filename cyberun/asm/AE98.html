AE98: Set Object Attributes
Used by the routines at UpdateObject, UpdateType14, UpdateCrystalStack and UpdateMapObjects.
Works out the area of attribute cells that is covered by the sprite that has just been drawn, as a width and height in cells, and fills it with the attribute at AF03 by calling FillAttributes.
SetObjectAttributes AE98 LD A,($A1D8) The rows of the sprite.
AE9B DEC A Step A back.
AE9C RET M Return if the sign flag is set.
AE9D LD D,A D=A.
AE9E LD HL,($A1D5) Fetch the word at A1D5 into HL.
AEA1 LD A,($5C29) The width of the sprite in bytes.
AEA4 LD B,A B=A.
AEA5 LD A,($A2FA) The sprite reaches past the right of the screen.
AEA8 AND A Jump to SetObjectAttributes_Width if A is zero.
AEA9 JR Z,SetObjectAttributes_Width
AEAB LD A,L A=L.
AEAC ADD A,B A gains B.

AEAF LD L,$08 L=08.

AEB6 JR NC,SetObjectAttributes_Right
AEB8 ADD A,$08 Add 08 to A.
AEBA LD L,A L=A.
