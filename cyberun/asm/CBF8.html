CBF8: Frame Corner Table
Four entries of seven bytes for DrawFrame, one for each corner. Each has the position of the corner graphic, the position of an area of attributes along the edge and then the area (width, height and attribute).
FrameCornerTable CBF8 DEFB $00,$BE,$00,$BF,$1E,$01,$4D Bottom left
CBFF DEFB $E8,$BE,$00,$17,$1E,$01,$4D Bottom right
CC06 DEFB $00,$17,$00,$B7,$01,$14,$4D Top left
CC0D DEFB $E8,$17,$E8,$B7,$01,$14,$4D Top right
