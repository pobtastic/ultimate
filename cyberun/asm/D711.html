D711: Finish Step
Used by the routines at DrawSlopeRows, DrawSlopeRowsFromEdge, DrawMirroredRowsFromEdge, DrawMirroredSlopeRows, DrawOneByteRows, DrawThreeByteRows, DrawTwoByteRows, DrawMirroredThreeByteRows, DrawMirroredTwoByteRows and DrawMirroredOneByteRows.
Finishes the drawing of a step. The screen address that the drawing has reached is stored in SegmentScreenAddress (at FinishStep only), the number of steps drawn is counted and the next step of the segment is started, until the segment has no steps left.
Input
HL Screen address
FinishStep D711 LD ($CE49),HL Update the segment screen address.
