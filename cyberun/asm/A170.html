A170: Find Attribute Address
Used by the routines at PrintText, UpdateStarLayer, DrawShipTop, FillAttributes, CopyAttributes and ColourSegment.
Finds the address in the attribute file of a pixel position. The vertical position is the number of the row of pixels, counted from the top of the screen.
Input
H Vertical position
L Horizontal position
Output
HL Attribute address
AttributeAddress A170 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
A171 LD A,L Fetch the horizontal position.
A172 RRC A Divide it by eight.
A174 RRC A
A176 RRC A
A178 AND %00011111 Keep the five bits of the column.
A17A LD L,A Store it in L.
A17B LD A,H Fetch the vertical position.
A17C RLC A Multiply it by four.
A17E RLC A
A180 LD C,A Keep a copy.
A181 AND %11100000 Keep the bits that give the row of the cell.
A183 OR L Combine them with the column.
A184 LD L,A Store the low byte of the address.
A185 LD A,C Fetch the multiplied vertical position.
A186 AND %00000011 Keep the bits that give the third of the screen.
A188 OR %01011000 Add the address of the attribute file.
A18A LD H,A Store the high byte of the address.
A18B POP BC Bring BC back off the stack.
A18C RET Return.
