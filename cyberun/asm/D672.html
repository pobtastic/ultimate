D672: Locate Picture
Used by the routines at SetUpPiece and SetUpFlatPiece.
Finds the picture for the piece. The offset of the picture is looked up in the table that SegmentPictureOffsets points at, using the value in E as the index, and added to the address of the picture at PicturePointer. The stack pointer is pointed at the result, so that the drawing routines can pop the words of the picture. The routine then works out how many rows to draw. If the piece is at the bottom of the screen the rows that are below the bottom are skipped by popping their words.
Input
E Index into the table of offsets, in words
