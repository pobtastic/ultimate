CACB: Draw Frame
Used by the routines at DrawHUD and ShowTitleScreen.
Draws the frame round the playfield from the values in the table at FrameParameters: a vertical line at each side, a double horizontal line at the top and bottom, a graphic at each of the four corners with a coloured line along each edge, and then the ticks along the edges.
Input
IX Address of the table (FrameParameters)
DrawFrame CACB LD L,(IX+$00) The low byte of the address of the left side.
CACE LD H,(IX+$01) The high byte of the address of the left side.
CAD1 LD E,(IX+$02) The low byte of the address of the right side.
CAD4 LD D,(IX+$03) The high byte of the address of the right side.
CAD7 LD B,(IX+$04) The number of rows in the two sides.
DrawFrame_Sides CADA LD (HL),$66 Plot the patterned byte 66 on the left side.
CADC LD A,$66 The same byte for the right side.
CADE LD (DE),A Plot it on the right side.
CADF CALL MoveUpARow Move the left side up one pixel row.
CAE2 CALL MoveUpARowDE Move the right side up one pixel row.
CAE5 DJNZ DrawFrame_Sides Repeat for each row of the sides.
CAE7 LD L,(IX+$05) The low byte of the address of the bottom rules.
CAEA LD H,(IX+$06) The high byte of the address of the bottom rules.
CAED LD E,(IX+$07) The low byte of the address of the top rules.
CAF0 LD D,(IX+$08) The high byte of the address of the top rules.
CAF3 CALL DrawLinePair Draw one pixel row of the top and bottom rules.
CAF6 CALL DrawLinePair Draw the next pixel row of those rules.
CAF9 DEC H Move the bottom rule up one pixel row.
CAFA DEC H Move the bottom rule up another pixel row.
CAFB DEC D Move the top rule up one pixel row.
CAFC DEC D Move the top rule up another pixel row.
CAFD CALL DrawLinePair Draw one pixel row of the inner rules.
CB00 CALL DrawLinePair Draw the next pixel row of the inner rules.
CB03 LD DE,$CC14 The corner graphics start at FrameCornerGraphics.
CB06 LD ($CE3A),DE Keep that address for the next corner.
CB0A LD E,(IX+$09) The low byte of the address of the corner table.
CB0D LD D,(IX+$0A) The high byte of the address of the corner table.
CB10 LD B,$04 There are four corners.
DrawFrame_Corners CB12 PUSH BC Keep the number of corners still to draw.
CB13 CALL FetchPosition Read the position of this corner graphic.
CB16 PUSH DE Keep the place in the corner table.
CB17 LD DE,($CE3A) The graphic for this corner.
CB1B CALL DrawBitmap Draw the corner graphic.
CB1E LD ($CE3A),DE The next corner graphic follows this one.
CB22 POP DE Back to the corner table.
CB23 CALL FetchPosition Read the position of the coloured edge.
CB26 CALL FillAttributes Colour that edge.
CB29 POP BC Bring back the number of corners.
CB2A DJNZ DrawFrame_Corners Repeat for each corner.
CB2C LD B,(IX+$0B) The number of ticks along each edge.
CB2F LD E,(IX+$0C) The low byte of the address of the tick table.
CB32 LD D,(IX+$0D) The high byte of the address of the tick table.
DrawFrame_Ticks CB35 PUSH BC Keep the number of ticks still to draw.
CB36 LD A,(DE) The pixel column of this tick.
CB37 LD L,A That column is its horizontal position.
CB38 LD H,(IX+$0E) The pixel row of the top edge.
CB3B PUSH DE Keep the place in the tick table.
CB3C LD DE,$C94D The top and bottom tick is HorizontalTickGraphic.
CB3F CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the top edge.
CB42 LD H,$BE The bottom edge is pixel row BE.
CB44 LD DE,$C94D The same top and bottom tick.
CB47 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the bottom edge.
CB4A POP DE Back to the tick table.
CB4B INC DE The next byte is the pixel row of the side ticks.
CB4C LD A,(DE) That row.
CB4D LD H,A It is their vertical position.
CB4E LD L,$00 The left edge is pixel column zero.
CB50 PUSH DE Keep the place in the tick table.
CB51 LD DE,$C955 The side tick is VerticalTickGraphic.
CB54 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the left edge.
CB57 LD L,(IX+$0F) The pixel column of the right edge.
CB5A LD DE,$C955 The same side tick.
CB5D CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the right edge.
CB60 POP DE Back to the tick table.
CB61 INC DE Move on to the next tick.
CB62 POP BC Bring back the number of ticks.
CB63 DJNZ DrawFrame_Ticks Repeat for each tick.
CB65 RET Return.
