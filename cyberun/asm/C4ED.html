C4ED: Draw Ship Top
Used by the routines at UpdateCrystalStack and ExplodeShip.
Draws the top of the ship, which is one of the six sprites for types 01 to 06: the one that is used depends on the horizontal speed of the ship, and type 06 is used while the ship has just been hit. Then it checks for the pause keys with CheckPauseAndSilence and fires the weapons with FireWeapons. Carries on with DrawMainFlame or DrawSideFlames on alternate frames. This is a best reading of the code.
