917C: Check Shot Collisions
Used by the routine at UpdateEnemies.
Checks whether any of the four objects at ShotSlots has hit the object described by the hit data at CollisionBox. If one has, bit 7 of its flags is set and effects are applied to the object that was hit.
Input
D Collision flag from A1D8
IX Address of the object that was hit
