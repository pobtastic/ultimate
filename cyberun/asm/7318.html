7318: Play Removal Sound
Used by the routine at DestroyObject.
Plays a rising sweep of ten beeps. It is used when an object is removed.
PlayRemovalSound 7318 LD C,$0A The removal sweep has ten beeps.
731A LD D,$18 The lowest beep has half-period 18.
731C JP PlaySweep Jump to PlaySweep.
