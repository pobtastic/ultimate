B731: Fire Weapons
Used by the routine at DrawShipTop.
Checks the controls for the fire key. The extra key is dealt with by CheckSpecialShot first. When fire is pressed, and the six frames since the last shot have passed, a shot is launched.
FireWeapons B731 CALL CheckSpecialShot Call CheckSpecialShot.
B734 LD A,($5C39) The frames before the next shot.
B737 AND A Jump to FireWeapons_Fire if A is zero.
B738 JR Z,FireWeapons_Fire
B73A DEC A Step A back.
B73B LD ($5C39),A One fewer frame before the next shot.
B73E RET Return.
FireWeapons_Fire B73F LD A,($5C37) The controls.
B742 BIT 4,A Return if bit 4 of A is clear.
B744 RET Z
B745 LD A,$06 A=06.
B747 LD ($5C39),A Six frames before the next shot.
B74A LD A,($B730) The weapon toggle.
B74D XOR %00000001 Flip the bits of A that are set in 01.
B74F LD ($B730),A Update the weapon toggle.
Continue on to LaunchShot.
