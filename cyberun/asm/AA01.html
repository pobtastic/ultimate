AA01: Bounce Horizontally
Used by the routine at CheckMapObjectCollisions.
Makes the ship rebound after hitting something to its left or right: the horizontal speed of the ship is reversed and halved by ReverseAndHalveSpeed, and the horizontal scroll speed is negated.
BounceHorizontally AA01 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
AA02 LD HL,($C8EC) Fetch the word at C8EC into HL.
AA05 CALL ReverseAndHalveSpeed Call ReverseAndHalveSpeed.
AA08 LD ($C8EC),HL Update the high byte of the ship's horizontal speed.
AA0B POP HL Bring HL back off the stack.
AA0C LD A,($CCBB) The horizontal scroll speed.
AA0F NEG Negate A.
AA11 LD ($CCBB),A Update the horizontal scroll speed.
AA14 RET Return.
