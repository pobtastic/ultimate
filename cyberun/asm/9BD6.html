9BD6: Beam Variables
The state shared by the four flickering beams drawn by UpdateBeams.
BeamVariables 9BD6 DEFB $00 The pattern of the beams, which is zero while they are switched off.
9BD7 DEFB $00 The attribute of the beams.
9BD8 DEFB $01 The number of frames until the beams change.
9BD9 DEFB $00 Set to 01 when at least one beam was drawn this frame.
