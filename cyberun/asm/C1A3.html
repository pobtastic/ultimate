C1A3: Star Layer Types
Two bytes for each layer of stars, of which the second is the base type of the stars in the layer. The type of a star is that plus bit 0 of its horizontal position.
StarLayerTypes C1A3 DEFB $07,$07
C1A5 DEFB $09,$09
C1A7 DEFB $0B,$0B
