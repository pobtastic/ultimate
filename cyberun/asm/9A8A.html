9A8A: Mask Map Table
Used by the routines at CompletePlanet and StartNewGame.
Applies a mask to every byte of the table at MapTable.
Input
E Mask
MaskMapTable 9A8A LD BC,$0122 Set the number of bytes in BC.
9A8D LD HL,$5C94 Use the table.
MaskMapTable_Loop 9A90 LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the table.
9A91 AND E Apply the mask.
9A92 LD (HL),A Store the byte.
9A93 INC HL Move on to the next one.
9A94 DEC BC Decrease the counter.
9A95 LD A,B Combine B and C.
9A96 OR C
9A97 JR NZ,MaskMapTable_Loop Jump back to MaskMapTable_Loop until the counter is zero.
9A99 RET Return.
