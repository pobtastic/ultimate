DB29: Move Up Edge Rows
Used by the routines at FillTwoEdgeRows and FillFourEdgeRows.
The end of the routines that fill the edge: moves up the rows that are left in the step and then goes round for the next step.
Input
B Number of rows already filled

DB2E LD A,($CE44) The segment row.
DB31 SUB B Subtract B from A.
DB32 LD ($CE44),A Update the segment row.
DB35 SRL B Shift B right.
MoveUpEdgeRows_Up DB37 DEC H Step H back.
DB38 CALL MoveUpARow Call MoveUpARow.
DB3B DJNZ MoveUpEdgeRows_Up Decrease B and jump to MoveUpEdgeRows_Up until none remain.
