B3DA: Shot Offsets
Two tables of eight bytes. The offset in the shot is added to the address of one of them by AddShotOffset.
ShotOffsets B3DA DEFB $4D,$9E,$7C,$7F,$3F,$E7,$FF,$FF
ShotOffsets_Second B3E2 DEFB $FF,$FF,$E7,$FC,$FE,$3E,$79,$B2
