725C: Play Change Sound
Used by the routine at ColourShip.
Plays a rising sweep of 0A beeps. It is used when the colour of the ship changes.
PlayChangeSound 725C LD C,$0A The colour-change sweep has 0A beeps.
725E LD D,$80 The lowest beep has half-period 80.
7260 JP PlaySweep Jump to PlaySweep.
