B8FC: Clear Shot
Used by the routines at ClearObjectSpeeds, FreeObjectSlot, LaunchShot and CheckTerrainCollision.
Clears offsets 0B, 0C, 0E and 0F of a shot.
Input
IX Address of the shot
ClearShot B8FC XOR A Clear A.
B8FD LD (IX+$0B),A Write the high byte of the horizontal acceleration.
B900 LD (IX+$0C),A Write the low byte of the horizontal acceleration.
B903 LD (IX+$0E),A Write the high byte of the vertical acceleration.
B906 LD (IX+$0F),A Write the low byte of the vertical acceleration.
B909 RET Return.
