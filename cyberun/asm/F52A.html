F52A: Finish Planet
Used by the routine at RunLife.
Used when the planet has been completed. It clears the playfield, runs CompletePlanet and plays a tune. After a short delay it clears the playfield and the map cells at CurrentPlayerState_MapCells again, and carries on with PrepareLife.
FinishPlanet F52A CALL ClearPlayfield Call ClearPlayfield.
F52D CALL CompletePlanet Call CompletePlanet.
