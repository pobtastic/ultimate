C6D2: Draw Main Flame
Used by the routines at UpdateCrystalStack, DrawShipTop and ExplodeShip.
Does nothing unless part 05 has been fitted. Draws the flame under the ship, from the object at MainFlameObject: a larger one (type 11) when going up and a smaller one (type 12) when not. This is a best reading of the code.
