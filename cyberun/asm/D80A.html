D80A: Next Segment
Used by the routine at DrawTerrain.
Moves on to the next segment of the script, using the address that ReadSegmentShapes stored in ScriptPointer, until the last has been drawn. Then the next tile is started: the vertical offset (and for the third tile the horizontal offset) is changed to move to the position of that tile. After the fourth tile the horizontal offset is put back.
NextSegment D80A LD SP,$5B7A Set the stack pointer to 5B7A.
D80D LD HL,($CE4D) Fetch the word at ScriptPointer into HL.
D810 LD A,($5C20) The segments still to draw.
D813 DEC A Step A back.
D814 LD ($5C20),A Update the segments still to draw.
D817 JP NZ,DrawTerrain_Segment Jump to DrawTerrain_Segment if the zero flag is clear.
