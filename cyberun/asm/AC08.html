AC08: Find Cell Bit
Used by the routines at UpdateBeams and MarkCellDestroyed.
Finds the byte of the bitmap at CurrentPlayerState_MapCells and the bit within it for a cell of the planet. Each of the three bytes of the bitmap holds the bits for four zones of the map, and each zone has two bits: one for the left half and one for the right half.
Input
D Map zone
E Horizontal position
Output
HL Address of the byte
B The bit number multiplied by eight
