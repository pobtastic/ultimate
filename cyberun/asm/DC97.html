DC97: Planet Attributes
The attributes for the sky and for the underground of each of the five planets, in that order. The last byte is not used, so the second attribute of the fifth planet is taken from the code at BeginLife.
PlanetAttributes DC97 DEFB $43,$06
DC99 DEFB $44,$46
DC9B DEFB $47,$43
DC9D DEFB $46,$47
DC9F DEFB $45
DCA0 DEFB $44
