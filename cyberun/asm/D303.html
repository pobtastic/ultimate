D303: Next Record
Used by the routine at DrawTerrain.
Moves on to the next five byte record of a segment in the tile record and stores it in BA09, then points IX back at the drawing record at ScrollWorld_Record with its screen address cleared.
Input
IX Address of the record of the segment
NextRecord D303 LD DE,$0005 DE=05.
D306 ADD IX,DE IX gains DE.
D308 LD ($BA09),IX The next segment record.
D30C LD IX,$DEDC The segment record.
D310 EXX Switch to the alternate registers.
D311 LD (IX+$01),$00 Store 00 at offset 01 of IX.
D315 LD (IX+$03),B Write B to offset 03 of IX.
D318 RET Return.
