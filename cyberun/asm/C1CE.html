C1CE: Check Item Delivery
Used by the routines at UpdateCrystalStack, RunFrame and ExplodeShip.
Does nothing unless the flag at F5F7 is set, which happens when item number 09 has been picked up. Once the ship is at the bottom of zone 0B, the item is delivered: it is no longer carried, and two pieces are made with CreatePiece in the last two slots of the map objects. Either way, carries on to draw the two pieces that are held at PieceVariables_FirstSprite and PieceVariables_SecondSprite and to set their attributes. This is a best reading of the code.
