C10E: Check Star Position
Used by the routine at UpdateStarLayer.
Finds the position of a star on the screen, from its position and the offset of its layer. It is stored in A1D5 and A1D6. In the third zone a star is also left out if it is below the level of the ground.
Output
F The zero flag is set if the star is not on the screen
