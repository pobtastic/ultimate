9EF5: Fade Out And Clear Playfield
Used by the routine at ExplodeShip.
Dissolves the playfield pixel by pixel and then clears it.
FadeOutAndClearPlayfield 9EF5 LD HL,$B708 Use the bottom left of the playfield.
9EF8 CALL FadeOutPlayfield Dissolve it.
9EFB JP ClearPlayfield Clear the playfield.
