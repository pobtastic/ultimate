BE26: Select Next Column
Used by the routine at SelectNextTile.
Sets the tile to look at next to the one in the column to the right of the current one, at the left of it, for SelectNextTile. The column goes round to zero after 5F.
SelectNextColumn BE26 LD A,($5C2A) The object reaches into the next column.
BE29 LD ($5C2C),A Update the segment screen column.
BE2C LD HL,($5C2E) Fetch the word at PieceColumn into HL.
BE2F INC H Advance H.
BE30 LD A,H A=H.
BE31 CP $60 Jump to SelectNextColumn_Store if A is not 60.
BE33 JR NZ,SelectNextColumn_Store
BE35 LD H,$00 Clear H.
SelectNextColumn_Store BE37 LD L,$08 L=08.
BE39 LD ($BF7F),HL Update the tile position.
BE3C LD A,($CE44) The segment row.
BE3F LD ($5C2D),A Update the segment screen row.
BE42 RET Return.
