AD27: Prepare Erase
Used by the routines at CountActiveEnemy, UpdateObject, UpdateCrystalStack and UpdateMapObjects.
Sets up the erasing of the old sprite of an object, from its position on the screen at the last frame. Also clears the visible flag of the object (bit 7 of offset 10) and keeps the wrap flag (bit 6) in SpriteVariables_RightEdge.
Input
IX Address of the object
PrepareErase AD27 CALL FetchFrameSprite Call FetchFrameSprite.
AD2A LD L,(IX+$11) The horizontal screen position.
AD2D LD H,(IX+$12) The vertical screen position.
AD30 LD ($A1D3),HL Update the sprite positions.
AD33 CALL ClipFirstSprite Call ClipFirstSprite.
AD36 CALL SelectEraseSprite Call SelectEraseSprite.
AD39 RES 7,(IX+$10) Reset bit 7 of offset 10 of IX.
AD3D LD A,(IX+$10) The flags.
AD40 AND %01000000 Keep the bits of A that are set in 40.
AD42 LD ($A2FA),A The wrap flag.
AD45 RET Return.
