CC63: Check Fuel Empty
Used by the routine at UpdateFuelGauge.
Used when the fuel gauge has reached the level of the fuel. If the ship is in its normal state, the gauge is at the empty row and the bottom seven bits of the frame counter are zero, the ship is put in state 1: out of fuel.
Input
B Pixel row of the fuel gauge
CheckFuelEmpty CC63 LD A,($8F53) The state of the ship.
CC66 AND A Return unless the ship is flying normally.
CC67 RET NZ
CC68 LD A,B The row of the needle.
CC69 CP $BE Return unless the needle is on the empty row.
CC6B RET NZ
CC6C LD A,($9A64) The frame counter.
CC6F AND %01111111 The bottom seven bits.
CC71 RET NZ Return unless they are all clear.
CC72 INC A The out-of-fuel state is one.
CC73 LD ($8F53),A Put the ship in that state.
CC76 RET Return.
