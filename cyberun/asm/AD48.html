AD48: Prepare Draw
Used by the routines at ProcessObjectFlags, UpdateObject and UpdateMapObjects.
Sets up the drawing of the new sprite of an object. Finds the sprite from its type and state, and its position on the screen (which frees the slot if it is off screen). If the object is exploding (bit 3 of offset 17), the explosion count in the bottom three bits is increased and the slot is freed when it reaches eight; the count is also used as the dissolve step in A302. The attribute of the sprite is taken from offset 0D, which holds two colours; the choice between them depends on bit 0 of the frame counter at FrameCounter.
