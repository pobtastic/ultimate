CEB5: Record Left Contact
Used by the routine at NextStep.
Used when the ship is in the same column as a step of a segment. When the horizontal speed is negative it sets bit 3 of the contact flags at ContactFlags and carries on at NextStep_Flags. Otherwise the next step is drawn.
FlagLeftContact CEB5 LD A,($CCBB) The horizontal scroll speed.
CEB8 BIT 7,A Jump to FinishStep_Next if bit 7 of A is clear.
CEBA JP Z,FinishStep_Next
CEBD LD A,($5C31) The contact flags.
CEC0 OR %00001000 Set the bits of A that are set in 08.
CEC2 LD ($5C31),A Update the contact flags.
CEC5 JP NextStep_Flags Jump to NextStep_Flags.
