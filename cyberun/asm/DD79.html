DD79: Look For Ground
Used by the routine at ScrollWorld.
Used while the world is scrolling in at the start of a life. If the flag at ScrollInFlag is 03 the scroll is finished (see FinishScrollIn). If not, the vertical scroll speed is set to 04. If the flag at ChequeredFloorFlag is zero the scroll lasts for the number of steps that are left in B. Otherwise it looks at the six bytes of the screen where the ship is, and at the row above them, and goes on scrolling until they are all blank. Then the flag is set to 03, the playfield is coloured and the tiles are made again.
LookForGround DD79 LD A,($5C3C) The scroll-in flag.
DD7C CP $03 Jump to FinishScrollIn if A is 03.
DD7E JP Z,FinishScrollIn
DD81 LD A,$04 A=04.
DD83 LD ($CCBA),A Update the vertical scroll speed.
DD86 LD A,($DDF2) The chequered floor flag.
