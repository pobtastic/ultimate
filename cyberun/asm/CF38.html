CF38: Colour Segment
Used by the routine at FinishSegment.
Colours the attribute cells that a segment covers, one step at a time. Nothing is coloured if ScrollInFlag is set or if the drawing record has no screen address or no length. The number of cells across at each step (one to three) chooses the routine that paints the cells of a column: a flat segment is painted three cells across. The colour is TopTileColour for a tile on the top row and BottomTileColour for one on the bottom row, with the colours at SkyAttribute and UndergroundAttribute used for some of the tiles on map rows 0B and 0C.
