8F54: Damage Ship
Used by the routine at ApplyCollisionEffects.
Damages the player's ship. A ship that has just been hit cannot be hit again for a few frames. Each hit changes the colour of the damage indicator, and the third hit destroys the ship.
Input
IX Address of the object that was hit

8F5F LD (IX+$17),A Store them.

8F6F SET 5,(IX+$10) Set bit 5 of the flags of the object.
