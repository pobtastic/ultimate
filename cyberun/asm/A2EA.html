A2EA: Reset Sprites
Used by the routines at UpdateEnemies, UpdateObject, UpdateCrystalStack, UpdateMapObjects and UpdateStarLayer.
Sets both sprites to have no rows and no width, and clears the flag that makes DrawSprites clip at the edge of the playfield.
ResetSprites A2EA LD HL,$0000 Clear HL.
A2ED LD ($A1D7),HL Set the number of rows of both sprites to zero.
A2F0 LD ($5C28),HL Set the width of both sprites to zero.
A2F3 XOR A Clear A.
A2F4 LD ($A2FA),A Clear the flag at SpriteVariables_RightEdge.
A2F7 RET Return.
