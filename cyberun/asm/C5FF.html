C5FF: Draw Side Flames
Used by the routines at CheckPauseAndSilence and DrawShipTop.
Does nothing unless part 04 has been fitted. Draws the flame at the side of the ship when it is moving to the left or to the right, from the object at SideFlameObject, and sets its attribute with SetFlameAttributes. This is a best reading of the code.
