71FE: Play Pick-Up Sound
Used by the routine at CheckItemPickup.
Plays the sound of the ship picking up an object, which is a rising sweep of 10 beeps.
PlayPickUpSound 71FE LD C,$10 The pick-up sweep has 10 beeps.
7200 LD D,$40 The lowest beep has half-period 40.
