C991: Fill Attributes
Used by the routines at DrawMenuOption, DamageShip, UpdateBeams, SetObjectAttributes, DrawShot, DrawSideFlames, DrawHUD, ColourFuelGauge, DrawFrame, ColourShip and ColourPlayfield.
Fills an area of the attributes with one value. The area starts with its width, its height and the attribute. The position is that of the bottom row of the area, and the rows are filled going up the screen.
Input
DE Address of the area
HL Position (H is the pixel row and L is the pixel column)
