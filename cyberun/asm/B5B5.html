B5B5: Draw Shot
Used by the routine at UpdateSpecialShot.
The end of the routines that update a shot. Moves the shot by the vertical scroll speed, then erases the old picture of the shot with EraseRows and draws the new one with DrawRows. If the shot has ended, its slot is made free; if it was a special shot that ends the flag at B832 is cleared. Otherwise the area of the screen under it is given its attribute by SetShotAttributes.
