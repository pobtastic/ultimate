8708: Control Icon Data
Two sets of three bytes: the X position, the current Y position and the control method selected for each player.
ControlIconPlayerOne 8708 DEFB $38,$6F,$00
ControlIconPlayerTwo 870B DEFB $B8,$6F,$00
