CCBF: Picture Offsets
Six bytes for each of the nine types of slope listed at SlopeParameters. The terrain drawing routine at DrawTerrain points SegmentPictureOffsets at the entry for the type of the current segment and LocatePicture uses the third and later bytes as offsets into the picture of the segment. The first two bytes are always zero. The values are repeated by the types that are the same shape.
