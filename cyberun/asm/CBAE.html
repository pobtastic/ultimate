CBAE: Draw Fuel Gauge Frame
Used by the routine at DrawHUD.
Draws the outline of the fuel gauge: a cap at the bottom and one at the top, and a column of dots down the right of the gauge.
DrawFuelGaugeFrame CBAE LD HL,$BFF0 The bottom of the gauge is at BFF0.
CBB1 LD DE,$CBDB The bottom cap is at FuelGaugeCaps_Second.
CBB4 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the bottom cap.
CBB7 LD HL,$43F0 The top of the gauge is at 43F0.
CBBA LD DE,$CBD1 The top cap is at FuelGaugeCaps.
CBBD CALL DrawBitmap Draw the top cap.
CBC0 LD HL,$53FE The column of dots starts at 53FE.
CBC3 LD B,$78 There are 78 rows of dots.
DrawFuelGaugeFrame_Dots CBC5 LD (HL),$10 Plot the left-hand dot.
CBC7 INC HL Move across to the right-hand dot.
CBC8 LD (HL),$10 Plot the right-hand dot.
CBCA DEC HL Back to the left-hand dot.
CBCB CALL MoveUpARow Move up one pixel row.
CBCE DJNZ DrawFuelGaugeFrame_Dots Repeat for every row of the gauge.
CBD0 RET Return.
