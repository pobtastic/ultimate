7721: Stack Graphic
The picture of the units that the ship carries as a stack: eighteen rows of two bytes, with no width or height. BuildStackGraphic copies as many rows as the stack is high (see StackHeight) into the ship work area at ShipWorkArea (starting at F6BB), each six bytes after the last. The data follows the picture of ship part 3, so AddShipPart also reads it as the mask of that part.
