9BCB: Random Numbers
The two random bytes made by the routine at RandomNumber, followed by the address of the next byte of memory it will mix in (high byte first).
RandomNumbers 9BCB DEFB $17 First random byte.
9BCC DEFB $8E Second random byte.
9BCD DEFB $A0,$00 Address of the next byte to mix in.
