A669: Next Row Of Second Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawSpritesDirectly.
Used by DrawSpritesDirectly when the first sprite has no rows left: it carries on with the rows of the second sprite until they are done.
NextRowOfSecondSprite A669 EX AF,AF' Switch to the count of the second sprite.
A66A DEC A Take one off.
A66B RET M Return if there are none left.
A66C EX AF,AF' Switch back.
A66D JP (IX) Draw a row of the second sprite.
