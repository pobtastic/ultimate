D319: Next Step
Used by the routine at FinishStep.
Moves to the next step of a segment: the pixel row goes up by the vertical step and the pixel column along by the horizontal step. Then the step is tested against the position at PositionAcross and PositionDown, and the flags in ContactFlags are set to say where they are in relation to each other.
