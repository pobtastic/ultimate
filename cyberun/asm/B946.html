B946: Find Tile Record
Used by the routine at FindTilesOverlapped.
Looks for the record of the tile at a position of the planet, in the four records that are kept at GameEntry and then every 25 bytes, which are for the tile at the map position in MapPosition and the ones next to it. If none of them match, the address of an empty record is returned.
Input
B Map zone
D Map column
Output
IX Address of the record of the tile
F The zero flag is set if the tile was found
