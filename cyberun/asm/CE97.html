CE97: Record Ceiling Contact
Used by the routine at NextStep.
Used when the ship is on the same row as a step of a segment in the bottom half of the tile. It sets bit 7 of the contact flags at ContactFlags. If the vertical speed is negative it also sets bit 1 (clearing bit 7) and copies the flags into LastContactFlags. It carries on at NextStep_Column.
FlagCeilingContact CE97 LD A,($5C31) The contact flags.
CE9A OR %10000000 Set the bits of A that are set in 80.
CE9C LD ($5C31),A Update the contact flags.
CE9F LD A,($CCBA) The vertical scroll speed.
CEA2 BIT 7,A Jump to NextStep_Column if bit 7 of A is clear.
CEA4 JP Z,NextStep_Column
CEA7 LD A,($5C31) The contact flags.
CEAA XOR %10000010 Flip the bits of A that are set in 82.
CEAC LD ($5C31),A Update the contact flags.
CEAF LD ($5C32),A Update the last contact flags.
CEB2 JP NextStep_Column Jump to NextStep_Column.
