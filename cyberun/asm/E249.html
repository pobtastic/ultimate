E249: Draw Flat Rows
Draws a flat piece of terrain, three bytes wide, going up the screen two pixel rows at a time. The words of the picture are popped from the stack (which DrawTerrain has pointed at the picture) and stored in a zigzag, so that a word is two screen bytes next to each other. The entry point at DrawFlatRows moves one column to the right and carries on at DrawFlatRows_Start. The three JR instructions at DrawFlatRows_Next, DrawFlatRows_PatchSecond and DrawFlatRows_PatchThird have their displacements set by DrawTerrain to choose how much of the three bytes is drawn. When one is changed to 33 or to EE the row is drawn from DrawFlatRows_NextColumn or DrawFlatRows, and when DrawFlatRows_PatchSecond or DrawFlatRows_PatchThird is changed to 24 or 26 the word is skipped (DrawFlatRows_SkipWord and DrawFlatRows_SkipOne).
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom left of the piece
SP Address of the picture
