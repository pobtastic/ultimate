BEE7: Find Tiles Overlapped
Used by the routine at SelectNextTile.
Finds the tile record for the position of the object using FindTileRecord, works out where the object is within the tile and stores it in ScratchValues to CE3D and counts the tiles that it overlaps (up to four) in TileNumber.
