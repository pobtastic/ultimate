71E0: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayArrivalSound, PlayWarble, PlayFlutterSound, PlayHitSound and PlayBumpSound.
Makes one cycle of a square wave with the speaker. The speaker is on for as long as it is off, which is set by B: the higher it is, the lower the note.
Input
B Half the length of the cycle
Output
B Unchanged
PlaySquareWave 71E0 LD A,$10 Turn the speaker on.
71E2 OUT ($FE),A Send that to the speaker.
71E4 LD A,B Keep the half-period.
PlaySquareWave_Delay 71E5 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Delay Hold this half of the cycle.
71E7 LD B,A Restore the half-period.
71E8 XOR A Turn the speaker off.
71E9 OUT ($FE),A Send that to the speaker.
71EB LD A,B Keep the half-period again.
PlaySquareWave_Wait 71EC DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Wait Hold the other half of the cycle.
71EE LD B,A Restore the half-period.
71EF RET Return.
