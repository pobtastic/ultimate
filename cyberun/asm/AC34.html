AC34: Free Object Slot
Used by the routines at FireShot, SpawnEnemy, StartDebrisPiece, StartCrystalObject, LaunchShot, CreatePiece, AddMapObjects and ResetLife.
Clears the fields of an object slot to zero, then marks the slot as free by setting its type to FF.
