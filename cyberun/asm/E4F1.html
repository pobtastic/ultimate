E4F1: Draw Mirrored Three Byte Rows
Draws a piece of terrain that is three bytes wide in the same way as DrawThreeByteRows, but passes every byte through the table of reversed bytes at F900 so that the piece is drawn back to front. The entry points at DrawMirroredThreeByteRows and DrawMirroredThreeByteRows_Two first move to the left by three columns and two columns.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom left of the piece
SP Address of the picture
DrawMirroredThreeByteRows E4F1 DEC L Step L back.
DrawMirroredThreeByteRows_Two E4F2 DEC L Step L back.
E4F3 DEC L Step L back.
DrawMirroredThreeByteRows_Start E4F4 LD B,$F9 B=F9.
E4F6 EXX Switch to the alternate registers.
E4F7 LD B,A B=A.
DrawMirroredThreeByteRows_Loop E4F8 EXX Switch to the alternate registers.
E4F9 POP DE Bring DE back off the stack.
E4FA LD C,E C=E.
E4FB LD A,(BC) Read BC.
E4FC LD (HL),A Write A where HL points.

E4FE LD C,D C=D.
E4FF LD A,(BC) Read BC.

E504 LD A,(BC) Read BC.

E506 DEC H Step H back.

E509 LD (HL),A Write A where HL points.
