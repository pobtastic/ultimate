A1D3: Sprite Positions
The positions and sizes of the two sprites that are drawn together by DrawSprites. The first sprite is the one that is erased and the second sprite is the one that is drawn.
SpritePositions A1D3 DEFB $00 Horizontal position of the first sprite.
A1D4 DEFB $00 Vertical position of the first sprite.
A1D5 DEFB $00 Horizontal position of the second sprite.
A1D6 DEFB $00 Vertical position of the second sprite.
A1D7 DEFB $00 Number of rows of the first sprite.
A1D8 DEFB $00 Number of rows of the second sprite.
