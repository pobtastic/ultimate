5E17: Terrain Data
Two bytes for each of the objects on the map, in order of their column. The top six bits of the first byte are the vertical position of the object (one more than its pixel row, in multiples of four) and the bottom two are bits 3 and 4 of its horizontal position. The second byte has the map row in its bottom five bits and the top three bits of the horizontal position in its top three bits. AddMapObjects decodes them.
