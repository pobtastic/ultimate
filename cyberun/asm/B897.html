B897: Make Shot Row
Used by the routine at UpdateSpecialShot.
Builds the picture of a shot from one of the six patterns in the table at ShotPatternTable, picked by the bottom four bits of offset 15, which go round from zero to five as the shot is updated. The picture is made in the buffer at F800 and its size is stored in A1D7. MakeShotColumn does the same for the buffer at BuildTables_Buffer and A1D8.
Input
IX Address of the shot
