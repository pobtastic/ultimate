E062: Free Distant Slots
Used by the routine at MakeTiles.
Frees any slots of objects that are not on one of the two columns and two rows that are on the screen.
Input
B Number of slots
IX Address of the first slot
FreeDistantSlots E062 LD A,($DDF7) The next column.
E065 LD C,A C=A.
E066 LD DE,$001A DE=1A.
E069 LD HL,($CE57) Fetch the word at MapPosition into HL.
E06C INC H Advance H.
FreeDistantSlots_Loop E06D LD A,(IX+$00) Read offset 00 of IX.
E070 INC A Advance A.
E071 JR Z,FreeDistantSlots_Next Jump to FreeDistantSlots_Next if the zero flag is set.
E073 LD A,(IX+$01) Read offset 01 of IX.
E076 CP L Jump to FreeDistantSlots_Row if A is L.
E077 JR Z,FreeDistantSlots_Row
E079 CP C Jump to FreeDistantSlots_Free if A is not C.
E07A JR NZ,FreeDistantSlots_Free
FreeDistantSlots_Row E07C LD A,(IX+$04) Read offset 04 of IX.
E07F CP H Jump to FreeDistantSlots_Next if A is H.
E080 JR Z,FreeDistantSlots_Next
E082 INC A Advance A.
E083 CP H Jump to FreeDistantSlots_Next if A is H.
E084 JR Z,FreeDistantSlots_Next
FreeDistantSlots_Free E086 LD (IX+$00),$FF Store FF at offset 00 of IX.
FreeDistantSlots_Next E08A ADD IX,DE IX gains DE.
E08C DJNZ FreeDistantSlots_Loop Decrease B and jump to FreeDistantSlots_Loop until none remain.
E08E RET Return.
