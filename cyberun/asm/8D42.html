8D42: Update Enemies
Used by the routines at RunLife and ExplodeShip.
Updates all nine enemy slots once a frame. The counter at 8F53 tracks the explosion of the player's ship: it counts up from one when the ship is destroyed, the debris is started when it reaches ten, and the ship is gone for good when it has gone past that point.
UpdateEnemies 8D42 LD A,($8F53) Fetch the explosion counter from 8F53.

8D49 LD ($8F53),A ...and store it.
UpdateEnemies_Debris 8D4C CP $0A Has the counter reached ten?
