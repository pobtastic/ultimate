D937: Find Segment Address
Used by the routines at NoteWallContact and SetUpPiece.
Finds the screen address of the start of the current segment from its pixel position in SegmentColumn and SegmentRow and the offsets in HorizontalOffset and VerticalOffset. The pixel position is stored in the drawing record at ScrollWorld_Record and in SegmentScreenColumn and SegmentScreenRow. If no step of the segment has been flagged (FlaggedSteps is zero) the address is also stored in SavedScreenAddress with the pixel row in SavedRow.
Input
IX Address of the drawing record at ScrollWorld_Record
Output
HL Screen address
