F4EE: Print Player
Used by the routines at RunGame, GameOver and SwapPlayers.
Prints the number of the current player, which is player one or player two, with the text at TextPlayer.
PrintPlayer F4EE LD A,($CA1C) The current player.
F4F1 ADD A,$1B Add 1B to A.
F4F3 LD ($F5E6),A The digit of the player.
F4F6 LD DE,$F5DB The text: player.
F4F9 JP PrintText Jump to PrintText.
