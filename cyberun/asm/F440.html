F440: Prepare Life
Used by the routines at ExplodeShip and FinishPlanet.
Resets the ship and everything else for the next life. If there are no ships left the game goes back to the title screen. If there are, the HUD is drawn again.
PrepareLife F440 CALL ResetLife Call ResetLife.
F443 LD A,($F5ED) The ships remaining.
F446 AND A Jump to RunGame if A is zero.
F447 JR Z,RunGame
F449 CALL DrawHUD Call DrawHUD.
Continue on to PlayLife.
