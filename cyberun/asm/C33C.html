C33C: Save Restart Position
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Called when the bottom two bits of the frame counter are zero. The position of the ship is saved when it is safe to restart there: that is when the row of the screen at 57C1 holds only 55, or when none of the four tiles that are on the screen has anything in it other than the tile A6. The position is kept at F5FA to F5FF. This is a best reading of the code.

C33F LD B,$1C B=1C.
