DDF8: Scroll World
Used by the routines at RunFrame and ScrollInWorld.
Moves the view of the world by the scroll speeds, which are the vertical speed at VerticalSpeed and the horizontal speed at HorizontalSpeed. The speeds are added to the offsets within the tile and when an edge of the tile is passed the map position at MapPosition moves to the next tile. If any tile has changed the four visible tiles are found again. Unless the map row is above row 03, where there is no terrain, the tile scripts are then run to draw the tiles and to make the boxes for the terrain. The map objects, beams and stars are updated, and the lines at the bottom of the frame that the drawing may have damaged are drawn again.
