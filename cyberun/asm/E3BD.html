E3BD: Slope Parameters
Three bytes for each of the types of slope 00 to 08. The routine at SetSlopeParameters copies to the variables at HorizontalStep. The second is added to the screen address for each step of a segment, so it is negative for a slope that goes to the left.
SlopeParameters E3BD DEFB $18,$03,$00
E3C0 DEFB $10,$02,$08
E3C3 DEFB $08,$01,$08
E3C6 DEFB $08,$01,$10
E3C9 DEFB $F0,$FE,$08
E3CC DEFB $F8,$FF,$08
E3CF DEFB $F8,$FF,$10
E3D2 DEFB $00,$01,$10
E3D5 DEFB $00,$FF,$10
