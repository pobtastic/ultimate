C27F: Draw Piece
Used by the routine at CheckItemDelivery.
Draws one of the two pieces that are next to the ship, if there is one. The position of the piece is at IX and its sprite is in DE. Pieces that are being delivered are drawn directly to the screen with DrawSpritesDirectly.
