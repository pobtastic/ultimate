9A72: Fill Attributes White
Used by the routines at ShowTitleScreen and RunGame.
Sets every attribute on the screen to bright white ink on black.
FillAttributesWhite 9A72 LD HL,$5800 Use the first attribute.
FillAttributesWhite_Loop 9A75 LD (HL),$47 Write the attribute.
9A77 INC HL Move on to the next one.
9A78 LD A,H Fetch the high byte of the address.
9A79 CP $5B Jump back to FillAttributesWhite_Loop if all of the attributes have not been written.
9A7B JR NZ,FillAttributesWhite_Loop
9A7D RET Return.
