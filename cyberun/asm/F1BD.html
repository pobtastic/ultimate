F1BD: Colour Ship
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Fills the attributes that the ship covers with its colour, which is white normally, yellow while the stack of units is full and cyan while the timer at 896F is running (changing back to the normal colour every eight frames during the last 20 frames). If the colour has changed it makes the sound at PlayChangeSound.
