882B: Fire Shot
Used by the routine at FireRandomShot.
Starts a shot (type 1C) from an object, in one of the three shot slots. The kind of shot is chosen by the caller using the value in ScratchValue.
Input
IX Address of the object that fires
Output
IY Address of the shot slot
