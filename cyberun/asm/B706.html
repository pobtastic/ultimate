B706: Update Shots
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Updates the four shots in the slots at ShotSlots. A shot with bit 7 of offset 16 set and a type of zero is a chasing shot, which is dealt with by UpdateChasingObject. The others are shots in use when offset 0B is not zero, and are dealt with by UpdateShot.
UpdateShots B706 LD IX,$B37A The shot slots.
B70A LD B,$04 B=04.
UpdateShots_Loop B70C PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
B70D PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
B70F BIT 7,(IX+$16) Jump to UpdateShots_Shot if bit 7 of offset 16 of IX is clear.
B713 JR Z,UpdateShots_Shot
B715 LD A,(IX+$00) Read offset 00 of IX.
B718 AND A Call UpdateChasingObject if A is zero.
B719 CALL Z,UpdateChasingObject
B71C JR UpdateShots_Next Jump to UpdateShots_Next.
UpdateShots_Shot B71E LD A,(IX+$0B) Read offset 0B of IX.
B721 AND A Call UpdateShot if A is not zero.
B722 CALL NZ,UpdateShot
UpdateShots_Next B725 POP IX Bring IX back off the stack.
B727 LD BC,$0018 BC=18.
B72A ADD IX,BC IX gains BC.
B72C POP BC Bring BC back off the stack.
B72D DJNZ UpdateShots_Loop Decrease B and jump to UpdateShots_Loop until none remain.
B72F RET Return.
