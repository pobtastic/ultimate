F26C: Show Title Screen
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the title screen, with a frame, the title text and two small graphics, and shows the control icons and the options with UpdateControlIcon and DrawMenuOptions. A tune plays until a key is pressed and then the menu runs with RunTitleMenu. When it has finished, the options are shown again and a short tune plays.
