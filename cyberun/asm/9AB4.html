9AB4: Play Note
Used by the routines at PlayMusic_WaitKey and PlayMusic.
Plays a single note of a tune. The bottom six bits of the note byte are the number of the note, where zero is a rest, and the top two bits are a multiplier for the length of the note.
Input
A Note byte
DE Address of the note byte
Output
DE Address of the next note byte
