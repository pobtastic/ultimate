39898: Update Beams
Used by the routine at ScrollWorld.
Draws, erases and flickers the horizontal beams that cross the screen from the visible map tiles numbered 166, and hurts the ship when it touches one. There are up to four beams, one for each of the four tiles around the ship that are held at 56465. The beams are switched on and off at random intervals with a random pattern and colour. A beam is skipped when the bit for its cell of the map is set in the three bytes from 62954, which are cleared when a planet is completed. What the tile 166 looks like is not yet known.
