57096: Make Tiles
Used by the routines at BeginLife, LookForGround, FinishScrollIn and ScrollWorld.
Finds the four tiles that are on the screen for a position on the map. The tile numbers are read from the map for two columns and two rows, and are stored in the table at VisibleTiles. Tiles that are above the first row, or below the last row (rows 28 and 29), are blank. While the world scrolls in at the start of a life tile 62 is replaced by 170. The address of the script of each tile is looked up in the table at TileScriptPointers and stored in the list at TileScriptAddresses. Unless the world is being scrolled in, the pick-ups and the map objects for the position are then put in their slots, and the slots of any that are no longer on the screen are freed.
