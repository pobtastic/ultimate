58715: Draw Mirrored One Byte Rows
Draws a piece of terrain that is one byte wide in the same way as DrawOneByteRows, but passes every byte through the table of reversed bytes at 63744. The entry point at DrawMirroredOneByteRows moves one column to the left first.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom of the piece
SP Address of the picture
DrawMirroredOneByteRows 58715 DEC L Step L back.
