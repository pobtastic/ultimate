52216: Frame Corner Table
Four entries of seven bytes for DrawFrame, one for each corner. Each has the position of the corner graphic, the position of an area of attributes along the edge and then the area (width, height and attribute).
FrameCornerTable 52216 DEFB 0,190,0,191,30,1,77 Bottom left
52223 DEFB 232,190,0,23,30,1,77 Bottom right
52230 DEFB 0,23,0,183,1,20,77 Top left
52237 DEFB 232,23,232,183,1,20,77 Top right
