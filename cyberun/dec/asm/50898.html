50898: Draw Main Flame
Used by the routines at UpdateCrystalStack, DrawShipTop and ExplodeShip.
Does nothing unless part 5 has been fitted. Draws the flame under the ship, from the object at MainFlameObject: a larger one (type 17) when going up and a smaller one (type 18) when not. This is a best reading of the code.
