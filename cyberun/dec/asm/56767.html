56767: Finish Scroll In
Used by the routine at LookForGround.
Ends the scroll at the start of a life. It clears the flag at ScrollInFlag and makes the tiles for the current position again. It sets the map column and row, the position and the speeds of the ship from the position of the view, moves the ship with MoveObject and then goes to the sound routine at 29168.
