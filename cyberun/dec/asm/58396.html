58396: Fill Strip With Pattern
Used by the routines at EndSegment and FillSlopeEdge.
Fills a strip of screen bytes with a pattern of two bytes, going up two pixel rows at a time, and then moves along to the next column of the strip by the amount in ColumnStep. The strip is not drawn where the column is within one character of the edge of the screen, and its height is reduced where it would go past the bottom of the screen or above the top of the terrain.
Input
B' Number of columns
C' Number of pairs of pixel rows
DE Pattern: the first byte in D and the second in E
HL' Screen address of the bottom of the first column
