43575: Build Carried Item Graphic
Used by the routines at RunFrame and BeginLife.
Builds the graphic of the item that is carried by the ship in the buffer at ShipWorkArea_Carried (six bytes to a row) and clears the part of the screen beside the ship where the item will be drawn. Each byte of the sprite of the item is looked up in the shift table at CopyGameDown and combined with the buffer using XOR.
