42916: Erase Mirrored Row Directly
Erases one row of a sprite that is flipped from left to right from the screen. Each byte of the sprite has its bits reversed by the table that B points to, and the screen is filled from right to left. The entry point depends on the width of the sprite in bytes, from six bytes at EraseMirroredRowDirectly to one byte at EraseMirroredRowDirectly_Sixth.
Input
B High byte of the table that reverses bits
DE' Address of the data of the sprite
HL' Screen address of the right of the row
EraseMirroredRowDirectly 42916 LD A,(DE) Fetch a byte of the sprite.
42917 LD C,A Use it to look up the byte with the bits reversed.
42918 LD A,(BC) Fetch it.
42919 CPL Invert it.
42920 AND (HL) Clear the pixels of the screen.
42921 LD (HL),A Store the byte.
42922 DEC L Move on to the next byte of the screen to the left.
42923 INC DE Move on to the next byte of the sprite.
