54664: Set Up Flat Piece
Used by the routine at SetUpPiece.
Sets up the drawing of the piece of picture for a step of a flat segment, which is three bytes wide. The jumps at DrawFlatRows_Next, DrawFlatRows_PatchSecond and DrawFlatRows_PatchThird and the instructions at 58021 and 58026 of DrawFlatRows are changed when the piece is at the edge of the screen (the column in the bottom five bits of the screen address is 24, 25, 28 or 29) so that only the part inside the area of the terrain is drawn. The routine then reads the shape at the end of the segment from StartShape and EndShape and finds the picture and the routine that draws it.
Input
C Number of steps left in the segment
IX Address of the drawing record at ScrollWorld_Record
