42616: Draw Aligned Row Directly
Draws one row of a sprite that is aligned to a byte on the screen. The entry point depends on the width of the sprite in bytes, from six bytes at DrawAlignedRowDirectly to one byte at DrawAlignedRowDirectly_Sixth.
Input
DE Address of the data of the sprite
HL Screen address of the row
DrawAlignedRowDirectly 42616 LD A,(DE) Fetch a byte of the sprite.
42617 OR (HL) Set the pixels of the screen.
42618 LD (HL),A Store the byte.
42619 INC L Move on to the next byte of the screen.
42620 INC DE Move on to the next byte of the sprite.

This entry point is used by the routine at DrawMirroredRowDirectly.
