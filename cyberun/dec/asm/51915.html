51915: Draw Frame
Used by the routines at DrawHUD and ShowTitleScreen.
Draws the frame round the playfield from the values in the table at FrameParameters: a vertical line at each side, a double horizontal line at the top and bottom, a graphic at each of the four corners with a coloured line along each edge, and then the ticks along the edges.
Input
IX Address of the table (FrameParameters)
DrawFrame 51915 LD L,(IX+0) The low byte of the address of the left side.
51918 LD H,(IX+1) The high byte of the address of the left side.
51921 LD E,(IX+2) The low byte of the address of the right side.
51924 LD D,(IX+3) The high byte of the address of the right side.
51927 LD B,(IX+4) The number of rows in the two sides.
DrawFrame_Sides 51930 LD (HL),102 Plot the patterned byte 102 on the left side.
51932 LD A,102 The same byte for the right side.
51934 LD (DE),A Plot it on the right side.
51935 CALL MoveUpARow Move the left side up one pixel row.
51938 CALL MoveUpARowDE Move the right side up one pixel row.
51941 DJNZ DrawFrame_Sides Repeat for each row of the sides.
51943 LD L,(IX+5) The low byte of the address of the bottom rules.
51946 LD H,(IX+6) The high byte of the address of the bottom rules.
51949 LD E,(IX+7) The low byte of the address of the top rules.
51952 LD D,(IX+8) The high byte of the address of the top rules.
51955 CALL DrawLinePair Draw one pixel row of the top and bottom rules.
51958 CALL DrawLinePair Draw the next pixel row of those rules.
51961 DEC H Move the bottom rule up one pixel row.
51962 DEC H Move the bottom rule up another pixel row.
51963 DEC D Move the top rule up one pixel row.
51964 DEC D Move the top rule up another pixel row.
51965 CALL DrawLinePair Draw one pixel row of the inner rules.
51968 CALL DrawLinePair Draw the next pixel row of the inner rules.
51971 LD DE,52244 The corner graphics start at FrameCornerGraphics.
51974 LD (52794),DE Keep that address for the next corner.
51978 LD E,(IX+9) The low byte of the address of the corner table.
51981 LD D,(IX+10) The high byte of the address of the corner table.
51984 LD B,4 There are four corners.
DrawFrame_Corners 51986 PUSH BC Keep the number of corners still to draw.
51987 CALL FetchPosition Read the position of this corner graphic.
51990 PUSH DE Keep the place in the corner table.
51991 LD DE,(52794) The graphic for this corner.
51995 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the corner graphic.
51998 LD (52794),DE The next corner graphic follows this one.
52002 POP DE Back to the corner table.
52003 CALL FetchPosition Read the position of the coloured edge.
52006 CALL FillAttributes Colour that edge.
52009 POP BC Bring back the number of corners.
52010 DJNZ DrawFrame_Corners Repeat for each corner.
52012 LD B,(IX+11) The number of ticks along each edge.
52015 LD E,(IX+12) The low byte of the address of the tick table.
52018 LD D,(IX+13) The high byte of the address of the tick table.
DrawFrame_Ticks 52021 PUSH BC Keep the number of ticks still to draw.
52022 LD A,(DE) The pixel column of this tick.
52023 LD L,A That column is its horizontal position.
52024 LD H,(IX+14) The pixel row of the top edge.
52027 PUSH DE Keep the place in the tick table.
52028 LD DE,51533 The top and bottom tick is HorizontalTickGraphic.
52031 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the top edge.
52034 LD H,190 The bottom edge is pixel row 190.
52036 LD DE,51533 The same top and bottom tick.
52039 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the bottom edge.
52042 POP DE Back to the tick table.
52043 INC DE The next byte is the pixel row of the side ticks.
52044 LD A,(DE) That row.
52045 LD H,A It is their vertical position.
52046 LD L,0 The left edge is pixel column zero.
52048 PUSH DE Keep the place in the tick table.
52049 LD DE,51541 The side tick is VerticalTickGraphic.
52052 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the left edge.
52055 LD L,(IX+15) The pixel column of the right edge.
52058 LD DE,51541 The same side tick.
52061 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the tick on the right edge.
52064 POP DE Back to the tick table.
52065 INC DE Move on to the next tick.
52066 POP BC Bring back the number of ticks.
52067 DJNZ DrawFrame_Ticks Repeat for each tick.
52069 RET Return.
