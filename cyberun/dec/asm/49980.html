49980: Save Restart Position
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Called when the bottom two bits of the frame counter are zero. The position of the ship is saved when it is safe to restart there: that is when the row of the screen at 22465 holds only 85, or when none of the four tiles that are on the screen has anything in it other than the tile 166. The position is kept at 62970 to 62975. This is a best reading of the code.
