51775: Draw HUD
Used by the routines at RunGame and PrepareLife.
Draws the status panel at the top of the screen and the frame round the playfield. The panel has the scores, the numbers of ships with a ship icon beside each, and the high score. The frame has the sides, the lines along the top and bottom, the corners and some ticks. Last of all it draws the fuel gauge at the right.
