62865: Swap Players
Used by the routines at StartNewGame, LoseLife and GameOver.
Does nothing if the second player has no ships. Otherwise it swaps the state of the two players, and the records for the scores and the numbers of ships that are printed at the top of the screen, so that the state of the player who is up is always at CurrentPlayerState. If the flag at 51740 is 153 it goes no further. If not it changes the number of the player, prints it and clears the playfield.
