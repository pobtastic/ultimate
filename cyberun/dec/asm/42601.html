42601: Next Row Of Second Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawSpritesDirectly.
Used by DrawSpritesDirectly when the first sprite has no rows left: it carries on with the rows of the second sprite until they are done.
NextRowOfSecondSprite 42601 EX AF,AF' Switch to the count of the second sprite.
42602 DEC A Take one off.
42603 RET M Return if there are none left.
42604 EX AF,AF' Switch back.
42605 JP (IX) Draw a row of the second sprite.
