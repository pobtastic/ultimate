45033: Build Ship
Used by the routines at RunFrame and BeginLife.
Builds the graphic of the ship from the parts that have been fitted. Copies the base of the ship to ShipWorkArea_ShipGraphic and adds each of the nine parts that are flagged at CurrentPlayerState_Parts with AddShipPart. Then works out the height of the ship from the parts, the position of the buffer to draw it from and the vertical position of the ship.
