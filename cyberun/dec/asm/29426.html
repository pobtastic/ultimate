29426: Play Shot Sound
Used by the routine at LaunchShot.
Plays noise that is used when a shot starts. The noise is different if bit 7 of offset 22 of the object is set.
Input
IX Address of the object
PlayShotSound 29426 LD C,15 A normal shot is 15 clicks.
29428 LD E,23 It uses pattern 23.
29430 BIT 7,(IX+22) Jump to PlayNoise if bit 7 of offset 22 of IX is clear.
29434 JP Z,PlayNoise
29437 LD C,E The other shot uses the pattern as its length.
29438 LD E,32 And pattern 32.
29440 JP PlayNoise Jump to PlayNoise.
