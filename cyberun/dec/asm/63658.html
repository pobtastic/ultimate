63658: Merge Shifted Byte
Used by the routines at BuildShiftedShapeA and BuildShiftedShapeB.
Merges a byte of a shape into the buffer, shifted to the right by the number of pixels that the table in D gives. The shifted byte is two bytes: the first is ORed into the byte at HL' and the second into the one after it.
Input
D Page of the table of shifted bytes
HL Address of the byte to shift
HL' Address in the buffer
Output
HL' Address of the second byte that was merged
