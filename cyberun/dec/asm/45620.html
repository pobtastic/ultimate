45620: Update Ship Speeds
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Reads the controls, then changes the horizontal and vertical speeds of the ship by the amounts in the table at ShipAccelerationTable, depending on what is pressed and on the speed it already has, limits them, and works out the vertical scroll speed in VerticalSpeed with UpdateVerticalScrollSpeed.
