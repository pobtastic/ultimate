52799: Vertical Distance
The number of pixel rows that the world moves vertically in a frame, which is the vertical speed without its sign. ScrollWorld sets it. DrawVerticalEdge uses it as the number of rows of the edge to fill, with FillFourEdgeRows if it is four and FillTwoEdgeRows otherwise.
