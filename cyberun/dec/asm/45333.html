45333: Read Controls
Used by the routine at UpdateShipSpeeds.
Reads the controls and keeps what is pressed at Controls (bit 0 is right, bit 1 is left, bit 3 is up, bit 4 is fire and bit 5 is the extra key). Up is ignored in one place near the start of the planet, and only fire and the extra key are used while the special shot is out. Moving uses fuel, which is worked out here and taken off by UseFuel: more for going up and sideways together, and half as much if part 6 is fitted or the value at Variables is not zero.
