49785: Piece Variables
Used to draw the pieces that are next to the ship. The first byte has bit 6 set when a piece is being delivered, and otherwise holds the direction that the ship was facing when it started. The second is set to 128 after a piece is drawn, and the others are the positions of the two pieces.
