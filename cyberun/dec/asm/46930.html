46930: Launch Shot
Used by the routine at CheckSpecialShot.
Launches a shot, from the first free slot out of the four at ShotSlots. It faces the way the ship is facing and starts at the ship. Three kinds of shot can be launched: an ordinary one, a chasing shot if the ship has part 8 and the weapon toggle at WeaponToggle is one, and a special shot if bit 2 of the toggle is set.
