29276: Play Change Sound
Used by the routine at ColourShip.
Plays a rising sweep of 10 beeps. It is used when the colour of the ship changes.
PlayChangeSound 29276 LD C,10 The colour-change sweep has 10 beeps.
29278 LD D,128 The lowest beep has half-period 128.
29280 JP PlaySweep Jump to PlaySweep.
