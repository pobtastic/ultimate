52857: Record Floor Contact
Used by the routine at NextStep.
Used when the ship is on the same row as a step of a segment. It sets bit 7 of the contact flags at ContactFlags. If the vertical speed is not negative it also sets bit 0 (clearing bit 7) and copies the flags into LastContactFlags. It carries on at NextStep_Column.
