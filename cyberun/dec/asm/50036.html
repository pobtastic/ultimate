50036: Run Frame
Used by the routines at RunLife and ExplodeShip.
Does the work for one frame of the game: increases the frame counter at FrameCounter, saves the position where the ship can restart with SaveRestartPosition (every fourth frame) and clears the scroll speeds. Unless the ship is out of control, it is then moved by UpdateShipSpeeds and tested against the terrain and the map objects. The shots are moved by UpdateShots and the rest of the world by ScrollWorld, which comes back at RunFrame_Draw. Then a part that has just been collected is fitted and the ship is drawn.
