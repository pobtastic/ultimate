43521: Bounce Horizontally
Used by the routine at CheckMapObjectCollisions.
Makes the ship rebound after hitting something to its left or right: the horizontal speed of the ship is reversed and halved by ReverseAndHalveSpeed, and the horizontal scroll speed is negated.
