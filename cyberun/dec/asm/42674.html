42674: Erase Aligned Row In Buffer
Erases one row of a sprite that is aligned to a byte from the buffer used by DrawSprites. The entry point depends on the width of the sprite in bytes, from six bytes at EraseAlignedRowInBuffer to one byte at 42704.
Input
DE' Address of the data of the sprite
HL' Address in the buffer
