44128: Update Map Objects
Used by the routine at ScrollWorld.
Runs once a frame to look after the six map objects in the slots at MapObjectSlots. For each used slot it erases the old sprite, removes the object if it is flagged for removal or lets it move and draws it in its new place, sets the attributes under it, and checks whether it has been shot.
