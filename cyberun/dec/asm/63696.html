63696: Next Shift
Used by the routine at BuildShiftedShapeB.
Moves on to the table for the next shift: it restores the strip and row counters from their copies and adds two to the page of the table. The Z flag is set when there are no more tables.
Input
D Page of the table of shifted bytes
Output
D Page of the table for the next shift
F The Z flag is set after the shift by six pixels
