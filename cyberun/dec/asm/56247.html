56247: Draw Slope Rows From Edge
Used by the routine at JumpToDrawingRoutine.
Draws the rows of a piece of a slope two bytes wide, going up the screen two pixel rows at a time, starting from the second column of the row that the screen address is in (or the next one if it is in the last two columns). The two jumps at DrawSlopeRowsFromEdge_PatchFirst and DrawSlopeRowsFromEdge_PatchSecond are changed by SetUpPiece to skip bytes when only part of the piece is to be drawn. The variable PieceColumn sets where the next piece starts on the row. The routine finishes with FinishStep.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address
SP Address of the picture
