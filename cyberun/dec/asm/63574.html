63574: Build Reverse Table
Used by the routine at BuildTables.
Builds the table at 63744 of every byte with its bits in reverse order, indexed by the byte. The drawing routines use it to draw tiles back to front. The table overwrites the routine at 63741 onwards, which is no longer needed once the shape tables have been built.
