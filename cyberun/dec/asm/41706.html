41706: Reset Sprites
Used by the routines at UpdateEnemies, UpdateObject, UpdateCrystalStack, UpdateMapObjects and UpdateStarLayer.
Sets both sprites to have no rows and no width, and clears the flag that makes DrawSprites clip at the edge of the playfield.
ResetSprites 41706 LD HL,0 Clear HL.
41709 LD (41431),HL Set the number of rows of both sprites to zero.
41712 LD (23592),HL Set the width of both sprites to zero.
41715 XOR A Clear A.
41716 LD (41722),A Clear the flag at SpriteVariables_RightEdge.
41719 RET Return.
