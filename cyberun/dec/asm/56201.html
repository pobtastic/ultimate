56201: Draw Slope Rows
Used by the routine at JumpToDrawingRoutine.
Draws the rows of a piece of a slope two bytes wide, going up the screen two pixel rows at a time. The words of the picture are popped from the stack: the bytes of the first word go to the left and right, and of the third word to the right and left, so that they make a zigzag. The second word of each group is not used. The jump at DrawSlopeRows_Patch has its displacement set by SetUpPiece to skip the first byte of each pair of rows when only part of the piece is drawn. When the pixel row is the top of a character the routine moves up to the character above.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
