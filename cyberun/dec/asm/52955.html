52955: Note Wall Contact
Used by the routine at NextStep.
Used when the ship has touched the side of a segment. For a segment that has a horizontal step the remaining length is limited to two steps if the slope goes the way that the tile is facing. If the ship has not touched a segment already the routine finds the screen address of the start with FindSegmentAddress and clears the first byte of the record. It carries on at FinishStep_Count.
