56155: Fill Edge Row Pair
Used by the routines at FillTwoEdgeRows and FillFourEdgeRows.
Stores the pattern in D and E in two pixel rows, one above the other, for each of the C cells across, but only the cells inside the area of the terrain. The pixel row in SegmentRow goes up by two and the screen address moves up a row and along by the column step.
Input
C Number of cells
DE Pattern for the two rows
HL Screen address
