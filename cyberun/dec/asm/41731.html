41731: Draw Sprites
Used by the routines at DrawObject and UpdateStarLayer.
Erases one sprite and draws another without flicker. It works out the rectangle that holds both sprites (which is at most seven bytes wide), copies that part of the screen to a buffer at 63488, erases the first sprite from the buffer, draws the second sprite into the buffer and then copies the buffer back to the screen. The sprites are set up by SelectEraseSprite, SelectDrawSprite and ClipSecondSprite or ClipFirstSprite. A sprite with no rows is not drawn.
Input
B High byte of the shift table for the second sprite
B' High byte of the shift table for the first sprite
DE Address of the data of the second sprite
DE' Address of the data of the first sprite
IX Routine that draws the second sprite
IY Routine that erases the first sprite
