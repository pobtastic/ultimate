46828: Test Shot Hits Ground
Used by the routines at UpdateShot and UpdateLeftShot.
Tests whether a shot has hit the ground or an object, using NudgeObject with a box that is 24 wide and 12 high (16 high if entered at TestShotHitsGround_Wide). A sound is made if the shot is not a special one.
Input
IX Address of the shot
Output
F The zero flag is set if nothing was hit
TestShotHitsGround 46828 LD B,12 B=12.
