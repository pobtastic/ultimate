37470: Add To Score
Used by the routines at CompletePlanet, ScoreDestroyedObject, UpdateType1E and CheckItemPickup.
Adds points to the score of player one and prints the new score. The score is held as three bytes of binary coded decimal at CurrentPlayerState.
