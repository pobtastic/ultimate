62603: Explode Ship
Used by the routine at RunLife.
Runs the frames of the explosion of the ship, once it has been destroyed, with the scroll speeds at zero and the fuel gauge empty, until the explosion counter at 36691 gets to 80. It then plays a tune, fades out the playfield and takes away a life, and goes on to PrepareLife.
