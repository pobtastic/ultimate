45858: Limit Vertical Speed
Used by the routine at UpdateShipSpeeds.
Keeps the vertical speed of the ship within its limits: no faster than 253 downwards, and no faster than half the value at VerticalLimit upwards.
Input
HL Vertical speed
Output
HL Limited vertical speed
