49029: Select Mirrored Erase
Used by the routine at RunFrame.
Used when the ship is erased. If it is facing left (bit 7 of 51453 or of A if entered at SelectMirroredErase_Entry) the horizontal position is moved to the other side of the sprite by its width in ScratchQuad and IY is pointed at the routine for the width in the table at EraseMirroredRowDirectly, which erases a mirrored sprite.
Input
HL' Position (if entered at SelectMirroredErase_Entry)
Output
IY Address of the routine
