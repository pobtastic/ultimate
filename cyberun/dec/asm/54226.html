54226: Set Up Piece
Used by the routine at NextStep.
Sets up the drawing of the piece of picture for a step of a segment. Normally the routine for the type of the segment and the address of its picture are looked up in the table at ShapeTablePointer (four bytes for each type: the routine, which goes into IY, and the picture) and the screen address for the start of the piece is found with FindSegmentAddress. Then, depending on the type of the segment and where on the screen it is, the routine sets the displacement of the jump at JumpToDrawingRoutine_Jump (in 55042) to choose a routine, and changes the instructions of the drawing routines to suit. Steps that were set up already start at SetUpPiece_Type.
Input
HL Address of the entry for the step in the table at ColumnPhaseTable
IX Address of the drawing record at ScrollWorld_Record
