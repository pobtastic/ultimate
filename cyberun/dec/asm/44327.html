44327: Prepare Erase
Used by the routines at CountActiveEnemy, UpdateObject, UpdateCrystalStack and UpdateMapObjects.
Sets up the erasing of the old sprite of an object, from its position on the screen at the last frame. Also clears the visible flag of the object (bit 7 of offset 16) and keeps the wrap flag (bit 6) in SpriteVariables_RightEdge.
Input
IX Address of the object
PrepareErase 44327 CALL FetchFrameSprite Call FetchFrameSprite.
44330 LD L,(IX+17) The horizontal screen position.
44333 LD H,(IX+18) The vertical screen position.
44336 LD (41427),HL Update the sprite positions.
44339 CALL ClipFirstSprite Call ClipFirstSprite.
44342 CALL SelectEraseSprite Call SelectEraseSprite.
44345 RES 7,(IX+16) Reset bit 7 of offset 16 of IX.
44349 LD A,(IX+16) The flags.
44352 AND %01000000 Keep the bits of A that are set in 64.
44354 LD (41722),A The wrap flag.
