46211: Update Shot
Used by the routine at UpdateShots.
Updates one of the shots that the ship has fired. Shots that have bit 7 of offset 21 set go to UpdateSpecialShot. A shot that is starting (with a length of zero at offset 14) is given a starting vertical position from ShipRow with a random step, and a random colour. Shots that are facing right continue at UpdateShot_Right and ones that are facing left at UpdateLeftShot.
