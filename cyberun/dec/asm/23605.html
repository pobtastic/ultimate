23605: Upside Down Segment
Set to 128 by SetSlopeParameters when the current segment is upside down, and cleared at the start of each segment. CheckTerrainCollision also uses it as the collision status: 128 once a rectangle has been hit, bit 6 after a vertical bounce, and bit 4 after a slope has been hit.
