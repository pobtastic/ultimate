57597: Object Kinds
One byte for each kind of object on the map. The top three bits are shifted down into bits 1 to 3 of offset 24 of the slot, where UpdateMapObjects passes them to FireShotWhenHit when they are not zero. If bit 4 is set the object is one of the special ones, and the bottom four bits are the offset in SpecialObjectStates of its state.
