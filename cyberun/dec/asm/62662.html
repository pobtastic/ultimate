62662: Lose Life
Used by the routine at ExplodeShip.
Saves the height of the stack of units and takes a ship away from the current player. If there are none left the game is over for the player. Otherwise, in a game with two players, the players are swapped.
LoseLife 62662 LD A,(51461) The stack height.
62665 LD (62976),A Update the height of the stack of units that the ship carries.
62668 LD A,(62957) The ships remaining.
See Infinite Ships.
