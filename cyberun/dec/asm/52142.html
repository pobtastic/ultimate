52142: Draw Fuel Gauge Frame
Used by the routine at DrawHUD.
Draws the outline of the fuel gauge: a cap at the bottom and one at the top, and a column of dots down the right of the gauge.
DrawFuelGaugeFrame 52142 LD HL,49136 The bottom of the gauge is at 49136.
52145 LD DE,52187 The bottom cap is at FuelGaugeCaps_Second.
52148 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the bottom cap.
52151 LD HL,17392 The top of the gauge is at 17392.
52154 LD DE,52177 The top cap is at FuelGaugeCaps.
52157 CALL DrawBitmap Draw the top cap.
52160 LD HL,21502 The column of dots starts at 21502.
52163 LD B,120 There are 120 rows of dots.
DrawFuelGaugeFrame_Dots 52165 LD (HL),16 Plot the left-hand dot.
52167 INC HL Move across to the right-hand dot.
52168 LD (HL),16 Plot the right-hand dot.
52170 DEC HL Back to the left-hand dot.
52171 CALL MoveUpARow Move up one pixel row.
52174 DJNZ DrawFuelGaugeFrame_Dots Repeat for every row of the gauge.
52176 RET Return.
