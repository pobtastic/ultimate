62716: Game Over
Used by the routine at LoseLife.
Ends the game for the current player. It prints the number of the player and the text GAME OVER, updates the high score and plays a tune. Then it waits for a while or until the 0 key is pressed, clears the playfield and (with two players) swaps the players.
