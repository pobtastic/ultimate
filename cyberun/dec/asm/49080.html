49080: Select Mirrored Draw
Used by the routines at DrawPiece and RunFrame.
Used when the ship is drawn. If it is facing left, moves the horizontal position to the other side of the sprite by its width in 23593 and points IX at the routine for the width in the table at DrawMirroredRowDirectly, which draws a mirrored sprite.
