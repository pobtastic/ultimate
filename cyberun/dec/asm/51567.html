51567: Draw Bitmap
Used by the routines at UpdateControlIcon, DrawHUD, DrawFrame, DrawFuelGaugeFrame and ShowTitleScreen.
Draws a bitmap on the screen. The bitmap starts with its width in bytes and its height in rows, followed by the bytes for each row. The position is that of the bottom row, and the rows are drawn going up the screen.
Input
DE Address of the bitmap
HL Position (H is the pixel row and L is the pixel column)
Output
DE Address after the bitmap

51571 LD A,(DE) Read DE.
51572 LD C,A C=A.
51573 INC DE Advance DE.
51574 CALL GetScreenAddress Call GetScreenAddress.
51577 LD A,B A=B.
51578 LD (23580),A The width of the bitmap.
DrawBitmap_Row 51581 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
51582 LD A,(23580) The width of the bitmap.
51585 LD B,A B=A.
DrawBitmap_Loop 51586 LD A,(DE) Read DE.

51589 INC HL Advance HL.
