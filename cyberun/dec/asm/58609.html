58609: Draw Mirrored Three Byte Rows
Draws a piece of terrain that is three bytes wide in the same way as DrawThreeByteRows, but passes every byte through the table of reversed bytes at 63744 so that the piece is drawn back to front. The entry points at DrawMirroredThreeByteRows and DrawMirroredThreeByteRows_Two first move to the left by three columns and two columns.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom left of the piece
