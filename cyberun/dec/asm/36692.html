36692: Damage Ship
Used by the routine at ApplyCollisionEffects.
Damages the player's ship. A ship that has just been hit cannot be hit again for a few frames. Each hit changes the colour of the damage indicator, and the third hit destroys the ship.
Input
IX Address of the object that was hit

36695 AND %11110000 Keep the top four bits.
36697 RET NZ Return if they are not zero.
36698 LD A,(IX+23) Fetch the second set of flags again.
36701 ADD A,240 Set the top four bits.

36706 LD A,(35183) Fetch the flag from 35183.

36710 JR Z,DamageShip_Hit Jump to DamageShip_Hit if it is zero.
36712 LD A,C Fetch the collision effects.

36715 CP 80 Jump to DamageShip_Hit if the remaining effects are not 80.
36717 JR NZ,DamageShip_Hit

36723 CALL PlayHitSound Play the explosion sound.
