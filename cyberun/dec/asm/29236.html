29236: Play Flutter Sound
Used by the routine at UpdateSpecialShot.
Plays a flutter of beeps for which the length and the notes depend on the frame counter.
PlayFlutterSound 29236 LD A,(39524) The frame counter.
29239 AND %00000111 Keep the bits of A that are set in 7.
29241 INC A The flutter is at least one beep long.
29242 ADD A,A Double the length of the flutter.
29243 ADD A,A Double the length again.
29244 ADD A,A Double the length once more.
29245 LD D,A That many half-periods will be played.
PlayFlutterSound_Loop 29246 LD A,D The next note starts from the count left.
29247 XOR %00000111 Flip the bits of A that are set in 7.
29249 INC A The half-period is one more than the count.
29250 LD B,A That is the half-period of this beep.
29251 CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave.
29254 DEC D One count of the flutter has been used.
29255 RET Z Return if the zero flag is set.
29256 DEC D A second count has been used.
29257 RET Z Return if the zero flag is set.
29258 JR PlayFlutterSound_Loop Jump to PlayFlutterSound_Loop.
