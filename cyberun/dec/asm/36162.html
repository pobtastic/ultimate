36162: Update Enemies
Used by the routines at RunLife and ExplodeShip.
Updates all nine enemy slots once a frame. The counter at 36691 tracks the explosion of the player's ship: it counts up from one when the ship is destroyed, the debris is started when it reaches ten, and the ship is gone for good when it has gone past that point.
