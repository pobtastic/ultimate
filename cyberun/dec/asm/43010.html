43010: Delay
Used by the routines at CompletePlanet, RunTitleMenu, ShortDelay, RunGame, GameOver, FinishPlanet and SwapPlayers.
Waits for a short time that is proportional to D.
Input
D Length of the delay
Delay 43010 LD E,255 Set the inner counter.
Delay_Loop 43012 DEC E Count down the inner counter.
43013 JR NZ,Delay_Loop Loop back to Delay_Loop until none remain.
43015 DEC D Count down the outer counter.
43016 JR NZ,Delay Loop back to Delay until none remain.
43018 RET Return.
