29464: Play Removal Sound
Used by the routine at DestroyObject.
Plays a rising sweep of ten beeps. It is used when an object is removed.
PlayRemovalSound 29464 LD C,10 The removal sweep has ten beeps.
29466 LD D,24 The lowest beep has half-period 24.
29468 JP PlaySweep Jump to PlaySweep.
