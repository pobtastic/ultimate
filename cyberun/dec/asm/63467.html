63467: Build Shape Tables
Used by the routine at BuildTables.
Builds the shifted copies of the descriptions of the ground textures (see ShapeDescriptions). Each description is merged into three buffers, shifted by two, four and six pixels to the right, so that the terrain can be drawn at any horizontal pixel position. Those buffers are in ShiftedShapesOne to 61710. Four of the descriptions are read in the format of BuildShiftedShapeB, two in the format that BuildShiftedShapeA handles, and the last three in the format of BuildShiftedShapeB again with HorizontalStepSize set to 255.
