42607: Draw Next Row
Used by the routine at EraseAlignedRowDirectly.
Used by DrawSpritesDirectly after a row of the first sprite has been erased: it draws a row of the second sprite, or erases another row of the first sprite when the second sprite has no rows left.
DrawNextRow 42607 EX AF,AF' Switch to the count of the second sprite.
42608 DEC A Take one off.
42609 JP M,DrawSpritesDirectly_SecondRow Jump to DrawSpritesDirectly_SecondRow if there are none left.
42612 EX AF,AF' Switch back.
42613 EXX Switch to the second sprite.
42614 JP (IX) Draw a row of the second sprite.
