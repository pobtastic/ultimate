57487: Load Map Objects
Used by the routine at MakeTiles.
Puts the objects of the map for the two columns that are on the screen into the slots for the map objects. The objects of a column are found by adding up the numbers of the objects in the columns before it, from the table at ColumnTable, to get the place in the tables at MapTable and TerrainData. If the second column wraps round to column zero its objects are the first in the tables. The slots of any objects that are free have their flags cleared.
