49123: Update Stars
Used by the routine at ScrollWorld.
Moves and draws the stars in the sky, in three layers of six stars. Only does anything when the ship is in one of the four zones at the top of the planet. The stars in the nearer layers move faster than the ones in the further layers when the screen scrolls, and are bigger. This is a best reading of the code.
