56481: Begin Life
Used by the routine at PlayLife.
Sets up the screen at the start of each life. It shows the fuel gauge as empty but fills the tank, so the gauge fills up, and chooses the attributes for the planet. It restores the position of the ship from the state of the current player (which starts at CurrentPlayerState) and makes the tiles for that position. It builds the ship with BuildShip and puts back any item that it was carrying. It then drops into the loop at ScrollInWorld that scrolls the world until the ship is in place.
