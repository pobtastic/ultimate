58667: Draw Mirrored Two Byte Rows
Draws a piece of terrain that is two bytes wide in the same way as DrawTwoByteRows, but passes every byte through the table of reversed bytes at 63744. The entry points at DrawMirroredTwoByteRows and DrawMirroredTwoByteRows_One first move to the left by two columns and one column, and the one at DrawMirroredTwoByteRows_Start does not.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom left of the piece
SP Address of the picture
