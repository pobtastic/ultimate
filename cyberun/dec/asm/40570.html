40570: Find Mirrored Slope Overlap
Finds the overlap with a slope that faces the other way by swapping the horizontal edges of the object, calling FindSlopeOverlap and swapping them back.
Input
IX Address of the object

40573 LD B,(IX+2) Fetch the right limit of the object.
40576 LD (IX+0),B Store the right limit as the left edge.
40579 LD (IX+2),A Store the left edge as the right limit.
40582 CALL FindSlopeOverlap Find the overlap with the slope.
40585 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the left edge of the object.
40588 LD C,(IX+2) Fetch the right limit of the object.
40591 LD (IX+0),C Store the right limit as the left edge.
40594 LD (IX+2),A Store the left edge as the right limit.
40597 LD A,(23580) Fetch the result.
40600 AND A Test it.
40601 RET Z Return if the box does not overlap.
