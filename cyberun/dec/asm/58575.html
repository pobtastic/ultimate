58575: Draw Two Byte Rows
Draws a piece of terrain that is two bytes wide, going up the screen two pixel rows at a time, using two words of the picture for each pair of rows. The entry points at DrawTwoByteRows and DrawTwoByteRows_One first move to the right by two columns and one column, and the one at DrawTwoByteRows_Start does not.
Input
A Number of pairs of pixel rows to draw
HL Screen address of the bottom left of the piece
SP Address of the picture
