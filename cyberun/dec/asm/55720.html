55720: Draw Vertical Edge
Used by the routine at FinishSegment.
Draws the edge of the terrain when the world is scrolling vertically, for the steps of a segment that have been counted in StepsDrawn. A strip across the segment is filled with either a chequered pattern (21930) or blank (zero), depending on whether the vertical speed in VerticalSpeed and the direction are the same, using FillTwoEdgeRows or FillFourEdgeRows. The routine finally carries on with FinishSegment_Colour. Purpose not yet known for the details of the positions.
