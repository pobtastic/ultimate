56697: Look For Ground
Used by the routine at ScrollWorld.
Used while the world is scrolling in at the start of a life. If the flag at ScrollInFlag is 3 the scroll is finished (see FinishScrollIn). If not, the vertical scroll speed is set to 4. If the flag at ChequeredFloorFlag is zero the scroll lasts for the number of steps that are left in B. Otherwise it looks at the six bytes of the screen where the ship is, and at the row above them, and goes on scrolling until they are all blank. Then the flag is set to 3, the playfield is coloured and the tiles are made again.
