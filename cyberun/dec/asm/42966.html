42966: Draw Mirrored Row Directly
Draws one row of a sprite that is flipped from left to right on the screen. Each byte of the sprite has its bits reversed by the table that B points to, and the screen is filled from right to left. The entry point depends on the width of the sprite in bytes, from six bytes at DrawMirroredRowDirectly to one byte at DrawMirroredRowDirectly_Sixth.
Input
B High byte of the table that reverses bits
DE Address of the data of the sprite
HL Screen address of the right of the row
DrawMirroredRowDirectly 42966 LD A,(DE) Fetch a byte of the sprite.
42967 LD C,A Use it to look up the byte with the bits reversed.
42968 LD A,(BC) Fetch it.
42969 OR (HL) Set the pixels of the screen.
42970 LD (HL),A Store the byte.
42971 DEC L Move on to the next byte of the screen to the left.
42972 INC DE Move on to the next byte of the sprite.
