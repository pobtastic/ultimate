45212: Add Ship Part
Used by the routine at BuildShip.
Adds one part to the graphic of the ship. The sprite of the part is found in the table at SpriteTables_ShipParts and the place in the graphic where it goes in the table at SpriteTables_ShipPartPositions. The height of the ship is made at least as tall as the part. The mask of the part is ANDed into the graphic, one row at a time, then its picture is ORed in.
Input
IX Address of the flag for the part, which is the number of the part
