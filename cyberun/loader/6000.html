6000: Copy Loader
The helper that the BASIC loader runs at 6000. It copies the move routine to FC00 the decrypt stub to 5B80 and the loading screen to 4000 then returns so that BASIC can load the encrypted game to 5E80.
Copy the routine that moves the game down.
Copy_Loader 6000 LD HL,$6022 Copy 10 bytes from Copy_Game_Down to FC00.
6003 LD DE,$FC00
6006 LD BC,$0010
6009 LDIR
Copy the decrypt stub.
600B LD HL,$602E Copy 20 bytes from Decrypt_Game to 5B80.
600E LD DE,$5B80
6011 LD BC,$0020
6014 LDIR
Copy the loading screen.
6016 LD HL,$6047 Copy 1B00 bytes from Loading_Screen to 4000.
6019 LD DE,$4000
601C LD BC,$1B00
601F LDIR
6021 RET Return.
