C5C8: Handle Ghost
Handles the graphic numbers 50 to 53 which are a ghost. The ghost moves in a straight line until it hits something, or does not move at all, and then it picks a new direction at random: its speed in X and in Y is 03 or 04 in either direction. The graphic of the ghost shows the way it moves, and is changed between its two frames each time. The ghost is deadly.
Input
IX Address of the ghost object
HandleGhost C5C8 CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Legs Set the pixel adjustments of the bottom half of a guard.
C5CB CALL ApplyGravityAndMove Apply gravity to the ghost and move it.
C5CE LD A,(IX+$09) Fetch the speed in X.
C5D1 OR (IX+$0A) Combine it with the speed in Y.
C5D4 JR Z,HandleGhost_NewDirection Jump to HandleGhost_NewDirection if the ghost is not moving.
C5D6 LD A,(IX+$0C) Fetch the flags that say what the ghost has hit.
C5D9 AND %00000011 Keep the bits for X and Y.
C5DB JR Z,HandleGhost_Draw Jump to HandleGhost_Draw if the ghost has not hit anything.
Choose a new direction.
HandleGhost_NewDirection C5DD LD A,($5BA5) Fetch the second random seed.
C5E0 AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
C5E2 ADD A,$04 Add 04 to get a number from 04 to 07.
C5E4 CALL LookUpGhostSpeed Look up the speed.
C5E7 LD (IX+$09),A Set the speed in X.
C5EA LD A,($5BA2) Fetch the frame counter.
C5ED AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
C5EF ADD A,$04 Add 04 to get a number from 04 to 07.
C5F1 CALL LookUpGhostSpeed Look up the speed.
C5F4 LD (IX+$0A),A Set the speed in Y.
C5F7 CALL SetGhostDirectionGraphic Choose the graphic of the ghost from its direction.
C5FA CALL PlaySoundFromPosition Play the sound of a moving object.
HandleGhost_Draw C5FD CALL ToggleGraphic Toggle the graphic of the ghost between its two frames.
C600 JP SetDeadlyFlagsAndDraw Make the ghost deadly and flag it to be drawn again.
