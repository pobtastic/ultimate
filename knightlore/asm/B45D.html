B45D: Play Sound From X Position
Used by the routines at HandleBlockMovingEastWest and HandleFireMovingEastWest.
Plays the tone for an object that is moving east or west, with a pitch taken from its X coordinate.
Input
IX Address of the object
Use the X coordinate as the pitch source.
PlaySoundFromXPosition B45D LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the X coordinate of the object.
