CA4A: Move Player North
Adds 03 to the speed in Y of the object.
Input
IX Address of the object
MovePlayerNorth CA4A LD A,(IX+$0A) Fetch the speed in Y and add 03 to it.
CA4D ADD A,$03
This entry point is used by the routine at MovePlayerSouth.
MovePlayer_SetY CA4F LD (IX+$0A),A Store the speed in Y.
CA52 RET Return.
