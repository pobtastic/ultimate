CA70: Handle Room Exit
Used by the routine at MovePlayer.
Sends the player to the next room if it has walked out through an arch. Only a player that is near an arch can leave the room, and not while it is still entering the room. The edge that counts is the one in the direction that the player is facing.
Input
IX Address of the object
Leave through the arch the player is facing, if it is near one.
HandleRoomExit CA70 LD A,(IX+$0C) Fetch the flags of the player.
CA73 AND %11110000 Keep the counter of the player entering the room.
CA75 RET NZ Return if the player is entering the room.
CA76 BIT 0,(IX+$07) Test whether the player is near an arch.
CA7A RET Z Return if it is not.
CA7B RES 0,(IX+$07) Clear the flag.
CA7F LD BC,$CA92 Point BC at RoomExitTable.
CA82 LD HL,($5BAB) Fetch the size of the room in X and Y from RoomSizeX.
CA85 PUSH HL Stack it for the routine to take off.
CA86 JP SetPlayerSpeed_Direction Jump to the routine for the direction the player is facing.
