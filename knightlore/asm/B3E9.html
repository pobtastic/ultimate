B3E9: Play Pitch From Table
Used by the routine at HandleBlock.
Plays four cycles of a tone whose pitch is taken from the eight values of PitchTable, which is chosen by the lowest three bits of the frame counter.
PlayPitchFromTable B3E9 LD A,($5BA2) Fetch the frame counter.
B3EC AND %00000111 Keep the lowest three bits.
B3EE LD L,A Set HL to the number of the entry in the table.
B3EF LD H,$00
B3F1 LD BC,$B3FB Point BC at PitchTable.
B3F4 ADD HL,BC Add the entry number to the address of the table.
B3F5 LD B,(HL) Fetch the pitch.
B3F6 LD C,$04 Set the number of cycles to play.
B3F8 JP PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
