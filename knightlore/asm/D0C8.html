D0C8: Read Keyboard
Used by the routine at ReadControls.
Reads the controls from the keyboard. Left is Z, C, M or B, right is X, V, symbol shift or N, walking forward is any key of the rows A to G and H to ENTER, jumping is any key of the rows Q to T and Y to P, and picking up and dropping an object is any key of the rows 1 to 5 and 6 to 0.
Output
C The controls
Left and right from the bottom row.

This entry point is used by the routines at ReadControls, ReadInterface2Joystick, ReadKempstonJoystick and ReadCursorJoystick.
Reads the keys that pick up and drop an object when a joystick with directional control is in use. The keys that count are Z, X, C, V, A to G, H to ENTER, Q to T and Y to P.
ReadControls_Finish D111 LD A,$7E Read from the keyboard;
Port Number Bit
0 1 2 3 4
7E SHIFT / SPACE Z / SYM X / M C / N V / B
D113 CALL ReadKeyboardPort
D116 AND %00011110 Keep the keys Z, X, C, V, symbol shift, M, N and B.
D118 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
D119 LD B,A Stash the keys in B.
D11A LD A,$99 Read the keys SHIFT to V, 1 to 5, 6 to 0 and SPACE to B.
D11C CALL ReadKeyboardPort
D11F OR B Combine the keys.
D120 POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
D121 JR Z,ReadControls_Store Jump to ReadControls_Store if no key is pressed.
D123 SET 5,C Set the directional pick up control.
