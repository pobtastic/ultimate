B98C: Next Sprite Number
Used by the routines at HandleCauldronBubbles and HandleRepelSpell.
Changes the graphic of an object to the next of a group of four, by adding one to the lowest two bits of the graphic number and ignoring the carry.
Input
IX Address of the object
Advance the lowest two bits of the graphic number.
NextSpriteNumber B98C LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the graphic number.
B98F LD C,A Stash it in C.
B990 AND %11111100 Keep the highest six bits.
B992 LD B,A Stash them in B.
B993 LD A,C Fetch the graphic number.
B994 INC A Add one.
B995 AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
B997 OR B Combine them with the highest six bits.
This entry point is used by the routine at ToggleGraphic.
SetGraphic B998 LD (IX+$00),A Store the new graphic number.
B99B RET Return.
