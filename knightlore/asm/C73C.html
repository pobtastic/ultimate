C73C: Handle Near Arch
Handles the graphic numbers 02 and 04 which are the near side of an arch and of a tree arch. The speeds in X, Y and Z of the arch are not used to move it, so they are used to keep the position of the middle of the doorway. The player, and any special object, that gets close enough to the arch is flagged as near it, and is nudged towards the middle line of the doorway, so that it goes through the middle.
Input
IX Address of the arch object
HandleNearArch C73C BIT 6,(IX+$07) Jump to HandleFlippedNearArch if the arch is flipped horizontally.
C740 JR NZ,HandleFlippedNearArch
C742 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the graphic number of the arch.
C745 CP $04 Jump to AdjustTreeArch if it is a tree arch.
C747 JR Z,AdjustTreeArch
C749 LD HL,$FDF9 Set HL to the adjustments of F9 in X and FD in Y.
This entry point is used by the routine at AdjustTreeArch.
The doorway is thirteen units further along the Y axis than the arch.
HandleNearArch_Position C74C CALL StorePixelAdjustments Set the adjustments.
C74F LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the Y coordinate of the arch.
C752 ADD A,$0D Add 0D to it.
C754 LD (IX+$0A),A Keep it as the Y coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
C757 LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the X coordinate of the arch.
C75A LD (IX+$09),A Keep it as the X coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
C75D LD HL,$060F Set H to the distance in Y that counts as near and L to the distance in X.
This entry point is used by the routine at HandleFlippedNearArch.
HandleNearArch_Near C760 LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the Z coordinate of the arch.
C763 LD (IX+$0B),A Keep it as the Z coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
C766 CALL FlagObjectsNearArch Flag the objects that are near the arch.
C769 JP NudgeObjectsToArch Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch.
