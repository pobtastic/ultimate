C73C: Handle Near Arch
The handler for the near side of an arch (objects 02 and 04). As an arch does not move, its speeds are used to keep the position of the middle of the doorway: the middle is 0D further along the y axis than the arch, or 0D further back along the x axis if the sprite is flipped. The player, and any special object, is flagged if near the arch, and is nudged towards the middle of the doorway.
Input
IX The address of the object
Unflipped: doorway 0D along y.
