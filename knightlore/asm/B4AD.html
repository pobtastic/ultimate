B4AD: Play Guard Footsteps Sound
Used by the routine at HandleGuardBottomHalf.
Plays the footsteps of a guard or the wizard, but only on every other frame of the walking animation. It carries on into PlayFootstepsSound_Position with a fixed pitch.
Input
IX Address of the object
Play on even frames of the walking animation.
PlayGuardFootstepsSound B4AD LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the graphic number of the object.
B4B0 AND %00000001 Test whether it is an odd frame of the animation.
B4B2 RET NZ Return if it is.
B4B3 LD B,$80 Set the pitch.
B4B5 LD A,($5BA2) Fetch the frame counter from FrameCounter.
B4B8 CPL Invert it.
B4B9 JR PlayFootstepsSound_Position Jump to PlayFootstepsSound_Position.
