BAAB: Game Completed
Used by the routine at GameOver.
Shows the message of the potion that is given when the player has completed the game, while the game complete tune plays, and then goes on to show the results.
Show the game completed message.
GameCompleted BAAB CALL ClearScreenBuffer Clear the screen buffer.
BAAE CALL ClearScreen Clear the screen and make the border black.
BAB1 LD DE,$BAD2 Point DE at GameCompleteColours.
BAB4 EXX Switch to the alternate registers to keep it there.
BAB5 LD HL,$BAD8 Point HL at GameCompleteCoordinates.
BAB8 LD DE,$BAE4 Point DE at GameCompleteText.
BABB LD B,$06 Set the number of lines of text to 06.
BABD XOR A Clear A.
BABE LD ($5BB8),A Flag that the border has to be drawn.
BAC1 CALL DisplayTextList Print the lines of text.
Play the tune and wait for a key to be pressed.
BAC4 LD DE,$B239 Point DE at GameCompleteTune.
BAC7 CALL PlayMusic Play the tune.
BACA LD B,$08 Set the number of times to wait to 08.
BACC CALL WaitForKeyPress Wait for a key to be pressed, up to a limit.
BACF JP GameOver_ShowResults Jump to GameOver_ShowResults to show the results screen.
