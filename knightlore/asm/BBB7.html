BBB7: Rating Table
The addresses of the eight ratings that can be given on the results screen. The rating is chosen by the number of rooms that have been visited (in steps of 20) and by whether the game was completed. A completed game always gets at least the fifth rating.
RatingTable BBB7 DEFW RatingText Fewer than 21 rooms visited: RatingText.
BBB9 DEFW RatingText_Average 21 to 40 rooms visited: RatingText_Average.
BBBB DEFW RatingText_Fair 41 to 60 rooms visited: RatingText_Fair.
BBBD DEFW RatingText_Good More than 60 rooms visited: RatingText_Good.
BBBF DEFW RatingText_Excellent Game completed, with fewer than 21 rooms visited: RatingText_Excellent.
BBC1 DEFW RatingText_Marvellous Game completed, with 21 to 40 rooms visited: RatingText_Marvellous.
BBC3 DEFW RatingText_Hero Game completed, with 41 to 60 rooms visited: RatingText_Hero.
BBC5 DEFW RatingText_Adventurer Game completed, with more than 60 rooms visited: RatingText_Adventurer.
