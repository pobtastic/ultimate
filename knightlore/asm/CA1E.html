CA1E: Get Facing Direction
Used by the routines at HandleTurning and SetPlayerSpeed.
Works out the direction an object is facing from the horizontal flip flag and bit 3 of the graphic number.
Input
IX Address of the object
Output
A The direction: 00 for west, 01 for east, 02 for north and
#N$03 for south
GetFacingDirection CA1E LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the flags of the object.
CA21 RRCA Move the horizontal flip flag to bit 4.
CA22 RRCA
CA23 AND %00010000 Keep it.
CA25 LD L,A Stash it in L.
CA26 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the graphic number.
CA29 AND %00001000 Keep bit 3.
CA2B OR L Combine it with the flip flag.
CA2C RRCA Move the two bits into the low end.
CA2D RRCA
CA2E RRCA
CA2F AND %00000011 Keep them.
CA31 RET Return.
