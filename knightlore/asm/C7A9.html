C7A9: Arch Direction Table
The routines that nudge an object towards the middle of a doorway, by the direction of the arch: the lowest bit is bit 3 of the graphic number of the arch, and the next is whether the sprite is flipped.
ArchDirectionTable C7A9 DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest Not flipped: NudgeObjectEastWest.
C7AB DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest
C7AD DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth Flipped: NudgeObjectNorthSouth.
C7AF DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth
