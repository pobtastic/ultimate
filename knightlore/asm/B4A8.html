B4A8: Play Middle Beep
Used by the routine at PauseGame.
Plays a beep of twenty-four cycles, with a higher pitch than PlayLowBeep. It is the sound of the game being paused and of it carrying on again.
Play twenty-four cycles of a middle tone.
PlayMiddleBeep B4A8 LD BC,$5018 Set the pitch and the number of cycles.
B4AB JR PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
