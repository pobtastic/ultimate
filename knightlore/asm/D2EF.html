D2EF: Set Border Attributes
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Sets the colours of the panel at the bottom of the screen: black for the two columns at the sides of the window of the sun and moon, and bright red for the window itself.
Colour the sides black and the sun/moon window bright red.
SetBorderAttributes D2EF XOR A Set A to the attribute for black on black.
D2F0 LD HL,$5AB6 Point HL at the attributes of the left side.
D2F3 LD BC,$0103 Set the width to 01 and the height to 03.
D2F6 CALL FillWindow Colour the left side.
D2F9 LD HL,$5ABD Point HL at the attributes of the right side.
D2FC LD BC,$0103 Set the width to 01 and the height to 03.
D2FF CALL FillWindow Colour the right side.
D302 LD A,$42 Set A to the attribute for bright red on black.
D304 LD HL,$5A97 Point HL at the attributes of the window.
D307 LD BC,$0604 Set the width to 06 and the height to 04.
D30A JP FillWindow Jump to FillWindow.
