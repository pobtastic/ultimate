CB29: Leave Room To The South
Sends the player to the room to the south if it has gone past the south edge of the room. The Y coordinate is set to 00 which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the south, and so puts it at the north side of the new room.
Input
HL The size of the room in X and Y
IX Address of the object
LeaveRoomToSouth CB29 POP HL Unstack the size of the room in X and Y.
CB2A LD A,$80 Work out the coordinate of the south edge of the room.
CB2C SUB H
CB2D LD H,A
CB2E LD A,(IX+$02) Add the speed in Y and the depth of the player to the Y coordinate.
CB31 ADD A,(IX+$0A)
CB34 ADD A,(IX+$05)
CB37 CP H Compare it with the south edge.
CB38 RET NC Return if the player is still in the room.
CB39 LD (IX+$02),$00 Set the Y coordinate to 00.
CB3D LD A,(IX+$08) Fetch the room number and take 10 off it to go to the room to the south.
CB40 SUB $10
CB42 JP ChangeRoom Jump to ChangeRoom.
