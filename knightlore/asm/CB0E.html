CB0E: Leave Room To The North
Sends the player to the room to the north if it has gone past the north edge of the room. The Y coordinate is set to FF which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the north, and so puts it at the south side of the new room.
Input
HL The size of the room in X and Y
IX Address of the object
LeaveRoomToNorth CB0E POP HL Unstack the size of the room in X and Y.
CB0F LD A,H Work out the coordinate of the north edge of the room.
CB10 ADD A,$80
CB12 LD H,A
CB13 LD A,(IX+$02) Add the speed in Y to the Y coordinate, and take the depth of the player off it.
CB16 ADD A,(IX+$0A)
CB19 SUB (IX+$05)
CB1C CP H Compare it with the north edge.
CB1D RET C Return if the player is still in the room.
CB1E LD (IX+$02),$FF Set the Y coordinate to FF.
CB22 LD A,(IX+$08) Fetch the room number and add 10 to go to the room to the north.
CB25 ADD A,$10
CB27 JR ChangeRoom Jump to ChangeRoom.
