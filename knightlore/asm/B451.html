B451: Play Sound From Height
Used by the routines at HandleDroppingBlock, HandleSpikedBall, HandleBallUpDown and MovePlayer.
Plays a tone for an object that is falling, with a pitch taken from the height of the object (its Z coordinate).
Input
IX Address of the object
PlaySoundFromHeight B451 LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the Z coordinate of the object.
