D320: Place Player At Door
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Puts the player in the right place when it has come into a room through an arch. The routine that moved the player out of the last room set one of its coordinates to 00 or FF which tells which way the player came. The player is put at the opposite edge of the room, just outside the wall, and at the height of the arch. If the coordinates are not one of these, as when the player starts the game or starts a new life, nothing is done.
Input
IX Address of the player
PlacePlayerAtDoor D320 LD A,($5BAB) Fetch the size of the room in X and take 02 off it.
D323 SUB $02
D325 LD L,A
D326 LD A,($5BAC) Fetch the size of the room in Y and take 02 off it.
D329 SUB $02
D32B LD H,A
D32C LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the X coordinate and test whether it is zero.
D32F AND A
D330 JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_East Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_East if it is, as the player came out of the west.
D332 INC A Add one to it.
D333 JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_West Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_West if it was FF as the player came out of the east.
D335 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the Y coordinate and test whether it is zero.
D338 AND A
D339 JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_North Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_North if it is, as the player came out of the south.
D33B INC A Add one to it.
D33C JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_South Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_South if it was FF as the player came out of the north.
D33E RET Return.
The player came out of the north, so it goes to the south side of the room.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_South D33F LD C,$C8 Set the sum of the coordinates of the arch to look for.
D341 CALL FindDoorHeight Find the arch and set the height of the player to it.
D344 LD A,$80 Start with 80.
D346 SUB H Take the size of the room in Y off it.
D347 SUB (IX+$05) Take the depth of the player off it.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetY D34A LD (IX+$02),A Set the Y coordinate of the player.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_Draw D34D SET 4,(IX+$07) Flag the legs of the player to be drawn.
D351 SET 4,(IX+$27) Flag the top half of the player to be drawn.
D355 LD A,(IX+$01) Give the top half the X coordinate of the legs.
D358 LD (IX+$21),A
D35B LD A,(IX+$02) Give the top half the Y coordinate of the legs.
D35E LD (IX+$22),A
D361 RET Return.
The player came out of the south, so it goes to the north side of the room.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_North D362 LD C,$51 Set the sum of the coordinates of the arch to look for.
D364 CALL FindDoorHeight Find the arch and set the height of the player to it.
D367 LD A,H Fetch the size of the room in Y.
D368 ADD A,$80 Add 80 to it.
D36A ADD A,(IX+$05) Add the depth of the player.
D36D JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetY Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetY.
The player came out of the east, so it goes to the west side of the room.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_West D36F LD C,$AE Set the sum of the coordinates of the arch to look for.
D371 CALL FindDoorHeight Find the arch and set the height of the player to it.
D374 LD A,$80 Start with 80.
D376 SUB L Take the size of the room in X off it.
D377 SUB (IX+$04) Take the width of the player off it.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetX D37A LD (IX+$01),A Set the X coordinate of the player.
D37D JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_Draw Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_Draw.
The player came out of the west, so it goes to the east side of the room.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_East D37F LD C,$37 Set the sum of the coordinates of the arch to look for.
D381 CALL FindDoorHeight Find the arch and set the height of the player to it.
D384 LD A,L Fetch the size of the room in X.
D385 ADD A,$80 Add 80 to it.
D387 ADD A,(IX+$04) Add the width of the player.
D38A JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetX Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetX.
