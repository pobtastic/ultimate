BD0C: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Shows the menu on which the player chooses the keyboard or one of the joysticks, and waits until the player starts the game. Keys 1 to 4 choose the control method, key 5 turns the directional control on or off and key 0 starts the game. The menu tune plays while this is going on, and the choices that are in force are shown by flashing. The control method is kept in ControlMethod in these bits: bits 1 and 2 are 00 for the keyboard, 01 for the Kempston joystick, 02 for the Cursor joystick and 03 for the Interface 2 joystick, and bit 3 is set if the directional control is on.
