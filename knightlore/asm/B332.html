B332: Music Note Table
The three bytes for each of the notes, where the notes are numbered from 00. The first two bytes are the delay between the clicks of the speaker, which is shorter for the higher notes, and the third is the basic length of the note, which is the number of cycles of the note to play. PlayNote uses it.
MusicNotes B332 DEFB $00,$00,$00 The rest has no delay and no length, as the rest does not use the table.
