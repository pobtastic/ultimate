BAE4: Game Complete Text
The six lines of the message that is shown when the game has been completed. Each character is stored as its number in the font at Font: 00 to 09 are the digits, 0A to 23 are the letters, 26 is a space and 27 is a percent sign. Bit 7 of the last character of each line is set.
