D55F: Clear Screen
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, GameOver and GameCompleted.
Clears the screen to bright yellow on black, with a black border.
ClearScreen D55F XOR A Make the border black.
D560 OUT ($FE),A
D562 CALL ClearAttributes Set the attributes of the screen to bright yellow on black.
D565 JR ClearScreenBitmap Jump to ClearScreenBitmap to clear the bitmap of the screen.
