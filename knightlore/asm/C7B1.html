C7B1: Nudge Object East West
Sets the adjustment of the speed in Y of an object, to bring it a step closer to the middle line of an arch that goes east and west.
Input
IX Address of the arch object
IY Address of the object
NudgeObjectEastWest C7B1 LD A,(IX+$0A) Fetch the Y coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
C7B4 CP (IY+$02) Compare it with the Y coordinate of the object.
C7B7 JR Z,NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore if the object is in the middle.
C7B9 LD A,$01 Set the adjustment to 01.
C7BB JR NC,NudgeObjectEastWest_Set Jump to NudgeObjectEastWest_Set if the object is south of the middle.
C7BD NEG Make the adjustment negative.
NudgeObjectEastWest_Set C7BF LD (IY+$0F),A Set the adjustment of the speed in Y of the object.
C7C2 JR NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore.
