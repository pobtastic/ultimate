D826: Calculate Screen Address
Used by the routines at DisplayObjectsCarried, CopyAreaToScreen and DrawChangedObjects.
Finds the address on the screen of the byte that holds a pixel.
Input
B Pixel Y coordinate (from the bottom of the screen)
C Pixel X coordinate
Output
DE Address on the screen
Build the display-file address from the pixel X and Y.
