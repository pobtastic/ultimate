922E: Sprite: Human Legs
The sprites of the legs of the human form of the player, which are also the bottom half of a guard and of the wizard. The graphic numbers 10 to 15 are the six frames of the legs walking when facing west (or north if flipped from left to right), and 18 to 1D are the frames when facing east (or south). The frames are in the order 1, 2, 3, 4, 3 and 2, so that the sprites are used more than once. The guard and the wizard use the graphic numbers 90 to 95 and 98 to 9D.
