BF45: Display Objects Carried
Used by the routine at DrawChangedObjects.
Draws the objects that the player is carrying on the panel, but only if they have changed since they were last drawn. The panel has room for three objects, which are drawn from the left: the object that was picked up last comes first. Each object is drawn in a space of 18 by 18 pixels, with a colour that goes with it.
DisplayObjectsCarried BF45 LD A,($5BB4) Fetch the flag that says the objects carried have changed from ObjectsCarriedChangedFlag.
BF48 AND A Test it.
BF49 RET Z Return if they have not changed.
BF4A XOR A Clear A.
BF4B LD ($5BB4),A Clear the flag at ObjectsCarriedChangedFlag.
This entry point is used by the routine at RunGame.
The objects are drawn using an object that is kept just for drawing the panel.
