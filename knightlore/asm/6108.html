6108: Font
The font that the game prints its text with, which has 28 characters of eight bytes each. A character in a message is its number in the font: 00 to 09 are the digits, 0A to 23 are the letters A to Z, 24 is a full stop, 25 is the copyright symbol, 26 is a space and 27 is a percent sign.
