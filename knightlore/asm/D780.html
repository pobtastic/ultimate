D780: Draw Aligned Columns
Unrolled code that draws the columns of a sprite that is on a byte boundary. Each column of a line takes a pair of bytes off the stack, which are the mask and the data, clears the bits of the mask in the byte of the buffer and sets the bits of the data. The code is entered at the column that gives the number of columns that the sprite has, and the last column carries on at DrawLines_Next.
The first column of a sprite that has five.
DrawAlignedColumns D780 POP DE Take the mask and the data of the column off the stack.
D781 LD A,(BC) Fetch the byte of the buffer.
D782 CPL Clear the bits of the mask and set the bits of the data.
D783 OR E
D784 CPL
D785 OR D
D786 LD (BC),A Store the byte and move on to the next one.
D787 INC BC
