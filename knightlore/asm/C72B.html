C72B: Store Pixel Adjustments
Used by the routines at HandleCauldronTop, SetPixelAdjustments, AdjustFlippedFarArch, HandleNearArch and HandleFlippedNearArch.
Sets the adjustments to the position of the sprite of an object.
Input
H Adjustment in Y
L Adjustment in X
IX Address of the object
StorePixelAdjustments C72B LD (IX+$12),L Set the adjustment in X.
C72E LD (IX+$13),H Set the adjustment in Y.
C731 RET Return.
