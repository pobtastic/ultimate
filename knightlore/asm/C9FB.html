C9FB: Set Player Speed
Used by the routine at MovePlayer.
Sets the speeds in X and Y of the player to walk forward. The adjustments of the speeds that arches make to nudge the player into doorways are added first, then the speed of 03 in the direction the player is facing.
Input
IX Address of the object
SetPlayerSpeed C9FB LD A,(IX+$09) Add the adjustment of the speed in X to the speed in X.
C9FE ADD A,(IX+$0E)
CA01 LD (IX+$09),A
CA04 LD A,(IX+$0A) Add the adjustment of the speed in Y to the speed in Y.
CA07 ADD A,(IX+$0F)
CA0A LD (IX+$0A),A
CA0D XOR A Clear the adjustments of the speeds.
CA0E LD (IX+$0E),A
CA11 LD (IX+$0F),A
CA14 LD BC,$CA32 Point BC at PlayerMovementTable.
This entry point is used by the routines at NudgeObjectsToArch and HandleRoomExit.
SetPlayerSpeed_Direction CA17 CALL GetFacingDirection Fetch the direction the player is facing.
CA1A LD L,A Use it as the index into the table.
CA1B JP RunGame_JumpToHandler Jump to the routine for the direction.
