BC66: Print Days
Used by the routines at RunGame and DisplaySunMoonFrame.
Prints the number of days that have passed on the panel, and makes the attributes there bright white.
PrintDays BC66 LD HL,$D9E2 Point HL at the position of the days in the screen buffer.
BC69 LD DE,$5BB9 Point DE at Days.
BC6C LD B,$01 Set the number of bytes to print to 01.
BC6E CALL PrintBCDNumber Print the number of days.
BC71 LD HL,$5AEF Point HL at the attributes of the days.
BC74 LD (HL),$47 Make the first one bright white.
BC76 INC L Move on to the next one.
BC77 LD (HL),$47 Make it bright white as well.
BC79 RET Return.
