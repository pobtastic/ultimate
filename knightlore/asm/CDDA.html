CDDA: Handle Human Top
Handles the top half of the player while it is in the form of a human, which is the graphic numbers 20 to 2F.
Input
IX Address of the object
HandleHumanTop CDDA CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Wide Set the pixel adjustments of the top half of a human.
CDDD JR HandlePlayerTop Jump to HandlePlayerTop.
