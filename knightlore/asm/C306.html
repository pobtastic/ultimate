C306: Start Transformation
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Starts the transformation of the player from human to wulf, or the other way round, when it is time to change (at the start of each day and each night). Nothing happens unless the change is due, and the player has been in the room for a while and is not jumping. Once the change starts the routine does not return to its caller, but to the one before, which skips the rest of the update of the player. The legs of the player are changed into the sparkles of HandleTransformation and the top half is hidden.
