C232: Draw Moving Object
Used by the routines at HandleRepelSpell, HandleSpecialObject, HandleChest and HandleTable.
Plays the sound of an object that is moving, and flags it to be wiped and drawn again.
Input
IX Address of the object
DrawMovingObject C232 CALL PlaySoundFromPosition Play the sound of a moving object.
C235 JP SetWipeAndDrawFlags Flag the object to be drawn again.
