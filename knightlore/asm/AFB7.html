AFB7: Start Life
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Starts a new life: puts the player objects back into the objects table and takes a life off the total. If the player has none left the game is over.
StartLife AFB7 CALL LoseLife Restore the player objects, take a life off and choose the player's graphics.
Continue on to RunGame.
