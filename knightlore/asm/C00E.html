C00E: Handle Pickup And Drop
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Picks up or drops an object when the player presses the control for it. A special object that is close enough is picked up. If the player carries three objects already, the one that has been carried longest is dropped in the place of the new one. Otherwise, if the player is carrying something and there is a free place for a special object in the room, the object that has been carried longest is dropped where the player stands, and the player is lifted up by 0C to stand on it. If the player is in the cauldron room and jumping above the cauldron, the object is dropped into the cauldron.
The routine looks for objects by making the player a little wider, deeper and taller, which is put right when the routine ends.
