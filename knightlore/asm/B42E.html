B42E: Play Bump Sound
Used by the routines at HandleBouncingBall, HandleFireMovingNorthSouth and HandleBallUpDown.
Plays four short bursts of a tone, whose pitches come from the first four bytes of the ROM, for a ball hitting the floor.
PlayBumpSound B42E LD HL,$0000 Point HL at the start of the ROM.
B431 LD E,$04 Set the number of bursts to play.
PlayBumpSound_NextBurst B433 LD C,$03 Set the number of cycles of each burst.
B435 LD A,(HL) Fetch the next byte of the ROM.
B436 INC HL Move on to the next one.
B437 OR %11000000 Make it a high pitch by setting its top two bits.
B439 LD B,A Set B to the pitch.
B43A CALL PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
B43D DEC E Decrease the burst counter and loop back to PlayBumpSound_NextBurst until all of the bursts have been played.
B43E JR NZ,PlayBumpSound_NextBurst
B440 RET Return.
