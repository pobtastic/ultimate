BCCA: Print Day Caption
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Prints the caption "DAY" on the panel. The colour of the caption is made to stand out against the colour of the room.
PrintDayCaption BCCA LD A,($5BAD) Fetch the attribute of the room.
BCCD CPL Invert it.
BCCE ADD A,$02 Add 02 to it.
BCD0 AND %00000111 Keep the ink colour.
BCD2 OR %01000000 Make it bright.
BCD4 LD ($BCE7),A Store it as the attribute of the caption.
BCD7 LD HL,$BCEC Point HL at DayCaptionFont.
BCDA LD ($5BC7),HL Use it to print.
BCDD LD DE,$BCE7 Point DE at DayCaption.
BCE0 LD HL,$0F70 Set the X coordinate to 70 and the Y coordinate to 0F.
BCE3 PUSH HL Stash the coordinates, as PrintText expects to find them there.
BCE4 JP PrintText Print the caption.
