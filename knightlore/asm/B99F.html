B99F: Handle Cauldron Top
Handles the top half of the cauldron, which is drawn 18 pixels to the left of and 0C pixels above where it is.
Input
IX Address of the object
HandleCauldronTop B99F LD HL,$0CE8 Set the pixel adjustments of 18 to the left and 0C up.
B9A2 JP StorePixelAdjustments Apply them and update the object.
