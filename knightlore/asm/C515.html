C515: Fill Window
Used by the routines at DisplayObjectsCarried, DisplaySunMoonFrame, SetBorderAttributes, ColourSunMoon and DrawChangedObjects.
Fills a rectangle of the screen buffer, or of the attribute file, with one byte. The rows of the rectangle are 20 bytes apart.
Input
A Byte to fill the rectangle with
B Width of the rectangle in bytes
C Height of the rectangle in lines
HL Address of the first byte
FillWindow C515 LD DE,$0020 Set DE to the length of a line.
FillWindow_Line C518 PUSH BC Stash the width and height.
C519 PUSH HL Stash the address of the start of the line.
FillWindow_Byte C51A LD (HL),A Fill a byte.
C51B INC HL Move on to the next byte.
C51C DJNZ FillWindow_Byte Jump back to FillWindow_Byte until the line is filled.
C51E POP HL Restore the address of the start of the line.
C51F ADD HL,DE Move on to the next line.
C520 POP BC Restore the width and height.
C521 DEC C Decrease the number of lines left.
C522 JR NZ,FillWindow_Line Jump back to FillWindow_Line until all of the lines have been filled.
C524 RET Return.
