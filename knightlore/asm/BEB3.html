BEB3: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Displays the menu, which is a list of eight lines of text (see DisplayTextList).
Point at the menu colours, positions and text.
DisplayMenu BEB3 LD DE,$BDA2 Point DE at MenuColours.
BEB6 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
BEB7 LD HL,$BDAA Point HL at MenuCoordinates.
BEBA LD DE,$BDBA Point DE at MenuText.
BEBD LD B,$08 Set the number of lines to 08.
