37422: Sprite: Human Legs
The sprites of the legs of the human form of the player, which are also the bottom half of a guard and of the wizard. The graphic numbers 16 to 21 are the six frames of the legs walking when facing west (or north if flipped from left to right), and 24 to 29 are the frames when facing east (or south). The frames are in the order 1, 2, 3, 4, 3 and 2, so that the sprites are used more than once. The guard and the wizard use the graphic numbers 144 to 149 and 152 to 157.
