51240: Handle Wulf Legs
Handles the bottom half of the player while it is in the form of a wulf, which is the graphic numbers 48 to 53 and 56 to 61.
Input
IX Address of the object
HandleWulfLegs 51240 CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Wulf Set the pixel adjustments of the legs of a wulf.
