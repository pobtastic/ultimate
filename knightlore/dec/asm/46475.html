46475: Sparkle Direction Table
The routines that give a sparkle its speeds in X and Y, chosen by the part of the room that the sparkle is in (bit 7 of the X coordinate and bit 7 of the Y coordinate).
SparkleDirectionTable 46475 DEFW SetSparkleSpeed X below 128 and Y below 128: X speed 252, Y speed 4.
46477 DEFW SetSparkleSpeed_PositivePositive X below 128 and Y at least 128: X speed 4, Y speed 4.
46479 DEFW SetSparkleSpeed_NegativeNegative X at least 128 and Y below 128: X speed 252, Y speed 252.
46481 DEFW SetSparkleSpeed_NegativePositive X and Y both at least 128: X speed 4, Y speed 252.
