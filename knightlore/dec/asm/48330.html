48330: Print Day Caption
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Prints the caption "DAY" on the panel. The colour of the caption is made to stand out against the colour of the room.
Choose a colour that stands out, then print "DAY".

48333 CPL Invert it.
48334 ADD A,2 Add 2 to it.
48336 AND %00000111 Keep the ink colour.
48338 OR %01000000 Make it bright.
48340 LD (48359),A Store the caption attribute at DayCaption.
