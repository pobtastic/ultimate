48330: Print Day Caption
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Prints the caption "DAY" on the panel. The colour of the caption is made to stand out against the colour of the room.
PrintDayCaption 48330 LD A,(23469) Fetch the attribute of the room.
48333 CPL Invert it.
48334 ADD A,2 Add 2 to it.
48336 AND %00000111 Keep the ink colour.
48338 OR %01000000 Make it bright.
48340 LD (48359),A Store it as the attribute of the caption.
48343 LD HL,48364 Point HL at DayCaptionFont.
48346 LD (23495),HL Use it to print.
48349 LD DE,48359 Point DE at DayCaption.
48352 LD HL,3952 Set the X coordinate to 112 and the Y coordinate to 15.
48355 PUSH HL Stash the coordinates, as PrintText expects to find them there.
48356 JP PrintText Print the caption.
