49975: Handle Transformation
Handles the graphic numbers 92 to 95 which are the legs of the player while the player transforms. Every fourth frame a different one of these graphics is chosen at random, and the sound of a transformation is played. When the number of steps has run out the player becomes human or wulf. If the player is killed during the transformation, the death sparkles start.
Input
IX Address of the legs of the player
