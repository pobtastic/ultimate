25169: Room Table
The objects of each of the 128 rooms of the game, in a table of entries that are of different lengths. Each entry is for a room number, in which the high four bits are the row of the room (the rows to the north have the larger numbers) and the low four bits are the column (the columns to the east have the larger numbers). RetrieveRoom finds the entry of a room and MakeRoomObjects builds the objects of the room from it.
Bytes Contents
0 The room number
1 The length of the rest of the entry, including this byte
2 The colour of the room in bits 0-2, and the size of the room in bits 3-7 (see RoomSizeTable)
3... The types of background object, which are the entries of the table at BackgroundTypeTable
255 Marks the end of the background objects. It is not there if the entry ends with them
Then The groups of foreground objects
A group of foreground objects is a byte that has the block type in bits 3-7 (see BlockTypeTable) and the number of times it is used, less one, in bits 0-2, followed by a position for each time. A position has the X coordinate in bits 0-2, the Y coordinate in bits 3-5 and the Z coordinate in bits 6-7, where each step of X and Y is 16 from 72 and each step of Z is 12 above the floor.
