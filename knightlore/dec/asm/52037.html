52037: Block Movement
Used by the routines at ApplyGravityAndMove and MovePlayer.
Stops an object moving through the walls of the room or through other objects. The speeds in X, Y and Z of the object are each brought closer to zero until the object can move without hitting anything. Whichever direction is hit is flagged in the low bits of the flags in byte 12 of the object: bit 0 for X, bit 1 for Y and bit 2 for Z. For the Z direction that means that the object is standing on something. An object that is flagged to be ignored in the 3D calculations is not adjusted.
Input
IX Address of the object

52054 LD L,0 Set the new speed in Y to zero.

The new speeds are kept in H for Z, C for X and L for Y. The Z direction goes first, and the floor of the room is tried before the objects.
