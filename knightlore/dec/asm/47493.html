47493: Toggle Graphic
Used by the routines at HandleBouncingBall, HandleFireMovingNorthSouth, HandleStationaryFire, HandleBallUpDown and HandleGhost.
Changes the graphic of an object between the two graphics of a pair, by flipping the lowest bit of the graphic number.
Input
IX Address of the object
ToggleGraphic 47493 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the graphic number.
47496 XOR %00000001 Flip the lowest bit.
47498 JR SetGraphic Jump to SetGraphic.
