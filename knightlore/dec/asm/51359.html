51359: Handle Turning
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Turns the player, using the left and right controls or, if a joystick with directional control is in use, the direction that is pressed. The directional controls turn the player a quarter turn at a time towards the direction pressed along the shortest way, and flag the player to walk once it is facing that direction. The facing of the player is stored as the horizontal flip flag and bit 3 of the graphic number.
Input
C The controls
IX Address of the object
Output
C The controls, with the walk control changed if directional control is in use
