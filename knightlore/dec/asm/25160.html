25160: Room Size Table
The sizes that a room can have, each as three bytes. The first two are the distances in X and Y from the middle of the room (128) to its walls, and the third is the Z coordinate of the floor. The size of a room is chosen by the high five bits of the colour and size byte of its entry in the table of rooms at RoomTable.
RoomSizeTable 25160 DEFB 64,64,128 Size 0: a room that is square.
25163 DEFB 32,64,128 Size 1: a room that is narrow in X.
25166 DEFB 64,32,128 Size 2: a room that is narrow in Y.
