51742: Get Facing Direction
Used by the routines at HandleTurning and SetPlayerSpeed.
Works out the direction an object is facing from the horizontal flip flag and bit 3 of the graphic number.
Input
IX Address of the object
Output
A The direction: 0 for west, 1 for east, 2 for north and
#N$03 for south
GetFacingDirection 51742 LD A,(IX+7) Fetch the flags of the object.
51745 RRCA Move the horizontal flip flag to bit 4.
51746 RRCA
51747 AND %00010000 Keep it.
51749 LD L,A Stash it in L.
51750 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the graphic number.
51753 AND %00001000 Keep bit 3.
51755 OR L Combine it with the flip flag.
51756 RRCA Move the two bits into the low end.
51757 RRCA
51758 RRCA
51759 AND %00000011 Keep them.
51761 RET Return.
