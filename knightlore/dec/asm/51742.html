51742: Get Facing Direction
Used by the routines at HandleTurning and SetPlayerSpeed.
Works out the direction that an object is facing from the horizontal flip flag and bit 3 of the graphic number: bit 6 of the flags of the object is made into bit 1 of the direction, and bit 3 of the graphic number into bit 0. The direction is 0 west, 1 east, 2 north and 3 south.
Input
IX The address of the object
Output
A The direction
Bit 6 of the flags to bit 1.
