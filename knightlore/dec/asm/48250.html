48250: Print Lives Graphic
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the graphic that goes next to the number of lives on the panel, and makes the attributes there bright white.
Draw the lives graphic and colour its attributes.

48254 LD (IX+0),140 Set the graphic number to 140.
48258 LD (IX+7),0 Clear the flags.

48273 LD A,71 Set the attribute to bright white.
