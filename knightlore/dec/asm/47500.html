47500: Next Sprite Number
Used by the routines at HandleCauldronBubbles and HandleRepelSpell.
Changes the graphic of an object to the next of a group of four, by adding one to the lowest two bits of the graphic number and ignoring the carry.
Input
IX Address of the object
Advance the lowest two bits of the graphic number.
NextSpriteNumber 47500 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the graphic number.
47503 LD C,A Stash it in C.
47504 AND %11111100 Keep the highest six bits.
47506 LD B,A Stash them in B.
47507 LD A,C Fetch the graphic number.
47508 INC A Add one.
47509 AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
47511 OR B Combine them with the highest six bits.
This entry point is used by the routine at ToggleGraphic.
SetGraphic 47512 LD (IX+0),A Store the new graphic number.
47515 RET Return.
