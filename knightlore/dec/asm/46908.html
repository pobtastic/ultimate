46908: Handle Guard Moving East West
Handles the top half of a guard that patrols east and west. The guard walks at a speed of 2 and turns round when it hits something. The bottom half of the guard, which is the next object in the table, is kept in step.
Input
IX Address of the top half of the guard
HandleGuardMovingEastWest 46908 CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Guard Set the pixel adjustments of a guard.
46911 BIT 0,(IX+13) Test whether the guard is heading east.
46915 LD A,2 Set the speed to 2.
46917 JR NZ,HandleGuardMovingEastWest_Speed Jump to HandleGuardMovingEastWest_Speed if the guard is heading east.
46919 NEG Make the speed negative.
HandleGuardMovingEastWest_Speed 46921 LD (IX+9),A Set the speed in X of the guard.
46924 LD (IX+41),A Set the speed in X of the bottom half of the guard too.
46927 CALL FaceDirectionOfMovement Choose the graphic of the guard from its direction.
46930 CALL ApplyGravityAndMove Apply gravity to the guard and move it.
46933 BIT 0,(IX+12) Jump to HandleGuardMovingEastWest_BottomHalf if the guard has not hit something along the X axis.
46937 JR Z,HandleGuardMovingEastWest_BottomHalf
46939 LD A,(IX+13) Fetch the direction flags of the guard.
46942 XOR %00000001 Turn the guard round.
46944 LD (IX+13),A Store them.
HandleGuardMovingEastWest_BottomHalf 46947 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the X coordinate of the guard.
46950 LD (IX+33),A Give it to the bottom half of the guard.
46953 JP SetDeadlyFlagsAndDraw Make the guard deadly and flag it to be drawn again.
