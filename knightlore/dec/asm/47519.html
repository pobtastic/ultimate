47519: Handle Cauldron Top
Handles the top half of the cauldron, which is drawn 24 pixels to the left of and 12 pixels above where it is.
Input
IX Address of the object
HandleCauldronTop 47519 LD HL,3304 Set the pixel adjustments of 24 to the left and 12 up.
47522 JP StorePixelAdjustments Apply them and update the object.
