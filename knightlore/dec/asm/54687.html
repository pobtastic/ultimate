54687: Draw Changed Objects
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Updates the playing area for the objects that have changed. First the sprites of the objects that moved are wiped out of the screen buffer: for each object in the list at ObjectsToDrawList that is flagged to be wiped, the area covered by the old and the new position of its sprite is cleared, and the area is kept on the stack. Then the objects are drawn in order, the sun or moon is moved, and the objects the player is carrying are shown. Last, each of the areas that were kept is copied from the buffer to the screen. When the whole room has to be drawn there is nothing to wipe, and the screen is updated by the game loop.
