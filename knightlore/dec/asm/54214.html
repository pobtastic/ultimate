54214: Retrieve Room
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Builds the objects of the room that the player is in from the room data. It looks for the room in the table of rooms at RoomTable, which has a variable length entry for each room. The entry has the number of the room, the length of the rest of the entry, the colour and size of the room, the types of background object that it has (which end with a 255 if there are groups of foreground objects after them) and the groups of foreground objects. The objects are put in the objects table from the first one after the player and the special objects. If the room is not in the table, none are made.
