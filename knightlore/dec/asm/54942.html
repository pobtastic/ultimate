54942: Build Lookup Tables
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Builds the two tables that the sprite routines use. The first is the set of tables of shifted bytes in pages 242 to 255: for each shift of one to seven bits to the right there is a page with the inverse of every byte shifted, and a page after it with the inverse of the bits that are pushed out into the next byte. The second is the table at ReversedByteTable of every byte with its bits reversed.
