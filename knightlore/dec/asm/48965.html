48965: Display Objects Carried
Used by the routine at DrawChangedObjects.
Draws the objects that the player is carrying on the panel, but only if they have changed since they were last drawn. The panel has room for three objects, which are drawn from the left: the object that was picked up last comes first. Each object is drawn in a space of 24 by 24 pixels, with a colour that goes with it.
