54256: Make Room Objects
Used by the routine at RetrieveRoom.
Makes the objects of a room from its entry in the table of rooms. The colour of the room is the low three bits of the byte after the length, with bright added, and the size of the room is the high five bits. The size gives the size of the room in X, Y and Z, from the table at RoomSizeTable. Each type of background object is the number of an entry in the table at BackgroundTypeTable, which holds the address of a list of objects that already have their coordinates. Each of these is copied into the objects table, along with the number of the room. The background objects end at a 255, or at the end of the entry if there are no foreground objects. Each group of foreground objects starts with a byte that has the number of a block in bits 3 to 7 and one less than the number of times it is used in bits 0 to 2, followed by that many positions. A block is a list of objects from the table at BlockTypeTable, each with a graphic, a size, flags and an offset, but no coordinates. A position has the X coordinate in bits 0 to 2, the Y coordinate in bits 3 to 5 and the Z coordinate in bits 6 and 7.
