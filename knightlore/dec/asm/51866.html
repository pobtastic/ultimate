51866: Leave Room To The West
Sends the player to the room to the west if it has gone past the west edge of the room. The X coordinate is set to 0 which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the west, and so puts it at the east side of the new room.
Input
HL The size of the room in X and Y
IX Address of the object
