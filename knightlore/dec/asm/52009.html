52009: Leave Room To The South
Sends the player to the room to the south if it has gone past the south edge of the room. The Y coordinate is set to 0 which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the south, and so puts it at the north side of the new room.
Input
HL The size of the room in X and Y
IX Address of the object
LeaveRoomToSouth 52009 POP HL Unstack the size of the room in X and Y.
52010 LD A,128 Work out the coordinate of the south edge of the room.
52012 SUB H
52013 LD H,A
52014 LD A,(IX+2) Add the speed in Y and the depth of the player to the Y coordinate.
52017 ADD A,(IX+10)
52020 ADD A,(IX+5)
52023 CP H Compare it with the south edge.
52024 RET NC Return if the player is still in the room.
52025 LD (IX+2),0 Set the Y coordinate to 0.
52029 LD A,(IX+8) Fetch the room number and take 16 off it to go to the room to the south.
