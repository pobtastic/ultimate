53665: Initial Player Data
The first eight bytes of each object of the player at the start of the game: the graphic number, the X, Y and Z coordinates, the width, the depth, the height and the flags.
InitialPlayerData 53665 DEFB 120,128,128,128,5,5,23,28 The legs, which are a sparkle, at the middle of the room, with a height of 23.
53673 DEFB 120,128,128,140,5,5,0,30 The top half, which is a sparkle too, 12 higher than the legs and with no height.
