52706: Handle Player Top
Used by the routine at HandleHumanTop.
Does all of the work for the top half of the player, which is a copy of the bottom half, placed 12 above it. The top half has no height, and is ignored in the 3D calculations. If it has been flagged as dead, this starts the death sparkles. Every so often the player looks to one side or the other: this picks a head that looks that way and keeps it for eight frames.
