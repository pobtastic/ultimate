45874: Music Note Table
The three bytes for each of the notes, where the notes are numbered from 0. The first two bytes are the delay between the clicks of the speaker, which is shorter for the higher notes, and the third is the basic length of the note, which is the number of cycles of the note to play. PlayNote uses it.
MusicNotes 45874 DEFB 0,0,0 The rest has no delay and no length, as the rest does not use the table.
