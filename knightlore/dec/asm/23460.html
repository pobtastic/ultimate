23460: Control Method
The way that the player chose to control the game on the menu.
Bits Meaning
3 The controls are directional (a joystick that moves the player in the direction that it is pushed)
1-2 0 is the keyboard, 1 is the Kempston joystick, 2 is the Cursor joystick and 3 is the Interface II joystick
