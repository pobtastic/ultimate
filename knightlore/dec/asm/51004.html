51004: Handle Near Arch
Handles the graphic numbers 2 and 4 which are the near side of an arch and of a tree arch. The speeds in X, Y and Z of the arch are not used to move it, so they are used to keep the position of the middle of the doorway. The player, and any special object, that gets close enough to the arch is flagged as near it, and is nudged towards the middle line of the doorway, so that it goes through the middle.
Input
IX Address of the arch object
HandleNearArch 51004 BIT 6,(IX+7) Jump to HandleFlippedNearArch if the arch is flipped horizontally.
51008 JR NZ,HandleFlippedNearArch
51010 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the graphic number of the arch.
51013 CP 4 Jump to AdjustTreeArch if it is a tree arch.
51015 JR Z,AdjustTreeArch
51017 LD HL,65017 Set HL to the adjustments of 249 in X and 253 in Y.
This entry point is used by the routine at AdjustTreeArch.
The doorway is thirteen units further along the Y axis than the arch.
HandleNearArch_Position 51020 CALL StorePixelAdjustments Set the adjustments.
51023 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the Y coordinate of the arch.
51026 ADD A,13 Add 13 to it.
51028 LD (IX+10),A Keep it as the Y coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
51031 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the X coordinate of the arch.
51034 LD (IX+9),A Keep it as the X coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
51037 LD HL,1551 Set H to the distance in Y that counts as near and L to the distance in X.
This entry point is used by the routine at HandleFlippedNearArch.
HandleNearArch_Near 51040 LD A,(IX+3) Fetch the Z coordinate of the arch.
51043 LD (IX+11),A Keep it as the Z coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
51046 CALL FlagObjectsNearArch Flag the objects that are near the arch.
51049 JP NudgeObjectsToArch Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch.
