48055: Rating Table
The addresses of the eight ratings that can be given on the results screen. The rating is chosen by the number of rooms that have been visited (in steps of 32) and by whether the game was completed. A completed game always gets at least the fifth rating.
RatingTable 48055 DEFW RatingText Fewer than 33 rooms visited: RatingText.
48057 DEFW RatingText_Average 33 to 64 rooms visited: RatingText_Average.
48059 DEFW RatingText_Fair 65 to 96 rooms visited: RatingText_Fair.
48061 DEFW RatingText_Good More than 96 rooms visited: RatingText_Good.
48063 DEFW RatingText_Excellent Game completed, with fewer than 33 rooms visited: RatingText_Excellent.
48065 DEFW RatingText_Marvellous Game completed, with 33 to 64 rooms visited: RatingText_Marvellous.
48067 DEFW RatingText_Hero Game completed, with 65 to 96 rooms visited: RatingText_Hero.
48069 DEFW RatingText_Adventurer Game completed, with more than 96 rooms visited: RatingText_Adventurer.
