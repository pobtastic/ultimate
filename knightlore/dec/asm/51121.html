51121: Nudge Object East West
Sets the adjustment of the speed in Y of an object, to bring it a step closer to the middle line of an arch that goes east and west.
Input
IX Address of the arch object
IY Address of the object
NudgeObjectEastWest 51121 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the Y coordinate of the middle of the doorway.
51124 CP (IY+2) Compare it with the Y coordinate of the object.
51127 JR Z,NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore if the object is in the middle.
51129 LD A,1 Set the adjustment to 1.
51131 JR NC,NudgeObjectEastWest_Set Jump to NudgeObjectEastWest_Set if the object is south of the middle.
51133 NEG Make the adjustment negative.
NudgeObjectEastWest_Set 51135 LD (IY+15),A Set the adjustment of the speed in Y of the object.
51138 JR NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore Jump to NudgeObjectsToArch_Restore.
