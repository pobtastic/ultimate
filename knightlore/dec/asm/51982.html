51982: Leave Room To The North
Sends the player to the room to the north if it has gone past the north edge of the room. The Y coordinate is set to 255 which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the north, and so puts it at the south side of the new room.
Input
HL The size of the room in X and Y
IX Address of the object
LeaveRoomToNorth 51982 POP HL Unstack the size of the room in X and Y.
51983 LD A,H Work out the coordinate of the north edge of the room.
51984 ADD A,128
51986 LD H,A
51987 LD A,(IX+2) Add the speed in Y to the Y coordinate, and take the depth of the player off it.
51990 ADD A,(IX+10)
51993 SUB (IX+5)
51996 CP H Compare it with the north edge.
51997 RET C Return if the player is still in the room.
51998 LD (IX+2),255 Set the Y coordinate to 255.
52002 LD A,(IX+8) Fetch the room number and add 16 to go to the room to the north.
52005 ADD A,16
52007 JR ChangeRoom Jump to ChangeRoom.
