46243: Play Low Beep
Used by the routines at GameMenu, HandlePickupAndDrop and HandleExtraLife.
Plays a low beep, which is sixteen cycles of a low tone. It tells the player that something has happened.
Play sixteen cycles of a low tone.
PlayLowBeep 46243 LD BC,32784 Set the pitch and the number of cycles.
46246 JR PlayWaveSequence Play the tone.
