51617: Move Player
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Moves the player by one frame. The speeds in X and Y are only set up while the player walks or is jumping, and they are cleared again afterwards. The speed in Z has gravity taken off it, which is more if the player is falling or has let go of the jump control.
