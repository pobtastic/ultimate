50469: Place Special Objects In Room
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Makes the list of the special objects that are in the room that the player is in, ready to be updated like any other object. Every entry of the table of special objects at SpecialObjectsTable that is in the room is copied to the objects table (up to two of them), with the width and depth of 5 the height of 12 and the flags for drawing and for being able to move. The address of the entry is kept at 16 bytes into the object, so that it can be updated by UpdateSpecialObjects. The rest of the objects table for the special objects is cleared.
