50782: Handle Portcullis
Handles a portcullis that is not moving. Nothing happens while another portcullis in the room is moving. A portcullis that is on the floor of the room, or no more than 31 above it, starts to rise on one frame in thirty-two. One that is higher than that starts to come down: at once for the first four times that a portcullis comes down, and after that on one frame in thirty-two.
Input
IX Address of the portcullis object
Start the portcullis rising or lowering when it is time.
