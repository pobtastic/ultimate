52557: Flag Overlapping Objects
Used by the routines at SetWipeAndDrawFlags and HandlePlayerTop.
Flags every object that overlaps the area of the screen covered by an object to be drawn again. The area is the one that is covered by the old and the new position of the sprite of the object together, so that the whole of it is redrawn.
Input
IX Address of the object
