53785: Visit Room
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Flags the room that the player is in as visited, in the bitmap of the rooms at RoomsVisitedBitmap. The bit that is set is chosen by changing the instruction at VisitRoom_Set, which is a SET instruction, by changing the byte that follows it.
Set the bit of this room in RoomsVisitedBitmap.
