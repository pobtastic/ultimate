51113: Arch Direction Table
The routines that nudge an object towards the middle of a doorway, by the direction of the arch: the lowest bit is bit 3 of the graphic number of the arch, and the next is whether the sprite is flipped.
ArchDirectionTable 51113 DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest Not flipped: NudgeObjectEastWest.
51115 DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest
51117 DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth Flipped: NudgeObjectNorthSouth.
51119 DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth
