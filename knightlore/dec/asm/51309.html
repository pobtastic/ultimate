51309: Handle Player Outside Room
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Handles the player being outside the room, which happens while the player walks through an arch. The player may fall, but is not allowed to rise.
Input
IX Address of the object
HandlePlayerOutsideRoom 51309 LD A,(IX+11) Fetch the speed in Z of the player.
51312 AND A Test whether it is negative.
51313 JP M,HandlePlayerLegs_Move Jump to HandlePlayerLegs_Move if it is, as the player is falling.
51316 XOR A Set the speed in Z to zero.
51317 LD (IX+11),A
51320 JR HandlePlayerLegs_Move Jump to HandlePlayerLegs_Move.
