55368: Calculate Attribute Address
Used by the routines at PrintText and DisplayObjectsCarried.
Finds the address in the attributes of the character square that holds a pixel.
Input
H Pixel Y coordinate (from the bottom of the screen)
L Pixel X coordinate
Output
DE Address of the attribute
