47167: Handle Stationary Fire
Handles a fire that stays where it is, flickering by changing frame and mirroring itself on every other pass. No fire like this appears in the game.
Input
IX Address of the fire object
Flicker the fire on every other pass.
HandleStationaryFire 47167 CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Ball Set the pixel adjustments of a ball.
47170 LD A,(23484) Fetch the fire seed from FireSeed.
47173 AND %00000001 Test the lowest bit.
47175 RET Z Return on every other pass.
47176 LD A,(23461) Fetch the second random seed from RandomSeed2.
47179 AND %01000000 Keep the bit that flips the sprite.
47181 XOR (IX+7) Combine it with the flags of the object.
47184 LD (IX+7),A Store the flags, which makes the fire flip at random.
47187 CALL ToggleGraphic Toggle the graphic of the fire between its two frames.
