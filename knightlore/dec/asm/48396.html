48396: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Displays the menu and waits for the control method to be chosen and the game to be started. The keys "1" to "4" choose the keyboard, the Kempston joystick, the cursor joystick and Interface II, "5" turns directional control on or off, and "0" starts the game. A low beep sounds when the choice changes. The menu tune is played once. The choice is held in ControlMethod.
