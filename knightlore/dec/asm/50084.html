50084: Display Sun Moon Frame
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Shows the window of the panel that holds the sun or the moon, and the sun or moon in it. The sun and the moon go along an arc from pixel X coordinate 176 to 224 which is the height from the table at SunMoonHeights. When one of them gets to the end the other takes over, the player transforms, and each time the sun comes back a day is added and the day is shown on the panel. When 64 days have passed the game is over. Nothing is done when the final animation is under way.
