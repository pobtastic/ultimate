Robot
In the rooms the objects have no colours of their own: the whole screen is given the colour of the room, which is different in each room. The sprites in these tables that are not in the scenes at the end of the game are shown in bright white.
The robot is made of two sprites, one for the bottom half and one for the top half, which are drawn one above the other.

Game Scenes
The objects in the scenes at the end of the game are coloured with the attributes that are in the tables at GameOverScene and GameCompletedScene. The alarm flashes between black and the colour shown. The top and bottom halves of the robot when the game has been completed turn bright white when it comes out of the oil.
