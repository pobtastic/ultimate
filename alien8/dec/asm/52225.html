52225: Place Player At Door
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Puts the player in the right place when it has come into a room through an arch. The routine that moved the player out of the last room set one of its positions to 0 or 255, which tells which way the player came. The player is put at the opposite edge of the room, just outside the wall, and at the height of the arch. If the positions are not one of these, as when the player starts the game or starts again after losing a life, nothing is changed.
Input
IX Address of the player
