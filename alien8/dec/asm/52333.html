52333: Find Door Height
Used by the routine at PlacePlayerAtDoor.
Looks through the first four pairs of objects of the room for an arch (a sprite number below 4) whose x and y positions add up to a given number. When it finds one it gives the player the height of the arch.
Input
C Sum of the x and y positions of the arch
IX Address of the player
Walk the first four pairs.
