46779: Play High Beep
Used by the routines at HandleClockworkMouse and HandleConveyorNorth.
Plays a high beep, which is 32 beeps at a pitch of 48 (higher than the middle beep at PlayMiddleBeep).
Set the pitch and the beep count.
PlayHighBeep 46779 LD BC,12320 Set the pitch to 48 and the number of beeps to 32.
