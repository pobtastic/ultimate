52446: Make Room Objects
Used by the routine at RetrieveRoom.
Makes the objects of a room from its entry in the room data. The colour of the room is the low three bits of the byte after the length, and the size of the room is the high two bits. The size gives the distance to the walls and the floor level, from the table at RoomSizes. Each type of block is the number of an entry in the table at BlockTypes, which holds the address of a template of one or more objects. These are copied into the objects table, along with the number of the room. The types of block end with a 255, or at the end of the entry if there are no more objects. After the 255 come the groups of other objects, each with a type and a number of places (see RoomData).

The offset has the half step along the x axis in bit 0, the half step along the y axis in bit 1, and the offset up the z axis in bits 2 to 7. The place has the x position in bits 0 to 2, the y position in bits 3 to 5 and the height in bits 6 and 7. The x position of the object is the x position times 16, plus 8 if the half step is set, plus 72.
