49618: Turning Right Sprite Table
The sprite number and the flags of the player when it starts to turn right, picked by the direction that the player is facing (see GetFacingDirection). The sprite is one of objects 24 to 27.
TurningRightSpriteTable 49618 DEFB 24,0 Facing west: sprite 24, not flipped.
49620 DEFB 25,0 Facing east: sprite 25, not flipped.
49622 DEFB 26,0 Facing north: sprite 26, not flipped.
49624 DEFB 27,64 Facing south: sprite 27, flipped.
