52391: Retrieve Room
Used by the routine at BuildRoomObjects.
Builds the objects of the room that the player is in from the room data. It looks for the room in the room data (see RoomData), which has a variable length entry for each room. The objects are put in the objects table from RoomObjects, which is the first one after the player and the special objects. If the room is not in the data, none are made.
Input
IX Address of the player
Point at the room data, then find the entry for this room.
