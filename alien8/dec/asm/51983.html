51983: Draw Panel
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the sprites of the panel at the bottom of the screen, using the table at PanelSprites. On each side there is a run of five sprites that go up in steps, a column of six sprites and two more sprites, and the middle of the panel has the extra life that is the symbol for the lives and four more sprites. The right side is the left side flipped.
After DrawPanel and UpdateScreen
Left side, then right, then the middle.
