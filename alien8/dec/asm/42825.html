42825: Update Panel
Used by the routines at RunGame and HandleValve.
Colours the screen and the status panel after DrawPanel has drawn it, then prints the carried objects, the "LIGHT YEARS" caption, the chambers activated and the lives. The colour of the room is made bright and used for the whole attribute file.
Fill the attribute file with the colour of the room.
