49027: Fill Window
Used by the routines at UpdatePanel, DisplayObjectsCarried, SetBorderAttributes and DrawChangedObjects.
Fills a rectangle of the screen buffer, or of the attribute buffer, with a byte. The rows of the rectangle are 32 bytes apart. The loop is unrolled into eight stores, and the code jumps into the middle of them to store only as many bytes as the width of the rectangle (which can be no more than eight): the number of stores to skip is worked out and written into the operand of the jump at FillWindow_Line.
Input
A Byte to fill the rectangle with
B Width of the rectangle in bytes
C Height of the rectangle in lines
HL Address of the first byte
Work out how many stores to skip.
