43681: Handle Clockwork Mouse
The update routine for the clockwork mouse (objects 116 to 119), which is deadly to touch. It runs along one axis for a random number of passes in IX+16, or until it hits something. Then it chooses a new random speed and turns through a right angle, alternating between the x and y axes. The sprite number says which way it faces, and the flip flag says which axis it uses.
