44390: Update Light Years
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Counts the light years down, prints the four digits in the counter box, inverts the last of them and copies the counter to the screen. When all of the digits and their animation steps are zero the game is over. The light years go down by one every eight frames.
Print the four digits into the buffer.
