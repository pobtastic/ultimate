48285: Display Objects Carried
Used by the routine at DrawChangedObjects.
Displays the objects which the player is carrying in the panel, if they have changed (see ObjectsCarriedChangedFlag). Each of the three places takes three cells across and is 24 pixels apart from the next. The place is cleared, the sprite of the object is printed in the screen buffer and copied to the screen, and the cells are coloured according to the shape of the object (see CarriedObjectColours). DisplayObjectsCarried_Display displays the objects whether they have changed or not.
After DisplayObjectsCarried
Return unless the objects have changed.
