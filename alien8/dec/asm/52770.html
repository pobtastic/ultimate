52770: Pause Game
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Pauses the game if CAPS SHIFT or SPACE is pressed on its own. The game waits for the key to be let go of, then for it to be pressed again and let go of again. There is a beep for the start and for the end of the pause.
Return unless CAPS SHIFT or SPACE is pressed on its own.
