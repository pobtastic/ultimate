44065: Colour Cells
Used by the routines at HandleEndingRobotTop, HandleEndingRobotBottom, HandleAttackingTool and HandleAlarm.
Colours the attribute cells covered by an object, using the colour in the object record. Cells which already have the colour 68 are left alone.
