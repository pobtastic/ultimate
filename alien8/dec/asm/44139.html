44139: Count Statistics
Used by the routine at GameOver.
Goes through the data for all of the rooms and counts those whose first object is a cryonaut. If the room holds a placed valve (objects 100 to 103) it is counted in ChambersFoundActivated; otherwise it is counted in ChambersNotActivated and its cryonauts are added to CryonautsLost. All of the totals are BCD. They are shown when the game is over.
Clear the totals and start at the first room.
