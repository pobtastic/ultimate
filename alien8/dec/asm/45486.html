45486: Face Direction Of Movement
Used by the routine at HandleBouncingMouse.
Sets the way a robot mouse faces to match the direction in which it is moving, if it is moving along one axis. The way it faces is held in bit 0 of its sprite number and bit 6 of its flags (see TurnMouseOneWay).
Input
IX The address of the object
Face along the axis with the larger speed.
