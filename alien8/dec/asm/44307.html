44307: Handle Egg Top
The handler for the top half of a broken egg (object 73), which is deadly to touch. Only one is active at a time (EggTopFlag is set), and none are while EggTopDisabledFlag is set. An inactive egg top becomes active with a chance of one in sixteen on each frame. While it is active it is moved with gravity and makes a sound when it lands. Until then it hangs in the air.
Input
IX The address of the object
Hang in the air until this egg is chosen to drop.
