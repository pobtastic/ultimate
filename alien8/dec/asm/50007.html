50007: Stop Object At Floor
Used by the routine at BlockMovement.
Stops an object from going through the floor. If the z position of the object after it has moved is below the floor of the room, the object is flagged as blocked along z, and its speed along z is brought closer to zero until the object no longer goes through the floor.
Input
H Speed along the z axis
IX The address of the object
Output
H The new speed along the z axis
Test the floor and slow z.
