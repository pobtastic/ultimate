52869: Update Screen
Used by the routines at RunGame, GameOver and DisplayTextList.
Copies the screen buffer to the screen. The buffer holds the lines of the screen from the bottom up, 32 bytes to a line, so the lines are copied from the bottom of the screen to the top.
