46769: Play Low Beep
Used by the routines at HandleAlarm, GameMenu, HandlePickupAndDrop and HandleExtraLife.
Plays a low beep, which is 16 beeps at a pitch of 128.
Set the pitch and the beep count.
PlayLowBeep 46769 LD BC,32784 Set the pitch to 128 and the number of beeps to 16.
