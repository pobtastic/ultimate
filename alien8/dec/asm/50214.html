50214: Leave Room To The South
Sends the player to the room to the south if it has gone past the south edge of the room. The y position is set to 0, which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the south and puts it at the north side of the new room.
Input
HL Distances to the walls along x and y
IX The address of the object
Leave if past the south edge.
