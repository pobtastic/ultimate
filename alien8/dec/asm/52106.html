52106: Print Border
Used by the routines at GameOver and DisplayTextList.
Draws the border around the playing area, using the sprites in the table at BorderSprites. The four corners are drawn first, then the top and bottom edges, which are each made of 24 sprites that are eight pixels wide, and the left and right edges, which are each made of 128 sprites that are one pixel high.
After PrintBorder and UpdateScreen
Corners, then the four edges.
