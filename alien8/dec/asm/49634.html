49634: Handle Turning Legs
The handler for the lower half of the robot while it is turning (objects 24 to 27). The turn takes two passes: the first counts the turn down while the player is moved, and the second sets the sprite that the player ends up with (see TurnedRightSpriteTable and TurnedLeftSpriteTable) and plays a footstep.
