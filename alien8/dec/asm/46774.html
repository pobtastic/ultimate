46774: Play Middle Beep
Used by the routine at PauseGame.
Plays a middle beep, which is 24 beeps at a pitch of 80 (higher than the low beep at PlayLowBeep). It is the sound of the game being paused and of it carrying on again.
Set the pitch and the beep count.
PlayMiddleBeep 46774 LD BC,20504 Set the pitch to 80 and the number of beeps to 24.
