49130: Handle Near Arch
The handler for the near side of an arch (object 2). As an arch does not move, its speeds are used to keep the position of the middle of the doorway: the middle is 13 further along the y axis than the arch, or 13 further back along the x axis if the sprite is flipped. The player is flagged if near the arch, and is nudged towards the middle of the doorway.
