25696: Room Sizes
The three sizes of room. Each has the distance from the middle of the room to its east and west walls, the distance to its north and south walls, and the level of the floor. The size is chosen by the top two bits of the second byte of the data for each room (see RoomData).
RoomSizes 25696 DEFB 64,64,64 Size 0: a square room.
25699 DEFB 32,64,64 Size 1: a room which is narrow from east to west.
25702 DEFB 64,32,64 Size 2: a room which is narrow from north to south.
