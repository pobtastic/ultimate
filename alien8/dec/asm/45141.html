45141: Handle Mouse Second Part
The handler for the second part of a robot mouse (object 11). It takes its position from the first part, which comes before it in the table of objects, and sits 12 lower. When the first part is moving it makes a noise and is redrawn.
Input
IX The address of the object
Follow the first part and redraw if it moves.
