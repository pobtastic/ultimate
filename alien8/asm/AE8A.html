AE8A: Calculate Pixel Position Of Other Object
Used by the routines at HandleSocket and HandlePickupAndDrop.
Works out the pixel position of the object at IY by calling CalculatePixelCoordinates with IX pointing at it. Neither IX nor IY is changed.
Input
IY The address of the object
Call CalculatePixelCoordinates for the object at IY.
CalculatePixelPositionOfOtherObject AE8A PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
AE8C PUSH IY Copy IY to IX (using the stack).
AE8E POP IX
AE90 CALL CalculatePixelCoordinates Call CalculatePixelCoordinates.
AE93 POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
AE95 RET Return.
