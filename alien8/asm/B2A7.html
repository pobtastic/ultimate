B2A7: Set Deadly Flags
Used by the routines at HandleLandMine, HandleSpikedDome, HandleEggTop, HandleDeadlyObject and SetDeadlyFlagsAndDraw.
Makes the object deadly to touch by setting bits 5 and 7 of its flags.
Input
IX The address of the object
Set bits 5 and 7 of the flags.
SetDeadlyFlags B2A7 LD A,(IX+$0D) Fetch the flags.
B2AA OR %10100000 Set bits 5 and 7.
B2AC LD (IX+$0D),A Store the flags.
B2AF RET Return.
