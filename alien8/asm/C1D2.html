C1D2: Turning Right Sprite Table
The sprite number and the flags of the player when it starts to turn right, picked by the direction that the player is facing (see GetFacingDirection). The sprite is one of objects 18 to 1B.
TurningRightSpriteTable C1D2 DEFB $18,$00 Facing west: sprite 18, not flipped.
C1D4 DEFB $19,$00 Facing east: sprite 19, not flipped.
C1D6 DEFB $1A,$00 Facing north: sprite 1A, not flipped.
C1D8 DEFB $1B,$40 Facing south: sprite 1B, flipped.
