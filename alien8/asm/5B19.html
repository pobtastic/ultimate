5B19: Position Offset
The offset applied to the positions of the objects as RetrieveRoom builds a room, set by the objects of type 00 in the data for the room (see RoomData). Bit 0 moves the objects half a place along the x axis, bit 1 half a place along the y axis, and bits 2 to 7 up the z axis.
