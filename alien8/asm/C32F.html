C32F: Player Movement Table
The routines that set the speed of the player, by the direction that the player is facing (see GetFacingDirection).
PlayerMovementTable C32F DEFW MovePlayerWest Facing west: MovePlayerWest.
C331 DEFW MovePlayerEast Facing east: MovePlayerEast.
C333 DEFW MovePlayerNorth Facing north: MovePlayerNorth.
C335 DEFW MovePlayerSouth Facing south: MovePlayerSouth.
