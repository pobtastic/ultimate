C2EE: Clear Speed
Used by the routines at HandleValve, HandleFatBottomBlock, HandleExtraLife and MovePlayer.
Sets the speeds of the object along the x and y axes to zero.
Input
IX The address of the object
ClearSpeed C2EE XOR A Write 00 to the speeds along x and y.
C2EF LD (IX+$09),A
C2F2 LD (IX+$0A),A
C2F5 RET Return.
