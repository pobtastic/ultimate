BEE0: Handle Extra Life
The handler for an extra life (object 0C). When the player touches it the object turns into a sparkle, it is removed from the table of special objects, the egg tops are allowed to move again and a life is added and printed in the panel. An extra life which has not been touched is moved with gravity and makes a sound when it moves.
Input
IX The address of the object
Test whether the player is near.

BEE3 PUSH IX Copy IX to IY (using the stack).
BEE5 POP IY
BEE7 LD IX,$5B88 Point IX at PlayerBottomObject.
BEEB INC (IX+$04) Make the player a little wider.
BEEE INC (IX+$05) Make the player a little deeper.
BEF1 CALL TestNearObject Call TestNearObject.
BEF4 DEC (IX+$04) Put the width of the player back.
BEF7 DEC (IX+$05) Put the depth of the player back.
BEFA PUSH IY Copy IY to IX (using the stack).
BEFC POP IX
BEFE JR NC,HandleExtraLife_Move Jump to HandleExtraLife_Move if the player is not near.
