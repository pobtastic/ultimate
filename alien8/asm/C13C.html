C13C: Handle Turning
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Turns the player to face the direction that was asked for. Where a joystick with directional control is in use the walk control is set when the player is already facing the direction that is pressed, and otherwise the player is turned. With the other methods of control the left and right controls turn the player. The routine returns early while the player is entering the room, jumping or not standing on something.
Turning starts by showing one of the sprites of objects 18 to 1B (see TurningRightSpriteTable and TurningLeftSpriteTable); HandleTurningLegs finishes the turn.
Input
C The controls
IX The address of the object
Output
C The controls, with the walk control changed
Use rotational control unless a joystick is directional.
