B4BA: Play Music
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, HandleEndingRobotBottom and GameCompleted.
Plays a tune, which cannot be stopped. A tune is a list of notes (see PlayNote) which ends with FF.
The tunes played by this routine are:
Tune Sound
Start-up Tune
Robot Tune
Game Complete Tune
Input
DE Address of the tune
Play each note until FF.
PlayMusic B4BA LD A,(DE) Fetch the next note.
B4BB CP $FF Jump to PlayMusic_End if it is FF.
B4BD JR Z,PlayMusic_End
B4BF CALL PlayNote Call PlayNote.
B4C2 JR PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic.
This entry point is used by the routine at PlayMusic_WaitKey.
PlayMusic_End B4C4 RET Return.
