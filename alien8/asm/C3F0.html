C3F0: Leave Room To The East
Sends the player to the room to the east if it has gone past the east edge of the room. The x position is set to FF, which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the east and puts it at the west side of the new room.
Input
HL Distances to the walls along x and y
IX The address of the object
Leave if past the east edge.
