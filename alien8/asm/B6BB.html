B6BB: Play High Beep
Used by the routines at HandleClockworkMouse and HandleConveyorNorth.
Plays a high beep, which is 20 beeps at a pitch of 30 (higher than the middle beep at PlayMiddleBeep).
Set the pitch and the beep count.
PlayHighBeep B6BB LD BC,$3020 Set the pitch to 30 and the number of beeps to 20.
B6BE JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence.
