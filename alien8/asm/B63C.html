B63C: Play Sound From Height
Used by the routines at HandleLandMine, HandleRemoteControlRobot, HandleEggTop, HandleCircuitMouse, HandleFallingBlock, HandleMovingBlock and MovePlayer.
Plays a tone for an object that is falling or rising, with a pitch taken from the height of the object.
Input
IX The address of the object
Use the height as the pitch source.
