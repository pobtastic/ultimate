C082: Flag Player Near Arch
Used by the routine at HandleNearArch.
Flags the player as being near an arch (bit 0 of the flags), if close to the middle of the doorway.
Input
H Reach along the y axis
L Reach along the x axis
IX The address of the arch object
Test that the player is flagged to be adjusted and is near the doorway.
