B3C5: Start Game Tune
The tune played when a game starts. Each byte is a note: bits 0 to 5 are the note number (see MusicNotes) and bits 6 and 7 are the length less one. The tune ends with FF.
StartGameTune B3C5 DEFB $25,$28,$2C,$25,$2A,$2D,$2C,$2A
B3CD DEFB $28,$2C,$2A,$28,$27,$2A,$28,$27
B3D5 DEFB $65,$FF
