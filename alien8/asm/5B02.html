5B02: Frame Counter
Counts the frames of the game, which also makes it an address that moves through the ROM, so that the byte found there is added into RandomSeed2 to stir it up. The low byte times the animations and the sound effects and is used as a source of random numbers.
FrameCounter 5B02 DEFW $0000
