B0DD: Turn Mouse Other Way
Used by the routine at HandleCircuitMouse.
Turns a robot mouse a quarter turn the other way round the cycle used by TurnMouseOneWay.
Input
IX The address of the object
Flip bit 0, then turn one way.
TurnMouseOtherWay B0DD LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the sprite number.
B0E0 XOR %00000001 Flip bit 0.
B0E2 LD (IX+$00),A Store the sprite number.
B0E5 JR TurnMouseOneWay Jump to TurnMouseOneWay.
