D135: Calculate Screen Address
Used by the routines at UpdateLightYears, DisplayObjectsCarried, CopyAreaToScreen and DrawChangedObjects.
Finds the address on the screen of the byte that holds a pixel. The pixel y position is from the bottom of the screen.
Input
B The pixel y position
C The pixel x position
Output
DE The address on the screen
Work out the byte from the position.
