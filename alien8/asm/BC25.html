BC25: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Displays the menu, which is a list of eight lines of text (see DisplayTextList).
Point at the menu colours, positions and text.
DisplayMenu BC25 LD DE,$BB14 Point DE at MenuColours.
BC28 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
BC29 LD HL,$BB1C Point HL at MenuCoordinates.
BC2C LD DE,$BB2C Point DE at MenuText.
BC2F LD B,$08 Set the number of lines to 08.
