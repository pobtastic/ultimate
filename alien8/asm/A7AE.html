A7AE: Colour Counter Box
Used by the routine at UpdatePanel.
Copies CounterBoxAttributes to the attribute file. The box is six cells wide and three cells high.
Copy the attributes of the box.
ColourCounterBox A7AE LD HL,$5A99 Point HL at 5A99 (in the attribute buffer).
A7B1 LD DE,$A7CA Point DE at CounterBoxAttributes.
A7B4 LD BC,$0603 Set the width to 06 and the height to 03.
ColourCounterBox_Row A7B7 PUSH BC Stash the size and the address of the row.
A7B8 PUSH HL
ColourCounterBox_Cell A7B9 LD A,(DE) Fetch the attribute of the cell.
A7BA INC DE Move on to the next attribute.
A7BB LD (HL),A Write it to the attribute file.
A7BC INC HL Move on to the next cell.
A7BD DJNZ ColourCounterBox_Cell Jump to ColourCounterBox_Cell until the row is done.
A7BF POP HL Restore the address of the row from the stack.
A7C0 LD A,$20 Set A to 20.
A7C2 CALL AddAToHL Call AddAToHL.
A7C5 POP BC Restore the size of the box from the stack.
A7C6 DEC C Jump to ColourCounterBox_Row until all of the rows are done.
A7C7 JR NZ,ColourCounterBox_Row
A7C9 RET Return.
