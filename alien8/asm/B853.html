B853: Game Completed Scene
The objects in the game completed scene (see GameCompleted). Each entry has five bytes: the sprite number, the flags, the colour, and the pixel x and y coordinates. The table ends with 00.
GameCompletedScene B853 DEFB $5C,$10,$07,$70,$80 Sprite 5C, flags 10, colour INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK, at pixel position (70, 80).
B858 DEFB $5D,$10,$07,$70,$90 Sprite 5D, flags 10, colour INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK, at pixel position (70, 90).
B85D DEFB $55,$10,$44,$6C,$20 Sprite 55, flags 10, colour INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT), at pixel position (6C, 20).
B862 DEFB $00 Terminator.
