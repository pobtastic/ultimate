B3D7: Robot Tune
The tune played when the robot has come back up out of the pot of oil. Each byte is a note: bits 0 to 5 are the note number (see MusicNotes) and bits 6 and 7 are the length less one. The tune ends with FF.

B3DF DEFB $28,$2C,$2A,$28,$27,$2A,$28,$27
