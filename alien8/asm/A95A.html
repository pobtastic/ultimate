A95A: Handle Ending Robot Top
The update routine for the top half of the robot in the game completed scene (object 5D). The bottom half (object 5C) is the previous object, handled by HandleEndingRobotBottom. This copies its pixel y position, colour and flags, 10 pixels higher.
Input
IX The address of the object
Sit on top of the bottom half and colour the cells.
HandleEndingRobotTop A95A LD A,(IX-$05) Fetch the pixel y position of the previous object.
A95D ADD A,$10 Add 10.
A95F LD (IX+$1B),A Store it as the pixel y position.
A962 LD A,(IX-$10) Fetch the colour of the previous object.
A965 LD (IX+$10),A Use the same colour.
A968 LD A,(IX-$19) Fetch the flags of the previous object.
A96B LD (IX+$07),A Use the same flags.
A96E JP ColourCells Jump to ColourCells.
