AC6B: Count Statistics
Used by the routine at GameOver.
Goes through the data for all of the rooms and counts those whose first object is a cryonaut. If the room holds a placed valve (objects 64 to 67) it is counted in ChambersFoundActivated; otherwise it is counted in ChambersNotActivated and its cryonauts are added to CryonautsLost. All of the totals are BCD. They are shown when the game is over.
Clear the totals and start at the first room.
CountStatistics AC6B LD DE,$5B3C Point DE at CryonautsLost.
AC6E LD B,$04 Set a counter of four.
