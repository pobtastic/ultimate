5B36: Light Years Remaining
The four digits of the light years remaining counter. Each byte holds a digit in its upper four bits and in its lower three bits the stage of the rolling animation that is used when the digit changes (see DecrementLightYears). The game is over when all four digits are zero.
LightYearsRemaining 5B36 DEFB $00,$00 Upper two digits.
5B38 DEFB $00 Third digit.
LightYearsRemaining_LastDigit 5B39 DEFB $00 Last digit.
