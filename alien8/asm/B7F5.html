B7F5: Messaging: Reprogramming
The message that is shown while the thrusters are being reprogrammed, starting with its attribute. It is printed with PrintTextStandardFont, and the last character has bit 7 set. In this font the character "<" is a hyphen.
ReprogrammingMessage B7F5 DEFB $C6 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) (FLASH: ON).
B7F6 DEFM "RE<PROGRAMMIN","G"+$80 "RE<PROGRAMMING"
