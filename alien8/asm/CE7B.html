CE7B: Clear Screen Buffer
Used by the routines at GameOver, GameCompleted, GameMenu and BuildRoomObjects.
Clears the buffer that the playing area is drawn in before it is copied to the screen.
ClearScreenBuffer CE7B LD BC,$1800 Set BC to 1800.
CE7E LD HL,$D200 Point HL at ScreenBuffer.
CE81 LD E,$00 Set E to 00.
CE83 JR FillMemory_WithValue Jump to FillMemory_WithValue.
