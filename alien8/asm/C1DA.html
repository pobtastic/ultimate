C1DA: Turning Left Sprite Table
The sprite number and the flags of the player when it starts to turn left, picked by the direction that the player is facing (see GetFacingDirection). The sprite is one of objects 18 to 1B.
TurningLeftSpriteTable C1DA DEFB $1B,$40 Facing west: sprite 1B, flipped.
C1DC DEFB $1A,$00 Facing east: sprite 1A, not flipped.
C1DE DEFB $18,$00 Facing north: sprite 18, not flipped.
C1E0 DEFB $19,$00 Facing south: sprite 19, not flipped.
