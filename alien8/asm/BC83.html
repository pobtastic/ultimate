BC83: Handle Last Appearing Sparkle
The handler for the last of the sparkles in which the player appears (object 3F). The object stops moving and gets back the sprite number which was kept at offset 10 of its record, and the object is handled again straight away with that sprite number.
Input
IX The address of the object
Stop the sparkle and restore the object.
HandleLastAppearingSparkle BC83 CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Appearing Call SetPixelAdjustments_Appearing.
