D0B9: Draw Lines
Used by the routine at PrintSprite.
Draws the lines of a sprite one after the other. For each line the code jumps into the unrolled code, at a place that depends on the width of the sprite, and that is set by the jump that is changed by PrintSprite. The code for a sprite that is not on a byte boundary takes a pair of bytes off the stack, which are the mask and the data, shifts them across the two bytes of the buffer that they fall into with the table of shifted bytes in H, and keeps what is left of the second byte to go with the next pair. A sprite on a byte boundary jumps into DrawAlignedColumns instead.
Jump into the unrolled columns.
DrawLines D0B9 EX AF,AF' Copy A to A'.
D0BA LD A,(BC) Fetch the first byte of the buffer.
DrawLines_Jump D0BB JR DrawLines_Jump Jump into the unrolled columns.
The patched jump at DrawLines_Jump lands here when the sprite is five columns wide.
