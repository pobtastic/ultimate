C25E: Handle Walking
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Animates the legs of the player, with a footstep sound, while the player is walking, entering the room or jumping. The frame of the animation is in the lowest two bits of the sprite number. When the player stops walking the legs carry on until they reach frame 01, where the feet are together. The routine does nothing while the player is turning (objects 18 to 1B).
Input
C The controls
IX The address of the object
Return while turning.
