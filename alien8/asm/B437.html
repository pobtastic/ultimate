B437: Game Complete Tune
The tune played when the game has been completed. Each byte is a note: bits 0 to 5 are the note number (see MusicNotes) and bits 6 and 7 are the length less one. The tune ends with FF.
GameCompleteTune B437 DEFB $1B,$1D,$1E,$1B,$1D,$1E,$20,$1D
B43F DEFB $1E,$20,$22,$1E,$1D,$1E,$20,$1D
B447 DEFB $1B,$1D,$1E,$1B,$1A,$1B,$1D,$1A
B44F DEFB $9B,$FF
