C397: Leave Room To The West
Sends the player to the room to the west if it has gone past the west edge of the room. The x position is set to 00, which PlacePlayerAtDoor takes as the sign that the player has come out of the west and puts it at the east side of the new room.
Input
HL Distances to the walls along x and y
IX The address of the object
Leave if past the west edge.
