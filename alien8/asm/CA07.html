CA07: Lose Life
Used by the routine at StartLife.
Puts the player back as it was when it entered the room (see PlayerCopy), and takes a life off. If there are no lives left the game is over.
Restore the player and take a life.
LoseLife CA07 LD HL,$CA1D Point HL at PlayerCopy.
CA0A LD DE,$5B88 Point DE at PlayerBottomObject.
CA0D PUSH DE Copy DE to IX (using the stack).
CA0E POP IX
CA10 LD BC,$0040 Set BC to 0040.
CA13 LDIR Copy the player back.
CA15 LD HL,$5B1A Point HL at Lives.
See Infinite Lives.
CA18 DEC (HL) Decrement it.
CA19 JP M,GameOver Jump to GameOver if the sign flag is set.
CA1C RET Return.
