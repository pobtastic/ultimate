B63F: Play Sound From Value
Used by the routines at HandleEndingRobotBottom, PlaySoundFromXPosition, PlaySoundFromYPosition and PlaySoundFromPosition.
Plays six beeps of a tone whose pitch is worked out from the value in A. PlaySoundFromHeight uses the height, PlaySoundFromXPosition the x position, PlaySoundFromYPosition the y position and PlaySoundFromPosition the sum of the three coordinates.
Input
A Value that the pitch is made from
Turn the value into a pitch and play six beeps.

B645 LD C,$06 Set the number of beeps to 06.
