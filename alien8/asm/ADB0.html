ADB0: Print Counter Digit
Used by the routine at UpdateLightYears.
Prints one digit of the light years counter. The upper four bits of the byte hold the digit and the lower three bits are an animation step. The glyph is taken from the font that number of rows later than the start of the glyph, so that the digit scrolls up like an odometer as it changes.
Input
A Digit and animation step
HL Address in the screen buffer
Output
HL Address of the next character
Find the glyph and print it.
