BC70: Handle Player Appearing
The handler for the sparkles in which the player appears at the start of a life (objects 38 to 3E). On every other frame it moves on to the next of the animation frames and plays a sound.
Input
IX The address of the object
Advance the sparkle on every other frame.

BC76 CPL Invert it.

BC79 RET NZ Return on every other frame.
BC7A INC (IX+$00) Move on to the next sprite number.
BC7D CALL PlayAppearingSound Call PlayAppearingSound.
