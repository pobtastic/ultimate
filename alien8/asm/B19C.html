B19C: Handle Homing Sparkle
The handler for the sparkles which fly towards the player (objects 4C to 4F). They move at a speed of four along each axis and step through their four animation frames.
Input
IX The address of the object
Head towards the player and redraw.
HandleHomingSparkle B19C CALL SetPixelAdjustments_Sparkle Call SetPixelAdjustments_Sparkle.
B19F LD BC,$0404 Set the speed along each axis to 04.
B1A2 CALL SetSpeedTowardsPlayer Call SetSpeedTowardsPlayer.
B1A5 CALL ApplyGravityAndMove Call ApplyGravityAndMove.
B1A8 CALL NextSpriteNumber Call NextSpriteNumber.
B1AB JP PlaySoundAndRedraw Jump to PlaySoundAndRedraw.
