C296: Move Player
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Moves the player by one frame. The speeds along x and y are only set up while the player walks, is jumping or is entering the room, and they are cleared again afterwards. The speed along z has gravity taken off it, which is more if the player is not rising with the jump control held down. If the player lands the jump is over.
Input
C The controls
IX The address of the object
