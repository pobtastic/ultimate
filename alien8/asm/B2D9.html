B2D9: Save Position
Used by the routine at HandleFatBottomBlock.
Saves the position of the object in SavedPosition.
Input

Copy x, y and z to SavedPosition.
SavePosition B2D9 LD HL,$5B1B Point HL at SavedPosition.

B2E1 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the y position.
B2E4 LD (HL),A Store it.

B2E9 LD (HL),A Store it.
