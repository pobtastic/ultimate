A631: Start Game
Used by the routine at GameEntry.
Clears every game variable from RandomSeed1 up to UnusedAfterObjects, keeps the low byte of FRAMES as RandomSeed1, checks that the loader started the game, and then carries on into InitialiseGame.
Clear the variables and keep the seed.
