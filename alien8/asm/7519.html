7519: Block Types
The addresses of the templates for the types of block that make up the walls, doors and other fixtures of a room, indexed by the type (see RoomData).
BlockTypes 7519 DEFW BlockTemplates Type 00: a door in the north wall (BlockTemplates).
751B DEFW BlockTemplate_DoorEast Type 01: a door in the east wall (BlockTemplate_DoorEast).
751D DEFW BlockTemplate_DoorSouth Type 02: a door in the south wall (BlockTemplate_DoorSouth).
751F DEFW BlockTemplate_DoorWest Type 03: a door in the west wall (BlockTemplate_DoorWest).
7521 DEFW BlockTemplate_FullWallWest Type 04: a full wall along the west side (BlockTemplate_FullWallWest).
7523 DEFW BlockTemplate_FullWallNorth Type 05: a full wall along the north side (BlockTemplate_FullWallNorth).
7525 DEFW BlockTemplate_WallWestWithOpening Type 06: a wall along the west side with an opening (BlockTemplate_WallWestWithOpening).
7527 DEFW BlockTemplate_WallNorthWithOpening Type 07: a wall along the north side with an opening (BlockTemplate_WallNorthWithOpening).
7529 DEFW BlockTemplate_LongWallWest Type 08: a long wall along the west side of a narrow room (BlockTemplate_LongWallWest).
752B DEFW BlockTemplate_LongWallNorth Type 09: a long wall along the north side of a narrow room (BlockTemplate_LongWallNorth).
752D DEFW BlockTemplate_TallDoorEast Type 0A: a tall door in the east wall (BlockTemplate_TallDoorEast).
752F DEFW BlockTemplate_TallDoorSouth Type 0B: a tall door in the south wall (BlockTemplate_TallDoorSouth).
7531 DEFW BlockTemplate_TallDoorWithPlatformEast Type 0C: a tall door with a platform in the east wall (BlockTemplate_TallDoorWithPlatformEast).
7533 DEFW BlockTemplate_TallDoorWithPlatformSouth Type 0D: a tall door with a platform in the south wall (BlockTemplate_TallDoorWithPlatformSouth).
