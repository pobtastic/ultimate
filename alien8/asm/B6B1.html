B6B1: Play Low Beep
Used by the routines at HandleAlarm, GameMenu, HandlePickupAndDrop and HandleExtraLife.
Plays a low beep, which is 10 beeps at a pitch of 80.
Set the pitch and the beep count.
PlayLowBeep B6B1 LD BC,$8010 Set the pitch to 80 and the number of beeps to 10.
B6B4 JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence.
