B4B9: Draw Panel
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and WinRound.
Draws the panel on the right of the screen, which holds the score, the hi-score and the sector map. It is 08 characters wide and 0F characters tall, and is built from the characters at PanelCharacters using the graphics at PanelGraphics, and coloured using the attributes at PanelAttributes.
