961A: Control Method
The method of control that has been chosen in the menu, which is in bits 1 and 2: 00 is the keyboard, 01 is the Kempston joystick, 02 is the cursor keys and 03 is Sinclair interface 2.
ControlMethod 961A DEFB $00
