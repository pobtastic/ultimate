B202: Clear Message Area
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Clears the pixels of the message area at the bottom of the screen: 18 bytes wide and 30 pixel rows tall, starting at row 12.
ClearMessageArea B202 LD HL,$5040 Point HL at the first pixel row of row 12.
B205 LD BC,$1830 The area is 18 bytes wide (B) and 30 pixel rows tall (C).
B208 JR ClearPixelBlock Clear it using ClearPixelBlock.
