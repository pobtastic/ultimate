961B: Scores
The scores of the two players, which are each three bytes of binary coded decimal with the most significant byte first.
Scores 961B DEFB $00,$00 Player 1, the first two bytes.
Scores_Player1_LastByte 961D DEFB $00 Player 1, the last byte.
Scores_Player2 961E DEFB $00,$00,$00 Player 2.
