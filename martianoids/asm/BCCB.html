BCCB: Battery Meter Data
The title of the battery meter. It starts with an attribute (bright yellow).
The box for the battery meter. It starts with an attribute (bright yellow on black) and ends with a character that has bit 7 set. The characters are each two rows tall.
Message_Battery BCCB DEFB $46 Attribute.
BCCC DEFM "BATTER","Y"+$80 "BATTERY".
BatteryMeterBox BCD3 DEFB $46 Attribute.
BCD4 DEFB $00 The left end.
BCD5 DEFB $01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01,$01 The middle.
BCE2 DEFB $82 The right end.
