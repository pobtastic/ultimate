C0CF: Play Music
Used by the routines at LoseGame, WinRound, EndRound and StartGameSequence.
Plays a tune, which cannot be stopped. A tune is a list of notes (see PlayNote) which ends with FF.
The tunes played by this routine are:
Tune Sound
Start Tune
Level Complete Tune
Game Over Tune
Input
DE Address of the tune
Play each note until FF.
PlayMusic C0CF LD A,(DE) Fetch the next note.
C0D0 CP $FF Jump to PlayMusic_Return if it is FF.
C0D2 JR Z,PlayMusic_Return
C0D4 CALL PlayNote Play the note using PlayNote.
C0D7 JR PlayMusic Loop back for the next note.
This entry point is used by the routine at PlayMusic_WaitKey.
PlayMusic_Return C0D9 RET Return.
