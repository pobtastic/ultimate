B5EB: Draw Border
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and GameOver.
Draws the decorative frame that surrounds the play window and the screen text: two rings of beads, one inside the other, each made of two characters that alternate. The frame is 18 characters wide and 18 characters tall, and the attributes are bright blue.
