B1C2: Fill Play Window Attributes
Used by the routines at StartRound, ClearPlayWindowWithAttributes and ShowGameOver.
Fills the attributes of the play window, which is 14 characters wide and 0E characters tall and starts at column 02 of row 02, with a single attribute.
Input
A Attribute
FillPlayWindowAttributes B1C2 LD HL,$5842 Point HL at the top left attribute of the play window.
B1C5 LD DE,$000C There are 0C attributes between the end of one window row and the start of the next.
B1C8 LD BC,$140E The window is 14 columns wide (B) and 0E rows tall (C).
This entry point is used by the routines at FillLargeWindowAttributes and FillMessageAttributes.
FillAttributes_Row B1CB PUSH BC Save the dimensions.
FillAttributes_Column B1CC LD (HL),A Write the attribute to the current cell.
B1CD INC L Move on to the next column.
B1CE DJNZ FillAttributes_Column Loop back until the row is complete.
B1D0 ADD HL,DE Move on to the start of the next row.
B1D1 POP BC Restore the dimensions.
B1D2 DEC C Decrease the row counter by one.
B1D3 JR NZ,FillAttributes_Row Loop back until every row has been filled.
B1D5 RET Return.
