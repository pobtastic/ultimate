BB73: Set Flashing Attribute
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Makes the line of the menu that is selected flash, by setting bit 7 of its attribute. The other lines have it cleared.
Input
A Number of the line to highlight
B Number of lines
HL Address of the attributes of the lines
SetFlashingAttribute BB73 AND A Jump to make the line flash if it is the first one.
BB74 JR NZ,HighlightMenuItem_Steady
HighlightMenuItem_Flash BB76 SET 7,(HL) Make the line flash...
BB78 JR HighlightMenuItem_NextLine ...and jump to move on to the next line.
HighlightMenuItem_Next BB7A DEC A Count down the lines.
BB7B JR Z,HighlightMenuItem_Flash Jump to make the line flash if it is the one that is selected.
HighlightMenuItem_Steady BB7D RES 7,(HL) Make the line steady.
HighlightMenuItem_NextLine BB7F INC HL Move on to the next attribute.
BB80 DJNZ HighlightMenuItem_Next Loop back until all of the lines have been done.
BB82 RET Return.
