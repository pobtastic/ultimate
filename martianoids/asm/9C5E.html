9C5E: Find Sector
Used by the routines at SendProgramPacket, MoveSpark, MoveDestroyer, FindSectorEnergy, MoveProgramPacket and UseActionKey_Up.
Finds the sector that an entity is in. The nine sectors are numbered from 00 for the top left to 08 for the bottom right.
Input
IX Entity
Output
D Sector number
FindSector 9C5E LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the X cell of the entity.
9C61 AND $30 Keep the sector column, which is a multiple of 10...
9C63 LD E,A ...in E.
9C64 LD A,(IX+$04) Fetch the Y cell of the entity.
9C67 AND $30 Keep the sector row...
9C69 XOR $30 ...and turn it round, as the first row of sectors is at the bottom of the map...
9C6B SUB $10 ...and make it a multiple of 10 from 00 to 20, from the top.
9C6D LD D,A Keep the sector row.
9C6E ADD A,E Add the column.
9C6F RRCA Halve it...
9C70 ADD A,D ...and add the row, so that the result is three times the row plus the column, multiplied by 08.
9C71 RRCA Shift down by three...
9C72 RRCA
9C73 RRCA
9C74 LD D,A ...to give the sector number.
9C75 RET Return.
