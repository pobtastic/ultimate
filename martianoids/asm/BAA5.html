BAA5: Messaging: Initials
The attributes, coordinates and text of the message that asks for the initials. The three characters of the third line, after "TODAYS", are replaced with the name of the place.
InitialsMessage_Attributes BAA5 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAA6 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAA7 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAA8 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAA9 DEFB $43 INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAAA DEFB $43 INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAAB DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
InitialsMessage_Coordinates BAAC DEFB $20,$38 Coordinates of the seven lines of text.
BAAE DEFB $18,$48
BAB0 DEFB $18,$58
BAB2 DEFB $48,$68
BAB4 DEFB $20,$78
BAB6 DEFB $28,$88
BAB8 DEFB $48,$98
InitialsMessage_Text BABA DEFM "CONGRATULATION","S"+$80 "CONGRATULATIONS".
BAC9 DEFM "YOU HAVE ACHIEVE","D"+$80 "YOU HAVE ACHIEVED".
BADA DEFM "TODAYS " "TODAYS" and a space.
InitialsMessage_PlaceName BAE1 DEFM " HIGHES","T"+$80 The name of the place is written over the spaces, then "HIGHEST".
BAEC DEFM "SCOR","E"+$80 "SCORE".
BAF1 DEFM "PLEASE REGISTE","R"+$80 "PLEASE REGISTER".
BB00 DEFM "YOUR INITIAL","S"+$80 "YOUR INITIALS".
BB0D DEFM "- - ","-"+$80 "- - -".
