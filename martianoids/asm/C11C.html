C11C: Play Rest
Used by the routine at PlayNote.
Waits for the length of a rest, which is one to four times as long as the shortest. It is used by PlayNote.
Input
DE Address of the note
Output
DE Address of the next note
PlayRest C11C LD A,(DE) Fetch the note and move on to the next one.
C11D INC DE
C11E RLCA Rotate the bits of the length to the bottom.
C11F RLCA
C120 AND $03 Keep them...
C122 INC A ...and add one to give the length.
C123 LD L,A Keep the length.
C124 LD BC,$430B Set the time of the shortest rest.
PlayRest_Loop C127 PUSH BC Save it.
PlayRest_Wait C128 DEC BC Count it down to zero.
C129 LD A,B
C12A OR C
C12B JR NZ,PlayRest_Wait
C12D POP BC Restore it.
C12E DEC L Loop back until the length has been counted down.
C12F JR NZ,PlayRest_Loop
C131 RET Return.
