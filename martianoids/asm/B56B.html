B56B: Print Hi-Score
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, WinRound and NewHighScore_Enter.
Prints the hi-score in big digits, in the panel on the right of the screen. The hi-score is the first entry in the table of high scores at HallOfFameTable.
Before After
PrintHiScore B56B LD HL,$40D9 Point HL at the display file position of the hi-score.
B56E LD DE,$B7C6 Point DE at HallOfFameTable.
B571 JR PrintScore_Digits Print it using PrintScore_Digits.
