C062: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayNoiseSound and PlayWaveSequence.
Makes a single cycle of a tone, by switching the speaker on and then off again. The length of each half of the cycle is set by the pitch. The border is set to black.
Input
B Pitch
PlaySquareWave C062 LD A,$10 Switch the speaker on.
C064 OUT ($FE),A
C066 LD A,B Keep the pitch.
PlaySquareWave_FirstWait C067 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_FirstWait Wait for a time that depends on the pitch.
C069 LD B,A Restore the pitch.
C06A XOR A Switch the speaker off.
C06B OUT ($FE),A
C06D LD A,B Keep the pitch.
PlaySquareWave_SecondWait C06E DJNZ PlaySquareWave_SecondWait Wait for the same time.
C070 LD B,A Restore the pitch.
C071 RET Return.
