BB12: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and GameMenu_Unused.
Shows the title and the menu, and waits for the player to choose a method of control with the keys 01 to 04, or to start the game with the key 00. The tune is played the first time through. The line of the menu for the method that has been chosen flashes. If nothing is done for a while then the hall of fame is shown.
