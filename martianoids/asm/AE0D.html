AE0D: Colour Sector Square
Used by the routine at DrawSectorMap.
Sets the attributes of a block of two by two characters.
Input
A Attribute
BC Offset from 5800
ColourSectorSquare AE0D LD HL,$5800 Point HL at the attribute file...
AE10 ADD HL,BC ...and add the offset.
AE11 LD (HL),A Colour the top left character...
AE12 INC L ...move to the right...
AE13 LD (HL),A ...and colour the top right character.
AE14 LD DE,$001F Use an offset of 1F...
AE17 ADD HL,DE ...to move to the character on the left of the next row.
AE18 LD (HL),A Colour the bottom left character...
AE19 INC L ...move to the right...
AE1A LD (HL),A ...and colour the bottom right character.
AE1B RET Return.
