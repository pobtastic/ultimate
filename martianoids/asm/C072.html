C072: Make Explosion Sound
Used by the routine at PrintMessage.
Makes a burst of noise, made of four short tones with pitches that come from bytes of the ROM picked at random.
MakeExplosionSound C072 LD A,R Pick a random page of the ROM...
C074 AND $1F
C076 LD H,A
C077 LD A,($9617) ...and a random place in it.
C07A LD L,A
C07B LD E,$04 There are four tones.
MakeExplosionSound_Loop C07D LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the ROM, and move on to the next one.
C07E INC HL
C07F AND $7F Keep the lower seven bits of it.
C081 OR $8F Make sure that the pitch is at least 8F.
C083 LD B,A Use it as the pitch.
C084 LD C,$01 Each tone is made of one cycle.
C086 CALL PlayWaveSequence Make the tone using PlayWaveSequence.
C089 DEC E Loop back until all four tones have been made.
C08A JR NZ,MakeExplosionSound_Loop
C08C RET Return.
