BA19: Enter Initials
Used by the routine at NewHighScore_Enter.
Asks the player for three initials. The turning controls change the letter, which cycles through the space and the letters A to Z, and the fire control for the first weapon accepts it.
Input
DE Address of the name
EnterInitials BA19 LD B,$03 There are three letters.
BA1B XOR A Clear the controls.
BA1C LD ($9625),A
BA1F LD HL,$5069 Point HL at the screen address of the first letter.

BA2F JR NZ,EnterInitials_Fire
