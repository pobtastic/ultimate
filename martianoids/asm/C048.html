C048: Make Bump Sound
Used by the routines at CheckPause, MoveThief, MoveHunter, MoveDome and HandleObstacle.
Makes a short, low tone.
MakeBumpSound C048 LD BC,$4030 Set the pitch to 40 and the number of cycles to 30.
C04B JP PlayWaveSequence Make the tone using PlayWaveSequence.
