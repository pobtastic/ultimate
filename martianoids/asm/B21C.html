B21C: Draw Play Window
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Copies the off-screen play window buffer to the display, and at the same time resets the buffer to the dithered grey background of the play window (alternate lines of 55 and AA). The buffer occupies F580 to FFFF: 70 lines of 18 bytes. Only the middle 14 bytes of each line are shown on screen, as the 02 bytes at each end are margins that let sprites overhang the edges of the window. The lines are copied from the bottom of the window to the top, working backwards through the buffer. The stack pointer is pointed at the display and each pair of bytes taken from the buffer is written using a PUSH instruction, which is much faster than storing the bytes one at a time.
Before After

This entry point is used by the routine at InitialiseGame.
Entered from 98E3 with DE pointing at the ROM, to fill the buffer with the dithered background without drawing anything. DE holds the screen address just after the bottom right byte of the window.
