BE5F: Add Map Cells
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Adds the cells of the map that can be seen to the end of the draw list. They are the cells in a diamond shape around the robot: twelve rows along the map, which run from six rows further on than the robot to five rows behind it. The rows have 01, 03, 05, 07, 09, 0B, 0D, 0B, 09, 07, 05 and 03 cells in them. Each empty cell is skipped. The position of each of the others on the screen is found from its place in the diamond, the position of the robot inside its own cell and the offsets in the template for the type of the cell, which are the same projection as AddEntity uses. Cells that are outside of the play window are not added.
