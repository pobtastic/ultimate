C01A: Make Random Beep
Used by the routine at MaterialiseEntity.
Makes a short tone of random pitch.
MakeRandomBeep C01A LD A,($9617) Fetch the random number.
C01D AND $3F Keep it between 20 and 3F...
C01F OR $20
C021 LD B,A ...to use as the pitch.
C022 LD C,$0C The tone is made of 0C cycles.
C024 JP PlayWaveSequence Make the tone using PlayWaveSequence.
