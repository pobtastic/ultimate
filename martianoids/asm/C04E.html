C04E: Play Noise Sound
Used by the routine at FireWeaponB.
Makes the sound of a shot. Plays 28 tones, each with a pitch worked out from a counter that counts down, so the sound falls in pitch.
Play 28 pulses of different lengths.
PlayNoiseSound C04E LD C,$28 Use 28 cycles.
This entry point is used by the routine at EnterInitials.
This entry point takes the number of pulses in C.
Play the next pulse.
PlayNoiseSound_Counter C050 LD A,C Fetch the number of the pulse.
C051 XOR %00111111 Change it.
C053 LD B,A Use it as the length of the pulse.
C054 CALL PlaySquareWave Make a single cycle using PlaySquareWave.
Loop back until all of the cycles have been made.
C057 DEC C Count down the pulses.
C058 JR NZ,PlayNoiseSound_Counter Loop back until all of the pulses have been played.
C05A RET Return.
