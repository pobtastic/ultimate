B585: Print Score
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, WinRound and StartGame.
Prints the score of the player in big digits, in the panel on the right of the screen.
Before After
PrintScore B585 LD HL,$4059 Point HL at the display file position of the score.
B588 LD DE,$961B Point DE at Scores.
This entry point is used by the routine at PrintHiScore.
PrintScore_Digits B58B LD B,$03 There are 03 bytes of the number to print, which makes six digits.
B58D JR PrintBigNumber Print the number using PrintBigNumber.
