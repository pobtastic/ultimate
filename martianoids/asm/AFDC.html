AFDC: Move Light Right
Moves a light one cell to the right. See MoveLightLeft.
Input
DE Address of the attribute that the light is in
IX Light
MoveLightRight AFDC LD L,E Point HL at the cell on the right.
AFDD LD H,D
AFDE INC L
AFDF JR MoveLightLeft_Move Jump to move the light.
