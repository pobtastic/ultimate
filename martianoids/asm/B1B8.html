B1B8: Reset Attributes
Used by the routine at ClearScreen.
Sets all 0300 attribute bytes to 47 (bright white ink on black paper).
ResetAttributes B1B8 LD E,$47 Use the attribute 47.
B1BA LD HL,$5800 Point HL at the start of the attribute file.
B1BD LD BC,$0300 There are 0300 attributes.
