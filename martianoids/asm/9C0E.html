9C0E: Sector Offsets
The offset into the map of the first cell of each sector. Sector 00 is the top left, sector 01 is the one to the right of it, and sector 08 is the bottom right. As the map starts with the bottom row of sectors, the offsets of the top row of sectors are the largest.
