9EB9: Battery Bar Data
The pattern of the pixels of a column of the battery bar, one byte per pixel row, followed by the number of rows that are skipped at the top and bottom of each of the first five columns, so that the bar has rounded ends.
BatteryBarPattern 9EB9 DEFB $80,$40,$40,$20,$20,$40,$40,$80 The pattern of a column.
BatteryBarRoundedEnd 9EC1 DEFB $03,$03,$03,$01,$01 The number of rows to skip for each of the first five columns.
