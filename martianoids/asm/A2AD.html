A2AD: Empty Cell
Used by the routines at MoveThief and MoveHunter.
Sets the current type of the cell that last blocked an entity to zero, leaving its initial type as it was.
EmptyCell A2AD LD HL,($962E) Fetch the address of the cell.
A2B0 LD A,(HL) Fetch its contents.
A2B1 AND $F0 Keep the initial type.
A2B3 LD (HL),A Store it.
A2B4 RET Return.
