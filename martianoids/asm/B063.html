B063: Next Sprite Number
Used by the routines at MoveSpark, MoveThief, MoveDestroyer, MoveSaucer, MoveDome and MoveRobotHorizontally.
Adds one to the lowest two bits of an entity's type, so a four-frame sprite moves on to the next frame. This is the handler for entity types 70 to 73, the activated terminal.
Input
IX Entity
NextSpriteNumber B063 PUSH BC Save BC.
B064 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the type of the entity...
B067 LD C,A ...and keep it in C.
B068 INC A Add one...
B069 AND $03 ...keep the lowest two bits, and put them in B.
B06B LD B,A
B06C LD A,C Fetch the type again...
B06D AND $FC ...replace the lowest two bits with the new ones.
B06F OR B
B070 LD (IX+$00),A Store the type of the entity.
B073 POP BC Restore BC.
B074 RET Return.
