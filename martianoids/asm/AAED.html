AAED: Turn Robot Right
Used by the routine at MoveRobot.
Turns the robot a quarter of a turn, so that its direction goes up by one. The robot is moved to the start of the cell, and it gets one of the types for turning (20 to 2F).
Input
A Controls
IX Robot
TurnRobotRight AAED RES 0,A Clear the bit of the controls for turning right...
AAEF LD ($9625),A ...and store them.
AAF2 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the type of the robot.
AAF5 ADD A,$04 Go forward by one direction...
AAF7 AND $0F
AAF9 OR $22 ...keep the bits that are used for the sprite and mark it as turning.
AAFB JR TurnRobot_Store Jump to store the type and finish.
