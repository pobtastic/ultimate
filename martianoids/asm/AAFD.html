AAFD: Move Robot Vertically
The handler for entity types 14 to 17 and 1C to 1F, which are the robot when it is heading in direction one or three (up and down the map).
Input
IX Robot
MoveRobotVertically AAFD RES 7,(IX+$00) Show the sprite without flipping it.
AB01 JR UpdateRobot Jump to move the robot.
