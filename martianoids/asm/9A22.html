9A22: Lose Game
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Shows the message that is used when every sector has been destroyed, plays the defeat tune and then ends the game.

9A2A EXX Switch to the shadow registers, as PrintTextList expects the attributes there.

9A2E LD DE,$9B32 Point DE at DefeatMessage_Text.
9A31 LD B,$04 There are four lines to print.

9A3F LD B,$00 ...using B as the second counter.

9A42 LD A,L Loop back until the high byte has counted down to zero.
9A43 OR H
9A44 JR NZ,LoseGame_Delay
