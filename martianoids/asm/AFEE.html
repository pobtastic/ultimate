AFEE: Move Light Down
Moves a light one cell down. See MoveLightLeft.
Input
DE Address of the attribute that the light is in
IX Light
MoveLightDown AFEE LD L,E Point HL at the cell that the light is in.
AFEF LD H,D
AFF0 LD BC,$0020 Use a row of 20 characters...
AFF3 ADD HL,BC ...and move down to the cell below.
AFF4 JR MoveLight_Test Jump to test the cell.
