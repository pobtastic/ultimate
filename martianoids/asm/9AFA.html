9AFA: Messaging: Round Result Text
The attributes, coordinates and text that are used by the screens shown at the end of a round. The attributes and coordinates are passed to PrintTextList, which takes the coordinates as pairs of an X position and a Y position in pixels. Every string has bit 7 of its last character set.
RoundResult_Attributes 9AFA DEFB $44 Attributes: INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) and INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
9AFB DEFB $43

9AFE DEFB $18,$38
