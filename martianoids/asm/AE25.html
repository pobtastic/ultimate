AE25: Sector State Attributes
The attributes that are used to show a sector, indexed by its state. The states with bit 2 set are flashing, which shows that the sector is under attack.
SectorStateAttributes AE25 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
AE26 DEFB $56 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:RED (BRIGHT).
AE27 DEFB $78 INK:BLACK, PAPER:WHITE (BRIGHT).
AE28 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
AE29 DEFB $D6 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:RED (BRIGHT) (FLASH: ON).
AE2A DEFB $D6 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:RED (BRIGHT) (FLASH: ON).
AE2B DEFB $D6 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:RED (BRIGHT) (FLASH: ON).
AE2C DEFB $D6 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:RED (BRIGHT) (FLASH: ON).
