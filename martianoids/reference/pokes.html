Infinite Lives
POKE A9AC,00
POKE B6C9,00
Removes the two instructions which would otherwise decrease Lives.
A9A9 LD HL,968B
A9AC DEC (HL)
A9AD XOR A
B6C6 LD HL,968B
B6C9 DEC (HL)
B6CA CALL A091
To:
A9A9 LD HL,968B
A9AC NOP
A9AD XOR A
B6C6 LD HL,968B
B6C9 NOP
B6CA CALL A091
Infinite Energy

POKE A6C4,00

A6C3 SBC HL,DE
A6C5 JR C,A6CB
To:
A6C2 AND A
A6C3 NOP
