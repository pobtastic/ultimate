47448: New High Score
Used by the routine at GameOver.
Puts the scores of the two players into the hall of fame, if they are high enough, starting with the higher of the two. If a score gets a place then the player is asked for their initials.
NewHighScore 47448 LD HL,38427 Point HL at the score of player 1.
47451 LD DE,38430 Point DE at Scores_Player2.
47454 CALL NewHighScore_Compare Compare the scores using NewHighScore_Compare.
47457 LD A,0 Clear the flag that says that a name has been entered.
47459 LD (38451),A
47462 JR Z,NewHighScore_Process Jump to insert player 1 first if the scores are the same...
47464 JR C,NewHighScore_Process ...or if player 1 has the higher score.
47466 CPL Otherwise swap the scores, so that the higher one is first.
47467 EX DE,HL Swap the two scores.
NewHighScore_Process 47468 CALL NewHighScore_Enter Insert the first score using NewHighScore_Enter.
47471 EX DE,HL Swap the scores back, and insert the other one.
