40795: Entity Handler Table
The routine that runs each frame for an entity of each type. The type is the first byte of the entity, and bit 7 (which flips the sprite) is ignored. The numbers are also the numbers of the sprites that are drawn for the entity. The handlers are shown in the order they appear in the table.
