38427: Scores
The scores of the two players, which are each three bytes of binary coded decimal with the most significant byte first.
Scores 38427 DEFB 0,0 Player 1, the first two bytes.
Scores_Player1_LastByte 38429 DEFB 0 Player 1, the last byte.
Scores_Player2 38430 DEFB 0,0,0 Player 2.
