44423: Clear Icon Area
Used by the routine at QueueProgram.
Clears an area of the screen that is four characters wide and thirty-two lines high, working upwards from the address that is given.
Input
HL Screen address of the bottom left
ClearIconArea 44423 PUSH BC Save the registers.
44424 PUSH DE
44425 PUSH HL
44426 LD C,32 There are 32 lines to clear.
ClearIconArea_Line 44428 LD B,4 There are four bytes in a line.
44430 XOR A Clear a byte...
ClearIconArea_Byte 44431 LD (HL),A ...on the screen.
44432 INC L Move on to the next byte.
44433 DJNZ ClearIconArea_Byte Loop back until the line has been cleared.
44435 DEC L Go back to the start of the line.
44436 DEC L
44437 DEC L
44438 DEC L
44439 LD A,H Fetch the high byte of the address...
44440 DEC H ...and move up one line.
44441 AND 7 Jump if that has not moved into the character above.
44443 JR NZ,ClearIconArea_Next
44445 LD A,H Otherwise fetch the high byte again, as it has moved up a row of characters...
44446 ADD A,8 ...add eight to it...
44448 LD H,A ...and store it.
44449 LD A,L Fetch the low byte...
44450 SUB 32 ...take one row of characters off it...
44452 LD L,A ...and store it.
ClearIconArea_Next 44453 DEC C Loop back until all of the lines have been cleared.
44454 JR NZ,ClearIconArea_Line
