42762: Animate Smoke
The handler for entity types 56 to 58, which are the sprites of smoke. It moves on to the next sprite, makes a sound and draws the smoke. The last sprite of the smoke is handled by EndSmoke.
Input
IX Entity
AnimateSmoke 42762 INC (IX+0) Move on to the next sprite.
42765 CALL MakeNoiseIfVisible Make the sound of an explosion using MakeNoiseIfVisible.
42768 JP AddEntity Draw the smoke using AddEntity.
