45146: Toggle Sprite
Used by the routines at MoveHunter and MovePellet.
Flips the lowest bit of an entity's type, which switches it between two sprites. This is the handler for entity types 2 to 7. Types 6 and 7 are the battery and share one picture. Nothing sets types 2 to 5.
Input
IX Entity
ToggleSprite 45146 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the type of the entity...
45149 XOR 1 ...flip the lowest bit...
45151 LD (IX+0),A ...and store it.
45154 RET Return.
