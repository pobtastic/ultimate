48042: Draw Game Screen
Used by the routine at WinRound.
Resets the map and draws the parts of the game screen that are not the play window: the rows of beads above the message area, the message area itself with its rounded box, and the box for the battery meter with its title. It is used when a game is started, and when a round has been completed.
