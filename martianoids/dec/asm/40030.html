40030: Find Sector
Used by the routines at SendProgramPacket, MoveSpark, MoveDestroyer, FindSectorEnergy, MoveProgramPacket and UseActionKey_Up.
Finds the sector that an entity is in. The nine sectors are numbered from 0 for the top left to 8 for the bottom right.
Input
IX Entity
Output
D Sector number
FindSector 40030 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the X cell of the entity.
40033 AND 48 Keep the sector column, which is a multiple of 16...
40035 LD E,A ...in E.
40036 LD A,(IX+4) Fetch the Y cell of the entity.
40039 AND 48 Keep the sector row...
40041 XOR 48 ...and turn it round, as the first row of sectors is at the bottom of the map...
40043 SUB 16 ...and make it a multiple of 16 from 0 to 32, from the top.
40045 LD D,A Keep the sector row.
40046 ADD A,E Add the column.
40047 RRCA Halve it...
40048 ADD A,D ...and add the row, so that the result is three times the row plus the column, multiplied by 8.
40049 RRCA Shift down by three...
40050 RRCA
40051 RRCA
40052 LD D,A ...to give the sector number.
40053 RET Return.
