45537: Fill Message Attributes
Used by the routine at DrawGameScreen.
Fills the attributes of the message area at the bottom of the screen, which is 24 characters wide and 6 characters tall and starts at column 0 of row 18, with a single attribute.
Input
A Attribute
FillMessageAttributes 45537 LD HL,23104 Point HL at the first attribute of row 18.
45540 LD DE,8 There are 8 attributes between the end of one row and the start of the next.
45543 LD BC,6150 The area is 24 columns wide (B) and 6 rows tall (C).
