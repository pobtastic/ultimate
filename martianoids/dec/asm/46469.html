46469: Print Score
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame, WinRound and StartGame.
Prints the score of the player in big digits, in the panel on the right of the screen.
Before After

46472 LD DE,38427 Point DE at Scores.
This entry point is used by the routine at PrintHiScore.
