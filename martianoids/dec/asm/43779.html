43779: Move Robot Horizontally
The handler for entity types 16 to 19 and 24 to 27, which are the robot when it is heading in direction zero or two (across the map).
Input
IX Robot
MoveRobotHorizontally 43779 SET 7,(IX+0) Show the sprite flipped.
This entry point is used by the routine at MoveRobotVertically.
This entry point is shared by both handlers. It does all of the work for the robot in a frame: it looks at the action key, shows the robot on the map, works out how far to move, moves the robot, checks whether it has hit an enemy and fires the weapons.
