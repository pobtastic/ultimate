45020: Move Light Right
Moves a light one cell to the right. See MoveLightLeft.
Input
DE Address of the attribute that the light is in
IX Light
MoveLightRight 45020 LD L,E Point HL at the cell on the right.
45021 LD H,D
45022 INC L
45023 JR MoveLightLeft_Move Jump to move the light.
