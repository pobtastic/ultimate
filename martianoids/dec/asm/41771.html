41771: Reduce Sector Energy
Used by the routines at MoveSpark and MoveDestroyer.
Takes one unit of energy off the sector that an entity is in. If the energy is down to five or fewer units then the player is told, and when it reaches zero the sector has been destroyed.
Input
IX Entity
