39886: Count Sector Programs
Used by the routine at StartRound.
Counts the installed programs (map cells with both their current and initial type equal to 2) in each of the nine sectors. The result is the amount of energy that each sector starts with.
CountSectorPrograms 39886 LD B,9 There are nine sectors, which are counted from the last to the first.
