42358: Check Enemy Overlap
Used by the routines at MoveThief, MoveHunter, SpawnEnemies and MoveDome.
Checks whether the entity overlaps any of the ten entities that are enemies (entities 4 to 13), apart from itself.
Input
IX Entity
DE X position
HL Y position
Output
IY The entity that has been hit
F The carry flag is set if there is one
