42204: Animate Program
The handler for entity types 116 to 119, which are the sprites of an installed program in the map. The stripe moves on to the next frame. sprites in turn.
Input
IX Entity
AnimateProgram 42204 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the type of the entity.
42207 LD E,A Keep the type.
42208 DEC A Take one off and limit it to the four sprites...
42209 AND 3
42211 LD D,A ...and keep that in D.
42212 LD A,E Fetch the type again.
42213 AND 252 Replace the sprite with the new one...
42215 OR D
42216 LD (IX+0),A ...and store the type.
42219 RET Return.
