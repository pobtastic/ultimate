49114: Play Sound Effect Step
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Called once a frame. If a sound effect is playing, one tone of it is made and the counter is counted down. An effect is started by storing a length and an effect number at SoundEffect.
Effect Tones Started when
0 4 The action key is used (44182).
1 5 A program packet is sent (40517).
2 7 A sector is activated (42048).
3 4 A program is destroyed (41972).
The effect number is stored and never loaded. GetTableEntry takes its index from A', which is whatever the frame left there. The lists at SoundEffectTable are used only when A' is 0, 1, 2 or 3. At the start of play A' is 80, so the first tone is read from outside those lists. The samples are the four effects as they sound then, one tone a frame. Effects 0 and 3 both ask for four tones, so they sound the same.
