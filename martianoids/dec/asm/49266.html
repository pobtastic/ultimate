49266: Make Explosion Sound
Used by the routine at PrintMessage.
Makes a burst of noise, made of four short tones with pitches that come from bytes of the ROM picked at random.

49275 LD E,4 There are four tones.
MakeExplosionSound_Loop 49277 LD A,(HL) Fetch a byte of the ROM, and move on to the next one.
49278 INC HL
49279 AND 127 Keep the lower seven bits of it.

49283 LD B,A Use it as the pitch.

49289 DEC E Loop back until all four tones have been made.
