49436: Play Rest
Used by the routine at PlayNote.
Waits for the length of a rest, which is one to four times as long as the shortest. It is used by PlayNote.
Input
DE The address of the rest
Output
DE The address after the rest
Work out the length of the rest in beats.

49437 INC DE Move on to the next note.

Wait for each beat.

49450 OR C
