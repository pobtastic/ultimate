48434: Delay
Used by the routines at ShowGameOver, EnterInitials and NewHighScore_Return.
Waits for a while. The time is about B times the number of times that HL can be counted down to zero, which is 65536 if it starts as zero.
Input
B Number of times to repeat the wait
HL Length of each wait
Delay 48434 DEC HL Count down HL until it reaches zero.
48435 LD A,L
48436 OR H
48437 JR NZ,Delay
48439 DJNZ Delay Loop back until the wait has been repeated B times.
48441 RET Return.
