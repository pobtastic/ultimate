49334: Play Music Until Key Pressed
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Plays a tune, but only the first time that it is used, and stops if a key is pressed. The tune is a list of notes that ends with 255. See PlayNote for what the notes are. After the tune has been played, nothing happens until TunePlayedFlag is cleared.
The tunes played by this routine are:

49338 AND A Return if the tune has already been played.
49339 RET NZ
