46265: Draw Panel
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and WinRound.
Draws the panel on the right of the screen, which holds the score, the hi-score and the sector map. It is 8 characters wide and 15 characters tall, and is built from the characters at PanelCharacters using the graphics at PanelGraphics, and coloured using the attributes at PanelAttributes.
