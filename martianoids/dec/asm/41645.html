41645: Empty Cell
Used by the routines at MoveThief and MoveHunter.
Sets the current type of the cell that last blocked an entity to zero, leaving its initial type as it was.
EmptyCell 41645 LD HL,(38446) Fetch the address of the cell.
41648 LD A,(HL) Fetch its contents.
41649 AND 240 Keep the initial type.
41651 LD (HL),A Store it.
