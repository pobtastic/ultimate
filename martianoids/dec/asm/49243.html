49243: Play Wave Sequence
Used by the routines at PlaySoundStep, MakeRandomBeep, MakeBeep, PlayMelody, MakeBumpSound, MakeExplosionSound and MakeNoiseIfVisible.
Makes a tone, which is made of a number of cycles.
Input
B Pitch (the higher it is, the lower the note)
C Number of cycles
PlayWaveSequence 49243 CALL PlaySquareWave Make a single cycle using PlaySquareWave.
49246 DEC C Loop back until all of the cycles have been made.
49247 JR NZ,PlayWaveSequence
49249 RET Return.
