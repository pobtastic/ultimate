44781: Start Light
Used by the routine at MoveLights.
Starts a new light, in a slot that is not in use. The light is given a random colour and direction, and a random delay. It starts at the top left of the frame of the screen, which is made of attributes of the value 72. The lights then move along the frame, one cell a frame.
