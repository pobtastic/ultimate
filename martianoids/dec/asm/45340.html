45340: Read Keyboard
Used by the routine at ReadControls.
Reads the keyboard. The keys C, M and B turn right, and X, V and N turn left. Any of A, S, D, F, G, Enter, L, K, J and H moves forward. The keys O, U, Q, E and T fire the second weapon, and P, I, Y, W and R fire the first one. Z, Symbol Shift and all of the number keys use the action key.

A The controls
C The controls
