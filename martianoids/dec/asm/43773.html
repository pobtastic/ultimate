43773: Move Robot Vertically
The handler for entity types 20 to 23 and 28 to 31, which are the robot when it is heading in direction one or three (up and down the map).
Input
IX Robot
MoveRobotVertically 43773 RES 7,(IX+0) Show the sprite without flipping it.
43777 JR UpdateRobot Jump to move the robot.
