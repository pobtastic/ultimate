45046: Turn Light Vertically
Used by the routines at MoveLightLeft and TurnLightHorizontally.
Tries to turn a light so that it goes up or down. If neither of the cells is part of the frame then the light is turned round, and the routine tries again, but only once.
Input
DE Address of the attribute that the light is in
IX Light
