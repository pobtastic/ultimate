45038: Move Light Down
Moves a light one cell down. See MoveLightLeft.
Input
DE Address of the attribute that the light is in
IX Light
MoveLightDown 45038 LD L,E Point HL at the cell that the light is in.
45039 LD H,D
45040 LD BC,32 Use a row of 32 characters...
45043 ADD HL,BC ...and move down to the cell below.
45044 JR MoveLight_Test Jump to test the cell.
