45506: Fill Play Window Attributes
Used by the routines at StartRound, ClearPlayWindowWithAttributes and ShowGameOver.
Fills the attributes of the play window, which is 20 characters wide and 14 characters tall and starts at column 2 of row 2, with a single attribute.
Input
A Attribute
FillPlayWindowAttributes 45506 LD HL,22594 Point HL at the top left attribute of the play window.
45509 LD DE,12 There are 12 attributes between the end of one window row and the start of the next.
45512 LD BC,5134 The window is 20 columns wide (B) and 14 rows tall (C).
This entry point is used by the routines at FillLargeWindowAttributes and FillMessageAttributes.
FillAttributes_Row 45515 PUSH BC Save the dimensions.
FillAttributes_Column 45516 LD (HL),A Write the attribute to the current cell.
45517 INC L Move on to the next column.
45518 DJNZ FillAttributes_Column Loop back until the row is complete.
45520 ADD HL,DE Move on to the start of the next row.
45521 POP BC Restore the dimensions.
45522 DEC C Decrease the row counter by one.
45523 JR NZ,FillAttributes_Row Loop back until every row has been filled.
45525 RET Return.
