45315: Finish Joystick Controls
Used by the routines at ReadInterface2, ReadKempstonJoystick and ReadCursorKeys.
Adds the keys that work with every method of control, and stores the controls. Any of the keys Z, X, C, V, Symbol Shift, M, N and B fires the first weapon.
