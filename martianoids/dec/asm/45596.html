45596: Draw Play Window
Used by the routine at MainLoop.
Copies the off-screen play window buffer to the display, and at the same time resets the buffer to the dithered grey background of the play window (alternate lines of 85 and 170). The buffer occupies 62848 to 65535: 112 lines of 24 bytes. Only the middle 20 bytes of each line are shown on screen, as the 2 bytes at each end are margins that let sprites overhang the edges of the window. The lines are copied from the bottom of the window to the top, working backwards through the buffer. The stack pointer is pointed at the display and each pair of bytes taken from the buffer is written using a PUSH instruction, which is much faster than storing the bytes one at a time.
