46571: Draw Border
Used by the routines at InitialiseGame and GameOver.
Draws the decorative frame that surrounds the play window and the screen text: two rings of beads, one inside the other, each made of two characters that alternate. The frame is 24 characters wide and 24 characters tall, and the attributes are bright blue.
