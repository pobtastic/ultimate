27092: Handle Chocolate
The handler for the chocolate (IDs 16 and 17), which drifts about in random directions. Every 16 frames a new random direction is picked and stored in byte 6 of the actor (bit 0 is set for down, and bit 1 is set for right). The speeds are changed by one every other frame towards that direction, up to a maximum speed of 2 in either direction.
While the chocolate is still entering from the right of the screen (a horizontal co-ordinate of 196 or more) it is only moved.
