29347: Move Bouncing Actor
Used by the routine at ChangeActorSpeeds.
Moves the actor by the speeds in bytes 4 and 5 of the actor, changing them to 2 or 254 (-2) when the actor reaches the edge of the play area. Bit 0 of byte 6 is set while the actor is moving down, and bit 1 is set while it is moving right. The ID of the actor is changed between its two frames, and the sprite is then drawn in its new position.

29384 LD A,(IX+7) Fetch the frame counter.

29388 AND %00000001 Keep only bit 0.
29390 XOR (IX+0) Flip bit 0 of the actor ID if bit 1 of the frame counter is set.

This entry point is used by the routines at HandlePeel and HandleHitActor.
