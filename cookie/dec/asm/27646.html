27646: Show Game Over 2UP
Used by the routines at GameOver_Controller and GameOver_ControllerRestart.
Shows "GAME OVER PLAYER 2" in the play area, and waits for a while. The example below stops before the pause.
Before After
GameOver_2UP 27646 LD A,"2"+128 Fetch the player number "2", with bit 7 set to end the message.
27648 JR GameOver_Write Jump to GameOver_Write.
