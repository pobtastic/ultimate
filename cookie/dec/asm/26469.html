26469: Handle Pike And Tin Can
The handler for the pike and the tin can (IDs 26 to 29), which are thrown out of a dustbin by the monster (see HandleMonster_Throwing). They fly in an arc; byte 4 of the actor is the horizontal speed, and byte 5 is the vertical speed, which is increased by one every 8 frames to make them fall. They bounce off the sides and the top of the play area, and are removed when they drop below the bottom of the screen. When they reach the floor they are handled in the same way as an actor which has been hit (see MoveHitActor).
