27678: Messaging: Game Over
The message for GameOver_1UP_Messaging and GameOver_2UP. The first byte is the text colour and the last byte is the player number (changed to 177 or 178 by GameOver_1UP_Messaging or GameOver_2UP).
GameOver_Text 27678 DEFM 71 "GAME OVER PLAYER X" (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) is the attribute).
27679 DEFM "GAME OVER PLAYER ","1"+128
