27531: Handle Falling Charlie
The handler for Charlie as he falls (ID 14), after his hat has gone (see HandleHat). Charlie falls one pixel at a time until his vertical co-ordinate reaches 180. Then the actor ID is changed to 24 which is handled by HandleEndOfLife, and the tune for losing a life is played (unless the tunes are muted).
Input
IX Actor
HandleFallingCharlie 27531 CALL StoreEntity Record the position of the sprite using StoreEntity.
27534 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.
27537 ADD A,1 Add 1 to make Charlie fall by one pixel.
27539 CP 180 Jump to HandleFallingCharlie_Finished if Charlie has fallen far enough.
27541 JR NC,HandleFallingCharlie_Finished
27543 LD (IX+2),A Update the vertical co-ordinate.
27546 CALL DisplayEntity Erase the sprite and draw it in its new position using DisplayEntity.
27549 JP ColourSprite Jump to ColourSprite to colour the sprite.
HandleFallingCharlie_Finished 27552 LD (IX+0),24 Write 24 to the actor ID.
27556 LD A,(24333) Fetch the sound mute flag.
27559 AND A Are the tunes muted?
27560 RET NZ Return if they are.
27561 LD DE,26065 Point DE at the tune for losing a life (MusicData_LoseLife).
27564 JP PlayMusic Jump to PlayMusic to play the tune.
