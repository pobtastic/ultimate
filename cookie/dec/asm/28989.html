28989: Remove All Actors
Used by the routines at HandleCake and NextActor.
Removes all ten of the actors, by writing zero to each of the IDs.
RemoveAllActors 28989 LD HL,24373 Point HL at the first actor (Charlie's slot).
28992 LD B,10 Set the actor counter to 10 (the number of actors).
28994 JR RemoveActors Jump to RemoveActors.
