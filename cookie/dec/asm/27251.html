27251: Lose Hat
Used by the routines at HandlePeel, HandlePikeAndTinCan, HandleCheese, HandleTackWasherBolt, HandleChocolate, HandleSugar and HandleCustard.
Called when an actor has hit Charlie. Charlie is replaced with Charlie without his hat (ID 12), and the hat (ID 13) is put 16 pixels above him. Charlie's co-ordinates are saved in bytes 4 and 5 of the actor. The hat is then handled by HandleHat, and all of the other actors are cleared away by ConvertActors.
LoseHat 27251 PUSH IX Save the pointer to the current actor on the stack.
27253 LD IX,24373 Point IX at ActorCharlie.
27257 CALL DrawEntity Erase Charlie using DrawEntity.
27260 LD (IX+0),12 Change Charlie's ID to 12 (Charlie without his hat).
27264 CALL DrawEntity Draw Charlie without his hat using DrawEntity.
27267 LD (IX+0),13 Change the ID to 13 (the hat).
27271 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the horizontal co-ordinate.
27274 LD (IX+4),A Save Charlie's horizontal co-ordinate in byte 4 of the actor.
27277 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.
27280 LD (IX+5),A Save Charlie's vertical co-ordinate in byte 5 of the actor.
27283 SUB 16 Subtract 16 to put the hat above Charlie's head.
27285 LD (IX+2),A Update the vertical co-ordinate.
27288 CALL DrawEntity Draw the hat using DrawEntity.
27291 POP IX Restore the pointer to the current actor from the stack.
27293 JP ConvertActors Jump to ConvertActors.
