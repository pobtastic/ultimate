28536: Handle Flour Shot
The handler for the flour shot (ID 3), which is fired by Charlie (see ControlCharlie_CreateShot). Byte 4 of the actor is the horizontal speed and byte 5 is the vertical speed. The shot moves until it reaches the right, left, bottom or top of the play area, and then it explodes (see ExplodeFlourShot).
Input
IX Actor
HandleFlourShot 28536 CALL StoreEntity Record the position of the sprite using StoreEntity.
28539 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the horizontal co-ordinate.
28542 ADD A,(IX+4) Add the horizontal speed.
28545 CP 196 Jump to ExplodeFlourShot if the shot has reached the right edge.
28547 JR NC,ExplodeFlourShot
28549 CP 1 Jump to ExplodeFlourShot if the shot has reached the left edge.
28551 JR C,ExplodeFlourShot
28553 LD C,A Keep the new horizontal co-ordinate in C.
28554 LD A,(IX+2) Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.
28557 ADD A,(IX+5) Add the vertical speed.
28560 CP 168 Jump to ExplodeFlourShot if the shot has reached the bottom.
28562 JR NC,ExplodeFlourShot
28564 CP 32 Jump to ExplodeFlourShot if the shot has reached the top.
28566 JR C,ExplodeFlourShot
28568 LD (IX+2),A Update the vertical co-ordinate.
28571 LD (IX+1),C Update the horizontal co-ordinate.
28574 CALL DisplayEntity Erase the sprite and draw it in its new position using DisplayEntity.
28577 JP ColourSprite Jump to ColourSprite to colour the sprite.
