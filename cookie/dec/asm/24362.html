24362: Pantry Highlight Mask
Has one bit set to show which shelf of the pantry is highlighted, which is bit 5 for the top shelf down to bit 1 for the bottom shelf. It is made by DrawPantry by shifting 32 right by the level (modulo 8), so for the levels which have no ingredient no shelf is highlighted.
