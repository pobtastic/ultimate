25832: Start Cake
Used by the routine at NextLevel.
Copies the templates for the five pieces of the cake into the actor slots which start at ActorSpawnSlots. This happens when the fifth level of a set of five has been completed (see NextLevel).
StartCake 25832 LD HL,25892 Point HL at the templates for the five pieces of the cake.
25835 LD DE,24405 Point DE at ActorSpawnSlots.
25838 LD BC,40 Set the number of bytes to copy to 40 (five actors of eight bytes).
25841 LDIR Copy the templates to the actor slots.
25843 RET Return.
