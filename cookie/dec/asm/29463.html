29463: Test Flour Collision
Used by the routines at HandleMonster, HandlePeel, HandlePikeAndTinCan, HandleCheese, HandleTackWasherBolt, HandleChocolate, HandleSugar and HandleCustard.
Tests whether the actor has been hit by a flour shot (they are in the two actor slots after Charlie). A shot is within range if it is between 10 pixels to the left and 18 pixels to the right of the actor, and between 18 pixels above and 10 pixels below it. If it is then the actor is coloured white, bit 7 of its ID is set and the counter in byte 7 is set to 64. The speeds of the shot are copied to the actor, the shot is made to explode, and the rising tone is played.
