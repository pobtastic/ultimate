27650: Show Current Game Over
Used by the routine at HandleEndOfLife.
Shows the "GAME OVER" message for the active player, using Flag_ActivePlayer to choose between 1UP and 2UP.
GameOver_Controller 27650 LD A,(24353) If Flag_ActivePlayer is zero, jump to GameOver_1UP_Messaging.
27653 AND A
27654 JR Z,GameOver_1UP_Messaging
27656 JR GameOver_2UP Else, jump to GameOver_2UP.
