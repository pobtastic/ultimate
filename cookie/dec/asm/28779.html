28779: Game Play Start
Used by the routine at GamePlayEntry.
Moves Charlie, using the controls which are read by ReadControls. Byte 4 of the actor is the horizontal speed and byte 5 is the vertical speed; each is 2 (or 254) while the control is pressed, and zero otherwise. Charlie can't move past the edges of the play area. While he is moving his ID changes between 1 and 2 every four frames, which animates him. The last direction in which he moved is kept in CharlieLastMovement.
While fire is being held down, a flour shot is fired every eight frames, if there is one of the two slots for the shots free. The shot goes in the last direction in which Charlie moved, at three times his speed.
