24363: Charlie Last Movement
The last direction in which Charlie moved. The low byte is the horizontal speed and the high byte is the vertical speed. It is updated by GamePlay_Start each time Charlie moves and is set to 0002 (moving right) by DisplayPlayerLives. Flour shots are fired in this direction, at three times the speed.
