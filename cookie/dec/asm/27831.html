27831: Handle Sugar
The handler for the sugar (IDs 8 and 9), which drifts about the screen. Byte 4 of the actor is the horizontal speed, and byte 5 is the vertical speed. Every 32 frames new random speeds are chosen (see SetRandomSpeeds); they are both either 2 or 254 (-2). The sugar bounces off the sides and the top and bottom of the play area, and as it goes it also moves up and down in a wave, which is made from the frame counter in byte 7 of the actor.
While the sugar is still entering from the right of the screen (a horizontal co-ordinate of 196 or more) it can't hit Charlie or be hit.
