28596: Read Controls
Used by the routine at GamePlay_Start.
Reads the controls which have been chosen in GameOptions; the keyboard, the Kempston joystick or the cursor joystick. The bits of the result are reset for the directions (and fire) which are being pressed.
Bit Control Keyboard
0 Right W
1 Left Q
2 Down E
3 Up R
4 Fire T
Output
A The controls
ReadControls 28596 LD A,(24332) Fetch the game options.

28601 CP 2 Jump to ReadKempstonJoystick if the Kempston joystick is being used.
28603 JR Z,ReadKempstonJoystick
28605 CP 4 Jump to ReadCursorJoystick if the cursor joystick is being used.
28607 JR Z,ReadCursorJoystick
28609 LD A,251 Read from the keyboard;
