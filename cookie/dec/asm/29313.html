29313: Handle Custard
The handler for the custard (IDs 4 and 5), which bounces around the play area diagonally. It is moved by MoveBouncingActor. While the custard is still entering from the right of the screen (a horizontal co-ordinate of 200 or more) it can't hit Charlie or be hit, and it is given a random vertical speed.
Input

29341 JR NZ,HandleHitActor
