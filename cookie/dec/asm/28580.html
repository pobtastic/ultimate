28580: Explode Flour Shot
Used by the routines at HandleFlourShot and TestFlourCollision.
Erases the flour shot, and changes it into an explosion (ID 38) which is handled by HandleExplosion.
Input
IX Flour shot
ExplodeFlourShot 28580 CALL DrawEntity Erase the sprite using DrawEntity.
28583 LD (IX+0),38 Change the actor ID to 38 (an exploding flour shot).
28587 LD (IX+7),3 Write 3 to byte 7 of the actor (the explosion counter).
28591 RET Return.
