5F2D: Active Player Level
The level of the active player. Bits 0-2 are the level within a set of five, and bits 3-5 are the set. It is FF for a player who has not started yet, so that NextLevel (which adds one to the level) starts them on level 00. Swapped with InactivePlayer_Level by ChangePlayer.
ActivePlayer_Level 5F2D DEFB $00
