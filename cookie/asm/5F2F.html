5F2F: Active Player Ingredients Needed
The number of ingredients which are still needed (in BCD). It is shown inside the mixing bowl. The level is finished when it reaches zero and there are no actors left in play. Swapped with InactivePlayer_IngredientsNeeded by ChangePlayer.
