6765: Handle Pike And Tin Can
The handler for the pike and the tin can (IDs 1A to 1D), which are thrown out of a dustbin by the monster (see HandleMonster_Throwing). They fly in an arc; byte 04 of the actor is the horizontal speed, and byte 05 is the vertical speed, which is increased by one every 08 frames to make them fall. They bounce off the sides and the top of the play area, and are removed when they drop below the bottom of the screen. When they reach the floor they are handled in the same way as an actor which has been hit (see MoveHitActor).

6782 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.

Every 08 frames, increase the vertical speed.
678A LD A,($5F1D) Fetch the frame counter.
678D AND %00000111 Keep only bits 0-2.
678F JR NZ,HandlePikeAndTinCan_Vertical Jump to HandlePikeAndTinCan_Vertical if the frame counter is not a multiple of 08.
6791 INC (IX+$05) Increase the vertical speed by one.
Move vertically.
HandlePikeAndTinCan_Vertical 6794 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the vertical speed.
6797 ADD A,(IX+$02) Add the vertical co-ordinate to get the new position.
679A CP $B8 Jump to HandlePikeAndTinCan_Remove if the actor has dropped below the bottom of the screen.
679C JR NC,HandlePikeAndTinCan_Remove

67A1 CP $24 Compare with 24 (the top of the play area).
67A3 CALL C,ReverseVerticalSpeed Call ReverseVerticalSpeed if the actor has reached the top of the play area.
Move horizontally.

67A9 AND A Is the actor moving left (is the speed negative)?

67B0 LD (IX+$01),A Update the horizontal co-ordinate.
67B3 CP $BC Compare with BC (the right edge of the play area).
67B5 CALL NC,ReverseHorizontalSpeed Call ReverseHorizontalSpeed if the actor has reached the right edge.
HandlePikeAndTinCan_Draw 67B8 CALL DisplayEntity Erase the sprite and draw it in its new position using DisplayEntity.
67BB JP ColourSprite Jump to ColourSprite to colour the sprite.
