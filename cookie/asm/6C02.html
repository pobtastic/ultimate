6C02: Show Current Game Over
Used by the routine at HandleEndOfLife.
Shows the "GAME OVER" message for the active player, using Flag_ActivePlayer to choose between 1UP and 2UP.
GameOver_Controller 6C02 LD A,($5F21) If Flag_ActivePlayer is zero, jump to GameOver_1UP_Messaging.
6C05 AND A
6C06 JR Z,GameOver_1UP_Messaging
6C08 JR GameOver_2UP Else, jump to GameOver_2UP.
