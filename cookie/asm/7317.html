7317: Test Flour Collision
Used by the routines at HandleMonster, HandlePeel, HandlePikeAndTinCan, HandleCheese, HandleTackWasherBolt, HandleChocolate, HandleSugar and HandleCustard.
Tests whether the actor has been hit by a flour shot (they are in the two actor slots after Charlie). A shot is within range if it is between 0A pixels to the left and 12 pixels to the right of the actor, and between 12 pixels above and 0A pixels below it. If it is then the actor is coloured white, bit 7 of its ID is set and the counter in byte 07 is set to 40. The speeds of the shot are copied to the actor, the shot is made to explode, and the rising tone is played.
