754A: Clip Sprite
Used by the routines at PrepareOldSprite and PrepareActorSprite.
The vertical co-ordinate is the bottom edge of a sprite. This stops sprites being drawn below the play area, which ends at 9F.
Input
H Vertical co-ordinate
Output
C The number of pixel rows which are below the play area
H The vertical co-ordinate (limited to 9F)
ClipSprite 754A LD A,H Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.
754B SUB $9F Subtract 9F (the bottom of the play area).
754D JP M,ClipSprite_Visible Jump to ClipSprite_Visible if the sprite is above the bottom of the play area.
7550 LD H,$9F Limit the vertical co-ordinate to 9F.
7552 LD C,A Keep the number of rows to clip in C.
7553 RET Return.
ClipSprite_Visible 7554 LD C,$00 Flag that no rows need to be clipped (C=00).
7556 RET Return.
