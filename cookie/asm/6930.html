6930: Handle Cheese
The handler for the cheese (IDs 12 and 13), which bounces up and down the screen like a ball. Byte 05 of the actor is the vertical speed; it is increased by one every eight frames (up to a maximum of 08), and half of it is added to the vertical co-ordinate. When the cheese reaches the top of the play area its vertical speed is reversed, and when it reaches the bottom it is thrown back up with a random speed, and a random horizontal speed. The horizontal movement is done by MoveBouncingActor_Horizontal.
While the cheese is still entering from the right of the screen (a horizontal co-ordinate of C4 or more) it only moves horizontally.
