7378: Display Lives
Used by the routines at StartLevel and StartLife.
Shows the number of lives which each of the players has, at the top of the screen; the number is followed by the icon for a life. If a player has no lives then their space is blanked. Charlie's last movement (CharlieLastMovement) is also reset to be 02 pixels to the right.
