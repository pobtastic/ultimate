713D: Remove All Actors
Used by the routines at HandleCake and NextActor.
Removes all ten of the actors, by writing zero to each of the IDs.
RemoveAllActors 713D LD HL,$5F35 Point HL at the first actor (Charlie's slot).
7140 LD B,$0A Set the actor counter to 0A (the number of actors).
7142 JR RemoveActors Jump to RemoveActors.
