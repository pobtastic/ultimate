5F2A: Pantry Highlight Mask
Has one bit set to show which shelf of the pantry is highlighted, which is bit 05 for the top shelf down to bit 01 for the bottom shelf. It is made by DrawPantry by shifting 20 right by the level (modulo 08), so for the levels which have no ingredient no shelf is highlighted.
