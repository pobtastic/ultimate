6C1E: Messaging: Game Over
The message for GameOver_1UP_Messaging and GameOver_2UP. The first byte is the text colour and the last byte is the player number (changed to B1 or B2 by GameOver_1UP_Messaging or GameOver_2UP).
GameOver_Text 6C1E DEFM $47 "GAME OVER PLAYER X" (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) is the attribute).
6C1F DEFM "GAME OVER PLAYER ","1"+$80
