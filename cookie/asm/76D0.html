76D0: Store Entity
Used by the routines at HandleCake, HandleBatter, HandleMonster, HandlePeel, HandlePikeAndTinCan, HandleCheese, HandleTackWasherBolt, HandleChocolate, HandleHat, HandleClearedActor, HandleFallingCharlie, HandleSugar, HandleLevelIntroduction, HandleFlourShot, GamePlay_Start and HandleCustard.
Copies the sprite ID and position of the actor into OldSprite_X, so that when the actor is moved, PrepareOldSprite knows where the old sprite has to be erased from.
Input
IX Actor
StoreEntity 76D0 LD A,(IX+$01) Copy the horizontal co-ordinate to OldSprite_X.
76D3 LD ($5F14),A
76D6 LD A,(IX+$02) Copy the vertical co-ordinate to OldSprite_Y.
76D9 LD ($5F15),A
76DC LD A,(IX+$00) Copy the sprite ID to OldSprite_ID.
76DF LD ($5F16),A
76E2 RET Return.
