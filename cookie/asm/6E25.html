6E25: Move Hit Actor
Used by the routine at HandleHitActor.
Moves an actor which has been hit by a flour shot (see TestFlourCollision). The actor moves by the speeds in bytes 04 and 05 which were taken from the flour shot. Byte 07 of the actor is a counter which was set to 40; each time this is called it is decreased, and when it reaches zero the actor recovers (see RecoverHitActor). The speed is changed to 04 (or FC) when the actor reaches the left (or right) edge of the play area or the top of it, and if the other speed is zero it is changed to a random one. When the actor reaches the bottom of the play area then LandHitActor deals with it.
Two bits of byte 06 of the actor are changed; bit 1 is set if the actor is less than 34 pixels from the middle of the screen (68), and bit 7 is set if the actor is moving towards the middle of the screen (which is when it is in the left half and moving right, or in the right half and moving left).

IX Actor

6E76 LD (IX+$06),A Update byte 06 of the actor with the result.
