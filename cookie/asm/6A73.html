6A73: Lose Hat
Used by the routines at HandlePeel, HandlePikeAndTinCan, HandleCheese, HandleTackWasherBolt, HandleChocolate, HandleSugar and HandleCustard.
Called when an actor has hit Charlie. Charlie is replaced with Charlie without his hat (ID 0C), and the hat (ID 0D) is put 10 pixels above him. Charlie's co-ordinates are saved in bytes 04 and 05 of the actor. The hat is then handled by HandleHat, and all of the other actors are cleared away by ConvertActors.
LoseHat 6A73 PUSH IX Save the pointer to the current actor on the stack.
6A75 LD IX,$5F35 Point IX at ActorCharlie.
6A79 CALL DrawEntity Erase Charlie using DrawEntity.
6A7C LD (IX+$00),$0C Change Charlie's ID to 0C (Charlie without his hat).
6A80 CALL DrawEntity Draw Charlie without his hat using DrawEntity.
6A83 LD (IX+$00),$0D Change the ID to 0D (the hat).
6A87 LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the horizontal co-ordinate.
6A8A LD (IX+$04),A Save Charlie's horizontal co-ordinate in byte 04 of the actor.
6A8D LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the vertical co-ordinate.
6A90 LD (IX+$05),A Save Charlie's vertical co-ordinate in byte 05 of the actor.
6A93 SUB $10 Subtract 10 to put the hat above Charlie's head.
6A95 LD (IX+$02),A Update the vertical co-ordinate.
6A98 CALL DrawEntity Draw the hat using DrawEntity.
6A9B POP IX Restore the pointer to the current actor from the stack.
6A9D JP ConvertActors Jump to ConvertActors.
