64E8: Start Cake
Used by the routine at NextLevel.
Copies the templates for the five pieces of the cake into the actor slots which start at ActorSpawnSlots. This happens when the fifth level of a set of five has been completed (see NextLevel).
StartCake 64E8 LD HL,$6524 Point HL at the templates for the five pieces of the cake.
64EB LD DE,$5F55 Point DE at ActorSpawnSlots.
64EE LD BC,$0028 Set the number of bytes to copy to 28 (five actors of eight bytes).
64F1 LDIR Copy the templates to the actor slots.
64F3 RET Return.
