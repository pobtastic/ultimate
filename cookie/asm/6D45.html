6D45: Create Introduction Ingredient
Used by the routine at StartLevelIntroduction.
Clears the screen and the attributes, and creates the actor for the ingredient which is needed for the level in the first of the six actor slots, using the data at IntroductionIngredients for the level (bits 0-2 of ActivePlayer_Level). The actor is created by CreateIntroductionActor.
CreateIntroductionIngredient 6D45 XOR A Clear the flag which says whether Charlie is running (CharlieRunningFlag).
