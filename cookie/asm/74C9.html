74C9: Messaging: Banner Text
The text of the labels printed by PrintBanner. Each string starts with its colour attribute, and the last character has bit 7 set.
1UP_Text 74C9 DEFM $47 "1UP" (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) is the attribute).
74CA DEFM "1U","P"+$80
2UP_Text 74CD DEFM $47 "2UP" (INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) is the attribute).
74CE DEFM "2U","P"+$80
HI_Text 74D1 DEFM $78 "HI" (INK:BLACK, PAPER:WHITE (BRIGHT) is the attribute).
74D2 DEFM "H","I"+$80
