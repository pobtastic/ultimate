6FB0: Controls: Kempston Joystick
Used by the routine at ReadControls.
Reads the Kempston joystick. The bits are flipped so that they are the same as those which are returned by ReadControls.
Output
A The controls (see ReadControls)
ReadKempstonJoystick 6FB0 IN A,($1F) Fetch the state of the Kempston joystick.
6FB2 CPL Flip the bits, to match the keyboard (a zero bit means pressed).
6FB3 RET Return.
