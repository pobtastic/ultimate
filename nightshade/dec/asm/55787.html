55787: Update Bumping Body
The routine for objects of type 22 and 30, which is the body of the player when the legs have bumped into something. The body stays in this pose while the legs are flagged as having bumped into something, and goes back to normal otherwise.
UpdateBumpingBody 55787 LD A,(IX-9) Update the body as normal (see UpdatePlayerBody) if the legs have not bumped into something.

55792 JR Z,UpdatePlayerBody
55794 LD (IX+7),A Copy the flags of the legs.
55797 LD A,(IX-16) Set the type to the bumping pose (sprite 6) for the kind of legs.
55800 AND 248
55802 OR 6
55804 LD (IX+0),A
