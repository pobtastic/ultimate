56168: Check Rotate Key
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerLegs.
Turns the view around when the key to turn the view has been pressed and then released. The view is turned by flipping the rotation flag at ViewRotatedFlag, and the player is given a short turn counter. The compass is redrawn.
