53234: Draw Objects In List
Used by the routine at DrawCellAndObjects.
Draws the objects in the list at ObjectList, from the furthest away to the nearest. It repeatedly finds the object that has no other object behind it, draws it, and then marks it as drawn by clearing bit 7 of the high byte of its entry in the list.
