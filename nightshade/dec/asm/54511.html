54511: Wall Strip Masks
The four masks used by DrawWallStrip to keep the bits of the buffer on the left of a wall strip. The index is the shift to the right in twos of pixels: 0, 2, 4 or 6.
WallStripMasks 54511 DEFB 0 Shift 0: 0.
54512 DEFB 192 Shift 2: 192.
54513 DEFB 240 Shift 4: 240.
54514 DEFB 252 Shift 6: 252.
