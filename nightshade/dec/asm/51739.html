51739: Set Flashing Attribute
Used by the routine at FlashMenuOptions.
Sets bit 7 (flash) of the attribute of one of a number of lines of the menu, and resets it in the other lines.
Input
A Number of the line to flash
B Number of lines
HL Address of the attribute of the first line
SetFlashingAttribute 51739 AND A Jump to FlashChosenLine_NoFlash unless this is the line to flash.
51740 JR NZ,FlashChosenLine_NoFlash
FlashChosenLine_Flash 51742 SET 7,(HL) Turn on flashing for the line, and jump to FlashChosenLine_Skip.
51744 JR FlashChosenLine_Skip
FlashChosenLine_Next 51746 DEC A Count down the lines, and jump to FlashChosenLine_Flash when the line to flash is reached.
51747 JR Z,FlashChosenLine_Flash
FlashChosenLine_NoFlash 51749 RES 7,(HL) Turn off flashing for the line.
FlashChosenLine_Skip 51751 INC HL Move on to the next line, and loop back until all of the lines have been done.
51752 DJNZ FlashChosenLine_Next
51754 RET Return.
