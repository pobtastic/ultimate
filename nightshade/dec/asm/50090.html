50090: Play Bump Sound
Used by the routines at UpdateCreature and UpdatePlayerBody.
Plays a burst of noise, if the object at IX has been drawn in the view (bit 1 of its flags).
Input
IX Object
PlayBumpSound 50090 BIT 1,(IX+7) Return if the object has not been drawn in the view, because it can't be heard.
