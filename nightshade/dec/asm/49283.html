49283: Update Creature
The routine for objects of type 112 to 127, the creatures. A creature wanders about the village and hurts the player if it touches. It vanishes when the player is more than three cells away. When a shot hits it, the kind of the shot and the kind of the creature are added together to choose one of four things to happen to it (see HitOutcomeTable).
UpdateCreature 49283 LD A,(IX+5) Set the velocity of the creature from its speed and the way it faces.
49286 CALL SetVelocityFromSpeed
49289 CALL TestMove Check whether the creature has run into the scenery.
49292 BIT 0,(IX+7) Check whether it did.
49296 CALL NZ,PlayBumpSound Play the bump sound if it did.
49299 RES 1,(IX+7) Clear the flag that shows that the creature has been drawn, ready for the next frame.
49303 CALL MoveObject Move the creature.
49306 CALL UpdateDirection Turn the creature to a new direction if it has gone far enough in this one.
49309 CALL SetSpriteFromDirection Choose the picture of the creature from the way it faces.
49312 LD C,3 Set the distance at which the creature gives up to 3 cells.
49314 CALL CheckPlayerClose Check whether the creature is close to the player.
49317 JR NC,UpdateCreature_Vanish Jump to UpdateCreature_Vanish if the player is further away than that.
49319 CALL CheckShotHit Check whether a shot has hit the creature.
49322 JR C,UpdateCreature_Hit Jump to UpdateCreature_Hit if one has.
49324 CALL CheckPlayerCollision Check whether the creature is touching the player.
49327 RET NC Return if it is not.
49328 LD BC,37 Set the points that touching a creature costs.
49331 CALL AddToScore Add the points to the score.
49334 JP HurtPlayer Hurt the player (see HurtPlayer).
A shot has hit the creature. Add together the kind of the creature and the kind of the shot (its type divided by four) to choose what happens.

49342 SRL A

49353 AND %00000011 Keep the result between 0 and 3.
49355 LD BC,49361 Point at the table of outcomes.
49358 JP JumpToTableRoutine Jump to the outcome that was chosen.
