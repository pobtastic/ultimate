58140: Read View Key
Used by the routines at ReadInterface2Joystick, ReadKempstonJoystick, ReadCursorJoystick and ReadKeyboard.
Reads the key for turning the view (Z or SYMBOL SHIFT), and keeps the controls in Controls.
