52496: Update Logo Piece
The routine for objects of type 152 to 157, the pieces of the title graphic that are shown during the ending. Each piece stays where it was placed. It is drawn, and its attributes are set to bright white.
The large piece (type 152) is the last piece in the object table, and waits for the ghosts before it: when the object before it has gone, the ending is over and the game starts again.
