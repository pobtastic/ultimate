54293: Clip Wall Strip Top
Used by the routine at DrawWallStrip.
Skips the rows of a wall strip that are below PixelBuffer. A is the number of those rows, negated. It starts drawing at 72 and jumps to DrawWallStrip_Draw.
Input
A The number of rows below the buffer, negated
HL The address of the graphic
