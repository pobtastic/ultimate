58810: Fill Memory
Used by the routines at StartGame and ClearPixels.
Fills memory with a byte. Entering at FillMemory fills it with zero.
Input
BC Number of bytes
E Byte to fill with (when entering at FillMemory_WithValue)
HL Address
FillMemory 58810 LD E,0 Use zero to fill the memory.
This entry point is used by the routine at ClearAttributes.
FillMemory_WithValue 58812 LD (HL),E Fill the memory, and loop back until all of the bytes have been filled.
58813 INC HL
58814 DEC BC
58815 LD A,B
58816 OR C
58817 JR NZ,FillMemory_WithValue
58819 RET Return.
