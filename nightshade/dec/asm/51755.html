51755: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Prints the eight lines of the game selection menu. The first time that it is called it also draws the frame around the screen.
Point at the menu colours, positions and text.
DisplayMenu 51755 LD DE,51554 Point DE at MenuLineAttributes.
51758 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
51759 LD HL,51562 Point HL at MenuLinePositions.
51762 LD DE,51578 Point DE at MenuText.
51765 LD B,8 Set the number of lines to 8.
Print one line from the three lists.
DisplayMenu_Loop 51767 LD A,(HL) Fetch the x position.
51768 INC HL Move on to the next pair of positions.
51769 INC HL
51770 PUSH HL Stash the address of the next pair on the stack.
51771 DEC HL Point HL at the y position.
51772 LD H,(HL) Fetch the y position.
51773 LD L,A Set the x position.
51774 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
51775 LD A,(DE) Fetch the attribute of the line.
51776 INC DE Move on to the next attribute.
51777 EX AF,AF' Keep the attribute in AF'.
51778 EXX Switch back to the main registers.
51779 CALL PrintString Call PrintString.
51782 POP HL Restore the address of the next pair from the stack.
51783 DJNZ DisplayMenu_Loop Jump to DisplayMenu_Loop until all of the lines have been printed.
