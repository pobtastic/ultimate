51755: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Prints the eight lines of the game selection menu. The first time that it is called it also draws the frame around the screen.
DisplayMenu 51755 LD DE,51554 Point at the attributes of the lines of the menu.
51758 EXX Swap to the shadow registers to keep the pointer to the attributes.
51759 LD HL,51562 Point at the positions of the lines and at the text of the lines, and set a counter for eight lines.
51762 LD DE,51578
51765 LD B,8
DisplayMenu_Loop 51767 LD A,(HL) Fetch the position of the line.
51768 INC HL
51769 INC HL
51770 PUSH HL Keep the pointer to the positions, and put the position of the line into HL.
51771 DEC HL
51772 LD H,(HL)
51773 LD L,A
51774 EXX Swap to the shadow registers.
51775 LD A,(DE) Fetch the attribute of the line, and keep it for printing.
51776 INC DE
51777 EX AF,AF'
51778 EXX Swap back to the main registers.
51779 CALL PrintString Print the line.
51782 POP HL Get the pointer to the positions back, and loop back until all eight lines have been printed.
51783 DJNZ DisplayMenu_Loop
51785 LD A,(48063) Return if the frame around the screen has been drawn.
51788 AND A
51789 RET NZ
51790 INC A Flag that the frame has been drawn.
51791 LD (48063),A
51794 JP DrawFrame Jump to DrawFrame to draw the frame.
