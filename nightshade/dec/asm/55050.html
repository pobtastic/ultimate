55050: Update Fragment
The routine for objects of type 140 to 143 the fragments that fly out of a monster that has been destroyed. A fragment moves in a straight line, and turns into a burst when it hits something.
UpdateFragment 55050 RES 1,(IX+7) Clear the flag that shows that the fragment has been drawn.
55054 LD A,(IX+5) Set the velocity of the fragment from its speed and direction.
55057 CALL SetVelocityFromSpeed
55060 CALL TestMove Check whether the scenery blocks the move.
55063 CALL MoveObject Move the fragment.
55066 CALL NextSpriteNumber Change the sprite of the fragment.
55069 BIT 0,(IX+7) Return if the fragment has not hit anything.
55073 RET Z
55074 LD (IX+0),12 Turn the fragment into a burst and return.
55078 RET
