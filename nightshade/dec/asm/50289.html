50289: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayBumpSound, PlaySweepSound and PlayMaterialiseSound.
Plays a single cycle of a tone on the speaker.
Input
B Pitch
PlaySquareWave 50289 LD A,16 Turn the speaker on.
50291 OUT (254),A
50293 LD A,B
PlaySquareWave_High 50294 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_High Wait, to make the first half of the cycle.
50296 LD B,A Turn the speaker off.
50297 XOR A
50298 OUT (254),A
50300 LD A,B
PlaySquareWave_Low 50301 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Low Wait, to make the second half of the cycle.
50303 LD B,A Get the pitch back and return.
50304 RET
