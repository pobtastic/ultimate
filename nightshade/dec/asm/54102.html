54102: Colour Wall Column
Used by the routine at DrawWallStrips.
Sets the attributes of a column of AttributeBuffer from the strip to the bottom of the view. The colour is bright green, cyan, yellow or white, from the lowest two bits of WallTile.
Input

Return unless the strip is in the view.
