54536: Project Cell
Used by the routines at DrawCell and DrawCellWithWalls.
Works out where the corner of the cell last set by GetMapCell is on the screen. With the distances from the player as dx and dy, the horizontal position is (dx + dy + 240) / 2 and the vertical position is (dy - dx + 432) / 4. They are put in ScreenXPosition and ScreenYPosition. When bit 0 of ViewRotatedFlag is set, the player's position is turned round.
Set the position of the corner of the cell.
