58195: Flip Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawSprite.
Turns a sprite upside down or round (or back again) when the flags of an object say that it should be the other way up or round from how it was last time. The sprite is changed in place, with bits 7 (upside down) and 6 (reversed) of its first byte showing how it is.
A sprite is made of a byte for the width in characters (bits 0 to 3) and the flags, a byte for the number of rows, and then the rows, with the bottom row first. Each row has a pair of bytes for each character: the mask and then the pixels.
