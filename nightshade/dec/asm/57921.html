57921: Read Controls
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerLegs.
Reads the controls, using the method that was chosen on the game selection menu (bits 1 and 2 of GameOptions), and keeps them in Controls.
The bits of the controls are 0 for left, 1 for right, 2 for forward, 3 for fire, 4 for down and 5 for turning the view.
The methods are the keyboard (0), a Kempston joystick (1), a Cursor joystick (2) and a Sinclair joystick (3).
