49919: Print Score
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Prints the score, as seven digits, in the status panel. The last two digits are always zero.
After PrintScore
PrintScore 49919 LD A,70 Use bright yellow ink (attribute 70), and keep it for printing the digits.
49921 EX AF,AF'
49922 LD HL,20710 Set the screen position of the score to 20710 (in the screen buffer).
This entry point is used by the routine at GameOver.
PrintScore_Position 49925 LD DE,48073 Point at the score, and set the number of bytes to print to four.
49928 LD B,4
49930 PUSH HL Keep the screen position.
49931 LD HL,27870 Point at the font of the digits.
49934 LD (48044),HL Set it as the font.
49937 POP HL Get the screen position back.
49938 JR PrintBCDNumber_LowerDigit Jump to PrintBCDNumber_LowerDigit to print the digits, starting with the lower digit of the first byte.
