57595: Build Shift Tables
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Builds the tables that the sprite drawing routines use to shift graphics sideways, and the table that reverses the bits in a byte.
There are three pairs of 256-byte pages for shifting a byte by two, four and six pixels (the low byte of the shifted result is in the first page of each pair, counting down from 255, and the high byte is in the second). 250 to 255 hold the shifts of the bytes as they are, and 242 to 247 hold the shifts of the bytes with their bits reversed. The page at ReverseBitsTable holds the bytes with their bits reversed.
Build the tables for the bytes as they are.
