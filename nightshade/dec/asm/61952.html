61952: Reversed Shift Tables
Six pages of tables that are built by BuildShiftTables, which shift the bytes with their bits reversed by two, four and six pixels. For each shift there is a page for the low byte of the result and a page for the high byte. The contents at the time of the snapshot are left over from loading.
