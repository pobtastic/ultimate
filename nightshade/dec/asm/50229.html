50229: Play Random Sound
Used by the routine at UpdateBurst.
Plays sixteen tones with pitches taken from a random place in the ROM, if the object at IX has been drawn in the view (bit 1 of its flags).
Input
IX Object
PlayRandomSound 50229 BIT 1,(IX+7) Return if the object has not been drawn in the view, otherwise clear the flag.
50233 RES 1,(IX+7)

50244 LD H,A

PlayRandomSound_Loop 50247 LD A,(HL) Play a short tone, with a pitch taken from the ROM, and loop back until all sixteen tones have been played.
