53661: Draw Cell
Used by the routines at DrawView and DrawCellAndObjects.
Draws a cell of the map from the two lists of strips for its tile. Each list is made of sixteen strips, which are drawn along the edge of the cell: the first list from left to right and the second list from right to left. Nothing is drawn if the cell is empty. When the view is rotated, the lists are drawn in the opposite order.
