51965: Draw Horizontal Edge
Used by the routines at DrawPlayingAreaBorder and DrawFrame.
Draws a number of blocks of two by two characters along a row.
Input
A' Attribute
B Number of blocks
DE Address of the four characters in a block
HL Screen address of the first block
DrawHorizontalEdge 51965 PUSH BC Keep the number of blocks and the address of the characters.
51966 PUSH DE
51967 CALL PrintCharacterBlock Print a block.
51970 INC L Move on two positions to the right.
51971 INC L
51972 POP DE Get the number of blocks and the address of the characters back.
51973 POP BC
51974 DJNZ DrawHorizontalEdge Loop back until all of the blocks have been drawn.
51976 RET Return.
