57672: Copy Pixels To Screen
Used by the routine at CopyBuffersToScreen.
Copies the pixels of the screen buffer onto the screen, using the stack to copy two bytes at a time.

The buffer holds 112 rows of 24 bytes, and the top row of the buffer is the bottom row of the playing area, so the rows of the buffer are copied from the bottom of the screen upwards. Only the 22 bytes of each row that are inside the border are copied, which are bytes 2 to 23 of the row.
