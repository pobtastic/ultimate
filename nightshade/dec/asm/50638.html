50638: Spawn Item
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Every sixteen frames, when the player is standing on a kind of tile that still has items to give, makes an item appear at the player's position. The item is placed in the first free one of the four objects from ObjectTable_Items, at a random position within the cell. The number of items that are left for each kind of tile is held in the table at ItemsLeft.
Wait until it is time to spawn an item.
