53000: Draw View
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Draws the view of the village in the pixel buffer, as seen from the player. The view is made of cells of the map, drawn from the furthest to the nearest, with the objects that are in each cell drawn after the cell.
The cells around the player are drawn in an order that depends on which of the five cells beside the player's cell are not empty. A pattern of five bits is made from them and used to find an entry in the table at ViewPatterns, which is made of five bytes for each pattern. Each byte gives one cell to draw: bits 0 and 1 choose what to draw (0 for only the objects in the cell, 1 for the cell with its walls, 2 for the cell); bits 4 and 5 give the column (0 for one back, 1 for one on, 2 or 3 for the same one); and bits 6 and 7 give the row in the same way.
