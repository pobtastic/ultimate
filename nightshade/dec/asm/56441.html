56441: Slow Player Down
Slows the player down when no control is used to move. The distance to the next position that is a multiple of eight units is found, and the speed is the distance (up to four units).
SlowPlayerDown 56441 LD A,(IX+3) Fetch the Y position of the player (moving up the screen).
SlowPlayerDown_Distance 56444 AND 7 Stop the player if the position is a multiple of eight units.
56446 JR Z,SlowPlayerDown_Stop
56448 NEG Work out the distance to the next multiple of eight units.
56450 ADD A,8
56452 LD C,4 Set the greatest speed to four.
56454 CP C Jump to SlowPlayerDown_Speed if the distance is four or more.
56455 JR NC,SlowPlayerDown_Speed
56457 SRL C Halve the greatest speed, and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Speed if the distance is two or more.
56459 CP C
56460 JR NC,SlowPlayerDown_Speed
56462 LD C,A Use the distance as the speed.
SlowPlayerDown_Speed 56463 LD A,C Set the speed and move the player.
56464 JR MovePlayer_Speed
SlowPlayerDown_Right 56466 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the X position of the player (moving right), and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Distance.
56469 JR SlowPlayerDown_Distance
SlowPlayerDown_Down 56471 LD A,(IX+3) Fetch the Y position of the player (moving down the screen).
SlowPlayerDown_Negate 56474 NEG Negate the position and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Distance.
56476 JR SlowPlayerDown_Distance
SlowPlayerDown_Left 56478 LD A,(IX+1) Fetch the X position of the player (moving left), and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Negate.
56481 JR SlowPlayerDown_Negate
SlowPlayerDown_Stop 56483 LD (IX+5),0 Set the speed of the player to zero and return.
56487 RET
