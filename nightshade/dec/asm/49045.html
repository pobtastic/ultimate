49045: Spawn Ghost
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Creates a ghost at the player's position every 256 frames. The ghost is placed in the first of the six short-lived objects at ObjectTable_Transient that is not close to the player, at a random position within the player's cell. The ghost becomes a burst if it appears on top of the player.
SpawnGhost 49045 LD A,(48050) Return unless the frame counter has wrapped round to a multiple of 256, so that a ghost appears every 256 frames.
