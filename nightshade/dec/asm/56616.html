56616: Update Direction
Used by the routines at UpdateCreature and UpdateMonster.
Changes the direction that an object is facing when the timer for the direction has run out (the lowest five bits of the direction and timer) or the object has hit something. The object turns a quarter to the left or to the right, chosen from the random seed, or, if it has been flagged as chasing the player (bit 5 of the flags), turns towards the player. A new timer is set.
