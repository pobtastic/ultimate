55715: Update Village Item
The routine for objects of type 48 to 63, the items that appear in the village (see SpawnItem). An item wanders about at random while the player stays in the cell that it appeared in, and is picked up when the player touches it. It turns into a burst, and goes back to the items that are available, when the player has gone from the cell or is dead.
