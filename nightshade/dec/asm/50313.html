50313: Pick Up Item
Used by the routines at UpdateMonsterItem and UpdateVillageItem.
Puts the object at IX into the first free place in the inventory (the eleven bytes from Inventory), removes the object from the village and draws the item in the inventory tube. Nothing happens if the inventory is full.
The number of the item is the type of the object minus three, or, for objects of type 48 and above, the kind of object (bits 2 and 3 of the type) plus five.

IX Object
