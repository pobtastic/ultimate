56921: Test Move
Used by the routines at UpdateCreature, UpdateFragment, UpdateShot, UpdateThrownItem, UpdateMonster, SpeedPlayerUp and SetDirectionFromVelocity.
Tests whether an object can move in the direction that it is facing, by testing the cells that its front edge touches (including the cells that the velocity takes it into) for walls. A wall changes the velocity so that the object stops at the wall, and sets the flag for a collision (bit 0 of the flags).
