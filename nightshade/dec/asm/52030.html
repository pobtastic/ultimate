52030: Print Character Block
Used by the routines at DrawCompassIcon, DrawPlayingAreaBorder, DrawFrame, DrawHorizontalEdge, DrawVerticalEdge and DrawLifeIcon.
Prints a block of four characters, in two rows of two, with the same attribute.
Input
A' Attribute
DE Address of the four characters
HL Screen address of the top left character
Output
DE Address after the four characters
