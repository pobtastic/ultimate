55991: Throw Item
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerLegs.
Throws the last item that was picked up, if the fire button has been pressed and the previous throw has finished. An item that belongs to a monster (items 1 to 4) is dropped into the object for the monster. Any other item becomes a shot, in one of the two objects from ObjectTable_Shots; if both are busy, the one that was not used last is turned into a burst.

See No Throw Delay.
