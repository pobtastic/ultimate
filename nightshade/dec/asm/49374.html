49374: Destroy Creature
Destroys the creature: the creature and the shot both turn into bursts, and 37 hundred points are added to the score.
HitOutcome_Destroy 49374 LD (IX+0),12 Turn the creature into a burst.
49378 LD BC,37 Set the points for destroying a creature.
49381 CALL AddToScore Add the points to the score.
This entry point is used by the routines at HitOutcome_Change and HitOutcome_Spore.
HitOutcome_BurstShot 49384 LD (IY+0),12 Turn the shot into a burst.
49388 RET Return.
