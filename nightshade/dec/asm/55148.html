55148: Spawn Bonus
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Makes a bonus appear near the player, in the object at ObjectTable_Bonus, if there is not one already. The bonus is a speed bonus (type 2) or a health bonus (type 3), depending on the frame counter. It is placed in a cell that is not solid and is at least two cells from the player, at a random place within the cell.
