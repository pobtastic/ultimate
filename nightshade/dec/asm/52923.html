52923: Hurt Player
Used by the routines at UpdateGhost and UpdateCreature.
Turns the object that has touched the player into a burst, and takes one point of health from the player. When the player has no health left, the player dies.
Input
IX Object that touched the player
HurtPlayer 52923 LD (IX+0),12 Turn the object that touched the player into a burst.
See Infinite Health.
52927 LD HL,48115 Take one point of health from the player, and return if there is some left.
52930 DEC (HL)
52931 RET NZ
