58682: Calculate Buffer Address
Used by the routines at DrawStripGraphic, DrawWallStrip and DrawSprite.
Works out the address in the screen buffer of a position in the window. The buffer is 24 bytes wide.
Input
H Y position (from 72 at the first row of the buffer)
L X position
Output
HL Address in the screen buffer
Work out the column and 24 times the row.
CalculateBufferAddress 58682 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
58683 SRL L Divide the X position by eight.
58685 SRL L
58687 SRL L

58690 LD L,H

58695 ADD HL,HL
58696 LD C,L Keep the result.
58697 LD B,H
58698 ADD HL,HL Multiply by two more, and add it to get 24 times the Y position.
58699 ADD HL,BC
Add the column and the address of the buffer.

58701 LD B,0
