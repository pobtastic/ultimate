53754: Draw Strips Left To Right
Used by the routine at DrawCell.
Draws a list of sixteen strips along the edge of a cell from left to right: eight strips going up and to the right, and then eight going down and to the right, which are mirrored.
Input
A Number of the list
DrawStripsLeftToRight 53754 LD BC,25252 Find the list of strips.
53757 CALL GetTableEntry
53760 PUSH HL Use the list of strips.
53761 POP IY
53763 XOR A Clear the flag for the strips to be mirrored.
53764 LD (48114),A
53767 LD BC,2048 Set a counter for eight strips, and the variant of the strips.
DrawStripsLeftToRight_Up 53770 CALL DrawStrip Draw a strip and step up and to the right, until eight strips have been drawn.
53773 CALL StepUpRightInList
53776 DJNZ DrawStripsLeftToRight_Up
53778 LD HL,(48056) Move the position of the strips down one step.
53781 LD DE,8
53784 ADD HL,DE
53785 LD (48056),HL
53788 LD A,1 Set the flag for the strips to be mirrored.
53790 LD (48114),A
53793 LD BC,2048 Set a counter for eight strips, and the variant of the strips.
DrawStripsLeftToRight_Down 53796 CALL DrawStrip Draw a strip and step down and to the right, until eight more strips have been drawn.
53799 CALL StepDownRightInList
53802 DJNZ DrawStripsLeftToRight_Down
53804 RET Return.
