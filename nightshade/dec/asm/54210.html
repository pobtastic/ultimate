54210: Draw Wall Strips
Used by the routine at DrawCellWithWalls.
Draws two wall strips, one above the other, for a step along the edge of a cell. A bit in DrawnColumns is set to record that the column has a wall in it, and if it already has one the step is skipped. The horizontal position in ScreenXPosition chooses the bit, and is written into the BIT instruction at DrawWallStrips_Test and the SET instruction at 54246. The lower strip is drawn at ScreenYPosition by DrawWallStrip, the column is coloured by ColourWallColumn, and the upper strip 64 above it. The step is skipped if either position is off the screen. It always moves on to the next two strips of the list.
Input
BC The count of steps in B
IY The address of the first strip of the step in its list
Return unless the column is on the screen.
