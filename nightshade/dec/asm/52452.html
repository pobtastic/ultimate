52452: Ending Pieces
The ten objects of the ending, used by GameOver. Each has a type, and the X and Y screen position that is stored in the fields at offsets 10 and 11.
The first five are the pieces of the title graphic in a row, then four ghosts, then the large piece of the title graphic.
