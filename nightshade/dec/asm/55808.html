55808: Update Player Body
Used by the routine at UpdateBumpingBody.
The routine for objects of type 32 to 47 the body of the player. The body follows the legs (the object before it), and has the same sprite number as the legs. A sound is played when the legs bump into something, and the body then changes to the bumping pose. When the player is standing still, the body occasionally looks around.
