50396: Colour Inventory Items
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Sets the attributes of the items in the inventory tube. Each item is a block of two by two attributes. Items 1 to 4 flash in the colours of items 5 to 8 when the player is within five cells of the monster that they belong to.
Colour each place in the inventory tube.
