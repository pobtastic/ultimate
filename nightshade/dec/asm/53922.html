53922: Clip Edge Strip Top
Used by the routine at DrawStripGraphic.
Skips the rows of a strip that are below PixelBuffer. A is the number of those rows, negated. It starts drawing at 72 and jumps to DrawStripGraphic_Draw.
Input

HL The address of the strip
