57885: Fill Buffer
Used by the routine at CopyBuffersToScreen.
Fills a buffer with a word, by pushing it onto the stack. The buffer is filled downwards from the address in HL.
Input
B Number of rows of 24 bytes
DE Word to fill the buffer with
HL Address after the end of the buffer
Output
HL Address of the start of the buffer
Keep the stack pointer, and point it at the end of the buffer.
