50176: Play Movement Sound
Used by the routine at SpeedPlayerUp.
Plays a short tick, which is alternately high and low, once every few frames. The ticks are made twice as often when the object is moving quickly.
Input
IX Object
PlayMovementSound 50176 LD HL,48120 Point at the counter of steps.
50179 INC (HL) Count a step.
50180 LD A,(IX+5) Fetch the speed of the object.
50183 CP 12 Jump to PlayMovementSound_Fast if the object is moving quickly.
50185 JR NC,PlayMovementSound_Fast
50187 LD A,(HL) Jump to PlayMovementSound_Low to make the low tick unless bit 2 of the step counter is set.
50188 BIT 2,A
50190 JR Z,PlayMovementSound_Low
50192 AND 3 Return unless the step counter is a multiple of four, otherwise play the high tick.
50194 RET NZ
50195 LD BC,16388
50198 JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_Low 50200 AND 3 Return unless the step counter is a multiple of four, otherwise play the low tick.
50202 RET NZ
50203 LD BC,24580
50206 JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_Fast 50208 LD A,(HL) Jump to PlayMovementSound_FastLow to make the low tick unless bit 1 of the step counter is set.
50209 BIT 1,A
50211 JR Z,PlayMovementSound_FastLow
50213 AND 1 Return unless the step counter is even, otherwise play the high tick.
50215 RET NZ
50216 LD BC,16388
50219 JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_FastLow 50221 AND 1 Return unless the step counter is even, otherwise play the low tick.
50223 RET NZ
50224 LD BC,24580
50227 JR PlayTone
