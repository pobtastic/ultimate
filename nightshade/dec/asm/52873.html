52873: Update Spore
The routine for objects of type 64 to 79, the spores. A spore wanders about at random, and vanishes when the player is more than three cells away. If a shot hits it, both the spore and the shot burst. If the player touches it, the spore bursts and the player is hurt. Either way, 5 hundred points are added to the score.
Wander, then flip the sprite if the view is turned.
