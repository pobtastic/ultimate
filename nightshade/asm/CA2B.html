CA2B: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Prints the eight lines of the game selection menu. The first time that it is called it also draws the frame around the screen.
DisplayMenu CA2B LD DE,$C962 Point at the attributes of the lines of the menu.
CA2E EXX Swap to the shadow registers to keep the pointer to the attributes.
CA2F LD HL,$C96A Point at the positions of the lines and at the text of the lines, and set a counter for eight lines.
CA32 LD DE,$C97A
CA35 LD B,$08
DisplayMenu_Loop CA37 LD A,(HL) Fetch the position of the line.
CA38 INC HL
CA39 INC HL
CA3A PUSH HL Keep the pointer to the positions, and put the position of the line into HL.
CA3B DEC HL
CA3C LD H,(HL)
CA3D LD L,A
CA3E EXX Swap to the shadow registers.
CA3F LD A,(DE) Fetch the attribute of the line, and keep it for printing.
CA40 INC DE
CA41 EX AF,AF'
CA42 EXX Swap back to the main registers.
CA43 CALL PrintString Print the line.
CA46 POP HL Get the pointer to the positions back, and loop back until all eight lines have been printed.
CA47 DJNZ DisplayMenu_Loop
CA49 LD A,($BBBF) Return if the frame around the screen has been drawn.
CA4C AND A
CA4D RET NZ
CA4E INC A Flag that the frame has been drawn.
CA4F LD ($BBBF),A
CA52 JP DrawFrame Jump to DrawFrame to draw the frame.
