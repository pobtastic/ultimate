C471: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayBumpSound, PlaySweepSound and PlayMaterialiseSound.
Plays a single cycle of a tone on the speaker.
Input
B Pitch
PlaySquareWave C471 LD A,$10 Turn the speaker on.
C473 OUT ($FE),A
C475 LD A,B
PlaySquareWave_High C476 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_High Wait, to make the first half of the cycle.
C478 LD B,A Turn the speaker off.
C479 XOR A
C47A OUT ($FE),A
C47C LD A,B
PlaySquareWave_Low C47D DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Low Wait, to make the second half of the cycle.
C47F LD B,A Get the pitch back and return.
C480 RET
