CA1B: Set Flashing Attribute
Used by the routine at FlashMenuOptions.
Sets bit 7 (flash) of the attribute of one of a number of lines of the menu, and resets it in the other lines.
Input
A Number of the line to flash
B Number of lines
HL Address of the attribute of the first line
SetFlashingAttribute CA1B AND A Jump to FlashChosenLine_NoFlash unless this is the line to flash.
CA1C JR NZ,FlashChosenLine_NoFlash
FlashChosenLine_Flash CA1E SET 7,(HL) Turn on flashing for the line, and jump to FlashChosenLine_Skip.
CA20 JR FlashChosenLine_Skip
FlashChosenLine_Next CA22 DEC A Count down the lines, and jump to FlashChosenLine_Flash when the line to flash is reached.
CA23 JR Z,FlashChosenLine_Flash
FlashChosenLine_NoFlash CA25 RES 7,(HL) Turn off flashing for the line.
FlashChosenLine_Skip CA27 INC HL Move on to the next line, and loop back until all of the lines have been done.
CA28 DJNZ FlashChosenLine_Next
CA2A RET Return.
