DB89: Choose Direction
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerLegs.
Works out the direction that the player is facing from the controls, which is held in bits 6 and 7 of the direction and timer of the player (0 is up the screen, 1 is right, 2 is down and 3 is left). A turn takes a few frames, which are counted in the lowest bits of the timer.
With the keyboard or when the option for directional control is not set, the controls for left and right turn the player. When it is set, the player turns towards the direction that the controls give, one quarter at a time, and moves when it faces that way.
Input
E Controls
IX Player
Output
E Controls, with bit 2 set only if the player is to move forward
Jump to SetFacingFlags while a turn is still counting.
