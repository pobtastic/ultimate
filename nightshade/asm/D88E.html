D88E: Place Items
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Places the four items that the monsters need (type 04 to 07) in random cells of the village that are not solid, in the objects from ObjectTable_MonsterItems. The cells come from bytes of the ROM, starting from a random place.
PlaceItems D88E CALL IncrementFrameCounter Increase the frame counter, and update the random number generator.
D891 LD IY,$BD1E Point IY at the first object, and set a counter of four items.
D895 LD B,$04
D897 LD DE,($BBAA) Make a random address in the first 1000 bytes of the ROM.
D89B LD A,D
D89C AND $0F
D89E LD D,A
PlaceItems_Cell D89F LD A,(DE) Fetch a column from the ROM.

D8A3 LD L,A

D8B6 PUSH DE
D8B7 PUSH HL

D8BB LD BC,$0010
D8BE PUSH IY

D8D1 LD (IY-$0C),H
