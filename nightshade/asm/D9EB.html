D9EB: Update Bumping Body
The routine for objects of type 16 and 1E, which is the body of the player when the legs have bumped into something. The body stays in this pose while the legs are flagged as having bumped into something, and goes back to normal otherwise.
UpdateBumpingBody D9EB LD A,(IX-$09) Update the body as normal (see UpdatePlayerBody) if the legs have not bumped into something.
D9EE BIT 0,A
D9F0 JR Z,UpdatePlayerBody
D9F2 LD (IX+$07),A Copy the flags of the legs.
D9F5 LD A,(IX-$10) Set the type to the bumping pose (sprite 06) for the kind of legs.
D9F8 AND $F8
D9FA OR $06
D9FC LD (IX+$00),A
D9FF RET Return.
