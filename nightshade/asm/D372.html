D372: Draw Cell With Walls
Used by the routine at DrawView.
Draws the walls of a building in a cell of the village, using the lists of strips in StripListTable. The list is chosen from the tile of the cell and ViewRotatedFlag. Nothing is drawn if the cell is empty. A list holds sixteen steps of two strips each, one above the other. They are drawn in two runs of eight, going up and to the right and then down and to the right, with the second run flipped by MirrorFlag, to make the two walls which can be seen. Each step is drawn by DrawWallStrips, and nothing more is drawn when all of the bits in DrawnColumns are set.
