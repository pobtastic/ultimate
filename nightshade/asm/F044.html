F044: Attribute Buffer
The buffer that the attributes of the playing area are made in before they are copied to the screen. It is 18 bytes wide and 0E rows high.
AttributeBuffer F044 DEFS $17
AttributeBuffer_FirstRowEnd F05B DEFS $0139
