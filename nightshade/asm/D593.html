D593: Jump To Table Routine
Used by the routines at StartGame, UpdateCreature, DrawObjectsInList, MovePlayer, UpdateDirection and TestMove.
Calls GetTableEntry to fetch a routine from a table, then jumps to IndirectJump so that the routine returns to the caller of this one.
Input
A The number of the entry
BC The address of the table
Fetch the address and jump to it.
JumpToTableRoutine D593 CALL GetTableEntry Call GetTableEntry.
D596 JP IndirectJump Jump to IndirectJump.
