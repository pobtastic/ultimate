D7D8: Update Burst
The routine for objects of type 0C to 0F, the bursts that show an object being destroyed. The burst changes to the next sprite, and the object is removed after the fourth sprite and a random sound is played.
UpdateBurst D7D8 INC (IX+$00) Change to the next sprite, and jump to UpdateBurst_Next unless that was the last one.
D7DB LD A,(IX+$00)
D7DE AND $03
D7E0 JR NZ,UpdateBurst_Next
D7E2 LD (IX+$00),A Remove the burst, and play a random sound.
D7E5 JP PlayRandomSound
UpdateBurst_Next D7E8 RES 1,(IX+$07) Clear the flag that shows that the burst has been drawn, and return.
D7EC RET
