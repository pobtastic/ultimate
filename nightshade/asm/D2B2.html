D2B2: Draw Strip Graphic
Used by the routine at DrawStrip.
Draws a strip which is sixteen pixels wide into PixelBuffer, combining it with what is already there. The strip starts with a byte for the number of rows, followed by two bytes for each row. It is drawn from the bottom row up, and is cut off at the bottom and top of the buffer. The position is in ScreenXPosition and the strip is moved to the right by 00, 02, 04 or 06 pixels, using ShiftTables and ReversedShiftTables. The strip is flipped if MirrorFlag is not zero.
After DrawStripGraphic
