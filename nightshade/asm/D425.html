D425: Draw Wall Strip
Used by the routine at DrawWallStrips.
Draws a wall strip, sixteen pixels wide, into PixelBuffer. The graphic has the same format as DrawStripGraphic, but it replaces the buffer. The masks from WallStripMasks are written into the AND instructions. The graphic is flipped if MirrorFlag is not zero.
Input
D The vertical position of the bottom of the strip
E The horizontal position of the strip
IY The address of the strip number
Find the graphic and clip it to the buffer.

D42B CALL GetTableEntry Call GetTableEntry.
D42E LD A,D Subtract 48 from the vertical position.

D438 CP (HL) Compare it with the height of the graphic.
