CD10: Update Logo Piece
The routine for objects of type 98 to 9D, the pieces of the title graphic that are shown during the ending. Each piece stays where it was placed. It is drawn, and its attributes are set to bright white.
The large piece (type 98) is the last piece in the object table, and waits for the ghosts before it: when the object before it has gone, the ending is over and the game starts again.
UpdateLogoPiece CD10 LD A,(IX-$10) Jump to UpdateLogoPiece_Finished if the object before this one has gone, which means the ending is over.
CD13 AND A
CD14 JR Z,UpdateLogoPiece_Finished
The smaller pieces start here, as they do not wait.
UpdateLogoPiece_Small CD16 LD A,$47 Use bright white ink (attribute 47).
