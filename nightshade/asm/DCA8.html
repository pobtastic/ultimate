DCA8: Speed Player Up
Used by the routine at MovePlayer.
Raises the speed of the player half of the way towards the maximum speed, keeping it even, and then moves the player.
SpeedPlayerUp DCA8 LD A,($BBBE) Work out the new speed, which is the average of the current speed and the maximum speed (MaximumSpeed), made even.
