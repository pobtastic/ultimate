C1FD: Draw Monster Icons
Used by the routines at StartGame and UpdateThrownItem.
Draws the icons of the four monsters in the status panel. The icon of a monster that is still alive is drawn as a plain shape. The icon of a monster that has been killed is drawn with all of its detail, in the colour of the monster.
