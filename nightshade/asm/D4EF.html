D4EF: Wall Strip Masks
The four masks used by DrawWallStrip to keep the bits of the buffer on the left of a wall strip. The index is the shift to the right in twos of pixels: 00, 02, 04 or 06.
WallStripMasks D4EF DEFB $00 Shift 00: 00.
D4F0 DEFB $C0 Shift 02: C0.
D4F1 DEFB $F0 Shift 04: F0.
D4F2 DEFB $FC Shift 06: FC.
