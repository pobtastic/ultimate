5E04: Map
The map of the village. It is 20 cells wide and 20 cells high, and each byte holds the tile that is in one cell, which is a number from 00 to 23. Tile 00 is open ground, and tile 01 is the solid tile that fills the edge of the village and much of the rest of it. The other tiles are the trees, graves, walls and other things that stand in the village. The cell is found from the column and the row by FindMapTile, and the tile decides how it is drawn (see StripListTable) and what the player can walk through (see RectangleListTable). The whole village is shown on the Map page.
When the view is turned round, the map is read backwards from the end.
