E5B0: Clear Attributes
Used by the routine at ClearScreen.
Sets the attributes of the screen to white ink on black paper.
ClearAttributes E5B0 LD HL,$5800 Point at 5800 (in the attribute buffer), and count the bytes.
E5B3 LD BC,$0300
E5B6 LD E,$47 Use the attribute for white ink on black paper.
E5B8 JR FillMemory_WithValue Jump to FillMemory_WithValue to fill them.
