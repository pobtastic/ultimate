CAFD: Draw Horizontal Edge
Used by the routines at DrawPlayingAreaBorder and DrawFrame.
Draws a number of blocks of two by two characters along a row.
Input
A' Attribute
B Number of blocks
DE Address of the four characters in a block
HL Screen address of the first block
DrawHorizontalEdge CAFD PUSH BC Keep the number of blocks and the address of the characters.
CAFE PUSH DE
CAFF CALL PrintCharacterBlock Print a block.
CB02 INC L Move on two positions to the right.
CB03 INC L
CB04 POP DE Get the number of blocks and the address of the characters back.
CB05 POP BC
CB06 DJNZ DrawHorizontalEdge Loop back until all of the blocks have been drawn.
CB08 RET Return.
