C4DC: Colour Inventory Items
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Sets the attributes of the items in the inventory tube. Each item is a block of two by two attributes. Items 01 to 04 flash in the colours of items 05 to 08 when the player is within five cells of the monster that they belong to.
ColourInventoryItems C4DC LD BC,$C52F Point at the table of colours, and at the lowest item at 5AC2 (in the attribute buffer).
C4DF LD HL,$5AC2
C4E2 EXX Swap to the shadow registers, point at the inventory and set a counter for the eleven places in it.
C4E3 LD DE,$BC03
C4E6 LD B,$0B
ColourInventoryItems_Loop C4E8 LD A,(DE) Fetch the item, and return if there are no more items.
C4E9 AND A
C4EA RET Z
C4EB CP $05 Jump to ColourInventoryItems_Monster if the item is one of the four that belong to monsters.
C4ED JR C,ColourInventoryItems_Monster
ColourInventoryItems_Colour C4EF EXX Look up the colour of the item in the table of colours.
C4F0 EX DE,HL
C4F1 LD L,A
C4F2 LD H,$00
C4F4 ADD HL,BC
C4F5 LD A,(HL)
C4F6 EX DE,HL
C4F7 LD (HL),A Colour the top two attributes of the item.
C4F8 INC HL
C4F9 LD (HL),A
C4FA LD DE,$FFDF Move to the row of attributes above.
C4FD ADD HL,DE
C4FE LD (HL),A Colour the bottom two attributes of the item.
C4FF INC HL
C500 LD (HL),A
C501 ADD HL,DE Move up one more row, to the next item.
C502 EXX Swap back to the main registers.
C503 INC DE Move on to the next place in the inventory, and loop back until all eleven have been coloured.
C504 DJNZ ColourInventoryItems_Loop
C506 RET Return.
Items 01 to 04 belong to the monsters. Find out whether the monster is near the player.
ColourInventoryItems_Monster C507 PUSH AF Keep the item and the pointer to the inventory.
C508 PUSH DE
C509 CPL Turn the number of the item into the number of the monster, from 03 to 00.
C50A ADD A,$05
C50C ADD A,A Multiply it by sixteen, to get the distance to the object of the monster.
C50D ADD A,A
C50E ADD A,A
C50F ADD A,A
C510 LD E,A Put the distance in DE.
C511 LD D,$00
C513 LD IX,$BD5E Start with the first monster.
C517 ADD IX,DE Add the distance, to point at the right monster.
C519 LD C,$05 Set the distance to 05 cells.
C51B CALL CheckPlayerClose Check whether the monster is close to the player.
C51E JR C,ColourInventoryItems_Flash Jump to ColourInventoryItems_Flash to make the item flash if the player is within five cells of the monster.
C520 POP DE Get the item and the pointer to the inventory back.
C521 POP AF
C522 JR ColourInventoryItems_Colour Jump to ColourInventoryItems_Colour to give the item its usual colour.
ColourInventoryItems_Flash C524 POP DE Get the item and the pointer to the inventory back.
C525 POP AF
C526 LD A,($BBB2) Use the lowest two bits of the frame counter, plus five, as the colour of the item, so that it flashes.
C529 AND $03
C52B ADD A,$05
C52D JR ColourInventoryItems_Colour Jump to ColourInventoryItems_Colour to colour the item.
