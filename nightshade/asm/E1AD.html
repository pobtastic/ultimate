E1AD: Copy Attributes To Screen
Used by the routine at CopyBuffersToScreen.
Copies the attributes of the attribute buffer onto the screen, in the same way as CopyPixelsToScreen. The buffer holds 14 rows of 24 bytes, and the top row of the buffer is the bottom row of the playing area.
CopyAttributesToScreen E1AD LD ($BBAE),SP Save the stack pointer, point it at the end of the last row of attributes of the playing area, count 14 rows, and point HL at the end of the first row of the buffer.
E1B1 LD SP,$59FD

E1B6 LD HL,$F05B
