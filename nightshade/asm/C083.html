C083: Update Creature
The routine for objects of type 70 to 7F the creatures. A creature wanders about the village and hurts the player if it touches. It vanishes when the player is more than three cells away. When a shot hits it, the kind of the shot and the kind of the creature are added together to choose one of four things to happen to it (see HitOutcomeTable).
UpdateCreature C083 LD A,(IX+$05) Set the velocity of the creature from its speed and the way it faces.
C086 CALL SetVelocityFromSpeed
C089 CALL TestMove Check whether the creature has run into the scenery.
C08C BIT 0,(IX+$07) Check whether it did.
C090 CALL NZ,PlayBumpSound Play the bump sound if it did.
C093 RES 1,(IX+$07) Clear the flag that shows that the creature has been drawn, ready for the next frame.
C097 CALL MoveObject Move the creature.
C09A CALL UpdateDirection Turn the creature to a new direction if it has gone far enough in this one.
C09D CALL SetSpriteFromDirection Choose the picture of the creature from the way it faces.
C0A0 LD C,$03 Set the distance at which the creature gives up to 03 cells.
C0A2 CALL CheckPlayerClose Check whether the creature is close to the player.
C0A5 JR NC,UpdateCreature_Vanish Jump to UpdateCreature_Vanish if the player is further away than that.
C0A7 CALL CheckShotHit Check whether a shot has hit the creature.
C0AA JR C,UpdateCreature_Hit Jump to UpdateCreature_Hit if one has.
C0AC CALL CheckPlayerCollision Check whether the creature is touching the player.
C0AF RET NC Return if it is not.
C0B0 LD BC,$0025 Set the points that touching a creature costs.
C0B3 CALL AddToScore Add the points to the score.
C0B6 JP HurtPlayer Hurt the player (see HurtPlayer).
A shot has hit the creature. Add together the kind of the creature and the kind of the shot (its type divided by four) to choose what happens.
UpdateCreature_Hit C0B9 LD A,(IX+$00) Work out the kind of the creature, by dividing its type by four.
C0BC SRL A
C0BE SRL A
C0C0 LD C,A Keep it in C.
C0C1 LD A,(IY+$00) Work out the kind of the shot, by dividing its type by four.
C0C4 SRL A
C0C6 SRL A
C0C8 ADD A,C Add the two kinds together.
C0C9 AND %00000011 Keep the result between 00 and 03.
C0CB LD BC,$C0D1 Point at the table of outcomes.
C0CE JP JumpToTableRoutine Jump to the outcome that was chosen.
