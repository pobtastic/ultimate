C55F: Check Player Collision
Used by the routines at SpawnGhost, UpdateGhost, UpdateCreature, UpdateSpore, UpdateSpeedBonus, UpdateHealthBonus, UpdateMonsterItem, UpdateMonster and UpdateVillageItem.
Checks whether the object at IX is touching the player. Nothing touches the player until the player has finished materialising, or when the player is dead.
Input
IX Object
Output
IY The player
F The carry flag is set if the object is touching the player
