D24B: Step Up Right In List
Used by the routine at DrawStripsLeftToRight.
Moves the position of the strips up eight pixels and right sixteen pixels, and moves on to the next strip in the list.
StepUpRightInList D24B LD DE,$FFF8 Set the step up to eight, and move on to the next strip in the list.
D24E JR StepInList
