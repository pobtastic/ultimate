E3D6: Mirror Sprite
Used by the routine at DrawMonsterIcons.
Turns a sprite round, and flips the flag in the sprite that shows whether it is reversed.
Input
DE Address of the sprite
MirrorSprite E3D6 PUSH DE Keep the address of the sprite, and jump to FlipSprite_Mirror to reverse it.
E3D7 JR FlipSprite_Mirror
