CA9D: Draw Frame
Used by the routines at DisplayMenu and GameOver.
Draws the frame around the whole screen, in yellow on blue.
After DrawFrame
Set the colour and the font.
DrawFrame CA9D LD A,$4E Use bright yellow ink on blue paper (attribute 4E), and keep it in AF'.
CA9F EX AF,AF'
CAA0 LD HL,$BB0A Set the font to the characters of the border.
CAA3 LD ($BBAC),HL
CAA6 LD DE,$CAE5 Point at the characters of the border.
Draw the four corners.
CAA9 LD HL,$4000 Draw the top left corner at 4000 (in the screen buffer).
CAAC CALL PrintCharacterBlock
CAAF LD HL,$401E Draw the top right corner at 401E (in the screen buffer).
CAB2 CALL PrintCharacterBlock
CAB5 LD HL,$50C0 Draw the bottom left corner at 50C0 (in the screen buffer).
CAB8 CALL PrintCharacterBlock
CABB LD HL,$50DE Draw the bottom right corner at 50DE (in the screen buffer).
CABE CALL PrintCharacterBlock
Draw the top and bottom edges.
CAC1 LD HL,$4002 Draw the top edge, fourteen blocks long, at 4002 (in the screen buffer).
CAC4 LD B,$0E
CAC6 CALL DrawHorizontalEdge
CAC9 LD HL,$50C2 Draw the bottom edge, fourteen blocks long, at 50C2 (in the screen buffer).
CACC LD B,$0E
CACE CALL DrawHorizontalEdge
Draw the sides.
CAD1 INC DE Point at the characters of the sides.
CAD2 INC DE
CAD3 INC DE
CAD4 INC DE
CAD5 LD HL,$4040 Draw the left edge, ten blocks long, at 4040 (in the screen buffer).
CAD8 LD B,$0A
CADA CALL DrawVerticalEdge
CADD LD HL,$405E Draw the right edge, ten blocks long, at 405E (in the screen buffer).
CAE0 LD B,$0A
CAE2 JP DrawVerticalEdge
