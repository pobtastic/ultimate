C0DE: Destroy Creature
Destroys the creature: the creature and the shot both turn into bursts, and 25 hundred points are added to the score.
HitOutcome_Destroy C0DE LD (IX+$00),$0C Turn the creature into a burst.
C0E2 LD BC,$0025 Set the points for destroying a creature.
C0E5 CALL AddToScore Add the points to the score.
This entry point is used by the routines at HitOutcome_Change and HitOutcome_Spore.
HitOutcome_BurstShot C0E8 LD (IY+$00),$0C Turn the shot into a burst.
C0EC RET Return.
