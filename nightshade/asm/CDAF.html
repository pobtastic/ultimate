CDAF: Messaging: Game Over Text
The strings that are shown on the game over screen. Each string starts with its attribute.
GameOverText CDAF DEFM $47 Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) + "GAME OVER".
CDB0 DEFM "GAME OVE","R"+$80
CDBA DEFM $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) + "PERCENTAGE OF GAME".
CDBB DEFM "PERCENTAGE OF GAM","E"+$80
CDCD DEFM $45 Attribute: INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) + "COMPLETED =".
CDCE DEFM "COMPLETED ","="+$80
CDD9 DEFM $46 Attribute: INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT) + "SCORE".
CDDA DEFM "SCOR","E"+$80
