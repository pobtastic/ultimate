E59D: Clear Screen
Used by the routines at StartGame and GameOver.
Clears the whole screen to white ink on black paper, and sets the border to black.
Before After ClearScreen
Clear the pixels, the attributes and the border.
ClearScreen E59D CALL ClearPixels Clear the pixels.
E5A0 CALL ClearAttributes Clear the attributes.
E5A3 LD A,$00 Set the border to black.
E5A5 OUT ($FE),A
E5A7 RET Return.
