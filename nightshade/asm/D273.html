D273: Draw Strip
Used by the routines at DrawCell and DrawStripsLeftToRight.
Draws one strip of a cell, which is a graphic that is sixteen pixels wide. A strip is not drawn if its position is outside the view.
Input
C Variant of the strip (0 or 1)
IY Entry in the list of strips
Return if the tile is outside the play area horizontally.
