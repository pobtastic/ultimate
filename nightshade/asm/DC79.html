DC79: Slow Player Down
Slows the player down when no control is used to move. The distance to the next position that is a multiple of eight units is found, and the speed is the distance (up to four units).
SlowPlayerDown DC79 LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the Y position of the player (moving up the screen).
SlowPlayerDown_Distance DC7C AND $07 Stop the player if the position is a multiple of eight units.
DC7E JR Z,SlowPlayerDown_Stop
DC80 NEG Work out the distance to the next multiple of eight units.
DC82 ADD A,$08
DC84 LD C,$04 Set the greatest speed to four.
DC86 CP C Jump to SlowPlayerDown_Speed if the distance is four or more.
DC87 JR NC,SlowPlayerDown_Speed
DC89 SRL C Halve the greatest speed, and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Speed if the distance is two or more.
DC8B CP C
DC8C JR NC,SlowPlayerDown_Speed
DC8E LD C,A Use the distance as the speed.
SlowPlayerDown_Speed DC8F LD A,C Set the speed and move the player.
DC90 JR MovePlayer_Speed
SlowPlayerDown_Right DC92 LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the X position of the player (moving right), and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Distance.
DC95 JR SlowPlayerDown_Distance
SlowPlayerDown_Down DC97 LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the Y position of the player (moving down the screen).
SlowPlayerDown_Negate DC9A NEG Negate the position and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Distance.
DC9C JR SlowPlayerDown_Distance
SlowPlayerDown_Left DC9E LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the X position of the player (moving left), and jump to SlowPlayerDown_Negate.
DCA1 JR SlowPlayerDown_Negate
SlowPlayerDown_Stop DCA3 LD (IX+$05),$00 Set the speed of the player to zero and return.
DCA7 RET
