D8E7: Place Monsters
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Places the four monsters (type 60, 64, 68 and 6C) in random cells of the village that are not solid, in the objects from ObjectTable_Monsters. The cells come from bytes of the ROM, starting from a random place.
Point at the first monster and a random place in the ROM.
