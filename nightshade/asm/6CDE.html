6CDE: Font
The graphics of the characters that are used for the score and the text, from 30 (the digit "0") to 5A (the letter "Z"). The graphic of a character is found by adding eight times its code to the font pointer, which is 6B5E for this font (see PrintString), so the characters with codes below 30 are not part of it.
