C3F4: Play Sweep Sound
Used by the routine at ThrowItem.
Plays a sequence of thirty-two tones.
Input
B Starting pitch
PlaySweepSound C3F4 LD C,$20 Set the number of tones to play to 20.
PlaySweepSound_Loop C3F6 LD A,C Subtract the starting pitch from the number of tones left to work out the pitch of the next tone, play it, and loop back until all 20 tones have been played.
