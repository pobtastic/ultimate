D26E: Step Up Left In List
Used by the routine at DrawCell.
Moves the position of the strips up eight pixels and left sixteen pixels, and moves on to the next strip in the list.
StepUpLeftInList D26E LD DE,$FFF8 Set the step up to eight and move the position.
