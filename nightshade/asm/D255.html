D255: Step Down Left In List
Used by the routine at DrawCell.
Moves the position of the strips down eight pixels and left sixteen pixels, and moves on to the next strip in the list.
StepDownLeftInList D255 LD DE,$0008 Set the step down to eight.
This entry point is used by the routine at StepUpLeftInList.
Move on to the next strip in the list, and move the position by the step (in DE) and left sixteen pixels.
