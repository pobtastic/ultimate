D74C: Update Health Bonus
The routine for objects of type 03 the bonuses that restore the player's health. A bonus vanishes when the player is more than four cells away. When the player touches it, the bonus turns into a burst and the player's health is made full.
UpdateHealthBonus D74C LD C,$04 Set the distance to 04 cells and check whether the bonus is that close to the player.
D74E CALL CheckPlayerClose
D751 JR C,UpdateHealthBonus_Close Jump to UpdateHealthBonus_Close if it is.
D753 LD (IX+$00),$00 Remove the bonus, because the player is too far away, and return.
D757 RET
UpdateHealthBonus_Close D758 CALL CheckPlayerCollision Return if the bonus is not touching the player.
D75B RET NC
D75C LD (IX+$00),$0C Turn the bonus into a burst.
D760 LD A,$03 Give the player full health.
D762 LD ($BBF3),A
D765 LD HL,$0207 Start the health bonus sound, which is sound effect 02 and has seven notes.
D768 LD ($BBF9),HL
D76B RET Return.
