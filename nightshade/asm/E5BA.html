E5BA: Fill Memory
Used by the routines at StartGame and ClearPixels.
Fills memory with a byte. Entering at FillMemory fills it with zero.
Input
BC Number of bytes
E Byte to fill with (when entering at FillMemory_WithValue)
HL Address
FillMemory E5BA LD E,$00 Use zero to fill the memory.
This entry point is used by the routine at ClearAttributes.
FillMemory_WithValue E5BC LD (HL),E Fill the memory, and loop back until all of the bytes have been filled.
E5BD INC HL
E5BE DEC BC
E5BF LD A,B
E5C0 OR C
E5C1 JR NZ,FillMemory_WithValue
E5C3 RET Return.
