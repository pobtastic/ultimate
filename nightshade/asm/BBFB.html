BBFB: Flash Colour
The paper colour (in bits 3 to 5) that the playing area is flashed with on this frame, which is set by UpdateDestroyedMonster when a monster has been destroyed. It is cleared at the end of every frame (see BEDD).
FlashColour BBFB DEFB $FE
