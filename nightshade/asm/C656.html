C656: Play Music
Used by the routines at StartGame, GameMenu, CheckPlayerDead, GameOver and UpdateLogoPiece.
Plays a tune. Each note is a byte, with the note in bits 0 to 5 (00 is a rest) and the length of the note minus one in bits 6 and 7. The tune ends with FF.
The tunes played by this routine are:
Tune Sound
Main Tune
Menu Selection Tune
Start-up Tune
Life Lost Tune
Game Over Tune
Game Complete Tune
Input
DE Address of the tune
PlayMusic C656 LD A,(DE) Fetch the next note, return if it marks the end of the tune, and otherwise play the note and loop back.
C657 CP $FF
C659 JR Z,PlayMusic_Return
C65B CALL PlayNote
C65E JR PlayMusic
This entry point is used by the routine at PlayMusicOnce.
PlayMusic_Return C660 RET Return.
