CEE5: Burst Spore And Shot
Used by the routine at UpdateSpore.
Turns the shot and the spore into bursts, and adds 05 hundred points to the score.
Input
IX Spore
IY Shot
BurstSporeAndShot CEE5 LD (IY+$00),$0C Turn the shot into a burst.
CEE9 LD (IX+$00),$0C Turn the spore into a burst.
This entry point is used by the routine at UpdateSpore.
BurstSporeAndShot_Score CEED LD BC,$0005 Add 05 hundred points to the score.
CEF0 JP AddToScore
This entry point is used by the routine at UpdateSpore.
The spore is too far from the player, so remove it.
BurstSporeAndShot_Remove CEF3 LD (IX+$00),$00 Remove the spore and return.
CEF7 RET
