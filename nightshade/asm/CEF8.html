CEF8: Next Sprite Number
Used by the routines at UpdateGhost, UpdateSpore, UpdateFragment, UpdateShot and UpdateVillageItem.
Moves an object on to the next of its four sprites, by adding one to the lowest two bits of the type and wrapping around.
Input
IX Object
NextSpriteNumber CEF8 LD A,(IX+$00) Add one to the lowest two bits of the type, wrapping round, to choose the next sprite.
CEFB LD C,A
CEFC INC A
CEFD AND $03
CEFF LD B,A
CF00 LD A,C Put them back in the type of the object.
CF01 AND $FC
CF03 OR B
CF04 LD (IX+$00),A
CF07 RET Return.
