DCE0: Set Velocity From Speed
Used by the routines at UpdateCreature, UpdateFragment, UpdateShot, UpdateThrownItem, UpdateMonster and SpeedPlayerUp.
Sets the velocity of an object along the X and Y axes from its speed and the direction that it faces. Only one axis is used, and the velocity is negative for the directions that face down the screen and to the left.
Input
A Speed
IX Object
SetVelocityFromSpeed DCE0 LD H,$00 Use the speed for the velocity along the X axis (bit 6 of the direction is set) in L...
DCE2 LD L,A
DCE3 BIT 6,(IX+$06)
DCE7 JR NZ,SetVelocityFromSpeed_Sign
DCE9 LD H,A ... or the velocity along the Y axis in H.
DCEA LD L,$00
SetVelocityFromSpeed_Sign DCEC BIT 7,(IX+$06) Jump to SetVelocityFromSpeed_Set unless the direction faces down the screen or to the left.
DCF0 JR Z,SetVelocityFromSpeed_Set
DCF2 LD A,L Negate the velocity along both axes.
DCF3 NEG
DCF5 LD L,A
DCF6 LD A,H
DCF7 NEG
DCF9 LD H,A
SetVelocityFromSpeed_Set DCFA LD (IX+$0A),L Set the velocities of the object.
DCFD LD (IX+$0B),H
DD00 RET Return.
