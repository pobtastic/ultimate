7571: Inventory Item Icons
The icons of the items in the inventory tube, with four characters (two by two) for each item, in the order of the characters on the screen: the top left, top right, bottom left and bottom right. The font pointer is set to 7571 to print them (see DrawInventoryItem). The first icon is blank, and the others are the items that are numbered from 01 (see PickUpItem).
