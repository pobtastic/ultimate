DCDC: Player Sprite Table
The flags and the sprite set for each direction of the legs of the player. Bits 6 and 7 are the flags for the sprite (bit 6 is to flip it), and bit 3 is bit 3 of the type of the legs.
PlayerSpriteTable DCDC DEFB $40,$08,$48,$00
