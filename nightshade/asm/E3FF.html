E3FF: Draw Sprite
Used by the routine at UpdateLogoPiece.
Draws the sprite of an object in the screen buffer, with its mask. The sprite is clipped at the top and bottom of the window by its position. A sprite is drawn by popping its bytes off the stack, so the code that does the drawing changes itself to fit the sprite.
Only the rows of a player sprite that the timer at MaterialiseTimer allows are drawn, so the player appears from the bottom to the top while materialising.
The bottom of the buffer is the top of the window, and the rows of a sprite are stored from the bottom upwards, so a sprite starts at its Y position and is drawn towards the top of the screen.
