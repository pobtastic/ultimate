DC60: Move Player
Used by the routine at UpdatePlayerLegs.
Moves the player. When the control to move forward is used, the speed of the player is raised towards the maximum speed in MaximumSpeed. Otherwise the player slows down and stops at a position that is a multiple of eight units from the edge of a cell.
Input
E Controls
IX Player
MovePlayer DC60 BIT 2,E Jump to SpeedPlayerUp if the control to move forward is used.
DC62 JR NZ,SpeedPlayerUp
DC64 LD A,(IX+$06) Fetch the direction of the player.

DC6B LD BC,$DC71 Jump to the routine for the direction, to find the part of the position that is along the way that the player is moving.
