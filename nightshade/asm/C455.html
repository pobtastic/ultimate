C455: Play Object Sound
Used by the routine at UpdateBall.
Plays a tone with a pitch that depends on the type of an object.
Input
IX Object
PlayObjectSound C455 LD A,(IX+$00) Work out the pitch of the tone from the type of the object and set the length of the tone to 06.
