C400: Play Movement Sound
Used by the routine at SpeedPlayerUp.
Plays a short tick, which is alternately high and low, once every few frames. The ticks are made twice as often when the object is moving quickly.
Input
IX Object
PlayMovementSound C400 LD HL,$BBF8 Point at the counter of steps.
C403 INC (HL) Count a step.
C404 LD A,(IX+$05) Fetch the speed of the object.
C407 CP $0C Jump to PlayMovementSound_Fast if the object is moving quickly.
C409 JR NC,PlayMovementSound_Fast
C40B LD A,(HL) Jump to PlayMovementSound_Low to make the low tick unless bit 2 of the step counter is set.
C40C BIT 2,A
C40E JR Z,PlayMovementSound_Low
C410 AND $03 Return unless the step counter is a multiple of four, otherwise play the high tick.
C412 RET NZ
C413 LD BC,$4004
C416 JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_Low C418 AND $03 Return unless the step counter is a multiple of four, otherwise play the low tick.
C41A RET NZ
C41B LD BC,$6004
C41E JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_Fast C420 LD A,(HL) Jump to PlayMovementSound_FastLow to make the low tick unless bit 1 of the step counter is set.
C421 BIT 1,A
C423 JR Z,PlayMovementSound_FastLow
C425 AND $01 Return unless the step counter is even, otherwise play the high tick.
C427 RET NZ
C428 LD BC,$4004
C42B JR PlayTone
PlayMovementSound_FastLow C42D AND $01 Return unless the step counter is even, otherwise play the low tick.
C42F RET NZ
C430 LD BC,$6004
C433 JR PlayTone
