C314: Print BCD Number
Used by the routine at PrintPercentage.
Prints the digits of BCD numbers.
Entering at PrintBCDNumber sets the font to the digits first. Entering at PrintBCDNumber_LowerDigit prints only the lower digit of the first byte.
Input
B Number of bytes
DE Address of the BCD number
HL Screen position of the first digit
PrintBCDNumber C314 PUSH HL Keep the screen position.
C315 LD HL,$6CDE Point at the font of the digits.
C318 LD ($BBAC),HL Set it as the font.
C31B POP HL Get the screen position back.
PrintBCDNumber_Loop C31C LD A,(DE) Fetch a byte of the number and move its upper digit down to the lower four bits.
C31D RRCA
C31E RRCA
C31F RRCA
C320 RRCA
C321 AND %00001111 Keep only the digit.
C323 CALL PrintCharacterWithAttribute Print the upper digit.
C326 INC L Move on to the next screen position.
This entry point is used by the routines at PrintPercentage and PrintScore.
PrintBCDNumber_LowerDigit C327 LD A,(DE) Fetch the byte again and keep only its lower digit.
C328 AND $0F
C32A CALL PrintCharacterWithAttribute Print the lower digit.
C32D INC L Move on to the next screen position.
C32E INC DE Move on to the next byte of the number.
C32F DJNZ PrintBCDNumber_Loop Loop back until all of the bytes have been printed.
C331 RET Return.
