E0FB: Build Shift Tables
Used by the routine at StartGame.
Builds the tables that the sprite drawing routines use to shift graphics sideways, and the table that reverses the bits in a byte.
There are three pairs of 256-byte pages for shifting a byte by two, four and six pixels (the low byte of the shifted result is in the first page of each pair, counting down from FF and the high byte is in the second). FA to FF hold the shifts of the bytes as they are, and F2 to F7 hold the shifts of the bytes with their bits reversed. The page at F900 holds the bytes with their bits reversed.
BuildShiftTables E0FB LD L,$00 Start with the byte 00.
BuildShiftTables_Normal E0FD LD D,$00 Start with the byte as a 16-bit number, and point HL at the first page of the tables.
E0FF LD E,L
E100 LD H,$FF
E102 LD B,$03
BuildShiftTables_NormalLoop E104 SLA E Shift the number left by two pixels, and store it in the next pair of pages.
E106 RL D
E108 SLA E
E10A RL D
E10C LD (HL),E
E10D DEC H
E10E LD (HL),D
E10F DEC H
E110 DJNZ BuildShiftTables_NormalLoop
E112 INC L Move on to the next byte, and loop back until all 256 have been done.
E113 JR NZ,BuildShiftTables_Normal
E115 LD L,$00 Start with the byte 00 again.
BuildShiftTables_Reversed E117 LD D,$00 Count eight bits, and get the byte.
E119 LD B,$08
E11B LD A,L
BuildShiftTables_Reverse E11C RRCA Reverse the bits of the byte.
E11D RL C
E11F DJNZ BuildShiftTables_Reverse
E121 LD E,C Start with the reversed byte as a 16-bit number, and point HL at the first page of the reversed tables.
E122 LD H,$F7
E124 LD B,$03
BuildShiftTables_ReversedLoop E126 SLA E Shift the number left by two pixels, and store it in the next pair of pages.
E128 RL D
E12A SLA E
E12C RL D
E12E LD (HL),E
E12F DEC H
E130 LD (HL),D
E131 DEC H
E132 DJNZ BuildShiftTables_ReversedLoop
E134 INC L Move on to the next byte, and loop back until all 256 have been done.
E135 JR NZ,BuildShiftTables_Reversed
E137 LD HL,$F900 Point HL at ReverseBitsTable.
BuildShiftTables_ReverseTable E13A LD D,L Start with the byte, and count eight bits.
E13B LD B,$08
BuildShiftTables_ReverseBits E13D SRL D Reverse the bits of the byte.
E13F RL E
E141 DJNZ BuildShiftTables_ReverseBits
E143 LD (HL),E Store the reversed byte, and loop back until all 256 have been done.
E144 INC L
E145 JR NZ,BuildShiftTables_ReverseTable
E147 RET Return.
