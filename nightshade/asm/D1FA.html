D1FA: Draw Strips Left To Right
Used by the routine at DrawCell.
Draws a list of sixteen strips along the edge of a cell from left to right: eight strips going up and to the right, and then eight going down and to the right, which are mirrored.
Input
A Number of the list
DrawStripsLeftToRight D1FA LD BC,$62A4 Find the list of strips.
D1FD CALL GetTableEntry
D200 PUSH HL Use the list of strips.
D201 POP IY
D203 XOR A Clear the flag for the strips to be mirrored.
D204 LD ($BBF2),A
D207 LD BC,$0800 Set a counter for eight strips, and the variant of the strips.
DrawStripsLeftToRight_Up D20A CALL DrawStrip Draw a strip and step up and to the right, until eight strips have been drawn.
D20D CALL StepUpRightInList
D210 DJNZ DrawStripsLeftToRight_Up
D212 LD HL,($BBB8) Move the position of the strips down one step.
D215 LD DE,$0008
D218 ADD HL,DE
D219 LD ($BBB8),HL
D21C LD A,$01 Set the flag for the strips to be mirrored.
D21E LD ($BBF2),A
D221 LD BC,$0800 Set a counter for eight strips, and the variant of the strips.
DrawStripsLeftToRight_Down D224 CALL DrawStrip Draw a strip and step down and to the right, until eight more strips have been drawn.
D227 CALL StepDownRightInList
D22A DJNZ DrawStripsLeftToRight_Down
D22C RET Return.
