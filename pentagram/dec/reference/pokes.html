Infinite Lives
POKE 49917,175
Replaces the instruction which would otherwise take a life away when the player is put back at the start. The new instruction clears the sign flag, so the jump to the game over screen is never made.
49914 LD HL,42785
49917 DEC (HL)
49918 JP M,ShowGameOverScreen
To:
49914 LD HL,42785
49917 XOR A
49918 JP M,ShowGameOverScreen
