50853: Move Player North
Adds three to the speed along y, for the player to walk along y away from zero.
Input

MovePlayerNorth 50853 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the speed along y.
50856 ADD A,3 Add three to it.
This entry point is used by the routine at MovePlayerSouth.
