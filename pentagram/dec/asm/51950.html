51950: Find Door
Used by the routine at PlacePlayerAtDoor.
Looks for the door in a particular wall of the room, and puts the player in the doorway in front of it. The doors are the first of the objects that were made for the room, and take two slots each (with their frames), so every other slot is checked, starting at the last, until one is found that does not hold a door (or up to four are checked).
The position that the player is put at is not the same as that of the door, but is the place that the door holds for it, which is 13 further along y than a door that is not mirrored, or 13 back along x than a mirrored one.
Input
C The wall to look in: 0 is the end of x, 1 is the start of x,
N$02 is the end of y and N$03 is the start of y
IX Object record of the player's legs

51964 CP 4 Return if it is not one of the types 6 to 9 of the doors and the frames.
51966 RET NC
51967 LD A,C Fetch the wall to look in.
51968 AND A Jump to FindDoor_EndOfX if it is the end of x.
51969 JR Z,FindDoor_EndOfX

51972 JR Z,FindDoor_StartOfX
51974 DEC A Jump to FindDoor_EndOfY if it is the end of y.
51975 JR Z,FindDoor_EndOfY
51977 LD A,(IY+2) Jump to FindDoor_PlacePlayerBesideDoorX if the door is at the start of y, which is where the y position of the object is less than 64.
51980 CP 64
51982 JR C,FindDoor_PlacePlayerBesideDoorX
FindDoor_Next 51984 ADD IY,DE Move on to the next door.
51986 DJNZ FindDoor_Loop Jump to FindDoor_Loop until all of the doors have been checked.
51988 RET Return.
FindDoor_PlacePlayerBesideDoorY 51989 LD A,(IY+2) Set the y position of the player to be 13 further along than the door.
51992 ADD A,13

52017 JR FindDoor_PlacePlayerAtDoorHeight Jump to FindDoor_PlacePlayerAtDoorHeight to set the height.
FindDoor_EndOfX 52019 LD A,(IY+1) Fetch the x position of the object.
52022 CP 192 Jump to FindDoor_PlacePlayerBesideDoorY if the door is at the end of x.
