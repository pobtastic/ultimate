48308: Display Menu
Used by the routine at GameMenu.
Prints the seven lines of the menu, each in its own colour and position. The first time that this is called the frame is drawn as well and the whole buffer is copied to the screen: after that only the attributes change, as the line that flashes is changed by writing them straight to the screen.
Point at the menu colours, positions and text.
DisplayMenu 48308 LD DE,48113 Point DE at MenuColours.
48311 EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
48312 LD HL,48120 Point HL at MenuPositions.
48315 LD DE,48134 Point DE at MenuText.
48318 LD B,7 Set the number of lines to 7.
This entry point is used by the routines at ShowVictoryScreen and ShowGameOverScreen.
Print one line from the three lists.
