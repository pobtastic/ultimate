46829: Block Movement
Used by the routines at ApplyGravityAndMove and MovePlayer.
Stops an object moving through the walls of the room or through other objects. The speeds along x, y and z of the object are each brought closer to zero until the object can move without hitting anything. Whichever direction is hit is flagged in the low bits of the collision flags: bit 0 for x, bit 1 for y and bit 2 for z. For z that means the object is standing on something. An object that is flagged to be ignored is not adjusted.
Input
IX The address of the object
