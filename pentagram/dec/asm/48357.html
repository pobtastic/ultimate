48357: Draw Frame
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the frame around the room into the buffer, from the pieces whose data starts at FramePieces. The frame is made of runs of the same piece, drawn one after another, each a fixed distance from the last. The temporary object at TemporaryObject is used to draw them.
