48473: Print Border
Used by the routines at DisplayMenu and ShowGameOverScreen.
Draws the frame around the title screen, and around the end screens, into the buffer, from the pieces whose data starts at MenuFramePieces. It is made of four corners, two long edges, and two shorter ones, each drawn as a run of pieces.
