49446: Fire Projectile
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Fires a projectile in the direction that the player is facing, if the fire key is pressed and it was not already being held down from before. There are two object records that are used for projectiles, so nothing is done if both are in use.
The projectile is a copy of the player that is changed into a projectile and moved on by twice its speed along the way that the player is facing. It is thrown away at once if it starts outside the room.
Input
IX Record of the object of the legs of the player

Copy the player to the free record, then change the copy into a projectile.
