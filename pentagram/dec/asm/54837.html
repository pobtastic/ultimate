54837: Play Footstep Sound
Used by the routine at StepLegs.
Makes the sound of a footstep, on every fourth call, which alternates between two pitches.
Play a tone on every fourth footstep.
PlayFootstepSound 54837 LD HL,42824 Take the count of the footsteps, FootstepCounter.
54840 INC (HL) Add one to it.
54841 LD A,(HL) Fetch it.
54842 BIT 2,A Jump to PlayFootstepSound_Other if bit 2 of the count is not set.
54844 JR Z,PlayFootstepSound_Other
54846 AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
54848 RET NZ Return if the count is not a multiple of four.
54849 LD BC,16386 Set the pitch to 64 and the length to 2.
54852 JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to make the tone.
Play the other pitch on the other fourth steps.
PlayFootstepSound_Other 54854 AND %00000011 Keep the lowest two bits.
54856 RET NZ Return if the count is not a multiple of four.
54857 LD BC,24578 Set the pitch to 96 and the length to 2.
54860 JR PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to make the tone.
