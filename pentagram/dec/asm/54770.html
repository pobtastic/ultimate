54770: Play Sound Effect
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Plays one note of the sound effect that is in progress, which is called once each frame. SoundEffectCounter is the number of notes left, and SoundEffectType picks the sequence of pitches from the table at SoundEffectSequences. The notes are played from the last of the sequence to the first. There is only a sound effect for entering a room, with a sequence of four notes, and one for the player picking up or dropping an object, with five. The sequences that the table has for the numbers 2 and 3 are never used.
Return if no notes are left.
