47988: Game Selection Menu
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Shows the title screen with the menu of control methods, and waits for the player to choose a method and start the game. The menu text is drawn again on every pass, so that the line that is chosen flashes. A tune is played while the menu is on the screen.
Keys 49 to 52 choose the keyboard, a Kempston joystick, a cursor joystick or an Interface II joystick, and key 48 starts the game. The choice is held in ControlMethod.
Clear the buffer and draw the menu.
