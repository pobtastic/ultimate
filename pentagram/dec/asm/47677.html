47677: Display Objects Carried
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Draws the three icons of the inventory in the bottom left of the screen. The icons are drawn into the buffer, which is copied to the screen, and the colours of the icons are put in the attributes. The temporary object at TemporaryObject is used to draw them.
Set up for the three entries of the inventory.

47679 LD IX,47818 Use the temporary object, TemporaryObject.
47683 LD B,3 Set a counter in B of three entries.
47685 LD HL,42790 Start with the first carried object, ObjectsCarried_Newest.
Work out the x co-ordinate of the icon, which is 16 plus 24 for each icon so far, and clear its place in the buffer.
