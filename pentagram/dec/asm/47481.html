47481: Apply Gravity And Move
Used by the routines at DropObject, RunProjectile, RunHomingCreature, RunFallingObject, RunBobbingBlock, RunWanderingSpark, RunHelperSource, RunWanderer and RunFourFrameWanderer.
Applies gravity to an object, moves it by its speeds (limited to where it is able to go by BlockMovement, which changes the speeds), and updates its position.
Input
IX Object record
ApplyGravityAndMove 47481 DEC (IX+11) Reduce the speed along z by one, which is gravity.
