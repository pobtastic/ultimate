47668: Check Inventory
Used by the routine at DrawChangedObjects.
Redraws the inventory if the flag to say that it has changed is set, and clears the flag. Otherwise returns.
CheckInventory 47668 LD A,(42784) Fetch the flag from ObjectsCarriedChangedFlag.
47671 AND A Return if the inventory has not changed.
47672 RET Z
47673 XOR A Clear A.
47674 LD (42784),A Write 0 to ObjectsCarriedChangedFlag, as the inventory is about to be redrawn.
