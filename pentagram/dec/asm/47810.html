47810: Inventory Colours
The attribute used for an inventory icon, which is chosen by the lowest three bits of the type of the object. They are bright blue, red, magenta, green, cyan, yellow and white ink on a black paper (twice for white).
InventoryColours 47810 DEFB 65 INK:BLUE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47811 DEFB 66 INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47812 DEFB 67 INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47813 DEFB 68 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47814 DEFB 69 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47815 DEFB 70 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
47816 DEFB 71 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
