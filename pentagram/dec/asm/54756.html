54756: Play Jump Sound
Used by the routine at HandleJump.
Makes the rattling noise of a jump, from sixteen single cycles of a tone whose pitch jumps about.
Play sixteen cycles of a rattling tone.
PlayJumpSound 54756 LD C,16 Set the number of cycles to 16.
PlayJumpSound_Loop 54758 LD A,C Fetch the number of cycles left.
54759 XOR %10100101 Scramble it.
54761 ADD A,C Add the number of cycles left again, to make the pitch.
54762 LD B,A Put the pitch in B.
54763 CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave to make a cycle of the tone.
54766 DEC C Count the cycle.
54767 JR NZ,PlayJumpSound_Loop Jump to PlayJumpSound_Loop until all of the cycles have been made.
54769 RET Return.
