54805: Sound Effect Pitches
The pitches of the notes of the sound effects, for PlaySoundEffect. The delay, which gets longer as the pitch gets lower.
SoundEffectPitches 54805 DEFB 64 The byte before the first sequence, which is never used.
54806 DEFB 112,64,112,80 The new room sequence, played from the end.
54810 DEFB 112,96,80,64,64 The pick up or drop sequence, played from the end.
54815 DEFB 80,96,112,128,128,128,48,96 Never used.
54823 DEFB 48,96
