50022: Messaging: Game Over Text
The three lines of text of the game over screen. The last character of each has bit 7 set. The percentage is printed under "COMPLETED".

50031 DEFM "PERCENTAGE OF QUES","T"+128 "PERCENTAGE OF QUEST"
