52446: Set Wipe And Draw Flags
Used by the routines at SetWipeAndDrawFlagsForObject, DropObject, RunExplosion, RunProjectile, RunDangerousMarked, HandlePlayerLegs, FinishObjectMove, RunLift, RunBobbingBlock, RunSliderX, RunSliderY, RunWanderingSpark, RunCollectableObject, RunHelperSource, RunWanderer, RunFourFrameWanderer and RunCrumblingBlock.
Flags an object as moved and in need of drawing, and flags the other objects that its movement affects so that they are drawn too.
Input
IX Object record
SetWipeAndDrawFlags 52446 LD A,(IX+7) Fetch the flags of the object.
52449 OR %00110000 Set bit 4 (needs drawing) and bit 5 (moved).
52451 LD (IX+7),A Store the flags.
52454 JP FlagOverlappingObjects Jump to FlagOverlappingObjects to mark the objects that overlap it.
