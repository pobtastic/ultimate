51117: Handle Near Arch
The handler of the object types 6 and 8, which are the doors in the walls of a room. A door does not change, but it checks for the player standing in the doorway, which makes the player pass into the room on the other side.
A door that is not mirrored is in a wall at one end of the x axis, and has the place that the player has to be at, in front of it, held in the speeds along x and y and the position along z of its record, which no other object uses as it does not move. The place is 13 further along y than the door. When the door is mirrored it is in a wall at one end of the y axis and the place is 13 back along x.
