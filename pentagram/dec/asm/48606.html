48606: Print Sprites
Used by the routines at DrawFrame and PrintBorder.
Draws a number of copies of the piece in the temporary object, one after another, each the same distance from the last.
Input
B Number of pieces
D Distance up the screen from one piece to the next
E Distance to the right from one piece to the next
IX Temporary object record
PrintSprites 48606 PUSH BC Stash the counter, the distance and the data pointer on the stack.
48607 PUSH DE
48608 PUSH HL
48609 CALL DrawObject_Sprite Call DrawObject_Sprite to draw the piece.
48612 POP HL Restore the data pointer, the distance and the counter.
48613 POP DE
48614 POP BC
48615 LD A,(IX+26) Move the piece on to the right.
48618 ADD A,E
48619 LD (IX+26),A
48622 LD A,(IX+27) Move the piece up.
48625 ADD A,D
48626 LD (IX+27),A
48629 DJNZ PrintSprites Decrease the counter and loop until all the pieces have been drawn.
48631 RET Return.
