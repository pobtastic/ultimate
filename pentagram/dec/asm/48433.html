48433: Frame Pieces
The pieces of the frame that DrawFrame draws. Each is four bytes: the type of the object that is drawn, its flags (which mirror the piece when bit 6 is set), and the x and y screen co-ordinates of the first one. The first six are drawn several times each.
FramePieces 48433 DEFB 62,64,16,52 Sloping edge on the left, mirrored, starting at the top.
48437 DEFB 62,0,224,52 Sloping edge on the right, starting at the top.
48441 DEFB 61,64,0,4 Upright edge on the left, mirrored, first kind of piece.
48445 DEFB 61,0,240,4 Upright edge on the right, first kind of piece.
48449 DEFB 60,64,0,20 Upright edge on the left, mirrored, second kind of piece.
48453 DEFB 60,0,240,20 Upright edge on the right, second kind of piece.
48457 DEFB 58,64,96,4 Piece in the middle of the bottom, on the left, mirrored.
48461 DEFB 58,0,144,4 Piece in the middle of the bottom, on the right.
48465 DEFB 59,64,0,52 Cap at the top of the upright edge on the left, mirrored.
48469 DEFB 59,0,240,52 Cap at the top of the upright edge on the right.
