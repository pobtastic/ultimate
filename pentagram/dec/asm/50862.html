50862: Move Player South
Takes three off the speed along y, for the player to walk along y towards zero.
Input
IX Record of the legs
MovePlayerSouth 50862 LD A,(IX+10) Fetch the speed along y.
50865 ADD A,253 Take three off it.
50867 JR MovePlayer_SetY Jump to MovePlayer_SetY to set the speed.
