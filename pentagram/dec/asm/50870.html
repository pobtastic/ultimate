50870: Build Room
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Builds the room that the player has gone into, and puts the player into it. The room is built in the buffer and the whole room is drawn on the next pass of the main loop.
BuildRoom 50870 LD IX,42863 Start with the first object record, ObjectsTable, which is the legs of the player.
50874 CALL ClearScreenBuffer Call ClearScreenBuffer to clear the buffer.
50877 CALL BuildRoomScenery Call BuildRoomScenery to make the scenery of the room.
50880 CALL PlacePlayerAtDoor Call PlacePlayerAtDoor to put the player at the door that it came through.
50883 CALL MarkRoomVisited Call MarkRoomVisited to note that the room has been visited.
50886 LD A,1 Set the flag in RoomRedrawFlag, to say that the room has to be drawn.
50888 LD (42769),A
50891 RET Return.
