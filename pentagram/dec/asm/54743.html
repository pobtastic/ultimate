54743: Play Click
Used by the routine at PauseGame.
Makes a click, which is a tone that gets a little lower each time that it is made, as it is a step on from the count at SoundEffectCounter which is also used by PlaySoundEffect.
PlayClick 54743 LD HL,42825 Take the counter of the sound effect, SoundEffectCounter.
54746 INC (HL) Add one to it.
54747 LD A,(HL) Fetch it.
54748 ADD A,64 Add 64 to make the pitch.
54750 LD B,A Put the pitch in B.
54751 LD C,12 Set the length of the tone.
54753 JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to make the tone.
