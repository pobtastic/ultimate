51842: Place Player At Door
Used by the routine at BuildRoom.
Moves the player to the doorway on the opposite side of a room to the one that it left the last room by. When the player goes through a door, TestDoorPassage sets the x or y position of the player to 0 or 255 to show which wall it went through. In the new room, the player is put in front of the door in the opposite wall, in the doorway, with the edge of the player nearest the centre of the room two units inside the wall. Nothing is done when the player is not at one of the edges, which is how a game starts.
