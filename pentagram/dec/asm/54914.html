54914: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayJumpSound, PlayProjectileSound and PlayWaveSequence.
Makes one cycle of a square wave on the speaker, in which the speaker is on for a time and then off for the same time. The value in B is kept.
Input
B Half the length of the cycle
PlaySquareWave 54914 LD A,16 Set bit 4 to turn the speaker on.
54916 OUT (254),A Write it to the ULA.
54918 LD A,B Keep the length of the delay in A.
PlaySquareWave_On 54919 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_On Wait while the speaker is on.
54921 LD B,A Get the length back.
54922 XOR A Set the speaker to off.
54923 OUT (254),A Write it to the ULA.
54925 LD A,B Keep the length of the delay in A.
PlaySquareWave_Off 54926 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Off Wait while the speaker is off.
54928 LD B,A Get the length back.
54929 RET Return.
