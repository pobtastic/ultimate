49922: Show Victory Screen
Used by the routine at RunPentagramStone.
Shows the screen for when the pentagram is complete, which is a frame with six lines of text in it. It is followed by the game over screen, which carries on from where this ends.
The victory screen
Show the victory screen.
ShowVictoryScreen 49922 CALL ClearScreenBuffer Call ClearScreenBuffer to clear the buffer.
49925 LD A,69 Set the attribute in A to bright cyan ink on a black paper.
49927 CALL FillAttributes Call FillAttributes to set all the attributes to it.
49930 LD DE,50153 Take the colours of the lines, VictoryColours.
49933 EXX Switch to the alternate registers.
49934 LD HL,50141 Take the positions of the lines, VictoryPositions.
49937 LD DE,50059 Take the text of the lines, VictoryText.
49940 LD B,6 Set a counter in BC' of six lines.
49942 XOR A Write 0 to BorderDrawnFlag, so that the frame will be drawn.
49943 LD (42804),A
49946 CALL DisplayMenu_Next Call DisplayMenu_Next to draw the lines of text and the frame, and to copy the buffer to the screen.
Play the victory tune.
49949 LD DE,55407 Take the victory tune, VictoryTune.
49952 CALL PlayMusic Call PlayMusic to play it.
