48113: Menu Colours
The attribute of each line of the menu. Bit 7, which makes the line flash, is set for the line that is chosen. The lines are the title, the four control methods, the line that starts the game and the copyright line.
MenuColours 48113 DEFB 67 The title: INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
