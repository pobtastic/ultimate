D6B5: Play Music
Used by the routines at RunGame, ShowVictoryScreen, ShowGameOverScreen and RunHelper.
Plays a tune until its end.
Input
DE Address of the tune
PlayMusic D6B5 LD A,(DE) Fetch the next note.
D6B6 CP $FF Jump to PlayMusic_Done if it is the end of the tune.
D6B8 JR Z,PlayMusic_Done
D6BA CALL PlayNote Call PlayNote to play it.
D6BD JR PlayMusic Jump back to PlayMusic to play the next.
This entry point is used by the routine at PlayMusicOnce.
PlayMusic_Done D6BF RET Return.
