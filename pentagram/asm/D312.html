D312: Initial Object Store
The entries of the object store at the start of a game, which InitialiseObjectStore copies to ObjectStore. An entry is the first sixteen bytes of the record of an object: the type, the position, the sizes, the flags and the room. The rooms and positions of the stones are set by InitialiseObjectStore, and the arcs have no flag to be drawn until CheckObjectsCollected sets it.
