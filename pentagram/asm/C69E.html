C69E: Move Player East
Adds three to the speed along x, for the player to walk along x away from zero.
Input
IX Record of the legs
MovePlayerEast C69E LD A,(IX+$09) Fetch the speed along x.
C6A1 ADD A,$03 Add three to it.
C6A3 JR MovePlayer_SetX Jump to MovePlayer_SetX to set the speed.
