5E10: Room Data
The records of the rooms of the game, which BuildRoomScenery reads when it builds a room. A record has the number of the room, a length byte, a byte for the ink colour and size of the room, and then the features of the room, which are pairs of a feature number and a parameter, ended by FF. Any groups of templates come after that, each a byte and the cells that follow it. A room can end before the FF, when it has no groups of templates.
