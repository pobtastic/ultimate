D615: Sound Effect Pitches
The pitches of the notes of the sound effects, for PlaySoundEffect. The delay, which gets longer as the pitch gets lower.
SoundEffectPitches D615 DEFB $40 The byte before the first sequence, which is never used.
D616 DEFB $70,$40,$70,$50 The new room sequence, played from the end.
D61A DEFB $70,$60,$50,$40,$40 The pick up or drop sequence, played from the end.
D61F DEFB $50,$60,$70,$80,$80,$80,$30,$60 Never used.
D627 DEFB $30,$60
