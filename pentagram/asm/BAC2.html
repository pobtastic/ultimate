BAC2: Inventory Colours
The attribute used for an inventory icon, which is chosen by the lowest three bits of the type of the object. They are bright blue, red, magenta, green, cyan, yellow and white ink on a black paper (twice for white).
InventoryColours BAC2 DEFB $41 INK:BLUE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC3 DEFB $42 INK:RED, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC4 DEFB $43 INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC5 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC6 DEFB $45 INK:CYAN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC7 DEFB $46 INK:YELLOW, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC8 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BAC9 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
