C8F7: Door Push Routines
The routines that NudgeObjectsToArch uses to push the player, for each of the four directions that the player can face. The first four are for doors of type 08 and the second four are for doors of type 06.
DoorPushRoutines C8F7 DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth The routines for doors of type 08.
C8F9 DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest
C8FB DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest
C8FD DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth
DoorPushRoutines_Type06 C8FF DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest The routines for doors of type 06.
C901 DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth
C903 DEFW NudgeObjectNorthSouth
C905 DEFW NudgeObjectEastWest
