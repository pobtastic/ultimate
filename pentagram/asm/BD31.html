BD31: Frame Pieces
The pieces of the frame that DrawFrame draws. Each is four bytes: the type of the object that is drawn, its flags (which mirror the piece when bit 6 is set), and the x and y screen co-ordinates of the first one. The first six are drawn several times each.
FramePieces BD31 DEFB $3E,$40,$10,$34 Sloping edge on the left, mirrored, starting at the top.
BD35 DEFB $3E,$00,$E0,$34 Sloping edge on the right, starting at the top.
BD39 DEFB $3D,$40,$00,$04 Upright edge on the left, mirrored, first kind of piece.
BD3D DEFB $3D,$00,$F0,$04 Upright edge on the right, first kind of piece.
BD41 DEFB $3C,$40,$00,$14 Upright edge on the left, mirrored, second kind of piece.
BD45 DEFB $3C,$00,$F0,$14 Upright edge on the right, second kind of piece.
BD49 DEFB $3A,$40,$60,$04 Piece in the middle of the bottom, on the left, mirrored.
BD4D DEFB $3A,$00,$90,$04 Piece in the middle of the bottom, on the right.
BD51 DEFB $3B,$40,$00,$34 Cap at the top of the upright edge on the left, mirrored.
BD55 DEFB $3B,$00,$F0,$34 Cap at the top of the upright edge on the right.
