BBF1: Menu Colours
The attribute of each line of the menu. Bit 7, which makes the line flash, is set for the line that is chosen. The lines are the title, the four control methods, the line that starts the game and the copyright line.
MenuColours BBF1 DEFB $43 The title: INK:MAGENTA, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
MenuColours_ControlMethods BBF2 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBF3 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBF4 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBF5 DEFB $44 INK:GREEN, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBF6 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
BBF7 DEFB $47 INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
