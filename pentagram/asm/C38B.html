C38B: Messaging: Victory Text
The six lines of text of the victory screen. The last character of each has bit 7 set. The last line refers to the next game.
VictoryText C38B DEFM "CONGRATULATION","S"+$80 "CONGRATULATIONS"
C39A DEFM "YOU HAVE COMPLEATE","D"+$80 "YOU HAVE COMPLEATED"
C3AD DEFM "THE PENTAGRA","M"+$80 "THE PENTAGRAM"
C3BA DEFM "YOUR ADVENTUR","E"+$80 "YOUR ADVENTURE"
C3C8 DEFM "CONTINUES I","N"+$80 "CONTINUES IN"
C3D4 DEFM "MIRE MAR","E"+$80 "MIRE MARE"
