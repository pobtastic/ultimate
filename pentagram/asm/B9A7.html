B9A7: Flag Overlapping Objects
Used by the routines at HandlePlayerTop and SetWipeAndDrawFlags.
Flags every object whose sprite overlaps the area that an object has just moved through, so that they are redrawn as well, otherwise they would be left with a piece cut out of them when the area was cleared. The area is the rectangle that covers both the old and the new places of the sprite of the object, which is in whole bytes, so only the columns are compared in bytes.
An object is flagged by setting bit 4 of its flags.
Input
IX Object record
Work out the size of the new sprite, which is stored in the object.
