BDDE: Print Sprites
Used by the routines at DrawFrame and PrintBorder.
Draws a number of copies of the piece in the temporary object, one after another, each the same distance from the last.
Input
B Number of pieces
D Distance up the screen from one piece to the next
E Distance to the right from one piece to the next
IX Temporary object record
PrintSprites BDDE PUSH BC Stash the counter, the distance and the data pointer on the stack.
BDDF PUSH DE
BDE0 PUSH HL
BDE1 CALL DrawObject_Sprite Call DrawObject_Sprite to draw the piece.
BDE4 POP HL Restore the data pointer, the distance and the counter.
BDE5 POP DE
BDE6 POP BC
BDE7 LD A,(IX+$1A) Move the piece on to the right.
BDEA ADD A,E
BDEB LD (IX+$1A),A
BDEE LD A,(IX+$1B) Move the piece up.
BDF1 ADD A,D
BDF2 LD (IX+$1B),A
BDF5 DJNZ PrintSprites Decrease the counter and loop until all the pieces have been drawn.
BDF7 RET Return.
