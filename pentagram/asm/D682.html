D682: Play Square Wave
Used by the routines at PlayJumpSound, PlayProjectileSound and PlayWaveSequence.
Makes one cycle of a square wave on the speaker, in which the speaker is on for a time and then off for the same time. The value in B is kept.
Input
B Half the length of the cycle
PlaySquareWave D682 LD A,$10 Set bit 4 to turn the speaker on.
D684 OUT ($FE),A Write it to the ULA.
D686 LD A,B Keep the length of the delay in A.
PlaySquareWave_On D687 DJNZ PlaySquareWave_On Wait while the speaker is on.
D689 LD B,A Get the length back.
D68A XOR A Set the speaker to off.
D68B OUT ($FE),A Write it to the ULA.
D68D LD A,B Keep the length of the delay in A.
PlaySquareWave_Off D68E DJNZ PlaySquareWave_Off Wait while the speaker is off.
D690 LD B,A Get the length back.
D691 RET Return.
