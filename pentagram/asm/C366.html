C366: Messaging: Game Over Text
The three lines of text of the game over screen. The last character of each has bit 7 set. The percentage is printed under "COMPLETED".
GameOverText C366 DEFM "GAME OVE","R"+$80 "GAME OVER"
C36F DEFM "PERCENTAGE OF QUES","T"+$80 "PERCENTAGE OF QUEST"
C382 DEFM "COMPLETE","D"+$80 "COMPLETED"
