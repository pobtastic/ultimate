C6EA: Print Quest Percentage
Used by the routine at ShowGameOverScreen.
Works out how much of the quest has been done, and prints it to the buffer for the game over screen. The percentage is the number of rooms that have been visited, halved and no more than 36 plus four for each of the objects that have been collected, plus six for each of the stones that have been put on the pentagram. That makes 64 at the most.
The percentage is kept in PercentComplete as binary coded decimal, and it is printed as three digits if it is 64 and otherwise as two.
