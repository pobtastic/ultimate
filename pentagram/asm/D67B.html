D67B: Play Wave Sequence
Used by the routines at PlayMovingSound, PlayClick, PlaySoundEffect, PlayFootstepSound, PlayExplosionSound and PlayObjectSound.
Makes a tone from a number of cycles of the same length.
Input
B Pitch
C Number of beeps
Play one beep per count.
PlayWaveSequence D67B CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave.
D67E DEC C Jump to PlayWaveSequence until all of the beeps have been played.
D67F JR NZ,PlayWaveSequence
D681 RET Return.
