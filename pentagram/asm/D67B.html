D67B: Play Wave Sequence
Used by the routines at PlayMovingSound, PlayClick, PlaySoundEffect, PlayFootstepSound, PlayExplosionSound and PlayObjectSound.
Makes a tone from a number of cycles of the same length.
Input
B Half the length of a cycle, in units of the time that DJNZ takes
C Number of cycles
PlayWaveSequence D67B CALL PlaySquareWave Call PlaySquareWave to make a cycle of the tone.
D67E DEC C Count the cycle.
D67F JR NZ,PlayWaveSequence Jump back to PlayWaveSequence until all of the cycles have been made.
D681 RET Return.
