D88F: Screen Buffer
The buffer that the room is drawn into before it is copied to the screen by UpdateScreen and DrawChangedObjects. It is 20 bytes wide and C0 pixel lines tall, in the order of the pixel lines of the screen but with the bottom line first. It is empty at the start of the game.
