BD59: Print Border
Used by the routines at DisplayMenu and ShowGameOverScreen.
Draws the frame around the title screen, and around the end screens, into the buffer, from the pieces whose data starts at MenuFramePieces. The four corners are drawn first, then the top and bottom edges, which are each made of 08 sprites that are 18 pixels apart, and the left and right edges, which are each made of 06 sprites that are 18 pixels apart.
