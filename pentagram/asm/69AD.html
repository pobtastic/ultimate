69AD: Feature Data
The features, each a list of objects of eight bytes, which are the type, the position along x, y and z, the half sizes along x and y, the size along z and the flags, and which ends with a 00 in place of the type of another object. BuildRoomScenery copies each into an object record, and sets its room to the parameter of the feature in the room. Some of the features start in the middle of another, and are what is left of it when its first objects are left out.
