C302: Show Victory Screen
Used by the routine at RunPentagramStone.
Shows the screen for when the pentagram is complete, which is a frame with six lines of text in it. It is followed by the game over screen, which carries on from where this ends.
The victory screen
Show the victory screen.
ShowVictoryScreen C302 CALL ClearScreenBuffer Call ClearScreenBuffer to clear the buffer.
C305 LD A,$45 Set the attribute in A to bright cyan ink on a black paper.
C307 CALL FillAttributes Call FillAttributes to set all the attributes to it.
C30A LD DE,$C3E9 Take the colours of the lines, VictoryColours.
C30D EXX Switch to the shadow registers.
C30E LD HL,$C3DD Take the positions of the lines, VictoryPositions.
C311 LD DE,$C38B Take the text of the lines, VictoryText.
C314 LD B,$06 Set a counter in BC' of six lines.
C316 XOR A Write 00 to BorderDrawnFlag, so that the frame will be drawn.
C317 LD ($A734),A
C31A CALL DisplayMenu_Next Call DisplayMenu_Next to draw the lines of text and the frame, and to copy the buffer to the screen.
Play the victory tune.
C31D LD DE,$D86F Take the victory tune, VictoryTune.
C320 CALL PlayMusic Call PlayMusic to play it.
