BD98: Menu Frame Pieces
The pieces of the frame that PrintBorder draws, in the same form as the pieces of FramePieces. The flags mirror a piece when bit 6 is set, and flip it upside down when bit 7 is set.
MenuFramePieces BD98 DEFB $05,$00,$00,$A8 Bottom left corner.
BD9C DEFB $05,$40,$E8,$A8 Bottom right corner, mirrored.
BDA0 DEFB $05,$C0,$E8,$00 Top right corner, mirrored and flipped.
BDA4 DEFB $05,$80,$00,$00 Top left corner, flipped.
BDA8 DEFB $04,$00,$18,$A8 Bottom edge, first piece.
BDAC DEFB $04,$80,$18,$00 Top edge, first piece, flipped.
BDB0 DEFB $03,$00,$D8,$A8 Bottom edge, end piece.
BDB4 DEFB $03,$80,$D8,$00 Top edge, end piece, flipped.
BDB8 DEFB $02,$00,$00,$18 Left edge, first piece.
BDBC DEFB $02,$40,$E8,$18 Right edge, first piece, mirrored.
