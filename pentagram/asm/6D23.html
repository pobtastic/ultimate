6D23: Template Data
The templates, each a list of objects of five bytes, which are the type, the half sizes along x and y, the size along z and the flags, and which ends with a 00 in place of the type of another object. The position of an object comes from the cell that BuildRoomScenery is making it for. Two of the template numbers are for the same template.
Template 09 and 16: A block that the player can push.
Template_Block 6D23 DEFB $5B,$08,$08,$0C,$10 The object of the type 5B, with half sizes of 08 and 08, a height of 0C and the flags 10.
