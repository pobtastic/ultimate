B638: Sort Jump Table
The routines that DrawObjectsInOrder uses for each combination of the codes for the three axes. The entries are in groups of three for the three codes of the height, in groups of three of those for the three codes of y, and in groups of three of those for the three codes of x.
ResolveObjectOrder_InFront is used when the object considered is in front of the other on some axes and behind on none, so it has to be drawn after the other one. ResolveObjectOrder_Behind is used when it is behind on some axes and in front on none, so it can be drawn before. ResolveObjectOrder is used when it is in front on some axes and behind on others, and ResolveObjectOrder_Overlap when the two overlap on all of them; in both cases there is nothing to say which comes first.
SortJumpTable B638 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder,ResolveObjectOrder,ResolveObjectOrder In front on x, behind on y.
B63E DEFW ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,$B66E In front on x, overlapping on y.
B644 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,$B66E In front on x and on y.
B64A DEFW ResolveObjectOrder,$B671,$B671 Overlapping on x, behind on y.
B650 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,$B6B0,$B671 Overlapping on x and on y.
B656 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,ResolveObjectOrder_InFront,$B66E Overlapping on x, in front on y.
B65C DEFW ResolveObjectOrder,$B671,$B671 Behind on x and on y.
B662 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder,$B671,$B671 Behind on x, overlapping on y.
B668 DEFW ResolveObjectOrder,ResolveObjectOrder,ResolveObjectOrder Behind on x, in front on y.
