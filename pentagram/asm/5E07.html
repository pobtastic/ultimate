5E07: Room Sizes
The three sizes that a room can have, which are picked by the room data (see BuildRoomScenery). Each is the distance from the centre of the room to its walls along x, then along y, and then the height of the floor.
RoomSizeTable 5E07 DEFB $40,$40,$80 Size 00: the walls are 40 from the centre along x and 40 along y, and the floor is at 80.
5E0A DEFB $20,$40,$80 Size 01: the walls are 20 from the centre along x and 40 along y, and the floor is at 80.
5E0D DEFB $40,$20,$80 Size 02: the walls are 40 from the centre along x and 20 along y, and the floor is at 80.
