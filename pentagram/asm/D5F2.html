D5F2: Play Sound Effect
Used by the routine at RunGame.
Plays one note of the sound effect that is in progress, which is called once each frame. SoundEffectCounter is the number of notes left, and SoundEffectType picks the sequence of pitches from the table at SoundEffectSequences. The notes are played from the last of the sequence to the first. There is only a sound effect for entering a room, with a sequence of four notes, and one for the player picking up or dropping an object, with five. The sequences that the table has for the numbers 02 and 03 are never used.
PlaySoundEffect D5F2 LD HL,$A749 Take the number of notes left, SoundEffectCounter.
D5F5 LD A,(HL) Fetch it.
D5F6 AND A Return if there are none.
D5F7 RET Z
D5F8 DEC (HL) Take one off.
D5F9 EX AF,AF' Keep the number of notes that were left in AF'.
D5FA INC HL Move on to the type of the effect.
D5FB LD A,(HL) Fetch it.
D5FC LD BC,$D60D Take the table of sequences, SoundEffectSequences.
D5FF CALL GetTableEntry Call GetTableEntry to fetch the address of the sequence into HL.
D602 EX AF,AF' Get the number of notes that were left.
D603 LD C,A Put it in C.
D604 LD B,$00 Clear B.
D606 ADD HL,BC Add it to the sequence, to point at the note.
D607 LD B,(HL) Fetch the pitch.
D608 LD C,$0C Set the length of the tone.
D60A JP PlayWaveSequence Jump to PlayWaveSequence to make the tone.
