C4C8: Handle Turning
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Turns the player to the left or the right, if the controls ask for it. A turn takes the player one quarter of the way round, and the player has to wait for two frames after that before it can turn again.
There is a second way of controlling the player, which is for use with a joystick and is chosen by bit 3 of the control method in ControlMethod. That bit is never set, so it is never used. In this way, four of the control bits stand for the four directions that the player can face, and the player turns until it faces the one that is asked for, and then walks.
Input
C Controls
IX Record of the legs
Output
C Controls, in which the bit for walking is set by the second way of
controlling the player
