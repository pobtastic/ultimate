C1BD: Projectile_Speeds
The speed along x and the speed along y of a projectile for each of the four directions that the player can face. A projectile starts out twice this far from the player.
ProjectileSpeeds C1BD DEFB $F8,$00 Facing the direction that numbers 00.
C1BF DEFB $08,$00 Facing the direction that numbers 01.
C1C1 DEFB $00,$08 Facing the direction that numbers 02.
C1C3 DEFB $00,$F8 Facing the direction that numbers 03.
