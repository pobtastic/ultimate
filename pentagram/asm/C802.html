C802: Test Door Passage
Used by the routine at HandleNearArch.
Finds out if the player is going through a door. This is so when the player is standing in the place in front of the door and is allowed to open doors, which means that the player has to be alive. If it is, the player is let pass through the walls, and once it has gone far enough through, it is put into the next room, which is the room of the door.
When the player enters the next room it is put at the other side of the room along the same axis, the player's two object records are saved in PlayerRecords so that the player starts here again after losing a life, and the main loop is restarted at RunGame_NewRoom to build the room.
