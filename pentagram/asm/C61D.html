C61D: Move Player
Used by the routine at HandlePlayerLegs.
Moves the legs of the player for a frame, using the controls, gravity and anything that has pushed the player. If the player is walking, or is in the air or has just entered the room, it is given a speed along x or y, and then it is moved as far as it is able to go.
Gravity works on the speed along z: it is cut by one for each frame while the player is rising with the jump key held down, and by two otherwise, which makes the player fall faster, and makes a jump that is shorter when the key is let go. When the player lands, the flag for jumping is cleared.
