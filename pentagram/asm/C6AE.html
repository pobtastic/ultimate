C6AE: Move Player South
Takes three off the speed along y, for the player to walk along y towards zero.
Input
IX Record of the legs
MovePlayerSouth C6AE LD A,(IX+$0A) Fetch the speed along y.
C6B1 ADD A,$FD Take three off it.
C6B3 JR MovePlayer_SetY Jump to MovePlayer_SetY to set the speed.
