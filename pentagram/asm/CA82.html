CA82: Place Player At Door
Used by the routine at BuildRoom.
Moves the player to the doorway on the opposite side of a room to the one that it left the last room by. When the player goes through a door, TestDoorPassage sets the x or y position of the player to 00 or FF to show which wall it went through. In the new room, the player is put in front of the door in the opposite wall, in the doorway, with the edge of the player nearest the centre of the room two units inside the wall. Nothing is done when the player is not at one of the edges, which is how a game starts.
Input
IX Object record of the player's legs
PlacePlayerAtDoor CA82 LD A,($A71D) Fetch the distance to the walls along x and take off two, which is how far the player is put inside the wall.
CA85 SUB $02
CA87 LD L,A Put it in L.
CA88 LD A,($A71E) Fetch the distance to the walls along y and take off two.
CA8B SUB $02
CA8D LD H,A Put it in H.
CA8E LD A,(IX+$01) Fetch the x position of the player.
CA91 AND A Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfX if the player went through the wall at the start of x.
CA92 JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfX
CA94 INC A Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfX if it went through the wall at the end of x, which is FF.
CA95 JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfX
CA97 LD A,(IX+$02) Fetch the y position of the player.
CA9A AND A Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfY if the player went through the wall at the start of y.
CA9B JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfY
CA9D INC A Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfY if it went through the wall at the end of y, which is FF.
CA9E JR Z,PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfY
CAA0 RET Return.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfY CAA1 LD C,$03 Set the wall to look for a door in, which is at the start of y.
CAA3 CALL FindDoor Call FindDoor to find the door and put the player in front of it.
CAA6 LD A,$80 Set the position of the wall at the start of y, which is the centre of the room.
CAA8 SUB H Subtract the distance to the walls less two...
CAA9 SUB (IX+$05) ...and the half size of the player along y, which puts the edge of the player nearest the centre of the room two units inside the wall.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerY CAAC LD (IX+$02),A Store it as the y position of the player.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_MarkPlayerForDrawing CAAF SET 4,(IX+$07) Flag the legs as needing to be drawn.
CAB3 SET 4,(IX+$27) Flag the body as needing to be drawn.
CAB7 LD A,(IX+$01) Copy the x position of the player to the body.
CABA LD (IX+$21),A
CABD LD A,(IX+$02) Copy the y position of the player to the body.
CAC0 LD (IX+$22),A
CAC3 RET Return.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfY CAC4 LD C,$02 Set the wall to look for a door in, which is at the end of y.
CAC6 CALL FindDoor Call FindDoor to find the door and put the player in front of it.
CAC9 LD A,H Fetch the distance to the walls less two.
CACA ADD A,$80 Add the position of the centre of the room...
CACC ADD A,(IX+$05) ...and the half size of the player along y.
CACF JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerY Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerY to store it.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_StartOfX CAD1 LD C,$01 Set the wall to look for a door in, which is at the start of x.
CAD3 CALL FindDoor Call FindDoor to find the door and put the player in front of it.
CAD6 LD A,$80 Set the position of the wall at the start of x, which is the centre of the room.
CAD8 SUB L Subtract the distance to the walls less two...
CAD9 SUB (IX+$04) ...and the half size of the player along x, which puts the edge of the player nearest the centre of the room two units inside the wall.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerX CADC LD (IX+$01),A Store it as the x position of the player.
CADF JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_MarkPlayerForDrawing Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_MarkPlayerForDrawing to finish.
PlacePlayerAtDoor_EndOfX CAE1 LD C,$00 Set the wall to look for a door in, which is at the end of x.
CAE3 CALL FindDoor Call FindDoor to find the door and put the player in front of it.
CAE6 LD A,L Fetch the distance to the walls less two.
CAE7 ADD A,$80 Add the position of the centre of the room...
CAE9 ADD A,(IX+$04) ...and the half size of the player along x.
CAEC JR PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerX Jump to PlacePlayerAtDoor_SetPlayerX to store it.
