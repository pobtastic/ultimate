60868: Extra Life Table
The sixteen places of the extra lives, with three bytes for each: the room x, the room y and the collected flag. The flag is set to 1 by Collect_Extra_Life when Sabreman picks up the extra life and is cleared at the start of a game by Clear_Collected_Flags. Place_Special_Object looks for the room of Sabreman in this table.
