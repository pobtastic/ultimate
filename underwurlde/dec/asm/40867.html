40867: Print Completion Percentage
Used by the routine at Show_Game_Over.
Prints YOU HAVE COMPLETED, the percentage of the Underwurlde that has been explored, and OF THE UNDERWURLDE.
The percentage comes from the room visit table at Room_Visit_Table. The first byte of each of the first 576 pairs of bytes is non-zero if the room has been visited, and the number of those is the count. The count is multiplied by 32 and has 128 added, so that its high byte is the count divided by eight and rounded. The division routine at Divide then divides that by 192 after multiplying it by 256 which makes the result about one sixth of the count. The result is held as two BCD digits in Completion_Percentage.
The count and the result are also stored at 24064 24066 and 24068 which nothing reads.
With 576 entries the result can not be more than 96, so the text 100 is never printed.
