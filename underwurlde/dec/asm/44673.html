44673: Set Up Rope
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Sets up the rope for the room that has just been drawn, using the map entry of the room (see Read_World_Map). Bit 3 of the second byte says whether the room has a rope.
The rope is the object in record 3 (type 23).
The length of the rope is stored in Rope_Length. It is half of the sum of 255 (or 160 if bit 2 is reset) and 256 times the two low bits of the second byte.
