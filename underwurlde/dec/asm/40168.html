40168: Draw Right Wall Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws the picture of the right wall of a special room, at x position 232 and y position 48. The picture is three bytes wide and 112 lines high (see Room_Graphics_Right_Wall).
The attributes of the picture are bright cyan (77). The picture is drawn by Draw_Wall_Picture.
Draw_Right_Wall_Picture 40168 LD HL,12520 Set the x position to 232 and the y position to 48 (12520).
40171 LD DE,33303 Point at the picture (33303).
40174 LD C,77 Set the attribute of the picture to 77.
40176 JR Draw_Wall_Picture Jump to Draw_Wall_Picture to draw the picture.
