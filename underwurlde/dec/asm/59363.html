59363: Handle Death
Handler for the object types 62 and 63 the sprites that Sabreman has when he dies (see Start_Dying). The sprite is drawn until the countdown in byte 10 reaches zero. Then he loses a life and the game is over if he has none left (see Show_Game_Over).
Otherwise his position is saved (see Saved_Position), all of the object records are cleared (see Clear_Object_Records), Sabreman is reset (see Reset_Sabreman), his position is put back and the room is drawn again, so he carries on where he died.
Input
IX Object record of Sabreman

59389 LD (36571),HL Save them.

59395 LD (36573),HL Save them.

59401 CALL Reset_Sabreman Reset Sabreman.
59404 LD HL,(36571) Fetch the saved x position and room x.
59407 LD (36635),HL Put them back in the record of Sabreman.

59413 LD (36637),HL Put them back in the record of Sabreman.
59416 LD A,16 Set the contact flags of Sabreman to 16 so that he stands on a floor.
59418 LD (36645),A
59421 CALL Draw_Room Draw the room.
59424 RET Return.
