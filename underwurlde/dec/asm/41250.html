41250: Unused
Never reached, as nothing jumps here. Plays 16 cycles of a tone whose half period is made from byte 6 of the object: it is inverted, rotated left and cut down to the low six bits, and then bit 6 is set.
Input
IX Object record
Unused_Play_Object_Tone 41250 LD A,(IX+6) Fetch byte 6 of the object.
41253 CPL Invert the bits.
41254 RLCA Rotate it left.
41255 AND %00111111 Keep only the low six bits.
41257 OR %01000000 Set bit 6.
41259 LD B,A Use the result as the half period.
41260 LD C,16 C=16 (the number of cycles).
41262 JP SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
