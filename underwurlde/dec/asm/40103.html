40103: Draw Floor Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws the picture of the floor of a special room, at x position 24 and y position 160. The picture is 26 bytes wide and 32 lines high (see Room_Graphics_Floor).
The attributes of the picture are bright magenta (75). The picture is drawn by Draw_Wide_Picture.
Draw_Floor_Picture 40103 LD HL,40984 Set the x position to 24 and the y position to 160 (40984).
40106 LD DE,30883 Point at the picture (30883).
40109 LD C,75 Set the attribute of the picture to 75.
40111 JR Draw_Wide_Picture Jump to Draw_Wide_Picture to draw the picture.
