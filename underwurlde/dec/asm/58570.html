58570: Move Eagle To Next Room
Used by the routine at Limit_Eagle_Vertical_Movement.
Moves the eagle and Sabreman into the next room. There is an entry point for each direction, and the first one (Move_Eagle_To_Next_Room) is for the room above. The eagle and Sabreman are put at the opposite edge of the new room, and the room is drawn.
Input
IX Object record of the eagle
Move_Eagle_To_Next_Room 58570 DEC (IX+4) Move the eagle to the room above.
58573 LD A,168 Set the y position of the eagle to 168.
58575 LD (IX+3),A
58578 LD A,184 Set the y position of Sabreman to 184.
58580 LD (36643),A
Move_Eagle_To_Next_Room_Set_Room_Y 58583 LD A,(IX+4) Fetch the room y of the eagle.
58586 LD (36638),A Set the room y of Sabreman.
Move_Eagle_To_Next_Room_Draw 58589 LD (IX+15),1 Set the redraw flags of the eagle to 1 so that it is drawn without being erased first.
58593 CALL Update_Object_Sprite Draw the eagle.
