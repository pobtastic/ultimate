39378: Clear Panel
Used by the routines at Draw_Top_Right_Corner, Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner, Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner and Draw_Top_Left_Corner.
Clears a rectangle of the screen to the right of the position in HL. There are three entry points, which select the size of the rectangle. They stand in for the parts of the frame of the room (see Draw_Room) that are not present.
Each of those routines stashed DE on the stack, so this routine ends by restoring it. The caller is returned to by the last instruction.
