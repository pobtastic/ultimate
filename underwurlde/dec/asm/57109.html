57109: Handle Explosion
Handler for the object types 76 to 79 (an explosion). It works like Handle_Dust, but the explosion starts with a counter of 9 and the two low bits of the type follow the two low bits of the counter, so the explosion goes through four frames.
Input
IX Object record
Handle_Explosion 57109 CALL StoreEntity Save the state of the explosion.
57112 LD A,(36636) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
57115 CP (IX+2) Jump to Remove_Unseen_Weapon to remove the explosion if it is in a different room x.
57118 JP NZ,Remove_Unseen_Weapon
57121 LD A,(36638) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
57124 CP (IX+4) Jump to Remove_Unseen_Weapon to remove the explosion if it is in a different room y.
57127 JP NZ,Remove_Unseen_Weapon
57130 DEC (IX+8) Decrease the counter and jump to Remove_Thrown_Weapon to remove the explosion when it reaches zero.
57133 JP Z,Remove_Thrown_Weapon
57136 LD A,(IX+8) Fetch the counter.
57139 AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits.
57141 LD B,A Store them in B.
57142 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the type.
57145 AND %11111100 Reset the two low bits.
57147 ADD A,B Add the two low bits of the counter.
57148 LD (IX+0),A Store the type.
57151 JP Update_Object_Sprite Jump to Update_Object_Sprite.
