59607: Handle Weapon Pickup
Handler for the object types 104 to 107 the four weapons that Sabreman can pick up (see Sprite_Weapon_1). The weapons are the small object records at Small_Object_Records. Byte 7 of a record holds the flags: bit 0 is set if there is no old image to erase, bit 1 is set while Sabreman carries the weapon and bit 2 is set while the weapon falls.
A weapon that lies on the floor is drawn again and again. When an action key has just been pressed (the flag at New_Action_Key_Press_Flag is 255) and Sabreman is touching it (see Check_Sabreman_Contact), he picks it up: the weapon moves to the x position 136 and the y position 15 which is its place in the panel at the top of the screen, bit 1 of the flags is set, the flag at New_Action_Key_Press_Flag is set to 254 to show that the key press has been used, and the type of the weapon is stored in the first of the weapon slots at Weapon_Slots.
Each later key press moves a carried weapon 24 pixels to the right in the panel and stores its type in the weapon slot that goes with the new x position (the x positions 160 and 184 both give the third slot, and none gives the second). A key press when the weapon has reached 184 makes Sabreman drop it. It is put at his position, with bit 1 of the flags reset, bit 2 set and the counter in byte 6 set to 16 and it falls (see Handle_Weapon_Pickup_Fall).
A weapon that is in a different room is dealt with by Handle_Distant_Weapon.
