43669: Rope End Position
The position of the end of the rope, that Position_Rope_End works out. The low byte is the x position and the high byte is the y position. The x position is the displacement of the rope (Rope_Displacement_Pixels) plus 128.
Rope_End_Position 43669 DEFB 0 The x position.
Rope_End_Position_Y 43670 DEFB 0 The y position.
