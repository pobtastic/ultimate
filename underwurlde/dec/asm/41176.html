41176: Unused
Never reached, as nothing jumps here. Plays 12 tones of one cycle each. The half period is the counter rotated right by one bit.
Unused_Play_Falling_Tones 41176 LD D,12 D=12 (the number of tones).
Unused_Play_Falling_Tones_Loop 41178 LD A,D Fetch the counter.
41179 RRCA Rotate it right.
41180 LD B,A Use the result as the half period.
41181 CALL SoundsClick Play the tone.
41184 DEC D Decrease the tone counter and loop back until all of the tones have been played.
41185 JR NZ,41178
41187 RET Return.
