38387: Wait For Key
Used by the routines at Show_Game_Over and Show_Ending_Screen.
Waits until key 0 is pressed. It gives up after 12 times 65536 passes round the inner loop if no key is pressed.

38389 LD HL,0 Set the inner counter to 0 which makes 65536 passes round the inner loop.
