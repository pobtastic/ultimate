42662: Handle Sabreman Falling
Handler for the object types 21 and 22 which are the falling sprites of Sabreman (21 when he falls to the left and 22 when he falls to the right). It is also where he lands from a jump or a fall.
The routine checks for contact with the falling stalactite, deals with the special states in the same way as Handle_Sabreman_Walking, lets him steer while he is not moving sideways, checks for contact with the bubbles and the creatures, moves him sideways and then applies the pull of gravity to the y velocity, which is increased by 2 each time up to a limit of 76. The y position changes by one sixteenth of the y velocity.
He lands when the y position reaches 159. Landing after a fall that reaches a y velocity of 116 or more kills him (see Start_Dying).

The countdown is zero. Now the routine checks whether he is falling onto the rope (the type 23 object, see Handle_Rope). The conditions are that the room y is 16 or more, that the number in Rope_Length (the length of the rope) is not less than C, that his x position is within 16 of 120 (where the rope hangs), and that record 3 holds the rope and Rope_Displacement_Pixels is zero.
