39780: Draw Bottom Left Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the bottom left corner of the frame of the room if bit 5 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is drawn at x position 0 y position 160 by Draw_Corner_Shape. Its shape depends on whether the floor (bit 4) and the left wall (bit 6) are present.
Input
DE Frame flags of the room in E
IX Object record of Sabreman

39784 LD A,E Copy the frame flags to A.
39785 LD DE,39812 Point at the table of corner shapes (Bottom_Left_Corner_Table).
39788 BIT 5,A Jump to 39795 to clear the area if the corner is not present.
39790 JP Z,Clear_Panel

39795 BIT 4,A Jump to Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall if the floor is not present.

39800 INC HL
Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall 39801 BIT 6,A Jump to Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape if the left wall is not present.
