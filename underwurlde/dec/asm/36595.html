36595: Score
The score, held as six BCD digits in three bytes. AddPointsToScore adds to it and prints it.
Score 36595 DEFB 0 The high byte (the two highest digits).
36596 DEFB 0 The middle byte.
Score_Low_Byte 36597 DEFB 0 The low byte (the two lowest digits).
