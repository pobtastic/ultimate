41750: Handle Sabreman On Rope
Handler for the object types 1 to 8 which Sabreman has while he hangs on and swings from the rope (the type 23 object, see Handle_Rope). The sprites are the ones that are called swimming (1 to 4 when he moves to the left and 5 to 8 when he moves to the right; the frame follows the x position).
If bit 3 or bit 6 of the redraw flags is set, he lets go at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go. Otherwise the routine checks for contact with the bubbles, the creatures and the falling stalactite, any of which sends him to Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope with B=16. Next it moves the rope with Update_Rope and, if he stays in the same room, reads the controls: a bottom row key (or the joystick fire button) makes him let go at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go, and the fire key throws a weapon.
The position and the velocity of the end of the rope (see Line_Step_Count) are then converted to the position and the velocity of Sabreman. If the y position is 159 or more and the room has a floor, he lets go and stands on the floor at Stand_Sabreman_On_Floor.
