39311: Flag Objects For Redraw
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Writes 1 to byte 15 of each of the nineteen object records, so that each of the sprites is drawn without being erased first (see Update_Object_Sprite).
Flag_Objects_For_Redraw 39311 LD HL,36617 Point at byte 15 of the first record (First_Weapon_Redraw_Flags).
39314 LD DE,16 Set the size of a record to 16.
39317 LD BC,4865 Set the number of records to 19 and the value to flag them with to 1.
Flag_Objects_For_Redraw_Loop 39320 LD (HL),C Set byte 15 of the record.
39321 ADD HL,DE Move on to the next record.
39322 DJNZ Flag_Objects_For_Redraw_Loop Decrease the counter and loop back until all of the records are flagged.
39324 RET Return.
