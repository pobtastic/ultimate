41223: Sounds: Weapon Pop
Used by the routine at Destroy_Creature.
Handler for the object type 139 which is also called directly to make a sound. It plays a run of tones of one cycle each, and the pitch of each one is made from a counter that falls by two each time.
The counter starts at the low byte of the frame counter plus one with its nibbles swapped, with the low four bits set and bit 7 cleared, so the sound is not always the same length. The half period is the counter exclusive ORed with 32 and rotated left.
