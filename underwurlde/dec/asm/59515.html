59515: Knock Sabreman Off Rope
Used by the routine at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope.
Makes Sabreman fall off the rope when something hits him (a bubble, a creature or the falling stalactite). He tumbles, with the y velocity that is in B. His x velocity is the distance that the end of the rope moved (see Rope_End_Position) and the countdown in byte 10 is set to 20. The rope is made a type 23 object again, and Sabreman is drawn, at Restore_Type_17_Object.
Input
B Y velocity
IX Object record of Sabreman
