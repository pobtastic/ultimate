40227: Draw Bottom Right Corner Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws a picture of the bottom right corner of the frame of a special room, at x position 232 and y position 160 (see Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Picture).
Input
DE Address of the picture
Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Picture 40227 LD HL,41192 Set the x position to 232 and the y position to 160 (41192).
40230 JR Draw_Corner_Picture Jump to Draw_Corner_Picture to draw the picture.
