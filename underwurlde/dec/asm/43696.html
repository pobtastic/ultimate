43696: Update Rope
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope and Handle_Sabreman_Falling.
Reads the controls and updates the rope that Sabreman hangs from.
The left and right controls (bits 0 and 1 of the controls, which are clear when the control is used) push the end of the rope by changing the velocity in Rope_Velocity by 2. That is only done while the displacement of the rope (Rope_Displacement_Pixels) is not more than the length of the rope divided by 8 with a maximum of 8.
The down and up controls (bits 2 and 3) make the rope longer, which lowers Sabreman, and shorter, which raises him. Then Move_Rope moves the rope and Position_Rope_End works out where its end is.
