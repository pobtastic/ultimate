59901: Weapon Placement Table
Four sets of four records of 8 bytes each, which Place_Weapon_Pickups copies to the small object records at Small_Object_Records (see Handle_Weapon_Pickup). A record has the type (104 to 107), the x position, the room x, the y position (159 which is the floor, or 143 for one of them), the room y and the attribute (66 red, 67 magenta, 68 green or 69 cyan). Byte 6 (the counter) is zero and byte 7 holds the flags 5 which are bit 0 (no old image to erase) and bit 2 (falling). Each set has one weapon of each type, but in different places.
