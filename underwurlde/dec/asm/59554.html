59554: Tumble Sabreman
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_Walking and Handle_Sabreman_Falling.
Makes Sabreman tumble after a creature has hit him (see Handle_Flying_Creature), which sets bit 3 of his redraw flags. The sprite id is 24 plus a random number from zero to three (the low bits of FRAMES). Bits 3 and 4 of the redraw flags are reset, he moves up by one pixel, and the countdown in byte 10 is set to 20. The routine goes on at Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling to carry on with the tumbling.
Input
IX Object record of Sabreman
Tumble_Sabreman 59554 LD A,(23672) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
59557 AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits.
59559 ADD A,24 Add 24.
59561 LD (IX+0),A Set the sprite id.
59564 LD A,(IX+15) Fetch the redraw flags.
59567 AND %11100111 Reset bits 3 and 4.
59569 LD (IX+15),A Store the redraw flags.
59572 DEC (IX+3) Move Sabreman up by one pixel.
59575 LD (IX+10),20 Set the countdown to 20.
59579 JP Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling Jump to Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling.
