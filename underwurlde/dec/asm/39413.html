39413: Draw Ceiling
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the ceiling of the room if bit 0 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The ceiling is made of thirteen tiles of two bytes by 32 lines, which are drawn from left to right starting at x position 24 and y position 16. Each tile is chosen from the table at Ceiling_Tile_Table. The first tile that is used depends on the sum of the room x and the room y, which makes the ceiling look different from room to room, and the next tiles come from the following entries of the table.
Finally the attributes of the area are set (see Set_Area_Attributes). The floor, drawn by Draw_Floor, uses this code too.
