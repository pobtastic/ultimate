59217: Handle Collectable
Handler for the object types 100 to 103 (the four kinds of gem, see Handle_Stalactite) and 127 (an extra life, see Extra_Life_Template).
An object that is in a different room from Sabreman is removed. When Sabreman touches the object (see Check_Sabreman_Contact) it is erased and the pickup sound is played (see SoundsSword). An extra life goes on at Collect_Extra_Life. For a gem the score goes up (see AddPointsToScore) by the type divided by two, as a number of hundreds, plus 153. Sabreman turns cyan, the gem timer at Gem_Timer is set to 80 and printed, and the gem is taken out of the entry of the room in the room visit table (see Set_Up_Stalactites), so that it does not come back when the room is drawn again. Bit 0 of byte 6 of the gem is taken to show which of the two bytes of the entry it came from.
Input
IX Object record of the gem or the extra life
