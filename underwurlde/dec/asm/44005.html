44005: Move Rope
Used by the routine at Update_Rope.
Makes the rope swing. The velocity of the end of the rope (Rope_Velocity) is changed by four towards the middle of the swing (it is increased when the displacement is negative and decreased when it is positive) and then it is multiplied by 32 and added to the displacement (Rope_Displacement).
When the rope is already at rest nothing changes. When the end of the rope is moving towards the middle, the change of velocity is left out on one call in eight (the test is made on the tick counter at GameTimer for a negative displacement and on FRAMES for a positive one).
If the length of the rope is less than 128 the displacement is limited to the length multiplied by 176 and divided by 256. When it would go further, the displacement is set to the limit and the velocity and the fraction are set to zero. For a longer rope the displacement is not limited.
