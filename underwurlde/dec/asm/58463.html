58463: Limit Eagle Sideways Movement
Used by the routine at Handle_Carrying_Eagle.
Deals with an eagle that has moved to a new x position that is outside the main part of the room.
The first entry point is for an x position below 128 and the second (Limit_Eagle_Sideways_Movement_Right_Gap) for an x position of 208 or more in a room that has no wall on the right. The eagle bounces off a wall on the left, flies through the gaps in the frame of the room and goes into the next room when it reaches the edge.
A gap sets bits 0 and 4 of the contact flags and of the flags of the room, so that the eagle bounces off it like it would off a ceiling or a floor.
Input
A New x position of the eagle
E Flags of the room
IX Object record of the eagle
