58914: Handle Stalactite
Handler for the object types 60 61 85 86 and 87 the stalactites that hang from the ceiling of a cave room. They are made by Build_Stalactite from the templates at Stalactite_Templates. A stalactite does not move.
It is drawn once, when bit 0 of its redraw flags is set. At the same time, if bit 3 of byte 6 is set, the stalactite has a gem in it. The gem is made as a copy of the record of the stalactite in the record that is 32 bytes on, with the type 100 plus the two low bits of the sum of the room x, the room y and byte 6 (so there are four kinds of gem). Its y position is that of the stalactite less the height of the stalactite (byte 12), its attribute is 69 (cyan) and it is handled by Handle_Collectable. Byte 6 of the stalactite is then cleared, so that only one gem is made.
The attributes of the cells that the stalactite covers are copied from the colour data on every call (see Colour_Data), which keeps the stalactite coloured whatever passes in front of it. The colour data is chosen by the two low bits of the type, using the pointers at Stalactite_Colour_Pointers.
