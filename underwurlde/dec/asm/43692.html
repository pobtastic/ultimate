43692: Room Flags
The flags of the room that Sabreman is in, which are the first byte of its world map entry (see World_Map). The handlers of Sabreman keep it up to date. The main loop tests bit 4 (the floor) at 37375 and Spawn_Falling_Stalactite tests bit 0 (the ceiling).
