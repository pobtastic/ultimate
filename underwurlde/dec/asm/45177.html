45177: Handle Flying Creature
Handler for the object types 48 to 51 (eagles) and 64 to 67 (vultures). The creature flies towards Sabreman, bounces off the decorations and the bubbles, bounces off the frame of the room and flies out of the room through its gaps.
If the creature is in a different room from Sabreman it is moved by Move_Creature_Horizontally (when the room x is different) or by Move_Creature_Vertically (when the room y is different).
In Sabreman's room, an eagle that is above him and touches him picks him up (the type changes to 92 and Handle_Carrying_Eagle takes over). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation and changes every two ticks, and bit 1 follows the direction of the x velocity.
The x velocity and the y velocity (bytes 8 and 9 of the record) each change by two on every call, in the direction of Sabreman, up to a limit of 64. A thrown weapon destroys the creature (see Destroy_Flying_Creature). If the creature touches Sabreman he is pushed away with an x velocity of 3 and tumbles.
The contact flags (byte 11 of the record) use the same bits as the flags of the room (see Draw_Room), and they are combined with those flags in E.
