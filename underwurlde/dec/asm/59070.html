59070: Build Stalactite
Used by the routine at Set_Up_Stalactites.
Makes the object record of one stalactite from a template (see Stalactite_Templates).
The byte that describes the stalactite (one of the two bytes of the entry of the room visit table, see Set_Up_Stalactites) is in B. Bits 1 and 2 of it choose the template. Bits 4 and 5 give the x position, which is that number of sixteen pixels more than C. Bits 6 and 7 give the y position, which is that number of sixteen pixels more than 119. Bit 3 is set if the stalactite has a gem in it (see Handle_Stalactite). Bit 0 tells the first stalactite of a room from the second.
The room x and the room y are those of the object at IX. The attribute is 70 (yellow). The byte is also stored in byte 6 of the record.
Input
A Template number multiplied by two (bits 1 and 2 of B)
B Byte that describes the stalactite
C Smallest x position
DE Address of the stalactite record
HL Address of the table of pointers to the templates
IX Object record of Sabreman
