42035: Handle Sabreman Walking
Handler for the object types 9 to 16 which are the walking sprites of Sabreman (9 to 12 when he walks to the left and 13 to 16 when he walks to the right). It is also the state that he is in when he is standing still.
Each time it saves the object state, handles being carried off (bit 6 of the redraw flags), tumbling (bit 7) and the special state at Tumble_Sabreman (bit 3), checks for contact with the bubbles, the stalactite and the creatures, reads the controls, moves him sideways, and finally keeps him inside the room, moving him to the next room when he walks off the edge.
