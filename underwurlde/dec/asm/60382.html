60382: Place Guardian
Used by the routine at Place_Special_Object.
Puts a guardian in the room, unless it has been destroyed already. It is the continuation of Place_Special_Object when the room of Sabreman is that of an entry of the table of guardians at Guardian_Table.
The rock formation is put in the room first (see Place_Rock_Formation). The guardian is made from a copy of the template at Extra_Life_Template. Its type is 128 134 132 or 130 which depends on the number of the entry, and it is placed at the x position 128 and the y position 159 with the attribute 3 (magenta). Byte 6 of the record holds the number of the entry, which Handle_Guardian uses to set the collected flag of the entry when the guardian is destroyed.
The record that is used is the first of the two special object records, First_Special_Object_Record.

B Number of entries left in the table of guardians, including this one

D Room y of Sabreman
