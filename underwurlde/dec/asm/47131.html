47131: Cave Frame Pictures
The pictures that make up the frame of a cave room (a room with a room y of 16 or more). Tiles for the ceiling, the floor and the walls are chosen by the tables at Ceiling_Tile_Table, Floor_Tile_Table, Left_Wall_Tile_Table and Right_Wall_Tile_Table. Each tile starts with its height and its width (see Draw_Tile). The pictures of the corners have no header, are three bytes wide and 32 lines high and are chosen by the tables at Top_Right_Corner_Table, Bottom_Right_Corner_Table, Bottom_Left_Corner_Table and Top_Left_Corner_Table.
The pixels of every picture come line by line, top line first. The walls are drawn by the tiles that hold them, and the pictures of the corners that have a wall are the first or the last 32 lines of those tiles.
Two more pictures at the end are not used.
