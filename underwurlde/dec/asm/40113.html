40113: Draw Left Wall Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws the picture of the left wall of a special room, at x position 0 and y position 48. The picture is three bytes wide and 112 lines high (see Room_Graphics_Left_Wall).
The attributes of the picture are bright cyan (77). The shared code from Draw_Wall_Picture onwards also draws the right wall (see Draw_Right_Wall_Picture).
Draw_Left_Wall_Picture 40113 LD HL,12288 Set the x position to 0 and the y position to 48 (12288).
40116 LD DE,33639 Point at the picture (33639).
40119 LD C,77 Set the attribute of the picture to 77.
This entry point is used by the routine at Draw_Right_Wall_Picture.
Fill in the attributes of the picture, 14 rows of three cells.
