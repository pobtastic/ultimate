57623: Handle Bubble
Handler for the object types 88 to 91 (a bubble that rises). A bubble is made by Spawn_Bubble, or by Release_Bubble.
The bubble rises by two pixels on each call. The contact flags (byte 11) are set to the flags of the room, which lets other objects stand on top of it (see Check_Bubble_Contact). The counter in byte 6 goes up on each call and makes the two low bits of the type, so the bubble goes through three frames.
A bubble in a room with a ceiling bursts when it reaches the ceiling and becomes a sparkle (the type 114 see Handle_Sparkle). A bubble in a room with no ceiling moves into the room above when it reaches the top. A bubble that has just been made in a room with a floor and a ceiling, and is 175 or more down the screen, is removed.
If the bubble is in a different room from Sabreman, Move_Distant_Bubble moves it.
