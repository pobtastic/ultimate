45018: Spawn Flying Creature
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Lets a flying creature into the room every now and then. Nothing happens unless the sum of the tick counter and the frame counter is a multiple of eight.
If one of the four creature records that start at First_Creature_Record is free, it is filled in with a type that comes from bits 2 and 4 of the tick counter: 48 (eagle), 52 (gargoyle), 64 (vulture) or 68 (jellyfish). Only the vultures and the jellyfish appear until the flag at Creature_Spawn_Flag has been set, which happens when Sabreman kills a guardian.
The creature starts in a room next to the one that Sabreman is in. It is on the left or the right if the room has a gap in that wall (the choice is made from bit 0 of the tick counter), and it is above or below otherwise. Its position inside the room, and its attribute, come from the frame counter.
Handle_Flying_Creature then makes the creature fly into the room that Sabreman is in.
Spawn_Flying_Creature 45018 LD A,(36566) Fetch the tick counter from GameTimer and keep it in B.
45021 LD B,A
45022 LD A,(23672) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
45025 ADD A,B Add the tick counter to make a pseudo-random number.
45026 AND %00000111 Keep only the low three bits.
45028 RET NZ Return unless the result is zero (a creature can only appear on one pass in eight).
45029 LD HL,36826 Point at the first of the four creature records (First_Creature_Record).
45032 LD DE,16 Set the size of a record to 16.
45035 LD B,4 Set the number of records to 4.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_Find_Record 45037 LD A,(HL) Fetch the type of the record.
45038 AND A Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_Choose_Type if the record is not in use (the type is zero).
45039 JR Z,Spawn_Flying_Creature_Choose_Type
45041 ADD HL,DE Move on to the next record.
45042 DJNZ Spawn_Flying_Creature_Find_Record Loop back until all of the records have been tried.
45044 RET Return as all of the records are in use.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_Choose_Type 45045 LD A,(43692) Fetch the flags of the room that Sabreman is in.
45048 LD E,A Store them in E.
45049 LD A,(36601) Fetch the creature spawn flag.
45052 AND A Test the flag.
45053 LD A,(36566) Fetch the tick counter.
45056 JR NZ,Spawn_Flying_Creature_Store_Type Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_Store_Type if the flag is set.
45058 OR %00010000 Set bit 4, which limits the choice to the vulture and the jellyfish.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_Store_Type 45060 PUSH AF Stash the tick counter on the stack.
45061 AND %00010100 Keep only bits 2 and 4.
45063 ADD A,48 Add 48 to make the type.
45065 LD (HL),A Store the type in the record.
45066 POP AF Restore the tick counter.
45067 INC HL Move on to the x position.
45068 BIT 0,A Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_Test_Left if bit 0 of the tick counter is set.
45070 JR NZ,Spawn_Flying_Creature_Test_Left
Bit 0 of the tick counter is reset, so use the right of the room if it has no right wall, and the left otherwise.
45072 BIT 2,E Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_From_Right if the room has no right wall.
45074 JR Z,Spawn_Flying_Creature_From_Right
45076 BIT 6,E Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_Vertically if the room has a left wall too.
45078 JR NZ,Spawn_Flying_Creature_Vertically
The creature comes in from the room on the left.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_From_Left 45080 AND %01111111 Keep only the low seven bits of the tick counter.
45082 ADD A,72 Add 72 to make the x position.
45084 LD (HL),A Store the x position.
45085 INC HL Move on to the room x.
45086 LD A,(36636) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
45089 DEC A Subtract one to get the room on the left.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_Store_Room_X 45090 LD (HL),A Store the room x.
45091 INC HL Move on to the y position.
45092 LD A,(23672) Fetch the low byte of the frame counter.
45095 AND %00111111 Keep only the low six bits.
45097 ADD A,64 Add 64 to make the y position.
45099 LD (HL),A Store the y position.
45100 INC HL Move on to the room y.
45101 LD A,(36638) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
45104 LD (HL),A Store it as the room y of the creature.
45105 INC HL Move on to the attribute.
45106 LD A,(23672) Fetch the low byte of the frame counter.
45109 AND %00000011 Keep only the low two bits.
45111 ADD A,66 Add 66 to make a bright attribute.
45113 LD (HL),A Store the attribute of the creature.
45114 RET Return.
Spawn_Flying_Creature_Test_Left 45115 BIT 6,E Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_From_Left if the room has no left wall.
45117 JR Z,Spawn_Flying_Creature_From_Left
45119 BIT 2,E Jump to Spawn_Flying_Creature_Vertically if the room has a right wall.
45121 JR NZ,Spawn_Flying_Creature_Vertically
The creature comes in from the room on the right.
