30343: Special Room Pictures

There are no tiles here: each part of the frame is a ready-made picture that is copied to the screen. \p A corner picture is three bytes wide and 32 lines high and starts with its twelve attribute bytes (three cells wide by four cells high), followed by the pixels, top line first.
