40232: Draw Special Room Parts
The routines that draw the parts of the frame of a special room that are chosen by the four tables at Special_Room_Frame_Tables. Each entry draws one to three pictures (see Room_Graphics) and returns to the code that called it. The entries are in the order of their addresses: they are not grouped by table.
Top right: the ceiling and a corner. Used for code 3 of the first table.
Draw_Special_Room_Parts 40232 CALL Draw_Ceiling_Picture Draw the ceiling.
40235 LD DE,33195 Point at the top right corner picture (33195).
40238 JR Draw_Top_Right_Corner_Picture Jump to Draw_Top_Right_Corner_Picture to draw the corner.

40243 CALL Draw_Top_Right_Corner_Picture Draw the corner.
