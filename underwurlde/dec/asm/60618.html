60618: Handle Guardian
Handler for the object types 128 to 135 the four guardians that stand in the caves: the imps (128 129 132 and 133 see Sprite_Imp_1), the spiders (130 and 131 see Sprite_Spider_1) and the bats (134 and 135 see Sprite_Bat_1). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation and it is flipped whenever bit 0 of the tick counter is set. Place_Guardian puts a guardian in its room, where it stays until Sabreman leaves the room (see Handle_Collectable_Remove).
A guardian is destroyed by one kind of weapon, which depends on bits 1 and 2 of its type: the daggers (the types 108 and 109) destroy a spider, the arrows (110 and 111) destroy the imp of the type 132 and the fire (112 and 113) destroys a bat. The imp of the type 128 cannot be destroyed. When a guardian is destroyed its collected flag is set (see Set_Collected_Flag), the flag at Creature_Spawn_Flag is set and the guardian explodes (see Destroy_Creature).
A guardian that is touched by Sabreman makes him tumble (bit 7 of his redraw flags is set) and pushes him to the left (his x velocity is set to 254).
