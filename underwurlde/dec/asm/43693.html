43693: Skip Rope Push
Used by the routine at Update_Rope.
Used by Update_Rope when the end of the rope has already swung as far as the length of the rope allows, so that the left and right controls are ignored. It restores the controls from AF' and carries on at Update_Rope_Fetch_Length.
Skip_Rope_Push 43693 EX AF,AF' Fetch the controls again from AF'.
43694 JR Update_Rope_Fetch_Length Jump to Update_Rope_Fetch_Length without pushing the rope.
