43683: Rope Displacement
The displacement of the end of the rope from the vertical, as a word. The low byte is a fraction. The high byte, at 43684 is the displacement in pixels (it is negative when the rope swings to the left). It is zero when the rope hangs straight down.
Rope_Displacement 43683 DEFB 0 The fraction.
Rope_Displacement_Pixels 43684 DEFB 0 The displacement in pixels.
