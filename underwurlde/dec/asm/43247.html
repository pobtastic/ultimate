43247: Handle Tumbling At Right Edge
Used by the routine at Handle_Sabreman_Tumbling.
Deals with Sabreman when he tumbles and his x position has gone past the right-hand side of the room. It is entered from 43454 with the new x position in A and the room flags in E.
If the x position is less than 207 nothing happens and the routine goes on at Apply_Tumbling_Gravity. If the right wall is solid (bit 2 of E), the x velocity is reversed and the x position is set to 207. If it is not, the contact flags are given the bits 17 and, once the x position reaches 232 he enters the room on the right.
Input
A New x position of Sabreman
E Flags of the room
