58518: Limit Eagle Vertical Movement
Used by the routine at Handle_Carrying_Eagle.
Deals with an eagle that has moved to a new y position that is outside the main part of the room.
The first entry point is for a y position of less than 128 (at the top of the room) and the second (Limit_Eagle_Vertical_Movement_Bottom_Gap) for a y position of 159 or more in a room that has no floor. The eagle bounces off a ceiling, flies through the gaps in the frame of the room and goes into the next room when it reaches the edge.
A gap sets bits 2 and 6 of the contact flags, so that the eagle bounces off it like it would off a wall.
Input
A New y position of the eagle
E Flags of the room
IX Object record of the eagle
