36906: Small Object Records
Five records of 8 bytes each. They are handled in the same way as the larger records, after all of those have been handled (see Finish_Object_Pass).
The first four are the weapons that Sabreman can pick up. Place_Weapon_Pickups copies their starting positions from Weapon_Placement_Table at the start of the game.
