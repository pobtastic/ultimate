40993: Draw Lives
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Draws a small Sabreman icon and prints the number of lives left (see PlayerLives) at the top right of the screen. The entry at Draw_Lives_Print_Number only prints the number of lives.
Before After Draw_Lives
Draw_Lives 40993 PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
40995 LD IX,41021 Point at the object record of the icon (Extra_Life_Icon_Record).
40999 CALL DrawEntity Draw the icon.
41002 CALL ColourEntity Colour the icon.
41005 POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
This entry point is used by the routines at Collect_Extra_Life and Handle_Death.
Draw_Lives_Print_Number 41007 LD HL,2288 Set the x position to 240 and the y position to 8 (2288).
41010 CALL ScreenAddress Convert the position into a screen address.
41013 LD DE,36570 Point at the number of lives (PlayerLives).
41016 LD B,1 Set the number of digits to print to 1.
41018 JP Print_Status_Panel_Digits Jump to Print_Status_Panel_Digits to print the digits.
