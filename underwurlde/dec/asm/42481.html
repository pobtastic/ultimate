42481: Handle Sabreman Jumping
Handler for the object types 17 to 20 which are the jumping sprites of Sabreman (17 and 18 when he jumps to the left and 19 and 20 when he jumps to the right).
The countdown in the object record is decreased on every call. When it reaches 3 the sprite moves on to the next frame, and when it reaches zero the jump is over and the sprite becomes 21 or 22 (falling), so that Handle_Sabreman_Falling takes over. The controls steer him left or right while he is in the air.
At the end of the jump the y position is moved up by 4 and the countdown is set to 12. If that takes him into the top part of the room and the room has a ceiling, he hits it and tumbles at Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling.
Input
IX Object record of Sabreman
Save the state of the object, then deal with the special states in the same way as Handle_Sabreman_Walking.
