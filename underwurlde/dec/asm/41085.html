41085: Play Low Tone
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_Falling and Handle_Sabreman_Tumbling.
Plays a tone of 64 cycles with a half period of 64.
Play_Low_Tone 41085 LD BC,16448 BC=16448 (half period 64 64 cycles).
41088 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
