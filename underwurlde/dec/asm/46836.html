46836: Check Weapon Record Hit
Used by the routine at Check_Weapon_Hit.
Checks whether a weapon is touching an object. The weapon hits the object if it is in the same room, less than 16 pixels away horizontally and less than 16 pixels away vertically (allowing 4 pixels for the difference in their sizes). When it hits, bit 0 of byte 7 of the weapon is set, which makes Move_Thrown_Weapon remove the weapon.
Input
HL Weapon record
IX Object record
Output
D 255 if the weapon hits the object, otherwise 0
E Type of the weapon
