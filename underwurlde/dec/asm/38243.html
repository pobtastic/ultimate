38243: Clear Workspace
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Sets up the registers to clear the 25 bytes of the sprite drawing workspace and the other variables from Previous_Sprite_Id to Creature_Spawn_Flag (which includes the score and the completion percentage).
Clear_Workspace 38243 LD BC,25 Set the number of bytes of the workspace to clear to 25.
38246 LD HL,36577 Point at the start of the workspace (Previous_Sprite_Id).
Continue on to FillMemory.
