57383: Handle Rock Fragments
Handler for the object types 81 to 83 (the pieces of a stalactite that has landed). The counter in byte 6 goes up on every call. The type goes up by one on every second call, and when it would go past 83 the piece moves four pixels to the left and is destroyed (see Destroy_Creature). The piece is removed if Sabreman is not in the room.
