58651: Check Eagle Release
Used by the routine at Handle_Carrying_Eagle.
Makes the eagle let go of Sabreman, if it is in the open part of the room (the x position is from 24 to 207 and the y position is from 72 to 159). If it is not, the routine returns and the eagle goes on carrying him.
When the eagle lets go, the routine does not return to its caller but carries on at Release_Sabreman, which ends the handler. The position of Sabreman is set to the position of the eagle, 16 further down but no lower than 159 if the room has a floor. The contact flags of Sabreman and the target of the eagle are cleared.
