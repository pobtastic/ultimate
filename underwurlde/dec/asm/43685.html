43685: Rope Length
The length of the rope, as a word. Handle_Rope makes the low byte grow to 56 when a rope is first drawn, and Lower_Rope and Raise_Rope change it when Sabreman climbs down or up. Each time the length passes a multiple of 128 the rope reaches the next room down.
Rope_Length 43685 DEFB 96 The low byte.
Rope_Length_High_Byte 43686 DEFB 0 The high byte.
