58059: Handle Carrying Eagle
Handler for the object types 92 to 95 an eagle that carries Sabreman (see Handle_Flying_Creature). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation, which follows bit 1 of the tick counter, and bit 1 is set when the x velocity is negative.
While Sabreman is being carried his record has the sprite id 96 to 99. The eagle puts his position in bytes 8 (x) and 9 (y) of his record (at Sabreman_Carried_X_Position and Sabreman_Carried_Y_Position), where Handle_Carried_Sabreman picks it up. The y position is 16 more than that of the eagle, so he hangs below it.
The eagle flies towards a target (byte 12 is the x position and byte 14 the y position, see Choose_Eagle_Target). Each call changes each velocity by one towards the target. The x velocity is kept between 208 and 55 and the y velocity between 199 and 55. The target changes when the eagle bounces off a decoration, a bubble, the floor, the ceiling or a wall. The eagle can also fly through the gaps in the frame of a room, and into the next room. The player can nudge the eagle with the controls (see Steer_Eagle).
The eagle lets go (see Check_Eagle_Release) when the low byte of FRAMES is zero, or when Sabreman has set bit 5 of his redraw flags, if it is in the open part of the room. If the sprite id of Sabreman is less than 28 (he is not being carried), the eagle lets go at once (see Release_Sabreman).
