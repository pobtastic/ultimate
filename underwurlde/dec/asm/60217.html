60217: Handle Coloured Decoration
Handler for the object types 121 to 126: the two pictures, the bookshelf, the hanging bowl, the bowl of flowers and the torch (see Sprite_Picture_1). They are coloured by copying the attributes from the colour data (see Colour_Data) rather than from the single attribute of the record, so that they are not drawn in one colour only. The other decorations are handled by Handle_Plain_Decoration.
A decoration is removed (see Handle_Collectable_Remove) when Sabreman is not in its room. The first time that it is handled Set_Decoration_Size gives it the size of its obstacle and it is drawn. It is then coloured every time that it is handled, by Handle_Stalactite_Colour_Copy.
Input
IX Object record of the decoration
Handle_Coloured_Decoration 60217 LD A,(36636) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
60220 CP (IX+2) Jump to Handle_Collectable_Remove to remove the decoration if it is in a different room x.
60223 JP NZ,Handle_Collectable_Remove
60226 LD A,(36638) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
60229 CP (IX+4) Jump to Handle_Collectable_Remove to remove the decoration if it is in a different room y.
60232 JP NZ,Handle_Collectable_Remove
60235 CALL PrepDraw2 Prepare the sprite of the decoration.
60238 BIT 0,(IX+15) Jump to Handle_Coloured_Decoration_Colour if the decoration has been drawn already (bit 0 of the redraw flags is clear).
60242 JR Z,Handle_Coloured_Decoration_Colour
60244 RES 0,(IX+15) Reset bit 0 of the redraw flags.
60248 CALL Set_Decoration_Size Copy the size of the obstacle to the object record.
60251 CALL DrawEntity Draw the decoration.
Handle_Coloured_Decoration_Colour 60254 LD A,(IX+0) Fetch the type of the decoration.
60257 SUB 121 Subtract 121 to make the number of the decoration.
60259 SLA A Double it, as the pointers are two bytes long.
60261 LD E,A Store the result in E.
60262 LD D,0 Clear the high byte of the offset.
60264 LD HL,60274 Point HL at Decoration_Colour_Pointers.
60267 ADD HL,DE Make HL point at the pointer for the decoration.
60268 LD E,(HL) Fetch the low byte of the pointer.
60269 INC HL Move on to the high byte.
60270 LD D,(HL) Fetch the high byte, which makes DE point at the colour data.
60271 JP Handle_Stalactite_Colour_Copy Jump to Handle_Stalactite_Colour_Copy to colour the decoration.
