39864: Draw Top Left Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the top left corner of the frame of the room if bit 7 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is drawn at x position 0 y position 16 by Draw_Corner_Shape. Its shape depends on whether the ceiling (bit 0) and the left wall (bit 6) are present.
Input
DE Frame flags of the room in E
IX Object record of Sabreman
Draw_Top_Left_Corner 39864 PUSH DE Stash the frame flags on the stack.
39865 LD HL,4096 Set the x position to 0 and the y position to 16 (4096).
39868 LD A,E Copy the frame flags to A.
39869 LD DE,39896 Point at the table of corner shapes (Top_Left_Corner_Table).
39872 BIT 7,A Jump to 39879 to clear the area if the corner is not present.
39874 JP Z,Clear_Panel
39876 SBC A,C
39877 PUSH HL Stash the position on the stack.
39878 EX DE,HL Exchange the registers so that HL holds the address of the table.
39879 BIT 0,A Jump to Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall if the ceiling is not present.
39881 JR Z,Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall
39883 INC HL Skip the shapes for a ceiling.
39884 INC HL
Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall 39885 BIT 6,A Jump to Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape if the left wall is not present.
39887 JR Z,Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape
39889 INC HL Skip the shapes for a left wall.
39890 INC HL
39891 INC HL
39892 INC HL
Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape 39893 JP Draw_Corner_Shape Jump to Draw_Corner_Shape to draw the shape.
