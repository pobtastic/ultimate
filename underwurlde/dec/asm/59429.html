59429: Clear Object Records
Used by the routines at Run_Game and Handle_Death.
Clears the object records from the record of Sabreman (at Sabreman_Record) up to the end of the records at Small_Object_Records, which are 272 bytes.
Clear_Object_Records 59429 LD HL,36634 Point HL at the record of Sabreman.
59432 LD BC,272 Set the number of bytes to clear to 272.
Clear_Object_Records_Loop 59435 LD (HL),0 Clear a byte.
59437 INC HL Move on to the next byte.
59438 DEC BC Decrease the byte counter and loop back until all of the object records have been cleared.
59439 LD A,B
59440 OR C
59441 JR NZ,Clear_Object_Records_Loop
