46931: Weapon Projectile Types
The type of the projectile that Throw_Weapon makes for each of the four kinds of weapon that Sabreman can carry (the types 104 to 107). A weapon that is thrown to the left has a type that is one more.
Weapon_Projectile_Types 46931 DEFB 56 Weapon 104 gives type 56 (see Handle_Thrown_Weapon).
46932 DEFB 110 Weapon 105 gives type 110 (see Handle_Projectile).
46933 DEFB 112 Weapon 106 gives type 112 (see Handle_Fire).
46934 DEFB 108 Weapon 107 gives type 108 (see Handle_Projectile).
