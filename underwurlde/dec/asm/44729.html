44729: Handle Plant
Handler for the object types 28 to 43 the plants (mushrooms, pods, flytraps, weeds and bellflowers) that stand in some of the rooms. Each plant has two frames, and bit 0 of its type picks the frame.
A plant is set up the first time that it is handled after its room has been drawn (see Handle_Plant_Set_Up). After that it makes Sabreman tumble if he touches it, by setting bit 7 of his redraw flags (see Start_Tumbling).
