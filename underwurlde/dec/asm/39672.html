39672: Draw Bottom Right Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the bottom right corner of the frame of the room if bit 3 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is drawn at x position 232 y position 160 by Draw_Corner_Shape. Its shape depends on whether the floor (bit 4) and the right wall (bit 2) are present.
Input
DE Frame flags of the room in E
IX Object record of Sabreman
