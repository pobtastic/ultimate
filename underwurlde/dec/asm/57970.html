57970: Start Tumbling Downwards
Used by the routine at Handle_Sabreman_Walking.
Makes Sabreman tumble downwards, as when a bubble pushes him against a ceiling. The sprite becomes 24 the countdown (byte 10) is set to 26 and the y velocity to 32.
The routine ends by drawing Sabreman and returning to the main loop.
