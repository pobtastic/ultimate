43301: Handle Sabreman Tumbling
Handler for the object types 24 to 27 which are the tumbling sprites of Sabreman. The sprite id is 24 plus bits 1 and 2 of the tick counter at GameTimer, so the frames follow each other as time passes.
He tumbles after the stalactite or a creature has hit him and when he bumps into a side wall while he is falling. The routine decreases the countdown, checks for contact with the bubbles and the creatures (which push him sideways with an x velocity of 2 or 254), moves him sideways and applies gravity in the same way as Handle_Sabreman_Falling, and goes on at Handle_Sabreman_Falling_Above_Floor once the y position is less than 159.
Unlike the falling handler the y velocity here has a limit of 118.
