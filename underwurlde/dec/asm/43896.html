43896: Position Rope End
Used by the routines at Handle_Rope and Update_Rope.
Works out the position of the end of the rope and the position of the point that it hangs from.
The end of the rope is below the point that it hangs from by a depth that is found from the displacement of the rope (Rope_Displacement_Pixels) and its length (Rope_Length). The routine squares the displacement, squares the length, subtracts the first square from the second and takes the square root of the result with Calculate_Square_Root. When the length is 256 or more, the length and the square of the displacement are halved before they are used.
The x position is the displacement plus 128 and it is stored in Rope_End_Position. The y position is stored in the byte after it. The point that the rope hangs from is stored in Rope_Anchor_Position: it is 36 pixels down at an x position of 128 when the length is less than 128 and 16 pixels down when it is longer.
