37236: Messaging: Copyright
The first byte is the attribute used for the whole message, followed by the text, which ends with a character that has bit 7 set. The character < is the copyright symbol and : is a full stop.
Message_Copyright 37236 DEFB 71 Attribute: INK:WHITE, PAPER:BLACK (BRIGHT).
37237 DEFM "<1984 A:C:G: ALL RIGHTS RESERVE","D"+128 "<1984 A:C:G: ALL RIGHTS RESERVED"
