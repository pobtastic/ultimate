57576: Move Distant Bubble
Used by the routine at Handle_Bubble.
Moves a bubble that is not in the room that Sabreman is in. The bubble rises by two pixels each time. The countdown in byte 10 removes the bubble when it reaches zero. A bubble in a room with a ceiling is removed when it reaches the ceiling. A bubble in a room with no ceiling moves into the room above when it reaches the top.
Input
