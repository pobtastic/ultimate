57176: Spawn Falling Stalactite
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Lets a stalactite fall from the ceiling of a cave room every now and then. Nothing happens in a special room, in a room that has no ceiling, or while the stalactite record at Falling_Stalactite_Record is in use. The stalactite is only made when the low four bits of the tick counter and the frame counter, combined, are zero.
The record is a copy of the record of Sabreman, so the stalactite falls from the same x position, with the type 80 the y position 64 the attribute 70 (yellow) and a y velocity of 16 (see Handle_Falling_Stalactite).

57182 LD A,(43692) Fetch the flags of the room that Sabreman is in.

57187 RET Z Return if the room has no ceiling.
57188 LD A,(36666) Fetch the type of the stalactite record.
57191 AND A Return if the record is already in use.

57197 LD A,(23672) Fetch the low byte of the frame counter.

57203 RET NZ Return unless they are zero.
