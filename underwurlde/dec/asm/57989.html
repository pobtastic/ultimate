57989: Check Sabreman Contact
Used by the routines at Handle_Flying_Creature, Handle_Collectable and Handle_Weapon_Pickup.
Checks whether an object is touching Sabreman.
Nothing is touched if the sprite id of Sabreman is 29 or more (he is dying or being carried), or if bit 6 of his redraw flags is set (he is being carried).
There is contact when the two are in the same room, the x positions differ by less than 16 and the y position of Sabreman, less four, differs from that of the object by less than 10. A tone is played on contact (see Play_Position_Sound).
Input
IX Object record
Output
C Contact result
Check_Sabreman_Contact 57989 LD C,0 Clear the contact result.
57991 LD A,(36634) Fetch the sprite id of Sabreman.
57994 CP 29 Return if it is 29 or more.
57996 RET NC
