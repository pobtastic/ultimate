43764: Lower Rope
Used by the routine at Update_Rope.
Makes the rope longer by two, which lowers Sabreman.
When the length reaches a multiple of 128 he is at the bottom of the room. If the room has a floor he cannot go on and the length is only made longer by one. Otherwise the length is made longer by two more, the world map entries of the room and of the room below are given the second byte that marks where the rope runs (see Set_Up_Rope), Sabreman moves down to the room below and the room is drawn.
