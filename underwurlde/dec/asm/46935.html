46935: Room Object X Positions
The x positions of the plant and the rock that Set_Up_Room_Objects puts in a room that has both a ceiling and a floor. The offset into the table is the sum of the room x and the room y, modulo 16. The plant gets the byte at that offset and the rock gets the byte after it.
