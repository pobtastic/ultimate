59201: Stalactite Template Pointers
Two tables of four pointers to the templates of the stalactites at Stalactite_Templates, which are chosen by bits 1 and 2 of the byte that describes the stalactite (see Build_Stalactite).
The first table is used for the first stalactite of a room (on the left, with an x position from 24) and the second for the second stalactite (on the right, with an x position from 160).
The first table, with the templates of the types 85 86 87 and 87.
