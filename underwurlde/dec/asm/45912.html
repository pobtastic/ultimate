45912: Move Creature Vertically
Used by the routines at Handle_Flying_Creature, Move_Creature_Horizontally, Handle_Jellyfish and Handle_Gargoyle.
Moves a creature one step closer to Sabreman's room when it is in a different room y (and the same room x). It works like Move_Creature_Horizontally but vertically. It is entered with the carry flag set if Sabreman's room y is less than the room y of the creature.
The creature is removed if the room has a floor and it is above Sabreman's room (or a ceiling and it is below), as it cannot get through. The x position is kept between 24 and 224.
