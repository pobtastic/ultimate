41095: Play Alternating Tone
Used by the routine at Handle_Sabreman_Walking.
Plays a short tone on one call in every four. Each call adds one to the sound counter (see Sound_Counter) and it returns at once unless the two low bits of the counter are zero. When it does play, it uses a half period of 64 if bit 2 of the counter is set and 96 if it is clear, and plays four cycles.
Play_Alternating_Tone 41095 LD HL,36598 Point at the sound counter (Sound_Counter).
41098 INC (HL) Add one to the counter.
41099 LD A,(HL) Fetch the counter.
41100 AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits of the sound counter.
41102 RET NZ Return unless the sound counter is a multiple of four.
41103 BIT 2,(HL) Jump to Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower if bit 2 of the counter is clear.
41105 JR Z,Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower
41107 LD BC,16388 BC=16388 (half period 64 four cycles).
41110 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower 41112 LD BC,24580 BC=24580 (half period 96 four cycles).
41115 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
