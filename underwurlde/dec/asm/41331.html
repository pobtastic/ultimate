41331: Sounds: Bounce
Used by the routine at Make_Dust_Puff.
Plays four cycles of a tone with a half period of 231 which is a low pitch. The counter in D is set to 64 and the tone is made from the counter after one has been taken off it.
The routine jumps to the tone player, which returns to the caller.
SoundsBounce 41331 LD D,64 D=64 (the counter).
41333 DEC D Decrease the tone counter and return if it is zero (this does not happen when the routine is entered at the start).
41334 RET Z
41335 LD A,D Fetch the counter.
41336 RRCA Rotate the counter right three times.
41337 RRCA
41338 RRCA
41339 LD B,A Use the result as the half period.
41340 LD C,4 Set the number of cycles to 4.
41342 JP SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
