45775: Move Creature Horizontally
Used by the routines at Handle_Flying_Creature, Handle_Jellyfish and Handle_Gargoyle.
Moves a creature one step closer to Sabreman's room when it is in a different room x. It does not draw the creature. It is entered with the carry flag set if Sabreman's room x is less than the room x of the creature.
The creature is flagged so that it will be drawn without being erased when it arrives. It accelerates by two on each call in the direction of Sabreman's room, up to a speed of 48 and its x position changes by a sixteenth of the speed. When the x position goes past 208 (or below 24) it moves into the next room.
The y position is kept at 64 or more, and when it is 128 or more it moves up two pixels each time.
A creature cannot leave its room through a wall. If the wall is on the side that it is going to, it is removed when it is in the same room y as Sabreman, and otherwise Move_Creature_Vertically moves it up or down.
