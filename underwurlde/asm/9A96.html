9A96: Top Right Corner Table
The addresses of the shapes of the top right corner. Each shape is three bytes wide and 20 lines high and has no header. There are four sets of four shapes, one set for each value of the sum of the room x and the room y (modulo four). Within a set the shapes are for: no ceiling and no right wall, ceiling only, right wall only, and both.
