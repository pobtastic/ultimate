B753: Weapon Projectile Types
The type of the projectile that Throw_Weapon makes for each of the four kinds of weapon that Sabreman can carry (the types 68 to 6B). A weapon that is thrown to the left has a type that is one more.
Weapon_Projectile_Types B753 DEFB $38 Weapon 68 gives type 38 (see Handle_Thrown_Weapon).
B754 DEFB $6E Weapon 69 gives type 6E (see Handle_Projectile).
B755 DEFB $70 Weapon 6A gives type 70 (see Handle_Fire).
B756 DEFB $6C Weapon 6B gives type 6C (see Handle_Projectile).
