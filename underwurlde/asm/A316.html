A316: Handle Sabreman On Rope
Handler for the object types 01 to 08 which Sabreman has while he hangs on and swings from the rope (the type 17 object, see Handle_Rope). The sprites are the ones that are called swimming (01 to 04 when he moves to the left and 05 to 08 when he moves to the right; the frame follows the x position).
If bit 3 or bit 6 of the redraw flags is set, he lets go at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go. Otherwise the routine checks for contact with the bubbles, the creatures and the falling stalactite, any of which sends him to Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope with B=10. Next it moves the rope with Update_Rope and, if he stays in the same room, reads the controls: a bottom row key (or the joystick fire button) makes him let go at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go, and the fire key throws a weapon.
The position and the velocity of the end of the rope (see Line_Step_Count) are then converted to the position and the velocity of Sabreman. If the y position is 9F or more and the room has a floor, he lets go and stands on the floor at Stand_Sabreman_On_Floor.

A31C AND %01001000 Keep only bit 3 (the special state) and bit 6 (being carried).

A321 CALL Check_Bubble_Contact Check for contact with the bubbles.
A324 BIT 2,C Jump to Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Hit if Sabreman touches a bubble (bit 2 of C).

A32B BIT 2,C Test bit 2 of C (set if Sabreman touches one).

A32F JP NZ,Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope Jump to Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope to knock Sabreman off the rope if he touches a bubble or a creature.
A332 CALL Check_Stalactite_Contact Check for contact with the falling stalactite.
A335 BIT 2,C Jump to Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope to knock Sabreman off the rope if the stalactite hits him.
A337 JP NZ,Knock_Sabreman_Off_Rope
A33A LD L,(IX+$02) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.

A344 POP HL Restore the room that Sabreman was in before the rope moved.
A345 LD E,(IX+$02) Fetch the room x that Sabreman is in now.
A348 LD D,(IX+$04) Fetch the room y that Sabreman is in now.
A34B AND A Compare the old room with the new room.
A34C SBC HL,DE
A34E JR NZ,Set_Position_From_Rope Jump to Set_Position_From_Rope if the swing of the rope has taken him to a different room.

A353 BIT 5,A Jump to Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go and let go of the rope if the "bottom row key" bit of the controls (bit 5) is clear.
A355 JR Z,Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go
A357 BIT 4,A Jump to Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Fire_Released if the fire key is not pressed (bit 4 of the controls is set).
A359 JR NZ,Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Fire_Released

A35E JR Set_Position_From_Rope Jump to Set_Position_From_Rope.

Sabreman is hit or lets go of the rope, and starts to fall from the position of the end of the rope. The velocities are taken from the change in that position and the sprite id becomes 15 or 16 (falling).
Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go A3A5 LD HL,($AA95) Fetch the position of the end of the rope.
A3A8 LD DE,($AA97) Fetch the previous position of the end of the rope.
A3AC LD A,H Copy the y position of the end of the rope to A.
A3AD SUB D Subtract the previous y position to get how far the end of the rope moved.
A3AE SLA A Multiply the movement by eight to get the y velocity.
A3B0 SLA A
A3B2 SLA A
A3B4 LD (IX+$09),A Give Sabreman the y velocity.
A3B7 LD A,L Copy the x position of the end of the rope to A.
A3B8 SUB E Subtract the previous x position to get how far the end of the rope moved.
A3B9 LD (IX+$06),A Give Sabreman that as his x velocity.
A3BC LD A,$15 Use the sprite id for falling to the left (15).
A3BE BIT 7,(IX+$06) Jump to Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go_Set_Sprite if he is moving to the left (the x velocity is negative).
A3C2 JR NZ,Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go_Set_Sprite
A3C4 ADD A,$01 Use the sprite id for falling to the right (16).
Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope_Let_Go_Set_Sprite A3C6 LD (IX+$00),A Set the sprite id.
A3C9 LD (IX+$0A),$20 Set the countdown to 20.
A3CD LD A,(IX+$06) Fetch the x velocity.
A3D0 OR (IX+$09) Combine it with the y velocity to see if Sabreman is moving at all.
A3D3 JP NZ,Restore_Type_17_Object Jump to Restore_Type_17_Object to start him falling if Sabreman is moving.
A3D6 LD (IX+$09),$10 Set the y velocity to 10 so that he falls.
A3DA JP Restore_Type_17_Object Jump to Restore_Type_17_Object.
