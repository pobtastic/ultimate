9C62: Reset Room Table
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Writes 00 to 07FF bytes starting at Room_Visit_Table, which empties the room visit table.
Reset_Room_Table 9C62 LD HL,$6000 Point at the start of the room visit table (Room_Visit_Table).
9C65 LD DE,$6001 DE=6001 (the second byte of the table, which is the destination of the copy).
9C68 LD BC,$07FF Set the byte count to 07FF.
9C6B LD (HL),$00 Write 00 to the first byte.
9C6D LDIR Copy the byte through the rest of the area.
9C6F RET Return.
