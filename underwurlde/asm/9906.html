9906: Draw Room
Used by the routines at Run_Game, Handle_Sabreman_Walking, Handle_Falling_At_Right_Edge, Handle_Sabreman_Falling, Handle_Tumbling_At_Right_Edge, Handle_Sabreman_Tumbling, Lower_Rope, Raise_Rope, Check_Bubble_Contact, Move_Eagle_To_Next_Room and Handle_Death.
Draws the room that Sabreman is in and then carries on with the main loop. It does not return to its caller: it resets the stack.
First the play area is cleared. Then, for the rooms in the lower part of the map (the room y is 10 or more), the eight parts of the frame of the room are drawn, in the order of the bits of the first byte of the world map entry (see Draw_Ceiling), the objects of the room are set up and the room is marked as visited.
The rooms with a room y below 10 are special rooms, which are drawn by Draw_Special_Room_Frame, and which get their decorations from Load_Room_Decorations. A room y of FF is the way out of the Underwurlde and shows the ending (see Show_Ending_Screen).
