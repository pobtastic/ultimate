C4F7: Sprite: Sabreman Swimming

The first two are also used for the types 62 and 63 of Sabreman when he is carried by an eagle (see Handle_Carried_Sabreman). The sprites at Sprite_Sabreman_Swimming_4 are the same poses the other way round.
