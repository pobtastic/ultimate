E8A2: Tumble Sabreman
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_Walking and Handle_Sabreman_Falling.
Makes Sabreman tumble after a creature has hit him (see Handle_Flying_Creature), which sets bit 3 of his redraw flags. The sprite id is 18 plus a random number from zero to three (the low bits of FRAMES). Bits 3 and 4 of the redraw flags are reset, he moves up by one pixel, and the countdown in byte 0A is set to 14. The routine goes on at Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling to carry on with the tumbling.
Input
IX Object record of Sabreman
Tumble_Sabreman E8A2 LD A,($5C78) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
E8A5 AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits.
E8A7 ADD A,$18 Add 18.
E8A9 LD (IX+$00),A Set the sprite id.
E8AC LD A,(IX+$0F) Fetch the redraw flags.
E8AF AND %11100111 Reset bits 3 and 4.
E8B1 LD (IX+$0F),A Store the redraw flags.
E8B4 DEC (IX+$03) Move Sabreman up by one pixel.
E8B7 LD (IX+$0A),$14 Set the countdown to 14.
E8BB JP Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling Jump to Continue_Sabreman_Tumbling.
