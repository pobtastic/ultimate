AF56: Set Up Room Objects
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Sets up the objects of a room that is in the lower part of the map (the room y is 10 or more) when the room is drawn.
The two decoration records that start at Object_Record_10 are emptied. The two before them are emptied too, unless they hold a bubble. What happens next depends on the first byte of the world map entry of the room (see Draw_Ceiling).
A room with both a ceiling and a floor gets a plant and a rock. Their x positions come from the table at Room_Object_X_Positions, and the rock is one of the four templates at Rock_Templates.
A room with no floor gets the objects that are set up by Set_Up_Stalactites. A room with a floor but no ceiling gets the two objects of type 54 that start at Spikes_Templates.
