B6F4: Check Weapon Record Hit
Used by the routine at Check_Weapon_Hit.
Checks whether a weapon is touching an object. The weapon hits the object if it is in the same room, less than 10 pixels away horizontally and less than 10 pixels away vertically (allowing 04 pixels for the difference in their sizes). When it hits, bit 0 of byte 07 of the weapon is set, which makes Move_Thrown_Weapon remove the weapon.
Input
HL Weapon record
IX Object record
Output
D FF if the weapon hits the object, otherwise 00
E Type of the weapon
