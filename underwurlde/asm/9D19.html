9D19: Draw Top Right Corner Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws a picture of the top right corner of the frame of a special room, at x position E8 and y position 10 (see Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Picture).
Input
DE Address of the picture
Draw_Top_Right_Corner_Picture 9D19 LD HL,$10E8 Set the x position to E8 and the y position to 10 (10E8).
9D1C JR Draw_Corner_Picture Jump to Draw_Corner_Picture to draw the picture.
