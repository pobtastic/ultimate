A021: Draw Lives
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Draws a small Sabreman icon and prints the number of lives left (see PlayerLives) at the top right of the screen. The entry at Draw_Lives_Print_Number only prints the number of lives.
Before After Draw_Lives
Draw_Lives A021 PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
A023 LD IX,$A03D Point at the object record of the icon (Extra_Life_Icon_Record).
A027 CALL DrawEntity Draw the icon.
A02A CALL ColourEntity Colour the icon.
A02D POP IX Restore IX from the stack.
This entry point is used by the routines at Collect_Extra_Life and Handle_Death.
Draw_Lives_Print_Number A02F LD HL,$08F0 Set the x position to F0 and the y position to 08 (08F0).
A032 CALL ScreenAddress Convert the position into a screen address.
A035 LD DE,$8EDA Point at the number of lives (PlayerLives).
A038 LD B,$01 Set the number of digits to print to 01.
A03A JP Print_Status_Panel_Digits Jump to Print_Status_Panel_Digits to print the digits.
