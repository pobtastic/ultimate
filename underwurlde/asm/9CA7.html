9CA7: Draw Floor Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws the picture of the floor of a special room, at x position 18 and y position A0. The picture is 1A bytes wide and 20 lines high (see Room_Graphics_Floor).
The attributes of the picture are bright magenta (4B). The picture is drawn by Draw_Wide_Picture.
Draw_Floor_Picture 9CA7 LD HL,$A018 Set the x position to 18 and the y position to A0 (A018).
9CAA LD DE,$78A3 Point at the picture (78A3).
9CAD LD C,$4B Set the attribute of the picture to 4B.
9CAF JR Draw_Wide_Picture Jump to Draw_Wide_Picture to draw the picture.
