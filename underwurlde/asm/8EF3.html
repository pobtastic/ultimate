8EF3: Score
The score, held as six BCD digits in three bytes. AddPointsToScore adds to it and prints it.
Score 8EF3 DEFB $00 The high byte (the two highest digits).
8EF4 DEFB $00 The middle byte.
Score_Low_Byte 8EF5 DEFB $00 The low byte (the two lowest digits).
