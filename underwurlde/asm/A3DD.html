A3DD: Reset Sabreman
Used by the routines at Run_Game and Handle_Death.
Copies the template at Sabreman_Template into the object record of Sabreman (Sabreman_Record).
Reset_Sabreman A3DD LD HL,$AA3C Point at the template of Sabreman (Sabreman_Template).
A3E0 LD DE,$8F1A Point at the object record of Sabreman (Sabreman_Record).
A3E3 LD BC,$0010 Set the number of bytes to copy to 10.
A3E6 LDIR Copy the template into the object record of Sabreman.
A3E8 RET Return.
