E2CB: Handle Carrying Eagle
Handler for the object types 5C to 5F an eagle that carries Sabreman (see Handle_Flying_Creature). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation, which follows bit 1 of the tick counter, and bit 1 is set when the x velocity is negative.
While Sabreman is being carried his record has the sprite id 60 to 63. The eagle puts his position in bytes 08 (x) and 09 (y) of his record (at Sabreman_Carried_X_Position and Sabreman_Carried_Y_Position), where Handle_Carried_Sabreman picks it up. The y position is 10 more than that of the eagle, so he hangs below it.
The eagle flies towards a target (byte 0C is the x position and byte 0E the y position, see Choose_Eagle_Target). Each call changes each velocity by one towards the target. The x velocity is kept between D0 and 37 and the y velocity between C7 and 37. The target changes when the eagle bounces off a decoration, a bubble, the floor, the ceiling or a wall. The eagle can also fly through the gaps in the frame of a room, and into the next room. The player can nudge the eagle with the controls (see Steer_Eagle).
The eagle lets go (see Check_Eagle_Release) when the low byte of FRAMES is zero, or when Sabreman has set bit 5 of his redraw flags, if it is in the open part of the room. If the sprite id of Sabreman is less than 1C (he is not being carried), the eagle lets go at once (see Release_Sabreman).
Input
IX Object record of the eagle
Handle_Carrying_Eagle E2CB CALL StoreEntity Save the state of the eagle.
E2CE LD A,($8F1A) Fetch the sprite id of Sabreman.
E2D1 CP $1C Jump to Release_Sabreman if he is not being carried.
E2D3 JP C,Release_Sabreman
E2D6 CALL Steer_Eagle Nudge the eagle if the player presses a direction.
E2D9 CALL Read_World_Map Read the world map entry of the room that the eagle is in. The flags of the room are returned in E.
E2DC LD A,($5C78) Fetch the low byte of FRAMES.
E2DF AND A Call Check_Eagle_Release to let go of Sabreman if the low byte of FRAMES is zero.
E2E0 CALL Z,Check_Eagle_Release
E2E3 LD A,($8F29) Fetch the redraw flags of Sabreman.
E2E6 BIT 5,A Test bit 5, which Sabreman sets to ask the eagle to let go.
E2E8 RES 5,A Reset bit 5.
E2EA LD ($8F29),A Store the redraw flags of Sabreman.
E2ED CALL NZ,Check_Eagle_Release Call Check_Eagle_Release to let go of Sabreman if bit 5 was set.
