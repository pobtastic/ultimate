9BB0: Right Wall Tile Table
The addresses of the tiles of the right wall, indexed by twice the sum of the room x and the room y. Each tile is three bytes wide and 38 lines high, and is preceded by its height and width (see Draw_Tile).
Right_Wall_Tile_Table 9BB0 DEFW $BD03,$BDAD,$BE57,$BF01
