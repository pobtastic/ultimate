A925: Handle Sabreman Tumbling
Handler for the object types 18 to 1B which are the tumbling sprites of Sabreman. The sprite id is 18 plus bits 1 and 2 of the tick counter at GameTimer, so the frames follow each other as time passes.
He tumbles after the stalactite or a creature has hit him and when he bumps into a side wall while he is falling. The routine decreases the countdown, checks for contact with the bubbles and the creatures (which push him sideways with an x velocity of 02 or FE), moves him sideways and applies gravity in the same way as Handle_Sabreman_Falling, and goes on at Handle_Sabreman_Falling_Above_Floor once the y position is less than 9F.
Unlike the falling handler the y velocity here has a limit of 76.

Apply gravity. The y velocity is increased by 02 each time up to a limit of 76 and the y position is changed by the y velocity divided by 10. A y position of 9F or more sends him to Handle_Sabreman_Falling_Reach_Floor when the room has a floor.
