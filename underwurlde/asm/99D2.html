99D2: Clear Panel
Used by the routines at Draw_Top_Right_Corner, Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner, Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner and Draw_Top_Left_Corner.
Clears a rectangle of the screen to the right of the position in HL. There are three entry points, which select the size of the rectangle. They stand in for the parts of the frame of the room (see Draw_Room) that are not present.
Each of those routines stashed DE on the stack, so this routine ends by restoring it. The caller is returned to by the last instruction.
Before After Clear_Panel
Input
HL Position (L is the x position, H is the y position)
Clear_Panel 99D2 LD BC,$0304 Set the size of the area to 03 bytes wide and 04 lines high (0304).
99D5 JR Clear_Panel_Rectangle Jump to Clear_Panel_Rectangle.
This entry point is used by the routine at Draw_Right_Wall.
Clear_Tall_Panel 99D7 LD BC,$0370 Set the size of the area to 03 bytes wide and 70 lines high (0370).
99DA JR Clear_Panel_Rectangle Jump to Clear_Panel_Rectangle.
This entry point is used by the routine at Draw_Ceiling.
Clear_Wide_Panel 99DC LD BC,$1A20 Set the size of the area to 1A bytes wide and 20 lines high (1A20).
Clear_Panel_Rectangle 99DF CALL ScreenAddress Convert the position into a screen address.
99E2 LD E,$00 Set the byte to write to 00.
99E4 LD D,B Keep the width of the rectangle for the next line.
Clear_Panel_Loop 99E5 LD (HL),E Clear a byte of the line.
99E6 INC L Move on to the next byte.
99E7 DJNZ Clear_Panel_Loop Decrease the width counter and loop back until the line is clear.
99E9 LD B,D Fetch the width of the rectangle again.
99EA LD A,L Fetch the low byte of the screen address.
99EB SUB B Subtract the width to move back to the start of the line.
99EC LD L,A Store the low byte of the address.
99ED CALL ScreenPosition1PixelBelow Move down one pixel line.
99F0 DEC C Decrease the line counter and loop back until all of the lines are clear.
99F1 JR NZ,Clear_Panel_Loop
99F3 POP DE Restore DE from the stack.
99F4 RET Return.
