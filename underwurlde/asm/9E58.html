9E58: Print Depth
Used by the routine at Print_Status_Panel.
Prints the text DEPTH and the depth of the room that Sabreman is in. The depth is worked out from the room y by adding one to the BCD base held in Status_Panel_Data for each row in the band. The entry at 9E66 leaves out the text.
