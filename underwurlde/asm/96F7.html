96F7: Display Entity
Used by the routine at Update_Object_Sprite.
Erases the sprite at its old position and draws it at the new one.
Both sprites are drawn with XOR, a row at a time, so that the rows are alternated between the two sprites and the screen is never without the sprite for long. If the sprites are offset vertically, the rows of the sprite that has no matching row in the other one are drawn first.
