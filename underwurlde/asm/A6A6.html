A6A6: Handle Sabreman Falling
Handler for the object types 15 and 16 which are the falling sprites of Sabreman (15 when he falls to the left and 16 when he falls to the right). It is also where he lands from a jump or a fall.
The routine checks for contact with the falling stalactite, deals with the special states in the same way as Handle_Sabreman_Walking, lets him steer while he is not moving sideways, checks for contact with the bubbles and the creatures, moves him sideways and then applies the pull of gravity to the y velocity, which is increased by 02 each time up to a limit of 4C. The y position changes by one sixteenth of the y velocity.
He lands when the y position reaches 9F. Landing after a fall that reaches a y velocity of 74 or more kills him (see Start_Dying).

The countdown is zero. Now the routine checks whether he is falling onto the rope (the type 17 object, see Handle_Rope). The conditions are that the room y is 10 or more, that the number in Rope_Length (the length of the rope) is not less than C, that his x position is within 10 of 78 (where the rope hangs), and that record 3 holds the rope and Rope_Displacement_Pixels is zero. When they hold, he grabs the rope: the sprite id is set to 01 record 3 is cleared (from then on Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope looks after the rope), and the second byte of the world map entry of each room below him is cleared down to the first room that has a floor.
