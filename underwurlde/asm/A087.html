A087: Play Alternating Tone
Used by the routine at Handle_Sabreman_Walking.
Plays a short tone on one call in every four. Each call adds one to the sound counter (see Sound_Counter) and it returns at once unless the two low bits of the counter are zero. When it does play, it uses a half period of 40 if bit 2 of the counter is set and 60 if it is clear, and plays four cycles.
Play_Alternating_Tone A087 LD HL,$8EF6 Point at the sound counter (Sound_Counter).
A08A INC (HL) Add one to the counter.
A08B LD A,(HL) Fetch the counter.
A08C AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits of the sound counter.
A08E RET NZ Return unless the sound counter is a multiple of four.
A08F BIT 2,(HL) Jump to Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower if bit 2 of the counter is clear.
A091 JR Z,Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower
A093 LD BC,$4004 BC=4004 (half period 40 four cycles).
A096 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
Play_Alternating_Tone_Lower A098 LD BC,$6004 BC=6004 (half period 60 four cycles).
A09B JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
