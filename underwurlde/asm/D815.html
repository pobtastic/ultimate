D815: Sprite: Debris
The four pieces of debris. The first is the falling piece of stalactite, which is the object type 50 (see Handle_Falling_Stalactite) and also the tile that is used to draw the structure of the type 88 (see Handle_Rock_Formation). The other three are the types 51 to 53 which are small pieces of rock (see Handle_Rock_Fragments).
