B079: Handle Flying Creature
Handler for the object types 30 to 33 (eagles) and 40 to 43 (vultures). The creature flies towards Sabreman, bounces off the decorations and the bubbles, bounces off the frame of the room and flies out of the room through its gaps.
If the creature is in a different room from Sabreman it is moved by Move_Creature_Horizontally (when the room x is different) or by Move_Creature_Vertically (when the room y is different).
In Sabreman's room, an eagle that is above him and touches him picks him up (the type changes to 5C and Handle_Carrying_Eagle takes over). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation and changes every two ticks, and bit 1 follows the direction of the x velocity.
The x velocity and the y velocity (bytes 08 and 09 of the record) each change by two on every call, in the direction of Sabreman, up to a limit of 40. A thrown weapon destroys the creature (see Destroy_Flying_Creature). If the creature touches Sabreman he is pushed away with an x velocity of 03 and tumbles.
The contact flags (byte 0B of the record) use the same bits as the flags of the room (see Draw_Room), and they are combined with those flags in E.
