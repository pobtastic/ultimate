9563: Clear Workspace
Used by the routine at Run_Game.
Sets up the registers to clear the 19 bytes of the sprite drawing workspace and the other variables from Previous_Sprite_Id to Creature_Spawn_Flag (which includes the score and the completion percentage).
Clear_Workspace 9563 LD BC,$0019 Set the number of bytes of the workspace to clear to 19.
9566 LD HL,$8EE1 Point at the start of the workspace (Previous_Sprite_Id).
Continue on to FillMemory.
