E570: Handle Carried Sabreman
Handler for the object types 60 to 63 which are the sprites of Sabreman while an eagle carries him (see Handle_Carrying_Eagle). Bit 1 of the type is set when he moves to the left and bit 0 follows bit 1 of the tick counter.
His position is taken from bytes 08 (x) and 09 (y) of his record, which the eagle sets. If bit 7 of the redraw flags is set (he has been hit) bit 5 is set to ask the eagle to let go, and the countdown in byte 0A is set to 0F.
If no eagle was carrying him in the last pass of the main loop (see Previous_Type_5C_Active_Flag) he drops, and so does he when bit 6 of the redraw flags is set (see Release_Sabreman). He starts to tumble, with the sprite id 18 and a y velocity of 10.
Touching a decoration puts him on top of it when he is on top of it or near its middle. If he touches the side of one he sets bit 5 to ask the eagle to let go. He is kept within the walls of the room. If the fire key is pressed he throws a weapon (see Throw_Weapon).
