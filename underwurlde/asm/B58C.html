B58C: Move Thrown Weapon
Used by the routines at Handle_Fire and Handle_Projectile.
Moves a weapon in flight. The projectiles (see Handle_Projectile) and the fire (see Handle_Fire) are moved by this routine too.
The object is removed if Sabreman is not in its room, or if bit 0 of byte 07 is set, which Check_Weapon_Record_Hit does when the weapon hits a creature. If it touches a decoration (see Check_Decoration_Contact) it bursts into dust (see Burst_Into_Dust) unless it lands on top, when it bounces.
The y velocity increases by four each time, up to 78. The x and y positions are moved by the velocities (multiplied by sixteen, with the fractions in bytes 0A and 0D). The weapon bursts when it hits a wall, bounces off the floor (making a puff of dust) and off the ceiling, and is removed when it leaves the room through a gap. While it is in a gap the contact flags of the weapon and the flags of the room are changed, as for a creature (see Handle_Flying_Creature_Set_Gap_Flags).
