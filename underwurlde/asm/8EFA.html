8EFA: Object Records
Nineteen records of 10 bytes each. Run_Game works through them one by one, using byte 00 of each record (the type of the object, which is also its sprite id) as an index into the handler table at Object_Handler_Table. A type of 00 marks a record that is not in use.
The first two records are the weapons that Sabreman throws. They are handled only as priority records (see Finish_Object_Pass). The third record is Sabreman. Reset_Sabreman fills it in using the template at Sabreman_Template.
Some of the other records have fixed uses: the fourth is the rope (type 17 which Set_Up_Rope sets up when the room has a rope) and the fifth is a falling stalactite (see Spawn_Falling_Stalactite).
Byte Contents
00 Type of the object (the sprite id)
01 X position
02 Room x
03 Y position
04 Room y
05 Attribute
06-0F Used differently by each type of handler
Most handlers use bit 0 of byte 0F (or of byte 07) as a flag to say that the sprite has no old image to erase (see Update_Object_Sprite). Byte 09 is often a vertical speed and bytes 0A and 0D are often the fractions of the x and y positions.
