E622: Handle Stalactite
Handler for the object types 3C 3D 55 56 and 57 the stalactites that hang from the ceiling of a cave room. They are made by Build_Stalactite from the templates at Stalactite_Templates. A stalactite does not move.
It is drawn once, when bit 0 of its redraw flags is set. At the same time, if bit 3 of byte 06 is set, the stalactite has a gem in it. The gem is made as a copy of the record of the stalactite in the record that is 20 bytes on, with the type 64 plus the two low bits of the sum of the room x, the room y and byte 06 (so there are four kinds of gem). Its y position is that of the stalactite less the height of the stalactite (byte 0C), its attribute is 45 (cyan) and it is handled by Handle_Collectable. Byte 06 of the stalactite is then cleared, so that only one gem is made.
The attributes of the cells that the stalactite covers are copied from the colour data on every call (see Colour_Data), which keeps the stalactite coloured whatever passes in front of it. The colour data is chosen by the two low bits of the type, using the pointers at Stalactite_Colour_Pointers.
