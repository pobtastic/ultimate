9AF8: Draw Bottom Right Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the bottom right corner of the frame of the room if bit 3 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is drawn at x position E8 y position A0 by Draw_Corner_Shape. Its shape depends on whether the floor (bit 4) and the right wall (bit 2) are present.
Input
DE Frame flags of the room in E
IX Object record of Sabreman
Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner 9AF8 PUSH DE Stash the frame flags on the stack.
9AF9 LD HL,$A0E8 Set the x position to E8 and the y position to A0 (A0E8).
9AFC LD A,E Copy the frame flags to A.
9AFD LD DE,$9B18 Point at the table of corner shapes (Bottom_Right_Corner_Table).
9B00 BIT 3,A Jump to 9B07 to clear the area if the corner is not present.
9B02 JP Z,Clear_Panel
9B04 SBC A,C
9B05 PUSH HL Stash the position on the stack.
9B06 EX DE,HL Exchange the registers so that HL holds the address of the table.
9B07 BIT 4,A Jump to Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Check_Right_Wall if the floor is not present.
9B09 JR Z,Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Check_Right_Wall
9B0B INC HL Skip the shapes for a floor.
9B0C INC HL
Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Check_Right_Wall 9B0D BIT 2,A Jump to Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Draw_Shape if the right wall is not present.
9B0F JR Z,Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Draw_Shape
9B11 INC HL Skip the shapes for a right wall.
9B12 INC HL
9B13 INC HL
9B14 INC HL
Draw_Bottom_Right_Corner_Draw_Shape 9B15 JP Draw_Corner_Shape Jump to Draw_Corner_Shape to draw the shape.
