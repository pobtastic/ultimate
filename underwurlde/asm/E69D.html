E69D: Stalactite Colour Pointers
Pointers into the colour data at Colour_Data, which are chosen by the two low bits of the type of the stalactite (see Handle_Stalactite).
Stalactite_Colour_Pointers E69D DEFW $DC1D Type 3C: the colour data at DC1D.
E69F DEFW Colour_Data Types 3D and 55: the colour data at Colour_Data.
E6A1 DEFW $DC1D Type 56: the colour data at DC1D.
E6A3 DEFW $DC2F Type 57: the colour data at DC2F.
