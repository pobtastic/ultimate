B358: Move Creature Vertically
Used by the routines at Handle_Flying_Creature, Move_Creature_Horizontally, Handle_Jellyfish and Handle_Gargoyle.
Moves a creature one step closer to Sabreman's room when it is in a different room y (and the same room x). It works like Move_Creature_Horizontally but vertically. It is entered with the carry flag set if Sabreman's room y is less than the room y of the creature.
The creature is removed if the room has a floor and it is above Sabreman's room (or a ceiling and it is below), as it cannot get through. The x position is kept between 18 and E0.
Input
IX Object record
E Flags of the room that the creature is in

B35C LD (IX+$0B),$00 Clear the contact flags.
B360 JR C,Move_Creature_Vertically_Move_Up Jump to Move_Creature_Vertically_Move_Up if Sabreman is above the creature.

B388 INC (IX+$04) Move the creature to the room below.
B38B LD A,$2C Set the y position to 2C.
