9CE8: Draw Right Wall Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws the picture of the right wall of a special room, at x position E8 and y position 30. The picture is three bytes wide and 70 lines high (see Room_Graphics_Right_Wall).
The attributes of the picture are bright cyan (4D). The picture is drawn by Draw_Wall_Picture.
Draw_Right_Wall_Picture 9CE8 LD HL,$30E8 Set the x position to E8 and the y position to 30 (30E8).
9CEB LD DE,$8217 Point at the picture (8217).
9CEE LD C,$4D Set the attribute of the picture to 4D.
9CF0 JR Draw_Wall_Picture Jump to Draw_Wall_Picture to draw the picture.
