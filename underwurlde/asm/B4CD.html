B4CD: Adjust Throw Facing
Used by the routine at Throw_Weapon.
Subtracts two (instead of one) from the type of Sabreman when Throw_Weapon works out which way he is facing, as his type is 15 or more. It carries on at Throw_Weapon_Test_Facing.
Input
A Type of Sabreman
Adjust_Throw_Facing B4CD SUB $02 Subtract two from the type.
B4CF JR Throw_Weapon_Test_Facing Jump to Throw_Weapon_Test_Facing.
