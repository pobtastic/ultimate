B4D1: Throw Weapon
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_On_Rope, Handle_Sabreman_Walking, Handle_Sabreman_Falling and Handle_Carried_Sabreman.
Throws a weapon that Sabreman is carrying. Nothing happens if bit 0 of the redraw flags of Sabreman is set or if he is not carrying a weapon (one of the types 68 to 6B in the three slots from Weapon_Slots).
One weapon at a time is thrown unless the counter in Fire_Key_Flag allows another: the counter is reset while both weapon records are empty, and while a weapon is in flight it goes up each time this routine is called, so a weapon is thrown only when its low three bits are zero.
The weapon record is a free one of the two records at Object_Records and Second_Weapon_Record. The type, the position and the attribute are copied from Sabreman. The type of the weapon is then changed to the type of the projectile for the weapon that was carried (from the table at Weapon_Projectile_Types), plus one if it is thrown to the left. The weapon starts 08 pixels to the side of Sabreman and 08 pixels higher, with an x velocity between 40 and 7F and an upward y velocity that comes from the frame counter. Bit 2 of the type of Sabreman, less one (or less two if his type is 15 or more), tells which way he is facing (set for the right). The weapon is not thrown if there is no room to its left.
