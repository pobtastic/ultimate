9D1E: Draw Bottom Left Corner Picture
Used by the routine at Draw_Special_Room_Parts.
Draws a picture of the bottom left corner of the frame of a special room, at x position 00 and y position A0 (see Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Picture).
Input
DE Address of the picture
Draw_Bottom_Left_Corner_Picture 9D1E LD HL,$A000 Set the x position to 00 and the y position to A0 (A000).
9D21 JR Draw_Corner_Picture Jump to Draw_Corner_Picture to draw the picture.
