E741: Stalactite Template Pointers
Two tables of four pointers to the templates of the stalactites at Stalactite_Templates, which are chosen by bits 1 and 2 of the byte that describes the stalactite (see Build_Stalactite).
The first table is used for the first stalactite of a room (on the left, with an x position from 18) and the second for the second stalactite (on the right, with an x position from A0).
The first table, with the templates of the types 55 56 57 and 57.
Stalactite_Template_Pointers E741 DEFW Stalactite_Templates
E743 DEFW $B7DB
E745 DEFW $B7EB
E747 DEFW $B7EB
The second table, with the templates of the types 3C 3D 57 and 57.
Stalactite_Template_Pointers_Right E749 DEFW $B7FB
E74B DEFW $B80B
E74D DEFW $B7EB
E74F DEFW $B7EB
