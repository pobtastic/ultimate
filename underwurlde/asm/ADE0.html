ADE0: Calculate Attribute Address
Used by the routines at MenuWriteText, ColourEntity, Set_Area_Attributes, PrintStringColour, Draw_Ceiling_Picture, Draw_Left_Wall_Picture, Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Picture, Draw_Line, Handle_Stalactite and Handle_Rock_Formation.
Converts a pixel position into the address of the matching cell of the attribute buffer.
Input
HL Position (L is the x position, H is the y position)
Output
HL Attribute buffer address
AttributeAddress ADE0 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
ADE1 LD A,L Fetch the x position.
ADE2 RRCA Divide it by 08.
ADE3 RRCA
ADE4 RRCA
ADE5 AND $1F Keep only five bits.
ADE7 LD L,A Store the column in L.
ADE8 LD A,H Fetch the y position.
ADE9 RLCA Multiply it by 04.
ADEA RLCA
ADEB LD C,A Store the result in C.
ADEC AND $E0 Keep only the bits that give the low part of the row.
ADEE OR L Combine them with the column.
ADEF LD L,A Store the low byte of the address in L.
ADF0 LD A,C Fetch the multiplied y position.
ADF1 AND $03 Keep only the bits that give the high part of the row.
ADF3 OR $58 Add the start of the attribute buffer.
ADF5 LD H,A Store the high byte of the address in H.
ADF6 POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
ADF7 RET Return.
