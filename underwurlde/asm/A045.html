A045: Add Points To Score
Used by the routines at Destroy_Creature and Handle_Collectable.
Adds to the score (see Score) and prints the new score at the top of the screen.
The amount is a pair of BCD bytes: B holds the hundreds and C the units. One is taken off C before it is added, so when C is not zero the score goes up by B hundreds plus C minus one. When C is 00 it goes up by B hundreds plus 99.
Before After AddPointsToScore
Input
B Hundreds to add, as two BCD digits
C Units to add, as two BCD digits (see above)
