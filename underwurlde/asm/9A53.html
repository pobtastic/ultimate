9A53: Draw Top Right Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the top right corner of the frame of the room if bit 1 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is three bytes wide and 20 lines high and is drawn at x position E8 y position 10. The shape depends on whether the ceiling (bit 0) and the right wall (bit 2) are present, and on the room x and y: the table at Top_Right_Corner_Table has a set of four shapes for each of four combinations of the room coordinates. The routine also draws the other corners, which jump to Draw_Corner_Shape.
