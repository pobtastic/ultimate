DF98: Handle Falling Stalactite
Handler for the object type 50 a stalactite that falls from the ceiling towards Sabreman (see Spawn_Falling_Stalactite).
The y velocity (byte 09) goes up by two on every call, to a limit of 6D and the y position and its fraction (byte 0D) change by sixteen times the velocity. The stalactite lands on a decoration if it touches the top of one near the middle of it. It lands on the floor if the room has one, and if the room has no floor it falls into the room below. When it lands it becomes the type 51 (see Handle_Rock_Fragments) and is not a stalactite any more.
If Sabreman is not in the room the stalactite carries on falling but is not drawn. The countdown in byte 0A which is reset to 50 while Sabreman is in the room, removes it when he has been away for long enough.
Input
IX Object record
Handle_Falling_Stalactite DF98 CALL StoreEntity Save the state of the stalactite.
DF9B LD A,(IX+$09) Fetch the y velocity.
DF9E ADD A,$02 Add two to the y velocity, as gravity pulls the stalactite down.
DFA0 CP $6E Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Move if the stalactite is falling at full speed (the y velocity has reached 6E).
DFA2 JR NC,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Move
DFA4 LD (IX+$09),A Store the y velocity.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Move DFA7 LD L,(IX+$09) Fetch the y velocity into L.
DFAA LD H,$00 Clear the high byte of the y velocity (as a word).
DFAC ADD HL,HL Multiply the y velocity by sixteen.
DFAD ADD HL,HL
DFAE ADD HL,HL
DFAF ADD HL,HL
DFB0 LD C,(IX+$0D) Fetch the fraction of the y position.
DFB3 LD B,(IX+$03) Fetch the y position.
DFB6 ADD HL,BC Add the y velocity.
DFB7 LD (IX+$0D),L Store the new fraction of the y position.
DFBA LD (IX+$03),H Store the new y position.
DFBD CALL Read_World_Map Read the world map entry of the room that the stalactite is in. The flags of the room are returned in E.
DFC0 LD A,E Copy the flags of the room to A.
DFC1 AND %00010000 Keep only bit 4 (the floor).
DFC3 LD A,H Fetch the new y position.
DFC4 JR Z,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_No_Floor Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_No_Floor if the room has no floor.
DFC6 CP $9F Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Land_On_Floor if the stalactite has reached the floor.
DFC8 JR NC,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Land_On_Floor
DFCA JR Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Decorations Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Decorations.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_No_Floor DFCC CP $BF Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Next_Room if the stalactite has fallen out of the bottom of the room.
DFCE JP NC,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Next_Room
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Decorations DFD1 CALL Check_Decoration_Contact Check for contact with the decorations.
DFD4 BIT 1,C Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Room if the stalactite is not on top of a decoration.
DFD6 JR Z,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Room
DFD8 BIT 0,C Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Room if the stalactite is not near the middle of the decoration.
DFDA JR NZ,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Room
DFDC LD A,B Copy A0 less the y position of the top of the decoration to A.
DFDD NEG Negate it.
DFDF ADD A,$A1 Add A1 to get the y position just below the top of the decoration.
DFE1 JR Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Shatter Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Shatter.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Land_On_Floor DFE3 LD A,(IX+$03) Fetch the y position.
DFE6 AND %11111000 Round it down to a multiple of eight.
DFE8 DEC A Subtract one.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Shatter DFE9 LD (IX+$03),A Store the y position.
DFEC LD (IX+$00),$51 Change the stalactite into the first piece of rock.
DFF0 LD (IX+$06),$00 Reset the counter in byte 06.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Check_Room DFF4 LD A,($8F1C) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
DFF7 CP (IX+$02) Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Count_Down if the stalactite is in a different room x.
DFFA JP NZ,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Count_Down
DFFD LD A,($8F1E) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
E000 CP (IX+$04) Jump to Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Count_Down if the stalactite is in a different room y.
E003 JP NZ,Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Count_Down
E006 LD (IX+$0A),$50 Reset the countdown to 50.
E00A JP Update_Object_Sprite Jump to Update_Object_Sprite.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Next_Room E00D LD (IX+$03),$40 Set the y position to 40.
E011 INC (IX+$04) Move the stalactite to the room below.
E014 BIT 0,(IX+$0F) Return if the stalactite has not been drawn yet.
E018 RET NZ
E019 JP RemoveEntity Erase the sprite of the stalactite.
Handle_Falling_Stalactite_Count_Down E01C DEC (IX+$0A) Decrease the countdown and jump to Remove_Unseen_Weapon to remove the stalactite when it reaches zero.
E01F JP Z,Remove_Unseen_Weapon
E022 SET 0,(IX+$0F) Set bit 0 of the redraw flags, as the stalactite is not on the screen.
E026 RET Return.
