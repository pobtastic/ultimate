EB7E: Decoration Size Table
Five bytes for each of the object types 74 to 7E which Set_Decoration_Size copies to the bytes 0A to 0E of the object record. They are the half width of the decoration, the width of its middle, its height, the depth from the top at which an object counts as being beside it and the offset of its middle from its x position (see Check_Object_Contact).
