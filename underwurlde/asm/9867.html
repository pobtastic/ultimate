9867: Colour Entity
Used by the routines at Draw_Menu_Icons, Update_Object_Sprite, Draw_Lives, Handle_Static_Object, Handle_Collectable and Handle_Weapon_Pickup.
Sets the attributes of the character cells that the sprite of the object covers to the attribute of the object. The width in cells and the height in pixels come from New_Sprite_Width and New_Sprite_Height, which are set when the sprite is prepared. It works upwards from the bottom row of cells.
The number of rows of cells is worked out from the number of pixel lines of the sprite and the position of the bottom of the sprite within its character cell.
