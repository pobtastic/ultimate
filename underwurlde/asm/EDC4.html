EDC4: Extra Life Table
The sixteen places of the extra lives, with three bytes for each: the room x, the room y and the collected flag. The flag is set to 01 by Collect_Extra_Life when Sabreman picks up the extra life and is cleared at the start of a game by Clear_Collected_Flags. Place_Special_Object looks for the room of Sabreman in this table.
Extra_Life_Table EDC4 DEFB $0A,$0B The room x and room y of the first extra life.
Extra_Life_Table_First_Flag EDC6 DEFB $00 The collected flag of the first extra life.
EDC7 DEFB $0D,$0E,$00 The other fifteen extra lives.
