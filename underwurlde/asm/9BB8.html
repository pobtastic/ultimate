9BB8: Draw Top Left Corner
Used by the routine at Draw_Room.
Draws the top left corner of the frame of the room if bit 7 of the frame flags is set, or clears its area if it is not.
The corner is drawn at x position 00 y position 10 by Draw_Corner_Shape. Its shape depends on whether the ceiling (bit 0) and the left wall (bit 6) are present.
Input
DE Frame flags of the room in E
IX Object record of Sabreman
Draw_Top_Left_Corner 9BB8 PUSH DE Stash the frame flags on the stack.
9BB9 LD HL,$1000 Set the x position to 00 and the y position to 10 (1000).
9BBC LD A,E Copy the frame flags to A.
9BBD LD DE,$9BD8 Point at the table of corner shapes (Top_Left_Corner_Table).
9BC0 BIT 7,A Jump to 9BC7 to clear the area if the corner is not present.
9BC2 JP Z,Clear_Panel
9BC4 SBC A,C
9BC5 PUSH HL Stash the position on the stack.
9BC6 EX DE,HL Exchange the registers so that HL holds the address of the table.
9BC7 BIT 0,A Jump to Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall if the ceiling is not present.
9BC9 JR Z,Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall
9BCB INC HL Skip the shapes for a ceiling.
9BCC INC HL
Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Check_Left_Wall 9BCD BIT 6,A Jump to Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape if the left wall is not present.
9BCF JR Z,Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape
9BD1 INC HL Skip the shapes for a left wall.
9BD2 INC HL
9BD3 INC HL
9BD4 INC HL
Draw_Top_Left_Corner_Draw_Shape 9BD5 JP Draw_Corner_Shape Jump to Draw_Corner_Shape to draw the shape.
