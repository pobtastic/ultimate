ECCA: Handle Guardian
Handler for the object types 80 to 87 the four guardians that stand in the caves: the imps (80 81 84 and 85 see Sprite_Imp_1), the spiders (82 and 83 see Sprite_Spider_1) and the bats (86 and 87 see Sprite_Bat_1). Bit 0 of the type is the frame of the animation and it is flipped whenever bit 0 of the tick counter is set. Place_Guardian puts a guardian in its room, where it stays until Sabreman leaves the room (see Handle_Collectable_Remove).
A guardian is destroyed by one kind of weapon, which depends on bits 1 and 2 of its type: the daggers (the types 6C and 6D) destroy a spider, the arrows (6E and 6F) destroy the imp of the type 84 and the fire (70 and 71) destroys a bat. The imp of the type 80 cannot be destroyed. When a guardian is destroyed its collected flag is set (see Set_Collected_Flag), the flag at Creature_Spawn_Flag is set and the guardian explodes (see Destroy_Creature).
A guardian that is touched by Sabreman makes him tumble (bit 7 of his redraw flags is set) and pushes him to the left (his x velocity is set to FE).
Input
IX Object record of the guardian
Handle_Guardian ECCA CALL StoreEntity Save the state of the guardian.
ECCD LD A,($8F1C) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
ECD0 CP (IX+$02) Jump to Handle_Collectable_Remove to remove the guardian if it is in a different room x.
ECD3 JP NZ,Handle_Collectable_Remove
ECD6 LD A,($8F1E) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
ECD9 CP (IX+$04) Jump to Handle_Collectable_Remove to remove the guardian if it is in a different room y.
ECDC JP NZ,Handle_Collectable_Remove
ECDF LD A,($8ED6) Fetch the tick counter.
ECE2 AND %00000001 Test bit 0 of the tick counter.
ECE4 JR Z,Handle_Guardian_Check_Weapon Jump to Handle_Guardian_Check_Weapon if it is not set.
ECE6 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the type of the guardian.
ECE9 XOR %00000001 Flip the frame of the animation.
ECEB LD (IX+$00),A Store the type.
Handle_Guardian_Check_Weapon ECEE CALL Check_Weapon_Hit Check whether a weapon has hit the guardian.
ECF1 LD A,D Jump to Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman if no weapon has hit it.
ECF2 AND A
ECF3 JR Z,Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman
ECF5 LD A,E Fetch the type of the weapon into A.
ECF6 CP $6C Jump to Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman if the weapon is not a dagger, an arrow or fire.
ECF8 JR C,Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman
ECFA RRA Halve the type of the weapon.
ECFB DEC A Subtract one.
ECFC AND %00000011 Keep the two low bits, which gives the kind of weapon (01 for the daggers, 02 for the arrows and 03 for the fire).
ECFE LD E,A Store the kind in E.
ECFF LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the type of the guardian.
ED02 RRA Halve it.
ED03 AND %00000011 Keep the two low bits, which gives the kind of weapon that destroys the guardian.
ED05 CP E Jump to Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman if the weapon is not the right one for the guardian.
ED06 JR NZ,Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman
ED08 LD HL,$EDF6 Point HL at Guardian_Table_First_Flag.
ED0B CALL Set_Collected_Flag Set the collected flag of this guardian.
ED0E LD A,$01 Set the flag that shows that a guardian has been destroyed.
ED10 LD ($8EF9),A
ED13 JP Destroy_Creature Jump to Destroy_Creature to make the guardian explode.
Handle_Guardian_Check_Sabreman ED16 CALL Check_Sabreman_Contact_Clear_Result Check for contact with Sabreman, without testing his sprite id.
ED19 BIT 2,C Jump to Update_Object_Sprite to draw the guardian if Sabreman is not touching it.
ED1B JP Z,Update_Object_Sprite
ED1E LD A,($8F29) Fetch the redraw flags of Sabreman.
ED21 OR %10000000 Set bit 7, which makes him tumble.
ED23 LD ($8F29),A Store the flags.
ED26 LD A,$FE Set the x velocity of Sabreman to FE which pushes him to the left.
ED28 LD ($8F20),A Store the velocity.
ED2B JP Update_Object_Sprite Jump to Update_Object_Sprite to draw the guardian.
