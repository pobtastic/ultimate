DF15: Handle Explosion
Handler for the object types 4C to 4F (an explosion). It works like Handle_Dust, but the explosion starts with a counter of 09 and the two low bits of the type follow the two low bits of the counter, so the explosion goes through four frames.
Input
IX Object record
Handle_Explosion DF15 CALL StoreEntity Save the state of the explosion.
DF18 LD A,($8F1C) Fetch the room x of Sabreman.
DF1B CP (IX+$02) Jump to Remove_Unseen_Weapon to remove the explosion if it is in a different room x.
DF1E JP NZ,Remove_Unseen_Weapon
DF21 LD A,($8F1E) Fetch the room y of Sabreman.
DF24 CP (IX+$04) Jump to Remove_Unseen_Weapon to remove the explosion if it is in a different room y.
DF27 JP NZ,Remove_Unseen_Weapon
DF2A DEC (IX+$08) Decrease the counter and jump to Remove_Thrown_Weapon to remove the explosion when it reaches zero.
DF2D JP Z,Remove_Thrown_Weapon
DF30 LD A,(IX+$08) Fetch the counter.
DF33 AND %00000011 Keep only the two low bits.
DF35 LD B,A Store them in B.
DF36 LD A,(IX+$00) Fetch the type.
DF39 AND %11111100 Reset the two low bits.
DF3B ADD A,B Add the two low bits of the counter.
DF3C LD (IX+$00),A Store the type.
DF3F JP Update_Object_Sprite Jump to Update_Object_Sprite.
