DC0B: Colour Data
The attributes of the cells that the stalactites and some of the decorations cover. Handle_Stalactite and Handle_Coloured_Decoration copy them to the screen after the sprite has been drawn, using the pointers at Stalactite_Colour_Pointers and Decoration_Colour_Pointers. The attributes of an object are in rows, with the bottom row first, and each row is as wide as the sprite is in cells.
