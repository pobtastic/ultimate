A07D: Play Low Tone
Used by the routines at Handle_Sabreman_Falling and Handle_Sabreman_Tumbling.
Plays a tone of 40 cycles with a half period of 40.
Play_Low_Tone A07D LD BC,$4040 BC=4040 (half period 40 40 cycles).
A080 JR SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
