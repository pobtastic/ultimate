A173: Sounds: Bounce
Used by the routine at Make_Dust_Puff.
Plays four cycles of a tone with a half period of E7 which is a low pitch. The counter in D is set to 40 and the tone is made from the counter after one has been taken off it.
The routine jumps to the tone player, which returns to the caller.
SoundsBounce A173 LD D,$40 D=40 (the counter).
A175 DEC D Decrease the tone counter and return if it is zero (this does not happen when the routine is entered at the start).
A176 RET Z
A177 LD A,D Fetch the counter.
A178 RRCA Rotate the counter right three times.
A179 RRCA
A17A RRCA
A17B LD B,A Use the result as the half period.
A17C LD C,$04 Set the number of cycles to 04.
A17E JP SoundsBeep Jump to SoundsBeep to play the tone.
