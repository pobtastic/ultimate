E825: Clear Object Records
Used by the routines at Run_Game and Handle_Death.
Clears the object records from the record of Sabreman (at Sabreman_Record) up to the end of the records at Small_Object_Records, which are 0110 bytes.
Clear_Object_Records E825 LD HL,$8F1A Point HL at the record of Sabreman.
E828 LD BC,$0110 Set the number of bytes to clear to 0110.
Clear_Object_Records_Loop E82B LD (HL),$00 Clear a byte.
E82D INC HL Move on to the next byte.
E82E DEC BC Decrease the byte counter and loop back until all of the object records have been cleared.
E82F LD A,B
E830 OR C
E831 JR NZ,Clear_Object_Records_Loop
Continue on to Check_Stalactite_Contact.
