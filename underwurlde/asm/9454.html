9454: Pause Briefly
Used by the routine at Finish_Object_Pass.
A short delay loop. It is the handler of type 00 the type of an empty object record, so a record that is not in use costs a little time instead of none. It is also used to debounce the pause key.
Pause_Briefly 9454 XOR A Start the delay counter at 00 which makes 0100 passes round the loop.
Pause_Briefly_Loop 9455 DEC A Decrease the delay counter and loop back until it is zero.
9456 JR NZ,Pause_Briefly_Loop
9458 RET Return.
