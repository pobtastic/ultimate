7687: Special Room Pictures
The pictures that make up the frame of the special rooms (the rooms with a room y below 10), which are drawn by Draw_Special_Room_Frame. There are no tiles here: each part of the frame is a ready-made picture that is copied to the screen. \p A corner picture is three bytes wide and 20 lines high and starts with its twelve attribute bytes (three cells wide by four cells high), followed by the pixels, top line first. The ceiling and the floor are 1A bytes wide and 20 lines high and the walls are three bytes wide and 70 lines high; these have no attribute bytes, because their attributes are filled in by the code that draws them.
