9A33: Ceiling Tile Table
The addresses of the tiles of the ceiling, indexed by twice the sum of the room x and the room y. The eight tiles in the first half are repeated in the second half. Each tile is two bytes wide and 20 lines high, and is preceded by its height and width (see Draw_Tile).
