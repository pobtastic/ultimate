B7CB: Stalactite Templates
The object records of the stalactites that hang from the ceiling of a cave (the types 55 56 57 3C and 3D). Build_Stalactite builds the stalactites of a room from them (see Stalactite_Template_Pointers and Stalactite_Template_Pointers_Right). The routine at Handle_Stalactite handles them. Bytes 0A to 0E give the size of the stalactite as an obstacle (see Check_Object_Contact).
