E8D7: Handle Weapon Pickup
Handler for the object types 68 to 6B the four weapons that Sabreman can pick up (see Sprite_Weapon_1). The weapons are the small object records at Small_Object_Records. Byte 07 of a record holds the flags: bit 0 is set if there is no old image to erase, bit 1 is set while Sabreman carries the weapon and bit 2 is set while the weapon falls.
A weapon that lies on the floor is drawn again and again. When an action key has just been pressed (the flag at New_Action_Key_Press_Flag is FF) and Sabreman is touching it (see Check_Sabreman_Contact), he picks it up: the weapon moves to the x position 88 and the y position 0F which is its place in the panel at the top of the screen, bit 1 of the flags is set, the flag at New_Action_Key_Press_Flag is set to FE to show that the key press has been used, and the type of the weapon is stored in the first of the weapon slots at Weapon_Slots.
Each later key press moves a carried weapon 18 pixels to the right in the panel and stores its type in the weapon slot that goes with the new x position (the x positions A0 and B8 both give the third slot, and none gives the second). A key press when the weapon has reached B8 makes Sabreman drop it. It is put at his position, with bit 1 of the flags reset, bit 2 set and the counter in byte 06 set to 10 and it falls (see Handle_Weapon_Pickup_Fall).
A weapon that is in a different room is dealt with by Handle_Distant_Weapon.
